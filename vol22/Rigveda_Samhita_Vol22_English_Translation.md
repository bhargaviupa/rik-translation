॥ पंचमाष्टके तृतीयोध्यायः ॥ (*‖ paṃcamāṣṭake tṛtīyodhyāyaḥ ‖*)

The third chapter in the fifth Aṣṭaka

Hymns 20–31 of the seventh Maṇḍala

**Introduction**

In the seventh Maṇḍala, the fourteen hymns in all, from the 18th hymn to the 31st hymn, are addressed to Indra. Of these, hymns 18–19 are included in the preceding chapter, that is, in Ṛ. Saṃ. Part 21. Therefore in this Part the twelve hymns from 20 to 31 are in praise of Indra. Since we have described Indra at length in Ṛ. Saṃ. Part 5, pages 569–582, and Part 16, pages 631–683, and elsewhere, from here onward special matters will be explained only where the need appears.

The twentieth hymn

## Sūkta 20 (Maṇḍala 7)

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> अथ तृतीयोऽध्याय आरभ्यते ॥ उग्रो जज्ञे इति दशर्चं तृतीयं सूक्तं
> वसिष्ठस्यार्षं त्रैष्टुभमैंद्रं । तथा चानुक्रम्यते । उग्रो दशेति ॥ आभिप्लविके च

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Sāyaṇa says that now the third chapter begins. The hymn beginning उग्रो जज्ञे (*ugro jajñe*) has ten verses and is the third hymn. Its seer is Vasiṣṭha, its metre is Triṣṭubh and its deity is Indra. As the Anukramaṇī states: उग्रो दश (*ugro daśa*). In the Ābhiplavika rite, and further

> इति निष्केवल्यं । आ. ७-२ । इति ॥ महाव्रतेऽपि निष्केवल्य एतत्सूक्तं । तथैव पंचमारण्यके सूत्रितं । उग्रो जज्ञे वीर्याय स्वधावानुदु ब्रह्माण्यैरत श्रवस्या । ऐ.

> मध्यंदिनः । आ. ९-२ । इति ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

on the fourth day this hymn is the nivid-dhāna hymn in the Niṣkevalya Śastra. This is laid down in the sūtra: चतुर्थस्योग्रो जज्ञे इति निष्केवल्यं (*caturthasyogro jajñe iti niṣkevalyaṃ*) (Ā. 7-2). In the Mahāvrata too this hymn is used in the Niṣkevalya Śastra; so it is laid down in the fifth Āraṇyaka: उग्रो जज्ञे वीर्याय स्वधावानुदु ब्रह्माण्यैरत श्रवस्या (*ugro jajñe vīryāya svadhāvānudu brahmāṇyairata śravasyā*) (Ai. Ā. 5-2-9). In the Cāturmāsya rites connected with Soma, in the place of the Vaiśvadeva, the first Pṛṣṭhya day is observed; there too this hymn is the nivid-dhāna hymn in the Niṣkevalya Śastra. It is laid down in the sūtra: जनिष्ठा उग्र उग्रो जज्ञे इति मध्यंदिनः (*janiṣṭhā ugra ugro jajñe iti madhyaṃdinaḥ*) (Ā. 9-2).

Anuvāda: Since the explanation of the second chapter of the fifth Aṣṭaka was completed in the previous Part, the explanation of the third chapter will now be begun. This hymn beginning उग्रो जज्ञे (*ugro jajñe*) is the third hymn in the second Anuvāka of the seventh Maṇḍala. It has ten Ṛks. For this hymn Vasiṣṭha is the seer; Indra is the deity; the metre is Triṣṭup. In the Anukramaṇī it is stated as उग्रो दश (*ugro daśa*). In the sacrifices connected with the Abhiplava, on the fourth day, when the mantras of the Niṣkevalya Śastra are recited, this hymn is to be recited as the nivid-dhāna hymn; this is set forth by the sūtra of the Āśvalāyana Śrauta Sūtra चतुर्थस्योग्रो जज्ञे इति निष्केवल्यं (*caturthasyogro jajñe iti niṣkevalyaṃ*) (Ā. 7-2).

In the Mahāvrata sacrifice too, this hymn is to be recited among the Niṣkevalya Śastra mantras; this is indicated in the fifth Āraṇyaka of the Aitareya Āraṇyaka by the sentence उग्रो जज्ञे वीर्याय स्वधावानुदु ब्रह्माण्यैरत श्रवस्या (*ugro jajñe vīryāya svadhāvānudu brahmāṇyairata śravasyā*) (Ai. Ā. 5-2-9). And in the sacrifices called Cāturmāsya connected with Soma, when the work of the first day of the sacrifice called Pṛṣṭhya is performed in the place of the Vaiśvadeva, this hymn is to be recited in the middle of the Niṣkevalya Śastra mantras as the nivid-dhāna hymn; this is set forth by the sūtra of the Āśvalāyana Śrauta Sūtra जनिष्ठा उग्र उग्रो जज्ञे इति मध्यंदिनः (*janiṣṭhā ugra ugro jajñe iti madhyaṃdinaḥ*) (Ā. 9-2).

#### Ṛk 1

##### ॥ संहितापाठः ॥ (Saṃhitā-pāṭha)

> उ॒ग्रो ज॑ज्ञे वी॒र्या॑य स्व॒धावा॒ञ्चक्रि॒रपो॒ नर्यो॒ यत्क॑रि॒ष्यन् ।

### Printed p. 3

> जग्मिर्युवा नृषदनमवोभिस्त्राता न इंद्र एनसो महश्चित् ॥

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> उग्रः । जज्ञे । वीर्याय । स्वधावान् । चक्रिः । अपः । नर्यः । यत् । करिष्यन् ।  
> जग्मिः । युवा । नृसदनं । अवःभिः । त्राता । नः । इंद्रः । एनसः । महः । चित् ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> स्वधावान् बलवानुग्र ओजस्व्यद्ग्रोर्वेंद्रो[?] वीर्याय वीर्यं कर्तुं जज्ञे । बभूव । नर्यो नरहितः सन् यत्कर्म करिष्यन् भवति तदपः कर्म चक्रिः कर्तैव ॥ चक्रिरिति किन्प्रत्ययस्य लिड्वद्भावान्न लोकाव्ययनिष्ठाखलर्थतृनामिति षष्ठीप्रतिषेधः ॥ अपि च नृषदनं यज्ञगृहं युवा नित्यतरुणः सन् अवोभी रक्षणैः सार्धं जग्मिर्गंता महश्चिन्महतोऽप्येनसः पापान्नोऽस्माकं त्राता रक्षिता च भवेति ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Indra is svadhāvān, that is, strong; he is fierce, that is, full of vigour; he is born to do heroic deeds. Being beneficial to men, whatever act he intends to do, he is the doer of that act. Further, being ever young, he goes to the house of the sacrifice (the abode of men) together with his protections, and is our protector from great sin as well. **NOTE**

##### प्रतिपदार्थ (Pratipadārtha)

- स्वधावान् (*svadhāvān*)—powerful
- उग्रः (*ugraḥ*)—one possessed of marvellous valour
- इंद्रः (*iṃdraḥ*)—Indra
- वीर्याय (*vīryāya*)—to perform heroic deeds
- जज्ञे (*jajñe*)—has been born
- नर्यः (*naryaḥ*)—being beneficial to mankind
- यत् (*yat*)—whatever act
- करिष्यन् (*kariṣyan*)—he resolves to do
- अपः (*apaḥ*)—that act
- चक्रिः (*cakriḥ*)—he certainly accomplishes
- युवा (*yuvā*)—ever-youthful Indra
- अवोभिः (*avobhiḥ*)—with his protections
- नृषदनं (*nṛṣadanaṃ*)—the house of the sacrifice
- जग्मिः (*jagmiḥ*)—having entered
- महश्चित् (*mahaścit*)—exceedingly great
- एनसः (*enasaḥ*)—from sin also (releasing us)
- नः (*naḥ*)—to us
- त्राता (*trātā*)—is the protector

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

Indra, who is powerful and of marvellous valour, has been born to perform heroic deeds. Whatever act he resolves to do for the benefit of mankind, that act he certainly accomplishes. Ever young Indra, entering the house of the sacrifice with his protections, releases us from exceedingly great sin and protects us.

##### English translation
The fierce and powerful (Indra) has been born for heroic (deeds); friendly to man he is the accomplisher of whatever act he undertakes to perform; ever youthful, he invests the (Sacrificial) hall with defences (against interruption); be our preserver, Indra, from heinous sin.

#### Ṛk 2

##### ॥ संहितापाठः ॥ (Saṃhitā-pāṭha)

> हंता॑ वृ॒त्रमिंद्रः॒ शूशु॑वानः॒ प्रावी॒न्नु वी॒रो ज॑रि॒तार॑मू॒ती ।  
> कर्ता॑ सु॒दासे॒ अह॒ वा उ॑ लो॒कं दाता॒ वसु॒ मुहु॒रा दा॒शुषे॑ भूत् ॥ २ ॥

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> हंता । वृत्रं । इंद्रः । शूशुवानः । प्र । आवीत् । नु । वीरः । जरितारं । ऊती ।  
> कर्ता । सुदासे । अह । वै । ऊं इति । लोकं । दाता । वसु । मुहुः । आ । दाशुषे । भूत् ॥ २ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> इंद्रः शूशुवानो वर्धमानः सन् वृत्रमसुरं हंता भवति ॥ तृनंतॆत्सादत्र षष्ठ्यभावः[?] ॥ वीरो वीरः सन् जरितारं स्तोतारं नु क्षिप्रमूती ऊत्या रक्षयो[?] प्रावीत् । प्रारक्षच्च । सुदासे राज्ञे लोकं जनपदं कर्ता च । यद्वा । सुदासे कल्याणदानाय यजमानाय लोकं कर्ता च भवति ॥ इहाप्युत्तरत्रापि तृनंतॆत्सात्षष्ठ्यभावः[?] । अह वा उ इति त्रयः पूरणाः ॥ दाशुषे यजमानाय वसु धनं मुहुर्भूयो भूयो दाता च भूत् । अभूत् । आ इति चार्थे ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Indra, growing in strength, becomes the slayer of Vṛtra the Asura. Being a hero, he quickly protects the praiser with his protections, and has protected him. He is also the maker of a world, that is, a country, for the king Sudās. Or else, for the sacrificer Sudās, who gives good gifts, he becomes the maker of a world. The particles अह वा उ (*aha vā u*) are three expletives. For the offering sacrificer he repeatedly becomes the giver of wealth, and has become so; आ (*ā*) is used in the sense of "and". **NOTE**

##### प्रतिपदार्थ (Pratipadārtha)

- इंद्रः (*iṃdraḥ*)—Indra
- शूशुवानः (*śūśuvānaḥ*)—growing
- वृत्रं (*vṛtraṃ*)—the Asura
- हंता (*haṃtā*)—becomes the slayer
- वीरः (*vīraḥ*)—the valiant Indra
- जरितारं (*jaritāraṃ*)—the praiser
- नु (*nu*)—quickly
- ऊती (*ūtī*)—with protections
- प्रावीत् (*prāvīt*)—has protected
- सुदासे (*sudāse*)—to the king named Sudās
- लोकं (*lokaṃ*)—the kingdom
- कर्ता (*kartā*)—becomes the one who provides
- अह वा उ (*aha vā u*)—moreover
- दाशुषे (*dāśuṣe*)—to the sacrificer who offers oblations
- वसु (*vasu*)—wealth
- मुहुः (*muhuḥ*)—many times
- दाता भूत् (*dātā bhūt*)—becomes the giver

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

Indra, who grows in strength, becomes the slayer of Vṛtra. The valiant Indra has protected the praiser with his protections. He provides a kingdom to the king named Sudās. To the sacrificer who offers oblations he gives abundant wealth many times.

##### English translation
Indra, dilating in bulk, is the slayer of Vritra: the hero defends his worshipper promptly with his protection, whether he be the giver of dominion to Sudas, or the donor repeatedly of wealth to the offerer (of oblation).

#### Ṛk 3

##### ॥ संहितापाठः ॥ (Saṃhitā-pāṭha)

> यु॒ध्मो अ॑न॒र्वा ख॑ज॒कृत्स॒मद्वा॒ शूरः॑ सत्रा॒षाट् ज॒नुषे॒मुषा॑ळ्हः ।  
> व्या॑स॒ इंद्रः॒ पृत॑नाः॒ स्वोजा॒ अधा॒ विश्वं॑ शत्रू॒यंतं॑ जघान ॥

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> युध्मः । अनर्वा । खजकृत् । समत्ऽवा । शूरः । सत्राषाट् । जनुषा । ईं । अषाळ्हः ।  
> वि । आसे । इंद्रः । पृतनाः । सुऽओजाः । अध । विश्वं । शत्रुऽयंतं । जघान ॥ ३ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> युध्मो योद्धानर्वाभिगंत्ररहितो युद्धेष्वपराङ्मुखो वा खजकृद्युद्धकृत् । खले खज इति युद्धनामसु पाठात् । समद्वा । समत् कलहः । तद्वान् शूरः शौर्योपेतो जनुषा जन्मना स्वभावत एव सत्राषाट् बहूनामभिभविताषाळ्हः स्वयं च केनाप्यनभिभूतः स्वोजाः सुबल ईमयमिंद्रः पृतनाः शत्रूणां सेना व्यासे । विक्षिपति । अधापि च शत्रूयंतं शात्रवमाचरंतं विश्वं सर्वं जघान हंति ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

He is the warrior (युध्मः (*yudhmaḥ*)), the one who has no अनर्वा (*anarvā*), that is, no opponent who comes against him, or who is not turned back in battles. खजकृत् (*khajakṛt*) is the maker of battle, since खज (*khaja*) is read among the names of battle. समद्वा (*samadvā*): समत् (*samat*) is strife, and he possesses it. He is a hero, endowed with heroism; by birth, that is, by nature, he is the overcomer of many (सत्राषाट् (*satrāṣāṭ*)), and himself not overcome by anyone (अषाळ्हः (*aṣāḷhaḥ*)); he is strong (स्वोजाः (*svojāḥ*)). This Indra scatters the armies of the enemies; and further he slays every one who acts as an enemy.

##### प्रतिपदार्थ (Pratipadārtha)

- युध्मः (*yudhmaḥ*)—excellent warrior,
- अनर्वा (*anarvā*)—one who does not turn back,
- खजकृत् (*khajakṛt*)—skilled in battle
- समद्वा (*samadvā*)—one devoted to battle
- शूरः (*śūraḥ*)—hero
- जनुषा (*januṣā*)—from birth
- सत्राषाट् (*satrāṣāṭ*)—one who defeats many
- अषाळ्हः (*aṣāḷhaḥ*)—one not struck down
- स्वोजाः (*svojāḥ*)—one of excellent strength
- ईं इंद्रः (*īṃ iṃdraḥ*)—this same Indra
- पृतनाः (*pṛtanāḥ*)—the enemy armies
- वि आसे (*vi āse*)—scatters
- अध (*adha*)—and
- शत्रूयंतं (*śatrūyaṃtaṃ*)—one who is hostile to him
- विश्वं (*viśvaṃ*)—all
- जघान (*jaghāna*)—destroys

### Printed p. 6

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

This same Indra, an excellent warrior, one who does not turn back, skilled in battle, devoted to battle, a hero, from birth the defeater of many, one not struck down, one of excellent strength, scatters the enemy armies. And he destroys all enemies who deal with him in a hostile way.

##### English translation
A warrior who turns not back in battle, a combatant, one engaged in tumults, a hero, victorious over (his) foes from birth, invincible, of great vigor this Indra, scatters (hostile) hosts and slays all (his) adversaries.

> नि । वज्रं । इंद्रः । हरिवान् । मिमिक्षन् । सं । अंधसा । मदेषु । वै । उवोच ॥ ४ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> हे तुविष्मो बहुधनेंद्र महित्वा महत्त्वेन तविषीभिर्बलैस्त्वोभे[?] चिदुभे अपि रोदसी द्यावापृथिव्यौ आ पप्राथ । आपूरितवानसि । अथ परोक्षस्तुतिः । हरिवानश्ववानिंद्रो वज्रं नि मिमिक्षन् शत्रुषु प्रसारयन् मदेषु यज्ञेषु निविष्टे वांधसा सोमेन समुवोच । संसेव्यते संगच्छते वा ॥ उच समवाये इति धातुः । वा इति पूरणः ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

O Indra, तुविष्मः (*tuviṣmaḥ*), rich in much wealth: by your greatness and by your powers you have filled both heaven and earth. Then follows praise in the third person: Indra, possessed of horses, casting the thunderbolt among the enemies, is joined with the Soma food in the exhilarations, that is, in the sacrifices. The root उच (*uca*) is used in the sense of union, and वा (*vā*) is an expletive. **NOTE**

##### प्रतिपदार्थ (Pratipadārtha)

- तुविष्मः (*tuviṣmaḥ*)—possessed of exceeding wealth
- इंद्र (*iṃdra*)—O Indra,
- महित्वा (*mahitvā*)—of great majesty
- तविषीभिः (*taviṣībhiḥ*)—by your powers
- उभे चित् रोदसी (*ubhe cit rodasī*)—both heaven and earth also
- आ पप्राथ (*ā paprātha*)—you have pervaded
- हरिवान् (*harivān*)—the one with horses
- इंद्रः (*iṃdraḥ*)—Indra
- वज्र (*vajra*)—the thunderbolt
- नि मिमिक्षन् (*ni mimikṣan*)—discharging it upon the enemies
- मदेषु (*madeṣu*)—in the sacrifices that cause joy
- अंधसा (*aṃdhasā*)—with the food in the form of Soma
- सं उवोच वै (*saṃ uvoca vai*)—unites
