**Ṛgveda Saṃhitā with the Bhāṣya of Sāyaṇa (with the Kannada translation and explanation)**

**Part 22**

**Fifth Aṣṭaka, Third Adhyāya**

**Sūktas 20–31 of the Seventh Maṇḍala**

**Introduction**

Of the seventh maṇḍala, the fourteen sūktas from the 18th to the 31st are all addressed to Indra. Of these, sūktas 18 and 19 are included in the preceding adhyāya, that is, in Ṛ. Saṃ. Part 21. Therefore the twelve sūktas from 20 to 31 in this part are in praise of Indra. Since we have described Indra at length in Ṛ. Saṃ. Part 5, pp. 569-582, and Part 16, pp. 631-683, and elsewhere, from here onwards particular matters will be described only where the need appears.

## Sūkta 20 (Maṇḍala 7)

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> अथ तृतीयोऽध्याय आरभ्यते ॥ उग्रो जज्ञे इति दशर्चं तृतीयं सूक्तं
> वसिष्ठस्यार्षं त्रैष्टुभमैंद्रं । तथा चानुक्रम्यते । उग्रो दशेति ॥ आभिप्लविके च

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Now the third adhyāya begins. The sūkta उग्रो जज्ञे (*ugro jajñe*) of ten Ṛks is the third sūkta; its seer is Vasiṣṭha, its metre is Triṣṭubh and its deity is Indra. So the Anukramaṇī says: उग्रो दश (*ugro daśa*). In the Ābhiplavika (the sentence continues in the next block).

### Printed p. 2

> तुर्थेऽहनि निष्केवल्य एतत्सूक्तं निविद्धानं । सूत्रितं च । चतुर्थस्योग्रो जज्ञे
> इति निष्केवल्यं । आ. ७-२ । इति ॥ महाव्रतेऽपि निष्केवल्य एतत्सूक्तं । तथैव पंचमारण्यके सूत्रितं । उग्रो जज्ञे वीर्याय स्वधावानुदु ब्रह्माण्यैरत श्रवस्या । ऐ.
> आ. ५-२-९ । इति ॥ सौमिकचातुर्मास्येषु वैश्वदेवस्य स्थाने प्रथमं पृष्ठ्यमहः ।
> तत्रापि निष्केवल्य एतत्सूक्तं निविद्धानं । सूत्रितं च । जनिष्ठा उग्र उग्रो जज्ञे इति
> मध्यंदिनः । आ. ९-२ । इति ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

On the fourth day this sūkta is the nividdhāna in the niṣkevalya recitation. It is prescribed in the sūtra चतुर्थस्योग्रो जज्ञे इति निष्केवल्यं (*caturthasyogro jajñe iti niṣkevalyaṃ*) (Ā. 7-2). In the Mahāvrata too this sūkta belongs to the niṣkevalya, and it is likewise prescribed in the fifth Āraṇyaka: उग्रो जज्ञे वीर्याय स्वधावानुदु ब्रह्माण्यैरत श्रवस्या (*ugro jajñe vīryāya svadhāvānudu brahmāṇyairata śravasyā*) (Ai. Ā. 5-2-9). In the Soma-sacrifices called Cāturmāsya, in the place of the Vaiśvadeva, comes the first Pṛṣṭhya day; there too this sūkta is the nividdhāna in the niṣkevalya, and it is prescribed in the sūtra जनिष्ठा उग्र उग्रो जज्ञे इति मध्यंदिनः (*janiṣṭhā ugra ugro jajñe iti madhyaṃdinaḥ*) (Ā. 9-2).

Translation: Since the explanation of the second adhyāya of the fifth aṣṭaka was completed in the previous part, the explanation of the third adhyāya is now begun. This sūkta beginning उग्रो जज्ञे (*ugro jajñe*) is the third sūkta in the second anuvāka of the seventh maṇḍala. It contains ten Ṛks. Vasiṣṭha is the seer of this sūkta; Indra is the deity; the metre is Triṣṭubh. In the Anukramaṇī it is stated as उग्रो दश (*ugro daśa*). In the sacrifices connected with the Abhiplava, when the niṣkevalya śastra mantras are recited on the fourth day, this sūkta is to be recited as the nividdhāna sūkta; this is set forth by the sūtra of the Āśvalāyana Śrauta Sūtra चतुर्थस्योग्रो जज्ञे इति निष्केवल्यं (*caturthasyogro jajñe iti niṣkevalyaṃ*) (Ā. 7-2).

In the Mahāvrata rite too, this sūkta is to be recited among the niṣkevalya śastra mantras; this is indicated in the Aitareya Āraṇyaka, in the fifth Āraṇyaka, by the sentence उग्रो जज्ञे वीर्याय स्वधावानुदु ब्रह्माण्यैरत श्रवस्या (*ugro jajñe vīryāya svadhāvānudu brahmāṇyairata śravasyā*) (Ai. Ā. 5-2-9). And in the Soma-sacrifices called Cāturmāsya, when the work of the first day of the sacrifice called Pṛṣṭhya is performed in the place of the Vaiśvadeva, this sūkta is to be recited as the nividdhāna sūkta in the middle of the niṣkevalya śastra mantras; this is set forth by the sūtra of the Āśvalāyana Śrauta Sūtra जनिष्ठा उग्र उग्रो जज्ञे इति मध्यंदिनः (*janiṣṭhā ugra ugro jajñe iti madhyaṃdinaḥ*) (Ā. 9-2).

Sūkta 20. Maṇḍala 7; Anuvāka 2; Sūkta 20. Aṣṭaka 5; Adhyāya 3; Varga 1, 2. Number of Ṛks in the sūkta: 10. Seer: Vasiṣṭha. Deity: Indra. Metre: Triṣṭubh.

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> स्वधावान् बलवानुग्र ओजस्व्यद्ग्रोर्वेंद्रो वीर्याय वीर्यं कर्तुं जज्ञे । बभूव । नर्यो नरहितः सन् यत्कर्म करिष्यन् भवति तदपः कर्म चक्रिः कर्तैव ॥ चक्रिरिति किन्प्रत्ययस्य लिड्वद्भावान्न लोकाव्ययनिष्ठाखलर्थतृनामिति षष्ठीप्रतिषेधः ॥ अपि च नृषदनं यज्ञगृहं युवा नित्यतरुणः सन् अवोभी रक्षणैः सार्धं जग्मिर्गंता महश्चिन्महतोऽप्येनसः पापान्नोऽस्माकं त्राता रक्षिता च भवेति ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Svadhāvān means mighty. The fierce one, the vigorous Indra, was born (became) for heroism, to perform heroic deed. Being beneficial to men, whatever deed he intends to do, that deed he is indeed the doer of. **NOTE** Moreover, ever young, he goes to the house of the sacrifice (the place of men's sitting) together with his protections; may he be our protector, even from great sin.

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

The mighty Indra, of marvellous prowess, has been born to perform heroic deeds. Whatever deed he resolves to do for the benefit of men, that deed he surely accomplishes. Ever young, Indra enters the house of the sacrifice with his protections and, releasing us from the gravest sin, protects us.

##### English translation
The fierce and powerful (Indra) has been born for heroic (deeds); friendly to man he is the accomplisher of whatever act he undertakes to perform; ever youthful, he invests the (Sacrificial) hall with defences (against interruption); be our preserver, Indra, from heinous sin.

#### Ṛk 2

##### ॥ संहितापाठः ॥ (Saṃhitā-pāṭha)

> हंता॑ वृ॒त्रमिंद्रः॒ शूशु॑वानः॒ प्रावी॒न्नु वी॒रो ज॑रि॒तार॑मू॒ती ।  
> कर्ता॑ सु॒दासे॒ अह॒ वा उ॑ लो॒कं दाता॒ वसु॒ मुहु॒रा दा॒शुषे॑ भूत् ॥ २ ॥

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> हंता । वृत्रं । इंद्रः । शूशुवानः । प्र । आवीत् । नु । वीरः । जरितारं । ऊती ।  
> कर्ता । सुदासे । अह । वै । ऊं इति । लोकं । दाता । वसु । मुहुः । आ । दाशुषे । भूत् ॥ २ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> इंद्रः शूशुवानो वर्धमानः सन् वृत्रमसुरं हंता भवति ॥ तृनंतॆत्सादत्र षष्ठ्यभावः ॥ वीरो वीरः सन् जरितारं स्तोतारं नु क्षिप्रमूती ऊत्या रक्षयो प्रावीत् । प्रारक्षच्च । सुदासे राज्ञे लोकं जनपदं कर्ता च । यद्वा । सुदासे कल्याणदानाय यजमानाय लोकं कर्ता च भवति ॥ इहाप्युत्तरत्रापि तृनंतॆत्सात्षष्ठ्यभावः । अह वा उ इति त्रयः पूरणाः ॥ दाशुषे यजमानाय वसु धनं मुहुर्भूयो भूयो दाता च भूत् । अभूत् । आ इति चार्थे ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Indra, growing in strength, becomes the slayer of Vṛtra, the asura. As a hero he has promptly protected the praiser with his protections, and protected him again. He becomes the maker of a world, a country, for King Sudās; or else, he becomes the maker of a world for the sacrificer Sudās, of good gifts. Here and in what follows too the genitive does not apply. The three words अह वा उ (*aha vā u*) are expletives. He has also been, again and again, the giver of wealth to the offerer, the sacrificer. The word आ (*ā*) is in the sense of "and".

##### प्रतिपदार्थ (Pratipadārtha)

- इंद्रः (*iṃdraḥ*)—Indra
- शूशुवानः (*śūśuvānaḥ*)—growing in strength
- वृत्रं (*vṛtraṃ*)—the asura
- हंता (*haṃtā*)—becomes the slayer
- वीरः (*vīraḥ*)—the valiant Indra
- जरितारं (*jaritāraṃ*)—the praiser
- नु (*nu*)—promptly
- ऊती (*ūtī*)—with protections
- प्रावीत् (*prāvīt*)—has protected
- सुदासे (*sudāse*)—to the king named Sudās
- लोकं (*lokaṃ*)—the kingdom
- कर्ता (*kartā*)—becomes the provider
- अह वा उ (*aha vā u*)—moreover
- दाशुषे (*dāśuṣe*)—to the sacrificer who offers oblations
- वसु (*vasu*)—wealth
- मुहुः (*muhuḥ*)—many times
- दाता भूत् (*dātā bhūt*)—provides

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

Indra, growing in strength, becomes the slayer of Vṛtra. The valiant Indra has protected the praiser with his protections. He provides the king named Sudās with a kingdom. He gives abundant gifts of wealth, many times, to the sacrificer who offers oblations.

### Printed p. 5

##### English translation
Indra, dilating in bulk, is the slayer of Vritra: the hero defends his worshipper promptly with his protection, whether he be the giver of dominion to Sudas, or the donor repeatedly of wealth to the offerer (of oblation).

#### Ṛk 3

##### ॥ संहितापाठः ॥ (Saṃhitā-pāṭha)

> यु॒ध्मो अ॑न॒र्वा ख॑ज॒कृत्स॒मद्वा॒ शूरः॑ सत्रा॒षाट् ज॒नुषे॒मुषा॑ळ्हः ।  
> व्या॑स॒ इंद्रः॒ पृत॑नाः॒ स्वोजा॒ अधा॒ विश्वं॑ शत्रू॒यंतं॑ जघान ॥

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> युध्मः । अनर्वा । खजकृत् । समत्ऽवा । शूरः । सत्राषाट् । जनुषा । ईं । अषाळ्हः ।  
> वि । आसे । इंद्रः । पृतनाः । सुऽओजाः । अध । विश्वं । शत्रुऽयंतं । जघान ॥ ३ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> युध्मो योद्धानर्वाभिगंत्ररहितो युद्धेष्वपराङ्मुखो वा खजकृद्युद्धकृत् । खले खज इति युद्धनामसु पाठात् । समद्वा । समत् कलहः । तद्वान् शूरः शौर्योपेतो जनुषा जन्मना स्वभावत एव सत्राषाट् बहूनामभिभविताषाळ्हः स्वयं च केनाप्यनभिभूतः स्वोजाः सुबल ईमयमिंद्रः पृतनाः शत्रूणां सेना व्यासे । विक्षिपति । अधापि च शत्रूयंतं शात्रवमाचरंतं विश्वं सर्वं जघान हंति ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Yudhma is a warrior; anarvā is one without an opponent who approaches him, or one not turned away in battles; khajakṛt is a maker of battle, since khaja is read among the names of battle. Samadvā is the possessor of samat, strife. He is a hero, endowed with heroism, by birth, by nature, the overcomer of many; unvanquished, himself overcome by none; svojā, of good strength. This Indra scatters the armies, the hosts of the enemies. And moreover he slays everyone, all who act as enemies.

##### प्रतिपदार्थ (Pratipadārtha)

- युध्मः (*yudhmaḥ*)—the excellent warrior
- अनर्वा (*anarvā*)—who never turns away
- खजकृत् (*khajakṛt*)—skilled in battle
- समद्वा (*samadvā*)—fond of battle
- शूरः (*śūraḥ*)—the hero
- जनुषा (*januṣā*)—from birth
- सत्राषाट् (*satrāṣāṭ*)—the vanquisher of many
- अषाळ्हः (*aṣāḷhaḥ*)—the unobstructed
- स्वोजाः (*svojāḥ*)—of excellent strength
- ईं इंद्रः (*īṃ iṃdraḥ*)—this same Indra
- पृतनाः (*pṛtanāḥ*)—the hostile armies
- वि आसे (*vi āse*)—scatters
- अध (*adha*)—and
- शत्रूयंतं (*śatrūyaṃtaṃ*)—who acts as an enemy against him
- विश्वं (*viśvaṃ*)—all
- जघान (*jaghāna*)—destroys

### Printed p. 6

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

This same Indra, the excellent warrior, who never turns away, skilled in battle, fond of battle, a hero, the vanquisher of many from birth, the unobstructed, of excellent strength, scatters the hostile armies. And he destroys all the enemies who act against him.

##### English translation
A warrior who turns not back in battle, a combatant, one engaged in tumults, a hero, victorious over (his) foes from birth, invincible, of great vigor this Indra, scatters (hostile) hosts and slays all (his) adversaries.

##### विशेषविषयगळु (Special Topics)

विषुणस्य (*viṣuṇasya*), शिश्नदेवा (*śiśnadevā*): Explaining these words, Yāska says in his Nirukta as follows:

विषुणः । स शर्धदर्यो विषुणस्य जंतोर्मा शिश्नदेवा अपि गुर्ऋतं नः । स उत्सहतां यो विषुणस्य जंतोर्विषमस्य मा शिश्नदेवा अब्रह्म-चर्याः । शिश्नं श्नथतेः । अपि गुर्ऋतं नः । सत्यं वा यज्ञं वा ॥ (*viṣuṇaḥ | sa śardhadaryo viṣuṇasya jaṃtormā śiśnadevā api gurṛtaṃ naḥ | sa utsahatāṃ yo viṣuṇasya jaṃtorviṣamasya mā śiśnadevā abrahma-caryāḥ | śiśnaṃ śnathateḥ | api gurṛtaṃ naḥ | satyaṃ vā yajñaṃ vā ‖*)

विषुणस्य (*viṣuṇasya*) means विषमस्य (*viṣamasya*): of misshapen form, having deformed limbs unlike what everyone has, or lacking understanding. Indra shows zeal in keeping such creatures under control. And शिश्नदेवाः (*śiśnadevāḥ*) means those who, without the discipline of chastity and the like, are given to evil acts such as intercourse with forbidden women and unlawful union with women, those who are outside morality. Or the meaning may also be said to be: wicked people who do not worship the gods such as Indra, but take the male organ itself for a god and are devoted to unlawful and immoral sports. The main sense of the explanation is that such people of wicked nature, who have no rule of morality, should not cause obstruction in the sacrifice we perform; that is, may Indra restrain such wicked ones and grant that our sacrifice be completed in full. The word ऋत (*ṛta*) means truth or sacrifice.

> अवः । बभूथ । शतम्ऽऊते । अस्मे इति । अभिक्षत्तुः । त्वावतः । वरूता ॥ ८ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> हे इंद्र ईशानं त्वां कीरिः स्तोता । कारुः कीरिरिति स्तोतृनामसु पाठात् । वसिष्ठोऽवसे रक्षणाय जुहाव हि । स्तौति हि ह्वयति वा । चिदिति पूरणः । अपि च हे श-तमूते बहुरक्षेंद्र अस्मे अस्माकं भूरेः प्रभूतस्य सौभगस्य धनस्यावो रक्षा ब-भूथ । बभूविथ । अभिक्षत्तुरभिहिंसकस्य त्वावतस्त्वत्सदृशस्य वरूता वारयिता च भव ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Sāyaṇa says: O Indra, the praiser (कीरिः (*kīriḥ*); he quotes the reading of the lists of names of praisers, "kāru, kīri"), Vasiṣṭha, has called you, the lord (ईशानं (*īśānaṃ*)), for protection (अवसे (*avase*)), that is, he praises or invokes you; चित् (*cit*) is a filler. Moreover, O Indra of a hundred aids, you have become the protector (अवः (*avaḥ*)) of our abundant good fortune, that is, of wealth. And be the warder-off (वरूता (*varūtā*)) of a harmful (अभिक्षत्तुः (*abhikṣattuḥ*)) one like yourself (त्वावतः (*tvāvataḥ*)).

- त्वां (*tvāṃ*)—you

- अवसे (*avase*)—for protection
- जुहाव हि (*juhāva hi*)—has called
- शतमूते (*śatamūte*)—O Indra, endowed with many protections
- अस्मे (*asme*)—our
- भूरेः (*bhūreḥ*)—exceedingly great
- सौभगस्य (*saubhagasya*)—of wealth
- अवः (*avaḥ*)—as protector
- बभूथ (*babhūtha*)—you have become
- त्वावतः (*tvāvataḥ*)—of one with strength like yours
- अभिक्षत्तुः (*abhikṣattuḥ*)—the harmful Asura

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

O Indra, the praiser has called you, the lord, for protection. O Indra, endowed with many protections, you have become the protector of our exceeding great wealth. Be the warder-off of the harmful Asura who has strength like yours.

##### English translation
The worshipper has invoked you the sovereign Indra, for protection: protector of many, you have been to us the guardian of great good fortune ; be our defender against every overpowering (assailant) like to you.

##### ॥ संहितापाठः ॥ (Saṃhitā-pāṭha)

> सखा॑यस्त इंद्र वि॒श्वह॑ स्याम नमोवृ॒धासो॑ महि॒ना त॑रुत्र ।  
> व॒न्वंतु॑ स्म॒ तेऽव॑सा समी॒के॒३॒॑ऽभी॑तिम॒र्यो व॒नुषां॒ शवां॑सि ॥

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> सखायः । ते । इंद्र । विश्वह । स्याम । नमःवृधासः । महिना । तरुत्र ।  
> वन्वंतु । स्म । ते । अवसा । सम्ऽईके । अभिऽइतिं । अर्यः । वनुषां । शवांसि ॥ ९ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> हे इंद्र ते तव नमोवृधासो नमसा स्तुत्या हविषा वा वर्धयितारो वयं विश्वह सर्वदा सखायः स्याम । भवेम । महिना महिम्ना तरुत्रात्यंतं तार-केंद्र ते तेवावसा रक्षणेन समीके संग्रामेऽर्योऽभीतिमभिगमनं वनुषां हिंस-कानां शवांसि बलानि च वन्वंतु । स्तोतारो हिंसंतु ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Sāyaṇa says: O Indra, we who increase you (नमोवृधासः (*namovṛdhāsaḥ*)) with homage, that is, praise or oblation, may be your friends at all times (विश्वह (*viśvaha*)). O Indra, who carry us exceedingly across (तरुत्र (*tarutra*)) by your greatness, by your protection let the enemy's (अर्यः (*aryaḥ*)) onset (अभीतिं (*abhītiṃ*)) and the strengths (शवांसि (*śavāṃsi*)) of the harmful (वनुषां (*vanuṣāṃ*)) in battle (समीके (*samīke*)) be overcome; let the praisers harm them. **NOTE**

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

O Indra, may we, who grow by your praises, be your friends on all days. O Indra, who protect exceedingly by your greatness, protected by you, may your praisers in battle destroy the enemy's resistance and the strengths of the violent.

##### English translation
May we, daily increasing in reverence, be (regarded) Indra,(as) your friends : through the protection of you, surpasser in greatness, may (your worshippers) repulse the attack of the foe in battle, the strength of the malevolent.
