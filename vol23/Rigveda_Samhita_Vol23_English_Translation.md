## Preface (Pīṭhike)

Although many deities are praised in the Vedas, and especially in the Ṛgveda, the chief among them are Indra, Agni, the Viśvedevas, the Aśvins, Dyāvāpṛthivī, Uṣas, the Maruts, Mitra, Varuṇa and Āditya. Even among these deities, no other deity has the position that Indra has. The position that Śiva and Viṣṇu hold in the Purāṇas and the like, and the rank and standing that Brahman (Parabrahman) holds in Vedānta, belong to Indra in the Ṛgveda. Indra is praised in many ways, for his qualities and his deeds of valour, as one who protects the good and also punishes the wicked. When the seers praise Indra, they pray that he grant them, as the reward for their praise and for sacrifices and other good rites, food and the like, wealth, a house, heroic sons and protection from the wicked; and that he destroy the Asuras, who are wicked and who create obstacles to the performance of good rites.

There are many ancient accounts that, when destroying the Asuras, he kills those Asuras who fight with deceit, fraud and crooked means by fighting them with the very same kind of means. To give an example, the cleverness Indra used when he had to destroy the Asura named Namuci is a delight. We shall relate it here briefly. Namuci was a demon. Many Ṛks of the Ṛgveda indicate that he was a renowned chief among the early inhabitants of this land who are called Dāsas. Since he was extremely strong, it was very hard for Indra to fight and conquer him. On this subject the destruction of Namuci is indicated in the Ṛks Ṛ. Saṃ. 1-53-7; 2-14-5; 7-19-5; 10-23-7; 5-30-7; 5-30-8; 6-20-6; 8-14-13, and in addition the Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa (Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda) gives a somewhat fuller account of it. That is what we have written here.

Formerly, after Indra had killed the Asura Vṛtra, he also destroyed other demons. But he could not conquer the demon Namuci. This one was extremely powerful and yielded to no kind of weapon. So Indra thought that he must conquer him by his own bodily strength and began a wrestling-fight with him. Yet the more they fought, the more Namuci's strength grew. Indra could not escape his grip. Then Indra asked Namuci to release him, and the Asura addressed Indra: "O Indra, if I am to release you, you must do as I say. Let us now make a pact with each other. You may again wish to fight me by some stratagem and kill me. On such an occasion the weapon you use to kill me must be neither wet nor dry. You must not kill me by day, nor by night. If you agree to these conditions, I shall release you." Indra agreed, "So be it", freed himself from his grip, and began to think how he might conquer him.

After some time, when Indra was wandering on the seashore, a device occurred to his mind: the foam of the sea fell within his sight. This foam is neither wet nor dry. "Therefore I shall make this my weapon and destroy Namuci. When the night has passed and the dawn begins, before the sun rises it is fully bright. That time, since it is not dark, is not night, and since the sun has not risen, is not day. If I fight Namuci at such a time and kill him with a weapon made of water-foam, I shall not have broken my word." Having resolved thus, he fought Namuci accordingly and cut off his head with that foam. Even so the demon did not die, and came chasing Indra in order to kill him. "O Indra, betrayer of a friend, stop, stop! You gave your word that you would not kill me, and now you have done such a deed of betrayal of a friend. Even now I shall not leave you unkilled," he said. Then Indra, frightened, created a special tree called the apāmārga and, for the increase of his vigour, made an offering in the fire with its sticks. By that Indra's strength increased and he destroyed that Namuci. Therefore it is said that those who wish to escape the afflictions of demons should make offerings with apāmārga sticks. Such delightful ancient accounts and episodes are abundant in the Vedas. The same, with slight variations, are described at length in the Purāṇas and the like.

## Sūkta 81 (Maṇḍala 7)

Sūkta 81 of the seventh Maṇḍala.

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> अथ षष्ठो व्याख्यायते । सप्तमे मंडले पंचमेऽनुवाके दश सूक्तानि
> व्याकृतानि । प्रतु, आदर्शीति षडृचमेकादशं सूक्तं वसिष्ठस्यार्षमुषस्यं । प्रथमाद्या अयुजो बृहत्यो द्वितीयाद्या युजः सतोबृहत्यः । तथा चानुक्रांतं ।
> प्रत्यु षट् प्रागाधमिति ॥ प्रातरनुवाक उषस्यॆ क्रत्ौ बार्हते छंदस्याश्विनशस्त्रे
> चेदं सूक्तं । सूत्रितं च । प्रत्यु अदर्शि सह वामेनेति बार्हतं । आ. ४-१४ । इति॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Sāyaṇa says that the sixth (chapter) is now explained; in the fifth anuvāka of the seventh Maṇḍala ten sūktas have been explained. The eleventh sūkta, of six Ṛks beginning प्रतु, आदर्शीति (*pratu, ādarśīti*), has Vasiṣṭha as seer and Uṣas as deity. The first and the other odd Ṛks are Bṛhatī, the second and the other even Ṛks are Satobṛhatī. He cites the Anukramaṇī: प्रत्यु षट् प्रागाधमिति (*pratyu ṣaṭ prāgādhamiti*). The sūkta is used in the Prātaranuvāka, in the Uṣasya (Uṣas) rite in the Bārhata metre, and in the Āśvina-śastra. The sūtra says: प्रत्यु अदर्शि सह वामेनेति बार्हतं (*pratyu adarśi saha vāmeneti bārhataṃ*) (Ā. 4-14).

Anuvāda: Five chapters of the fifth Aṣṭaka are completed so far, so we now begin the explanation of the sixth chapter. Since the ten sūktas of the fifth anuvāka of the seventh Maṇḍala were explained in the previous chapter, we now begin the commentary on the eleventh sūkta. This sūkta, beginning "Praty u ādarśi", is the eleventh sūkta in the fifth anuvāka of the seventh Maṇḍala. It has six Ṛks. Vasiṣṭha is its seer; the goddess Uṣas is its deity. Its odd-numbered Ṛks, that is Ṛks 1, 3, 5, are in Bṛhatī metre. Its even-numbered Ṛks, that is Ṛks 2, 6, 6, are in Satobṛhatī metre. **NOTE** In the Anukramaṇī it is said "Praty u ṣaṭ prāgāthaṃ". It is explained by the sūtra of the Āśvalāyana Śrauta-sūtra, "Praty u ādarśi saha vāmeneti bārhataṃ" (Ā. 4-14), that this sūkta is used, at the time of reciting the Prātaranuvāka mantras, for the Bṛhatī-metre Ṛks connected with the Upasya rite and for the Āśvina-śastra mantras.

Sūkta 81. Maṇḍala 7; Anuvāka 5; Sūkta 81. Aṣṭaka 5; Adhyāya 6; Varga 1. Number of Ṛks in the sūkta: 6. Seer: Vasiṣṭha. Deity: Uṣas. Metre: 1, 3, 5 Bṛhatī; 2, 4, 6 Satobṛhatī.

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> प्रति । ऊं इति । आदर्शि । आयती । उच्छंती । दुहिता । दिवः ।  
> अप्रो इति । महि । व्ययति । चक्षसे । तमः । ज्योतिः । कृणोति । सूनरी ॥ १ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> आयत्यागच्छंती व्युच्छंती तमांसि निवासयंती वर्जयंती दिवो द्युलोकस्य सूर्यस्य वा दुहिता पुत्री एवंभूतोषाः प्रत्यदर्शि । सर्वैः प्रतिदृश्यते । उ
> इति पूरकः । सैषा महि महत्तमो नैशमंधकारं । अप उ इति निपातद्वयसमुदायोऽपेत्यस्यार्थे । अपो व्ययति । अपवृणोति । किमर्थं । चक्षसे दर्शनार्थं ।
> एवं कृत्वा सूनरी जनानां सुष्टु नेत्रुषा ज्योतिः प्रकाशं कृणोति । करोति ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

Uṣas, the daughter of heaven (or of the Sun), is seen by all as she comes, dawning, driving away the darknesses. उ (*u*) is a filler. She, the greatest, removes the darkness of night (अप उ (*apa u*) is a pair of particles in the sense of "away"; अपो व्ययति (*apo vyayati*) means "uncovers"), for the sake of sight. Having done so, she, the good leader of people, makes light.

##### प्रतिपदार्थ (Pratipadārtha)

- उच्छंती (*ucchaṃtī*)—destroying the darkness
- आयती (*āyatī*)—and coming
- दिवः (*divaḥ*)—of the heavenly world
- दुहिता (*duhitā*)—the daughter, the goddess Uṣas
- प्रति उ अदर्शि (*prati u adarśi*)—is visible to all
- चक्षसे (*cakṣase*)—for the sake of sight
- महि (*mahi*)—great
- तमः (*tamaḥ*)—darkness
- अपो व्ययति (*apo vyayati*)—she destroys
- सूनरी (*sūnarī*)—Uṣas, the excellent guide of people
- ज्योतिः (*jyotiḥ*)—light
- कृणोति (*kṛṇoti*)—she produces

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

The goddess Uṣas, who comes destroying the darkness, the daughter of the heavenly world, is visible to all. For the sake of sight she destroys the great darkness. Uṣas, the excellent guide of people, produces light.

##### English translation
The daughter of heaven is everywhere beheld advancing and shedding light: she drives away the deep darkness that objects may meet the eye: the kind guide of man, she diffuses light.

##### विशेषविषयगळु (Special Topics)

उ (*u*)—Here the particle उ (*u*) is used to fill out the pāda. This word has no specific meaning.

#### Ṛk 2

##### ॥ संहितापाठः ॥ (Saṃhitā-pāṭha)

> उदु॒स्रियाः॑ सृजते॒ सूर्यः॒ सचा॑ उ॒द्यन्नक्ष॑त्रमर्चि॒वत् ।  
> तवेदु॑षो॒ व्युषि॒ सूर्य॑स्य च॒ सं भ॒क्तेन॑ गमेमहि ॥ २ ॥

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> उत् । उस्रियाः । सृजते । सूर्यः । सचा । उत्यत् । नक्षत्रं । अर्चिवत् ।  
> तव । इत् । उषः । विउषि । सूर्यस्य । च । सं । भक्तेन । गमेमहि ॥ २ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> सूर्यः सर्वस्य प्रेरक आदित्य उस्रिया रश्मिन् सचा सह युगपदेवोत्सृजते । उद्गमयति । तथोद्यदुद्धच्छत् प्रादुर्भवन्नक्षत्रं नभसि दृश्यमानं ग्रहनक्षत्रादिकमर्चिवद्दीप्तिमत्करोति । सौरेण तेजसा हि नक्तं चंद्रप्रभृतीनि नक्षत्राणि
> संते । सुषुम्नाः सूर्यरश्मिश्चंद्रमा गंधर्वः । वा. सं. १८-४८ । इति हि निगमः ।
> एवं च सति हे उष उषोदेवते तव सूर्यस्य च व्युषि विवासने प्रकाशने सति
> भक्तेनान्नेन सं गमेमहि । वयं संगच्छेमहि । इच्छब्दः पूरकः ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

The Sun, the impeller of all, sends forth his rays at the same moment; and rising, he makes the planets and stars seen in the sky shine. For by the Sun's brilliance the moon and the other stars shine at night. The Nigama (Vā. Saṃ. 18-48) says: सुषुम्नाः सूर्यरश्मिश्चंद्रमा गंधर्वः (*suṣumnāḥ sūryaraśmiścaṃdramā gaṃdharvaḥ*). This being so, O Uṣas, when you and the Sun dawn forth, may we be united with food (भक्तेन (*bhaktena*)). The word इत् (*it*) is a filler.

##### प्रतिपदार्थ (Pratipadārtha)

- सूर्यः (*sūryaḥ*)—the Sun-god, the impeller of all
- उस्रियाः (*usriyāḥ*)—his rays
- सचा (*sacā*)—together with Uṣas
- उत् सृजते (*ut sṛjate*)—he makes them shine and spreads them
- उद्यत् (*udyat*)—rising
- नक्षत्रं (*nakṣatraṃ*)—the stars and the rest
- अर्चिवत् (*arcivat*)—he makes them radiant
- उषः (*uṣaḥ*)—O goddess Uṣas
- तव इत् (*tava it*)—of you, and
- सूरस्य च (*sūrasya ca*)—of the Sun
- व्युषि (*vyuṣi*)—when the simultaneous manifestation takes place
- भक्तेन (*bhaktena*)—with food
- सं गमेमहि (*saṃ gamemahi*)—may we be united

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

The Sun-god, the impeller of all, makes his rays shine and spreads them together with Uṣas. As soon as he rises he makes the stars and the rest radiant. O goddess Uṣas, when you and the Sun manifest at the same time, may we be united with food; that is, may we obtain food.

> प्रति॑ त्वा दुहितर्दिव॒ उषो॑ जी॒रा अ॑भुत्सहि ॥  
> या वह॑सि पु॒रु स्पा॒र्हं व॑नन्वति॒ रत्नं॒ न दा॒शुषे॒ मयः॑ ॥ ३ ॥

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> प्रति । त्वा । दुहितः । दिवः । उषः । जीराः । अभुत्सहि ।  
> या । वहसि । पुरु । स्वाह्यं । वनन्स्‌वति । रत्नं । न । दाशुषे । मयः ॥ ३ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> हे दिवो दुहितर्दिवोऽंतरिक्षाज्जायमाने हे उष उषोदेवते त्वा त्वां
> जीराः क्षिप्रकारिणो वयं प्रत्यभुत्स्महि । प्रतिबोद्धारो भवेम । हे वनन्वति ।
> वननं संभजनं संभक्तव्यं धनं वा । तद्वति या त्वं पुरु बहु स्पार्हं स्पृहणीयं
> धनं वहसि प्रापयसि दाशुषे हवींषि दत्तवते यजमानाय रत्नं रमणीयं
> धनमिव मयः सुखं च या त्वं वहसि तां त्वां प्रत्यभुत्स्महीत्यन्वयः ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

O daughter of heaven, born from the sky, O Uṣas, may we, the prompt ones (जीराः (*jīrāḥ*)), become awakeners of you. वनन्वति (*vananvati*): वननं (*vananaṃ*) is sharing, or wealth to be shared; she who possesses it. You who bring much desirable wealth, and delight, like a precious treasure, to the sacrificer who has given oblations — you we would awaken. This is the construction.

##### प्रतिपदार्थ (Pratipadārtha)

- दिवः दुहितः (*divaḥ duhitaḥ*)—O daughter of the sky
- वनन्वति (*vananvati*)—possessing wealth that serves for enjoyment

##### भावार्थ (Bhāvārtha)

O goddess Uṣas, daughter of the sky, possessor of wealth that serves for enjoyment, you who bring very abundant and much-desired wealth, and who provide happiness, like delightful wealth, to the sacrificer who offers oblations — grant that we, the prompt ones, may have the power to awaken such a one as you.

May we, prompt adorers, awaken you, Ushas, daughter of heaven bountiful divinity, who bring ample and desirable (wealth) and happiness, like riches, to the donor (of the oblation).

#### Ṛk 4

##### ॥ संहितापाठः ॥ (Saṃhitā-pāṭha)

> उ॒च्छंती॒ या कृ॒णोषि॑ मं॒हना॑ महि प्र॒ख्यै दे॑वि॒ स्व॑र्दृ॒शे ॥  
> तस्या॑स्ते रत्न॒भाज॑ ईमहे व॒यं स्याम॑ मा॒तुर्न सू॒नवः॑ ॥४॥

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> उच्छंती । या । कृणोषि । मंहना । महि । प्रख्यै । देवि । स्वः । दृशे ।  
> तस्याः । ते । रत्नभाजः । ईमहे । वयं । स्याम । मातुः । न । सूनवः ॥ ४ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> हे महि महति देवि दानादिगुणयुक्त उषोदेवते व्युच्छंती तमांसि
> वर्जयंती मंहना महिम्मा युक्ता । यद्वा । मंहतिर्दानकर्मा । दानयुक्ता ।
> या त्वं स्वः सर्वं जगत् प्रख्यॆ प्रबोधनार्थं दृशे दर्शनार्थं च कृणोषि
> करोषि तस्यास्तादृश्या रत्नभाजो रत्नानां रमणीयानां धनानां भाजयित्रा :
> सेवॆयित्रा ईमहे। याचामहे । किं। रत्नभाज इति समभिव्याहाराद्रत्नानीति गम्यते।
> अपि च वयं तव प्रियतमाः स्याम । भवेम । मातुर्न सूनवः । यथा मातुर्जनन्याः सूनवः पुत्राः प्रियतमा भवंति तद्वत् ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

O great goddess Uṣas, endowed with qualities such as bounty, who dawn, driving away the darkness, endowed with greatness (मंहना (*maṃhanā*)); or else, मंहति (*maṃhati*) is a root meaning giving, so "endowed with giving" — you who make the whole world (स्वः (*svaḥ*)) able for awakening (प्रख्यै (*prakhyai*)) and for seeing (दृशे (*dṛśe*)): to such a one, the distributor (रत्नभाजः (*ratnabhājaḥ*)) of delightful riches, we ask. What do we ask? From the context of रत्नभाज (*ratnabhāja*) it is understood: riches. And may we be most dear to you, as sons are most dear to a mother, their begetter.

O great goddess Uṣas, you who are endowed with greatness and destroy the darkness, and who make the whole world capable of awakening and of seeing — to you, possessor of delightful wealth, we pray that we may be dear to you as children are dear to their mothers.

#### Ṛk 5

##### ॥ संहितापाठः ॥ (Saṃhitā-pāṭha)

> तच्चि॒त्रं राध॒ आ भ॒रोषो॒ यद्दी॑र्घ॒श्रुत्त॑मं ।  
> यत्ते॑ दिवो दुहितर्मर्त॒भोज॑नं॒ तद्रा॑स्व भु॒नजा॑महै ॥५॥

##### ॥ पदपाठः ॥ (Pada-pāṭha)

> तत् । चित्रं । राधः । आ । भर । उषः । यत् । दीर्घश्रुत् तमं ।  
> यत् । ते । दिवः । दुहितः । मर्तभोजनं । तत् । रास्व । भुनजामहै ॥ ५ ॥

##### ॥ सायणभाष्यम् ॥ (Sāyaṇa-bhāṣya)

> हे उषः तच्चित्रं चायनीयं राधो धनमा भर । आहर । अस्मभ्यं प्रयच्छ ।
> युद्धनं दीर्घश्रुुत्तमं देशकालयोर्विप्रकर्षेऽप्यतिशयेन श्रोतव्यं । अपि च हे
> दिवो दुहितः ते तव त्वया देयं मर्तभोजनं मर्तानां मनुष्याणां भोगयोग्यं यदन्नमस्ति तद्रास्व । देह्यस्मभ्यं । वयं च त्वद्दत्तं धनमन्नं च भुनजामहै । अभ्यवहरेम ॥

##### Gist of the bhāṣya

O Uṣas, bring (आ भर (*ā bhara*)) that wonderful (चित्रं (*citraṃ*)), desirable wealth (राधः (*rādhaḥ*)); give it to us — the wealth which is most renowned (दीर्घश्रुत्तमं (*dīrghaśruttamaṃ*)), to be heard of in the highest degree even across distance of place and time. And, O daughter of heaven, whatever food of yours, to be given by you, is fit for the enjoyment of mortals (humans), give (रास्व (*rāsva*)) that to us; and may we enjoy (भुनजामहै (*bhunajāmahai*)) the wealth and food given by you.
