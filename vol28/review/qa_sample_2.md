- **Method:** read-only. For each page, the English between `### Printed p. N` and the next heading was read side by side with the Kannada between `<!-- printed N -->` and the next mark. Recorded exceptions (Kannada prose as Sanskrit blocks on pp. 41, 89–99, 271, 299, 320, 629; absent pp. 162, 604, 605) were ignored.
