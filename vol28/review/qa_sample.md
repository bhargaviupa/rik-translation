## Summary

ERRORs: 4. MINORs: 5. Pages with no findings: 16 of 24.

**F1 (ERROR, p. 61, E 2323 / K 2491–2493).** Relation garbled. E: "he is also described as begotten by whomever begotten; and he is praised as the father-like protector of all, gods, humans and the rest." K: ಯಾರು ಯಾರಿಂದ ಉತ್ಪನ್ನವಾಗಿದ್ದಾನೆಂದು ವರ್ಣಿತನಾಗಿರುವನೋ ಅಂತಹ ದೇವಮಾನವಾದಿಗಳಿಗೆಲ್ಲ ಪಿತೃರೂಪನೂ ಪಾಲಕನೂ ಆಗಿದ್ದಾನೆಂದು ಪ್ರಶಂಸಿಸಲ್ಪಟ್ಟಿದ್ದಾನೆ. Correct: "he is praised as the father and protector of all those, gods, men and the rest, by whom he is described as having been begotten."

**F2 (ERROR, p. 299, E 9017 / K 11164–11171).** Kannada prose printed as a Sanskrit block (Devanagari + IAST). The quoted block begins "तिस्र एव देवताः (नि. ७-५) ऎंब निरुक्तवचनवन्नु दाहरिसि …" and includes "पृथिवी, अंतरिक्ष मत्तु द्युलोकाभिमानिगळ संख्यॆयु मूरे आदरू सह … विवरिसिद्दारॆ. निरुक्तकाररू सह—", which is Kannada prose already translated in E 9015. Correct: the block should hold only the Nirukta quotation "तिस्र एव देवता इति नैरुक्ताः । अग्निः पृथिवीस्थानः । … अपि वा कर्मपृथक्त्वात् ॥". Same class as the known pages, but p. 299 is not on that list.

**F3 (ERROR, p. 302, E 9094 / K 11260–11262).** Metres reversed. E: "The first five Ṛks, up to the Ṛk तंतुं तन्वन् (*taṃtuṃ tanvan*), and the Ṛks other than the eighth are in the Jagatī metre". K: ತಂತುಂ ತನ್ವನ್ ಎಂಬ ಋಕ್ಕಿನವರೆಗಿರುವ ಮೊದಲಿದ ೫ ಋಕ್ಕುಗಳನ್ನೂ, ೮ ನೇ ಋಕ್ಕನ್ನು ಬಿಟ್ಟು ಉಳಿದ ಋಕ್ಕುಗಳು ಜಗತೀಛಂದಸ್ಕವು. Correct: "Leaving out the first five Ṛks, up to the Ṛk तंतुं तन्वन्, and the eighth, the remaining Ṛks are in the Jagatī metre". E contradicts its own table (E 9099: "1-5, 8, Triṣṭubh").

**F4 (ERROR, p. 583, E 20365 / K 22358–22359).** Omitted quotation. E ends "… पालयिता वा (*…*). Of him there is this Ṛk." and the Ṛk is missing; p. 584 starts with a fragment "द्बृहस्पतिर्विरवेणा विकृत्य ॥" (E 20368). K continues the Nirukta quotation: ಅಶ್ನಾಪಿನದ್ಧಂ ಮಧು ಪರ್ಯಪಶ್ಯನ್ಮತ್ಸ್ಯಂ ನ ದೀನ ಉದನಿ ಕ್ಷಿಯಂತಂ । ನಿಷ್ಕಜ್ಜಭಾರ ಚಮಸಂ ನ ವೃಕ್ಷಾ- (part of Ni. 10-12, completed on p. 584). Correct: the whole Nirukta passage (ಬೃಹಸ್ಪತಿಃ। … ತಸ್ಯೈಷಾ ಭವತಿ। ಅಶ್ನಾಪಿನದ್ಧಂ … ವೃಕ್ಷಾ-|ದ್ಬೃಹಸ್ಪತಿರ್ವಿರವೇಣಾ ವಿಕೃತ್ಯ ॥ …) as one quotation.

**F5 (MINOR, p. 201, E 6220 / K 7677–7681).** Addition in the gist. E: "(रंधयं (*raṃdhayaṃ*) from the root रध्यति (*radhyati*) meaning to bring under control)". The bhāṣya has only "रंधयं। वशमनयं" ("I brought under control"); no root is named. Also the gist's "so I desired (for Kutsa) the Vetasu peoples" misreads "अभिष्टयेऽभीच्छते कुत्साय" ("for Kutsa, who desired it").

**F6 (MINOR, p. 428, E 14086 / K 16237).** Supplied word dropped. E: "ह (*ha*)—to that radiance". K: (ತತ್) ಹ—ಆ ತೇಜಸ್ಸಿಗೆ. Correct: "(तत्) ह—to that radiance".

**F7 (MINOR, p. 460, E 15472 / K 17531–17532).** Gist. E: "The two verses beginning with the words on the path (Pathyā)". Bhāṣya: "स्वस्ति नः पथ्यास्त्विति द्वे". Correct: "The two verses beginning स्वस्ति नः पथ्यासु" (as E 15476 itself has).

**F8 (MINOR, p. 583, E 20337, 20347, 20358, 20365).** Name spelling. E: "Brihaspati" on this page, against "Bṛhaspati" elsewhere (e.g. E 16677, E 20368 ff.). Correct: "Bṛhaspati".

**F9 (MINOR, p. 625, E 21842–21844 / K 23919–23927).** Attribution. E: "To support this meaning, the last part gives an example—". K: ಎಂದರೆ ರಾತ್ರಿಯ ಅಪರಕಾಲವು. ಕೊನೆಯ ಭಾಗವು ಈ ಅರ್ಥವನ್ನು ಸಮರ್ಥಿಸುವುದಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಒಂದು ಉದಾಹರಣೆಯನ್ನು ಕೊಟ್ಟಿರುವರು— (ಕೊಟ್ಟಿರುವರು needs a personal subject, Yāska). Correct: "that is, the latter time, the last part of the night. To support this meaning he (Yāska) gives an example—". Also in E 21842 "Therefore the time of night is called नक्ता (Ni. 8-11)" drops ಎಂದು ಅರ್ಥವಿವರಣೆ ಮಾಡಿರುವರು ("so he has explained").
