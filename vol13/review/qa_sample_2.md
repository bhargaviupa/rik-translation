## Summary

### List of findings

1. **p. 90, ERROR (omission)** — E 2292: "रोदसी ईं जोषत् (*rodasī īṃ joṣat*)—मरुत्पत्नी विद्युत् (*marutpatnī vidyut*) and so on:". K 3284–3285 gives the whole Sāyaṇa phrase: "ಮರುತ್ಪತ್ನೀ ವಿದ್ಯುತ್ ನಾ ಏತನ್ನಾಮಿಕಾ ಇಯಮಪಿ ಏನಂ ಮರುತ್ಸಂಘಂ ಸೇವತೇ ।". The words after "vidyut" are left out and replaced by "and so on".
2. **p. 210, MINOR (placement)** — E 5271–5272: the Wilson translation ("Maruts, through fear of that violent Indra, I fly, trembling…") stands on p. 209, before the p. 210 heading and before the Bhāvārtha. In K 7491–7493 it is on p. 210, after the Bhāvārtha.
3. **p. 210, MINOR (sense)** — E 5282: "Yet Indra, displaying his valour together with the Maruts, states plainly that he got no help at all from them". K 7499–7500 "ಇಂದ್ರನು ಮರುತ್ತುಗಳೊಂದಿಗೆ ತನ್ನ ಪರಾಕ್ರಮವನ್ನು ಪ್ರಕಟಿಸಿ" here means Indra declares his prowess *to* the Maruts. Correct reading: "Yet Indra, declaring his prowess to the Maruts, states plainly…".
4. **p. 247, ERROR (omission)** — E 6124: "…; and that for this very reason the heavenly world, too, has this name." K 8785 quotes the Nirukta at this point: "ಮತ್ತು ಏತೇನ ದೌರ್ವ್ಯಾಖ್ಯಾತಾ ಈ ಕಾರಣದಿಂದಲೇ ಮ್ಯುಲೋಕಕ್ಕೂ ಈ ಹೆಸರು". The quotation ⟦ಏತೇನ ದೌರ್ವ್ಯಾಖ್ಯಾತಾ⟧ is missing from E.
5. **p. 247, MINOR (format/addition)** — E 6116–6118: a new heading "##### विशेषविषयगळु (Special Topics)" and a leading "…" stand before "each has its own meaning". The Special Topic started on p. 246 (E 6096ff.). K 8771–8773 has no heading here; the text simply continues.
6. **p. 271, ERROR (wrong term)** — E 6694: "Indra, … surpasses heaven and earth." K 9607–9608: "ಭೂಮ್ಯಂತರಿಕ್ಷಗಳನ್ನು ಮಾರಿದಾನೆ". Correct: "surpasses the earth and the atmosphere" (this matches E's own Pratipadārtha, "रोदसी—earth and atmosphere").
7. **p. 271, MINOR (omission)** — E 6703: "The word इत्था (*itthā*) is read among the names meaning "true", श्रत् (*śrat*) and so on (Ni. 3-13)." K 9623: "ಇತ್ಥಾ-ಬಟ್, ಶ್ರತ್ ಇತ್ಯಾದಿ ಸತ್ಯ ನಾಮಗಳಲ್ಲಿ". The first name, ⟦ಬಟ್⟧ (baṭ), is left out.
8. **p. 271, MINOR (placement)** — K 9629 prints "(ಋ. ಸಂ. ೫-೩೨-೬)" on p. 271, directly after the ṛk त्यं चिदित्था…. E puts "(Ṛ. Saṃ. 5-32-6)" on p. 272 (E 6708).
9. **p. 295, MINOR (Pratipadārtha)** — E 7249: "- कर्म (*karma*)—rites (for the rites), with praises (satisfied)". K 10429 has the gloss "ಉಕ್ಥಾಶಸ್ತ್ರಗಳಿಂದ (ತೃಪ್ತನಾಗಿ)" (the headword is lost in the print). In E this gloss is merged into the कर्म entry as "with praises" and does not stand as its own entry ("by the ukthas and śastras (satisfied)").
10. **p. 330, ERROR (sense)** — E 8112: "(let the clouds, other than the thunderbolt, remain in their own place, the sky, proclaiming your greatness)". K 11664–11665: "ವಜ್ರಾಯುಧದಿಂದ ಭಿನ್ನವಾದ ಮೇಘಗಳು". Correct: "(let the clouds, split by the thunderbolt, remain …)".
11. **p. 330, MINOR (stray duplicate)** — E 8119: "used as an epithet of the word योन्नौ (*yonnau*) (योन्‌नौ (*yonnau*))". K 11674 has the word once: "ಯೋನ್‌ನೌ ಎಂಬ ಪದಕ್ಕೆ". The bracketed repeat is an addition.
12. **p. 457, MINOR (format/addition)** — E 11019–11021: a new heading "##### विशेषविषयगळु (Special Topics)" and a leading "…the vessel of the sacrifice". The Special Topic began on p. 454 (E 10974). K 16060 continues with no heading.
13. **p. 457, MINOR (omission)** — E 11035: "*Sutukā?*—Sāyaṇa explains this word as "possessed of excellent strength"." K 16085 quotes Sāyaṇa's words first: "ಸಾಯಣರು ಶೋಭನಬಲೋಪೇಶತಾಃ ಶ್ರೇಷ್ಠವಾದ ಬಲದಿಂದ ಕೂಡಿದವು". The Sanskrit ⟦ಶೋಭನಬಲೋಪೇಶತಾಃ⟧ is missing.
14. **p. 470, MINOR (term)** — E 11383: "After her ritual bath the sage Agastya…". K 16550: "ಋತುಸ್ನಾನಾನಂತರ". Correct: "After her bath following her season (ṛtu-snāna)", which is the sense of the Bṛ. De. "ṛtusnātām".
15. **p. 510, ERROR (attribution)** — E 12279 (Gist): "Others say that, thrown into the fire, he escaped." The Sāyaṇa text (K 17965) reads "ಅಪರೇಽಗ್ನೌ ಕ್ಷಿಪ್ತಮಪಾಲಾಯತಾಮಿತ್ಯಾಹುಃ". The verb is dual with an accusative object, so it is the two Aśvins who protected him. Correct: "Others say that the two (Aśvins) protected him when he had been thrown into the fire."
16. **p. 510, MINOR (sense)** — E 12279: "Thrown thus, he praised the two Aśvins". The bhāṣya (K 17964) has "ಸ ಚ ತಸ್ಯಮಾನೋ…ತುಷ್ಟಾವ", i.e. *tapyamānaḥ*. Correct: "Being scorched (tormented by the heat), he praised the two Aśvins".
17. **p. 643, ERROR (omission)** — E 15492: "राजर्षिरभवद्दार्भ्यो रथवीतिरिति (*rājarṣirabhavaddārbhyo rathavītiriti*) … देवान्पुरविग्रहान् (*devānpuravigrahān*) **NOTE** त्यावाश्वो (*tyāvāśvo*)". The note in `notes.tsv` (w0643) says "long quotation not located in the supplied text". In fact the whole Bṛhaddevatā passage is printed in K 22661–22678, from "ರಾಜರ್ಷಿರಭವದ್ದಾರ್ಭ್ಯೋ ರಥವೀತಿರಿತಿ ಶ್ರುತಃ । ಸ ಯಕ್ಷಮಾಣೋ ರಾಜಾತ್ರಿಮಭಿಗಮ್ಯ ಪ್ರಸಾದ್ಯ ಚ ॥" to "… ತಾಂಸ್ತುಲ್ಯವಯಸೋ ದೃಷ್ಟ್ವಾ ದೇವಾನ್ಪುರವಿಗ್ರಹಾನ್ । ತ್ಯಾವಾಶ್ವೋ" (about 20 ślokas, continued on p. 644 to "(ಬೃ. ದೇ. ೫-೫೦ ರಿಂದ ೮೦)"). On p. 643, E has only its first and last words.
