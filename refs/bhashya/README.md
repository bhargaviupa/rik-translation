**What it covers in this series:** Maṇḍala 1 Sūktas 1–121 only, i.e. the Kannada volumes of the First Aṣṭaka (Vols 1 to about 9). It does **not** cover Vol 12 (Sūktas 158–165), Vols 13–16 or Vol 17 (Maṇḍalas 3–4).

**Rules of use**
- Its accuracy has not been checked: it is machine-read like our own source, so a difference between it and a Kannada volume may be an error on either side, or a real difference between the two editions.
- It is used only to **flag** places (a difference becomes a `**NOTE**` candidate for the user). The supplied Kannada text stays the base; nothing is corrected from this file.
- Keep it in the private repository with the other reference texts (`refs/mandala/`).
