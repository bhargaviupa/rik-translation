## SBh 1.152.0.1

⟦युवं वस्त्राणि⟧: this is the twelfth sūkta, of seven Ṛks, of Dīrghatamas. By the general rule for what is not specified it is in the triṣṭubh metre. It is addressed to Mitra and Varuṇa, because ⟦मैत्रावरुणं हि⟧ was said in the preceding place. The application of the sūkta is the one shown by the indications in the text. In the animal sacrifice for Mitra and Varuṇa the first Ṛk is the yājyā of the omentum. In the section ⟦प्रदानानाम्⟧ it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦युवं वस्त्राणि पीवसा वसाथे प्र बाहवा सिसृतं जीवसे नः⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ३. ८⟧). The same Ṛk is applied in the samāvartana, at the putting on of new garments. And it is laid down in the sūtra (⟦आश्व. गृ. ३. ८. ९⟧) that he acts after having put on two unwashed garments with the Ṛk ⟦युवं वस्त्राणि⟧.

## SBh 1.152.1.1

O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦पीवसा⟧, stout, ⟦युवं⟧, you two. Or else ⟦पीवसा⟧, stout, uncut, ⟦वस्त्राणि⟧, garments fit for covering, ⟦वसाथे⟧, you put on. Or else ⟦पीवसा⟧, with thick, abundant splendour, you wear ⟦वस्त्राणि⟧; the meaning is that you bear unbroken splendours which stand in the place of garments. Moreover, of you two the ⟦सर्गाः⟧, creations, are ⟦अच्छिद्राः⟧, uncut, and ⟦मन्तवो⟧ ⟦ह⟧, to be thought upon. You two who are such, ⟦विश्वा⟧, all, ⟦अनृतानि⟧, untruths, unpleasant things, sins, ⟦अवातिरतं⟧, destroy. In ⟦विश्व⟧ here, in the Saṃhitā, there is by ⟦ऋत्यकः⟧ the keeping of the original form, and the shortening.

## SBh 1.152.1.2

⟦ऋतेन⟧, with the fruit, or with the means of it, the sacrifice, ⟦सचेथे⟧, you are united; the meaning is that you join it to us.

## SBh 1.152.2.1

⟦एषाम्⟧, among these two. The plural is to show honour. Or else the plural is with regard to their attendants. Among these two, ⟦त्वः⟧ ⟦चन⟧, one alone, ⟦एतत्⟧, this action in the form of ability that is about to be stated, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦चिकेतत्⟧, knows in a special way; the meaning is that he carries it out. He is described. That one is ⟦सत्यः⟧, not to be thwarted, or existing among the good, ⟦मन्त्रः⟧, possessed of thought, ⟦कविशस्तः⟧, to be praised by the kavis, the wise, ⟦ऋघावान⟧, possessed of the power to harm. By this it is said that the possibility of the ability about to be stated has been set forth. What is it? Anticipating this question he says: ⟦त्रिरश्रिम्⟧. A weapon furnished with three edges is ⟦त्रिरश्रि⟧; him who has it. The suffix with the sense of possession is elided.

## SBh 1.152.2.2

This is an indication of more: the meaning is, one who is furnished with all weapons. Or else one who has this name. ⟦चतुरश्रिः⟧, he who has more weapons even than that. There is not only the ability of weapons; but ⟦उग्रः⟧, with strength raised up of himself, ⟦हन्ति⟧, he slays the enemy of exceeding strength. Moreover, by the ability of one alone of you two, ⟦देवनिदो⟧ ⟦ह⟧, the revilers of the gods, for their part, ⟦प्रथमाः⟧, though they are able, ⟦अजूर्यन्⟧, become decayed of themselves. From the root jṝ, with the suffix śyan, there is the vowel u by ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧.

## SBh 1.152.2.3

Among you two even one is of great strength. What need is there to speak of the ability of the two united? This is the purport. Or else: ⟦एषां⟧, of you two, ⟦एतत्⟧, this ability which is about to be stated in what follows, one who has the marks of being true and the rest, ⟦वि चिकेतत्⟧, knows in a special way. The rest is as before.

## SBh 1.152.3.1

O ⟦मित्रावरुणा⟧, Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦पद्वतीनां⟧, of the creatures that have feet, men and the like, ⟦प्रथमा⟧, arising first, ⟦अपात्⟧, without feet, is Uṣas. Since she has no revolving of her own day by day, as the sun, the moon and the like have, and since her going is by the going of the sun alone, she is spoken of figuratively both as ⟦अपात्⟧ and as ⟦एति⟧, "she goes". ⟦तत्⟧, such, ⟦वां⟧, of you two, well-known deed, ⟦कः⟧ ⟦आ⟧ ⟦चिकेत⟧, who knows, facing it? The root is ⟦कित⟧, in the sense of knowing.

## SBh 1.152.3.2

The meaning is that it is to be praised. Because Mitra and Varuṇa are the deities of day and night, and because the interval between day and night is itself the dawn, this is their deed. Moreover ⟦अस्य⟧, of this world, ⟦भारं⟧, the sustaining, ⟦गर्भः⟧, the child that stands in the place of the embryo of you two, the Āditya. For he arises at the time between day and night, and those two alone are Mitra and Varuṇa, according to the śruti ⟦अहोरात्रे वै मित्रावरुणौ⟧ (⟦तै. सं. २. ४. १०. १⟧), "Day and night indeed are Mitra and Varuṇa". He brings it, ⟦चित्⟧: he does indeed sustain it on every side. Or else he takes away its ⟦भारं⟧, its torpor. ⟦चित्⟧ is used in the sense of restriction. How is that? That is stated. ⟦ऋतं⟧, the truth of the world, in the form of light, movement and the like, ⟦पिपर्ति⟧, he fills. ⟦अनृतम्⟧, what is unlike that which was stated, the darkness and the stopping of movement and the like, ⟦नि⟧ ⟦तारीत्⟧, he overcomes, he sinks; the meaning is that he destroys it. The deed of the child, in the form of bearing the burden of the world, is from your ability: thus.

## SBh 1.152.4.1

⟦कनीनां⟧, of the lovely ones, the bright ones, the dawns that stand in the place of maidens. From the root kan, in the sense of shining, there is the suffix iñ. By ⟦कृदिकारादक्तिनः⟧ there is the feminine suffix ṅīṣ. Or else, before the ending ām, there is the Vedic samprasāraṇa of the word kanyā.

## SBh 1.152.4.2

Of them the ⟦जारम्⟧, the Āditya, ⟦प्रयन्तमित⟧, going forward only, ⟦परि⟧ ⟦पश्यामसि⟧, we see all round; ⟦उपनिपद्यमानं⟧, sitting down even for a mere moment, ⟦न⟧, we do not see him. This too is the ability of those two. The same one is described further. ⟦अनवपृग्णा⟧, that is, ⟦अनवपृग्णानि⟧, and for that very reason spread out, splendours, ⟦वसानम्⟧, covering himself with; ⟦प्रियं⟧, who is dear to all; of ⟦मित्रस्य⟧ and of ⟦वरुणस्य⟧ the ⟦धाम⟧, who is the place of their splendour. Or else ⟦प्रियं⟧ is connected with ⟦धाम⟧: the place of the love of those two. Such an Āditya, moving about by your permission, ⟦पश्यामसि⟧, we see.

## SBh 1.152.5.1

That same Āditya, although ⟦अनश्वः⟧, without a swift-going horse, and likewise ⟦अनभीशुः⟧, without the cords that stand in the place of reins, the support to hold on to, yet ⟦अर्वा⟧ ⟦जातः⟧, he has become possessed of swift going. This Āditya, who is of such a kind, ⟦कनिक्रदत्⟧, causing an exceeding cry. From the root krand ending in the elided yaṅ, with the suffix śatṛ, the form is laid down ready-made in the sūtra that begins with dādharti.

## SBh 1.152.5.2

⟦ऊर्ध्वसानुः⟧, being one whose rising is higher and higher, ⟦पतयत्⟧, he goes. ⟦अचित्तं⟧, which is not within the range of thought, ⟦ब्रह्म⟧, great, the deed of the form stated, in ⟦मित्रे⟧ and in ⟦वरुणे⟧, of both of them the ⟦धाम⟧, the place of splendour, ⟦युवानः⟧, joining, placing it upon the place of splendour of those two, and likewise ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦गृणन्तः⟧, praising in an eminent way, saying "It arises from the splendour of you two", ⟦जुजुषुः⟧, they serve. The meaning is: men praise the deed of the sun, which consists in moving about in the mid-region that has no support, as dependent on Varuṇa and Mitra.

## SBh 1.152.6.1

⟦मामतेयं⟧, the son of Mamatā, me, Dīrghatamas, ⟦ब्रह्मप्रियं⟧, the dearest to the great rite, or the gladdener of the great Agni, ⟦अवन्तीः⟧, that is, ⟦अवन्त्यः⟧, gladdening, ⟦धेनवः⟧, the cows, ⟦सस्मिन्⟧, in their own, ⟦ऊधन्⟧, udder, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦पीपयन्⟧, may they cause to swell on every side. From the root pyāy, in the causative, there is the Vedic substitution of pī.

## SBh 1.152.6.2

The meaning is: by the milk in the udder, which is to be given to you two, they protect us. For the śruti says ⟦मित्रावरुणाभ्यां पयस्या⟧ (⟦तै.सं.७.५.२२.१⟧), "The payasyā for Mitra and Varuṇa". Having thus given the oblation, ⟦वयुनानि⟧, the knowledge concerning the performance, ⟦विद्वान्⟧, knowing, understanding that the remainder of what is offered is to be eaten, Dīrghatamas, ⟦पित्वः⟧, of the food left over from the offering, food of that kind, ⟦आसा⟧, with the mouth, to eat it, ⟦भिक्षेत⟧, may he beg of you. Moreover ⟦विवासन्⟧, attending upon you two, ⟦अदितिम्⟧, unbroken, not deficient, this rite ⟦उरुष्येत्⟧. The meaning is: in such a way that he may beg for what is left, and in such a way that he may protect the rite by completing it, so did they cause to swell.

## SBh 1.152.7.1

In the animal sacrifice for Mitra and Varuṇa this is the anuvākyā of the omentum. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦आ वां मित्रावरुणा हव्यजुष्टिमा यातं मित्रावरुणा⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ३. ८⟧).

## SBh 1.152.7.2

O ⟦देवौ⟧, you whose nature it is to shine, ⟦मित्रावरुणा⟧, Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦वां⟧, you two, ⟦हव्यजुष्टिं⟧, to the enjoying of the oblation, ⟦नमसा⟧, by praise marked by obeisance, ⟦अवसा⟧, with protection as the motive, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦ववृत्याम्⟧, may I cause to turn hither. Or else: by obeisance, and ⟦अवसा⟧, by food, ⟦आ ववृत्याम्⟧, may I cause you to turn hither. Moreover, O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦अस्माकं⟧, belonging to us, ⟦ब्रह्म⟧, the great rite that is now being performed, ⟦पृतनासु⟧, in battles, ⟦सह्याः⟧, that is, ⟦सह्यात्⟧, may it overcome the enemies. The meaning is: may I overcome the enemies by this rite. Moreover ⟦अस्माकं⟧, our, ⟦दिव्या⟧, being in heaven, ⟦वृष्टिः⟧, rain, ⟦सुपारा⟧, carrying well across: "may it be" is to be supplied. For when there is rain, the rite is completed by means of rice and the like: this is the purport.
