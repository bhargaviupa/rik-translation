## SBh 1.5.0.1

The sūkta ⟦आ तु⟧ is the second sūkta and has ten Ṛks, because, with ⟦सुरूपकृत्नुं दश⟧ continuing from the preceding, it is listed in the Anukramaṇī as ⟦आ तु युञ्जन्ति⟧. The ṛṣi, the metre, the deity and the applications are as before. The special application is this: in the atirātra, in the third paryāya, in the śastra of the Maitrāvaruṇa, this group of three Ṛks is the stotriya. For in the section ⟦अतिरात्रे पर्यायाणाम्⟧ it is said ⟦आ त्वेता नि षीदत⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ, ६. ४⟧).

## SBh 1.5.1.1

The word ⟦तु⟧ is an indeclinable in the sense of "quickly". In order to connect it with the two preverbs āṅ, the word ⟦इत⟧ is to be repeated. O ⟦सखायः⟧, friends, priests, come, come quickly to this rite; the repetition is in the sense of respect. And having come, ⟦नि⟧ ⟦षीदत⟧, sit down. And having sat down, ⟦इन्द्रमभि⟧ ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦गायत⟧, praise Indra from all sides, with excellence. What kind of friends? ⟦स्तोमवाहसः⟧: they carry, that is, offer in this rite the stomas such as the trivṛt and the pañcadaśa.

## SBh 1.5.1.2

⟦आ तु आ⟧: being indeclinables, they have the accent on the first syllable. ⟦इत⟧: from the root ⟦इण्⟧ in the sense of going; by ⟦द्व्यचोऽतस्तिङः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ३. १३५⟧) there is lengthening in the saṃhitā. ⟦नि⟧: being an indeclinable, it has the accent on the first syllable. ⟦सीदत⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦पाघ्राध्मास्थाम्नादाण्दृशि°⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. ३. ७८⟧) the substitute sīda comes for the root sad. By ⟦सदिरप्रतेः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. ३. ६६⟧) there is the change to ṣ in the saṃhitā. ⟦अभि⟧: by the statement ⟦उपसर्गाश्चाभिवर्जम्⟧ (⟦फि. सू. ८१⟧) the accent is on the end of the nominal stem.

## SBh 1.5.1.3

⟦स्तोमवाहसः⟧: by ⟦अर्तिस्तुसुहुधृक्षिक्षुभायावापदियक्षिनीभ्यो मन्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. १. १३७⟧) the word ⟦स्तोम⟧ ends in the suffix man added to the root stu, and because the suffix has n as a marker it has the accent on the first syllable. They carry the stoma, thus ⟦स्तोमं वहन्तीति⟧, and so ⟦स्तोमवाहसः⟧. By ⟦वहिहाधाञ्भ्यश्छन्दसि⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ६६०⟧) the suffix asun is added. There, since ⟦णित्⟧ continues, vṛddhi of the penultimate takes place by ⟦अत उपधायाः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. २. ११६⟧). When the accent of the original form of a latter member ending in a kṛt suffix would be obtained, the compound has the accent on the first syllable by the Uṇādi rule ⟦गतिकारकयोरपि पूर्वपदप्रकृतिस्वरत्वं च⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ६६६⟧).

## SBh 1.5.2.1

The two words ⟦सखायोऽभिप्रगायत⟧ continue here. O ⟦सखायः⟧, friends, priests, ⟦सचा⟧, you all together; or else ⟦सचा⟧, in mutual association: when the Soma, ⟦सोमे⟧, is ⟦सुते⟧, pressed, and under way, ⟦इन्द्रम् अभिप्रगायत⟧, sing to Indra. What kind of Indra? ⟦पुरूतमम्⟧: he exhausts, that is, wearies, ⟦पुरून्⟧, many, enemies; thus he is ⟦पुरूतमः⟧. He is ⟦ईशानं⟧, the lord, the master, of ⟦पुरूणां⟧, many, ⟦वार्याणां⟧, desirable, riches.

## SBh 1.5.2.2

⟦पुरूतमम्⟧: from the root ⟦तमु ग्लाने⟧, which has a causative sense contained within it, the suffix ac of the pacādi class is added. Although the suffix, being marked with c, would give the accent on the last syllable, yet setting aside the accent of the original form of a latter member ending in a kṛt suffix, the latter member has the accent on its first syllable by ⟦परादिश्छन्दसि बहुलम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. २. १९९⟧). ⟦पुरूणाम्⟧: from the root ⟦पॄ पालनपूरणयोः⟧, with ⟦कुः⟧ continuing, the suffix ku is added by ⟦पॄभिदिव्यधिगृधिधृषिभ्यः⟧ (⟦उ. सू. १. २३⟧). Since the suffix is marked with k, guṇa is prohibited (⟦पा. सू. १. १. ५⟧); by ⟦उदोष्ठ्यपूर्वस्य⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. १. १०२⟧) the vowel u results, followed by r by ⟦उरण् रपरः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. १. १. ५१⟧).

## SBh 1.5.2.3

By the accent of the suffix the word ⟦पुरु⟧ has the accent on its last syllable. **NOTE**{What is meant by ⟦अतो मतुपि⟧ here, in a sentence about the ending nām after ⟦पुरु⟧?} Therefore, in the case of matup, the ending nām that follows the word ⟦पुरु⟧, which is short and has the accent on its last syllable, has the accent on its last syllable by ⟦नामन्यतरस्याम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १७७⟧). ⟦ईशानम्⟧: from the root ⟦ईश ऐश्वर्ये⟧; because the root has an unaccented marker, the suffix śānac that follows it, being an unaccented sārvadhātuka suffix of laṭ, is unaccented. ⟦वार्याणाम्⟧: from the root ⟦वृङ् संभक्तौ⟧, by ⟦ऋहलोर्ण्यत्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. १२४⟧).

## SBh 1.5.2.4

In the rule enjoining the suffix kyap, only the root ⟦वृञः⟧ is taken, not the root ⟦वृङः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. १०९. १⟧). Setting aside the svarita of the suffix, which comes by ⟦तित्स्वरितम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १८५⟧), the word ending in ṇyat has the accent on its first syllable by ⟦ईडवन्दवृशंसदुहां ण्यतः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. २१४⟧). But in ⟦यतोऽनावः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. २१३⟧) the suffix ṇyat is not included, because it has two markers.

## SBh 1.5.2.5

This is by the restriction ⟦एकानुबन्धग्रहणे न द्व्यनुबन्धकस्य⟧. ⟦सचा⟧: from the root ⟦षच समवाये⟧; s replaces the initial ṣ of the root by ⟦धात्वादेः षः सः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. ६४⟧). Because the root belongs to the group beginning with sampad, the suffix kvip is added in the sense of the action (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. १०८.९⟧). The case is the instrumental singular. By the accent of the root it has the accent on its first syllable. By the principle ⟦सर्वे विधयश्छन्दसि विकल्प्यन्ते⟧ (⟦परिभा. ३५⟧) the rule ⟦सावेकाचः⟧ does not apply. On the view that ⟦सचा⟧ is an indeclinable, the accent on its first syllable is plain.

## SBh 1.5.3.1

The word ⟦घ⟧ is an indeclinable in the sense of emphasis. It is connected with all the words ⟦सः⟧. ⟦स⟧ ⟦घ⟧: that very Indra, endowed with the qualities stated in the preceding mantra, ⟦नः⟧, our, ⟦योगे⟧, in the connection with a human aim not obtained before, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦भुवत्⟧, may he be present, ⟦आभवतु⟧; the meaning is: may he accomplish the human aim. ⟦सः⟧, he alone, ⟦राये⟧, for the sake of wealth, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦भुवत्⟧, may he be present, ⟦आभवतु⟧. ⟦सः⟧, he alone, ⟦पुरंध्यां⟧, in a woman, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦भुवत्⟧, may he be present. Or else, in manifold intelligence may he be present. ⟦पुरंधिर्बहुधीः⟧ (⟦निरु. ६. १३⟧), says Yāska. ⟦सः⟧, he alone, ⟦वाजेभिः⟧, together with foods to be given, ⟦नः⟧, to us, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गमत्⟧, may he come, ⟦आगच्छतु⟧.

## SBh 1.5.3.2

⟦घ⟧: it is unaccented by ⟦चादयोऽनुदात्ताः⟧ (⟦फि. सू. ८४⟧). In the saṃhitā there is lengthening by ⟦ऋचि तुनुघमक्षुतङ्कुत्रोरुष्याणाम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ३. १३३⟧). ⟦योगे⟧: because the suffix ghañ is marked with ñ, it has the accent on its first syllable. ⟦भुवत्⟧ is ⟦भूयात्⟧, "may he be": when the benedictive follows the root bhū, the suffix aṅ is added by ⟦लिङ्याशिष्यङ्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ८६⟧). Because it is marked with ṅ, there is no guṇa, and the substitute uvaṅ takes place.

## SBh 1.5.3.3

By ⟦किदाशिषि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. १०४⟧) the augment yāsuṭ does not occur, according to the statement ⟦अनित्यमागमशासनम्⟧ (⟦परिभा. ९३.२⟧). The lowering of the accent by ⟦तिङ्ङतिङः⟧ takes place. By ⟦ऊडिदंपदाद्यप्पुम्रैद्युभ्यः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १७१⟧) the case ending of ⟦राये⟧ is accented. ⟦पुरंध्याम्⟧: ⟦पुरंधिः⟧ is ⟦पुरुधीः⟧; because the word belongs to the group beginning with pṛṣodara (⟦पा. सू. ६. ३. १०९⟧), the substitute am comes for the vowel u, and the vowel ī is shortened.

## SBh 1.5.3.4

It has the accent on its first syllable by ⟦आद्युदात्तप्रकरणे दिवोदासादीनां छन्दस्युपसंख्यानम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. २. ९१. १⟧). Or else, ⟦पुरं शरीरं धीयतेऽस्यामिति⟧: the city, that is, the body, is placed in her; the suffix ki is added by ⟦कर्मण्यधिकरणे च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३.३.९३⟧). The non-elision is Vedic. By ⟦नब्विषयस्यानिसन्तस्य⟧ (⟦फि. सू. २६⟧) the word ⟦पुर⟧ has the accent on its first syllable. Because the compound belongs to the group beginning with dāsībhāra (⟦पा. सू. ६. २. ४२⟧), the accent is that of the original form of the former member. ⟦गमत्⟧: from the root gam, the leṭ with tip.

## SBh 1.5.3.5

By ⟦इतश्च लोपः परस्मैपदेषु⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. ९७⟧) the vowel i is elided. By ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. २. ४. ७३⟧) there is luk of śap. By ⟦लेटोऽडाटौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. ९४⟧) the augment aṭ is added. Since it is unaccented, by ⟦आगमा अनुदात्ताः⟧ (⟦पा. म. ३. १. ३. ७⟧), only the accent of the root remains. ⟦वाजेभिः⟧: because it belongs to the group beginning with vṛṣa, it has the accent on its first syllable.

## SBh 1.5.4.1

⟦समत्सु⟧, in battles, ⟦यस्य⟧, whose, Indra's, ⟦संस्थे⟧, yoked to the chariot, ⟦हरी⟧, the two horses, the enemies ⟦न⟧ ⟦वृण्वते⟧, do not resort to; the meaning is: seeing the chariot and the two horses they flee. ⟦तस्मा⟧ ⟦इन्द्राय⟧, for that Indra, for his satisfaction, O priests, ⟦गायत⟧, make praise. Among the forty-six names of battle beginning with ⟦रणः⟧, ⟦समत्सु समरणे⟧ (⟦नि. २. १७. २२⟧) is read.

## SBh 1.5.4.2

⟦संस्थे⟧: the chariot stands well, thus it is ⟦संस्थो रथः⟧; the suffix ka is added by ⟦आतश्चोपसर्गे⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. १३६⟧). The accent is that of the original form of a latter member ending in a kṛt suffix. ⟦वृण्वते⟧: by the accent of the suffix the vowel a is accented. ⟦सति शिष्टस्वरबलीयस्त्वमन्यत्र विकरणेभ्यः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १५८. ९-११⟧). The lowering of the accent by ⟦तिङ्ङतिङः⟧ does not occur, because of the prohibition ⟦यद्वृत्तान्नित्यम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. १. ६६⟧). Although the rule is stated in the ablative (⟦पा. सू. १. १. ६७⟧), here the operation is wanted even when something intervenes. They carry the chariot; thus ⟦हरी⟧ are the two horses, ⟦अश्वौ⟧.

## SBh 1.5.4.3

With ⟦इन्⟧ continuing (⟦उ. सू. ४. ५५७⟧), the suffix in is added by ⟦हृपिषिरुहिवृतिविदिच्छिदिकीर्तिभ्यश्च⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४, ५५८⟧). Because it is marked with n, it has the accent on its first syllable. ⟦समत्सु⟧: kvip after the root att with the prefix sam. ⟦शत्रवः⟧: ⟦शतिः⟧ is a root found in the sūtras, in the sense of harming. ⟦रुशतिभ्यां क्रुन्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ५४३⟧). Because it is marked with n, it has the accent on its first syllable. ⟦तस्मै⟧: the accent that ⟦सावेकाचः०⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १। १६८⟧) gives to the case ending is prohibited by ⟦न गोश्वन्साववर्ण⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १८२⟧), so that only the accent of the nominal stem remains.

## SBh 1.5.5.1

⟦इमे⟧ ⟦सोमासः⟧, these Somas, prepared in this rite, go ⟦सुतपाव्ने⟧, to the drinker of the pressed Soma, that is, to the one who drinks the pressed Soma; the dative is in the sense of the genitive. For ⟦वीतये⟧, for the eating of that drinker, ⟦यन्ति⟧, they go: they reach him alone. What kind of Somas? ⟦सुता:⟧, pressed; ⟦शुचयः⟧, pure, because they have been purified by the daśāpavitra. ⟦दध्याशिरः⟧: those Somas of which the curd that is being poured in is the āśīḥ, the destroyer of faults, are ⟦दध्याशिरः⟧.

## SBh 1.5.5.2

⟦सुतपाव्ने⟧: he drinks the pressed Soma, thus ⟦सुतपावा⟧. Because the suffix vanip is marked with p, only the accent of the root remains. In the compound, setting aside the accent of the original form of the former member that stands in the accusative, the accent is that of the original form of a latter member ending in a kṛt suffix. ⟦शुचयः⟧: the root ⟦शुच दीप्तौ⟧. With ⟦इन्⟧ continuing, the suffix in is added by ⟦इगुपधात्कित्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ५५९⟧). Because the suffix is marked with k, there is no guṇa of the short penultimate vowel. Because it is marked with n, it has the accent on its first syllable. ⟦वीतये⟧: from the root ⟦वि गतिप्रजनकान्त्यशनखादनेषु⟧, the suffix ktin, accented, is added by ⟦मन्त्रे वृषेषपचमनविदभूवीरा उदात्तः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३, ३. ९६⟧). ⟦सोमासः⟧: the root ⟦षुञ् अभिषवे⟧; the suffix man is added by the rule beginning ⟦अर्तिस्तुसुहुसृधृक्षि°⟧ (⟦उ. सू. १. १३७⟧). Because it is marked with n, it has the accent on its first syllable. By ⟦आज्जसेरसुक्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. १. ५०⟧) the augment asuk is added.

## SBh 1.5.5.3

⟦दध्याशिरः⟧: it holds and nourishes, thus ⟦दधि⟧. The root is ⟦डुधाञ् धारणपोषणयोः⟧. By ⟦आदृगमहनजनः किकिनौ लिट् च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. १७१⟧) the suffix kin is added. Because it is treated like liṭ, there is doubling. Because the suffix is marked with k, the vowel ā is elided. Because it is marked with n, it has the accent on its first syllable. The root ⟦शॄ⟧ is in the sense of harming. ⟦शृणाति हिनस्ति⟧: it harms, when it is being poured into the Soma, **NOTE**{How are ⟦सोमस्य स्वाभाविकं रसम्⟧ and ⟦नीरसं दोषं⟧ construed with ⟦शृणाति हिनस्ति⟧ and ⟦ऋजीषत्वप्रयुक्तं⟧?} the natural juice of the Soma, or the fault of tastelessness brought about by its being ṛjīṣa; thus it is ⟦इत्याशीः⟧. When the suffix kvip is added, there are the vowel i and the following r by ⟦ऋत इद्धातोः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७, १. १००⟧). Those Somas whose āśīḥ is curd alone are ⟦दध्याशिरः⟧. In the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the original form of the former member.

## SBh 1.5.6.1

⟦सुक्रतो⟧, O one of good works, or of good wisdom, ⟦इन्द्र⟧, ⟦त्वं⟧, you, ⟦सुतस्य⟧, of the pressed Soma, ⟦पीतये⟧, for the drinking, and ⟦ज्यैष्ठ्याय⟧, for being the eldest among the gods, ⟦सद्यः⟧, in that very moment, ⟦वृद्धो⟧ ⟦अजायथाः⟧, you became grown, you became endowed with vigour through growth.

## SBh 1.5.6.2

⟦पीतये⟧: from the root ⟦पा पाने⟧, the suffix ktin is added by ⟦स्थागापापचो भावे⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. ९५⟧). The vowel ī comes by the rule beginning ⟦घुमास्था⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ६६⟧). Although the suffix is marked with n, it has the accent on the suffix by vyatyaya (interchange). Or else ⟦उत्तरसूत्रगतमुदात्तपदमत्रापि⟧: the word "udātta" belonging to the following rule is to be connected here also. ⟦सद्यः⟧: by the rule ⟦सद्यः परुत्परारि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. ३. २२⟧), in the sense of "on the same day", the change of samāna to sa and the suffix dya are stated as irregular forms.

## SBh 1.5.6.3

It has the accent on the suffix. ⟦वृद्धः⟧: the root ⟦वृधु वृद्धौ⟧. It has the accent on the suffix. ⟦ज्यैष्ठ्याय⟧: the state of the eldest, ⟦ज्येष्ठस्य भावो⟧, is the abstract noun; the suffix ṣyañ is added by ⟦गुणवचनब्राह्मणादिभ्यः कर्मणि च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५, १, १२४⟧). Because it is marked with ñ, it has the accent on its first syllable.

What kind of Somas? ⟦आशवः⟧, pervading **NOTE**{Does ⟦सदनत्रये⟧ mean the three savanas?} in the three seats, or in the model and the modified sacrifices. ## SBh 1.5.7.2

⟦गिर्वणः⟧: they praise, thus ⟦गिरः⟧ are praises, ⟦स्तुतयः⟧. The root is ⟦गॄ⟧, in the sense of sound. When kvip is added, there are the vowel i and the following r by ⟦ऋत इद्धातोः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. १. १००⟧).

## SBh 1.5.7.3

He is served with songs, thus ⟦गीर्भिर्वन्यते सेव्यते⟧; so he is ⟦गिर्वणाः⟧. The root is ⟦वन षण संभक्तौ⟧; ⟦संभक्तिः सेवा⟧, the sambhakti is service. The suffix asun is added by ⟦सर्वधातुभ्योऽसुन्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ६२८⟧). ⟦प्रचेतसे⟧: in the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the original form of the former member.

## SBh 1.5.8.1

O ⟦शतक्रतो⟧, of many works, or of many wisdoms, Indra, ⟦त्वां⟧, you, ⟦स्तोमाः⟧, the stotras of the Sāma singers, ⟦अवीवृधन्⟧, have made grow. Likewise the ⟦उक्था⟧, the śastras of the Bahvṛcas, ⟦त्वाम्⟧ ⟦अवीवृधन्⟧, have made you grow. Since formerly it was so, therefore now also ⟦नः⟧, our, ⟦गिरः⟧, praises, ⟦त्वां⟧ ⟦वर्धन्तु⟧, may they make you grow; may they make you very grown.

## SBh 1.5.8.2

⟦स्तोमाः⟧: the suffix man is marked with n, so it has the accent on its first syllable. ⟦अवीवृधन्⟧: the root ⟦वृधु वृद्धौ⟧; from the stem with the causative suffix, in the aorist, with the suffix caṅ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ४८⟧), the rule ⟦उर्ऋत्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. ४. ७⟧) prescribes ṛ for the ṛ of the penultimate syllable of vṛdh, and so the guṇa, though it is the more internal operation, is set aside. Then come doubling (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. ११⟧), the retention of the initial consonant (⟦पा. सू. ७, ४. ६०⟧), treatment like the desiderative (⟦पा. सू. ७. ४. ९३⟧), the vowel i (⟦पा. सू. ७. ४, ७९⟧), lengthening (⟦पा. सू. ७. ४. ९४⟧), and the augment aṭ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ७१⟧).

## SBh 1.5.8.3

⟦उक्था उक्थानि⟧: by ⟦पातॄतुदिवचिरिचिसिचिभ्यस्थक्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. २. १६४⟧) the suffix thak is added to the root vac. Because it is marked with k, there is samprasāraṇa. By ⟦शेश्छन्दसि बहुलम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. ७०⟧) there is elision of śi and of n. By the accent of the suffix it has the accent on its last syllable. Because of incompatibility, even when a word in the vocative follows, there is no treating of it as part of the following word, and so there is no unaccented state. ⟦वर्धन्तु⟧: from the root vṛdh, which has the causative sense contained within it, the parasmaipada is used by a change of voice.

## SBh 1.5.9.1

⟦इन्द्रः⟧, ⟦इमं⟧, this, ⟦वाजं⟧, food in the form of Soma, ⟦सनेत्⟧, may he share in, may he partake of. What kind of Indra? ⟦अक्षितोतिः⟧, one whose protection is unharmed; the meaning is: he never at any time lets go of protection. What kind of food? ⟦सहस्रिणं⟧, having the number a thousand, by its being present in the model sacrifice and in the modified sacrifices. ⟦यस्मिन्⟧, in which, ⟦वाजे⟧, food, ⟦विश्वानि⟧, all, ⟦पौंस्या⟧: ⟦पौंस्यानि⟧, manly powers, that is, strengths, are present; such a food: thus it is construed with what precedes.

## SBh 1.5.9.2

⟦अक्षितोतिः⟧: now, this root ⟦क्षि क्षये⟧ is intransitive. Since it has no object, the suffix kta must be in the locative sense, in the sense of the action, or in the agent sense. Now if it were here in the agent sense or the locative sense, then, because the suffix ṇyat is not prescribed in those two senses, with ⟦क्षियः⟧ continuing (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ५९⟧), there would have to be lengthening by ⟦निष्ठायामण्यदर्थे⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ६०⟧).

## SBh 1.5.9.3

And so, with the change to n in the niṣṭhā by ⟦क्षियो दीर्घात्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. २. ४६⟧), it would be ⟦अक्षीण⟧ and not ⟦अक्षित⟧. Now, if ⟦क्षित⟧ is taken as relating to the action, by ⟦नपुंसके भावे क्तः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. ११४⟧), then, since that has the sense of ṇyat, there is the prohibition ⟦°अण्यर्थे⟧, so that there is neither lengthening nor change to n, and ⟦क्षितमिति⟧ results.

## SBh 1.5.9.4

But then the negative compound does not fit with what is in hand, and it must be a bahuvrīhi, ⟦न विद्यते क्षितमत्र⟧, "in which there is no kṣita". And then by ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. २. १७२⟧) the latter member would have the accent on its last syllable. Again, in the bahuvrīhi with the word ⟦ऊति⟧, with the accent of the original form of the former member, that same accent would remain, and so the desired accent on the first syllable would not result. This is true. For this very reason, here the root ⟦क्षि⟧ is taken as having the causative sense contained within it. Therefore, because it is transitive, this niṣṭhā is in the object. And then, because of the prohibition ⟦°अण्यदर्थे⟧, neither lengthening nor change to n will occur. And so in the negative compound, "not ⟦क्षिता⟧" is ⟦अक्षिता⟧, meaning ⟦अक्षयिता⟧. And there the negative particle has the accent on its first syllable, by the accent of the original form of a former member that is an indeclinable. Again, in the bahuvrīhi with the word ⟦ऊति⟧, that same accent will remain; so there is no fault whatever.

## SBh 1.5.9.5

⟦रि क्षि चिरि जिरि दाश दृ जिघांसायाम्⟧: or else, from ⟦क्षिणोतेर्हिँसार्थस्य⟧, in the sense of harming, the niṣṭhā is in the object. And so in the sense ⟦चाहिंसितोतिरित्यर्थे⟧ the accent is established in the manner stated; thus there is no fault. ⟦सनेत्⟧: the root ⟦वन षण संभक्तौ⟧, of the bhū class. ⟦वाजम्⟧: because it belongs to the group beginning with vṛṣa, it has the accent on its first syllable. ⟦इन्द्रः⟧: because the suffix ra is marked with n, it has the accent on its first syllable. ⟦सहस्रिणम्⟧: ⟦सहस्रमस्यास्ति⟧, he has a thousand; the suffixes ini and ṭhan by ⟦अत इनिठनौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. २. ११५⟧).

## SBh 1.5.9.6

The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦विश्वानि⟧: from the root viś, with the suffix kvan (⟦उ. सू. १. १४९⟧); because it is marked with n, it has the accent on its first syllable. ⟦पुंसः कर्माणि⟧, the deeds of a man, are ⟦पौंस्यनि⟧. Because brāhmaṇa and the rest form a group of unlimited extent, the suffix ṣyañ is added by ⟦गुणवचनब्राह्मणादिभ्यः कर्मणि च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. १. १२४⟧). Because it is marked with ñ, it has the accent on its first syllable. For the nominative plural, the substitute ḍā comes by the rule beginning ⟦सुपां सुलुक्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. १. ३९⟧).

## SBh 1.5.9.7

Now, since by ⟦स्त्रीपुंसाभ्यां नञ्स्नञौ भवनात्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ४. १.८७⟧) the suffixes nañ and snañ are prescribed in the senses of "descendant" and the rest, up to ⟦धान्यानां भवने क्षेत्रे खञ्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. २. १⟧), as ⟦पुंसोऽपत्यं⟧ is ⟦पौंस्नः⟧ and ⟦पुंस आगतः⟧ is ⟦पौंस्नः⟧ and so on, in the same way, in this sense also, ⟦पुंसो भावः कर्म⟧, "the state or the action of a man", setting aside ṣyañ, the form must be only ⟦पौंस्नानि⟧; how then is ⟦पौंस्यानीति⟧ said? The answer: in the rule ⟦आ च त्वात्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. १. १२०⟧), because of the mention of ⟦त्वात्⟧ as a limit, up to ⟦ब्रह्मणस्त्वः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. १. १३६⟧) there is co-existence of tva and tal with the suffixes ⟦इमनिजादिभिः⟧. In the same way, there too, from the word ⟦च⟧, there is co-existence of nañ and snañ with ⟦ष्यञादिभिः⟧; there is no relation of blocked and blocker.
