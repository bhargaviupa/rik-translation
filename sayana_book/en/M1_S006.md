## SBh 1.6.0.1

Of the six sūktas beginning with ⟦सुरूप⟧, for the third, the sūkta ⟦युञ्जन्ति⟧, the number of mantras, the ṛṣi, the metres, the deities and the application are to be understood as before. In that sūkta of ten Ṛks, the first three and the last one, these four, are addressed to Indra.

## SBh 1.6.0.2

Beginning with the fourth Ṛk, ⟦आदह⟧, six Ṛks are addressed to the Maruts. Among them the two Ṛks ⟦वीळु चित्⟧ and ⟦इन्द्रेण⟧, although addressed to the Maruts, are in truth also addressed to Indra. All this has been stated in the Anukramaṇikā: ⟦सुरूपकृत्नुं दशैन्द्रमा तु युञ्जन्त्याहेत्येताः षण्मारुत्यो वीळु चिदिन्द्रेणेत्यैन्द्र्यौ च⟧, that is, the ten beginning with ⟦सुरूपकृत्नुं⟧ are addressed to Indra; the six marked by ⟦युञ्जन्त्याहेत्येताः⟧ are addressed to the Maruts; and ⟦वीळु चित्⟧ and ⟦इन्द्रेण⟧ are addressed to Indra. In this sūkta the tṛca ⟦युञ्जन्ति⟧ is the anurūpa of the Brāhmaṇācchaṃsin in the third night-paryāya. And so, in the section ⟦अतिरात्रे पर्यायाणाम्⟧, it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦योगे योगे तवस्तरं युञ्जन्ति ब्रध्नमरुषम्⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ६. ४⟧).

## SBh 1.6.1.1

Indra is endowed with supreme lordship, and his supreme lordship is established by his abiding in the forms of Agni, Vāyu, Āditya and the stars. ⟦ब्रध्नम्⟧: Indra abiding in the form of the sun; ⟦अरुषं⟧: abiding in the form of Agni, who is free from harming; ⟦चरन्तं⟧: spreading everywhere in the form of Vāyu. This Indra, ⟦परि⟧ ⟦तस्थुषः⟧, the living beings of the three worlds, standing round about, ⟦युञ्जन्ति⟧, join to their own rite as deity. Of that same Indra, ⟦रोचना⟧, the shining ones, that is, the stars, which are particular embodiments of him, ⟦दिवि⟧, in the world of heaven, ⟦रोचन्ते⟧, give light. That this mantra has the meaning stated is explained in another Brāhmaṇa: He says ⟦युञ्जन्ति ब्रध्नम⟧; ⟦असौ वा आदित्यो ब्रध्नः⟧, yonder sun is Bradhna; ⟦आदित्यमेवास्मै युनक्ति⟧, he yokes the sun alone for him. He says ⟦अरुषम⟧; ⟦अग्निर्वा अरुषः⟧, Agni is Aruṣa.

## SBh 1.6.1.2

⟦अग्निमेवास्मै युनक्ति⟧, he yokes Agni alone for him. He says ⟦चरन्तम⟧; ⟦वायुर्वै चरन्⟧, Vāyu is the moving one; ⟦वायुमेवास्मै युनक्ति⟧, he yokes Vāyu alone for him. He says ⟦परि तस्थुष⟧; ⟦इमे वै लोकाः परि तस्थुषः⟧, these worlds are those standing round about; ⟦इमानेवास्मै लोकान्युनक्ति⟧, he yokes these worlds alone for him. He says ⟦रोचन्ते रोचना दिवी⟧; ⟦नक्षत्राणि वै रोचना दिवि⟧, the stars are the shining ones in heaven; ⟦नक्षत्राण्येवास्मै रोचयति⟧, he makes the stars alone shine for him (⟦तै. ब्रा, ३, ९. ४. १-२⟧). Among the twenty-five names meaning "great" it is read: ⟦महः ब्रध्नः⟧ (⟦नि. ३. ३, २⟧). The sun too is Bradhna only because of being great.

## SBh 1.6.1.3

⟦युञ्जन्ति⟧: it is udātta on its first syllable by the accent of the suffix, the ending ⟦अन्तेः⟧. ⟦व्रध्नम्⟧: udātta on the last syllable of the stem. ⟦अरुषम्⟧: ⟦उष रुष रिष हिंसार्थाः⟧, the roots uṣ, ruṣ and riṣ have the sense of harming. Those who harm are ⟦रुषा⟧, harmers. By ⟦इगुपधज्ञाप्रीकिरः कः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. १३५⟧) the suffix ka comes, and it is udātta by the accent of the suffix. He for whom there are no ⟦रुषा⟧ is ⟦अरुषः⟧. By ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. २. १७२⟧) the last syllable of the latter member is udātta.

## SBh 1.6.1.4

By ⟦अमि पूर्वः⟧ (⟦पा, सू. ६. १, १०७⟧) the earlier form is the single substitute, and by ⟦एकादेश उदात्तेनोदात्तः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. २. ५⟧) it is udātta. ⟦चरन्तम्⟧: since śap has p as an indicatory letter it is anudātta; and the śatṛ, too, being anudātta by the accent of a lasārvadhātuka ending, only the accent of the root remains. ⟦तस्थुषः⟧: after ⟦तिष्ठते⟧ the substitute kvasu comes for liṭ (⟦पा, सू. ३. २. १०७⟧).

## SBh 1.6.1.5

By ⟦वस्वेकाद्धसाम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७, २. ६७⟧) the augment iṭ is set aside, although it is more internal, and by the maxim ⟦संप्रसारणं संप्रसारणाश्रयं च बलीयः⟧ (⟦पा. म. ६. १. १७. २⟧), saṃprasāraṇa, and what depends on saṃprasāraṇa, is the stronger. When śas follows, since the stem is a bha (⟦पा. सू. १. ४. १८⟧), there is saṃprasāraṇa of vasu by ⟦वसोः संप्रसारणम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १३१⟧). Then comes parapūrvatva **NOTE**{what exactly is meant here by parapūrvatva, the substitution of which vowel for which}. By the rule that s of a substitute or of a suffix becomes ṣ after the stated letters (⟦पा. सू. ८. ३. ५९⟧). ⟦वसोः⟧ is udātta by the accent of the suffix. The next word is ⟦रोचन्ते⟧.

## SBh 1.6.1.6

The personal ending is anudātta by the accent of a lasārvadhātuka. Śap is anudātta because it has p as an indicatory letter. Only the accent of the root remains. ⟦रोचना⟧: by ⟦अनुदात्तेतश्च हलादेः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. १४९⟧) the suffix yuc comes; by ⟦युवोरनाकौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. १. १⟧) the substitute ana; by ⟦चितः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १६३⟧) it is udātta on the last syllable. ⟦दिवि⟧: by ⟦ऊडिदम्⟧ and the rest (⟦पा.सू. ६.१.१७१⟧) the case-ending is udātta.

## SBh 1.6.2.1

⟦अस्य⟧: of this Indra, who is denoted by words such as ⟦ब्रध्न⟧ and who abides here and there in forms such as the sun, ⟦रथे⟧, in the chariot, ⟦हरी⟧, two horses having this name, the charioteers ⟦युञ्जन्ति⟧, yoke. That the two horses of Indra have the name Hari is because it is read ⟦हरी इन्द्रस्य रोहितोऽग्नेः⟧ (⟦नि. १. १५. १⟧). What are the two Haris like? ⟦काम्या⟧: to be desired. ⟦विपक्षसा⟧: those two horses of which the two sides of the chariot are various are ⟦विपक्षसौ⟧; the meaning is that they are harnessed to the two sides of the chariot. ⟦शोणा⟧: red in colour. ⟦धृष्णू⟧: bold. ⟦नृवाहसा⟧: carriers of ⟦नृणां⟧, men, that is, persons, headed by Indra and his charioteer.

## SBh 1.6.2.2

⟦अस्य⟧: because it refers back to what was said by ⟦ब्रध्नम्⟧, by ⟦इदमोऽन्वादेशेऽशनुदात्तस्तृतीयादौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. २. ४.३२⟧) the substitute aś comes. Because it has ś as an indicatory letter (⟦पा. सू. १, १. ५५⟧) it replaces the whole, and it is anudātta. The case-ending is anudātta itself, so the whole word is anudātta. ⟦काम्या⟧: ⟦कमु कान्तौ⟧, the root kam in the sense of desire. By ⟦कमेर्णिङ्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ३०⟧) the suffix ṇiṅ comes after kam. After ⟦कामयते⟧ the suffix yat comes by ⟦अचो यत्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ९७⟧).

## SBh 1.6.2.3

As an exception to the svarita of a suffix with t as an indicatory letter, it is udātta on its first syllable by ⟦यतोऽनावः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. २१३⟧). By ⟦सुपां सुलुक्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७, १, ३९⟧) ḍā is the substitute for the dual ending. Because the two carry the chariot they are ⟦हरी⟧. By ⟦हृपिषि⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ५५८⟧) and the rest, the suffix in comes. Because it has n as an indicatory letter it is udātta on its first syllable. ⟦विपक्षसा⟧: by ⟦पचिवचिभ्यां सुट् च⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ६५९⟧) the suffix ⟦असुन⟧ and the augment suṭ come after pac. Those two of which the two sides, ⟦पक्षसी⟧, are different.

## SBh 1.6.2.4

The word ⟦वि⟧ is udātta because it is an indeclinable. By the accent of the first member only that remains. ḍā is the substitute for the dual ending. ⟦रथे⟧: because they delight, ⟦रमन्ते⟧, in it, it is ⟦रथः⟧; the root is ⟦रमु क्रीडायाम्⟧, ram in the sense of play. By ⟦हनिकुषिनीरमिकाशिभ्यः क्यन्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ३. १५९⟧) the suffix kthan comes. Because it has k as an indicatory letter, the m is dropped by ⟦अनुदात्तोपदेश⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ३७⟧) and the rest. By the accent of the n-marked suffix it is udātta on its first syllable. ⟦शोणा⟧: ⟦शोणृ वर्णगत्योः⟧, the root śoṇ in the senses of colour and going. Because the two are the instrument of going, the suffix ghañ is used in the sense of instrument.

## SBh 1.6.2.5

Because it has ñ as an indicatory letter it is udātta on its first syllable. By ⟦सुपां सुलुक्⟧ the substitute is ḍā. ⟦धृष्णू⟧: ⟦ञिधृषा प्रागल्भ्ये⟧, the root dhṛṣ in the sense of boldness. By ⟦त्रसिगृधिधृषिक्षिपेः क्नुः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. १४०⟧) the suffix knu comes. Because it has k as an indicatory letter there is no guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦नृवाहसा⟧: because the two carry men, ⟦नॄन् वहतः⟧, the suffix asun comes after vah by ⟦वहिहाधाञ्भ्यश्छन्दसि⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ६६०⟧). Since ⟦णित्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ६५७⟧) continues, there is vṛddhi. Because it has n as an indicatory letter it is udātta on its first syllable. By the accent of the latter member, which ends in a kṛt suffix, only that remains.

## SBh 1.6.3.1

O ⟦मर्याः⟧, mortals, "look at this wonder" is to be supplied. What is the wonder? It is said. This Indra, in the form of the sun, ⟦अजायथाः⟧, arose, having come together, day by day, with ⟦उषद्भिः⟧, burning rays, or with the times of dawn. Or, treating the setting of the sun itself as death, and using the plural in place of the singular by interchange, an address is made: "O mortal, every day you ⟦अजायथाः⟧", thus it is to be construed. Doing what? ⟦केतुं⟧ ⟦कृण्वन्⟧: making in the morning knowledge, ⟦अकेतवे⟧, for the living being, ⟦प्राणिने⟧, who is without knowledge because at night he is overcome by sleep. ⟦पेशः⟧: making form, ⟦अपेशसे⟧, for the object which has no form, because at night it is not distinct, being covered by darkness; making form manifest in the morning by the removal of the darkness. Yāska says ⟦पेश इति रूपनाम पिंशतेः⟧ (⟦निरु. ८. ११⟧). The two datives ⟦अकेतवे⟧ and ⟦अपेशसे⟧ are to be seen as having the sense of the genitive.

## SBh 1.6.3.2

⟦केतुम्⟧: the accent is that of the stem. ⟦कृण्वन्⟧: ⟦कृवि हिंसाकरणयोश्च⟧, the root kṛv in the senses of harming and doing. The substitute śatṛ for laṭ. By ⟦इदितो नुम् धातोः⟧ (⟦पा, सू. ७. १, ५८⟧) the augment num comes. Where śap in the agent sense would be expected, by ⟦धिन्विकृण्व्योर च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १, ८०⟧) the suffix u comes, and in conjunction with it a replaces the letter v. By ⟦अतो लोपः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ४८⟧) the a is dropped. Because that is treated as the thing it replaced, the guṇa of the short penultimate vowel of what precedes (⟦पा. सू. ७. ३. ८६⟧) does not occur. The a is udātta by the accent of the suffix. ⟦अकेतवे⟧: in the bahuvrīhi, by ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. २. १७२⟧) the last syllable of the latter member is udātta. ⟦पेशः⟧.

## SBh 1.6.3.3

By ⟦नब्विषयस्यानिसन्तस्य⟧ (⟦फि. सू. २६⟧) it is udātta on its first syllable. ⟦मर्याः⟧: by ⟦छन्दसि निष्टर्क्य°⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३, १. १२३⟧) and the rest, the form from ⟦म्रियते⟧ is given by nipātana. The vocative has nighāta (⟦पा. सू. ८. १. १९⟧). Because of the lack of syntactic connection, what precedes is not treated as a part of what follows. ⟦अपेशसे⟧: by ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम्⟧ the last syllable of the latter member is udātta. ⟦सम्⟧.

## SBh 1.6.3.4

It is an indeclinable and is udātta on its first syllable. ⟦उषद्भिः⟧: ⟦उष प्लुष दाहे⟧, the roots uṣ and pluṣ in the sense of burning; with blazing rays. Where the substitute śatṛ for laṭ, and śap, would be expected, śa comes by interchange. By ⟦सार्वधातुकमपित्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. १. २. ४⟧), because it is treated as having ṅ as an indicatory letter, the guṇa of the short penultimate vowel does not occur. The śa is udātta by the accent of the suffix. Of the śatṛ that follows, because it comes after a root ending in a at its enunciation, there is anudātta by the lasārvadhātuka rule. By ⟦एकादेश उदात्तेनोदात्तः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. २. ५⟧). ⟦अजायथाः⟧: in the sense of ⟦अजायत⟧, by interchange of person, there is nighāta.

## SBh 1.6.4.1

Here there is a special application. On the twenty-fourth day, in the morning pressing, in the śastra of the Brāhmaṇācchaṃsin, there are the two Ṛks ⟦आदह स्वधामनु⟧ and one, ⟦इन्द्रेण सं हि दृक्षसे⟧. This tṛca is called ṣaḷahastotriya. And so it has been laid down in the sūtra, in the section ⟦चतुर्विंशे होताजनिष्ट⟧: ⟦इन्द्रेण सं हि दृक्षस आदह स्वधामन्वित्येका द्वे च⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ७.२⟧). Although this sūkta is addressed to Indra, still in the six Ṛks beginning with ⟦आदह⟧ the Maruts are described, because it is said in the Anukramaṇikā ⟦प्रायेणैन्द्रे मरुतः⟧ (⟦अनु. २. २२⟧). ⟦आत्⟧ is an indeclinable in the sense of immediate sequence; ⟦अह⟧ is in the sense of emphasis. ⟦आदह⟧: immediately after the rainy season. ⟦स्वधामनु⟧.

## SBh 1.6.4.2

After this, looking to the food or the water that is to be produced, the gods, the Maruts, ⟦एरिरे⟧, set in motion, ⟦गर्भत्वम्⟧, the form of an embryo of water in the middle of the cloud. They set in motion Parjanya, the maker of water. To show that they do so every year the word ⟦पुनः⟧ is used. What are the Maruts like? ⟦दधानाः⟧: bearing, ⟦नाम⟧, a name, ⟦यज्ञियं⟧, fit for the sacrifice. In the seven groups of the Maruts names fit for the sacrifice, such as ⟦ईदृङ्चान्यादृङ्च⟧, are recorded elsewhere. Among the twenty-eight names of food beginning with ⟦अन्धः⟧ it is read ⟦ऊर्क् रसः स्वधा⟧ (⟦नि. २. ७. १७⟧). Among the hundred and one names of water beginning with ⟦अर्णः⟧ it is read ⟦तेजः स्वधा अक्षरम्⟧ (⟦नि. १. १२. ९७⟧).

## SBh 1.6.4.3

⟦आदह⟧: two indeclinables, udātta on their first syllables. ⟦स्वधाम्⟧: because it holds, ⟦दधाति⟧, its own world, ⟦स्वं लोकं⟧, that is, nourishes it, it is ⟦स्वधा⟧. By ⟦आतोऽनुपसर्गे कः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. ३⟧) the suffix ka comes. The accent is that of the latter member ending in a kṛt suffix. The two words ⟦अनु⟧ and ⟦पुनः⟧ are indeclinables, udātta on their first syllables. ⟦गर्भत्वम्⟧: the state of an embryo. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦एरिरे⟧: after the root ⟦ईर गतौ⟧, which has the causative sense included, being a root with anudātta as an indicatory letter, the substitute irec comes for jha of liṭ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. ८१⟧). Because it has c as an indicatory letter it is udātta on its last syllable. In ⟦सह सुपा⟧ (⟦पा. सू. २. १. ४⟧), by dividing the rule at ⟦सुपा⟧, even though a tiṅ-ending word is compounded with ā, by ⟦समासस्य⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. २२३⟧) it is udātta on its last syllable.

## SBh 1.6.4.4

By ⟦इजादेश्च गुरुमतोऽनृच्छः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ३६⟧) the suffix ām does not occur, because this is a mantra. In the bahuvrīhi, by the accent of the first member, only that remains. ⟦मखः⟧: the accent is that of the stem. Because strength, ⟦सहो⟧, is in this act of worship, it is ⟦सहस्वत्⟧. Because by ⟦तसौ मत्वर्थे⟧ (⟦पा. सू. १. ४. १९⟧) the designation bha prevents the designation pada, the s does not change to ru. By ⟦मादुपधायाश्च मतोर्वोऽयवादिभ्यः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. २. ९⟧) the m of matup becomes v. The word ⟦सहस्⟧ is udātta on its first syllable by ⟦नब्विषयस्यानिसन्तस्य⟧ (⟦फि. सू. २६⟧). Because matup has p as an indicatory letter, only that remains. ⟦काम्यैः⟧: by ⟦कमेर्णिङ्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ३०⟧).

## SBh 1.6.8.3

By ⟦अत उपधायाः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. २. ११६⟧) there is vṛddhi. The mit-ness obtained by ⟦जनीजॄष्क्नसुरञ्जोऽमन्ताश्च⟧ (⟦भ्वा. ग. सू.⟧) through the root ending in am, is prohibited by ⟦न कम्यमिचमाम्⟧ (⟦भ्वा. ग. सू.⟧), so that the shortening of the penultimate vowel by ⟦मितां ह्रस्वः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ९२⟧) does not occur. After the causative form, ⟦अचो यत्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ९७⟧). Elision of ṇi (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४.५१⟧). Where ⟦तित्स्वरितम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १८५⟧) would be expected, it is udātta on its first syllable by ⟦यतोऽनावः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. २१३⟧).

## SBh 1.6.9.1

O ⟦परिज्मन्⟧, all-pervading host of the Maruts, ⟦अतः⟧, from this atmosphere, the station of the host of the Maruts, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गहि⟧, come to this rite. ⟦दिवो⟧ ⟦वा⟧: or come together from the world of heaven. ⟦रोचनादधि⟧: or come together from the shining orb of the sun. The meaning is: at the time of our rite, from wherever you stand, from all of it come. For what purpose the coming? It is said. ⟦अस्मिन्⟧, the priest engaged in this rite, ⟦गिरः⟧, the praises, ⟦सम्⟧ ⟦ऋञ्जते⟧, accomplishes well. Yāska says ⟦ऋञ्जतिः प्रसाधनकर्मा⟧ (⟦निरु० ६. २१⟧), that is, the verb ṛñj has the sense of accomplishing. The meaning is: come to hear these praises. Although, since the mantra is being used by the priest, the verb of the root ⟦ऋञ्जति⟧ should be in the first person, yet because it is stated in the indirect form, the third person is used. And Yāska states the mark of the indirect form: ⟦तास्त्रिविधा ऋचः परोक्षकृताः प्रत्यक्षकृता आध्यात्मिक्यश्च । तत्र परोक्षकृताः सर्वाभिर्नामविभक्तिभिर्युज्यन्ते प्रथमपुरुषैश्चाख्यातस्य⟧ (⟦निरु. ७. १⟧), that is, "Those Ṛks are of three kinds: indirect, direct, and concerning the self. Among them the indirect ones are joined with all the case-endings of nouns and with the third persons of the verb."

## SBh 1.6.9.2

⟦अतः⟧: by ⟦पञ्चम्यास्तसिल्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. ३. ७⟧) the suffix tasil comes in the sense of the ablative. By ⟦एतदोऽश्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. ३. ५⟧) the substitute is aś. Because it has ś as an indicatory letter it replaces the whole (⟦पा. सू. १. १. ५५⟧). By ⟦लिति⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १९३⟧) the syllable before the suffix is udātta. ⟦परिज्मन्⟧: ⟦अज गतिक्षेपणयोः⟧, the root aj in the senses of going and throwing. By ⟦अन्येभ्योऽपि दृश्यन्ते⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. ७५⟧) the suffix manin comes. The dropping of a is Vedic. The vocative has nighāta. ⟦गहि⟧: after gam, by ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. २. ४. ७३⟧) there is elision of śap.

## SBh 1.6.9.3

Because hi is treated as having ṅ as an indicatory letter (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. ८७⟧) the m is dropped by ⟦अनुदात्तोपदेश°⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ३७⟧) and the rest. The elision of hi by ⟦अतो हेः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १०५⟧) does not occur; for, when that is to be done, because by ⟦असिद्धवदत्रा भात्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. २२⟧) the dropping of m is treated as not having happened, the base does not end in a. ⟦दिवः⟧: by ⟦ऊडिदम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १७१⟧) and the rest the case-ending is udātta. ⟦वा⟧: it is anudātta by ⟦चादयोऽनुदात्ताः⟧ (⟦फि. सू. ८४⟧). ⟦रोचनात्⟧: ⟦रुच दीप्तौ⟧, the root ruc in the sense of shining. By ⟦अनुदात्तेतश्च हलादेः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. १४९⟧) the suffix yuc comes.

## SBh 1.6.9.4

By ⟦युवोरनाकौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. १. १⟧) the substitute is ana. Because it has c as an indicatory letter it is udātta on its last syllable. ⟦अधि⟧: by ⟦अधिपरी अनर्थकौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. १. ४, ९३⟧) it has the designation karmapravacanīya; since the designation nipāta is combined with it, it is udātta on its first syllable by ⟦निपाता आद्युदात्ताः⟧ (⟦फि. सू. ८०⟧). ⟦अस्मिन्⟧: because it is a secondary mention of just what was first indicated by ⟦परिज्मन्⟧, by ⟦इदमोऽन्वादेशेऽशनुदात्तस्तृतीयादौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. २. ४. ३२⟧) the substitute aś is anudātta. Because it has ś as an indicatory letter it replaces the whole. The case-ending is anudātta by ⟦अनुदात्तौ सुप्पितौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ४⟧), so the whole word is anudātta. ⟦ऋञ्जते⟧: ⟦ऋजि भृजी भर्जने⟧, the roots ṛj and bhṛj in the sense of frying. Because of the connection with the prefix ⟦सम्⟧ it has the sense of accomplishing. There is nighāta. ⟦गिरः⟧: the accent is that of the stem.

## SBh 1.6.10.1

⟦इन्द्रं⟧: to the god Indra, ⟦सातिं⟧: the giving of wealth, ⟦अधि⟧ ⟦ईमहे⟧: we ask in excess. From which world? It is said. ⟦इतः⟧: from this visible, ⟦पार्थिवात्⟧, world of the earth; or ⟦दिवो⟧ ⟦वा⟧, from the world of heaven; or ⟦महः⟧, from the great, vast, ⟦रजसः⟧ ⟦वा⟧, atmospheric world which colours the birds and the rest. The meaning is: may this Indra, bringing it from wherever it may be, give us wealth. Among the seventeen verbs of asking it is read ⟦ईमहे यामि⟧ (⟦नि. ३. १९. १⟧).

## SBh 1.6.10.2

⟦इतः⟧: after the word ⟦इदं⟧ the suffix tasil comes in the sense of the ablative. By ⟦इदम इश्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. ३. ३⟧) the substitute is iś. Because it has ś as an indicatory letter it replaces the whole. Here the scope of ⟦ऊडिदम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १७१⟧) is ⟦आभ्याम्⟧ and ⟦एभिः⟧. The scope of ⟦लिति⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १९३⟧) is ⟦पचनं⟧ and ⟦पाचकः⟧. Both are nitya. There, because it is the later, by ⟦विप्रतिषेधे परं कार्यम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. १. ४. २⟧), the vowel i is udātta by ⟦लिति⟧.

## SBh 1.6.10.3

Afterwards, because tasi has the designation vibhakti by ⟦प्राग्दिशो विभक्तिः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. ३. १⟧), the udātta-ness stated by ⟦ऊडिदम्⟧ and the rest for a case-ending which is not a sarvanāmasthāna takes place. "By ⟦सकृद्गतौ विप्रतिषेधे यद्बाधितं तद्बाधितमेव⟧ (⟦परिभा. ४०⟧), in a conflict that has once taken its course, what has been set aside stays set aside; so ⟦ऊडिदम्⟧ does not apply again." If you say this, no; because it is accepted that ⟦लक्ष्यानुरोधेन पुनः प्रसङ्गविज्ञानं च⟧ (⟦परिभा. ३९⟧), and re-applicability is recognised according to the requirement of the examples.

## SBh 1.6.10.4

"But in that case, even in ⟦यतस्तत⟧ and the like, the udātta-ness of tasil by ⟦सावेकाचः०⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १६८⟧), though set aside by the later accent of the suffix with l as an indicatory letter, would occur." If you say this, no; because for the two words ⟦यत्तच्⟧, when they end in an a-vowel before sau, there is prohibition by ⟦न गोश्वन्साववर्ण⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १८२⟧). And this re-applicability, ⟦पुनः प्रसङ्गविज्ञानं च⟧, is not universal, because it is resorted to only here and there, according to the requirement of the examples. ⟦सातिम्⟧: ⟦षणु दाने⟧, the root san in the sense of giving.

## SBh 1.6.10.5

By ⟦धात्वादेः षः सः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. ६४⟧) there is the change to s. The suffix ktin comes in the sense of the action. By ⟦जनसनखनां सञ्झलोः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ४२⟧) the n becomes ā. Because of the prohibition ⟦तितुत्रतथसिसुसरकसेषु च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. २. ९⟧) there is no iṭ. Where the accent of an n-marked suffix would be expected, ⟦उदात्तः⟧ continuing, by ⟦ऊतियूतिजूतिसातिहेतिकीर्तयश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. ९७⟧) it is udātta on its last syllable by nipātana.

## SBh 1.6.10.6

⟦ईमहे⟧: ⟦ईङ् गतौ⟧, the root īṅ in the sense of going. Of śyan too there is elision by ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. २. ४. ७३⟧). Because this root is treated as having ṅ as an indicatory letter, when the lasārvadhātuka is anudātta by ⟦तास्यनुदात्तेन्ङिददुपदेशात्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १८६⟧), only the accent of the root remains. And there is no nighāta by ⟦तिङ्ङतिङः⟧, because of the prohibition ⟦चवायोगे प्रथमा⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. १. ५९⟧).

## SBh 1.6.10.7

For in the two following clauses also, because of the connection with ⟦वा⟧, since otherwise the sentence would not be complete, a tiṅ-ending must necessarily be supplied; with reference to that, this is the first tiṅ-ending. ⟦दिवः⟧: by ⟦उडिदम्⟧ and the rest the case-ending is udātta. ⟦पार्थिवात्⟧: ⟦प्रथ प्रख्याने⟧, the root prath in the sense of renown. It spreads, ⟦प्रथते⟧, so it is ⟦पृथिवी⟧. By ⟦प्रथेः षिवन् संप्रसारणं च⟧ (⟦उ. सू. १. १४८⟧) the suffix ṣivan comes. By ⟦षिद्गौरादिभ्यश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ४. १. ४१⟧) the suffix ṅīp comes.

## SBh 1.6.10.8

It is udātta by the accent of the suffix. By the nighāta of the remainder, the word ⟦पृथिवी⟧ is anudātta at its beginning. In the sense "a modification of the earth", ⟦ओरञ्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ४. ३. १३९⟧) continuing, by ⟦अनुदात्तादेश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ४. ३. १४०⟧) the suffix añ comes. By ⟦यस्येति च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १४८⟧) the ī is dropped. By ⟦तद्धितेष्वचामादेः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. २. ११७⟧) there is vṛddhi of the first vowel.

## SBh 1.6.10.9

The r follows (⟦पा. सू. १. १. ५१⟧). By ⟦ञ्नित्यादिर्नित्यम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १९७⟧) it is udātta on its first syllable. ⟦अधि⟧: because it is an indeclinable it is udātta on its first syllable. ⟦इन्द्रम्⟧: ending in the suffix ran, it is udātta on its first syllable. ⟦महः⟧: of ⟦महतः⟧ the dropping of a and t is Vedic. By ⟦सावेकाचः°⟧ the case-ending is udātta. ⟦रजसः⟧: by ⟦नब्विषयस्यानिसन्तस्य⟧ it is udātta on its first syllable.
