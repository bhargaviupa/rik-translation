## SBh 1.7.0.1

The sūkta of ten Ṛks that begins with ⟦इन्द्रम्⟧ is the fourth among those that begin with ⟦सुरूपकृत्नुम्⟧ and so on. The ṛṣi, metre, deity and application are as before. The special application is now stated. In the Mahāvrata, in the niṣkevalya śastra, the sūkta is ⟦इन्द्रमिद्गाथिनः⟧. And so it is laid down in the fifth Āraṇyaka: ⟦शिरो गायत्रमिन्द्रमिद्गाथिनः⟧ (⟦ऐ. आ. ५. २. १⟧). Likewise, on the twenty-fourth day, in the śastra of the Brāhmaṇācchaṃsin, ⟦इन्द्रमिद्गाथिनः⟧ is the tṛca of the ṣaḷahastotriya (six-day hymn group), because it is laid down in the sūtra which, beginning with ⟦चतुर्विंशे होताजनिष्ट⟧, continues ⟦आ याहि सुषुमा हि त इन्द्रमिद्गाथिनो बृहत्⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ७. २⟧). In the Atirātra, in the first paryāya, in the śastra of the Acchāvāka, this same tṛca is the anurūpa. And it is laid down: ⟦इन्द्राय मद्वने सुतमिन्द्रमिद्गाथिनो बृहत्⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ६. ४⟧).

## SBh 1.7.1.1

⟦गाथिनः⟧ means the Udgātṛs, who are endowed with sāmans that are sung. ⟦इन्द्रमित्⟧ means Indra alone. ⟦बृहत्⟧ means: with the sāman named Bṛhat, which arises in the Ṛk ⟦त्वामिद्धि हवामहे⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. ६. ४६. १⟧). ⟦अनूषत⟧ means: they praised. ⟦अर्किणः⟧ means the Hotṛs, who are provided with mantras that are the means of worship. ⟦अर्केभिः⟧ means: with mantras in the form of Ṛks they praised Indra alone. Those other priests, the Adhvaryus, who remain, praised Indra alone with ⟦वाणीः⟧, that is, with speeches in the form of yajus. Yāska has said that the word arka refers to a mantra: ⟦अर्को मन्त्रो भवति यदेनेनार्चन्ति⟧ (⟦निरु. ५. ४⟧). Among the fifty-seven names of speech that begin with ⟦श्लोकः⟧, ⟦वाशी वाणी⟧ (⟦नि. १. ११. १२⟧) is read.

## SBh 1.7.1.2

⟦गाथिनः⟧: from the root gā (to sing), by the rule ⟦उषिकुषिगार्तिभ्यस्थन्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. २. १६१⟧), the suffix than. Because the suffix is marked with n, the initial syllable is acute. Those to whom songs belong are gāthinaḥ: by the rule ⟦व्रीह्यादिभ्यश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. २. ११६⟧) the suffix ini. By the accent of the suffix, the vowel i is acute, and that remains when what follows is present. ⟦बृहत्⟧ stands for ⟦बृहता⟧ (with the great one): for the instrumental singular ending there is deletion, by the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुक्⟧.

## SBh 1.7.1.3

⟦पृषद्बृहन्महज्जगच्छतृवच्च⟧ (⟦उ. सू. २. २४१⟧): by this rule the word bṛhat is laid down irregularly with the final syllable acute. ⟦अर्केभिः⟧: the root ⟦अर्च⟧, in the sense of worship. Those by which one worships (arcyate) are arkas, that is, mantras. By the rule ⟦पुंसि संज्ञायां घः प्रायेण⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. ११८⟧) the suffix gha. By the rule ⟦चजोः कु घिण्ण्यतोः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. ३. ५२⟧) the substitution of a velar. By the accent of the suffix, the final syllable is acute.

## SBh 1.7.1.4

By the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. १. १०⟧) the substitution of ais for bhis does not take place. The mantras called arkas, which are the means of praise, belong to these; therefore they are arkiṇaḥ. ⟦वाणीः⟧: by the rule ⟦वृषादीनां च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. २०३⟧) the initial syllable is acute. The prohibition of lengthening to the preceding similar vowel, which the rule ⟦दीर्घाज्जसि च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १०५⟧) lays down, is made optional by the rule ⟦वा छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १०६⟧); therefore there is lengthening.

## SBh 1.7.1.5

The nominative is used in the sense of the instrumental. ⟦अनूषत⟧: the root ⟦णु⟧, in the sense of praise. By the rule ⟦णो नः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. ६५⟧) the n is substituted. In the aorist the ātmanepada is used by irregularity. The substitution of ad for jha is by ⟦पा. सू. ७. १. ५⟧. The absence of iṭ for sic and the lengthening of the vowel u are Vedic. Because the root belongs to the kuṭādi class, sic is treated as ṅit (⟦पा. सू. १.२.१⟧), and therefore there is no guṇa (⟦पा. सू. १. १. ५⟧).

## SBh 1.7.2.1

⟦इन्द्र⟧ ⟦इत्⟧: Indra alone. ⟦हर्योः⟧: of the two horses named Hari. ⟦सचा⟧: together, simultaneously. ⟦आ⟧ ⟦संमिश्लः⟧: one who unites them well on every side. Of what sort are the two horses? ⟦वचोयुजा⟧: the meaning is that they are yoked to the chariot at Indra's mere word, being well trained. This Indra is ⟦वज्री⟧, equipped with the vajra, and ⟦हिरण्ययः⟧, golden; the meaning is that he is adorned with all ornaments.

## SBh 1.7.2.2

⟦हर्योः⟧: the two that carry are hari. The suffix is in; because it is marked with n, the initial syllable is acute. ⟦सचा सह⟧ (⟦निरु. ५. ५⟧) has been stated. ⟦संमिश्लः⟧: mixing is miśra; this comes from the root miśrayati by the suffix ghañ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. १८⟧). One whose mixing is complete (samyak) is saṃmiśra. The l is Vedic. The meaning is: one who mixes well. In a bahuvrīhi compound the accent of the original form of the first member holds. ⟦वचोयुजा⟧ is taken up next.

## SBh 1.7.2.3

Those two that are yoked by speech are vacoyujau. For the genitive dual of this word, by the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुक्⟧ the ending is replaced by ā. The word yuj has its final syllable acute by the accent of the root. By the accent of the second member of a compound ending in a kṛt suffix, that same accent remains. ⟦वज्री⟧: he has a vajra. By the rule ⟦अत इनिठनौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. २. ११५⟧) the suffix ini; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦हिरण्ययः⟧: by the rule ⟦ऋत्व्यवास्त्व्यवास्त्वमाध्वीहिरण्ययानि च्छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १७५⟧) the dropping of the m of the word hiraṇmaya is laid down by nipātana. The vowel a is acute by the accent of the suffix. Together with the preceding unaccented vowel, by the rule ⟦एकादेश उदात्तेनोदात्तः⟧ the single substitute is acute.

## SBh 1.7.3.1

This Indra caused the sun ⟦आ⟧ ⟦रोहयत्⟧, to rise, ⟦दिवि⟧ in the sky (the heavenly world), ⟦दीर्घाय⟧ for long, that is, for continuous, ⟦चक्षसे⟧ seeing. The meaning is: the darkness that Vṛtra the Asura had formerly cast upon the world, Indra removed, and so, to bring about the sight of living beings, he placed the sun in the sky. And that sun ⟦गोभिः⟧ with his own rays ⟦वि⟧ ⟦ऐरयत्⟧ specially drove on, for the sake of seeing, ⟦अद्रिं⟧ the whole world, headed by the mountains; the meaning is that he lit it up. Or else Indra himself ⟦व्यैरयत्⟧ specially drove on ⟦अद्रिं⟧ the cloud, ⟦गोभिः⟧ with waters as the cause. Among the fifteen names of rays, ⟦खेदयः किरणाः गावः⟧ (⟦नि. १. ५. ३⟧) is read. Among the thirty names of cloud, ⟦अद्रिः ग्रावा⟧ (⟦नि. १. १०.१⟧) is read.

## SBh 1.7.3.2

⟦दीर्घाय⟧: by the accent of the nominal stem, the final syllable is acute. ⟦चक्षसे⟧: from the root cakṣ, by the rule ⟦सर्वधातुभ्योऽसुन्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ६२८⟧) the suffix asun. Because of the word bahula (variously), the substitution of khyā does not take place. Because it is marked with n, the initial syllable is acute. ⟦सूर्यम्⟧: he impels (suvati), therefore he is sūrya. The root is ⟦षू⟧, in the sense of impelling. By the rule ⟦धात्वादेः षः सः⟧, s replaces ṣ. By the rule beginning ⟦राजसूयसूर्य⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ११४⟧) the suffix kyap and its augment ruṭ are laid down by nipātana. Because kyap is marked with k, there is no guṇa. Because it is marked with p, it is unaccented.

## SBh 1.7.3.3

The accent of the root alone remains. ⟦रोहयत्⟧: from the causative of the root ruh, in the imperfect; by the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दस्यमाङयोगेऽपि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ७५⟧) the augment aṭ does not occur, and there is nighāta. ⟦दिवि⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦ऊडिदम्⟧ the case ending is acute. ⟦अद्रिम्⟧: by the rule ⟦अदिशदिभूशुभिभ्यः क्रिन्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ५०५⟧) the suffix krin. Because the animals eat grass and the like here, it is adri. Because it is marked with n, the initial syllable is acute. ⟦ऐरयत्⟧: the root ⟦ईर गतौ⟧ (īr, in the sense of going); laṅ from the causative; nighāta.

## SBh 1.7.4.1

O ⟦इन्द्र⟧, you who are ⟦उग्रः⟧, unassailable by enemies, protect us ⟦अव⟧ in battles (⟦वाजेषु⟧), with ⟦उग्राभिः⟧ unassailable ⟦ऊतिभिः⟧ protections directed to us. Likewise protect us ⟦सहस्रप्रधनेषु⟧ ⟦च⟧, that is, even in great battles that bring the gain of thousands of elephants, horses and the like.

## SBh 1.7.4.2

⟦वाजेषु⟧: by the rule ⟦वृषादीनां च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. २०३⟧) the initial syllable is acute. ⟦नोऽव⟧: in the changes of the s of nas into ru, o and guṇa, the natural state laid down by ⟦प्रकृत्यान्तःपादम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. ११५⟧) does not occur, because of the prohibition ⟦अव्यपरे⟧. ⟦अव⟧: the root ⟦अव रक्षणे⟧ (ava, in the sense of protecting). By the rule ⟦तिङ्ङतिङः⟧, nighāta. Although the verb ava that is heard, being the first verb, in relation to the verb understood with ⟦सहस्रप्रधनेषु च⟧, would incur the prohibition of nighāta by the rule ⟦चवायोगे प्रथमा⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. १. ५९⟧), nevertheless, because the ca after ⟦वाजेषु⟧ has been dropped, and because the prohibition is made optional by the rule ⟦चादिलोपे विभाषा⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. १. ६३⟧), nighāta takes effect here.

## SBh 1.7.4.3

The word sahasra has its middle syllable acute by the rule ⟦कर्दमादीनां च⟧ (⟦फि. सू. ५९⟧). ⟦सहस्रप्रधनेषु⟧ is an epithet of ⟦वाजेषु⟧ (battles). In a bahuvrīhi compound the accent of the original form of the first member holds. ⟦उग्रः⟧: the root ⟦उच⟧, in the sense of coming together; c is replaced by g. By the rule ⟦ऋज्रेन्द्रा⟧ (⟦उ. सू. २. १८६⟧) the suffix ran. By irregularity the final syllable is acute. ⟦ऊतिभिः⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦ऊतियूति⟧ the suffix ktin, which is acute.

## SBh 1.7.5.1

⟦वयम्⟧: we, the performers, ⟦हवामहे⟧ call ⟦इन्द्रं⟧ ⟦महाधने⟧, for the sake of abundant wealth. ⟦अर्भे⟧: when even a small, meagre wealth is the occasion, we call Indra. Of what sort is the Indra? ⟦युजं⟧: a helper, or one who is composed. ⟦वृत्रेषु⟧: when enemies that obstruct the gaining of wealth have arrived, ⟦वज्रिणं⟧ he is equipped with the vajra to ward them off.

## SBh 1.7.5.2

Although the word mahādhana is read among the names of battle, it is not taken here in that sense: if it were a bahuvrīhi, "that in which there is great wealth, namely in battle", the final acute would not be established. ⟦महाधने⟧: it is great and it is wealth; by the rule ⟦समासस्य⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. २२३⟧) the final syllable is acute. ⟦अर्भे⟧: by the rule ⟦अर्तिगॄभ्यां भन्⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ३. ४३२⟧). Because it is marked with n, the initial syllable is acute. ⟦हवामहे⟧: the root ⟦ह्वेञ्⟧, in the sense of challenging and of calling. Because it is marked with ñ, the ātmanepada is used when the fruit goes to the agent (⟦पा. सू. १. ३. ७२⟧).

## SBh 1.7.5.3

In place of laṭ, mahiṅ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. ७८⟧). By the rule ⟦टित आत्मनेपदानाम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. ७९⟧) e replaces the ṭi. In the active, śap (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ६८⟧). The rule ⟦ह्वः संप्रसारणम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. ३२⟧) being continued, saṃprasāraṇa takes place by the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. ३४⟧): u in place of v.

## SBh 1.7.5.4

Parapūrvatva. Guṇa and the substitution av. By the rule ⟦अतो दीर्घो यञि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. ३. १०१⟧) there is lengthening. By the rule ⟦तिङ्ङतिङः⟧, nighāta. ⟦युजम्⟧: kvip after the root ⟦युज समाधौ⟧ (yuj, in the sense of composing). By the rule ⟦युजेरसमासे⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. १. ७१⟧) the augment num does not occur; for that rule, because of the indication ⟦युजेः⟧, does not apply to a root without the vowel i.

## SBh 1.7.5.5

Or else, by the maxim ⟦अनित्यमागमशासनम्⟧ (⟦परिभा. ९३. २⟧), num does not occur even for the root ⟦युजिर् योगे⟧ (yujir, in the sense of joining). ⟦वृत्रेषु⟧: the root ⟦वृतु वर्तने⟧ (vṛt, in the sense of existing). Those that exist in opposition are vṛtras, that is, hostile groups. By the rule beginning ⟦स्फायितञ्चि⟧ (⟦उ. सू. २. १७०⟧) the suffix rak. Because it is marked with k, there is no guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦वज्रिणम्⟧: by the rule ⟦अत इनिठनौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. २. ११५⟧) the suffix ini. The accent is that of the suffix.

## SBh 1.7.6.1

O ⟦सत्रादावन्⟧, giver together of all our desired fruits, therefore, O ⟦वृषन्⟧, Indra, giver of rain, for the ripening of rice and the like, ⟦अपा⟧ ⟦वृधि⟧: open ⟦नः⟧ for our sake ⟦अमुं⟧ ⟦चरुं⟧, this visible cloud. Likewise ⟦अस्मभ्यम्⟧, for our sake, he is ⟦अप्रतिष्कुतः⟧, free of a counter-word: of whatever is asked by us, in every case he does not utter the counter-word "no". Therefore, in our regard, he is never obstructed. With just this in mind, Yāska says: ⟦अप्रतिष्कुतोऽप्रतिष्कृतोऽप्रतिस्खलितो वा⟧ (⟦निरु. ६. १६⟧). ⟦वृषन्⟧: nighāta of a vocative. ⟦अमुम्⟧: by the accent of the nominal stem, the final syllable is acute. ⟦चरुम्⟧: that which moves (carati) is caru. By the rule ⟦भृमृशीतॄचरित्सरितनिधनिमिमस्जिभ्य उः⟧ (⟦उ. सू. १. ७⟧) the suffix u.

## SBh 1.7.6.2

By the accent of the suffix, the final syllable is acute. ⟦सत्रादावन्⟧: the word satrā is in the sense of "together". One who gives together the whole group of desired fruits is satrādāvā. By the rule ⟦आतो मनिन्क्वनिब्बनिपश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. ७४⟧) the suffix vanip. By the rule ⟦आमन्त्रितस्य च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १९८⟧) the initial syllable is acute. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda, there is no nighāta. ⟦अप⟧: by the rule ⟦निपातस्य च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ३. १३६⟧) there is lengthening. A particle has its initial syllable acute. ⟦वृधि⟧: the root ⟦वृञ् वरणे⟧ (vṛ, in the sense of choosing). The ending sip of loṭ; for it, by the rule ⟦सेर्ह्यपिच्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. ८७⟧) hi.

## SBh 1.7.6.3

By the rule ⟦स्वादिभ्यः श्नुः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ७३⟧) the suffix śnu. For it, by the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. २. ४. ७३⟧) there is luk (deletion). By the rule ⟦श्रुशृणुपॄकृबृभ्यश्छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १०२⟧) the substitution of dhi for hi. Because it is ṅit, there is no guṇa of the preceding. Nighāta. ⟦अस्मभ्यम्⟧: from the word asmad, by the rule ⟦भ्यसो भ्यम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. १. ३०⟧) the substitution of bhyam. By the rule ⟦शेषे लोपः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. २.९०⟧) the deletion of the d. By the rule ⟦बहुवचने झल्येत्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. ३. १०३⟧) the change to e does not occur.

## SBh 1.7.6.4

It is stated: ⟦अङ्गवृत्ते पुनर्वृत्तावविधिर्निष्ठितस्य⟧ (⟦पा. म. ७.१. ३०⟧). By the accent of the nominal stem the vowel a in sma is acute. In the alternative of the substitution abhyam by the rule ⟦भ्यसोऽभ्यम्⟧, by the rule ⟦शेषे लोपः⟧ the remainder of the word ad up to the m is deleted. Then, by the accent that follows the loss of the acute, the initial a of abhyam is acute (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १६१⟧). ⟦अप्रतिष्कुतः⟧: not sounded back by anyone. The root ⟦कुङ्⟧, in the sense of sounding. ## SBh 1.7.7.6

Only after a kāraka. And ⟦प्रकृतिः प्रहृतिः⟧ (⟦का. ६. २. १५१⟧) is given as the counter-example. It may be objected: the Ṛk that is the instrument is denoted by the suffix ktin in the word ⟦स्तुति⟧, "that by which one praises"; and by the word su only the instrument is qualified, not the meaning of the root. So here in ⟦सुष्टुति⟧ the word su will refer only to a kāraka. But in ⟦प्रकृतिः⟧ and the like the word pra is only a qualifier of the meaning of the root; hence the counter-example is justified. No. If so, because su would have no connection with an action, the name upasarga laid down by ⟦उपसर्गाः क्रियायोगे⟧ (⟦पा. सू. १. ४. ५९⟧) would not apply to it. And then ṣatva by the rule beginning ⟦उपसर्गात्सुनोति सुवति⟧ would not occur. It may be objected: the instrument is denoted by ktin.

## SBh 1.7.7.7

And an instrument is the means of an action. So su, which qualifies the instrument, has connection with the action contained in the instrument, and so will have the status of upasarga. No. If that were so, by the rule ⟦यत्क्रियायुक्ताः तं प्रति उपसर्गसंज्ञका⟧ (⟦पा. सू. १. ४. ६०. ३⟧) su would be an upasarga only in relation to the meaning of the root kṛ, not in relation to the meaning of the root stu; therefore ṣatva would not occur. It may be objected: su will qualify that instrument by way of the meaning of the root stu. For if a praise is beautiful, its instrument too is beautiful. So because it is connected with the meaning of the root stu, it will have the status of upasarga in relation to that, and ṣatva too will occur. And through that, since it qualifies the instrument, the word su will also denote a kāraka; and so, without conflict with the Vṛtti, the word suṣṭuti will be within the scope of the rule ⟦मन्क्तिन⟧ and so on. In ⟦प्रकृतिः⟧ and ⟦प्रहृति⟧, ktin in the abstract sense has been given as the counter-example. No.

## SBh 1.7.7.8

There, the word pra refers to the instrument. Even in the example of ktin in the instrument, only qualification of the meaning of the root is intended, not also, through that, qualification of the meaning of the suffix; so the counter-example is justified. But in ⟦सुष्टुति⟧ the working of su extends as far as the instrument denoted by ktin; so it is an example, not a counter-example. No. Does the word su here qualify, by direct statement, both the meaning of the stem and the meaning of the suffix; or does it qualify one by direct statement and the other by implication? Even if it refers to both, is it simultaneously or in sequence? In the first case there is the consequence that the qualifying word must be repeated for each thing qualified. In the second case there is the fault of a function that resumes after a pause. And a function resumed after a pause has not been seen or accepted by anyone in words, cognitions or actions. Therefore it does not refer to both by direct statement. Now if the intention is by direct statement in one place and by implication in the other: there, if the connection with the meaning of the root is by implication, ṣatva fails; if the connection with the meaning of the suffix is by implication, the accent of ⟦मन्क्तिन्⟧ and so on fails.

## SBh 1.7.7.9

If, even where the connection with the kāraka is by implication, it is called an example, then ⟦प्रकृतिः⟧ and ⟦प्रहृति⟧ and the like would not be counter-examples. For the word pra, even though by direct statement it is connected only with the meaning of the root, its connection by implication with the instrument, who can prevent? This is the direction. Therefore here, let the accent be established by accepting that su refers to connection with the meaning of the suffix only, and let ṣatva be Vedic. Or let the word be a bahuvrīhi: that in which there is a beautiful praise. And thus ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम्⟧ will give the final acute. Or else: those that praise well are suṣṭutayaḥ; so the Ṛks that are instruments are called by the word stuti. When, by the rule ⟦क्तिच्क्तौ च संज्ञायाम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. १७४⟧), the suffix ktic is used, the final acute will result because it is marked with c. And it must not be asked how Ṛks that are instruments can be denoted by ktic, a suffix of the agent. For, like "the logs cook", their being an instrument is justified by intending the predominance of their own function.
