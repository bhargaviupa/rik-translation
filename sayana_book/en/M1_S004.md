## SBh 1.4.0.1

In the first maṇḍala, in the second anuvāka, there are four sūktas. Among them the sūkta beginning ⟦सुरूप⟧, of ten Ṛks, is the first, because it is listed as ⟦सुरूपकृत्नुं दश⟧. As in the preceding sūkta, the Gāyatra metre and Madhucchandas continue, so these two are its ṛṣi and metre. Because of the indication in the fourth Ṛk, ⟦इन्द्रं पृच्छा⟧, the deity is Indra. In the abhiplava ṣaḍaha, at the morning pressing of the brāhmaṇācchaṃsin, when the stoma is increased, the six sūktas beginning ⟦सुरूपकृत्नुमूतये⟧ and so on are for insertion. And it is laid down in the section called ⟦अभिप्लवपृष्ठयाहानि⟧: ⟦ब्राह्मणाच्छंसिनः सुरूपकृत्नुमूतय इति षट् सूक्तानि⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ७. ५⟧). The first three sūktas are to be recited in the mahāvrata, in the niṣkevalya, in the auṣṇiha tṛca and in the āśīti.

## SBh 1.4.0.2

And Śaunaka has said: ⟦सुरूपकृत्नुमूतय इति त्रीण्येन्द्र सानसिं रयिमिति सूक्ते⟧ (⟦ऐ. आ. ५, २. ५⟧). On the twenty-fourth day, at the midday pressing, the stotriya tṛca of the brāhmaṇācchaṃsin, ⟦सुरूपकृत्नुमूतये⟧, is optional, because it is laid down in the section ⟦होत्रकाणाम्⟧: ⟦मदे मदे हि नो ददिः सुरूपकृत्नुमूतये⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ७. ४⟧). In the agniṣṭoma, in the vaiśvadeva śastra, ⟦सुरूपकृत्नुमूतये⟧ is the dhāyyā, because it is laid down: ⟦सुरूपकृत्नुमूतये तक्षन्रथम्⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ५. १८⟧).

## SBh 1.4.1.1

⟦सुरूपकृत्नुं⟧: Indra, the doer of a deed endowed with beautiful form. ⟦ऊतये⟧: for the sake of protecting us. ⟦द्यविद्यवि⟧: every day. ⟦जुहूमसि⟧: we call. The illustration of the calling: ⟦गोदुहे⟧, for the sake of one who milks a cow, ⟦सुदुघामिव⟧, like a cow that gives milk well. As in the world, for the one who is the milker of the cow, a cow that is to be milked is called towards him; in the same way. Among the twelve names of day beginning with ⟦वस्तोः⟧, ⟦द्यविद्यवि⟧ is read (⟦नि. १. ९. १२⟧).

## SBh 1.4.1.2

⟦सुरूपकृत्नुम्⟧: one who does is ⟦कृत्नुः⟧; by the rule ⟦कृहनिभ्यां क्नुः⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ३. ३१०⟧). Because the suffix is marked with k, there is no guṇa. The insertion of t is Vedic. The accent is the udātta at the end of the compound. ⟦ऊतये⟧: from the root av; the word ⟦उदात्तः⟧ continuing from the preceding rule, by ⟦ऊतियूतिजूतिसातिहेतिकीर्तयश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. ९७⟧) the suffix ktin is irregularly formed as udātta. ⟦सुदुघाम्⟧: one who milks well is ⟦सुदुघा⟧; by ⟦दुहः कब्धश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. ७०⟧) there is the suffix ka and dh for the h. Because it is marked with k, there is no guṇa.

## SBh 1.4.1.3

Because ka has p, it is anudātta; by the root's accent the vowel u is udātta. When the compound is made with su as a gati, by the accent of the base of the final member of a kṛt compound that same accent stands. In the compound with iva, by ⟦इवेन विभक्त्यलोपः पूर्वपदप्रकृतिस्वरत्वं च⟧ (non-elision of the case ending, and the accent of the base of the first member), that same accent stands. One who milks a cow is ⟦गोधुक्⟧; the suffix kvip by the rule beginning ⟦सत्सूद्विष°⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. ६१⟧). The accent is that of the base of the final member of a kṛt compound. ⟦जुहूमसि⟧: from the root hve, in the first person plural of laṭ, by ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. २. ४, ७६⟧) the substitution of ślu for śap.

## SBh 1.4.1.4

By ⟦अभ्यस्तस्य च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. ३३⟧), for the root hve, which is the cause of the reduplicated form, saṃprasāraṇa takes place even before the doubling. By ⟦संप्रसारणाञ्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १०८⟧) the following vowel takes the form of the preceding one. By ⟦हलः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ५. २⟧) there is lengthening. Then by ⟦श्लौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १०⟧) there is doubling. The reduplicative syllable is shortened (⟦पा. सू. ७. ४. ५९⟧), and there is the change to a palatal and to a voiced unaspirated sound (⟦चुत्वजश्त्वे⟧; ⟦पा. सू. ७. ४. ६२; ८. ४. ५३⟧).

## SBh 1.4.1.5

By ⟦इदन्तो मसिः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. १. ४६⟧) there is the augment i. By the accent of the suffix, the m is udātta. ⟦द्यविद्यवि⟧: the word dyo is final-udātta by the accent of the nominal base. By ⟦नित्यवीप्सयोः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. १. ४⟧) there is doubling. By ⟦तस्य परमाम्रेडितम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. १. २⟧) and ⟦अनुदात्तं च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. १. ३⟧) the second one is anudātta.

## SBh 1.4.2.1

⟦सोमपाः⟧: O Indra, drinker of Soma, one who drinks Soma. ⟦नः⟧: our; ⟦सवना⟧: the three pressings; ⟦उप⟧: near, towards; ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गहि⟧: come. And having come, ⟦सोमस्य⟧: the Soma; ⟦पिब⟧: drink. ⟦रेवतः⟧: of you, who are wealthy; ⟦मदः⟧: the joy; ⟦गोदा⟧ ⟦इत्⟧: is indeed the giver of cows. The meaning is that when you are delighted, cows are obtained by us.

## SBh 1.4.2.2

⟦उप⟧: because it is a particle, it is initial-udātta. ⟦सवना⟧: that in which Soma is pressed is ⟦सवनानि⟧; the suffix lyuṭ is used in the sense of the locus (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. ११७⟧). The case ending is replaced by ḍā, and there is elision of ṭi. By ⟦लिति⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १९३⟧) the vowel a that precedes the suffix is udātta. ⟦गहि⟧: from the root gam, by ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. २, ४, ७३⟧) there is luk of śap.

## SBh 1.4.2.3

Because hi is ṅit, there is elision of m by the rule beginning ⟦अनुदात्तोपदेश⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ३७⟧). When the luk of ⟦अतो हेः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १०५⟧) is to be made, the elision of m, which belongs to the section beginning with ābhāt, becomes as if ineffective by ⟦असिद्धवदत्रा भात्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. २२⟧). ⟦सोमपाः⟧: by ⟦आमन्त्रितस्य च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. १. १९⟧) there is nighāta. Even though that is treated as non-existent, with reference to what precedes there is nighāta of ⟦पिब⟧ by ⟦तिङ्ङतिङः⟧.

## SBh 1.4.2.4

Nor is the preceding word, too, treated as non-existent by the principle of being like a part of what follows, because that is absent through non-connection. One who gives cows is ⟦गोदाः⟧. By ⟦क्विप् च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. ७६⟧) kvip would come; but setting aside kvip, even though it comes later and is of the same form, because the rule is one laid down for the particular word, by ⟦अतो मनिन्व्ननिध्वनिपश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. २. ७४⟧) the suffix vic is used. For in kvip, by the rule beginning ⟦घुमास्था°⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ६६⟧), the ā of the root would become ī. ⟦रेवान्⟧.

## SBh 1.4.2.5

⟦रयिर्धनमस्यास्तीति⟧: rayi is wealth; one who has it is formed with the suffix matup. By ⟦ह्रस्वनुड्भ्यां मतुप⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १७६⟧) matup is udātta. By ⟦छन्दसीरः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ८. २. १५⟧) there is the change to va. By ⟦रयेर्मतौ बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. ३७. ६⟧) there is saṃprasāraṇa and the following vowel taking the form of the preceding one, and guṇa. ⟦मदः⟧: by ⟦मदोऽनुपसर्गे⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. ६७⟧) the suffix ap. Because it is p-marked, it is anudātta.

## SBh 1.4.3.1

⟦अथ⟧: after the drinking of Soma. O Indra, ⟦ते⟧: your; ⟦अन्तमानाम्⟧: of those nearest, those who stand exceedingly close; ⟦सुमतींना⟧: of men possessed of good minds, of good wisdom; standing among them, ⟦विद्याम⟧: may we know you. Or else: ⟦सुमतीनां⟧ means of good thoughts concerned with the performance of rites, and "for the sake of obtaining" is to be supplied; the meaning is "may we remember you for the gaining of understanding". You also, ⟦नः⟧ ⟦अति⟧ ⟦मा⟧ ⟦ख्यः⟧: passing us by, do not proclaim your own nature to others. But ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गहि⟧: come to us alone.

## SBh 1.4.3.2

⟦अथ⟧: this is a particle and is initial-udātta. By ⟦निपातस्य च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ३. १३६⟧) there is lengthening. ⟦अन्तमानाम्⟧: those who are very near; the suffix tamap in the sense of the superlative (⟦पा. सू. ५. ३. ५५⟧). By ⟦तमे तादेश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १४९. ९⟧) there is elision of the initial ti. ⟦अन्तिकः⟧ is "near": one who has an end, anta, is ⟦अन्तिकः⟧; by ⟦अत इनिठनौ⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. २. ११५⟧) the suffix ṭhan. Because it is n-marked, it is initial-udātta. For of the excellence of distance there is no end.

## SBh 1.4.3.3

But of the excellence of nearness, whatever is near to a thing is that thing's end; thus, because it has an end, what is near is called antika. ⟦विद्याम⟧: from the root vid in liṅ, by ⟦यासुट् परस्मैपदेषूदात्तो ङिच्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. १०३⟧) the augment yāsuṭ is udātta. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda, there is no nighāta by ⟦तिङ्ङतिङः⟧. ⟦सुमतीनाम्⟧: in the word ⟦मति⟧, though it ends in ktin, by ⟦मन्त्रे वृषइषपचमनविदभूवीरा उदात्तः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. ९६⟧) the vowel i is udātta. ⟦शोभना मतिर्येषां ते सुमतय⟧: those who have good thought are sumati-people (⟦सुमतय⟧): in the bahuvrīhi, setting aside the accent of the base of the first member, by ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. २. १७२⟧) the end of the latter member is udātta.

## SBh 1.4.3.4

Even in the karmadhāraya, "good thoughts are ⟦सुमतय⟧", setting aside the accent of the base of a first member that is an indeclinable, the accent is final-udātta alone, by the accent of the base of the final member of a kṛt compound. Therefore, as with matup, the ending nām that follows the word ⟦सुमति⟧, which is short and final-udātta, is udātta by ⟦नामन्यतरस्याम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १७७⟧). ⟦ख्यः⟧: from the root ⟦ख्या⟧, in the sense of telling, in luṅ, with sip, by ⟦अस्यतिवक्तिख्यातिभ्योऽङ्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ५२⟧) the substitute aṅ for cli. By ⟦अतो लोप इटि च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ६४⟧) there is elision of ā. By ⟦इतश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. १००⟧) there is elision of i, and the change to ru and visarga.

## SBh 1.4.3.5

By ⟦न माङ्योगे⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ७४⟧) there is no aṭ augment. ⟦गहि⟧: from the root gam, when luk of śap has occurred by ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ (⟦पा. सू. २. ४. ७३⟧), the elision of m, which is due to hi being ṅit under ⟦अनुदात्तोपदेश⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. ३७⟧), is as if ineffective by ⟦असिद्धवदत्रा भात्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. २२⟧); therefore the luk by ⟦अतो हेः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १०५⟧) does not take place.

## SBh 1.4.4.1

Here the hotṛ speaks to the yajamāna. O yajamāna, you, ⟦इन्द्रं⟧ ⟦परेहि⟧: go away to Indra, to Indra's neighbourhood. Having gone, ⟦पृच्छ⟧: ask me, the hotṛ, ⟦विपश्चितं⟧: the intelligent one: put the question thus: "Has this hotṛ praised well or not?" ⟦यः⟧: Indra, who, ⟦ते⟧: to your, the yajamāna's, ⟦सखिभ्यः⟧: friends, the priests, ⟦वरं⟧: the best, wealth, sons and the like, ⟦आ⟧: completely gives; "gives" is the remainder to be supplied. Such an Indra: this is to be connected with what precedes. Of what kind again? ⟦विग्रं⟧: intelligent; ⟦अस्तृतम्⟧: unharmed. Among the twenty-four names of the intelligent beginning with ⟦विप्रः⟧, the words ⟦विग्र⟧ and vipaścit are read (⟦नि. ३. १५. २, १६⟧).

## SBh 1.4.4.2

⟦विग्रम्⟧: it is initial-udātta because it belongs to the class beginning with ⟦वृष⟧. ⟦अस्तृतम्⟧: the accent is that of the base of the first member, an indeclinable. In the word ⟦इन्द्र⟧, by the rule beginning ⟦ऋज्रेन्द्राग्र°⟧ (⟦उ. सू. २. १८६⟧) the suffix ran. Because it is n-marked, it is initial-udātta. ⟦सखिभ्यः⟧: by ⟦समाने ख्यश्चोदात्तः⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. ५७६⟧), when ⟦समाने⟧ is the upapada, the suffix iṇ after the root khyā. Since the word ⟦डित्⟧ continues, because it is ḍit there is also elision of ṭi (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १४३⟧). In conjunction with that, there is elision of y. The upapada is udātta. By ⟦समानस्य छन्दस्यमूर्धप्रभृत्युदर्केषु⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ३. ८४⟧) there is the substitution of sa. Hence the word ⟦सखि⟧ is initial-udātta. ⟦वरः⟧ is that which is ⟦व्रियते⟧, from the root vṛ; by the rule beginning ⟦ग्रहवृदृनिश्चिगमश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ३. ५८⟧) the suffix ap. Because it is p-marked, only the accent of the root remains.

## SBh 1.4.5.1

⟦नः⟧: relating to us, that is, our priests; this is the remainder to be supplied. Let them, ⟦ब्रुवन्तु⟧: praise Indra. ⟦उत⟧: and also; O ⟦निदः⟧, revilers, men, ⟦निः⟧ ⟦आरत⟧: go out from this region. ⟦अन्यतश्चित्⟧: go out from another region also. Of what kind are the priests? ⟦इन्द्रे⟧: in Indra, ⟦दुवः⟧: service, ⟦दधानाः⟧: performing. The word ⟦इ⟧ is in the sense of emphasis. The meaning is: let them remain always performing service alone.

## SBh 1.4.5.2

Those who revile are ⟦निदः⟧; the root ⟦णिदि⟧, in the sense of blame; in kvip the absence of num is Vedic. Because the ending is anudātta, the accent is that of the root. Even though it is a vocative, because it belongs to another sentence and is not subsequent to a word that belongs to its own sentence, there is no nighāta; so it is initial-udātta alone. ⟦अन्यतः⟧: by ⟦लिति⟧ the vowel before the suffix is udātta. ⟦चित्⟧ is in the sense of ⟦अपि⟧. Thereby it is understood: not only from here, but having gone out from here, go out from elsewhere also. This connection of the senses of the two roots is indicated by luṅ in ⟦आरत⟧, for it is laid down in the section on the connection of roots. ⟦आरत⟧: from the root ṛ, by ⟦छन्दसि लुङ्लङलिटः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. ६⟧) luṅ in the sense of loṭ.

## SBh 1.4.5.3

The substitute ta for the second person plural. By ⟦सर्तिशास्त्यर्तिभ्यश्च⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. १. ५६⟧) the substitute aṅ for cli. By ⟦ऋदृशोऽङि गुणः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ७. ४. १६⟧) there is guṇa. The augment ā(ṭ). ⟦दधानाः⟧: setting aside the final-udātta that would arise because śānac is c-marked, by being later, by ⟦अभ्यस्तानामादिः⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. १. १८९⟧) it is initial-udātta. ⟦दुवः⟧: by ⟦नब्विषयस्यानिसन्तस्य⟧ (⟦फि. सू. २६⟧) it is initial-udātta.

## SBh 1.4.6.1

O ⟦दस्म⟧, Indra, destroyer of enemies: by your favour, ⟦अरिः⟧ ⟦उत⟧: even enemies, ⟦नः⟧: us, ⟦सुभगान्⟧: endowed with good wealth, ⟦वोचेयुः⟧, ⟦उच्यासुः⟧: may call. ⟦कृष्टयः⟧: men, who are our friends, say so; what need is there to say that? This is the remainder to be supplied. Therefore we, possessed of wealth, ⟦इन्द्रस्य⟧ ⟦शर्मणि⟧: in the happiness obtained by Indra's favour, ⟦स्यामेत्⟧: may indeed be. Among the twenty-eight names of wealth beginning with ⟦मघम्⟧, ⟦रयिः क्षत्रं भगः⟧ (⟦नि. २. १०. १०⟧) is read.
