## Introduction

The sūkta that begins ⟦इंद्र त्वेति⟧ has nine Ṛks. It is the second sūkta, it is Viśvāmitra's, its metre is Gāyatrī and its deity is Indra. So it is recorded in the Anukramaṇī: ⟦इंद्र त्वा गायत्रं हीति⟧. In the Agniṣṭoma, at the morning pressing, this sūkta belongs to the śastra of the Brāhmaṇācchaṃsin. It is also prescribed in the Sūtra: ⟦इंद्र त्वा वृषभमुद्देवभीति तिस्रः । आ. ५-१०⟧.

The first Ṛk is the prasthita-yājyā of the Brāhmaṇācchaṃsin at the morning pressing. It is prescribed thus: ⟦इंद्र त्वा वृषभं⟧ and ⟦मरुतो यस्य । आ. ५-५⟧.

## Ṛk 1

Indra is so called because he delights and sports in the Soma juice that flows in the drop. Or else: he is Indra because he sees this whole world directly. The vocative of that word is "O Indra". O Indra, you who are a bull, a showerer of desires, we call you at the Soma that has been pressed, that is, pressed with the pressing stones. We invite you to drink the Soma. Such as you are, drink the Soma that is intoxicating and has food as its nature; drink it.

Yāska explained the word Indra in many ways: "Indra: he tears Iḍā, or he gives Iḍā, or he holds Iḍā, or he splits Iḍā, or he supports Iḍā; or he runs to the drop; or he delights in the drop; or ⟦वेंद्दे भूतानीति⟧." **NOTE**{The words ⟦वेंद्दे भूतानीति⟧ look damaged and cannot be construed as supplied; I have left them untranslated.} "Then, because ⟦तद्यदेनं प्राणैः सम्यंधंस्तदिंद्रस्येंद्रत्वमिति⟧." **NOTE**{⟦सम्यंधंस्⟧ looks like a misreading and I cannot construe the clause; the sense is presumably that Indra's name Indra comes from this.} Wherever a meaning fits, that meaning is to be accepted. The followers of Upamanyu say that Indra is so called from seeing this: he sees this Brahman directly. So it is heard in the Āraṇyaka: ⟦स एतमेव पुरुषं ब्रह्मततमपश्यदिदमदर्शमिती ३⟧, "He saw this very Person, the widespread Brahman, and said, 'I have seen this.'" **NOTE**{Some words of this quotation are run together in the text and their letters are uncertain.} Then: ⟦तस्मादिंद्रो नामेदंड्रो ह वै नाम तमिदंद्रं संतमिंद्र इत्याचक्षते परोक्षेण परोक्षप्रिया इव हि देवाः⟧, "Therefore he has the name Idandra; though he is Idandra, they call him Indra, indirectly; for the gods love what is indirect." **NOTE**{The two forms of the name Idandra in this quotation differ from each other and look uncertain.} The reference is ⟦ऐ. आ. २-४-३⟧.

Another account: the word is Indra. The root is ⟦इदि⟧, in the sense of supreme lordship. Supreme lordship is the state of being the form of the world through his own māyā. With this in view it is heard: ⟦इंद्रो मायाभिः पुरुरूप ईयत इति⟧, "Indra moves with many forms through his māyās."

Grammar: when the word ⟦इंदु⟧ stands as the first member, the suffix ḍa is added to the root ram, as taught by ⟦रमतेरन्येभ्योऽपि दृश्यत इति डः⟧, since it is seen also after other roots; the ukāra of the earlier member is dropped. Or else, from the root ⟦इदि⟧ in the sense of supreme lordship, the word is irregularly formed as ending in the suffix ran, by the rule beginning ⟦इत्यस्मादृजेंद्राग्रेत्यादिना⟧. In this way these derivations are to be understood. The vocative has the accent on the first syllable, as a word of address.

⟦हवामहे⟧: it is the present tense, laṭ, of the root hve, with saṃprasāraṇa, by the rule ⟦हैयतेर्बहुलं छंदसीति संप्रसारणे लटि रूपं⟧, that in the Veda this is frequent. **NOTE**{The block gives the root in a form that is not the usual root hve, and the Pada-pāṭha has ⟦हनामहे⟧ where the Saṃhitā has ⟦हवामहे⟧.}

⟦मध्वः⟧: the word madhu ends in the suffix un, by the rule beginning ⟦मधुशब्दः फलिपाटीत्यादिनोन्प्रत्ययांतः⟧. Because the rule on augments is not constant, ⟦आगमानुशासनस्यानित्यत्वान्नु मभावः⟧: the augment num does not occur. Because a rule that comes with a technical name is not constant, ⟦संज्ञापूर्वकस्य विधेरनित्यत्वाद्दुणाभावः⟧: there is no guṇa. **NOTE**{The block reads ⟦दुणाभावः⟧; I took it to mean absence of guṇa.} The semivowel substitution, ⟦यणादेशः⟧, takes place. ⟦अंधसः⟧: from the root ad, ⟦अदेर्नुम्⟧, the augment num; and ⟦धश्चेत्यसुन्⟧, the suffix asun, with dh. ⟦नित्सर्वः⟧: because it is nit, the accent is on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 2

For the Brāhmaṇācchaṃsin at the morning pressing, the Ṛk beginning ⟦इंद्र क्रतुविदमिति शस्त्रयाज्या⟧ is the śastra-yājyā. It is prescribed: ⟦इंद्र क्रतुविदं सुतमिति याज्या । आ. ५-१०⟧.

O Indra, you who obtain intelligence, that is, who gain knowledge of the rite, desire to drink this Soma that has been pressed. O you who are praised by many, Indra, drink this Soma, which is satisfying (⟦तर्पकमास्यायनकारिणिममं⟧). **NOTE**{The words ⟦तर्पकमास्यायनकारिणिममं⟧ are run together and the word cannot be construed as supplied; only "satisfying" is rendered.} When it is drunk, pour this Soma over, that is, sprinkle it in the belly. The sense is: hold it so that it does not fall below the navel.

Grammar: ⟦हर्य⟧ is the imperative, ⟦लोटि रूपं⟧, of the root ⟦हर्य गतिकांत्योरित्यस्य⟧, which means to go and to desire; the accent is lowered. ⟦पुरुष्टुत⟧: by ⟦स्तुतस्तोमयोश्चंदसीति संहितायां षत्वं⟧ the letter ṣ occurs in the saṃhitā. **NOTE**{The block has ⟦चंदसी⟧ where ⟦छंदसि⟧ would be expected; the Pada-pāṭha has ⟦पुरुस्तुत⟧ and ⟦क्रत्युविदं⟧ where the Saṃhitā has ⟦पुरुष्टुत⟧ and ⟦क्रतुविदं⟧.} ⟦वृष सेचन इत्यस्य लोटि रूपं⟧: the imperative of the root vṛṣ, to sprinkle; ⟦आण उत्तरत्वान्निघातः⟧: because it follows ā, the accent is lowered. ⟦तातृपिं⟧: from the root trp, in the sense of pleasing, by ⟦प्रीणन इत्यस्माच्छंदसि सदादिभ्यो दर्शनादिति किन्⟧, the suffix kin in the Veda, since it is seen after roots like sad. **NOTE**{The bhāṣya has ⟦तातृपिं⟧ while the Saṃhitā has ⟦तातृसिं⟧ and the Pada-pāṭha ⟦ततृसिं⟧.} ⟦तस्य लिड्वद्भावाद्दिर्वचनादि⟧: because it behaves like liṭ, there is doubling and the rest. ⟦संहितायामन्येषामपीत्यभ्यासस्य दीर्घः⟧: in the saṃhitā the reduplicating syllable is lengthened. ⟦नित्त्वादाद्युदात्तः⟧: because it is nit, the accent is on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 3

O you who are praised (⟦स्तवान⟧) by the singers, lord of the Viśas, that is, of the Maruts, O Indra, together with all the gods who are to be worshipped, advance this sacrifice of ours, which is ⟦धितावानं⟧. Vāna is an oblation: it is shared, that is, it is received. Dhita is placed. So the sacrifice is one of which the oblation is placed, that is, in which the oblation is collected. The sense is: make it prosper greatly, make it complete by accepting the oblations. ⟦णः⟧: ⟦उपसर्गाद्बहुलमिति संहितायां णत्वं⟧, the change to ṇ is in the saṃhitā by the rule that it is frequent after a preverb.

⟦धितावानं⟧: from the root ⟦डुधाण् धारणपोषणयोरित्यस्मान्निष्ठा⟧, the root dhā in the sense of holding and nourishing, comes the past participle. ⟦बहुव्रीहौ पूर्वपदस्वरः⟧: in a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member. ⟦तिरः⟧: ⟦तरतेर्व्यत्ययेन शः⟧, from the root tṛ the suffix śa by interchange; ⟦प्रत्ययस्वरः⟧, the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦स्तवान⟧: the root is ⟦ष्टुञ् स्तुत्⟧; **NOTE**{The block reads ⟦स्तुत्⟧ for the sense of the root, probably praise.} ⟦सम्यानच् स्तुव इत्युपपदाभावेऽपि कर्मण्यानच् प्रत्ययः⟧: although by the rule on stu the suffix ānac requires an accompanying word, here even without one the suffix ānac is added in the sense of the object. ⟦आमंत्रितत्वान्निघातः⟧: because it is a vocative, the accent is lowered.

## Ṛk 4

O lord of the good, Indra, these Soma juices, delighting and bright, pressed and given by us, go forth to the ⟦क्षयं⟧. A kṣaya is that in which food and the like dwell, that is, the belly. They go forth with force. The sense is: hold them.

Grammar: ⟦क्षयं⟧: from the root ⟦क्षि निवासगत्योरित्यस्मादधिकरणे घप्रत्ययः⟧, to dwell and to go, the suffix gha in the locative sense. ⟦प्रत्ययस्वरेणांतोदात्ते प्राप्ते क्षयो निवास इत्याद्युदात्तत्वं⟧: the accent on the last syllable, by the suffix, was to come, but by the rule that kṣaya in the sense of dwelling is accented on the first syllable, it has that accent. ⟦चंद्रासः⟧: the root is ⟦चदि आह्लादने दीप्ता च⟧, in the sense of delighting, and shining. **NOTE**{The block has ⟦दीप्ता⟧ where a locative would be expected.} From it, by ⟦अस्मात्सा यितंचीत्यादिना रक्षत्ययः⟧, the suffix is added. **NOTE**{The words ⟦सा यितंचीत्यादिना रक्षत्ययः⟧ look damaged; I take the suffix to be ra but cannot construe the rule as supplied.} By ⟦आज्ञसेरसुगित्यसुक्⟧ the augment asuk occurs; ⟦प्रत्ययस्वरः⟧, the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦इंदवः⟧: ⟦उंदेरिच्चा देरित्युप्रत्ययः⟧, from the root und, with i in place of the first vowel, the suffix u. ⟦निदित्यनुवृत्तेराद्युदात्तः⟧: because the word nit continues from before, the accent is on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 5

O Indra, hold in your belly the Soma that has been pressed and is to be chosen by all. These bright Soma juices are yours, ⟦द्युक्षासः⟧: they dwell in heaven. Therefore hold them. The sense is that.

Grammar: ⟦दधिष्क⟧ is the imperative, ⟦दधातेर्लोटि रूपं⟧, of the root dhā. **NOTE**{The block has ⟦दधिष्क⟧ where the Ṛk has ⟦दधिष्वा⟧ and the Pada-pāṭha ⟦दधिष्व⟧.} The augments are anudātta, so the augment iṭ is anudātta, and ⟦इतीटोऽनुदात्तत्वात्पत्ययस्वरः⟧ the accent is that of the suffix. **NOTE**{⟦पत्ययस्वरः⟧ is probably a misreading of the word for the suffix-accent.} ⟦वरेण्यं⟧: the root is ⟦वृज् संभक्तौ⟧, to share; the suffix is ⟦इत्येण्प्रत्ययः⟧, eṇ; ⟦वृषादित्वादाद्युदात्तः⟧, because it belongs to the vṛṣa group, it is accented on the first syllable. ⟦द्युक्षासः⟧: ⟦क्षियतेरन्येभ्योऽपि दृश्यत इति डप्रत्ययः⟧, from the root kṣi the suffix ḍa, since it is seen also after other roots; ⟦कृदुत्तरपदस्वरः⟧, the accent is that of the final member, a kṛt word. **NOTE**{The Pada-pāṭha reads ⟦तप । म्यक्षासक⟧, which is damaged, where the Saṃhitā has a different text.}

## Ṛk 6

Girvaṇas is one to be served with songs. So Yāska says: ⟦गिर्वणा देवो भवति गीर्भिरेनं⟧ ⟦वनयंति⟧, "Girvaṇas: the god is so called, for they serve him with songs." The reference is ⟦नि. ६-१४⟧. O Indra, such as you are, drink for us this Soma that has been pressed. For you are sprinkled with the streams of the honey, that is, of the Soma that intoxicates. O Indra, ⟦त्वादातमित्त्वया शोधितं विशदीकृतमेव यशोऽन्नमस्मासु भवति⟧: the glory, which is food, purified and made clear by you, is among us. **NOTE**{The Pada-pāṭha has the word and ⟦इत्⟧ separately; the block runs them together with ⟦त्वया⟧, and also has ⟦नोऽस्मदियं⟧.}

Grammar: ⟦गिर्वणः⟧: ⟦संभक्तावित्यस्मादसुन्⟧, from the root van in the sense of sharing, the suffix asun; ⟦गिर उपधाया दीर्घाभावश्चांदसः⟧, the non-lengthening of the penultimate of gir is Vedic. ⟦षाष्टिकमाद्युदात्तत्वं⟧: the accent on the first syllable is the one taught in the sixth book, concerning a word of address. **NOTE**{The text has ⟦अमंत्रितस्यॆ⟧ with a damaged final letter.} ⟦पाहि⟧: the imperative of the root ⟦पा पान इत्यस्माल्लोटि रूपं⟧. Because ⟦आमंत्रितस्याविद्यमानत्वेन⟧ the word of address is treated as non-existent, and the verb is at the beginning of a pāda, ⟦पादादित्वादनिघातः⟧ the accent is not lowered; ⟦प्रत्ययस्वरः⟧ the accent is that of the suffix.

⟦त्वादातं⟧: the root is ⟦दैप् शोधने⟧, to purify. ⟦सत्यपि पकारे नानुबंधकृतमनेजंतत्वमित्येजंत एवायं⟧: even though there is the letter p, the property of not ending in e, ai, o, au is not made by an anubandha, so this root ends in ej. ⟦तत अदेच इत्यात्वं⟧: then the ā-substitution. **NOTE**{The words ⟦तत अदेच⟧ are as supplied; a rule on ending in ej is presumably meant.} ⟦अस्मात्कर्मणि क्तः⟧: from it the suffix kta in the passive sense. ⟦दाधा घ्नदाबित्यत्रादाबिति प्रतिषेधेन घुसंज्ञाया अभावाद्दो दद्योरिति ददादेतो न भवति⟧: because of the prohibition in that rule (⟦दाधा घ्नदाबित्यत्रादाबिति⟧), the technical name ghu does not apply, and so the substitution of dad by the rule on do does not occur. **NOTE**{The rule-name and the prohibition are written with b in the block, where p is written in the root's name above.} ⟦तैति युष्मच्छब्दस्य तृतीया⟧: the instrumental of the word yuṣmad; ⟦कर्तकरणे कृता बहुलमिति समासः⟧: the compound is by the rule on the agent or instrument with a kṛt word, which is frequent. **NOTE**{The text has ⟦तैति⟧, which I take as the instrumental form but cannot construe.} ⟦तृतीया कर्मणीति पूर्वपदप्रकृतिस्वरः⟧: the accent is that of the nature of the first member. ⟦यशः⟧: the root is ⟦अशू व्याप्तौ⟧, to pervade; ⟦अशेयुट्टेत्यसुन्⟧, the suffix asun; ⟦तत्संनियोगेन धातोर्युडागमः⟧, in connection with it the root takes the augment yuṭ. ⟦नित्त्वादाद्युदात्तः⟧: because it is nit, the accent is on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 7

⟦वनिनः⟧: ⟦वनं देवताविषयं संभजनमस्यास्तीति वनी⟧, vana is a worship that has the deities as its object, and one who has it is vanin. Of that sacrificer who worships the gods, the brilliant and imperishable, complete and free from decay, oblations, Soma and the others, join Indra from all sides: ⟦अभितः सचंते⟧, they come together (⟦समवयंति⟧). Among them, ⟦सोमं पीत्वा वावृधे⟧, having drunk the Soma he grows strong: that Indra increases. **NOTE**{The Pada-pāṭha has ⟦ववृधे⟧ where the Saṃhitā has ⟦वावृधे⟧, and the block's ⟦मध्यॆ⟧ has a damaged letter.}

Grammar: ⟦वनिनः⟧: ⟦अत इनिठनानितीनिप्रत्ययः⟧, the suffix ini, and its accent follows. **NOTE**{The rule-name is written irregularly in the block.} ⟦सचंते⟧: ⟦षच समवाय इत्यस्य लट रूपं⟧, the present tense of the root sac, in the sense of union. **NOTE**{The block has the nominative-looking ending for the locative laṭ.} ⟦अक्षिता⟧: ⟦क्षि क्षय इत्यस्य निष्ठा⟧, the past participle of the root kṣi, to decay. ⟦अत्र ण्यदर्थ इति दीर्घाभावः⟧: here, because the sense is that of the suffix ṇyat, there is no lengthening. ⟦तस्मादेव क्षियो दीर्घादिति निष्ठानत्ताभावः⟧: and for the same reason, by the rule on kṣi after a long vowel, ta does not become na. ⟦समासे तस्य स्वरः⟧: in the compound with the negative, the accent is that of that compound. ⟦पीत्वी⟧: ⟦पा पान इत्यस्मात् क्वाप्रत्ययस्य स्नात्वादयश्चेति⟧, from the root pā, to drink, the suffix ktvā, and ⟦निपातनात् त्वी इत्यादेशः⟧, by irregular formation the substitution ⟦त्वी⟧. ⟦प्रत्ययस्वरः⟧: the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦घुमास्थागापेत्यादिना ईत्वं⟧: the ī-substitution is by the rule beginning thus.

## Ṛk 8

O Indra, slayer of Vṛtra, from the near place, the nearer side, or from the very distant place, aiming at us, come to us. Having come, accept these songs of ours, which are our praises, that is, our speech; serve them.

Grammar: ⟦परावतः⟧: ⟦उपसर्गाच्छंदसि धात्वर्थ इति वतिः⟧, the suffix vati, by the rule on a preverb in the Veda in the sense of the root. ⟦प्रत्ययस्वरः⟧: the accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 9

O Indra, ⟦यद्यस्मिन्नरावतमतिविप्रकृष्टस्य देशस्य⟧: wherever you are called, in the region between the very distant place and the near place, in their middle region, where you are rightly sacrificed to, from that place come to this sacrifice, for the drinking of the Soma. **NOTE**{The block reads ⟦यद्यस्मिन्नरावतम⟧ and ⟦अर्नावतं⟧ where the Ṛk and Pada-pāṭha have ⟦परावतं⟧ and ⟦अर्वावतं⟧, and ⟦सोमपानार्थवागच्छ⟧ is run together.}

Grammar: ⟦परावतं अर्नावतं । अंतरांतरेण युक्ते । पा. २-३-४⟧, ⟦इति द्वितीया⟧, the second case is used. ⟦ततः⟧: ⟦पंचम्यास्तसिल्⟧, the suffix tasil in the sense of the fifth case. ⟦लित्त्वरः⟧: the accent follows from the suffix being lit. **NOTE**{The text has ⟦लित्त्वरः⟧, which probably means the accent of lit.}
