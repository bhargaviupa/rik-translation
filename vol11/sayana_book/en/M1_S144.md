## SBh 1.144.0.1

The fifth sūkta, beginning ⟦एति प्र होता⟧, has seven Ṛks, is that of Dīrghatamas and is in the Jagatī metre. Because ⟦आग्नेयं तु तत्⟧, "but that is addressed to Agni", was said at the earlier place, this one too is addressed to Agni. And so the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦एति प्र सप्त जागतम्⟧, "eti pra: seven Ṛks, in Jagatī." Its use in the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra has been stated.

## SBh 1.144.1.1

This ⟦होता⟧, the adhvaryu, who accomplishes the offering, in order to do ⟦व्रतं⟧, the rite, in the form of the giving of the oblation and the like, ⟦अस्य⟧, of this Agni**NOTE**{SBh 1.144.1.1: the text reads ⟦अगनेः⟧; translated as agneḥ, "of Agni"; a typo?}, being furnished ⟦मायया⟧, with the knowledge of the particular things to be done, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦एति⟧, goes forth excellently, to offer. ⟦माया⟧ is a name of knowledge, because ⟦माया वयुनम्⟧ (⟦नि. ३. ९. ९⟧) is read among the names of it. Of what kind is he? ⟦दधानः⟧, holding, ⟦धियं⟧, an understanding, that is ⟦ऊर्ध्वाम्⟧, risen on high, and ⟦शुचिपेशसं⟧, endowed with a beautiful form. ⟦पेश⟧ is a name of form. This is because it is said: ⟦आ चतुर्थात्कर्मणोऽभिसमीक्षेतेदं करिष्यति⟧, "He should look ahead as far as the fourth act: this he will do." And having come, ⟦क्रमते⟧, he takes, ⟦स्रुचः⟧, the ladles, the juhū and the others, which are ⟦दक्षिणावृतः⟧, turning towards the right. Of what kind are the ladles**NOTE**{SBh 1.144.1.1: the text reads ⟦सुचः⟧; translated as srucaḥ, "the ladles"; a typo?}? ⟦याः⟧, those which, ⟦प्रथमं⟧, at the time when the vessels are set down, ⟦निंसते⟧, ⟦धाम⟧, the place, ⟦अस्य⟧, of this Agni. The root is ⟦निंस⟧, in the sense of kissing.

## SBh 1.144.1.2

They kiss, they resort to: that is the meaning. First he goes forth in his mind to the order of what is to be performed, afterwards he takes the ladles: that is the meaning. The word ⟦ह⟧ expresses what is well known. Or: ⟦होता⟧, the caller of the gods, this Agni, being furnished with a particular knowledge, ⟦एति⟧, comes, to ⟦व्रतं⟧, the rite, the Jyotiṣṭoma and the like, ⟦अस्य⟧, of this sacrificer. He comes towards the place of the sacrifice. Of what kind is he? ⟦दधानः⟧, bearing, ⟦धियं⟧, an act, in the form of the moving of the flames and the like, which goes upward and is ⟦शुचिपेशसं⟧, endowed with a beautiful form. And having come, ⟦अभि⟧ ⟦क्रमते⟧, he steps upon, ⟦स्रुचो⟧, the ladles, the juhū, the upabhṛt and the others, full of ghee, which are ⟦दक्षिणावृतः⟧, of the character stated. The meaning is: the flames creep forward to accept the ghee. ⟦याः⟧, the ladles which, ⟦प्रथमं⟧, earlier, at the time when the oblations are set down, having reached it, ⟦निंसते⟧, kiss, ⟦धाम⟧, the place, marked as the vedi, of this Agni. The word ⟦ह⟧ expresses what is well known. The root is ⟦णिसि चुम्बने⟧, ṇis in the sense of kissing. Because it belongs to the second class, śap is elided. Because it has an indicatory i, there is the augment num.

## SBh 1.144.2.1

⟦दोहनाः⟧, the streams, ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, of water, ⟦योनौ⟧, in the place of their origin, ⟦देवस्य⟧ ⟦सदने⟧, in the place of the shining Sun, being ⟦परीवृताः⟧, pervaded on all sides by his rays, ⟦अभि⟧ ⟦अनूषत⟧, become new. They simply come into being, or they are praised. When? To this he says: ⟦यत्⟧, when, ⟦अपामुपस्थे⟧, in the lap of the waters, within them, ⟦विभृतः⟧, being borne by them in a special way, this Agni ⟦आवसत्⟧, takes possession and dwells: at that time, that is the meaning. ⟦अध⟧, now, ⟦अधयत्⟧, the whole world drinks, ⟦स्वधाः⟧, the waters, which are like nectar. They are specified: ⟦याभिः⟧, those together with which this Agni ⟦ईयते⟧, or with which he meets the earth, those it drinks. In the form of lightning he makes the world drink the waters: this is the praise of Agni.

## SBh 1.144.3.1

⟦सवयसा⟧, the two of the same age. By the word ⟦वयः⟧ capacity is indicated: the two of equal capacity. ⟦तदित्⟧, at that very time, at the very time when he has grown, ⟦वितरित्रता⟧, crossing intensely, ⟦मिथः⟧, each one, ⟦समानमर्थं⟧, the common purpose, the accomplishment, the hotṛ and the adhvaryu ⟦युयूषतः⟧, wish to mix, each by his own activity, ⟦वपुः⟧, the body of Agni. The recitation of the yājyā and the like after the praiṣa is the activity of the hotṛ, and the offering after the vaṣaṭ call is that of the adhvaryu: this is the division. Here the following is to be considered**NOTE**{SBh 1.144.3.1: the text reads ⟦अत्रेदमनुसंधयम्⟧; translated as anusaṃdheyam, "is to be considered"; a typo?}. ⟦सवयसा⟧, the two of the same age, ⟦मिथः⟧, each one, ⟦वितरित्रता⟧, wishing in a special way to cross to the common purpose, the gaining of pleasure. After the root tṝ ending in the elided intensive suffix yaṅ, before the suffix śatṛ, the form is laid down ready-made in the rule beginning with dādharti (⟦पा. सू. ७. ४. ६५⟧). The long ā stands for the dual ending.

## SBh 1.144.3.2

A wife and a husband ⟦युयूषतः⟧, wish to mix, ⟦तद्वपुस्⟧, that very body of each other: they embrace each other. In the same way the hotṛ and the adhvaryu together wish to mix Agni. ⟦आदीम्⟧, immediately afterwards, ⟦हव्यः⟧, the Āhavanīya Agni, being ⟦सारथिः⟧, one whose nature is to move, or, like a charioteer, the conductor of the chariot that consists of the sacrifice, ⟦समयस्त⟧**NOTE**{SBh 1.144.3.2: the bhāṣya first cites the verb as ⟦समयस्त⟧, without the anusvāra which the Ṛk and the end of this unit have; a typo?}, accepts well, ⟦आ⟧, from every side, ⟦रश्मीन्⟧, the streams of ghee, which are long like reins, ⟦अस्मत्⟧, that belong to us. On this two comparisons are stated. ⟦भगो⟧ ⟦न⟧: the Sun, who is to be served by all; as he receives worship from every side, so Agni. ⟦सारथिः⟧, the driver of a chariot, as he takes ⟦रश्मीन्⟧, the reins, of the carrier, the bearer, a horse or the like: as he takes these, so this Agni too ⟦समयंस्त⟧, accepts, the oblations.

## SBh 1.144.4.1

⟦यमीम्⟧, this Agni whom ⟦द्वा⟧, two, ⟦सवयसा⟧, of the same age, or of equal capacity, who are present ⟦समाने⟧, in the same, ⟦योना⟧, place of origin, the place where the fruit arises, the sacrifice, ⟦मिथुना⟧, who like a married couple are busy with one task, ⟦समोकसा⟧, who have the same dwelling, whose place is the ground of the sacrifice to the gods: such a hotṛ and adhvaryu ⟦सपर्यतः⟧, worship, gladden with praise and with the giving of the oblation. When? He says: ⟦दिवा⟧ ⟦न⟧, as by day, ⟦नक्तं⟧, so by night too; the meaning is: always. Agni, being worshipped in this way, though ⟦पलितः⟧, aged, earlier, in the state of embers, after the kindling ⟦अजनि⟧, is born, ⟦युवा⟧, young, or one who mixes everything with his brilliance. Being thus born, he, with regard to ⟦मानुषा⟧ ⟦युगा⟧, the pairs that belong to Manu, in the form of wife and husband or in the form of hotṛ and adhvaryu, ⟦पुरु⟧ ⟦चरन्⟧, eating much ghee and the like, remains ⟦अजरः⟧, undecayed, free from old age.

## SBh 1.144.5.1

⟦तम्⟧, this Agni, who is ⟦देवं⟧, shining, ⟦दश⟧ ⟦धीतयः⟧, the fingers, ten in number, ⟦व्रिशः⟧, that is viśaḥ, separated from one another, ⟦हिन्वन्ति⟧, gladden, at the time of the churning. Likewise ⟦मर्तासः⟧, we men, the sacrificers, ⟦हवामहे⟧, call him, ⟦ऊतये⟧, for protection. What is his distinction? That is stated. This Agni, like arrows ⟦प्रवत⟧, going forth excellently, ⟦धनोः⟧, from a bow: ⟦आ⟧, as men let these loose upon the enemy. ⟦आ⟧ is in the sense of comparison.

## SBh 1.144.5.2

In the same way ⟦सः⟧, that Agni, from his own body, which stands in the place of the bow, ⟦ऋण्वति⟧, makes go, the rays, ⟦प्रवतः⟧, which have excellent speed. The root rivi has the sense of going. Here it has the causative sense included in it. Because it has an indicatory i, there is the augment num. The vocalisation of the r is Vedic. The word ⟦अधि⟧ has no meaning of its own; it only repeats the meaning of the root.

## SBh 1.144.5.3

Moreover, Agni, grown in this way, ⟦अभिव्रजद्भिः⟧, by those who move about on every side for the sake of the performance, or who go towards the object that is to be known, for their sake, ⟦अधित⟧, holds, that is, makes known, ⟦नवा⟧, new, fresh, ⟦वयुना⟧, knowledges, which concern the performance. Or: he holds the new things distinguished by knowledge, the praises and the like, that are made by the hotṛ and the others, who go towards**NOTE**{SBh 1.144.5.3: in the second explanation the word is spelt ⟦अभिव्रजद्धिराभिमुख्येन⟧ with ddh; translated as abhivrajadbhiḥ, as in the Ṛk; a typo?} him face to face.

## SBh 1.144.5.4

After the root dhā, in the Vedic aorist, there is the short i by ⟦स्थाध्वोरिच्च⟧, the rule for i in sthā and the roots called ghu. The sic that follows is treated as having an indicatory k. By ⟦ह्रस्वादङ्गात्⟧, the rule for s after a stem ending in a short vowel, the s is elided.

## SBh 1.144.6.1

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦त्वं⟧, you, ⟦राजसि⟧, rule over, ⟦दिव्यस्य⟧, what is in heaven, the gods and the rest, or, in the form of lightning, over the rain and the like. This is a name of lordship. Likewise ⟦पार्थिवस्य⟧, of what is connected with the earth, men and the rest, you are the lord. Is it in dependence on another? No, he says: ⟦त्मना⟧, by yourself alone, by your own power alone. For this there is a comparison: ⟦पशुपाइव⟧. As a herdsman by his own power alone is able in the activities of penning the cattle, leading them out and the like, so you. By this comparison complete independence is stated. Moreover, since this is so, therefore ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦बर्हिः⟧, sacrifice, ⟦एते⟧, these two well-known ones, ⟦एनी⟧, of the colour called eta, bright, heaven and earth, ⟦आशाते⟧, reach, pervade.

## SBh 1.144.6.2

By ⟦वर्णादनुदात्तात्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ४. १. ३९⟧), the rule for a colour-word with an unaccented final, there is the suffix ṅīp, and n in place of the t.

## SBh 1.144.6.3

Of what kind are these two? ⟦बृहती⟧, great, very widely extended; ⟦अभिश्रिया⟧, which have attained lordship, or which are to be served on every side; ⟦हिरण्ययी⟧, beneficial and delightful; ⟦वक्वरी⟧, sounding with great skill.

## SBh 1.144.6.4

After the root vac, before the suffix vanip, there are the suffix ṅīp and the r by ⟦वनो र च⟧. The guttural is Vedic. Or it is from the root ⟦वकि कौटिल्ये⟧, vak in the sense of crookedness, with Vedic absence of the augment num. Such heaven and earth reach ⟦बर्हिः⟧.

## SBh 1.144.7.1

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦जुषस्व⟧, enjoy the oblation. Or be pleased with the praise. Moreover, ⟦प्रति हर्य⟧, desire again, ⟦तत्⟧, such, pleasing, ⟦वचः⟧, praise in the form of speech. O ⟦मन्द्र⟧, you whose nature is to gladden, or who are to be praised; O ⟦स्वधावः⟧, you who possess food marked as the oblation; O ⟦ऋतजात⟧, you who are born for the sake of the sacrifice; O ⟦सुक्रतो⟧, you of good deeds, or of good understanding. ⟦यः⟧, you who are such, ⟦प्रत्यङ्ङसि⟧, are approved, are favourable, ⟦विश्वतः⟧, to the whole world, the stationary and the moving; not one whose face is turned away: that is the meaning.

## SBh 1.144.7.2

Likewise ⟦दर्शतः⟧, worth seeing by all. Moreover, ⟦संदृष्टौ⟧, when fully seen, you are ⟦रण्वः⟧, one whose nature is to delight, or the delighter of all. Or: when there is the full sight of you, every man becomes ⟦क्षयः⟧, possessed of a dwelling. For this there is a comparison: ⟦पितुमानिव⟧. As the owner of very abundant food is one with whom all should dwell and to whom all should go, so you.
