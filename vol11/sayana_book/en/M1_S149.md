## SBh 1.149.0.1

The hymn beginning ⟦महः सः⟧, of five Ṛks, is the ninth hymn; it is of Dīrghatamas, is in the virāj metre and is addressed to Agni: in the virāj, because the characteristic stated in ⟦दशकास्त्रयो विराळेकादशका वा⟧, "three pādas of ten syllables are a virāj, or pādas of eleven" (⟦अनु. ६. ७-८⟧), is present. And so it is said in the Anukramaṇī: ⟦महः स वैराजम्⟧, "the hymn 'mahaḥ sa' is in the virāj". Its ritual use is to be gathered from the indications in it.

## SBh 1.149.1.1

The ⟦पतिः⟧, the protector, the master, of ⟦रायः⟧, wealth in the form of cows and the like, which is ⟦महः⟧, great, worthy of honour: ⟦सः⟧, that Agni, ⟦दन्⟧, giving, bestowing what is desired.

## SBh 1.149.1.2

Of the root dā, "to give", before śatṛ, there is the Vedic elision of śap. Since that suffix is ārdhadhātuka by the rule ⟦छन्दस्युभयथा⟧, "in the Veda both ways", there is the elision of the vowel ā by the rule ⟦आतो लोप इटि च⟧, "elision of ā, also before iṭ".

## SBh 1.149.1.3

⟦आ⟧, facing us, towards our place of sacrifice to the gods, ⟦ईषते⟧, he goes. Moreover he is the ⟦इनः⟧, the master, even of ⟦इनस्य⟧, a master; the meaning is that he is the lord of all. He, being such, ⟦आ⟧, resorts to, ⟦पदे⟧, the place of the altar, which is the abode, of ⟦वसुनः⟧, wealth. By force of the preverb a suitable verb is supplied.

## SBh 1.149.1.4

Or else the connection is: he is the master also of wealth, which is fit for dwelling. Or else: the obtaining of wealth is well known. Moreover him, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦ध्रजन्तम्⟧, who is approaching, for the sake of the pressing, the ⟦अद्रयः⟧, the sacrificers who possess the pressing-stones, ⟦विधन्नित्⟧, do indeed attend upon.

## SBh 1.149.1.5

The root is ⟦विध विधाने⟧, vidh in the sense of arranging; it is of the tud class. In laṅ the augment aṭ is absent by the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧, "variously in the Veda". The absence of the loss of accent is Vedic. Or else, since the word tad is heard in the earlier part, this is connected by supplying the word yad here, and therefore there is no loss of accent. Or else: the stones for pressing, with their own sounds, having come near, do indeed honour him; the meaning is that they produce delight.

## SBh 1.149.2.1

⟦सः⟧, he, being such, ⟦यः⟧, which Agni is the ⟦वृषा⟧, the sprinkler, the meaning being the producer, of ⟦रोदस्योः⟧, heaven and earth too, ⟦नरां⟧ ⟦न⟧, as of men: being thus the producer of all, and being endowed with ⟦श्रवोभिः⟧, with glories that are heard of everywhere, he ⟦अस्ति⟧, exists. It was said that he is the producer of all; he states the ground for that. Since he is ⟦जीवपीतसर्गः⟧: he whose creation, whose order of creating, is drunk, is tasted, by living beings of various kinds, is so called.

## SBh 1.149.2.2

By the rule ⟦तृतीया कर्मणि⟧, "a word in the instrumental, before a participle in the sense of the object", the first member keeps its original accent. In the bahuvrīhi again there is the same accent. Here the order of creating is this: "this Agni, making the oblation that has been given into him reach the sun, together with the sun's rays, in the form of lightning, having rained with water by way of the cloud, makes it reach all living beings"; hence he is the producer by way of producing the rain. Now he states that he is the producer directly. ⟦यः⟧, which Agni, ⟦सस्राणः⟧, having entered, ⟦योनौ⟧, into the womb, ⟦शिश्रीत⟧, carries through the seed that has been poured in; the meaning is that he transforms it into the shape of the body of a man, a beast and the like. If Agni did not cook it, it would only fill; it is not produced. **NOTE**{The sense of ⟦पूरयेदेव⟧, "it would only fill", is doubtful in "if Agni did not cook it, …, it is not produced"; is a word meaning "it would only putrefy" intended?} Therefore he is the producer quite directly. The meaning is that such an Agni exists as one possessed of glory. Or else: which Agni, ⟦नरां⟧ ⟦न⟧, as of men, of the sacrificers, who are endowed with zeal in the rites.

## SBh 1.149.2.3

⟦नरा मनुष्या नृत्यन्ति कर्मसु⟧, "narāḥ are men; they dance in their works" (⟦निरु. ५. १⟧): so says Yāska. As he is for them the showerer of heaven and the other things desired, so he is of heaven and earth too. By the word that denotes the support, those who rest on it are indicated. The purport is: of those who live in the world of the earth, by bestowing rain; of those who live in the world of heaven, by conveying the oblation. Such an Agni, by reason of foods, the Soma, the ghee and the like, which consist of the oblation, being one whose nature is tasted by particular living beings, the sacrificers, or by all without distinction, exists pre-eminent. The meaning is that, having gratified this Agni with oblations, they enjoy the enjoyments they desire. Moreover, which Agni, having entered into his own place, which is the altar, cooks the puroḍāśa and the like that has come into him: this is the Agni that exists as one of great majesty.

## SBh 1.149.3.1

⟦यः⟧, which Agni, ⟦नार्मिणीं⟧, possessed of sport, the uttaravedi that belongs to the sacrificers; or else that which stands in the mind of men, that ground towards which the sacrificers have, for the sake of the sacrifice, the thought of Agni's coming: that ⟦पुरं⟧, that place, he ⟦आ⟧ ⟦अदीदेत्⟧, illumines. Of what kind is he? ⟦अत्यः⟧: one whose habit is to go on to the place that is wanted; ⟦कविः⟧, one whose sight goes beyond. An illustration of this: ⟦अर्वा⟧, skilled in going, ⟦नभन्यो⟧ ⟦न⟧, like the wind, which arises in the sky and is possessed of the sky.

## SBh 1.149.3.2

Moreover he is ⟦शतात्मा⟧: "a hundred", "a thousand" are expressions for what is unlimited. His having manifold forms is with regard to the houses of this and that sacrificer, or with regard to the Āhavanīya, the Gārhapatya and the others. Or else, through the division into Mitra and Varuṇa, Agni's having the form of Mitra and the others is well known in revealed texts such as ⟦त्वमग्ने वरुणो जायसे यत्⟧, "You, O Agni, are Varuṇa when you are born" (⟦ऋ. सं. ५.३.१⟧), and ⟦इन्द्रं मित्रं वरुणमग्निमाहुः⟧, "They call Agni Indra, Mitra, Varuṇa" (⟦ऋ. सं. १.१६४.४६⟧). The meaning of the second mantra is that they declare Agni himself to have Indra and the others as his nature. He, being such, is ⟦सूरो⟧ ⟦न⟧, like the sun, ⟦रुरुक्वान्⟧, shining.

## SBh 1.149.3.3

The root is ⟦रुच दीप्तौ⟧, ruc in the sense of shining. There is kvasu in place of the Vedic liṭ. Therefore "he, such an Agni, exists in an excellent way": thus the construction is with the earlier Ṛk.

## SBh 1.149.4.1

This Agni is ⟦द्विजन्मा⟧: born from the two fire-sticks. Or else: from the churning there is the first birth; after his arising there is, as it were, a second birth in the form of the consecrations, the Pavamāna offerings and the like. Thus he has two births. Or else it is because he is produced from heaven and earth. Such an Agni, ⟦अभि⟧ ⟦शुशुचानः⟧, lighting up on all sides, ⟦त्री⟧ ⟦रोचनानि⟧, the three luminous ones, the places that are the earth and the rest, or the Gārhapatya and the rest: and not only the three, but ⟦विश्वा⟧ ⟦रजांसि⟧, all of them too, the worlds that are the earth and the rest, whose nature is to give colour, ⟦शुशुचानः⟧, lighting up on all sides, illumining, being the ⟦होता⟧, the invoker of the gods, ⟦यजिष्ठः⟧, the best sacrificer, he ⟦अस्थात्⟧, stands, ⟦सधस्थे⟧, in the common place, the place of the sacrifice, ⟦अपां⟧, of the waters, those of the sprinkling vessel and the like.

## SBh 1.149.5.1

⟦यो⟧ ⟦द्विजन्मा⟧, he who is twice-born, ⟦सः⟧, he alone, is the ⟦होता⟧, the one who accomplishes the offering, because it is only the fire produced from the two fire-sticks that, by way of the Gārhapatya, becomes the Āhavanīya; or the invoker of the gods. He, ⟦अयं⟧, this one, ⟦विश्वा⟧, all, ⟦वार्याणि⟧, things worthy to be chosen (the first syllable is udātta by the rule ⟦ईडवन्दवृशंसदुहां ण्यतः⟧, "of īḍ, vand, vṛ, śaṃs and duh with ṇyat"), ⟦श्रवस्या⟧, through śravasyā: śravas is the food that consists of the oblation; through the wish for that. After the word śravas ending in kyac there is the suffix a, in the sense of the action, by the rule "a pratyayāt", "a after a root that ends in a suffix".

## SBh 1.149.5.2

⟦दधे⟧: he holds. ⟦यः⟧ ⟦मर्तः⟧, the mortal who ⟦ददाश⟧, gives, ⟦अस्मै⟧, to this Agni of the nature described, he becomes ⟦सुतुकः⟧, one who has good sons.
