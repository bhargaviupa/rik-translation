## SBh 1.147.0.1

The hymn beginning ⟦कथा ते⟧, of five Ṛks, is the seventh hymn; it is of Dīrghatamas, is addressed to Agni, and is in the triṣṭubh metre. The Anukramaṇikā is ⟦कथा⟧. The ritual use of this hymn in the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra has been stated by this: ⟦त्रिमूर्धानमिति त्रीणि⟧, "the three hymns beginning 'trimūrdhānam'".

## SBh 1.147.1.1

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦ते⟧, the rays that belong to you, which are ⟦शुचयन्तः⟧, displaying, making manifest, their brightness everywhere, and ⟦आशुषाणाः⟧: ⟦आशु⟧, quickly, partaking; or partaking of the swift ones. The meaning is that they quickly partake even of the wind and the like. Or else: drying up on all sides. Or else: pervading everything. Of the root aś, "to pervade", there is kānac in place of liṭ; by an exchange there are the suffix u and sic, and thus the word has two conjugational signs. Of the root śuṣ, "to dry", preceded by āṅ, with the sense of the causative included in it, before śānac there is the Vedic elision of śap. From the root san, "to partake", with the word āśu as the accompanying word, there is aṇ in the sense of the object. Such rays, ⟦कथा⟧, by what means, ⟦ददाशुः⟧, do they give, ⟦आयोः⟧, life, length of life (the genitive is in the sense of the object), accompanied by ⟦वाजेभिः⟧, foods?

## SBh 1.147.1.2

The root is ⟦दा दाने⟧, dā in the sense of giving. It is a Vedic liṭ. The meaning is: favour me with that means. If it is asked where the granting of boons by the rays has been seen, it is told. ⟦यत्⟧, since, ⟦उभे⟧, both, the food and the life spoken of, ⟦तोके⟧, in the son, ⟦तनये⟧, in the one who extends the family without a break; or else ⟦तोके⟧, in the son, and ⟦तनये⟧, in his son and so on: ⟦दधानाः⟧, holding these, the ⟦देवाः⟧, the sacrificers who carry on their business, ⟦रणयन्त⟧, take delight, or cause delight, or simply make sound, ⟦सामन्⟧, in the sāman, the Rathantara and the like, which belongs to ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, the sacrifice. The meaning is: since, accompanied by food, life, sons and the like, having sacrificed with Soma, **NOTE**{The Wikisource text has ⟦सोमेनेष्वात⟧, taken as a typo for someneṣṭvā, "having sacrificed with Soma"; is that right?} they take delight in the sāman, therefore it is understood that the rays give. The meaning is: favour me too with such rays of yours.

## SBh 1.147.2.1

O ⟦यविष्ठ⟧, youngest one; O ⟦स्वधावः⟧, Agni who are possessed of it through the food that consists of the oblation; of ⟦मे⟧, my, ⟦वचसः⟧, speech which has the form of praise, which is ⟦मंहिष्ठस्य⟧, exceedingly worthy of honour, ⟦प्रभृतस्य⟧, brought about with excellence, ⟦अस्य⟧, which is being made now. The genitive is in the sense of the object.

## SBh 1.147.2.2

The speech so described ⟦बोध⟧, know; the meaning is: be praised. O Agni, in the world ⟦त्वः⟧ (here Yāska, having said ⟦त्वो नेम इत्यर्धस्य⟧, "tva and nema are names of a half" (⟦निरु. ३. २०⟧), cited this very passage as the example), one person, ⟦पीयति⟧: this has the sense of killing; he injures. The meaning is that he does not honour you with sacrifice and the like. ⟦त्वः⟧, one, ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦गृणाति⟧, utters what is favourable. Between those two, O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, I, ⟦वन्दारुः⟧, whose habit is to salute, ⟦वन्दे⟧, praise, ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦तन्वं⟧, body, your form.

## SBh 1.147.3.1

Here they tell a legend. There were two seers named Ucathya and Bṛhaspati. Of these, Ucathya had a wife named Mamatā. And she was pregnant. Bṛhaspati took hold of her and lay with her. When the moment of the emission of the semen had come, the seed that was in the womb spoke out: "O sage, do not discharge your seed; I am dwelling here from before; do not make a mingling of seed." Addressed thus, Bṛhaspati, his seed having been held back by force, cursed: "O embryo, since you have thus made an obstruction of the seed, therefore go to long darkness; be blind from birth." Being cursed thus, Dīrghatamas was born of Mamatā. And when he was born, through the pain of the darkness he praised Agni.

## SBh 1.147.3.2

And he, pleased by the praise, removed the blindness: so they tell. That is what is said here. O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦ते⟧, those belonging to you, ⟦ये⟧ ⟦पायवः⟧, which well-known protecting rays, ⟦पश्यन्तः⟧, seeing, ⟦मामतेयं⟧, the son of Mamatā, Dīrghatamas, ⟦अन्धं⟧, blind, that is, understanding "this one is blind, therefore he is to be protected by us", protected him ⟦दुरितात्⟧, from the sorrow that had come upon him in an evil way: **NOTE**{The Wikisource text has ⟦प्राप्ताद्दुःखत्⟧, plainly a typo for prāptād duḥkhāt, "from the sorrow that had come".} those ⟦सुकृतः⟧, makers of happiness, ⟦विश्ववेदाः⟧, he of all knowledge, ⟦ररक्ष⟧, protects. The purport is: for the protecting of us. The meaning is that by them he will protect us too. Us who are thus protected, the ⟦रिपवः⟧, the enemies, desire and the like, ⟦दिप्सन्तः⟧, wishing to harm, ⟦नाह⟧ ⟦देभुः⟧: the word aha has the sense of restriction; they are indeed not able to harm.

## SBh 1.147.3.3

The root is ⟦दम्भु दम्भे⟧, dambh in the sense of deceit. By the supplementary rule ⟦श्रन्थिग्रन्थिदम्भिस्वञ्जीनामिति वक्तव्यम्⟧, "it should be stated: of śranth, granth, dambh and svañj" (⟦का. १. २. ६. १⟧), liṭ has the indicatory k, and so by the rule ⟦अनिदिताम्⟧, "of those that have no indicatory i", there is the elision of n. Since that elision is treated as not having taken effect, the vowel e and the elision of the reduplication do not apply, and so those two are enjoined by ⟦दम्भेश्चेति वक्तव्यम्⟧, "it should be stated: and of dambh" (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १२०. ४⟧). Or else the seer of the hymn, Dīrghatamas, himself speaks of himself in the third person.

## SBh 1.147.4.1

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦नः⟧, us, who are protected by you: ⟦यः⟧, he who is ⟦अघायुः⟧, possessed of the wish for evil in the form of killing and the like, ⟦अररिवान्⟧, a non-giver, the meaning being one who obstructs gifts to us (of the root rā, "to give", there is kvasu in place of the Vedic liṭ), ⟦अरातीवा⟧, himself possessed of non-giving. By the saying ⟦छन्दसि वनिपमिच्छन्ति⟧, "in the Veda they want vanip", there is vanip in the sense of the possessive suffix.

## SBh 1.147.4.2

The enemy who, practising enmity, ⟦द्वयेन⟧, by a counsel that is twofold through the division into mental and verbal. "I do not give" is the mental counsel. Preventing a gift by imputing blame and the like is the verbal counsel. Or else: by a twofold counsel of contradictory form, which has deceit as its cause. What contrary thing he does, that he does not proclaim; and what pleasant thing he says, **NOTE**{The Wikisource text has ⟦बूते⟧, plainly a typo for brūte, "says"; is that right?} that he does not do; he does one thing and says another: such is the twofoldness of the form of the counsel. Before it was by the division into mental and verbal, now it is by the division into verbal and bodily: such is the distinction. The enemy who by such a counsel ⟦मर्चयति⟧, threatens, or makes subservient, us: ⟦सः⟧, that one part of the counsel, the one of mental form, ⟦पुनः⟧ ⟦अस्तु⟧ ⟦गुरुः⟧ ⟦अस्मै⟧. The dative is in the sense of the genitive.

## SBh 1.147.4.3

Let it in return be the swallower of the very one who employs it. Likewise by ⟦दुरुक्तैः⟧, by evil words, verbal ones that have the form of blame, ⟦तन्वं⟧, his own body, ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦मृक्षीष्ट⟧, let him wipe away, let him destroy step by step. Or else: let that twofold counsel be ⟦अस्मै⟧, of the very one who employs it, the swallower. There are other harsh speeches too, employed for our humiliation. By those ill-spoken words let that enemy destroy his own body alone; the meaning is: let it turn upon his own self alone and burn it.

## SBh 1.147.5.1

In the preceding mantra it was said: "Let that counselling reach the one who does it with a twofold crooked counsel, not us." Here, however, Agni is prayed to: "protect from that counselling." ⟦उत⟧ ⟦वा⟧, O ⟦सहस्य⟧: saha is a name of strength; O Agni who exist in that; ⟦यः⟧ ⟦मर्त्यः⟧, which mortal, a man whose nature is to die, ⟦प्रविद्वान्⟧, knowing deceitful counselling excellently, ⟦द्वयेन⟧, with a twofold counsel in the manner stated before, ⟦मर्चयति⟧, makes subservient, or threatens, ⟦मर्तं⟧, a man: ⟦अतः⟧, from such threatening, or from the one who does it, O ⟦स्तवमान⟧ ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni who are being praised, ⟦पाहि⟧, protect, me, ⟦स्तुवन्तं⟧, who am making praise. Moreover ⟦नः⟧, us, ⟦माकिः⟧ ⟦धायीः⟧, do not set as fit ⟦दुरिताय⟧, for the sorrow caused by him, brought on by the evil counselling. The meaning is: do not make us a vessel of misfortune.

## SBh 1.147.5.2

The root is ⟦धि धारणे⟧, dhi in the sense of holding. By an exchange there is the augment iṭ. Before sic there is vṛddhi. By the rule ⟦न माङ्योगे⟧, "not in connection with mā", the augment aṭ is absent.
