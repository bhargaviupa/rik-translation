## SBh 1.146.0.1

The hymn beginning ⟦त्रिमूर्धानम्⟧, of five Ṛks, is the sixth hymn; it is of Dīrghatamas and addressed to Agni, because it was said at an earlier place ⟦आग्नेयं तु तत्⟧, "but that is addressed to Agni". The Anukramaṇikā is ⟦त्रिमूर्धानम्⟧. In the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra, in the triṣṭubh metre, there is the ritual use of the three hymns that begin with this one. It is laid down in the sūtra, in the section ⟦अथैतस्याः⟧: ⟦त्रिमूर्धानमिति त्रीणि⟧, "the three hymns beginning 'trimūrdhānam'" (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ४. १३⟧).

## SBh 1.146.1.1

By this Ṛk Agni is praised in the form of the sacrifice. ⟦त्रिमूर्धानं⟧: endowed with three heads in the form of the three pressings. By the rule ⟦द्वित्रिभ्यां पाद्दन्मूर्धसु बहुव्रीहौ⟧, "after dvi and tri, in pād, dat and mūrdhan, in a bahuvrīhi", the final syllable of the latter member is udātta.

## SBh 1.146.1.2

Moreover ⟦सप्तरश्मिं⟧: joined with the seven regulating metres; ⟦अनूनम्⟧: not defective, of complete fruit; ⟦अग्निम्⟧: that which is to be accomplished by Agni, or that which is endowed with the qualities of moving and the like; ⟦पित्रोरुपस्थे⟧, in the lap of heaven and earth, ⟦निषत्तं⟧, seated. The meaning is that it is in the middle; that is to say, because it is to be accomplished by the substance that is on the earth and by the deities that are in the world of heaven. Moreover, the sacrifice that belongs to ⟦अस्य⟧, this Agni, ⟦ध्रुवस्य⟧, who is motionless, ⟦चरतः⟧, who eats the oblation, the sacrifice which is ⟦आपप्रिवांसं⟧, the filler on all sides, of all the shining things, the celestial cars of the gods, which have come from ⟦दिवः⟧, from the world of heaven: that ⟦गृणीषे⟧, praise, laud. Or else it is this Agni himself that is spoken of. Like one having three heads, he is here endowed with the three places called earth, mid-region and the world of heaven; the meaning is that he pervades the three worlds. Or he abides in the three places, the Gārhapatya and the others. ⟦सप्तरश्मिं⟧: having seven flames; ⟦अनूनम्⟧: not less; ⟦निषत्तम्⟧ in the lap, of ⟦पित्रोः⟧, heaven and earth, in their bosom. Because the form is given ready-made in the rule ⟦नसत्तनिषत्त⟧, the n of the niṣṭhā suffix is absent.

## SBh 1.146.1.3

It means seated. ⟦आपप्रिवांसं⟧: filling on all sides, of desires. Agni, who is distinguished by the characteristics stated, ⟦गृणीषे⟧, praise, laud. Moreover, of ⟦अस्य⟧, this Agni, ⟦चरतः⟧, who goes everywhere with his flames, ⟦ध्रुवस्य⟧, who is unshaken because he is held, ⟦दिवो⟧, who shines, ⟦विश्वा⟧, all, the ⟦रोचना⟧, the shining lustres, "pervade everywhere": this is to be supplied. "Praise such an Agni": this is the direction of the inner self.

## SBh 1.146.2.1

⟦उक्षा⟧: the sprinkler; the meaning is that he is able to bestow the fruit. For that very reason ⟦महान्⟧: this Agni, who by his greatness, by his own form, is like a bull in its pride, ⟦अभि⟧, having stepped over, having pervaded, ⟦एने⟧, heaven and earth, ⟦ववक्षे⟧, carries; the meaning is that he pervades. Of the root vah, "to carry", in leṭ, **NOTE**{The Wikisource text has ⟦वहतेलेंटि⟧, plainly a typo for vahater leṭi, "of vah, in leṭ"; is that right?} there is the Vedic ślu-elision of śap. By the rule ⟦लोपस्त आत्मनेपदेषु⟧, "elision of t in the middle endings", there is the elision of t. By the rule ⟦सिब्बहुलम्⟧, "sip variously", there is sip.

## SBh 1.146.2.2

The meaning is: by bestowing rain and the like and by carrying the oblation he protects the dwellers of the two worlds, gods and men. Moreover, this one is ⟦अजरः⟧, free from old age, ⟦ऋष्वः⟧, great, worthy of worship. This is a name of "great", because it is read among the names of that: ⟦ऋष्वः उक्षः⟧ (⟦नि. ३. ३. ३⟧). Likewise ⟦इतऊतिः⟧, being one who has obtained protection, ⟦तस्थौ⟧, he stands, he exists. Or else: this one, who has the qualities stated, is ⟦इतऊतिः⟧, one who thus has a going hither, who has a going towards our place of sacrifice to the gods, and ⟦तस्थौ⟧, he exists. After that, ⟦सानौ⟧, on the elevated spot, which is the altar, of ⟦उर्व्या⟧, the wide earth, ⟦पदो⟧ ⟦नि⟧ ⟦दधाति⟧, he sets down, he makes, his steps. Moreover the ⟦अरुषासः⟧, the shining ones, of ⟦अस्य⟧, this Agni, ⟦रिहन्ति⟧, lick, the ⟦ऊधः⟧, the mid-region, which stands in the place of an udder. Or else: his ⟦ऊधः⟧, the sacrifice, which stands in the place of an udder, the sacrificers, who are ⟦अरुषास⟧, shining with the lustre of sacred knowledge, ⟦रिहन्ति⟧: they lick the desired heaven and the like.

## SBh 1.146.3.1

⟦समानम्⟧, one and the same, ⟦वत्सं⟧, Agni, who stands in the place of a calf, who is a cause of joy like a son: facing him, ⟦संचरन्ती⟧, moving together, the two ⟦धेनू⟧, who gratify by doing what is good for Agni, the two cows who are the wife and the sacrificer, ⟦विष्वक्⟧ ⟦वि⟧ ⟦चरतः⟧, move about. The meaning is that they make him grow well by putting on fuel, by sweeping and the like, which resemble the giving of the breast to drink and the like. Of what kind are those two? ⟦सुमेके⟧: of good deeds, or of good pouring; the meaning is that they are skilled in attendance. Moreover ⟦अनपवृज्यान्⟧: free from anything that has to be removed. By the rule ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम्⟧, "after nañ and su", the final syllable of the latter member is udātta.

## SBh 1.146.3.2

⟦अध्वनः⟧: the paths, the regions bordering on Agni, which are free from hair and other impure things, ⟦मिमाने⟧, the two who bring them about. Moreover ⟦विश्वान्केतान्⟧, all the knowledges, which concern his being made to grow, ⟦महः⟧, great, ⟦अधि⟧, exceedingly, ⟦दधाने⟧, the two who hold them. Two such cows, in the form of the adhvaryu and the sacrificer, or in the form of wife and husband, move about in all directions. **NOTE**{The Wikisource text has here ⟦विश्वग्विचरतः⟧ where the Ṛk and unit 1 have viṣvak; a typo?}

## SBh 1.146.4.1

⟦धीरासः⟧, the wise, the intelligent, who know the performance, the adhvaryu and the others, **NOTE**{The Wikisource text has ⟦अध्वर्वादयः⟧, plainly a typo for adhvaryvādayaḥ, "the adhvaryu and the others"; is that right?} ⟦नयन्ति⟧, lead, cause to reach, this Agni, who is ⟦अजुर्यम्⟧, unaging, undecayed, to the ⟦पदं⟧, the place, which is the altar, from the place of the churning, or from the Gārhapatya. Or else the sacrificers and the others, whose characteristics are about to be stated, make him go to being a place, an abode, the abode of all desires. Of what kind are they? ⟦कवयः⟧: those whose sight goes beyond, the intelligent; or the learned, because of the revealed text ⟦ये वा अनूचानास्ते कवयः⟧, "those who are learned, they indeed are the kavis" (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. २. २⟧). Moreover ⟦नाना⟧ ⟦हृदा⟧, with an understanding of many kinds, ⟦रक्षमाणाः⟧, holding him. Moreover they, being of this kind, are ⟦सिषासन्तः⟧, wishing to share in, the ⟦सिन्धुं⟧, the flowing one, Agni, who makes the fruits flow by way of the sacrifice. Of the root san, before the desiderative suffix san, the augment iṭ is absent because of the option given in the rule ⟦सनीवन्तर्ध⟧; by the rule ⟦जनसनखनाम्⟧, "of jan, san and khan", there is the vowel ā.

## SBh 1.146.4.2

⟦पर्यपश्यन्त⟧: they look all around; the meaning is that they wish to serve him. Moreover ⟦सूर्यः⟧, the impeller of all, this Agni, for ⟦एभ्यः⟧, for these who act thus, **NOTE**{The Wikisource text has ⟦कुर्वद्यःश⟧, plainly a typo for kurvadbhyaḥ, "for those who act"; is that right?} ⟦नॄन्⟧, for the men, the leaders, ⟦आविः⟧ ⟦अभवत्⟧: out of favour to them he became visible. In the word ⟦नॄन्⟧ here there is an exchange of number. Or it is to be construed thus: "in order to favour ⟦नॄन्⟧, living beings".

## SBh 1.146.5.1

This Agni, in ⟦काष्ठासु⟧, in the ten directions, which exist overstepping one another, is ⟦दिदृक्षेण्यः⟧: by way of favour he becomes joined with sight. Or else: he is one whom it is to be wished to see, who has become the object of the wish to see.

## SBh 1.146.5.2

From the root dṛś, "to see", ending in the desiderative san, in the sense of the kṛtya suffixes, the suffix kenya comes by the rule ⟦तवैकेन्केन्य⟧ and so on. For that very reason he is ⟦जेन्यः⟧: he becomes one whose habit is to appear everywhere, or one whose habit is to conquer. Moreover, at the time of giving, he is ⟦ईळेन्यः⟧, worthy of praise. For what purpose is this so? ⟦जीवसे⟧, for the living, of ⟦महः⟧, the great, a god and the like, or of ⟦अर्भाय⟧, the small, the little, the sacrificer and the like: by carrying the oblation, and by bestowing wealth; such is the distinction. He states the ground for this. ⟦यत्⟧ ⟦अह⟧, since indeed, ⟦पुरुत्रा⟧, in many places (the suffix trā is by the rule beginning ⟦देवमनुष्य⟧), this Agni, ⟦मघवा⟧, who possesses the food that consists of the oblation, ⟦विश्वदर्शतः⟧, who possesses what is to be seen in all spheres, ⟦एभ्यो⟧ ⟦गर्भेभ्यः⟧: the dative is in the sense of the genitive.

## SBh 1.146.5.3

Of these priests, who are to be protected exceedingly like an embryo, like a child, he ⟦सूः⟧ ⟦अभवत्⟧, became, that is, is, the begetter, the producer. Therefore "the kavis, holding him with a manifold understanding, lead him to the place": thus the connection is with the earlier Ṛk.
