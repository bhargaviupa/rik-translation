## SBh 1.139.0.1

The sixth hymn, ⟦अस्तु श्रौषट्⟧, has eleven Ṛks. On this the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦अस्तु श्रौषळेकादश वैश्वदेवी मैत्रावरुण्याश्विन्यस्तिस्र। ऐन्द्र्याग्नेयो मारत्यैन्द्राग्नी बार्हस्पत्या वैश्वदेव्यन्त्या त्रिष्टुप्पञ्चमी बृहती वैश्वदेवमेतदेवमन्यासामपि सूक्तप्रयोगे वैश्वदेवत्वं सूक्तभेदप्रयोगे यल्लिङ्गं सा देवता⟧, "'astu śrauṣaṭ', eleven Ṛks: one addressed to the Viśve Devas, one to Mitra and Varuṇa, three to the Aśvins, one to Indra, one to Agni, one to the Maruts, one to Indra and Agni, one to Bṛhaspati, one to the Viśve Devas; the last is a triṣṭubh, the fifth a bṛhatī; this hymn is addressed to the Viśve Devas; in the same way the other Ṛks too belong to the Viśve Devas when the hymn is used as a hymn, and when the hymn is used divided, the deity is the one whose mark the Ṛk bears" **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦ऐन्द्र्याग्नेयो⟧ and ⟦मारत्यैन्द्राग्नी⟧ in the Anukramaṇikā's words; are these typos for the feminine āgneyī and for mārutī, as translated?}. The seer is Parucchepa. The last Ṛk is a triṣṭubh, the fifth a bṛhatī. The others are atyaṣṭis, since nothing special is stated. The whole hymn is addressed to the Viśve Devas. In it, the deity of the first Ṛk is the Viśve Devas.

## SBh 1.139.0.2

Of the second, Mitra and Varuṇa. Then three have the Aśvins as their deity. The sixth is Indra's. The seventh is Agni's. The eighth is the Maruts'. The ninth is Indra and Agni's. The tenth is Bṛhaspati's. The eleventh is the Viśve Devas'. The ritual use of the hymn is to be gathered from the indications in its wording. On the sixth day of the Daśarātra, in the Praüga-śastra, the first Ṛk is the first in the triplet to the Viśve Devas. In the section ⟦षष्ठस्य⟧ it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦अस्तु श्रौषळो षु णो अग्ने⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८.१.१२⟧), "'astu śrauṣaṭ'; 'o ṣu ṇo agne'."

## SBh 1.139.1.1

I, ⟦पुरः⟧, in front, on the uttaravedi, ⟦दधे⟧, have set, ⟦अग्निम्⟧, the fire called Āhavanīya, ⟦धिया⟧, by the rite of carrying it forward and the rest. ⟦तच्छर्धः⟧, that Agni, who is such or who is strong; or else ⟦तच्छर्धः⟧, that strength of such a kind which is the Maruts' own form, ⟦दिव्यं⟧, which is in heaven, ⟦नु⟧, quickly, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦वृणीमहे⟧, we resort to, turned towards it. Moreover ⟦इन्द्रवायू⟧, Indra and Vāyu, ⟦वृणीमहे⟧, we resort to. ⟦यत्⟧, for which reason, ⟦विवस्वति⟧, in the radiant, ⟦नाभा⟧, navel, in the place that is the navel of the earth, the place of sacrificing to the gods, which has the form of the vedi; or else, in the navel, the sacrifice, which binds every fruit to itself, for the Śruti says ⟦यज्ञमाहुर्भुवनस्य नाभिम्⟧ (⟦तै. सं. ७.४.१८. २⟧), "They call the sacrifice the navel of the world"; ⟦क्राणा⟧, bringing about the making known of its own meaning, ⟦नव्यसी⟧, a newer, speech in the form of praise, ⟦संदायि⟧, is bound. The form is a Vedic luṅ in the passive.

## SBh 1.139.1.2

The meaning is: it is employed by us. Because praise is made, therefore ⟦अस्तु⟧ ⟦श्रौषट्⟧, let there be a hearing of this praise; or, let the troop of the Maruts, or Agni, be a hearer. On the view that it is Indra and Vāyu, the singular number has regard to each of them singly. ⟦अध⟧, thereafter, ⟦नः⟧ ⟦धीतयः⟧, our rites, which have the form of praise and the like, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦सु⟧ ⟦उप⟧ ⟦यन्तु⟧, let them go to you, eminently and well. Moreover ⟦देवाँ⟧ ⟦अच्छा⟧ ⟦न⟧, like a thing that has reached the gods, Agni and the others, face to face, ⟦धीतयः⟧, our rites, let them go near, let them arrive close by.

## SBh 1.139.2.1

O ⟦मित्रावरुणौ⟧, ⟦यत्⟧, for which reason, ⟦ऋतात्⟧, from the sun, ⟦त्यत्⟧, that water, ⟦अधि⟧, in abundance, ⟦आददाथे⟧, you give on every side. Water of what kind? ⟦अनृतं⟧, perishable, of a nature to dry up. By what means? ⟦स्वेन⟧ ⟦मन्युना⟧, by your own brilliance. Mitra and Varuṇa, the gods who preside over day and night, do indeed release the rain; the Śruti beginning ⟦अहोरात्रे वा⟧ has been quoted already before. The manyu is qualified: ⟦दक्षस्य⟧, of the able one, ⟦स्वेन⟧ ⟦मन्युना⟧, by the ability that is your very self, that is to be thought upon. Moreover, O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦युवोः⟧ — the elision of the final is Vedic — the form belonging to you two, ⟦हिरण्ययं⟧, golden.

## SBh 1.139.2.2

In the rule beginning ⟦ऋत्व्यवास्त्व्यवास्त्व°⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ४. १७५⟧) the elision of the syllable ma of the suffix mayaṭ is laid down as a ready-made form.

## SBh 1.139.2.3

⟦इत्था⟧, thus, in the manner about to be stated, ⟦सद्मसु⟧, in the seats of the sacrifice, ⟦अधि⟧ ⟦अपश्याम⟧, may we see. By what means? That is said: ⟦धीभिः⟧, by rites that have you as their aim, in the form of sacrifice and the like, ⟦मनसा⟧ ⟦चन⟧, and with a mind attached to you, ⟦स्वेभिरक्षभिः⟧, with our own senses, the eye and the others, that are turned to nothing else. Moreover ⟦सोमस्य⟧, Soma, ⟦स्वेभिरक्षभिः⟧, with our senses **NOTE**{the unit glosses ⟦सोमस्य⟧ with the bare ⟦सोम⟧; the case meant, and so the construction of this clause, is doubtful.}. The plural number has regard to the nose and the others.

## SBh 1.139.3.1

On the sixth day of the Pṛṣṭhya, in the Praüga-śastra, ⟦युवां स्तोमेभिः⟧ is the triplet to the Aśvins. In the section ⟦षष्ठस्य⟧ it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦युवां स्तोमेभिर्देवयन्तो अश्विनावर्मह इन्द्र⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८. १⟧), "'yuvāṃ stomebhir devayanto aśvinā'; 'avar maha indra'."

## SBh 1.139.3.2

O ⟦अश्विना⟧, you two gods, the Aśvins, ⟦आयवः⟧ — this is a name for men — the sacrificers, ⟦देवयन्तः⟧, desiring you two for themselves, praise ⟦युवां⟧, you two, ⟦स्तोमेभिः⟧, with praises, ⟦आश्रावयन्तइव⟧, as though making ⟦श्लोकं⟧, the speech that has the form of praise of you, a thing heard on every side. ⟦श्लोक⟧ is a name of speech, because it is stated among those names in ⟦श्लोकः धारा⟧ (⟦नि. १. ११. १⟧) **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦तन्नासूक्तत्वात्⟧; is this a typo for tannāmasūktatvāt, "because it is stated among its names", as translated?}. Of what kind are they? ⟦अभ्यायवः⟧, going towards, ⟦युवां⟧, you two, ⟦हव्या⟧, with the oblation, ghee and the like.

## SBh 1.139.3.3

"To gladden you" is to be supplied. Or, making the oblations reach you face to face. Moreover, O ⟦विश्ववेदसा⟧, you who possess all wealth, ⟦युवोः⟧, belonging to you two, ⟦विश्वाः⟧ ⟦श्रियः⟧, all the fortunes that are to be resorted to, in the form of wealth, gold and the like, ⟦अधि⟧, in abundance — "they enjoy" is to be supplied. Moreover ⟦पृक्षश्च⟧, food too, by your favour, ⟦अधि⟧, in abundance, they obtain. Moreover, O ⟦दस्रा⟧, you two destroyers of enemies, ⟦वां⟧, your, ⟦हिरण्यये⟧ ⟦रथे⟧, on the golden chariot, full of honey, ⟦पवयः⟧, its fellies, ⟦प्रुषायन्ते⟧, drip, flow.

## SBh 1.139.3.4

The roots are ⟦प्रुष प्लुष स्नेहनसेचनपूरणेषु⟧, pruṣ and pluṣ in the senses of moistening, sprinkling and filling. By the rule ⟦छन्दसि शायजपि⟧, "in the Veda śāyac too", the substitute śāyac takes the place of śnā even where hi does not follow.

## SBh 1.139.3.5

The purport is: because the chariot is furnished with vessels full of honey, through its swift going the honeys flow upon the wheels. ⟦पवी रथनेमिर्भवति यद्विपुनाति भूमिम्⟧ (⟦निरु. ५. ५.⟧), "pavi is the felly of a chariot, because it cleaves the ground", says the Nirukta. That the chariot of the Aśvins is full of honey is well known in passages such as ⟦त्रयः पवयो मधुवाहने रथे⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. १, ३४. २⟧), "three fellies on the honey-carrying chariot". Moreover, on such a chariot, ⟦हिरण्यये⟧, which delights the heart — "carry the sweet oblation" is to be supplied.

## SBh 1.139.4.1

O ⟦दस्रा⟧, you two Aśvins, ⟦अचेति⟧, your deed that is about to be stated is known by all. What is it? That is said: ⟦नाकं⟧, to heaven, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦ऋण्वथः⟧, you go in a special way.

## SBh 1.139.4.2

The root is ⟦ऋणु गतौ⟧, ṛṇu in the sense of going; it is of the class beginning with tan; in leṭ there is the augment aṭ. That is to say, immediately after the completion of the sacrifice. The word u expresses restriction. Or else: ⟦नाकम्⟧: ⟦अकं⟧ is pain; to the sacrifice, which is free of that, you go; you do indeed go in a special way. For that purpose ⟦वां⟧ ⟦रथयुजः⟧, the yokers of the chariot that belongs to you, the charioteers **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦सारथथः⟧; is this a typo for sārathayaḥ, "charioteers", as translated?}, ⟦दिविष्टिषु⟧, when the seekings of heaven, the seekings of the sacrifice whose nature is to shine, or the goings to it, are the occasion, ⟦युञ्जते⟧, yoke the horses to the chariot; or, yoke you to the chariot when it has been yoked. The same are qualified further.

## SBh 1.139.4.3

⟦दिविष्टिषु⟧, in the goings through the sky, the goings in the sky that has no support, ⟦अध्वस्मानः⟧, not causing the ruin of the chariot and of him who rests on it. Moreover, O ⟦दस्रा⟧, you two Aśvins, ⟦वां⟧, of you two, ⟦बन्धुरे⟧, furnished with three bandhuras. A bandhura is a particular piece of wood that is the support for the binding of the yoke. That the chariot is furnished with three such is well known in passages such as ⟦रथो यो वां त्रिवन्धुरः⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. ८. २२. ५⟧), "the chariot of you two which has three bandhuras". ⟦हिरण्यये⟧, golden, ⟦रथे⟧ ⟦अधि⟧, on top of such a chariot, ⟦स्थाम⟧, we have made you stand. In the Vedic laṅ the augment aṭ is absent by the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧. The udātta on the first syllable is Vedic.

## SBh 1.139.4.4

Of what kind are you two? ⟦पथेव⟧, by a good path, a road, in the sky that has no support, ⟦यन्तौ⟧, going, towards ⟦रजः⟧, heaven, whose nature is to delight; for it has been said ⟦लोका रजांस्युच्यन्ते⟧, "the worlds are called rajas". ⟦अनुशासता⟧, disciplining, making submissive, the enemies who are not favourable. Since the root śās, before the suffix śatṛ, is termed abhyasta by the rule ⟦जक्षित्यादयः⟧, the first syllable is udātta by the rule ⟦अभ्यस्तानामादिः⟧. Moreover ⟦अञ्जसा⟧, in the chief way, ⟦शासता⟧, making submissive by force, ⟦रजः⟧, the excellent water that is characterised as rain.

## SBh 1.139.5.1

O ⟦शचीवसू⟧ — śacī is a name for a rite — you two whose wealth is the rites, the Jyotiṣṭoma and the others, performed by us, ⟦शचीभिः⟧, with our rites, the sacrifices and the like, as the occasion, ⟦दिवा⟧ ⟦नक्तं⟧, by day and by night, ⟦दशस्यतम्⟧, give what is desired.

## SBh 1.139.5.2

This is a Vedic form of the root ⟦दाशृ दाने⟧, dāś in the sense of giving. Or else: the root daśasya, which has the sense of giving, is to be looked for among the roots beginning with kaṇḍū.

## SBh 1.139.5.3

⟦वां⟧, of you two, ⟦रातिः⟧, the giving, ⟦कदा⟧ ⟦चन⟧, always, at the time of sacrifice and also at a time when there is no sacrifice, ⟦मा⟧ ⟦उप⟧ ⟦दसत्⟧, may it not become exhausted. The root is ⟦दसु उपक्षये⟧, das in the sense of wasting away. In luṅ, aṅ takes the place of cli by the rule ⟦पुषादिद्युतात्⟧.

## SBh 1.139.5.4

Not yours alone, but ours too: ⟦रातिः⟧, the giving, the offering of oblations and the like, or the giving of things of every kind to those who ask, ⟦कदा⟧ ⟦चन⟧, in every condition too, may it not become exhausted; let it continue always. The meaning is: may I too always give with you in view, and do you two also always give what is desired.

## SBh 1.139.6.1

On the sixth day of the Pṛṣṭhya, in the triplet to Indra of the Praüga-śastra, the third Ṛk is this one, ⟦वृषन्निन्द्र⟧. The sūtra is ⟦वृषन्निन्द्र वृषपाणास इन्दवः सुषुमा यातमद्रिभिः⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८. १⟧), "'vṛṣann indra vṛṣapāṇāsa indavaḥ'; 'suṣumā yātam adribhiḥ'."

## SBh 1.139.6.2

At that same pressing, other Ṛks are inserted before the prasthita-yājyās and the offering is to be made with both. There, this Ṛk is to be inserted before the prasthita-yājyā of the hotṛ. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦षष्ठस्य प्रातःसवने प्रस्थितयाज्यानां पुरस्तादन्याः कृत्वोभाभ्यामनवानन्तो यजन्ति⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८, १⟧), "At the morning pressing of the sixth day, having put other Ṛks before the prasthita-yājyās, they offer with both without taking breath."

## SBh 1.139.6.3

O ⟦इन्द्र⟧, ⟦वृषन्⟧, showerer of desires, ⟦इमे⟧, these, that are seen in front, ⟦इन्दवः⟧, Somas, are ⟦सुताः⟧, pressed for your sake. Of what kind are they? ⟦वृषपानासः⟧, to be drunk by you, the showerer of what is desired; ⟦अद्रिसुतासः⟧, pressed with stones, with firm rocks that are the means of pressing; ⟦उद्भिदः⟧, causing the rain to break forth. The same are qualified again: ⟦उद्भिदः⟧, causing strength to break forth, or sprung up on a mountain and the like; such Somas are ⟦तुभ्यं⟧ ⟦सुतासः⟧, pressed for your sake alone. Moreover ⟦ते⟧, such Somas, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦मन्दन्तु⟧, let them satisfy. Where? That is said: ⟦दावने⟧, in the sacrifice, which has giving in it; or, for the giving of what is desired. Likewise ⟦महे⟧, for great, ⟦चित्राय⟧, honoured, wonderful, or of many kinds, ⟦राधसे⟧, wealth. Moreover, O ⟦गिर्वाहः⟧, bearer of the praises that are ours: the suffix is asun by the rule ⟦वहिहाधाञ्भ्यः⟧.

## SBh 1.139.6.4

Being ⟦स्तवमानः⟧, praised, ⟦गीर्भिः⟧, by our praises, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गहि⟧, come. Being ⟦सुमृळीकः⟧, one who gladdens well, ⟦नः⟧, us, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गहि⟧, come.

## SBh 1.139.7.1

On the sixth day of the Daśarātra, at the morning pressing, this Ṛk, ⟦ओ षू णो अग्ने⟧, is to be put in before the prasthita-yājyā of the Neṣṭṛ; the two are made one and the offering is with both. In the section ⟦षष्ठस्य⟧ it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦ओ षु णो अग्ने शृणुहि त्वमीळितोऽग्निं होतारम्⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८. १⟧), "'o ṣu ṇo agne śṛṇuhi tvam īḷitaḥ'; 'agniṃ hotāram'." At that same pressing, in the Praüga-śastra, it is the second Ṛk. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦अस्तु श्रौषळो षु णो अग्ने⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८. १⟧).

## SBh 1.139.7.2

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦त्वं⟧, you, ⟦ईळितः⟧, being praised by us, ⟦ओ⟧ ⟦षु⟧ ⟦शृणुहि⟧, hear ⟦नः⟧, our invocation, or our praise. ⟦ओ⟧ is a single particle made up of a combination of two particles. Hear well, turned towards us indeed.

## SBh 1.139.7.3

Where the rule beginning utaś ca pratyayāt would apply, the ending hi is not elided, by the statement ⟦छन्दसि वावचनम्⟧, "in the Veda an option is to be stated". And having heard, ⟦देवेभ्यः⟧, to those whose nature is to shine, ⟦यज्ञियेभ्यः⟧, who are worthy of the sacrifice, that is, who have a share in the oblation and a share in the praise — the suffix is gha by the rule ⟦यज्ञर्त्विग्भ्याम्⟧ — to them ⟦ब्रवसि⟧, tell, the rite that belongs ⟦यज्ञियेभ्यः⟧, to the sacrificers, who are worthy of the sacrifice. From the root brū, in leṭ, there is the augment aṭ.

## SBh 1.139.7.4

To gods of what kind? ⟦राजभ्यः⟧, who shine. Now the gods are spoken to directly. Here they tell a story. The great seers named Aṅgirases, in former times, having gladdened the gods with praise for the sake of the sacrifice, asked for cows. They, being pleased, gave the cow that yields what is wished. And being unable to milk the milk of that cow when they had got her, they entreated the god Aryaman. And he, being entreated, milked the milk for the Agnihotra and the like. That is what is said here. ⟦यत्⟧, which, ⟦त्या⟧ **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦त्या⟧ where the Ṛk has the accusative of this word; is it a typo?}, that well-known, ⟦धेनुं⟧, cow, who gladdens with milk and the like, ⟦ह⟧, ⟦देवाः⟧, O gods, ⟦अङ्गिरोभ्यः⟧, for their sake, ⟦अदत्तन⟧, you gave. The substitute tana is by the rule ⟦तप्तनप्तनथनाश्च⟧.

## SBh 1.139.7.5

⟦तां⟧, that cow, ⟦अर्यमा⟧, the god who controls all, ⟦कर्तरि⟧, in you, Agni, the producer of all, ⟦सचा⟧, together with the other gods, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦दुह्रे⟧, milked in various ways. From the root duh, in liṭ, by the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ **NOTE**{the unit names the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧ for the liṭ of duh without saying what it effects; is something missing here?}; by the statement ⟦छन्दसि वा⟧ there is no reduplication. Or it is in laṭ itself, with the elision of ta by the rule ⟦लोपस्त°⟧.

## SBh 1.139.7.6

⟦एषः⟧, this Aryaman alone, ⟦वेद⟧, knows, ⟦तां⟧, that cow, ⟦मे⟧ ⟦सचा⟧, in union with me; the meaning is, Aryaman knows and I too know. Such a cow is milked for your sake alone: this is the praise of Agni.

## SBh 1.139.8.1

⟦मो षु वो अस्मत्⟧ is the prasthita-yājyā. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦मो षु वो अस्मदभि तानि पौंस्यौ षू णो अग्ने⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८. १⟧), "'mo ṣu vo asmad abhi tāni pauṃsyā'; 'o ṣū ṇo agne'."

## SBh 1.139.8.2

O Maruts, ⟦वः⟧, belonging to you, ⟦तानि⟧, those well-known, ⟦पौंस्या⟧, strengths, ⟦सना⟧, everlasting, constant, ⟦द्युम्नानि⟧, endowed with light. Or else: ⟦सना⟧, to be resorted to, ⟦द्युम्नानि⟧, possessed of wealth or possessed of fame; the suffix that has the sense of possession is elided. In ⟦मो⟧, a combination of two particles, the word u expresses restriction.

## SBh 1.139.8.3

⟦अस्मत्⟧, us, ⟦मो⟧ ⟦षु⟧ ⟦अभि⟧ ⟦भूवन्⟧, may they well not at all overpower. Or else: may they not be gone away from us. ⟦उत⟧, and also. Or else, the word ⟦द्युम्नानि⟧ is connected with what follows: ⟦द्युम्नानि⟧, the riches or the fames that are ours, ⟦मा⟧ ⟦जारिषुः⟧, may they not become wasted. ⟦उत⟧, and also, ⟦अस्मत्⟧ ⟦पुरा⟧, our cities, by secondary indication the living beings who are in them, may they not ⟦जारिषुः⟧, become worn out, destroyed. Moreover ⟦वः⟧, belonging to you, ⟦चित्रं⟧, worthy of honour, of many kinds, ⟦नव्यं⟧, new, or to be praised, ⟦अमर्त्यम्⟧, not subject to death, or hard to obtain among mortals, ⟦यत्⟧, what there is; ⟦घोषात्⟧, the ghoṣas — the fifth case stands by interchange.

## SBh 1.139.8.4

The cattle and the like that are endowed with sound, or the villages, towns and the like indicated by the word ghoṣa, that there are: ⟦तत्⟧, all that, ⟦युगेयुगे⟧, at each several time indicated by the word yuga, ⟦अस्मासु⟧, in us alone — "let it be" is to be supplied — not another's. Moreover, O ⟦मरुतः⟧, ⟦यच्च⟧ ⟦दुस्तरं⟧, and the wealth that is hard to cross to, hard to acquire, ⟦दिधृत⟧, hold, that in us. Moreover ⟦दुस्तरं⟧, what is not harmed by enemies, all that too set in us in a special way.

## SBh 1.139.9.1

On the sixth day of the Pṛṣṭhya, before the prasthita-yājyā of the Acchāvāka, comes this Ṛk, ⟦दध्यङ् ह मे⟧. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦दध्यङ् ह मे जनुषं पूर्वो अङ्गिरा इत्येवमेव माध्यंदिने⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८. १⟧), "'dadhyaṅ ha me januṣaṃ pūrvo aṅgirāḥ': in just the same way at the midday pressing."

## SBh 1.139.9.2

⟦दध्यङ्⟧, the great seer of this name, born in the family of Atharvan; and he is ⟦पूर्वः⟧, the one who fills all, or the ancient one; ⟦अङ्गिराः⟧, a live coal, that is, brilliant like one. Or else: the live coals themselves became the Aṅgirases, for the Śruti says ⟦येऽङ्गारा आसंस्तेऽङ्गिरसोऽभवन्⟧ (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. ३. ३४⟧), "What were live coals, they became the Aṅgirases." ⟦अङ्गिरा अङ्गारा अङ्कना अञ्चनाः⟧ (⟦निरु. ३. १७.⟧), "aṅgiras: live coals; they mark, they move", says the Nirukta. And it is the great seer of this name. And ⟦प्रियमेधः⟧, one to whom wisdom is dear.

## SBh 1.139.9.3

⟦प्रियमेधः प्रिया अस्य मेधाः⟧ (⟦निरु. ३.१७⟧), "priyamedha: dear to him are his wisdoms", says Yāska. And he is ⟦कण्वः⟧, wise. And he is ⟦अत्रिः⟧, free of the three kinds of pain; ⟦अत्रिर्न श्रयः⟧ (⟦निरु.३.१७⟧), "Atri: not three", says the Nirukta **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦अत्रिर्न श्रयः⟧; is the last word a typo for trayaḥ, "three", as translated?}. ⟦मनुः⟧, one who has thought, the great seer of this name. All these great-souled great seers ⟦विदुः⟧, know, ⟦मे⟧ ⟦जनुषं⟧, the birth of Parucchepa, the son of Divodāsa. From the root vid, the substitute us takes the place of jhi by the rule ⟦विदो लटो वा⟧.

## SBh 1.139.9.4

Moreover ⟦ते⟧, those already named, ⟦पूर्वे⟧, who are of former time, ⟦मे⟧: the meaning is that they know our fathers and the others. ⟦मनुः⟧, Manus, possessors of knowledge: since they are of former time and are Manus, therefore ⟦विदुः⟧, they know; this is the purport. The ending jas is elided by the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुक्⟧.

## SBh 1.139.9.5

He states the ground for their knowing. ⟦तेषां⟧, of them, Dadhyañc and the others, ⟦देवेषु⟧, among the other great seers whose nature is to shine, there is ⟦आयतिः⟧, a connection of long duration. Therefore they know: this is the meaning. And what follows from that? He says: ⟦अस्माकं⟧ ⟦नाभयः⟧, our life-breaths, which have a bond with the living soul, or our sacrifices, which are bound to their fruit, rest ⟦तेषु⟧, in them alone. Since it is they who have to say "This man is of distinguished birth, therefore he is fit for the sacrifice", there is dependence on them. ⟦तेषां⟧, of them, Dadhyañc and the others, ⟦पदेन⟧, by reason of the standing, the greatness, ⟦गिरा⟧, with speech in the form of praise, ⟦महि⟧, greatly, exceedingly, ⟦नमे⟧, every person bows. Moreover, being fit for the sacrifice through their favour, and being furnished ⟦गिरा⟧, with speech in the form of praise, ⟦इन्द्राग्नी⟧, to Indra and Agni, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦नमे⟧, I bow, turned towards them; the meaning is, I praise and I bow. The middle voice of the root nam is by interchange.

## SBh 1.139.9.6

Or else: the form is in the reflexive passive, with the prohibition of yak by the rule ⟦न दुहस्नुनमाम्⟧; I am bowed of my own accord.

## SBh 1.139.10.1

This ⟦होता⟧, the divine one, Agni, or the human one, the invoker of the gods, ⟦यक्षत्⟧, let him recite the yājyā. From the root yaj, in leṭ, there is the augment aṭ; sip is by the rule ⟦सिब्बहुलम्⟧.

## SBh 1.139.10.2

⟦वनिनः⟧, those who have a sharing, or who desire the oblation, the gods, ⟦वन्त⟧, let them partake of, ⟦वार्यं⟧, the Soma, which is to be chosen.

## SBh 1.139.10.3

From the root van, in the Vedic laṅ, the middle voice is by interchange; śap is elided by the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧; the elision of the final is Vedic. For that purpose ⟦बृहस्पतिः⟧, the protector of the great mantra or of the great oblation, the Adhvaryu, or the divine Bṛhaspati himself, ⟦यजति⟧, performs the sacrifice. Of what kind is he? ⟦वेनः⟧, desiring. By what means? That is said: ⟦उक्षभिः⟧, with the Somas, which sprinkle, ⟦पुरुवारेभिः⟧, which are to be chosen by many; likewise ⟦उक्षभिः⟧, with the waters, which are able to sprinkle, arisen as rain by way of the sun through the oblations that are being offered and are their cause. We, the sacrificers, ⟦दूरआदिशं⟧, that whose ⟦आदिक्⟧, announcement, is in a far place: the case-ending is not elided, by the rule ⟦हलदन्तात्सप्तम्या:०⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६.३.९⟧).

## SBh 1.139.10.4

⟦अद्रेः⟧ — the singular is used for the class — of the stone that is the means of pressing, ⟦श्लोकं⟧, the speech, the sound, ⟦जगृभ्म⟧, we take in with our ears. The plural is for honour. Now he is spoken of in the third person. ⟦सुक्रतुः⟧, this sacrificer, whose rites are good, ⟦अध⟧, then, immediately after the completion of the sacrifice, ⟦अररिन्दानि⟧ — this is a name of water, because it is read among those names in ⟦अररिन्दानि ध्वस्मन्वत्⟧ (⟦नि. १. १२. २६⟧) — the waters that are characterised as rain, ⟦त्मना⟧, by himself, ⟦अधारयत्⟧, holds.

## SBh 1.139.10.5

⟦ररिर्दाता⟧: rari is a giver. There is no other giver of this quenching of thirst; they give such a quenching of thirst, hence the word ararindāni. Or else: ⟦अररिः⟧ is going hither and thither; the suffix is the uṇādi suffix ari; then the suffix ka by the rule ⟦आतोऽनुपसर्गे कः⟧; since the word belongs to the class beginning with pṛṣodara, the desired form and accent are established. They give that, hence the word ararindāni, the waters; the meaning is, givers of movement, for the Śruti says ⟦आपोमयः प्राणः⟧ (⟦छा. उ. ६. ५. ४⟧), "The breath is made of water." Or else: ⟦ररिर्दानम्⟧, rari is giving; that such giving as is not found in the other elements is ⟦अररि⟧.

## SBh 1.139.10.6

They give that, hence the word ararindāni. The meaning is: they give the enjoyment that benefits the world and cannot be given by others. The meaning is that he has produced such waters by the sacrifice. Moreover ⟦सुक्रतुः⟧, this one, whose rites are good, held ⟦पुरु⟧, many, ⟦सद्मानि⟧, seats, houses fit for dwelling in at ease. Or else: Bṛhaspati, who is also ⟦सुक्रतुश्च⟧, this Adhvaryu, ⟦अधारयत्⟧, has held, ⟦अररिन्दानि⟧, the waters of the character stated, which are a means of pressing. Likewise, ⟦सुक्रतुः⟧, of good understanding, he held ⟦पुरु⟧ ⟦सद्मानि⟧, the seats, the places where the oblations are set down.

## SBh 1.139.11.1

## SBh 1.139.11.3

You gods who dwell in the mid-region ⟦एकादश⟧ ⟦स्थ⟧, are of that number.
