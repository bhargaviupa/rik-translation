## SBh 1.151.0.1

⟦मित्रं न यम्⟧: this is the eleventh sūkta, of nine Ṛks, of Dīrghatamas, in the jagatī metre, addressed to Mitra and Varuṇa. The first Ṛk is addressed to Mitra alone. On this the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦मित्रं नव मैत्रावरुणं हि जागतं मैत्र्याद्या⟧, "'Mitram': nine Ṛks, addressed to Mitra and Varuṇa indeed, in jagatī; the first is addressed to Mitra". The application is the one shown by the indications in the text.

## SBh 1.151.1.1

⟦यम्⟧, whom, Agni, ⟦शिम्या⟧: this is a name for action, because it is stated among the names of that, ⟦शिमी शक्तिः⟧ (⟦नि. २.१.२४⟧); by the action of producing light, rain and the like, he stands ⟦मित्रं⟧ ⟦न⟧, like Mitra. Or else this word ⟦शिम्या⟧ is connected with the verb. ⟦गोषु⟧, for the sake of cows, many of them, which are the motive, ⟦गव्यवः⟧, desiring cows for themselves. Here, although ⟦गव्यवः⟧ alone would be enough, and not ⟦गोषु⟧, yet in ⟦गव्यव⟧ the ownership of cows is conveyed, and that might be by one cow, or even by two or three. Therefore, to make it understood that many are to be longed for, there is the word ⟦गोषु⟧.

## SBh 1.151.1.2

As in ⟦गवामसि गोपतिः⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. ७, ९८.६⟧), "Of cows you are the lord of cows", and ⟦गणानां त्वा गणपतिम्⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. २.२३.१⟧), "You, the lord of hosts of the hosts". Likewise ⟦स्वाध्यः⟧, those who think well upon him, the producers of the fruit, or those of good meditation **NOTE**{⟦शोभनध्वाना⟧ ("of good sound") is taken as a typo for śobhanadhyānā, "of good meditation"; is that right?}, ⟦विदथे⟧, in the sacrifice, him who is present ⟦अप्सु⟧, in the mid-region, in the form of lightning. ⟦आप⟧ is a name of the mid-region, because it is stated among the names of that, ⟦आपः पृथिवी⟧ (⟦नि. १. ३.८⟧). The arising of Agni in the mid-region is well known in other mantras such as ⟦यदग्ने दिविजा अस्यप्सुजा वा⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. ८.४३. २८⟧), "Since, O Agni, you are born in heaven or born in the waters", and ⟦अप्स्वग्ने सधिष्टव⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. ८. ४३. ९⟧), "In the waters, O Agni, is your seat".

## SBh 1.151.1.3

⟦प्रियं⟧, the dearest of all, or the one who gladdens, ⟦यजतं⟧, who is to be worshipped, Agni, ⟦जनुषां⟧, of those that have birth, the living beings, ⟦अवः⟧ ⟦प्रति⟧, making their protection the motive, ⟦शिम्या⟧, by the action of churning and the like, ⟦जीजनन्⟧, the sacrificers bring forth. Or else ⟦अप्सु⟧, with the waters as the motive, ⟦जीजनन्⟧, they bring him forth: the meaning is, for the benefit of all living beings and for the sake of rain. Of Agni thus brought forth, ⟦पाजसा⟧, by the strength, and ⟦गिरा⟧, by the terrifying sound, ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth, ⟦अरेजेताम्⟧, trembled. The trembling on account of Agni is well known in śruti passages such as ⟦अरेजेतां रोदसी होतृवूर्ये⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. १. ३१.३⟧), "Heaven and earth trembled at the choosing of the hotṛ".

## SBh 1.151.1.4

And it is not to be said, "Agni, brought forth for the protection of all, produced fear in the two worlds: what is this?", because this is praise in the form of blame; for it ends in the praise "he grew so great that the two worlds are afraid". Although Agni alone is set forth here, and Mitra is only brought in as an illustration, yet, since Agni whose place is heaven is himself Mitra, the mantra's being addressed to Mitra is not contradicted. Or else: the god named Mitra, who stands ⟦मित्रं न⟧, like a friend, the officiating priests make manifest in the sacrifice and in the mid-region. The rest is to be construed in the same way, with this difference.

## SBh 1.151.2.1

O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦यत्⟧, for which reason, ⟦वां⟧, you two, ⟦पुरुमीळ्हस्य⟧, of him who pours out in abundance what is desired, ⟦सोमिनः⟧ ⟦स्वाभुवः⟧, who has the Soma and whose nature it is to be well present. The genitive is in the sense of the object.

## SBh 1.151.2.2

⟦त्यत्⟧, that action of the character stated, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦मित्रासो⟧ ⟦न⟧, like friends, the officiating priests who are friends, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦दधिरे⟧, uphold in an eminent way. ⟦ह⟧ is an expletive. ⟦अध⟧, for this reason, ⟦गातुं⟧, your going, ⟦अर्चते⟧: the fourth case is in the sense of the sixth **NOTE**{⟦षष्य््र्थे⟧ is taken as a typo for ṣaṣṭhyarthe, "in the sense of the sixth case"; is that right?}; of him who honours it, of the sacrificer who worships, ⟦क्रतुं⟧, the rite, ⟦विदतं⟧, know. From the root vid, in loṭ, there is by transposition the suffix śa.

## SBh 1.151.2.3

⟦उत⟧, and moreover, O ⟦वृषणा⟧, you two showerers of desires, ⟦पस्त्यावतः⟧: ⟦पस्त्या⟧ is a name for a house, because it is stated among the names of that, ⟦पस्त्या दुरोणे⟧ (⟦नि. ३. ४. ६⟧); of the sacrificer who has houses such as the sadas and the prācīnavaṃśa, the call or the praise, ⟦श्रुतं⟧, hear. The elision of the conjugational suffix is Vedic.

## SBh 1.151.2.4

Or else: since those who stand ⟦मित्रासो न⟧, like friends, of the king of this name, Purumīḷha, ⟦सोमिनः⟧, who has the Soma, they who are ⟦स्वाभुवः⟧, whose nature it is to be well present, able each in his own function, ⟦दधिरे⟧, uphold; ⟦अध⟧, for this reason, of him, ⟦अर्चते⟧, who worships, know the ⟦क्रतुं⟧, and so on as before.

## SBh 1.151.3.1

O ⟦वृषणा⟧, showerers of desires, O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦वां⟧, belonging to you two, ⟦जन्म⟧, the birth, the arising, ⟦रोदस्योः⟧, from heaven and earth; for the arising of all is from heaven and earth alone. Or else by Mitra is meant Agni, the benefactor of all, and by Varuṇa the Āditya, the remover of darkness, because the two have the earth and heaven as their places. Then, in the previous mantra Agni was praised with Mitra as the illustration. From this it is understood that Agni himself is Mitra. So also elsewhere: ⟦अग्निं मित्रं न क्षितिषु प्रशंस्यम्⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. २. २. ३⟧), "Agni, to be praised among the peoples like Mitra", and ⟦मित्रं न ये सुधितं भृगवो दधुः⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. ६. १५. २⟧), "whom the Bhṛgus set in place, well placed like Mitra". ⟦प्रवाच्यं⟧, praiseworthy, such a birth of you two, ⟦महे⟧, for great, ⟦दक्षसे⟧, strength in regard to all things, ⟦क्षितयः⟧, men, the sacrificers, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦भूषन्⟧, adorn on all sides; the meaning is that they extol it.

## SBh 1.151.3.2

He states why the birth is praiseworthy. ⟦यदीं⟧, for which very reason. Or else ⟦ईम्⟧ is without meaning, because it has been said that in metrical texts the words kam, īm, id and u are without meaning, ⟦मिताक्षरेष्वनर्थकाः⟧. ⟦ऋताय⟧, for the sacrifice, ⟦भरथः⟧, you bring ability. And ⟦यत्⟧, for which reason, ⟦अर्वते⟧, for the sacrificer also, who has reached you, you bring the desired fruit. Or else, in both places the fourth case is used because the object is treated as the recipient; the meaning is that you support the sacrifice and the sacrificer. And since ⟦होत्रया⟧, by speech in the form of praise, and ⟦शिम्या⟧, by the action of offering the oblation and the like, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦वीथः⟧, you reach in an eminent way. The meaning is: therefore they adorn the praiseworthy birth. ⟦होत्रा⟧ is a name of speech, because it is read among the names of that, ⟦होत्रा गीः⟧ (⟦नि. १. ११. ३५⟧).

## SBh 1.151.4.1

O ⟦असुरा⟧, you two strong ones, Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦या⟧ ⟦क्षितिः⟧, the ground which, being the place of sacrifice to the gods, is ⟦महि⟧, greatly, exceedingly, ⟦प्रिया⟧, dearer, or gladdening, to you two, ⟦सा⟧ ⟦क्षितिः⟧, that ground, ⟦प्र⟧, has been prepared in an eminent way. Because a preverb is heard, a suitable verb is to be supplied. "Sit down upon it" is to be supplied. It is well known that the ground of sacrifice is dear to the gods. And having sat down, O ⟦ऋतावानौ⟧, you two who possess ṛta, ⟦बृहत्⟧, grown great, ⟦ऋतम्⟧, our sacrifice, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦घोषथः⟧; the meaning is: praise it, saying "It has been accomplished without defect". Since ⟦युवं⟧, you two, ⟦बृहतः⟧, of the great, ⟦दिवः⟧, world of heaven, ⟦धुरि⟧, at the yoke, ⟦दक्षं⟧, able to carry the satisfying.

## SBh 1.151.4.2

Or else ⟦बृहतो दिवो⟧, the great gods who dwell in the world of heaven, ⟦दक्षं⟧: "to gladden" is to be supplied. Or else the genitive is in the sense of the object: ⟦दिवो⟧, of the nature of shining, ⟦बृहतः⟧, abundant, ⟦दक्षम्⟧, able to accomplish what is desired, ⟦आभुवं⟧, whose nature it is to be on all sides, ⟦अपः⟧, the action in the form of the Soma sacrifice, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦युञ्जाथे⟧, you attend upon; the meaning is that you accept the oblation that is there. There is an illustration of the use: ⟦धुरि⟧, in the sustaining of the strength of the body, for the sake of that, ⟦गां⟧ ⟦न⟧, like a milch cow. As men use her, so. Or else the word for the source is used for its product: like milk and the like. Therefore ⟦ऋतम्⟧ ⟦आघोषथः⟧, you praise the sacrifice.

## SBh 1.151.5.1

O Mitra and Varuṇa, you two, ⟦मही⟧, on the great. In the locative singular there is the long vowel homogeneous with the preceding one. By ⟦ईदूतौ च सप्तम्यर्थे⟧ (⟦पा. सू. १. १. १९⟧) it is pragṛhya.

## SBh 1.151.5.2

⟦अत्र⟧, on this earth, ⟦महिना⟧, by your greatness, the cows which, ⟦वारं⟧, to a desirable place, ⟦ऋण्वथः⟧, you cause to go. This verb has the sense of the causative contained in it. For the activity of going and the like depends on those two, and Mitra and Varuṇa are the presiding deities of heaven and earth. These same ⟦धेनवः⟧, cows, at the time of evening, by your greatness ⟦अरेणवः⟧, faultless, or not lost, through not being carried off by thieves and the like, ⟦तुजः⟧, givers of milk (the root tuj has the sense of giving), ⟦सद्मन⟧, in the abode, the cow-pen, ⟦आ⟧, have taken their place, or come. Because a preverb is heard, a suitable verb is to be supplied. ⟦ताः⟧, the cows that have been made to do thus, ⟦उपरताति⟧, that is, ⟦उपरतातौ⟧.

## SBh 1.151.5.3

There is elision of the ending of the seventh case. In the mid-region, which has the spreading of the clouds, when it is covered with clouds, ⟦सूर्यं⟧, the impeller of all, or the one of good valour, of excellent ability, according to another śruti, ⟦सुवीर्यो मर्या यथा गोपायत इति तत्सूर्यस्य सूर्यत्वम्⟧ (⟦तै. ब्रा. २. २. १०⟧), "'As one of good valour, O men, he guards': that is why Sūrya is Sūrya"; towards the Āditya ⟦स्वरन्ति⟧, they make a sound, for the sake of roaming at ease. The root is ⟦स्वृ⟧, in the senses of sounding and of paining; it belongs to the first class.

## SBh 1.151.5.4

At what time? That is stated. ⟦निम्रुचः⟧ ⟦उषसः⟧: at all the times of evening and at the times of dawn too. There is an illustration of this: ⟦तक्ववीरिव⟧. ⟦तक्वा⟧ is a thief. The man who goes after him, who pursues him: as he cries out, so. Thus the roaming at ease of the cows that are penned in depends on you: this is the praise of the two.

## SBh 1.151.6.1

O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦केशिनीः⟧, the flames of Agni, which have hair, ⟦वां⟧, you two, ⟦ऋताय⟧, for the sake of the sacrifice, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦अनूषत⟧; the meaning is that they honour you by delivering the oblation. Where? That is stated. O ⟦मित्र⟧, O ⟦वरुण⟧, both of you, ⟦यत्र⟧, in which sacrifice, ⟦गातुं⟧, the going, or the place of sacrifice to the gods. Gātu is a name of the earth, because it has been stated, ⟦गातुः पूषा⟧ (⟦नि. १. १. २०⟧).

## SBh 1.151.6.2

⟦अर्चथः⟧, you honour; the meaning is that you accept. And having come, ⟦त्मना⟧, by yourselves alone, ⟦अव⟧ ⟦सृजतं⟧, send forth the rain, facing downwards. Having done so, ⟦धियः⟧, our rites, ⟦पिन्वतं⟧, make to grow: this is the meaning. Having done thus, ⟦विप्रस्य⟧, of the wise sacrificer, ⟦मन्मनां⟧, of the praises that are full of thought, ⟦युवं⟧, you two, ⟦इरज्यथः⟧, are the lords, the masters. The verb irajyati has the sense of lordship, because it has been stated among the names of that, ⟦इरज्यति पत्यते⟧ (⟦नि. २. २१. १⟧).

## SBh 1.151.7.1

⟦यः⟧ ⟦ह⟧, the sacrificer who indeed, ⟦वां⟧, with you two in view, or to you two, ⟦यज्ञैः⟧, with the sacrifices as the occasion, ⟦शशमानः⟧, praising, ⟦दाशति⟧, removes his own ownership of the oblation and the like and brings about your ownership of it. He alone who is ⟦कविः⟧, learned, according to the śruti ⟦येऽनूचानास्ते कवयः⟧ (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. २.२⟧), "Those who are learned are the kavis"; or else ⟦कविर्मेधावी⟧, the kavi is the wise man; ⟦होता⟧, the sacrificer who rightly accomplishes the offering, ⟦मन्मसाधनः⟧, being furnished with the means, such as the esteemed materials and the sacrificial fees, ⟦यजति⟧, performs the Soma sacrifice and the like. ⟦तम्⟧, that sacrificer alone, ⟦सुमतिं⟧, of excellent understanding, ⟦उप⟧, having regard to him, ⟦गच्छथः⟧, you indeed reach. ⟦अध्वरं⟧, his sacrifice, ⟦वीथः⟧, you desire.

## SBh 1.151.7.2

Moreover, ⟦अस्मयू⟧, you two who desire us, ⟦गिरः⟧, the praises, ⟦अच्छ⟧, towards them, ⟦गन्तं⟧, go. The meaning is: having reached me, who am such, accept the praises. Here, although ⟦तम्⟧ appears to be a general reference, yet, because ⟦अस्मयू⟧ is said, it is understood that Dīrghatamas has spoken of himself, in the manner of one not present. Or else: he who gives and he who sacrifices, to him you go and you desire his sacrifice. Therefore, ⟦अस्मयू⟧, you two who desire us, to me, ⟦सुमतिं⟧, of excellent understanding, ⟦अच्छ⟧, towards my ⟦गिरः⟧, praises, go.

## SBh 1.151.8.1

O ⟦ऋतावाना⟧, you who possess the sacrifice, or who possess the water of rain **NOTE**{⟦वृष्दट्युकवन्तौ⟧ is taken as a typo for vṛṣṭyudakavantau, "possessing the water of rain"; is that right?}, Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦युवां⟧, you two, ⟦प्रथमा⟧, the first, ⟦यज्ञैः⟧, with the means of sacrificing, ⟦गोभिः⟧, with the products of the cow, milk and the like, ⟦अञ्जते⟧, the sacrificers make manifest. The meaning is that in the sacrifices they offer the gods' share with milk and the like. Or else ⟦गोभिः⟧ ⟦अञ्जते⟧, they praise. There is an illustration of this: ⟦प्रयुक्तिषु⟧, in undertakings, ⟦मनसो⟧ ⟦न⟧. The genitive is in the sense of the object.

## SBh 1.151.8.2

Like the mind. As men make that manifest first, make it the chief thing, because all activity is preceded by it. Moreover, ⟦वां⟧, to you two, ⟦मन्मना⟧, full of thought, ⟦संयता⟧, going rightly to you alone, with such a mind, ⟦गिरः⟧, praises, ⟦भरन्ति⟧, they bring about. Since this is so, therefore, being endowed ⟦अदृप्यता⟧, not overbearing, ⟦मनसा⟧, with an excellent mind, ⟦रेवत्⟧, the rite of ours that is rich in wealth, ⟦आशाथे⟧, you reach, you pervade; the meaning is that you give. From the root aś, in liṭ, the augment iṭ is absent, by the statement that the teaching of augments is not invariable **NOTE**{⟦अनित्यभागमशासनम्⟧ is taken as a typo for anityam āgamaśāsanam, "the teaching of augments is not invariable"; is that right?}.

## SBh 1.151.9.1

O Mitra and Varuṇa, you two ⟦रेवत्⟧, rich in wealth, ⟦वयः⟧, food, ⟦दधाथे⟧, both of these you hold. Since this is so, therefore ⟦रेवत्⟧, rich in wealth: because the two are seen together, ⟦वयः⟧ is understood; food distinguished by wealth, O ⟦नरा⟧, leaders, you two ⟦आशाथे⟧, reach; the meaning is that you give it to us. Of what kind? ⟦मायाभिः⟧, by the wisdom of you two, ⟦इतऊति⟧, which has a going hither, towards us, or which has protection from here, ⟦माहिनम्⟧: this is a name for what is great; exceedingly great. Whence comes such ability of you two who are such? To this **NOTE**{⟦इस्यत⟧ is taken as a typo for ity ata, "to this (he says)"; is that right?} he says: ⟦वां⟧, of you two, the godhead, ⟦अहभिः⟧, with the days, that is, with the nights.

## SBh 1.151.9.2

Here by the word for day the night is denoted, because the day is denoted separately by the word dyo. Together with these, ⟦द्यावः⟧, the days, ⟦न⟧ ⟦आनशुः⟧, do not reach it. The meaning is: day and night, both, do not cross it. ⟦उत⟧, and moreover, ⟦सिन्धवः⟧, the rivers, whose nature it is to flow, ⟦न⟧, do not reach it either; likewise ⟦देवत्वं⟧, the godhead, of you two, ⟦पणयः⟧, the Asuras too, ⟦न⟧, did not reach. ⟦मघं⟧, the wealth also, ⟦न⟧, they did not reach. The meaning is that in the three worlds there are none at all who overcome you two.
