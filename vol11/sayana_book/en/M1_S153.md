## SBh 1.153.0.1

⟦यजामहे वाम्⟧: this is the fourteenth **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦चतुर्दशं⟧, "fourteenth", but the sūkta before it (1.152) is called the twelfth, so that "thirteenth" is expected; is this a typo of the Wikisource text?} sūkta, of four Ṛks, of Dīrghatamas, in the triṣṭubh metre, addressed to Mitra and Varuṇa. The Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦यजामहे चतुष्कम्⟧, "'Yajāmahe': a group of four". The application is the one shown by the indications in the text.

## SBh 1.153.1.1

O ⟦घृतस्नू⟧, you who make ghee flow, Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦महः⟧, the great ones, ⟦वां⟧, you two, ⟦यजामहे⟧, we worship, the sacrificers and the officiating priests. Of what kind are we? ⟦सजोषाः⟧, endowed with the same affection. By what means? That is stated. ⟦हव्येभिः⟧, with the sacrificial cake and the like, ⟦नमोभिः⟧, which are marked by obeisances. ⟦अध⟧, and moreover, ⟦यत्⟧, for which reason, ⟦वां⟧, you two, ⟦अध्वर्यवः⟧, our adhvaryus too: here the word na has the sense of "too"; they too, ⟦धीतिभिः⟧, by their own actions, ⟦भरन्ति⟧, nourish; therefore we worship.

## SBh 1.153.2.1

O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦वां⟧, of the sacrifice that belongs to you two, there is the ⟦प्रस्तुतिः⟧, the beginning, only the resolve "I perform it"; ⟦न⟧ ⟦प्रयुक्तिः⟧, the whole performance has not been accomplished. By that much alone, of you two the ⟦धाम⟧, the place of splendour, ⟦अयामि⟧, I reach. And ⟦सुवृक्तिः⟧, one who wins you two over well, I am, because of your acceptance. But ⟦यत्⟧, when, ⟦विदथेषु⟧, in the sacrifices, ⟦वां⟧, to you two, ⟦होता⟧, this adhvaryu who accomplishes the offering, ⟦अनक्ति⟧, comes with the oblation; the meaning is that he makes the offering. Or else ⟦होता⟧, the caller of the gods, the officiating priest of this name, by the śastra and the like ⟦वाम्⟧ ⟦अनक्ति⟧, makes you two manifest. Then, ⟦सूरिः⟧, knowing your greatness, ⟦इयक्षन्⟧, desiring to perform the sacrifice, I, O ⟦वृषणौ⟧, showerers of desires, ⟦वां⟧, of you two, the ⟦सुम्नं⟧, the happiness that belongs to you, characterised by godhead: "I reach" is to be supplied.

## SBh 1.153.3.1

O ⟦मित्रावरुणा⟧, Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦ऋताय⟧, for the sacrifice that belongs to you, ⟦हविर्दे⟧, for the giver of the oblation. By ⟦आतो मनिन्⟧ there is the suffix vic. When the stem is bha, by ⟦आतो धातोः⟧ there is elision of the vowel ā.

## SBh 1.153.3.2

And ⟦जनाय⟧, for the sacrificer, ⟦धेनुः⟧, the gladdening cow, ⟦अदितिः⟧, being not poor, having much milk, ⟦पीपाय⟧, may she swell with milk again and again. From the root pyāy there is the Vedic liṭ in the sense of prayer. By ⟦लिड्यङोश्च⟧ there is the substitution of pī.

## SBh 1.153.3.3

The meaning is: the sacrifice whose oblation is the payasyā and the like, and the sacrificer in the vows of milk and the like. If it is asked "At what time?", to that he says: ⟦यत्⟧, when, ⟦सः⟧, that well-known, ⟦रातहव्यः⟧, king of this name, ⟦सपर्यन्⟧, honouring with praise, ⟦मानुषः⟧ ⟦होता⟧ ⟦न⟧, like the officiating priest of this name who belongs to a man, the sacrificer, ⟦विदथे⟧, in the sacrifice, ⟦वां⟧ ⟦हिनोति⟧, gladdens you two with the oblation and the like. "Then may she swell", he says.

## SBh 1.153.4.1

⟦उत⟧, and moreover, O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦वां⟧, you two, ⟦अन्धः⟧, the food that is to be eaten, in the form of milk or in the form of the sacrificial cake and the like, ⟦देवीः⟧, whose nature it is to shine, ⟦गावः⟧, the cows, ⟦आपश्च⟧, and the waters that make rice and the like grow, ⟦मद्यासु⟧, who are to be gladdened by you, ⟦विक्षु⟧, among the people, who are the sacrificers, for the increase of these, ⟦पीपयन्त⟧, may they cause to swell. ⟦उतो⟧, and moreover, ⟦नः⟧, belonging to us, ⟦अस्य⟧, of this sacrifice, ⟦पूर्व्यः⟧, of former time, ⟦पतिः⟧, the protector, Agni, ⟦दन्⟧, a giver: "may he be" is to be supplied. For all the oblations too are offered in Agni. Since this is so, therefore, O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦वीतं⟧, eat the sacrificial cake and the like. ⟦उस्रियायाः⟧, of the cow that pours forth milk and the like, ⟦पयसः⟧, the milk, ⟦पातम्⟧, drink. Or else: ⟦वीतं⟧, eat the part of the payasyā that has become solid, ⟦पातम्⟧, drink the part that has the form of flowing.
