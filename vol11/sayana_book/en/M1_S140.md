## SBh 1.140.0.1

In the twenty-first anuvāka there are seventeen sūktas. Among them ⟦वेदिषदे⟧ is the first sūkta, of thirteen Ṛks. Its seer is Dīrghatamas, the son of Ucathya; it is addressed to Agni. The last two Ṛks are triṣṭubhs. The rest, by the convention "ending in triṣṭubhs", are jagatīs. The tenth is a triṣṭubh or a jagatī. The convention of the atyaṣṭi metre has ceased. On this the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦वेदिषदे सप्तोना दीर्घतमा औचथ्य आग्नेयं तु द्वित्रिष्टुबन्तं तु त्रिष्टुब्दशमी वा⟧, "The sūkta 'vediṣade' has seven less; Dīrghatamas son of Ucathya; but it is addressed to Agni, and it ends in two triṣṭubhs; the tenth is optionally a triṣṭubh." In the section of the Prātaranuvāka that belongs to Agni, in the jagatī metre, the six sūktas beginning with this one, leaving out the third, are employed. Likewise in the Āśvina-śastra there is the employment of the six sūktas with the third left out.

## SBh 1.140.0.2

And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦वेदिषद इति षण्णां तृतीयमुद्धरेत्⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ४. १३⟧), "Of the six beginning with 'vediṣade' he should take out the third."

## SBh 1.140.11.1

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦इदम्⟧, this oblation of ours, in the form of Soma and the like. Of what kind is it? ⟦दुर्धितात्⟧, than what is badly placed, the hard wood and the like, ⟦अधि⟧, above, ⟦सुधितं⟧, well placed, well given. ⟦मन्मनः⟧, than what is estimable, ⟦प्रियादु चित्⟧, even than what is pleasing to you — the word u is for emphasis; the word cit has the sense of the word "even" — ⟦ते⟧, to you, ⟦प्रेयः⟧, most exceedingly dear, may it be. What then? He says: O Agni, ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦तन्वः⟧, of the body, of the flame kindled by the ghee, the ⟦शुचि⟧, spotless, ⟦शुक्रं⟧, bright, ⟦यत्⟧, lustre which ⟦रोचते⟧, shines, ⟦तेन⟧, together with that, bestowing ⟦रत्नं⟧, what is delightful, gems, pearls and the like, ⟦अस्मभ्यं⟧ ⟦वनसे⟧, favour us. From the root van the middle voice comes by transposition.

## SBh 1.140.12.1

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦उत⟧, and also, ⟦रथाय⟧, for speeding, ⟦गृहाय⟧, for him who is the abode of all, ⟦नः⟧, for our sacrificer, ⟦नावं⟧, a ship that carries across the round of worldly existence, a ship that consists of the Soma sacrifice. For in such passages of scripture as ⟦यज्ञो वै सुतर्मा नौः⟧ (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. १. १३⟧), "the sacrifice indeed is the ship that crosses well", it is praised as having the form of a ship. Of what kind is it? ⟦नित्यारित्रां⟧, furnished with the fixed priests as its wooden implements for pulling the water; ⟦पद्वतीम्⟧ — by the rule ⟦ह्रस्वनुड्भ्याम्⟧, "after a short vowel and after nuṭ", the suffix matup is udātta —

## SBh 1.140.12.2

furnished with feet in the form of the substance, the deity, the mantra and the like. Such a ship, consisting of the sacrifice, ⟦रासि⟧, give, bring about. The root rā has the action of giving, because it is read among the names of that: ⟦दासति राति⟧ (⟦नि. ३. २०. ४⟧). That same ship is further specified. ⟦उत⟧, and also, ⟦या⟧, the ship which makes ⟦अस्माकं⟧ ⟦वीरान⟧, those connected with us, our sons and the like, ⟦मघोनः⟧, possessed of food marked as oblation. ⟦जनांश्च⟧, and us the sacrificers also, the ship which ⟦पारयात्⟧, may carry across, may take over. From the root pāray, in leṭ, there is the augment āṭ.

## SBh 1.140.12.3

May it bring us to the far shore of that which consists of many sorrows such as birth and death, to the world of Brahman, the seat of unsurpassed happiness; because ⟦एष वः पुण्यः सुकृतो ब्रह्मलोकः⟧ (⟦मु. उ. १.३.६⟧), "This is your holy world of Brahman, well made", is what is heard by him who performs sacrifice and the like. And having brought us there thus, ⟦च⟧ ⟦या⟧, the ship which also may bring us to ⟦शर्म⟧, happiness, the unsurpassed Brahman; because it is said ⟦परस्यान्ते कृतात्मानः प्रविशन्ति परं पदम्⟧, "At the end of the para, those whose selves are perfected enter the highest abode." This is by way of producing purity of mind, according to the scripture ⟦विविदिषन्ति यज्ञेन दानेन⟧ (⟦बृ. उ. ४. ४. २२⟧), "they seek to know by sacrifice, by giving".

## SBh 1.140.13.1

O Agni, ⟦नः⟧, our, ⟦उक्थं⟧, śastra, ⟦अभि⟧ ⟦जुगुर्याः⟧, praise, facing it; encourage it; accept it as "well recited".

## SBh 1.140.13.2

The root is ⟦गॄ⟧, in the sense of sounding. In liṅ, by the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧, "variously in the Veda", there is ślu of the class-sign. By the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧, "variously in the Veda", there is the substitution of u.

## SBh 1.140.13.3

Likewise ⟦द्यावाक्षामा⟧, let heaven and earth also encourage it, by providing the means of the sacrifice. Likewise the ⟦सिन्धवः⟧, the rivers, whose nature is to flow, and which are ⟦स्वगूर्ताः⟧, going of their own accord, ⟦गव्यं⟧, what comes from the cow, milk and the like, by way of producing grass and the like for the cows, and ⟦यव्यम्⟧ — this stands for more: the sacrificial cake and the like made from barley, rice and so on — ⟦यन्तः⟧, causing these to reach us, let them encourage it. Likewise the ⟦अरुण्यः⟧, those yoked with red-coloured cows **NOTE**{The text has ⟦अरूणवर्णगोयुक्ताः⟧ with a long ū; is "red-coloured", with short u, meant?}. The possessive suffix is dropped.

## SBh 1.140.13.4

They are the Dawns, which are the means of the redness. Because they are the cause of that, they are called by that word. ⟦दीर्घाहा⟧, all the days, at all time, ⟦इषम्⟧, what is desired, food, sap and the like, and ⟦वरं⟧, what is to be chosen, the best, other means of sacrifice also, ⟦वरन्त⟧, let them cause to reach us. The root is ⟦वृङ् संभक्तौ⟧, vṛ in the sense of sharing. From it, with the causative sense included, there is the Vedic laṅ. By transposition there is the class-sign śap. Let them accomplish it. All this is from the favour of Agni; so, because it is a praise of Agni alone, its being addressed to Agni is not contradicted.
