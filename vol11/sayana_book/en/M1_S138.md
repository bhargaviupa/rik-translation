## SBh 1.138.0.1

The fifth hymn, ⟦प्रप्र पूष्णः⟧, of four Ṛks, is Parucchepa's, in the atyaṣṭi metre, and has Pūṣan as its deity. It is stated in the Anukramaṇī: ⟦प्रप्र चतुष्कं पौष्णम्⟧, "'prapra', a set of four, addressed to Pūṣan." Its ritual use is to be gathered from the indications in its wording.

## SBh 1.138.1.1

⟦तुविजातस्य⟧, of him who has arisen for the sake of many sacrificers, ⟦अस्य⟧, of this, ⟦पूष्णः⟧, nourisher, the god of this name, ⟦तवसः⟧ ⟦महित्वं⟧, the greatness of the strength, ⟦प्रप्र⟧ ⟦शस्यते⟧, is praised by all. The repetition serves to fill the pāda.

## SBh 1.138.1.2

⟦न⟧ ⟦तन्दते⟧: no one harms that either. Moreover ⟦अस्य⟧, of this Pūṣan, ⟦स्तोत्रं⟧, the praise, ⟦न⟧ ⟦तन्दते⟧, does not harm, does not cease. The meaning is that every person, too, praises him; the purport is, because he is the nourisher of the world. For this reason ⟦अहं⟧, I too, the sacrificer, ⟦सुम्नयन्⟧, desiring happiness, ⟦अर्चामि⟧, praise him. Of what kind is he? ⟦अन्त्यूतिम्⟧, one whose protection is close at hand. By the rule ⟦कादिलोपो बहुलम्⟧, "the elision of ka and the like takes place variously", the syllable ka is elided.

## SBh 1.138.1.3

The meaning is: one who grants a boon immediately after the praise. ⟦मयोभुवं⟧, the producer of happiness, or the one who brings it into being. ⟦यः⟧, which Pūṣan, ⟦मखः⟧, possessing the sacrifice: the suffix that has the sense of possession is elided.

## SBh 1.138.1.4

He ⟦आयुयुवे⟧, joins on all sides, ⟦मनः⟧, the mind, ⟦विश्वस्य⟧, of every praiser, the officiating priest; the purport is, because he grants boons quickly. Moreover ⟦देवः⟧, shining, this one ⟦आयुयुवे⟧, joins, ⟦मखः⟧, the sacrifice, up to its completion. Or else: if it is asked what comes to the sacrificer when the mind of everyone is joined, it is said: ⟦मखो⟧, the one who carries the sacrifice through, this god Pūṣan, ⟦आयुयुवे⟧, joins on all sides, the mind of the sacrificer. That it is the mind of the sacrificer is obtained from the force of the context. The form is the Vedic liṭ of the root yu.

## SBh 1.138.2.1

O ⟦पूषन्⟧, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦अजिरं⟧, who go towards the place of sacrifice, ⟦यामनि⟧, when going is the occasion, ⟦स्तोमेभिः⟧, with praises, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦कृण्वे⟧, I make eminent. There is an illustration of this: ⟦यामनि⟧, in swift going, ⟦अजिरं⟧ ⟦न⟧, like a swift-going horse; as it is praised, so. Moreover ⟦यथा⟧, so that, ⟦ऋणवः⟧, you may go, ⟦मृधः⟧, with a view to battles, so I make you eminent. Or else: so that you ⟦ऋणवः⟧, may go towards our sacrifice. The root is ⟦ऋणु गतौ⟧, ṛṇu in the sense of going; it is of the class beginning with tan; in leṭ there is the augment aṭ.

## SBh 1.138.2.2

So I make you. Moreover ⟦मृधः⟧, the harmful fighters, in battle, ⟦पीपरः⟧, you carry to the far shore. It is a form of the causative root pāray in luṅ, with caṅ.

## SBh 1.138.2.3

There is an illustration of this: ⟦उष्ट्रो⟧ ⟦न⟧. As a camel, a carrier, carries a load across, so ⟦मृधः⟧, from battle, ⟦पीपरः⟧, you carry to the far shore. For this reason ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦मयोभुवं⟧, who bring happiness into being, ⟦देवं⟧, of a shining nature, Pūṣan, ⟦मर्त्यः⟧, I, a man whose nature is to die, ⟦हुवे⟧, call. For what purpose? ⟦सख्याय⟧, for being named as an equal, for the doing of what is dear. Being thus called, ⟦अस्माकमाङ्गूषान्⟧, our loud-sounding praises — ⟦आङ्गूषः स्तोम आघोषः⟧, "āṅgūṣa is a praise, a loud sound", says Yāska (⟦५.११⟧) — ⟦द्युम्निनस्कृधि⟧, make possessed of brilliance. Or else: make the praises possessed of fame or possessed of food; the meaning is, pleased by our praise, do you so. Moreover ⟦वाजेषु⟧, in battles, ⟦द्युम्निनः⟧, possessed of food, ⟦कृधि⟧, make us; the meaning is, having conquered our enemies, place their food in me.

## SBh 1.138.3.1

O ⟦पूषन्⟧, god, ⟦यस्य⟧ ⟦ते⟧, of you who are well known as a protector, ⟦सख्ये⟧, when there is friendship, the doing of good, ⟦क्रत्वा⟧ ⟦चित⟧, by action, by an excellent rite alone. By the rule ⟦जसादिषु च्छन्दसि वावचनम्⟧, "in the rules beginning with that on jas an option is to be stated for the Veda", the substitute nā is absent.

## SBh 1.138.3.2

⟦सन्तः⟧, being those who gladden you, ⟦विपन्यवः⟧, those whose nature it is to praise in a special way. Or else: this is a name for the wise. The wise, the sacrificers, ⟦अवसा⟧, being protected by the protection given by you, ⟦बुभुज्रिरे⟧, enjoy enjoyments. ⟦इति⟧, in this very way, always, ⟦क्रत्वा⟧, by the rite, ⟦बुभुज्रिरे⟧, they enjoy; or, all are protected. From the root bhuj, in the liṭ of the Veda, there is the augment ruṭ by the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧.

## SBh 1.138.3.3

⟦तां⟧, such, ⟦नवीयसीं⟧, newer, or praiseworthy, protection, ⟦अनु⟧, following, that is, immediately after the protection, we ⟦ईमहे⟧, ask, you for ⟦नियुतं⟧ ⟦रायः⟧, riches of that number. Moreover, O ⟦उरुशंस⟧, you who are to be praised in many ways, you, ⟦अहेळमानः⟧, not being angry, being well-disposed towards us, ⟦सरी⟧ ⟦भव⟧, be one to be approached by us. From the root sṛ there is the uṇādi suffix ini; the first syllable is udātta.

## SBh 1.138.3.4

Moreover, you, ⟦वाजेवाजे⟧, in all battles too, ⟦सरी⟧ ⟦भव⟧, be of a going nature; the meaning is, be one who goes in front.

## SBh 1.138.4.1

O ⟦अजाश्व⟧, Pūṣan. ⟦अजाश्वेति पूषणमाह⟧ (⟦निरु. ४. २५⟧), "by 'ajāśva' he speaks of Pūṣan", says Yāska. ⟦नः⟧, our, ⟦अस्याः⟧, for this: the sixth case stands in the sense of the fourth.

## SBh 1.138.4.2

⟦सातये⟧, for gain, ⟦अहेळमानः⟧, not being angry, not showing disregard, and being ⟦ररिवान्⟧, a giver, ⟦सु⟧, well, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦भुवः⟧, be close at hand.

## SBh 1.138.4.3

From the root rā there is the suffix kvasu in place of the Vedic liṭ. Even though the designation pada is absent, a Vedic separation of the parts is seen in ⟦पपिवांसं⟧, tasthivāṃsam and the like. ⟦ऊ⟧ fills the pāda.

## SBh 1.138.4.4

Moreover, of us ⟦श्रवस्यताम्⟧, who desire food, O ⟦अजाश्व⟧, you who are such that goats alone stand for you in the place of horses — this is a name of Pūṣan — and being a giver, be close at hand. ⟦अजाः पूष्णः⟧ (⟦नि. १. १५, ५⟧), "goats are Pūṣan's", says Yāska. ⟦ओ⟧ is a single particle made up of a combination of two particles. O ⟦दस्म⟧, destroyer of enemies, ⟦पूषन्⟧, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦ओ⟧ ⟦षु⟧ ⟦ववृतीमहि⟧, may we cause to turn, from every side indeed and thoroughly. From the root vṛt, with the sense of the causative included in it, in liṅ, ślu takes the place of śap by the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧.

## SBh 1.138.4.5

The meaning is: we turn you back from going to another sacrifice. By what means? That is said: ⟦साधुभिः⟧ ⟦स्तोमेभिः⟧, with praises able to gladden you. Moreover, O Pūṣan, ⟦आघृणे⟧, you who make the rain flow on all sides; or else, ⟦आघृणे⟧, you who bring, who accept, the oblations: ⟦नहि⟧ ⟦अतिमन्ये⟧, I do not at all show you disrespect. Not merely a not overstepping; but ⟦ते⟧ ⟦सख्यं⟧, your friendship, your being a doer of good, ⟦नहि⟧ ⟦अपह्नुवे⟧, I do not at all deny **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦नैवापयामि⟧ as the gloss of ⟦अपह्नुवे⟧; the verb is doubtful, and "I do not at all deny" is translated from the context.}, I do not give up; the meaning is, I proclaim it on every side.
