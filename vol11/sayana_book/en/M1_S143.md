## SBh 1.143.0.1

The fourth**NOTE**{SBh 1.143.0.1: the text reads ⟦चतुर्थे⟧ (locative) beside the neuter nominative of "sūkta"; translated as caturtham, "the fourth sūkta"; a typo?} sūkta, beginning ⟦प्र तव्यसीम्⟧, has eight Ṛks and is that of Dīrghatamas. And so it is stated in the Anukramaṇī: ⟦प्र तब्यसीमष्टावाग्नेयं तु तत् त्रिष्टुबन्तम्⟧, "pra tavyasīm**NOTE**{SBh 1.143.0.1: the Anukramaṇī quotation reads ⟦तब्यसीम⟧ with b; translated as tavyasīm, the opening word of the sūkta; a typo?}: eight Ṛks; but that is addressed to Agni; it ends in a Triṣṭubh." Because ⟦तु तत्⟧, "but that", is said, Agni is the deity of the group of eight sūktas that begins with this one. The last Ṛk is a Triṣṭubh; the rest, by the convention about what "ends in a Triṣṭubh", are Jagatīs. Its use in the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra has been stated. In the Agniṣṭoma, in the Āgnimāruta-śastra, this sūkta is the one addressed to Jātavedas in which the nivid is set. In the section beginning ⟦अथ यथेतम्⟧ it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦प्र तव्यसीं नव्यसीमापो हि ष्ठेति तिस्रः⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ५. २०⟧), "The sūkta pra tavyasīṃ navyasīm; the three Ṛks beginning āpo hi ṣṭha." On the first day of the Daśarātra too it is the sūkta to Jātavedas in which the nivid is set, and on the middle day of the Aśvamedha too it is the one in which the nivid is set. And so there is the teacher's extension of the rule: ⟦ऐकाहिकान्युपसंशस्य तेषु निविदो दध्यादेवमेवाग्निमारुते⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. १०. १०⟧), "Having recited in addition the sūktas of the one-day rite, he should set the nivids in them; in just the same way in the Āgnimāruta."

## SBh 1.143.1.1

I, ⟦अग्नये⟧, for Agni, ⟦तव्यसीं⟧, that is tavīyasīm, which increases him exceedingly. The root tav, which has the sense of growth, is a root taught only in a sūtra. After this root, when it ends in the suffix tṛc, there is the suffix īyasun by ⟦तुश्छन्दसि⟧, the rule for a word in tṛ in the Veda. By ⟦तुरिष्ठेमेयःसु⟧, the rule for tṛ before iṣṭhan, imanic and īyasun, the tṛ is elided. The elision of the long ī is Vedic.

## SBh 1.143.1.2

⟦नव्यसीं⟧, newer, unprecedented, ⟦धीतिं⟧, the rite marked as a sacrifice, the rite that has the qualities stated, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦भरे⟧, I perform excellently. Likewise ⟦वाचो⟧ ⟦मतिं⟧, the rite in the form of praise, which has the character stated, I perform. For what kind of Agni? ⟦सूनवे⟧, for the son, ⟦सहसः⟧, of strength. Moreover, ⟦यः⟧, the Agni who is ⟦अपां⟧ ⟦नपात्⟧, the grandson of them. ⟦अद्भ्यः ओषधयः ओषधीभ्योऽग्निः⟧, "from the waters the herbs, from the herbs Agni": thus Agni is the grandson. Or else: he who does not make the waters fall; the sense is: because in the form of the fire of lightning he is the one who sends the rain. Likewise ⟦प्रियः⟧, he who gladdens the sacrificer, or who is dearest to him, ⟦होता⟧, his accomplisher of the offering, that Agni, being ⟦ऋत्वियः⟧, one whose time has come, for whom the moment of giving has arrived, ⟦पृथिव्यां⟧, on the earth marked as the vedi, together with ⟦वसुभिः⟧, riches such as cows, which are fit for dwelling, ⟦न्यसीदत्⟧, sits down firmly.

## SBh 1.143.2.1

⟦सः⟧, that aforesaid ⟦अग्निः⟧, Agni, ⟦जायमानः⟧, being produced from the two kindling-sticks, or having appeared in the pieces of wood, at that very time, ⟦परमे⟧, in the excellent ⟦व्योमनि⟧, place of the vedi, which is possessed of manifold protection, first ⟦आविः⟧ ⟦अभवत्⟧, became visible, ⟦मातरिश्वने⟧, to Vāyu, who moves in the mid-region; because this is well known from other Vedic texts such as ⟦त्वमग्ने प्रथमो मातरिश्वन आविर्भव⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. १. ३१.३⟧), "You, O Agni, the first, become manifest to Mātariśvan." The meaning is: he blazed up through contact with the wind. Or else: he who breathes, who is active, in the "mātṛ", the maker of the fruit, that is the sacrifice, is Mātariśvan, the sacrificer. For his sake. Moreover, of Agni ⟦समिधानस्य⟧, who is growing well by means of the fuel, ⟦मज्मना⟧: this is a name of strength; by the strong ⟦क्रत्वा⟧, deed, act, the activity of flaming and the like, ⟦शोचिः⟧ ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦अरोचयत्⟧, lit up exceedingly, ⟦द्यावा⟧ ⟦पृथिवी⟧, both heaven and earth. The word ⟦शोचिः⟧ in the middle is Vedic. Or it is to be construed as: of him who is kindled by a strong act. The meaning is: of him who is being kindled by the powerful activity of kindling and the like.

## SBh 1.143.3.1

⟦अस्य⟧, of this Agni who is being praised, ⟦त्वेषाः⟧, the lights, are ⟦अजराः⟧, free from old age, undecayed; the meaning is: unceasing. By the rule beginning ⟦नञो जरमर⟧ the first syllable of the latter member is udātta.

## SBh 1.143.3.2

Likewise ⟦भानवः⟧, the rays, ⟦अस्य⟧, of this Agni, ⟦सुप्रतीकस्य⟧, whose face is beautiful. Since the light has already been mentioned, the sparks are to be understood here. And they are ⟦सुसंदृशः⟧, those that see well, rightly; the meaning is: spread on every side. ⟦सुद्युतः⟧, shining well on every side. Likewise, of this Agni, ⟦भात्वक्षसः⟧: ⟦त्वक्ष⟧ is a name of strength, because ⟦त्वक्षः शर्धः⟧ (⟦नि. २. ९, ६⟧) is read among the names of strength; whose strength is shining. ⟦अक्तुः⟧: this is a name of the night**NOTE**{SBh 1.143.3.2: the text reads ⟦सत्रिनामैतत्⟧; translated as rātrināmaitat, "this is a name of the night"; a typo?}, because it is stated among the names of it: ⟦अक्तुः ऊर्म्या⟧ (⟦नि. १. ७. ४⟧).

## SBh 1.143.3.3

The nominative is in the sense of the accusative. ⟦अति⟧, having passed beyond, the aktu, the darkness of night that anoints the world, ⟦सिन्धवः⟧, flowing, pervading everywhere, ⟦अससन्तः⟧, not sleeping in their own activities, unceasing, and for that very reason ⟦अजराः⟧, ⟦न⟧ ⟦रेजन्ते⟧, they do not tremble. In burning, cooking and the like they do not waver, or they are not made to waver by others. Or: the word ⟦न⟧ expresses a comparison. ⟦भात्वक्षसो⟧, of the Sun, of such a one whose strength is light itself**NOTE**{SBh 1.143.3.3: the text reads ⟦भा एवं त्वक्षो बलं यस्य⟧; translated as bhā eva, "light itself"; is ⟦एवं⟧ a typo for eva?}; ⟦सिन्धवो⟧ ⟦न⟧, like the rays. As these, whose nature is to flow, which are pervading, having passed beyond the aktu, the darkness that breaks**NOTE**{SBh 1.143.3.3: the text here reads ⟦भञ्जकं तमः⟧, "the darkness that breaks", where the same unit above has ⟦जगदञ्जकं⟧, "that anoints the world"; is ⟦भञ्जकं⟧ a typo for añjakam?}, tremble without sleeping, so the lights, of the character described, of this Agni of shining strength too tremble everywhere; the meaning is: they pervade.

## SBh 1.143.4.1

⟦विश्ववेदसं⟧, possessed of all wealth. ⟦वेद⟧ is a name of wealth, because ⟦वेदः वरिवा⟧ (⟦नि. २. १०. ४⟧) is read among the names of it. Such an Agni, ⟦यम्⟧, whom ⟦भृगवः⟧, those born in the gotra of Bhṛgu, the roasters of sin, ⟦पृथिव्याः⟧, of the vedi: the word for the whole is used for a part. Or, by the Vedic text beginning ⟦एतावती वै पृथिवी⟧ (⟦तै. सं. २. ६. ४. ३⟧), "so great indeed is the earth", the vedi is the earth. In the navel of it, on the uttaravedi, ⟦मज्सना⟧**NOTE**{SBh 1.143.4.1: the bhāṣya cites the word as ⟦मज्सना⟧, while the Ṛk and the Pada-pāṭha have majmanā; a typo?}, with the strength, as the cause, ⟦भुवनस्य⟧, of the multitude of beings, ⟦आ⟧, face to face, ⟦ईरिरे⟧, set in motion, established: ⟦तम्⟧, that ⟦अग्निं⟧, Agni, ⟦स्वे⟧, in his own ⟦दमे⟧, house, on the uttaravedi**NOTE**{SBh 1.143.4.1: the text reads ⟦उत्तवेद्यां⟧; translated as uttaravedyām, "on the uttaravedi", as earlier in the unit; a typo?}, ⟦गीर्भिः⟧, with praises, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦हिनुहि⟧, reach.

## SBh 1.143.4.2

The root is ⟦हि गतौ वृद्धौ च⟧, hi in the senses of going and of growing. By the statement that the rule "and after the u of a suffix" is optional in the Veda,

the ending hi is not elided. ⟦यः⟧, the Agni who, being ⟦एकः⟧, the chief, or he alone, ⟦राजति⟧, is the lord ⟦वस्वः⟧, of wealth, of riches such as cows. "To give it" is to be supplied. ⟦राजति⟧ has the sense of lordship, because ⟦क्षियति राजति⟧ (⟦नि. २. २१. ४⟧) is read among the names of it. For this there is a comparison: ⟦वरुणो⟧ ⟦न⟧, like the Sun, who wards off. As he rules over everything, so does Agni.

## SBh 1.143.5.1

⟦यः⟧, the Agni who, ⟦वराय⟧, for warding off, for restraining, ⟦न⟧, is not possible. On this three comparisons are stated. ⟦मरुतां⟧ ⟦स्वनः⟧ ⟦इव⟧, like the sound of the Maruts: as that cannot be seized, so he. Likewise ⟦सेनेव⟧, like an army, ⟦सृष्टा⟧, let loose by a powerful one for the destruction of the enemies: as that cannot be held back by others, so he. Likewise ⟦यथा⟧, as, ⟦दिव्या⟧, the heavenly, that which is in the sky, ⟦अशनिः⟧, thunderbolt, simply falls and is not averted, so he. He shows that there is such power. This ⟦अग्निः⟧, Agni, ⟦तिगितैः⟧, with whetted, sharpened.

## SBh 1.143.5.2

The change of the final sound is Vedic. ⟦जम्भैः⟧, with teeth, with flames that take the place of teeth, ⟦अत्ति⟧, he eats those who oppose us. Likewise ⟦भर्वति⟧, he injures.

## SBh 1.143.5.3

The root is ⟦भर्व हिंसायाम्⟧, bharv in the sense of injuring. But Yāska says: ⟦भर्वतिरत्तिकर्मा⟧ (⟦निरु. ९, २३⟧), "bharvati has the sense of eating". Although the meaning of both atti and bharvati is eating alone, still a subordinate difference between them is to be understood. For this there is a comparison: ⟦योधो⟧ ⟦न⟧, like a hero who strikes. As he eats ⟦शत्रून्⟧, the enemies, so Agni. Moreover, ⟦सः⟧, that Agni, ⟦न्यृञ्जते⟧, adorns thoroughly, the meaning being that he burns, ⟦वना⟧, the forests, the masses of trees and the like**NOTE**{SBh 1.143.5.3: the text reads ⟦वृक्षदिसमूहान्⟧; translated as vṛkṣādisamūhān, "masses of trees and the like"; a typo?}. ⟦ऋञ्जतिः प्रसाधनकर्मा⟧, "ṛñjati has the sense of adorning", says Yāska.

## SBh 1.143.6.1

May this ⟦अग्निः⟧, Agni, ⟦कुवित्⟧, many times, ⟦असत्⟧, be ⟦वीः⟧, one who desires ⟦उचथस्य⟧, the uktha, the praise, ⟦नः⟧, of ours.

## SBh 1.143.6.2

After the root as, in leṭ, there is the augment aṭ. Or**NOTE**{SBh 1.143.6.2: the text reads ⟦यहा⟧; translated as yadvā, "or"; a typo?}: ⟦उचथस्य⟧ is to be connected with "of us", who are sprung from the gotra of the great ṛṣi of this name. Likewise ⟦वसुः⟧, he who makes all dwell, or who stands in the place of wealth, ⟦वसुभिः⟧, with riches that make one dwell, ⟦आवरत्⟧, may he cover, ⟦कामम्⟧, exceedingly, or what is wished for, ⟦कुवित्⟧, very abundantly. The meaning is: by granting what is wished for may he bring desire to an end. After the root vṛ, in leṭ, there is the augment aṭ. The elision of the class-sign is Vedic**NOTE**{SBh 1.143.6.2: the text reads ⟦छान्हसो⟧; translated as chāndaso, "Vedic"; a typo?}.

## SBh 1.143.6.3

May this Agni, being ⟦चोदः⟧, the impeller of us in the rites, ⟦कुवित्⟧, much, ⟦तुतुज्यात्⟧, hasten, that is, impel, ⟦धियः⟧, the rites, ⟦सातये⟧, for gain. The root tuj has the sense of impelling. The ślu in place of śap is Vedic.

## SBh 1.143.6.4

⟦तम्⟧, that Agni, ⟦शुचिप्रतीकं⟧, of beautiful limbs, of beautiful flames, ⟦अया⟧ ⟦धिया⟧, with this understanding in the form of praise, ⟦गृणे⟧, I utter; the meaning is: I praise.

## SBh 1.143.7.1

⟦घृतप्रतीकं⟧, whose beginning is ghee, because he is offered to with portions of ghee in the fore-offerings and the like. Or: a pratīka is a limb; the meaning is: of blazing flame. Moreover, ⟦धूर्षदं⟧, sitting at the yoke, at the carrying through, the one who carries the sacrifice through, ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, of the sacrifice, ⟦वः⟧, that belongs to you; ⟦अग्निं⟧, Agni, ⟦मित्रं⟧ ⟦न⟧, like a friend, ⟦समिधानः⟧, one who is lighting him with pieces of fuel, ⟦ऋञ्जते⟧, adorns. ## SBh 1.143.7.2
