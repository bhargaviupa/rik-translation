## SBh 1.157.0.1

In the twenty-second anuvāka there are eight sūktas. Of these the first sūkta, beginning ⟦अबोध्यग्निर्ज्मः⟧, is of six Ṛks, of Dīrghatamas, and addressed to the Aśvins. Because of the word tu in ⟦जागतं तु⟧, "but in jagatī", at the earlier place, this one too is in the jagatī metre. The last two Ṛks are triṣṭubhs. The Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦अबोधि षळाश्विनं त्वन्त्ये त्रिष्टुभौ⟧, "'Abodhi': six, addressed to the Aśvins; but the last two are triṣṭubhs". Its employment is in the jagatī metre of the Prātaranuvāka and of the Āśvina-śastra. It is laid down in the section ⟦अथाश्विनः⟧, "now the one of the Aśvins": ⟦अबोध्यग्निर्ज्म  एष स्य भानुः⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ४. १५⟧), which names the sūkta beginning "abodhy agnir jmaḥ" and the one beginning "eṣa sya bhānuḥ".

## SBh 1.157.1.1

This ⟦अग्निः⟧, Agni, having been set in place, being ⟦ज्मः⟧, connected with jmā, with the earth characterised as the altar, ⟦अबोधि⟧, has been awakened. Jmā is a name of the earth, because it is stated among its names in ⟦ग्मा ज्मा⟧ (⟦नि. १.१.३⟧), "gmā, jmā". Moreover, ⟦सूर्यः⟧, the sun, ⟦उदेति⟧, rises. The ⟦मही⟧, the great, ⟦उषाः⟧, dawn, by ⟦अर्चिषा⟧, by her excellent lustre, being ⟦चन्द्रा⟧, gladdening to living beings, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦आवः⟧, drives the darknesses away. Of the root vas in luṅ there is the Vedic **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦छान्दुसः⟧; chāndasaḥ, "Vedic", is translated: is it a typo?} elision of cli. The augment āṭ is by ⟦छन्दस्यपि दृश्यते⟧, "it is seen in the Veda too".

## SBh 1.157.1.2

Since this dawn rises, and since the sun rises, and since this Agni is awakened, for this reason, O Aśvins, the ⟦रथं⟧, the chariot that belongs to you, ⟦यातवे⟧, for going to the place of sacrifice to the gods, ⟦आयुक्षाताम्⟧, yoke with two asses, facing this way. Likewise may ⟦सविता⟧, the approver of every act, the ⟦देवः⟧, the god, the ⟦जगत्⟧, that which moves, the multitude of living beings, ⟦पृथक्⟧, in accordance with the act of each, ⟦प्रासावीत्⟧, impel, give his approval to.

## SBh 1.157.2.1

O ⟦अश्विना⟧, Aśvins, ⟦यत्⟧, when the ⟦वृषणं⟧, the one that rains down rain and the like, the ⟦रथं⟧, the chariot, ⟦युञ्जाथे⟧, you yoke, then ⟦नः⟧ ⟦क्षत्रम्⟧, our strength, or the Kṣatriya class, with ⟦घृतेन⟧, with water, with ⟦मधुना⟧, with what is sweet, ⟦उक्षतं⟧; the meaning is, make it grow. Or else: sprinkle it with ghṛta, with that which has the form of flowing, with madhu, with the sweet nectar; the meaning is, with the madhu that is in your chariot make our strength grow. That the chariot of the Aśvins is full of madhu is well known in such passages as ⟦मधुवाहनो रथः⟧, "the chariot that carries madhu". Moreover, in ⟦अस्माकं⟧ ⟦पृतनासु⟧, in our own offspring in the form of men, sons, servants and the like, the ⟦ब्रह्म⟧, the lustre of the brahman, ⟦जिन्वतम्⟧.

## SBh 1.157.2.2

Pṛtanā is a name of men, because it is stated among their names in ⟦विवस्वन्तः पृतनाः⟧ (⟦नि. २. ३. २५⟧), "vivasvantaḥ, pṛtanāḥ". Or else: among the pṛtanās, among the offspring of others, the brahma, their abundant food, as ours, ⟦जिन्वतं⟧, gladden. Brahma is a name of food, because it is stated among its names in ⟦ब्रह्म वर्चः⟧ (⟦नि. २. ७. २५⟧), "brahma, varcaḥ". And ⟦वयं⟧, we, in ⟦शूरसाता⟧, in śūrasāti, in the battle that is attended by the striking and the like of heroes, ⟦धना⟧, their riches of many kinds, ⟦भजेमहि⟧, may we obtain.

## SBh 1.157.3.1

Let the ⟦रथः⟧, the chariot, of ⟦अश्विनोः⟧, of the Aśvins, ⟦अर्वाङ्⟧, facing towards us, ⟦यातु⟧, go. Of what kind? ⟦त्रिचक्रः⟧, furnished with three wheels; ⟦मधुवाहनः⟧, the carrier of madhu; ⟦जीराश्वः⟧, having swift-going horses; ⟦सुष्टुतः⟧, for that very reason praised by us; ⟦त्रिवन्धुरः⟧, having three pieces of timber, low and raised. The vandhura is the place where the charioteer rests. ⟦मघवा⟧, wealthy, and for that very reason ⟦विश्वसौभगः⟧, endowed with all good fortune. May such a chariot of the Aśvins, for ⟦नः⟧, for our ⟦द्विपदे⟧, offspring, sons and the like, and for the ⟦चतुष्पदे⟧, the cattle, ⟦शं⟧, happiness, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦वक्षत्⟧, bring here. The augment aṭ comes to the root vah in leṭ; and there is sip.

## SBh 1.157.4.1

O ⟦अश्विना⟧, Aśvins, ⟦युवं⟧, you two, ⟦नः⟧, our ⟦ऊर्जं⟧, strength, or the food that is able to give life, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦वहतं⟧, make reach us. Moreover, ⟦नः⟧, us, with ⟦मधुमत्या⟧, endowed with sweetness, ⟦कशया⟧, with speech in the form of the granting of what is desired, ⟦मिमिक्षतं⟧, sprinkle, gladden. Kaśā is a name of speech, because it is stated among its names in ⟦कशा धिषणा⟧ (⟦नि. १.११.४३⟧), "kaśā, dhiṣaṇā". Or else: with the kaśā, with the whip that strikes the horses, which is madhumatī, possessed of sweet rain, endowed with sweetness, sprinkle us. Madhu is a name of water, because it is stated among its names in ⟦मधु पुरीषम्⟧ (⟦नि. १. १२. ११⟧), "madhu, purīṣam". By the whip, by the striking of the horses, they will come quickly and give the fruit in the form of rain: hence the whip's possessing madhu. Moreover, ⟦आयुः⟧, the length of life, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦तारिष्टं⟧, make grow. The root tṝ preceded by pra has the sense of making grow.

## SBh 1.157.4.2

Likewise ⟦रपांसि⟧ ⟦मृक्षतं⟧, cleanse the sins without remainder. Yāska says: ⟦रपो रिप्रमिति पापनामनी भवतः⟧ (⟦निरु. ४.२१⟧), "rapas and ripra are two names of sin". Moreover, ⟦द्वेषः⟧, what is hateful, the enemy and the like, ⟦सेधतं⟧, destroy. And this is to come about: ⟦सचाभुवा⟧, being together, possessing a being-together in our affairs, ⟦भवतं⟧; the meaning is, may you two come to be of one mind. ## SBh 1.157.5.1

O ⟦अश्विनौ⟧, Aśvins, ⟦युवं⟧ ⟦ह⟧, you two indeed, ⟦जगतीषु⟧ ⟦अन्तः⟧, within those whose nature it is to move, the cows, the ⟦गर्भं⟧, the embryo, ⟦धत्थः⟧, you hold. Not in the cows only, but ⟦युवं⟧, you two, within ⟦विश्वेषु⟧, all, ⟦भुवनेषु⟧, multitudes of beings, hold the embryo; because by way of the rain, in the form of food, you are the nourishers of the seed that is in the womb. Moreover, O ⟦वृषणौ⟧, showerers of desires, ⟦युवं⟧, you two, ⟦अग्निं⟧ ⟦च⟧, Agni, ⟦अपश्च⟧, and the waters, and ⟦वनस्पतीन्⟧, the trees, ⟦ऐरयेथां⟧, have set in motion. Agni for the enjoying of the oblations, and, having accepted them, the rain-waters, and by the waters the herbs, trees and the rest: these you have sent forth for our sake.

## SBh 1.157.6.1

O Aśvins, ⟦युवं⟧, you two, ⟦ह⟧, indeed, by ⟦भेषजेभिः⟧, by remedies, by medicines, since the lexicon says ⟦भेषजौषधभैषज्यान्यगदो जायुः⟧, "bheṣaja, auṣadha, bhaiṣajya, agada, jāyu", and ⟦अगदं तत्रौषधम्⟧, "agada: auṣadha is used in that sense": by these you ⟦स्थः⟧, are, ⟦भिषजा⟧, physicians. The word ha serves to show that this is well known from such passages as ⟦अश्विनौ हि देवानां भिषजौ⟧, "For the Aśvins are the physicians of the gods". ⟦अथो⟧, and also, by ⟦रथ्येभिः⟧, by the horses that draw the chariot, you are ⟦रथ्या⟧, possessors of a chariot. The word ha serves to show that this is well known from such passages as ⟦आ वां रथो अश्विना⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. १. ११८. १⟧), "Hither your chariot, O Aśvins". The particular connection of these two with the chariot is well known in other mantras such as ⟦युवोर्हि पूर्वं सवितोषसो रथमृताय चित्रं घृतवन्तमिष्यति⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. १. ३४. १०⟧), "For before the dawn Savitṛ sends forth your bright chariot, rich in ghee, for the rite".

## SBh 1.157.6.2

⟦अथो⟧, and also, your ⟦क्षत्रं⟧, strength, is ⟦अधि⟧ ⟦ह⟧, exceeding indeed. The word ha is in the sense of what is well known. Since this is so, therefore, O ⟦उग्रा⟧, Aśvins of uplifted power, ⟦यः⟧, the sacrificer who, ⟦हविष्मान्⟧, furnished with oblations such as clarified butter and the sacrificial cake, ⟦वां⟧, to you two, ⟦मनसा⟧, with a mind that depends on you, ⟦ददाश⟧, gives the oblation and the like, him ⟦धत्थः⟧, you uphold; the meaning is, you favour him exceedingly. The purport is: you two, of abundant strength, having mounted the chariot and come to our place of sacrifice to the gods, upholding me, the giver of the oblation, heal us at our sacrifice.

## SBh 1.157.6.3

⟦वेदार्थस्य प्रकाशेन तमो हार्दं निवारयन् । पुमर्थांश्चतुरो देयाद्विद्यातीर्थमहेश्वरः⟧: "Dispelling the darkness of the heart by the illumination of the meaning of the Veda, may Vidyātīrtha-Maheśvara grant the four aims of man." Thus, in the commentary on the Ṛk-saṃhitā, the Vedārthaprakāśa of Mādhava, composed by Sāyaṇācārya, who bears the burden of the empire of the glorious king Vīra Bukka, king of kings, supreme lord, promoter of the Vedic path, the second adhyāya in the second aṣṭaka is finished.
