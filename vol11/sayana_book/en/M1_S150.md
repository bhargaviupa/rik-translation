## SBh 1.150.0.1

⟦पुरु त्वा⟧: this is the tenth sūkta, consisting of three Ṛks; it is of Dīrghatamas, addressed to Agni, in the uṣṇih metre. The Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦पुरु तृचमौष्णिहम्⟧, "'Puru': a group of three Ṛks, in uṣṇih".

## SBh 1.150.0.2

Its application is in the prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra, in the uṣṇih metre. In the section ⟦अथैतस्याः⟧ it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦पुरु त्वा त्वामग्ने⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ४. १३⟧).

## SBh 1.150.1.1

O Agni, ⟦त्वा⟧, to you, ⟦पुरु⟧, much, ⟦वोचे⟧, I speak. Or else the connection is "having given much". The meaning is: I utter wishes such as "Give a son, give wealth". Is it just so, with nothing more? No, he says: since I am ⟦दाश्वान्⟧, one who has given the desired oblation, therefore I speak. It is not to be thought, "How is anything to be given to one who speaks in the way common to others?" For I, O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦तव⟧ ⟦स्वित्⟧, yours alone,

⟦आ⟧, facing you, am ⟦अरिः⟧, one who goes to you, a servant by bringing the oblation and the like. There is an illustration of this. As ⟦महस्य⟧, of a great, ⟦तोदस्येव⟧, as of a master who disciplines, ⟦शरणे⟧, in his house, a slave born in the house or the like lives ⟦आ⟧, on every side, bound, so do I too. Since this is so, therefore I speak of the much that is desired; do you too give all of it: this is the meaning. On this there is the Nirukta: ⟦बहु दाश्वांस्त्वामेवाभिह्वयाम्यरिरमित्र ऋच्छतेः । ईश्वरोऽप्यरिरेतस्मादेव। यदन्यदेवत्या अग्नावाहुतयो हूयन्त इत्येतद्दृष्ट्वैवमवक्ष्यत् तोदस्येव शरण आ महस्य तुदस्येव शरणेऽधि महतः⟧ (⟦निरु. ५. ७⟧), "Having given much, I call upon you alone. Ari is an enemy; the word is from the root meaning to go. A lord too is ari, from this same root. Having seen that the oblations belonging to other deities are offered in Agni, he would have spoken thus. 'As in the house of a great chastiser': as in the house of a great goader."

## SBh 1.150.2.1

In the previous mantra it was said, "I make known the much that I myself desire". Here, however, he prays that no gift is to be given to those who are unlike himself, who are without giving and the like. O Agni, I speak to you in a special way. Because a preverb is heard, a suitable verb is to be supplied. Or else, because it stands near, ⟦वोचे⟧ is carried over. The opposite of what I made known **NOTE**{⟦व्यजिज्ञपन्⟧ is a third person plural, "they made known"; it is translated as the first person singular vyajijñapam, which the context asks for: is that the word meant?} for our sake, that I speak. The meaning is: I say that nothing is to be given to one whose nature is about to be stated. He states their nature. ⟦अनिनस्य⟧, of one who has no lord, of one who makes you no lord; likewise ⟦धनिनः⟧, of one who possesses wealth in full. Since a gift to one who is wealthy already is purposeless, that is forbidden. Or else the meaning is: of one who possesses wealth that is of no use for sacrifice and the like. Moreover ⟦प्रहोषे⟧, in order to offer in an eminent way, ⟦अररुषः⟧, of one who does not give, in the form of the sacrificial fee. Or else the connection is of ⟦प्रहोषे⟧ with ⟦अनिनस्य⟧.

## SBh 1.150.2.2

The meaning is: of one who is not able to offer in an eminent way. Here by the word ⟦इन⟧, "lord", the ability that resides in him is indicated. The word cit has the sense of adding. Moreover ⟦कदा⟧ ⟦चन⟧ ⟦प्रजिगतः⟧, of one who does not at any time at all praise the gods in an eminent way. Here ⟦चन⟧ is a combination of two particles; of these the word ca is used in adding, the word na in negation. Moreover ⟦अदेवयोः⟧, of one who does not desire the gods for himself. The meaning is: I say specially that nothing is to be given to these. By the description of the nature of those who are blamed, since he himself is not such, it comes to be said that this "I speak much" is indeed proper. Or else ⟦चन⟧ is a synonym of the word cit. Even of one who is corrupt in the manner stated, ⟦प्रजिगतः⟧ at some time, who praises you at any time whatever, I speak specially: I say in a special way that to such a one something is to be given. What need is there to say that it is to be given for our sake? This is the purport.

## SBh 1.150.3.1

O Agni, ⟦विप्र⟧, wise one, the ⟦मर्त्यः⟧, mortal, who worships you, ⟦सः⟧, that mortal, the sacrificer, ⟦दिवि⟧, in the world of heaven, becomes ⟦चन्द्रः⟧, one who gladdens all, like the moon. Or else he becomes the moon itself. The Chandogas hand down that sacrificers attain to being the moon: ⟦पितृलोकादाकाशमाकाशाच्चन्द्रमसमेष सोमो राजा⟧ (⟦छा. उ. ५. १०. ४⟧), "From the world of the fathers to space, from space to the moon; this is King Soma"; and also ⟦सोमलोके विभूतिमनुभूय⟧ (⟦प्र. उ. ५.४⟧), "having enjoyed lordly power in the world of Soma". So too in the Muṇḍaka the attaining of the world of heaven is handed down: ⟦एह्येहीति तमाहुतयः सुवर्चसः सूर्यस्य रश्मिभिर्यजमानं वहन्ति⟧, "Saying 'Come, come', the radiant oblations carry that sacrificer by the rays of the sun.

## SBh 1.150.3.2

⟦तं नयन्येताः सूर्यस्य रश्मयो यत्र देवानां पतिरेकोऽधिवासः⟧, "These rays of the sun lead him to where the one lord of the gods has his dwelling", and ⟦एष वः पुण्यः सुकृतो ब्रह्मलोकः⟧ (⟦मु. उ. १. २. ५-६⟧), "This is your holy, well-made world of Brahman". The same man is described further. ⟦महः⟧, even than the great, he is ⟦व्राधन्तमः⟧, the most grown; the meaning is that he is the best even of the other gods. Therefore, O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦ते⟧, yours, ⟦प्रप्रेत्⟧, in an eminent way indeed, ⟦वनुषः⟧, sharers, ⟦स्याम⟧, may we be. Or else: having pleased you, ⟦प्रप्रेत्⟧ ⟦स्याम⟧, may we be eminent indeed.
