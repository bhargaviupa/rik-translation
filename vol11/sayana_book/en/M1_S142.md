## SBh 1.142.0.1

The third sūkta, beginning ⟦समिद्धः⟧, has thirteen Ṛks and is in the Anuṣṭubh metre. Of the first Ṛk the deity is the Agni named Samidh, the kindled Agni. Of the second and those that follow, Tanūnapāt, Narāśaṃsa, Iḷa, Barhis, the divine Doors, Dawn and Night, the two divine hotṛs who are wise, the three goddesses Sarasvatī, Iḷā and Bhāratī, Tvaṣṭṛ, Vanaspati and Svāhākṛti: these are the deities in order. Of the last Ṛk the deity is Indra. "Sarasvatī, Iḷā and Bhāratī" is the explanation of "the three goddesses". On this the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦समिद्ध आप्रिय आनुष्टभमन्त्यैन्द्री⟧, "The sūkta beginning samiddhaḥ consists of Āprī verses; it is in Anuṣṭubh**NOTE**{SBh 1.142.0.1: the Anukramaṇikā quotation reads ⟦आनुष्टभम⟧, translated as ānuṣṭubham ("in Anuṣṭubh"); is the missing vowel a typo of the Wikisource text?}; the last Ṛk is addressed to Indra."

## SBh 1.142.0.2

Here, by Idhma, Tanūnapāt and the rest, which are words for the parts of the sacrifice, the sacrifice alone is spoken of. Therefore that alone is the deity: this is the view of Kātthakya. Agni alone is spoken of by Samidh and the rest. Therefore he alone is the deity: this is the view of Śākapūṇi. In the Nirukta, on the question "of what deity are the fore-offerings and the after-offerings?", other views, such as that they have the metres for their deities, are shown in many ways, and Brāhmaṇa passages and the like are cited in support of them; and then it is settled as the final conclusion that the other views**NOTE**{SBh 1.142.0.2: the text reads ⟦इतरे पक्षी⟧; translated as itare pakṣāḥ, "the other views" (plural); is the long ī a typo?} are statements of praise and that the offerings belong to Agni alone. This is done by the passage beginning ⟦अथ किंदेवताः प्रयाजानुयाजाः⟧ (⟦निरु. ८ . २१⟧), "Now, of what deity are the fore-offerings and the after-offerings?"

## SBh 1.142.0.3

In the animal sacrifice this is the Āprī-sūkta of those born in the gotra of Aṅgiras. In the section beginning ⟦एकादश प्रयाजाः⟧, "eleven fore-offerings", it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦समिद्धो अद्येति सर्वेषां यथऋषि बा⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ३. २⟧), "The sūkta samiddho adya for all, or**NOTE**{SBh 1.142.0.3: the sūtra ends in ⟦बा⟧; translated as vā, "or"; a typo?} according to the ṛṣi." It is so here because "according to the ṛṣi" is mentioned.

## SBh 1.142.0.4

The Āprī-sūktas**NOTE**{SBh 1.142.0.4: the unit begins ⟦अप्रीसूक्तानि⟧; translated as āprīsūktāni, "the Āprī-sūktas"; a typo?} are of three kinds. Among them the four of Vasiṣṭha, of Atri, of Vadhryaśva and of Gṛtsamada have Narāśaṃsa. Those of Medhātithi and of Dīrghatamas and that of the Praiṣas have both Narāśaṃsa and Tanūnapāt. The others have Tanūnapāt. Among them this sūkta, being that of Dīrghatamas, has both.

## SBh 1.142.1.1

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, you who are ⟦समिद्धः⟧, well kindled, and who bear this name, ⟦अद्य⟧, on this day of sacrifice, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦वह⟧, bring to our sacrifice, ⟦देवान्⟧, the gods, the eaters of the oblations, Agni and the rest; ⟦यतस्रुचे⟧, for the sacrificer whose ladle is raised for the sacrifice, for his sake.

## SBh 1.142.1.2

And having brought them, ⟦सुतसोमाय⟧, for him who has pressed the Soma for the sacrifice, ⟦दाशुषे⟧, who has given the oblation. Although the gods are being invoked only now, still he is called ⟦दाशुषे⟧, "one who has given", with regard to an earlier performance or with regard to what is to come. Or, for the sacrificer who is a giver of the oblation, for his sake, ⟦तनुष्व⟧, spread out, ⟦तन्तुं⟧, the sacrifice, which is ⟦पूर्व्यं⟧, good for the former gods, or belonging to former time.

## SBh 1.142.2.1

O ⟦तनूनपात्⟧, Tanūnapāt. On the view of Kātthakya, ⟦तनूनपात् आज्यम्⟧, "Tanūnapāt is the ghee", the cows are the "tanū", because enjoyments arise in them. From them the milk is produced, and from the milk the ghee: thus the ghee is the grandson. The sacrifice is that which possesses it. On the view that it is Agni, the waters are called "tanū". From them come the herbs and trees, and from these Agni: thus he is the grandson. The sense is: from the two kindling-sticks. O Agni who bear this name, of the sacrificer who is ⟦विप्रस्य⟧, wise, ⟦शशमानस्य⟧, who is lauding, praising you, ⟦दाशुषः⟧, who has given the oblation, ⟦मावतः⟧, who is like me.

## SBh 1.142.2.2

By ⟦युष्मदस्मद्भ्यां छन्दसि सादृश्ये⟧, the rule that in the Veda the suffix vatup comes after yuṣmad and asmad in the sense of likeness, there is the suffix vatup. By ⟦आ सर्वनाम्नः⟧, the rule that ā is substituted in a pronoun, there is the long ā.

## SBh 1.142.2.3

⟦उप⟧, having approached, ⟦यज्ञम्⟧, the sacrifice, which is ⟦घृतवन्तं⟧, furnished with dripping ghee, and ⟦मधुमन्तं⟧, furnished with sweetness, possessing it through a sweet substance such as the Soma, ⟦मासि⟧, you fashion it, or you become measured. The meaning is: you stay until its completion. The root is ⟦मा माने⟧, mā in the sense of measuring. It belongs to the second class.

## SBh 1.142.3.1

⟦देवेषु⟧, among the gods, ⟦शुचिः⟧, pure or shining, ⟦पावकः⟧, the purifier of food that is impure, ⟦अद्भुतः⟧, one who, as though he had not been, comes to be at this very moment; the meaning is: a thing of wonder. ⟦देवः⟧, whose nature is to shine, ⟦यज्ञियः⟧, fit to accomplish the sacrifice: such is ⟦नराशंसः⟧, Agni who is to be praised by men, the priests who lead the rite. Having come ⟦दिवः⟧, from the world of heaven, ⟦मध्वा⟧, with sweet juice, ⟦त्रिः⟧ ⟦आ⟧ ⟦मिमिक्षति⟧, he sprinkles three times on all sides ⟦यज्ञम्⟧, our sacrifice; the meaning is that he makes it savoury. Or: three times he joins it with sweet fruit; that is the meaning. On the view of Kātthakya, ⟦नराशंसो यज्ञः⟧, "Narāśaṃsa is the sacrifice", the god who presides over the sacrifice, shining, having come from the world of heaven, three times sprinkles the sacrifice that is being performed. This god Narāśaṃsa "three times, ā": by force of the preverb a fitting verb is supplied: he comes three times. Or he brings the fruit three times. The rest is to be construed as before.

## SBh 1.142.4.1

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, you who are ⟦ईळितः⟧, to be praised by all, and who bear the name Iṭ, ⟦इह⟧, at this sacrifice, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦वह⟧, call hither ⟦इन्द्रम्⟧, Indra, who is ⟦चित्रं⟧, worthy of honour, and ⟦प्रियं⟧, who gladdens us, or who is filled with gladness to accept the oblation. O ⟦सुजिह्व⟧, you whose flame is beautiful through the drinking of ghee, ⟦इयं⟧, this, ⟦मम⟧, my, ⟦मतिः⟧, speech in the form of praise, which is a means of thinking, ⟦वच्यते⟧, is uttered, ⟦अच्छ⟧, to reach, face to face, ⟦त्वा⟧, you; it is our thought itself that is uttered.

## SBh 1.142.4.2

⟦हिः⟧ serves to fill the verse. When the suffix yak follows the root vac, the single substitution of the former vowel by ⟦संप्रसारणाच्च⟧ is made optional by ⟦वा छन्दसि⟧, "optionally in the Veda"; and so, in its absence, there is the semivowel.

## SBh 1.142.4.3

Or else this is to be explained as referring to the sacrifice that presides over the Iḍ, that is, the puroḍāśa and the like. In the same way, in what follows, by the parts of the sacrifice and by the deities connected with the sacrifice the sacrifice alone is spoken of, and so it is to be explained as referring to that.

## SBh 1.142.5.1

⟦यज्ञे⟧, at the Soma sacrifice, which is ⟦स्वध्वरे⟧, possessed of good subordinate sacrifices such as the Prāyaṇīya, the priests, ⟦स्तृणानासः⟧, strewing ⟦बर्हिः⟧, the darbha grass, and ⟦यतस्रुचः⟧, having the juhū and the other vessels held in control, ⟦वृञ्जे⟧, set apart, that is, they make ready, ⟦इन्द्राय⟧, for Indra, ⟦शर्म⟧, a house, a means of happiness, which is ⟦देवव्यचस्तमम्⟧, in the highest degree going to the gods, and ⟦सप्रथः⟧, spread out on every side.

## SBh 1.142.6.1

⟦द्वारो⟧ ⟦देवीः⟧, the deities who preside over the doors of the sacrifice, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦श्रयन्तां⟧, let them resort in various ways, let them be opened. For what purpose? ⟦देवेभ्यः⟧ ⟦प्रयै⟧, for going forth, for the arrival of the gods.

## SBh 1.142.6.2

By ⟦प्रयै रोहिष्यै अव्यथिष्यै⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ३. ४. १०⟧), the rule that sets down the forms prayai, rohiṣyai and avyathiṣyai, the word is laid down ready-made.

## SBh 1.142.6.3

Of what kind are they? ⟦ऋतवृधः⟧, increasers of the sacrifice, worthy of honour; ⟦महीः⟧, great; ⟦पावकासः⟧, purifiers; ⟦पुरुस्पृहः⟧, much to be desired; ⟦असश्चतः⟧, not clinging together; the meaning is: standing far apart from one another.

## SBh 1.142.7.1

⟦भन्दमाने⟧, being praised by all, ⟦उपाके⟧, close to each other, ⟦सुपेशसा⟧, of beautiful form. ⟦पेश⟧ is a name of form. ⟦यह्वी⟧, great, or having the form of offspring, because they are born every day. ⟦यहु⟧ is a name of offspring, because ⟦यहुः सूनुः⟧ (⟦नि. २. २. ११⟧) is read among the names of it. ⟦ऋतस्य⟧ ⟦मातरा⟧, the two makers of the sacrifice. Because the sacrifice is completed through the coming of these two, motherhood of the sacrifice is ascribed to them figuratively. Or it is because the Atirātra and the like are accomplished by a day and a night. ⟦नक्तोषासा⟧, the deities of day and night, distinguished by the qualities stated, ⟦सुमत्⟧, of themselves alone; the meaning is: unasked by us. Or: of themselves, ⟦बर्हिः⟧, on the strewn darbha grass, ⟦आ⟧, on all sides, let them ⟦सीदताम्⟧, sit down. Or ⟦ऋतस्य⟧ is a genitive in the sense of the object: let them come to the sacrifice; and having come to it, let them of themselves alone sit on the barhis, the strewn darbha grass.

## SBh 1.142.8.1

⟦मन्द्रजिह्वा⟧, the two whose flames gladden the gods, ⟦जुगुर्वणी⟧, the two who favour the sacrificers that praise, that laud, intensely. From the root gṝ, "to praise", ending in the elided intensive suffix yaṅ, with the suffix kvip, the form is achieved as a Vedic one. With that as the accompanying word, after the root van there is the suffix in by ⟦छन्दसि वनसनरक्षिमथाम्⟧, the rule for van, san, rakṣ and math in the Veda.

## SBh 1.142.8.2

⟦कवी⟧, wise, of far-reaching sight, ⟦दैव्या⟧ ⟦होतारा⟧, the two hotṛs connected with the gods, this one here and the one whose place is the middle region, both of them; ⟦सिध्रं⟧, which is the means of gaining the fruit. After the root sidh there is the suffix rak by the rule beginning ⟦स्फायितञ्चि⟧.

## SBh 1.142.8.3

⟦दिविस्पृशं⟧, touching the gods who dwell in the world of heaven; the sense is: because the oblation is accepted by them. Such a sacrifice, ⟦इमं⟧, this, ⟦यज्ञम्⟧, sacrifice, ⟦नः⟧, of ours, ⟦अद्य⟧, today, ⟦यक्षताम्⟧, let the two perform, or let them honour; the meaning is: let them carry it through well.

## SBh 1.142.9.1

⟦शुचिः⟧, pure; ⟦मरुत्सु⟧, among those who, by indication through the opposite**NOTE**{SBh 1.142.9.1: the text reads ⟦विरूद्धलक्षणया⟧ with long ū; translated as viruddhalakṣaṇayā, "by indication through the opposite"; a typo?}, do not die, ⟦देवेषु⟧, among the gods; or among the praisers whose nature is to shine, the Maruts, the priests; ⟦अर्पिता⟧, fixed; ⟦होत्रा⟧, she who brings the offering about; ⟦भारती⟧, the speech that belongs to Bharata, the Sun, and whose place is heaven. Likewise ⟦इळा⟧, the speech of the earth, in the form of the praiṣa and the like; and ⟦मही⟧, the great, ⟦सरस्वती⟧: ⟦सर⟧ is a name of water; she who possesses it, the speech of the middle region, in the form of thunder and the like. These three, the deities who preside over the speech of the three places, being ⟦यज्ञियाः⟧, worthy of the sacrifice, ⟦सीदन्तु⟧, let them sit down on ⟦बर्हिः⟧, the strewn grass.

## SBh 1.142.10.1

⟦नः⟧, for us, ⟦तत्⟧, such, ⟦तुरीयं⟧**NOTE**{SBh 1.142.10.1: the bhāṣya cites the word as ⟦तुरीयं⟧, while the Ṛk and the Pada-pāṭha have turīpam; is it a typo of the Wikisource text?}, swiftly pervading, ⟦अद्भुतं⟧, appearing at once, being as though it had not been. Or this is a name of what is great: great in its essence. ⟦पुरु⟧ ⟦वारं⟧, for the sake of many living beings indeed, ⟦अरम्⟧, sufficient, able. Or ⟦पुरु⟧ ⟦वारं⟧ is: much and abundant; the word vā has the sense of "and"; ⟦अरम्⟧, sufficient. ⟦त्मना⟧, by itself, ⟦पुरु⟧.

## SBh 1.142.10.2

This is an adverb, "much". Water of this kind, ⟦त्वष्टा⟧, the maker of rain and the like. The word Tvaṣṭṛ is explained thus by Yāska himself: ⟦त्वष्टा तूर्णमश्नुत इति नैरुक्तास्त्विषेर्वा स्याद्वृद्धिकर्मणस्त्वक्षतेर्वा स्यात्करोतिकर्मणः⟧ (⟦निरु. ८. १३⟧), "Tvaṣṭṛ: he pervades swiftly, say the etymologists; or it may be from the root tviṣ, which means to increase, or it may be from the root tvakṣ, which means to make." The Agni of lightning who bears that name, ⟦अस्मयुः⟧, the god who desires us, ⟦नः⟧ ⟦पोषाय⟧, for our nourishment, ⟦राये⟧, and for prosperity, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦ष्यतु⟧, let him make go in various ways the water of the kind described, which stands ⟦नाभा⟧, in the navel, in the navel-place of the cloud, which holds the rain bound.

## SBh 1.142.10.3

The root is ⟦षोऽन्तकर्मणि⟧, ṣo in the sense of bringing to an end. By ⟦ओतः श्यनि⟧, the rule for o before śyan, the o is elided. By ⟦उपसर्गात्सुनोति⟧, the rule for sunoti and the rest after a preverb, there is the cerebral ṣ. Or: in our ⟦नाभा⟧, in the navel of the sacrifice that belongs to us, on the uttaravedi; ⟦राये⟧: the dative is in the sense of the genitive; for the nourishment, the thriving, of wealth, of property such as cows. The root so with a preverb has the sense of releasing. Let him release the rain of the kind described.

## SBh 1.142.11.1

O ⟦वनस्पते⟧, protector of the forests, god who presides over the sacrificial post, Agni, ⟦अवसृजन्⟧, letting them act freely, setting the priests to work each at his own task, ⟦त्मना⟧, by yourself, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦यक्षि⟧, approach and sacrifice to ⟦देवान्⟧, the gods, the eaters of the oblation. By ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧, "variously in the Veda", the suffix śap is elided.

## SBh 1.142.11.2

⟦देवः⟧, shining, ⟦मेधिरः⟧, possessed of wisdom, ⟦अग्निः⟧, the Āhavanīya fire, ⟦देवेषु⟧, in the midst of both kinds, ⟦सुसूदति⟧, impels, accepts, ⟦हव्या⟧, the oblations. The root is ⟦षूद क्षरणे⟧, ṣūd in the sense of flowing. By ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧, "variously in the Veda", ślu takes the place of śap. By ⟦लेटोऽडाटौ⟧, the rule that aṭ and āṭ are added to leṭ, there is the augment aṭ. Or it is the Agni of the sacrificial post himself who is spoken of in the third person. That Agni accepts the oblations well.

## SBh 1.142.12.1

⟦पूषण्वते⟧, for him who, through Pūṣan, possesses him. By ⟦अनो नुट्⟧, the rule for nuṭ after an, there is the augment nuṭ. By ⟦ह्रस्वनुड्भ्यां मतुप्⟧, the rule for matup after a short vowel and after nuṭ, the suffix matup is udātta.

## SBh 1.142.12.2

⟦मरुत्वते⟧, for him who, through the Maruts, possesses them, ⟦विश्वदेवाय⟧, for the host of all the gods, ⟦वायवे⟧, and for the god Vāyu, whose nature is to move, and likewise ⟦गायत्रवेपसे⟧: ⟦वेप⟧ is a name of form; the word gāyatra stands also for the other sāmans; for him whose form is the Gāyatra, for such a one, ⟦इन्द्राय⟧, for Indra, in order to give ⟦हव्यं⟧, the oblation, ⟦स्वाहा⟧ ⟦कर्तन⟧, make the utterance of svāhā, O priests. After the root kṛ, in the imperative, there is the substitute tanap by ⟦तप्तनप्तनथनाश्च⟧, the rule for tap, tanap, tana and thana.

## SBh 1.142.13.1

O Indra, ⟦स्वाहाकृतानि⟧: the making, the doing, that is the utterance, preceded by the word svāhā, of the deities that are about to be worshipped: these are the svāhā-makings. ⟦हव्यानि⟧, the oblations, that possess them. Here, since in the fore-offerings the vaṣaṭ call is given, the meaning is: the oblation made with svāhā. Having approached that oblation, ⟦वीतये⟧, for eating it, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गहि⟧, come. After the root gam there is the Vedic elision of śap. By the rule beginning ⟦अनुदात्तोपदेश⟧ the nasal is elided. Likewise, O ⟦इन्द्र⟧, Indra, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गहि⟧, come with the praises as your aim. Having come, ⟦श्रुधि⟧, hear ⟦हवम्⟧, our call. Since the priests ⟦हवन्ते⟧, call, ⟦त्वाम्⟧, you, ⟦अध्वरे⟧, at the sacrifice, whose nature is non-injury, therefore hear. In the root hve there is the vocalisation of the semivowel by ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧, "variously in the Veda".
