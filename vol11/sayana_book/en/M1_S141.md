## SBh 1.141.0.1

⟦बळित्था⟧ is the second sūkta, of thirteen Ṛks; it is of Dīrghatamas and in the jagatī metre. The last two Ṛks are triṣṭubhs. Agni is the deity. Its employment in the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra has been stated.

## SBh 1.141.1.1

⟦देवस्य⟧, of the one whose nature is to shine, the Sun, ⟦दर्शत⟧ **NOTE**{The text has ⟦दर्शत⟧ without the final nasal of the Ṛk's word; is that word meant?}, to be seen by all, ⟦तत्⟧, that well-known ⟦भर्गः⟧, lustre, ⟦इत्था⟧, thus, in the manner in which it is seen by all, ⟦वपुषे⟧, for the functioning of the body, for its firmness, ⟦धायि⟧, is held by all. Both ⟦बट्⟧ and ⟦इत्था⟧ are names of truth, because they are read among its names: ⟦बट् श्रत् सत्रा इत्था⟧ (⟦नि. ३. १०. १. ५⟧). That the body is sustained through the digestion of what has been eaten by the fire of the belly is well known. He states the ground for that: ⟦यतः⟧ ⟦सहसः⟧ ⟦जनि⟧.

## SBh 1.141.1.2

The meaning is: for the reason that he was produced by strength at the time of the churning, therefore he is held for the sake of strength. ⟦यदीं⟧, this well-known lustre of Agni, which benefits the world, which ⟦मतिः⟧, my understanding, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦ह्वरते⟧, approaches, or, having approached, abides by. The root is ⟦ह्वृ कौटिल्ये⟧, hvṛ in the sense of crookedness. ⟦साधते⟧, and by that very lustre it accomplishes what it desires for itself. Moreover, ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, of Agni who accomplishes the sacrifice, by his lustre, the ⟦धेनाः⟧, the speeches of all living beings, ⟦सस्रुतः⟧, going alike, proceeding all together — from the root sru there is the suffix kvip in the sense of the agent — ⟦अनयन्त⟧, lead, carry on the dealings of the world.

## SBh 1.141.1.3

The middle voice is by transposition. Or else: ⟦ऋतस्य⟧ ⟦धेना⟧, the speeches in the form of mantras that belong to the sacrifice, ⟦सस्रुतो⟧, proceeding all together through the mouth of the hotṛ and the others, ⟦अनयन्त⟧, lead. They carry out the praise of Agni. Or they carry out the sacrifice by the lustre of Agni; because of the scripture ⟦तेजोमयी वाक्⟧ (⟦छा, उ. ६. ५. ४⟧), "speech consists of lustre". The meaning is: for this reason the understanding approaches it. Dhenā is a name of speech, because it is read among its names: ⟦धेना ग्नाः⟧ (⟦नि. १. ११. ३९⟧).

## SBh 1.141.2.1

This Agni is ⟦पृक्षः⟧, the one who brings about food by cooking and the like; ⟦वपुः⟧, possessed of body, the cause of the growth of the body; ⟦पितुमान्⟧, joined with food marked as oblation; ⟦नित्यः⟧, everlasting. Being such, first, having the earth as his station, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦शये⟧, he lies. The meaning is: carrying out the worldly work of burning, cooking and the like, and the Vedic work of sacrifice and the like, he abides firm on the earth. Likewise, ⟦सप्तशिवासु⟧ ⟦मातृषु⟧, in the rains, which bring good to the seven worlds and which stand in the place of mothers, being beneficent, ⟦द्वितीयम्⟧, secondly, he lies. He abides having the middle region as his station, or in the form of lightning. ⟦अस्य⟧ ⟦वृषभस्य⟧, of this showerer, of this very fire of lightning, for that which is connected with him as his helper, ⟦दोहसे⟧, for the milker, for the ray of the Sun, which makes the water that is in the Sun rain down. From the root duh there is the suffix asu in the sense of the agent. For the sake of its activity, ⟦तृतीयम्⟧, thirdly, he lies.

## SBh 1.141.2.2

He abides having heaven as his station. Or else: of this showerer, the well-known Sun, for the milker that belongs to him, the ray, for the sake of its activity. It is well known that the rays of the Sun in the hot season draw up the water that is on the earth, bring it to the Sun, hold it there for a long time, and then, having reached the middle region again and stayed in the clouds, rain it down in the rainy season. Hence the ray is a milker. Such an Agni of three stations, of great fortune, ⟦योषणः⟧, those whose nature is to mingle, all the quarters, ⟦जनयन्त⟧, produce. The meaning is: he is spread in all the quarters. Of what kind is he? ⟦दशप्रमतिम्⟧, possessing eminent thought in the ten quarters, so as to be worthy of worship. Or else the meaning is: produced by the ten fingers at the time of the churning, and pervading in the three worlds and in the ten quarters.

## SBh 1.141.3.1

⟦ईम्⟧, this Agni, ⟦यत्⟧, for which reason, ⟦महिषस्य⟧ — this is a name of what is great, because it is read among its names: ⟦महिषः अभ्वः ऋभुक्षा:⟧ (⟦नि. ३. ३. ८⟧) — of the great sacrifice, ⟦बुध्नात्⟧, from the root, beginning from the establishing of the fire, ⟦वर्पसः⟧ — this is a name of form — for the accomplishment of its form, which consists of a beginning and a conclusion, the ⟦ईशानासः⟧, those capable of their own operations, the ⟦सूरयः⟧, the wise priests, ⟦शवसा⟧, by strength, in the form of the mantras and the like, or of the body, ⟦निः⟧ ⟦क्रन्त⟧, bring out, accomplish, produce from the wood. Or else: this Agni, ⟦बुध्नात्⟧, from the beginning, starting from the churning, for the accomplishment of ⟦महिषस्य⟧ ⟦वर्पसो⟧, of the great form that is capable of accepting the oblations, they bring out.

## SBh 1.141.3.2

From the root kṛ, in the Vedic luṅ, by the rule ⟦मन्त्रे घस०⟧, the one beginning "in a mantra, after ghas", there is elision of cli. By transposition there is the substitution of ant.

## SBh 1.141.3.3

Moreover, ⟦यदीम्⟧, this very Agni, ⟦प्रदिवः⟧, from of old; the meaning is, beginning from time without beginning, not only just now. As before, so now, ⟦मध्वः⟧, of the honey, of what is like honey, ⟦आधवे⟧, at the stirring, the throwing in, when that is the occasion, ⟦गुहा⟧ ⟦सन्तं⟧, being hidden in the cave, on the altar, in the pieces of wood, ⟦मातरिश्वा⟧, the wind that moves in the middle region, in due order ⟦मथायति⟧, stirs; the meaning is, he rouses him. Or else: he who breathes, who is active, in the mother, that is, in the sacrifice, the mother of the fruit, is ⟦मातरिश्वा⟧, the sacrificer; he, ⟦गुहा⟧ ⟦सन्तम्⟧, him who is hidden in the two kindling-sticks, for the sake of his appearing, in due order ⟦मथायति⟧, churns; the meaning is, he produces him. The meaning is: because he is of such a nature, therefore he is to be praised.

## SBh 1.141.4.1

⟦यत्⟧, because, ⟦पितुः⟧, of the food marked as the animal — the nominative stands in the place of the genitive — of that, ⟦परमात्⟧, by reason of its being supreme; the meaning is, for its excellence. For that purpose ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦परि⟧ ⟦नीयते⟧, he is led round thoroughly; the meaning is, the carrying of the fire round it is performed. Or else: ⟦पितुः⟧, from the father, the producer, ⟦परमात्⟧, the excellent one, that is, from the Agni marked as the Gārhapatya, towards the Āhavanīya he is led forth, he is led thoroughly. ⟦परि⟧ is without meaning. Moreover the ⟦पृक्षुधः⟧, those that are being placed in the midst of the burning in the animal-offerings, the sacrificial cakes and the like. Or else: there is the vocalization of the word ma **NOTE**{The text speaks of the vocalization "of the word ma"; is "of the word pra" meant, which gives the first syllable of this word?}; the root is ⟦क्षुध बुभुक्षायाम्⟧, kṣudh in the sense of the desire to eat; the suffix is kvip.

## SBh 1.141.4.2

They are those to be hungered for greatly, those greatly desired as food, those wished to be eaten. The ⟦वीरुधो⟧, ⟦दंसु⟧, in the midst of the teeth, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦रोहति⟧, mount; the meaning is, they enter. ⟦यत्⟧, because, ⟦उभा⟧, both, the Adhvaryu and the sacrificer, or both the kindling-sticks, ⟦अस्य⟧, of Agni, ⟦जनुषं⟧, the birth, the origin, ⟦इन्वतः⟧, pervade; the meaning is, they take it upon themselves. The root is ⟦इवि व्याप्तौ⟧, iv in the sense of pervading. Because it has an indicatory i there is the augment num.

## SBh 1.141.4.3

⟦आदित्⟧, for that very reason, this Agni, ⟦शुचिः⟧, pure, ⟦घृणा⟧, by his favour towards the sacrificers; or else ⟦घृणा⟧ means possessed of radiance, the suffix of possession being dropped; or, by his radiance; ⟦यविष्ठः⟧, the most youthful, ⟦अभवत्⟧, became. The meaning is: he became capable of accepting.

## SBh 1.141.5.1

This Agni, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦शुचिः⟧, being ablaze, ⟦आदित्⟧, just after that — since in the preceding mantra it was said that he became the most youthful — ⟦मातॄः⟧, the ten quarters, which produce him and stand in the place of mothers, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦अविशत्⟧, enters on all sides. The quarters are mothers because it was said before: ⟦दशप्रमतिं जनयन्त योषणः⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं.१. १४१. २⟧). The meaning is: he has pervaded the quarters with his light. The mothers are further specified. ⟦यासु⟧, the mothers in whom, ⟦अहिंस्यमानः⟧, not being oppressed by others, ⟦उर्विया⟧, widely, very abundantly, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦ववृधे⟧, he became grown in various ways, those he entered.

## SBh 1.141.5.2

The idea is: just as in the world some brilliant man, well nourished by his mother through the drinking at the breast, the pressing and the like, having shone forth in the world, comes back and embraces his mother, so it is here. At what time? On that he says: ⟦यत्⟧, when, the ⟦सनाजुवः⟧, those separated for a long time, thrown on at the very time of the setting down, and for that very reason ⟦पूर्वाः⟧, the plants that come first, in due order ⟦अरुहत्⟧, he mounts. From the root ruh there is the Vedic luṅ. By the rule ⟦कृमृदृरुहिभ्यश्छन्दसि⟧, "after kṛ, mṛ, dṛ and ruh in the Veda", aṅ is substituted for cli.

## SBh 1.141.5.3

He blazes on top of them. Likewise, ⟦नव्यसीषु⟧, in the newer ones, which for that very reason are ⟦अवरासु⟧, for that very reason inferior in comparison with the earlier ones, in the plants that come later, ⟦नि⟧ ⟦धावते⟧, he goes thoroughly, or goes low; the meaning is, he enters inside. The meaning is: when he burns, having entered the pieces of wood from below and from above, then he became illuminated in the quarters.

## SBh 1.141.6.1

⟦होतारं⟧, the one who accomplishes the offering, or the one who calls the gods: such an Agni, ⟦दिविष्टिषु⟧, at the seekings of the day, these being the occasion, ⟦वृणते⟧, they resort to. ⟦आदित्⟧, just thereafter, likewise, ⟦पप्रचानासः⟧, the priests and the sacrificers, who unite themselves with him by oblation and by praise — in place of the Vedic liṭ of the root pṛc there is kānac —

## SBh 1.141.6.2

⟦ऋञ्जते⟧, adorn him. Like what? ⟦भगमिव⟧. As people adorn him, so. Or else: like a great king; the meaning is, one to be honoured like the radiance of the Sun. ⟦ऋञ्जतिः प्रसाधनकर्मा⟧, "ṛñjati has the action of adorning", says Yāska. ⟦यत्⟧, because this Agni, ⟦पुरुष्टुतः⟧, being praised by many, or in many ways, ⟦क्रत्वा⟧, with action, with work in the form of sacrifice and the like, and ⟦मज्मना⟧, with the strength of the body, the ⟦देवान्⟧ who are joined with these, the priests, who carry on the dealings — or else the gods, who enjoy the sacrifice, in order to join them with action and with strength — likewise ⟦मर्तं⟧, the sacrificer, and ⟦शंसं⟧, the praise, or him as praiseworthy; this one who is ⟦विश्वधा⟧, possessed of all wealth, or else who consists of all, ⟦वेति⟧, desires those just named. For what purpose? ⟦धायसे⟧, for the food marked as oblation. Therefore they choose Agni as hotṛ and adorn him.

## SBh 1.141.7.1

⟦यत्⟧, when this Agni, ⟦वातचोदितः⟧, driven by the wind — by the rule ⟦तृतीया कर्मणि⟧, "an instrumental before a passive participle", the first member keeps its own accent — being ⟦यजतः⟧, to be worshipped by all, fit to be worshipped, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦अस्थात्⟧, stands spread out, becomes one who has pervaded on all sides. There is an illustration for this. ⟦अनाकृतः⟧, unchecked, of unimpeded advance, ⟦वक्वा⟧, speaking much — from the root vac there is the suffix vanip by the rule ⟦अन्येभ्योऽपि दृश्यन्ते⟧, "they are seen after other roots also"; because of the mention of "seen" in it there is the substitution of the guttural — ⟦ह्वारो⟧ ⟦न⟧, like a crooked man, like a jester and the like. From the causative of the root hvṛ there is the suffix ac of the pacādi group.

## SBh 1.141.7.2

As he, with regard to ⟦जरणाः⟧, praises, stands spread out, goes on making them in various ways, so this one also is settled, unchecked, of unhindered advance. Then, ⟦तस्य⟧, of that Agni, ⟦पत्मन्⟧, on the flying, the path, in the form of the sacrifice, ⟦रजः⟧, the world, whose nature is delighting, ⟦आ⟧ stood, that is, stands upon it, resorts to it on all sides; the meaning is, it sacrifices in the fire in many ways. Of what kind is he? ⟦धक्षुषः⟧, burning — in place of the liṭ of the root dah there is kvasu; ⟦कृष्णजंहसः⟧, of black path — it is gone over again and again, hence jaṃhā is a path; from the root han ending in the elision of yaṅ there is the suffix asun in the sense of the object; the absence of the guttural is Vedic; ⟦शुचिजन्मनः⟧, so called because he is produced from the two kindling-sticks accompanied by mantras; ⟦व्यध्वनः⟧, of various flames, of unfixed path.

## SBh 1.141.8.1

This Agni is ⟦शिक्वभिः⟧, by ropes, ⟦कृतः⟧, bound, ⟦रथो⟧ ⟦न⟧, like a chariot. As that goes by its own parts, the wheels and the like, so he, ⟦यातः⟧, being set down, ⟦अरुषेभिः⟧, by moving ⟦अङ्गेभिः⟧, limbs in the form of flames and the like, ⟦द्याम्⟧, to the middle region, being gladdened by the ghee, ⟦ईयते⟧, goes. The root is ⟦ईङ् गतौ⟧, ī in the sense of going.

## SBh 1.141.8.2

⟦आत्⟧, after his growth, ⟦अस्य⟧, of Agni, ⟦ते⟧, those ⟦सूरयः⟧, courses, paths, become ⟦कृष्णासः⟧, black-coloured. Since they become so, for that purpose ⟦दक्षि⟧, he burns the pieces of wood. There is a transposition of the person. The elision of śap is Vedic.

## SBh 1.141.8.3

Moreover, from the ⟦त्वेषथात्⟧, the blazing lustre, of the kindled Agni, ⟦वयः⟧, those that have movement, the birds, beasts and the like, ⟦ईषते⟧, go. The root is ⟦ईष गत्यादौ⟧, īṣ in the sense of going and so on. By the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧, "variously in the Veda", there is elision of śap.

## SBh 1.141.8.4

They flee. There is an illustration for this: like ⟦वय⟧ from the ⟦त्वेषथात्⟧ of a ⟦शूरस्य⟧; as if fleeing in fear from the blazing lustre of some valiant man. Thus, touching the middle region, he burns on all sides: so he is praised at all times in the form of the forest fire.

## SBh 1.141.9.1

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, by ⟦त्वया⟧, you indeed, ⟦वरुणः⟧, the god who presides over the night, becomes ⟦धृतव्रतः⟧, one who has taken up the work of raining and the like. Likewise ⟦मित्रः⟧, the god who presides over the day, ⟦शाशद्रे⟧, destroys the darkness.

## SBh 1.141.9.2

The root is ⟦शद्लृ शातने⟧, śad in the sense of destroying. There is the Vedic liṭ. By the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧, "variously in the Veda", there is the augment ruṭ. Because it belongs to the group beginning with tuj, the reduplication is lengthened. Likewise ⟦अर्यमा⟧, the god who stands between the two, becomes ⟦सुदानवः⟧, one of good giving.

## SBh 1.141.9.3

The plural number is by transposition. Or else: these, Varuṇa and the others, ⟦सुदानवः⟧, of good giving, the doers each of his own task, by you indeed ⟦शाशद्रे⟧, are made sharp. The word hi is meant to show that this is well known from another mantra. For ⟦त्वमग्ने वरुणो जायसे⟧ (⟦ऋ. सं. ५, ३. १⟧), "You, O Agni, are born as Varuṇa", is the other mantra.

## SBh 1.141.9.4

From the root śad there is the Vedic liṭ in the sense of the object. By the rule ⟦इरयो रे⟧, "re in place of irec", re comes in. By the rule ⟦हि च⟧, "and with hi", there is no loss of accent. He states the ground for Varuṇa and the others being dependent on Agni. ⟦यत्⟧, for which reason, O Agni, ⟦सीं⟧, on all sides, ⟦क्रतुना⟧, by your work, ⟦विश्वथा⟧, like all, as if consisting of all, ⟦विभुः⟧, pervading all in the nature of Mitra and Aryaman, being ⟦परिभूः⟧, one who overpowers on all sides by his own lustre, in due order ⟦अजायथाः⟧, you are born, you appear. There is an illustration for this. ⟦अरान्⟧, the pegs of a chariot, ⟦नेमिः⟧ ⟦न⟧, like the outer rim. As that stands encompassing them, so this Agni stands encompassing Varuṇa and the others.

## SBh 1.141.10.1

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦यविष्ठ⟧, most youthful one, ⟦त्वं⟧, you, ⟦शशमानाय⟧, for him who praises you — ⟦शशमान: शंसमानः⟧ (⟦निरु, ६.८⟧), "śaśamāna means praising", says Yāska — ⟦सुन्वते⟧, for the sacrificer who performs the pressing for your sake, for the sake of his sacrifice, the ⟦रत्नं⟧, the delightful oblation, which is ⟦देवताति⟧, being spread, extended, among the gods, ⟦इन्वसि⟧, you pervade. The meaning is: you make the oblation given by us reach the gods. The root inv has the action of pervading, because it is read among the names of that: ⟦इन्वति ननक्ष⟧ (⟦नि. २. १८. १⟧). O ⟦सहसो⟧ ⟦युवन्⟧.

## SBh 1.141.10.2

By the word yuvan his origin is indicated: O you who were produced by strength at the time of the churning; or, O mingler of strength. Or else: O son of strength, ⟦युवन्⟧, ever young; ⟦महिरत्न⟧, you whose great and delightful wealth is to be given to us; you who are ⟦नव्यं⟧, newer, or praiseworthy, ⟦तं⟧, such a one, an eater of oblations, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦वयं⟧, we the sacrificers, ⟦कारे⟧, in the sound that is marked as praise, ⟦नु⟧, quickly, ⟦धीमहि⟧, set. The meaning is: at the time of giving the oblation we praise you quickly indeed. There is an illustration for this. ⟦भगं⟧ ⟦न⟧, like the Sun. Or else, like a great king. As people praise him, so.

## SBh 1.141.11.1

O Agni, you, ⟦अस्मे⟧, in us, what is ⟦स्वर्थं⟧, well to be reached, ⟦दमूनसं⟧, to be subdued, to be earned — by the rule ⟦दमेरूनसिः⟧ (⟦उ. सू. ४. २३५⟧), "after dam, ūnasi", there is the suffix ūnasi —

## SBh 1.141.11.2

⟦रयिं⟧ ⟦न⟧ ⟦पपृचासि⟧, as you unite us with wealth. Likewise ⟦भगं⟧, to be resorted to by all, ⟦दक्षं⟧ ⟦न⟧, as you unite us with a son who is energetic, or wide awake, and ⟦धर्णसिं⟧, skilled in retaining learning and the like; the meaning is, you give both. The root is ⟦पृची संपर्के⟧, pṛc in the sense of contact. In leṭ there is the augment āṭ. By the rule ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧, "variously in the Veda", there is ślu of the class-sign.

## SBh 1.141.11.3

Now he speaks of him in the third person. ⟦यः⟧, the Agni who, ⟦रश्मीँरिव⟧, as he does his own rays — as he ⟦यमति⟧, controls them, spreads them out, so he — ⟦उभे⟧ ⟦जन्मनी⟧, the two worlds that are the ground of birth, this world and the other world, controls, restrains. Likewise, ⟦सुक्रतुः⟧, of good works, he, ⟦ऋते⟧, at our sacrifice, ⟦देवानां⟧, belonging to the gods, ⟦शंसं⟧, the praise, ⟦च⟧ ⟦आ⟧ holds out, gives at all times. Or else: in the first half too, ⟦पपृचासीत्यपि⟧ is to be construed as a third person, so as to be indirect.

## SBh 1.141.12.1

The word uta has the sense of api; and that is in the sense of possibility. That Agni, ⟦नः⟧, our call, ⟦उत⟧ ⟦शृणवत्⟧: would that he might hear it. Or else: ⟦नः⟧, us, may he cause to be heard, may he make known among the gods. From the root śru, in leṭ, there is the augment aṭ. Of what kind is he? ⟦सुद्योत्मा⟧, shining well.

## SBh 1.141.12.2

The root is ⟦द्युत दीप्तौ⟧, dyut in the sense of shining. By the rule ⟦अन्येभ्योऽपि दृश्यन्ते⟧, "they are seen after other roots also", there is the suffix manin. ⟦जीराश्वः⟧, whose horses go swiftly; ⟦होता⟧, the caller of the gods; ⟦मन्द्रः⟧, whose nature is to rejoice; ⟦चन्द्ररथः⟧, furnished with a chariot made of gold. Moreover this one, ⟦अमूरः⟧, of unbewildered strength, ⟦वस्यः⟧, fit to be dwelt with, ⟦सः⟧, that well-known ⟦अग्निः⟧, ⟦नेषतमैः⟧, by works, or by praises, that lead exceedingly, ⟦नः⟧, us also ⟦नेषत्⟧, would that he might lead; may he make us reach the far end of the sacrifice. From the root nī, in leṭ **NOTE**{The text has ⟦नयतेलेंटि⟧; is "of nī, in leṭ" meant?}, there is the augment aṭ. By the rule ⟦सिब्बहुलम्⟧, "sip, variously", there is sip.

## SBh 1.141.12.3

For what purpose? ⟦वामं⟧, desirable, ⟦सुवितं⟧, well to be reached, heaven, which is longed for by all, ⟦अच्छ⟧, to reach it, facing it, may he lead us.

## SBh 1.141.13.1

This ⟦अग्निः⟧, by ⟦अर्कैः⟧, mantras that are the means of worship, which are ⟦शिमीवद्भिः⟧, possessed of works such as the giving of the oblation, ⟦अस्तावि⟧, has been praised by us the sacrificers. Śimī is a name of work, because it is read among its names: ⟦शिमी शक्तिः⟧ (⟦नि. २. १. २४⟧). Of what kind is he? ⟦साम्राज्याय⟧: this is a dative in the place of the genitive of the object, "of sovereignty". The state of shining perfectly, ⟦प्रतरं⟧, in a higher degree, exceedingly, ⟦दधानः⟧, holding. By the rule ⟦अमु च च्छन्दसि⟧, "and amu in the Veda", there is the suffix amu.

## SBh 1.141.13.2

Moreover, this Agni, ⟦अमी⟧ ⟦च⟧, these priests who are present before us, or the sons and the like, ⟦ये⟧, who, ⟦वयं⟧ ⟦च⟧, and we also, who have been known before as praisers, ⟦अति⟧, exceedingly, ⟦निष्टतन्युः⟧, let them sound forth. The root stan preceded by nis has the action of sounding.

## SBh 1.141.13.3

The meaning is: hereafter too our sons, grandsons and the rest, and we, praise Agni. There is an illustration for this: ⟦मिहं⟧ ⟦न⟧ ⟦सूरः⟧. As ⟦सूरः⟧, the Sun, makes ⟦मिहं⟧, the cloud whose nature is to pour down, sound for the sake of rain, so.

## SBh 1.141.13.4

In liṅ there is the Vedic ślu of the class-sign. By the rule which lays down, for stan after abhi and nis, when the name of a sound is meant (⟦पा. सू. ८. ३. ८६⟧), there is the cerebral ṣ.
