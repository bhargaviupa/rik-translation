## SBh 1.156.0.1

The sūkta beginning ⟦भवा मित्रः⟧, of five Ṛks, is the seventeenth sūkta; it is of Dīrghatamas. Because ⟦जागतं तु⟧, "but in jagatī", has been said, this one too is in the jagatī metre. Because ⟦वैष्णवं हि⟧, "addressed to Viṣṇu indeed", has been said, it is addressed to Viṣṇu. In the Anukramaṇikā it is listed as ⟦भव पञ्च⟧, "'Bhava': five".

## SBh 1.156.0.2

In the Ukthya, at the third pressing, this sūkta is employed in the śastra of the acchāvāka. It is laid down in the section ⟦उक्थ्ये तु होत्रकाणाम्⟧, "but in the Ukthya, of the hotrakas": ⟦भवा मित्रः सं वां कर्मणा⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ६. १⟧), which names the sūkta beginning "bhavā mitraḥ" and the one beginning "saṃ vāṃ karmaṇā". On both sides of the caru for Soma an offering is to be made with ghee **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦वृतेन⟧; ghṛtena, "with ghee", is translated, as the next words speak of the ghee-offering: is it a typo?}; there, at the later ghee-offering, the Ṛk ⟦उरु विष्णो वि क्रमस्व⟧ is the yājyā of the archetype. But on the tenth day, in its place, this Ṛk beginning ⟦भवा मित्रः⟧ is to be used. It is laid down in the section **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦खडे⟧; khaṇḍe, "in the section", is translated: is it a typo?} ⟦दशमेऽहनि⟧, "on the tenth day": ⟦उरु विष्णो वि क्रमस्वेति घृतयाज्यास्थाने भवा मित्रः⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८. १२⟧), "in the place of the ghee-yājyā 'uru viṣṇo vi kramasva', the Ṛk 'bhavā mitraḥ'".

## SBh 1.156.1.1

O Viṣṇu, ⟦मित्रो⟧ ⟦न⟧, like Mitra. Mitra is one who protects from miti, from pain: a friend, or the sun. The Nirukta says ⟦प्रमीतेस्त्रायते⟧ (⟦निरु. १०. २१⟧), "he protects from perishing". Like him, ⟦शेव्यः⟧, good in the matter of happiness, a maker of happiness; ⟦घृतासुतिः⟧: he by whom ghṛta, water, is brought forth is such. Or else: he to whom ghṛta, clarified butter, is brought, towards him, is such. ⟦विभूतद्युम्नः⟧, of abundant fame, or of abundant food; ⟦एवयाः⟧, the one who joins protection to us, who brings it; ⟦सप्रथाः⟧, broad on every side. With each epithet the connection is: "be so for us", ⟦भव⟧. The long vowel is by the rule ⟦द्व्यचोऽतस्तिङः⟧, "of the a of a finite verb of two vowels". ⟦उ⟧ fills out the quarter-verse.

## SBh 1.156.1.2

O ⟦विष्णो⟧, since you are such, ⟦अध⟧, for this reason, ⟦ते⟧, your ⟦स्तोमः⟧, particular hymn of praise, by ⟦विदुषा⟧, by the sacrificer who knows your greatness, is ⟦अर्ध्यः⟧, worthy of being increased again and again. The meaning is that it is not completed by being done a single time. Likewise your ⟦यज्ञश्च⟧, sacrifice too, by ⟦हविष्मता⟧, by that sacrificer, is ⟦राध्यः⟧, to be duly accomplished. Or else: by the one who knows, the hotṛ, the hymn of praise is to be accomplished, and by him who has the oblation the sacrifice is to be accomplished.

## SBh 1.156.2.1

⟦यः⟧, the mortal who, to ⟦पूर्व्याय⟧, to him who belongs to former time, the meaning is, to the eternal one; to ⟦वेधसे⟧, to the maker of the manifold world; to ⟦नवीयसे⟧, to the ever new, the meaning is, to the exceedingly delightful one, or to him who is to be praised; to ⟦सुमज्जानये⟧, to him who was born of himself alone. From the root jan there is the uṇādi suffix iṇ.

## SBh 1.156.2.2

Yāska says: ⟦सुमत्स्वयमित्यर्थः⟧ (⟦निरु. ६. २२⟧), "sumat has the meaning 'by oneself'". Or else: that which gladdens exceedingly is sumat; he who has a wife of that kind is so called. To him: the meaning is, to the husband of Śrī, whose nature it is to gladden the whole world.

## SBh 1.156.2.3

In a bahuvrīhi, by ⟦जायाया निङ्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ५. ४. १३४⟧), "niṅ in place of jāyā", there is the substitute niṅ at the end of the compound. There is elision before a val sound.

## SBh 1.156.2.4

To ⟦विष्णवे⟧, to the pervading one, who has the said qualities, ⟦ददाशति⟧, he gives the oblation and the like. Moreover, of ⟦अस्य⟧, of this Viṣṇu, ⟦महतः⟧, of him of great majesty, the ⟦महि⟧, the great, the venerable, ⟦जात⟧, the birth, the origin **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦जात⟧ without the final nasal, where the Ṛk has jātam, and ⟦उत्पतिं⟧, translated as utpattim, "origin": are these typos?}, the birth in the form of Hiraṇyagarbha and the rest, ⟦ब्रवत्⟧, he should tell. The augment aṭ comes to the root brū in leṭ.

## SBh 1.156.2.5

He should proclaim it. ⟦सेदु⟧: the word u has the sense of the word api. He too, the giver and the praiser, being joined with ⟦श्रवोभिः⟧, with foods or with fames, ⟦अभि⟧, facing it, ⟦असत्⟧, goes to, reaches, the ⟦युज्यं⟧ ⟦चित⟧ **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦चित⟧ without the final virāma, where the Ṛk has cit: is it a typo?}, that station which is indeed to be gone to by all.

## SBh 1.156.3.1

O ⟦स्तोतारः⟧, praisers, ⟦तमु⟧, that Viṣṇu alone, the ⟦पूर्व्यं⟧, who is worthy of the first place, established without beginning, the ⟦गर्भं⟧ of ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, who is as the embryo of the sacrifice; the meaning is, who has arisen as the sacrifice itself. This is from the passage of the Veda ⟦यज्ञो वै विष्णुः⟧ (⟦श. ब्रा. १. १. २. १३⟧), "The sacrifice indeed is Viṣṇu". Or else: of ṛta, of water, the garbha, the cause of the embryo; the meaning is, the producer of water. This is from the smṛti ⟦अप एव ससर्जादौ⟧ (⟦मनु. १.८⟧), "He created the waters alone in the beginning". Viṣṇu who is such, ⟦यथा⟧ ⟦विद⟧, as you know him, so, ⟦जनुषा⟧, by birth, of your own selves alone, not on account of any gaining of a boon or the like, ⟦पिपर्तन⟧, gladden him with praise and the like. The meaning is: as far as you know his greatness, so far.

## SBh 1.156.3.2

It is the second person plural of the root vid in laṭ. In ⟦विद ऋतस्य⟧ here, in the Saṃhitā, the original form is kept by the rule ⟦ऋत्यकः⟧.

## SBh 1.156.3.3

Moreover, of ⟦अस्य⟧, of this Viṣṇu of great majesty, the ⟦नाम⟧ ⟦चित्⟧, the appellation to which all are to bow, which sets forth that he is the self of all, this name "Viṣṇu", ⟦जानन्तः⟧, understanding that it grants the aims of man, ⟦आ⟧, on all sides, ⟦विवक्तन⟧, speak it, proclaim it. Or else: nāma, the bending in the form of the sacrifice, the transformation of Viṣṇu alone, for the accomplishing of heaven and final release for all, into the form of the iṣṭi and the rest, or into the form of the substance and the deity: you who know that fully, tell it, praise him.

## SBh 1.156.3.4

Of the root vac in loṭ, the ślu in place of śap is Vedic. By ⟦बहुलं छन्दसि⟧, "variously in the Veda", the reduplication takes the vowel i. As before, there is the substitute tana.

## SBh 1.156.3.5

Now, having beheld him directly, he says: O ⟦विष्णो⟧, god who are the self of all, of ⟦महः⟧, of the great one, ⟦ते⟧, of you, the ⟦सुमतिं⟧, the good praise, or the mind whose nature is splendour, ⟦भजामहे⟧, we serve, we the sacrificers.

## SBh 1.156.4.1

At the carrying forward of Agni and Soma this Ṛk beginning ⟦तमस्य राजा⟧ is to be used. It is laid down in the section ⟦अग्नीषोमौ प्रणेष्यत्सु⟧, "when they are about to carry forward Agni and Soma": ⟦तमस्य राजा वरुणस्तमश्विनेत्यर्धर्च आरमेत्⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ४. १०⟧), "at 'tam asya rājā varuṇas tam aśvinā', at the half-Ṛk, he should stop".

## SBh 1.156.4.2

⟦मारुतस्य⟧: the assemblage of the Maruts, of the officiating priests, is māruta; of him who has that. The suffix that has the sense of matup is dropped. Or else: of him who is connected with the Maruts, the gods. Of ⟦वेधसः⟧, of the wise one, ⟦अस्य⟧, of this sacrificer, ⟦तं⟧, that well-known ⟦क्रतुं⟧, sacrifice **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦याग⟧ without the final nasal as the gloss of kratum; yāgam, "sacrifice", is translated: is it a typo?}, Viṣṇu who is the sacrifice itself, because of the passage of the Veda ⟦यज्ञो वै विष्णुः⟧, "The sacrifice indeed is Viṣṇu": ⟦राजा⟧, the shining ⟦वरुणः⟧, Varuṇa, ⟦सचन्त⟧, attends on it. ⟦तम्⟧, that kratu, the sacrifice, in the same way ⟦अश्विना⟧, the two Aśvins attend on. The other gods too attend on it, serve it. Moreover, ⟦विष्णुः⟧, Viṣṇu who pervades as the three pressings, ⟦सखिवान्⟧, being joined with friends such as the sacrificer, the ⟦उत्तमम्⟧, the most excellent, ⟦अहर्विदम्⟧, knower of the day; the meaning is, the producer of heaven.

## SBh 1.156.4.3

The ⟦दक्षं⟧, the strength that has the form of the capacity to grant the fruit, ⟦दाधार⟧, he has held. Moreover, ⟦व्रजं⟧ ⟦च⟧, the cloud too. Vraja is a name of the cloud, because it is read among its names in ⟦व्रजः चरुः⟧ (⟦नि. १. १०. ११⟧), "vrajaḥ, caruḥ". That, for the sake of the rain-water, ⟦अपोर्णुते⟧, he makes to be without its covering. This is because it is the sacrifice alone that, by way of the oblation, produces the rain. Or else: of māruta, of the troop of the Maruts, the host of gods, of the vedhas, of the ordainer, the creator, Viṣṇu, the kratu, the work that has the form of protecting and the like, Varuṇa and the others attend on, because protecting depends on him. And he, having friends, being accompanied by helpers such as Indra and the Maruts, has held the dakṣa of the said character, the strength that has the form of the capacity to produce rain and the like, and likewise he uncovers the cloud.

## SBh 1.156.5.1

⟦यः⟧, the Viṣṇu who, ⟦दैव्यः⟧, existing in heaven, ⟦सुकृत्तरः⟧, the best among those who grant good fruit, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦विवाय⟧, comes.

## SBh 1.156.5.2

It is the form of the root vī in liṭ. For what purpose? ⟦सचथाय⟧, for accompanying, for giving help in the sacrifice. For whom? ⟦इन्द्राय⟧. He who makes irā, the food that consists of the oblation, flow is indra, the sacrificer; because the derivation stated by Yāska, ⟦इन्द्र इरां दृणातीति इदंकरणादित्याग्रायणः⟧ (⟦निरु. १०, ८⟧), "Indra: because he tears the irā; 'because he does this', says Āgrāyaṇa", holds good here too.

## SBh 1.156.5.3

For him, for the sacrificer of the said form, ⟦सुकृते⟧, the maker of good praise. And having come, ⟦वेधाः⟧, the ordainer of the desired fruit, ⟦त्रिषधस्थः⟧, he who has common abodes that are three in number, Viṣṇu whose abodes are the three pressings, or who has the three abodes beginning with the earth, the ⟦आर्यम्⟧, the sacrificer who is to be approached, ⟦अजिन्वत्⟧, he gladdens. The root jivi has the sense of gladdening. Num comes because it has i as its indicatory letter.

## SBh 1.156.5.4

For that purpose, in the ⟦भागे⟧ of ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, in the share of the sacrifice, which has the form of what remains of the offering, that ⟦यजमानम्⟧, sacrificer, he gives a share to, he makes a sharer; the meaning is that he brings him near. Or else: in the bhāga of ṛta, in the fruit of the sacrifice, he gives the sacrificer a share **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦आभजत⟧ and then ⟦आभिजत्⟧ for the Ṛk's ā abhajat; both are translated as ābhajat: are they typos?}: he sets him up as its owner.
