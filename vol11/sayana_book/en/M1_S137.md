## SBh 1.137.0.1

Salutation to the revered Gaṇeśa. ⟦यस्य निःश्वसितं वेदा यो वेदेभ्योऽखिलं जगत् । निर्ममे तमहं वन्दे विद्यातीर्थमहेश्वरम्⟧: "Him whose breath the Vedas are, who out of the Vedas fashioned the whole world, that Vidyātīrtha-Maheśvara I salute."

## SBh 1.137.0.2

Now, in the second aṣṭaka, the second adhyāya begins. The fourth hymn, ⟦सुषुमा यातम्⟧, consisting of three Ṛks, is Parucchepa's and is in the atiśakvarī metre **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦अतिशाक्करं⟧ here and ⟦अतिशाक्वरम्⟧ twice below; is the first a typo for atiśākvaram?}, because it is furnished with sixty syllables **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦षट्यक्षरोपेतत्वात्⟧; is this a typo for ṣaṣṭyakṣaropetatvāt, "because it has sixty syllables", as translated?}. Because the word tu was used in the preceding place, it is addressed to Mitra and Varuṇa. Here the general rule of the atyaṣṭi metre is not resorted to, because the hymn is specified by the word ⟦अतिशाक्वरम्⟧. On this the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦सुषुम तृचमतिशाक्वरम्⟧, "The hymn 'suṣuma' is of three Ṛks, in atiśakvarī."

## SBh 1.137.0.3

On the sixth day of the Daśarātra, at the morning pressing, in the Praüga-śastra, this hymn is the third triplet, the one that has Mitra and Varuṇa as its deity. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦स्तीर्णं बर्हिरिति तृचौ सुषुमा यातमद्रिभिः⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८.१⟧), "The two triplets beginning 'stīrṇaṃ barhiḥ'; 'suṣumā yātam adribhiḥ'." Likewise, on this same day, the first Ṛk is to be inserted before the prasthita-yājyā of the Maitrāvaruṇa priest. In the section ⟦षष्ठस्य प्रातःसवने⟧, "At the morning pressing of the sixth day", it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦सुषुमा यातमद्रिभिः⟧ (⟦आश्व. श्रौ. ८. १⟧).

## SBh 1.137.1.1

O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦यातम्⟧, come towards our sacrifice. If it is asked what there is to come for, to that he says: ⟦अद्रिभिः⟧, with the stones that are the means of pressing, ⟦सुषुम⟧, we have pressed the Soma.

## SBh 1.137.1.2

This is a form of the root su, to press, in liṭ. Hence the Somas are ⟦गोश्रीताः⟧: the word for the source is used for its product; they are mixed with milk. For there is the Brāhmaṇa passage ⟦तस्मान्मैत्रावरुणं पयसा श्रीणाति⟧ (⟦तै. सं. ६. ४. ८. १⟧), "Therefore he mixes the draught of Mitra and Varuṇa with milk." The Somas are ⟦मत्सराः⟧, causes of satisfaction. ⟦मत्सरः सोमो मन्दतेस्तृप्तिकर्मणः⟧ (⟦निरु. २. ५⟧), "matsara is the Soma, from the root mand, which has the sense of satisfying": so says Yāska. ⟦इमे⟧, these, stand in front, drawn. Therefore come. The same are qualified again: ⟦इमे⟧, these, ⟦सोमासो⟧, Somas, are ⟦मत्सराः⟧, causes of exhilaration, or Somas that have rivalry, in the matter of exhilarating, towards the Somas found in other sacrifices. Of what kind are you two? ⟦राजाना⟧, of a shining nature, ⟦दिविस्पृशा⟧, dwelling in the world of heaven. By the supplementary rule ⟦हृद्यु्तभ्यां ङेरुपसंख्यानम्⟧ (⟦पा. सू. ६. ३. ९. १⟧), that the locative ending ṅi after hṛd and div is to be added to the cases of non-elision, the case-ending is not elided **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦हृद्यु्तभ्यां⟧; is this a typo for hṛddyubhyām, "after hṛd and div", as translated?}.

## SBh 1.137.1.3

You two, being such, ⟦अस्मत्रा⟧, in the midst of us, or being our protectors, you two ⟦उप⟧ ⟦गन्तम्⟧, come, to the sacrifice that is ⟦नः⟧, connected with us. Moreover, O ⟦मित्रावरुणा⟧, you two gods of these names, ⟦वां⟧, for the sake of you two, ⟦इमे⟧, these, ⟦सोमाः⟧, Somas, are ⟦गवाशिरः⟧. As before, the word for the source is used for its product: with ⟦गोभिः⟧, with milk, they have become ⟦आशिरो⟧, mixed. Moreover they are ⟦गवाशिरः⟧: they have become mixed with ⟦गोभिः⟧, with waters. Or else: they are mixed with ⟦गोभिः⟧, with mantras that have the character of praise. For that very reason they have become ⟦शुक्राः⟧, bright; for the Śruti says ⟦आशिरमव नयति सशुक्रत्वाय⟧ (⟦तै. सं. ६. १. ६. ५⟧), "He pours in the admixture, so that it may have brightness."

## SBh 1.137.2.1

O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦यातम्⟧, come. Since, for your sake, the ⟦इन्दवः⟧, those able to moisten, the ⟦सोमासः⟧, the Somas, being ⟦दध्याशिरः⟧, having a mixing with curd, ⟦इमे⟧, these, stand in front, drawn with the cup, therefore come. Moreover the Somas, ⟦दध्याशिरः⟧, into which the admixture is being put, are ⟦सुतासः⟧, pressed with the stones with the mantra, made ready; "therefore come" is to be supplied. Because there is a pouring in of curd in the Āditya cup, ⟦दध्याशिर⟧ is said with reference to that. ⟦उत⟧, and also, ⟦वां⟧, for the gratification, for the protection, of you two, ⟦उषसो⟧ ⟦बुधि⟧, when there is the awakening that belongs to the dawn. The suffix is kvip in the sense of the action itself, the kvip taught for the class beginning with sampad. By the rule ⟦सावेकाचः⟧ the case-ending is udātta.

## SBh 1.137.2.2

That is, when the light has arisen. Not at the awakening of the dawn alone, but ⟦सूर्यस्य⟧ ⟦रश्मिभिः⟧ ⟦साकं⟧, together with the rays of the sun, when the light has arisen for the offering of the oblation, ⟦सुतः⟧, the Soma is pressed. Now the two are spoken of separately. ⟦मित्राय⟧, for the one who is beneficial to all, the god of this name who presides over the day, and likewise ⟦वरणाय⟧ **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦वरणाय⟧ where the Ṛk has the name of Varuṇa in the dative; is it a typo?}, for the one who covers, the god who presides over the night: for their sake "the Soma is pressed" is to be supplied. Moreover ⟦ऋताय⟧, for the sacrifice, for that which carries it through, for its sake; ⟦पीतये⟧, for drinking. For the gods' drinking of the Soma is for the accomplishment of the sacrifice. Or else, the suffix is ktic in the sense of the agent.

## SBh 1.137.2.3

⟦पीतये⟧, for the drinker, the officiating priest and the others, ⟦ऋताय⟧, for the sacrifice, for that which carries it through, ⟦चारुः⟧, fit to be consumed, fit to be eaten, the Soma is ⟦सुतो⟧, pressed.

## SBh 1.137.3.1

⟦वां⟧, belonging to you two, ⟦अंशुं⟧, the Soma in the form of a creeper, ⟦दुहन्ति⟧, the Adhvaryus make ready. Like what? ⟦तां⟧, that well-known one who serves for the Agnihotra and the like, ⟦वासरीं⟧, whose milk covers all her limbs; the meaning is, one who has much milk. Or else: one who covers with milk and the like.

## SBh 1.137.3.2

The root is ⟦वस आच्छादने⟧, vas in the sense of covering. The suffix is the uṇādi suffix ari. By the rule ⟦कृतिकारादक्तिनः⟧ the feminine suffix ṅīṣ is added **NOTE**{the unit has ⟦कृतिकारादक्तिनः⟧; is this a typo for kṛdikārādaktinaḥ, the rule for ṅīṣ after a kṛt-stem in i other than ktin?}. Or else: the suffix is the uṇādi suffix ara alone, and then aṇ in the word's own sense.

## SBh 1.137.3.3

⟦धेनुं⟧ ⟦न⟧, like a cow that gladdens. By what means? That he says: ⟦अद्रिभिः⟧, with the stones that are the means of pressing. Moreover ⟦अद्रिभिः⟧, with those same means, ⟦सोमं⟧ ⟦दुहन्ति⟧, for your sake alone they milk: having drenched the Soma in the form of a creeper with water, for your sake alone, they milk out the juice. Moreover, being ⟦अर्वाञ्चा⟧, turned towards us, ⟦सोमपीतये⟧, for the drinking of the Soma, ⟦अस्मत्रा⟧, you two who are our protectors, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦गन्तम्⟧, come near, to the sacrifice that is ⟦नः⟧, ours. Moreover ⟦वां⟧, for your sake, ⟦अयं⟧, this, ⟦सोमः⟧, Soma, is ⟦सुतः⟧, pressed, ⟦नृभिः⟧, by the leaders, the officiating priests who carry the sacrifice through. Moreover it is ⟦सुतः⟧, pressed, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦पीतये⟧, for drinking in full; therefore come: this is the meaning.
