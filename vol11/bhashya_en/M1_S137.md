## Introduction

**NOTE**{the reference text opens this adhyāya with a salutation to Gaṇeśa and a benedictory verse before these words; the supplied text has neither} Now the second adhyāya of the second aṣṭaka begins. The fourth hymn, ⟦सुषुमा यातमिति⟧, consists of three Ṛks; it is Parucchepa's and is in the atiśakvarī metre, because it has sixty syllables. Because the word ⟦तु⟧ was used in the preceding place, it is addressed to Mitra and Varuṇa. Here the general convention of the atyaṣṭi metre is not resorted to, because the hymn is specified as being in atiśakvarī. On this the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦सुषुम तृचमतिशाक्वरमिति⟧, "The triplet 'suṣuma' is in atiśakvarī."

On the sixth day of the Daśarātra, at the morning pressing, in the Praüga-śastra, this hymn is the third triplet, the one that has Mitra and Varuṇa as its deity. And this is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦स्तीर्णं बर्हिरिति⟧ ⟦तृचौ सुषुमा यातमद्रिभिः⟧ (⟦आ. ८-१⟧), "The two triplets beginning 'stīrṇaṃ barhiḥ'; 'suṣumā yātam adribhiḥ'." Likewise, on this same day, the first Ṛk is to be inserted before the prasthita-yājyā of the Maitrāvaruṇa priest. This is laid down in the sūtra in the section ⟦षष्ठस्य प्रातःसवन⟧: ⟦सुषुमा यातमद्रिभिः⟧ (⟦आ. ८-१⟧).

## Ṛk 1

O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦आ यातं⟧: come to our sacrifice. If it is asked what there is to come for, to that he says: ⟦अद्रिभिः⟧, with the stones that are the means of pressing, ⟦सुषुम⟧: we have pressed the Soma. This is a form of the root su in liṭ. Hence the Somas are ⟦गोश्रीताः⟧: here the word for the source is used for its product; they are mixed with milk. For there is the Brāhmaṇa passage ⟦तस्मान्मैत्रावरुणं पयसा श्रीणातीति⟧, "Therefore he mixes the draught of Mitra and Varuṇa with milk." **NOTE**{the reference text adds a reference (Taittirīya-saṃhitā 6.4.8.1) after this quotation; the supplied text has none} The Somas are ⟦मत्सराः⟧, causes of satisfaction. Yāska says: ⟦मत्सरः सोमो मंदतेस्तृप्तिकर्मण⟧ (⟦नि. २-५⟧), "matsara is the Soma, from the root mand, which means to satisfy." ⟦इमे⟧: these stand in front, drawn. Therefore come.

The same Somas are qualified again: these ⟦सोमासः⟧, Somas, are ⟦मत्सराः⟧, causes of exhilaration, or Somas that have rivalry, in the matter of exhilarating, towards the Somas found in other sacrifices.

Of what kind are you two? ⟦राजाना⟧, of a shining nature; ⟦दिविस्पृशा⟧, dwelling in the world of heaven. By the supplementary rule that, after hṛd and div, the non-elision of the locative ending ṅi is to be added (⟦पा. ६-३-९-१⟧), the case-ending is not elided. You two, being such, ⟦अस्मत्रा⟧, in the midst of us, or being our protectors, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦गंतं⟧, come, to the sacrifice that is ⟦नः⟧, connected with us.

Moreover, O Mitra and Varuṇa, you two gods of these names, ⟦वां⟧, for the sake of you two, these Somas are ⟦गवाशिरः⟧. As before, the word for the source is used for its product: they have become mixed with ⟦गोभिः⟧, with milk. Moreover they are ⟦गवाशिरः⟧: they have become mixed with ⟦गोभिः⟧, with waters. Or else: they are mixed with ⟦गोभिः⟧, with mantras that have the character of praise. For that very reason they have become ⟦शुक्राः⟧, bright; for the Śruti says ⟦आशिरमव नयति सशुक्रत्वाय⟧ (⟦तै. सं. ६-१-५⟧), "He pours in the admixture, so that it may be endowed with brightness."

## Ṛk 2

O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦आ यातं⟧, come. Since, for your sake, the ⟦इंदवः⟧, those able to moisten, the ⟦सोमासः⟧, the Somas, being ⟦दध्याशिरो⟧, having an admixture of curd, ⟦इमे⟧, these, stand in front, drawn with the cup, therefore come. Moreover the Somas, ⟦दध्याशिरो⟧, into which the admixture is being put, are ⟦सुतासः⟧, pressed with the stones to the accompaniment of the mantra, made ready; "therefore come" is to be supplied. Because there is a pouring in of curd in the Āditya cup, ⟦दध्याशिर⟧ is said with reference to that.

⟦उत⟧, and also, ⟦वां⟧, for the gratification, for the protection, of you two, ⟦उषसः⟧ ⟦बुधि⟧, when there is the awakening that belongs to the dawn. The suffix is kvip, in the sense of the action itself, of the class beginning with sampad; by the rule ⟦सावेकाच⟧ the case-ending is udātta. That is, when the light has arisen. It is not the awakening of the dawn alone **NOTE**{the supplied text has the nominative ⟦बोधः⟧ here; the reference text has the locative bodhe, "not only at the awakening of the dawn"}; rather, ⟦सूर्यस्य रश्मिभिः साकं⟧, together with the rays of the sun, when the light has arisen for the offering of the oblation, ⟦सुतः⟧, the Soma is pressed.

Now the two are spoken of separately. ⟦मित्राय⟧, for the one who is beneficial to all, the god of this name who presides over the day, and likewise ⟦वरुणाय⟧, for the one who covers, the god who presides over the night: for their sake "the Soma is pressed" is to be supplied. Moreover ⟦ऋताय⟧, for the sacrifice, for that which carries it through; for that purpose ⟦पीतये⟧, for drinking. For the gods' drinking of the Soma is for the accomplishment of the sacrifice. Or else, the suffix is ktic in the sense of the agent: ⟦पीतये⟧, for the drinker, the officiating priest and the others; ⟦ऋताय⟧, for the sacrifice, for that which carries it through. ⟦चारुः⟧, fit to be consumed, fit to be eaten, the Soma is ⟦सुतः⟧, pressed.

## Ṛk 3

⟦वां⟧, belonging to you two, ⟦अंशुं⟧, the Soma in the form of a creeper, ⟦दुहंति⟧: the Adhvaryus make it ready. Like what? ⟦तां⟧, that well-known one who serves for the Agnihotra and the like, ⟦वासरीं⟧, whose milk covers all her limbs; the meaning is, one who has much milk. Or else: one who covers with milk and the like. The root is ⟦वस आच्छादने⟧, vas in the sense of covering; the suffix is the uṇādi suffix ari; by the rule ⟦कृदिकारादक्तिन⟧ the feminine suffix ṅīṣ is added. Or else: the suffix is simply the uṇādi suffix ara, and then aṇ is added in the word's own sense. ⟦धेनुं न⟧, like a cow that gladdens.

By what means? That he says: ⟦अद्रिभिः⟧, with the stones that are the means of pressing. Moreover ⟦अद्रिभिः⟧, with those same means, ⟦सोमं⟧ ⟦दुहंति⟧, they milk the Soma for your sake alone: having drenched the Soma in the form of a creeper with water, for your sake alone, they milk out the juice.

Moreover, being ⟦अर्वांचा⟧, turned towards us, ⟦सोमपीतये⟧, for the drinking of the Soma, ⟦अस्मत्रा⟧, you two who are our protectors, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦गंतं⟧, come near, to the sacrifice that is ⟦नः⟧, ours. Moreover ⟦वां⟧, for your sake, ⟦अयं⟧, this Soma is ⟦सुतः⟧, pressed, ⟦नृभिः⟧, by the leaders, the officiating priests who carry the sacrifice through. Moreover it is pressed ⟦आ⟧ ⟦पीतये⟧, for drinking in full. Therefore come: this is the meaning.
