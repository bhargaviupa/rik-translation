## Ṛk 1

**NOTE**{the supplied text has no introduction to this sūkta; the reference text has one} The Agni whom (⟦यं⟧), by ⟦शिम्या⟧ — this is a name for action, because ⟦शिमी शक्ति⟧ is said in the list of its names **NOTE**{the reference text adds references for the two word-lists quoted in this block (Nighaṇṭu 2.1.24 and 1.3.8); the supplied text gives none} — that is, by the action of producing light, rain and the like, stands like Mitra (⟦मित्रं न⟧, that is, as Mitra does). Or else ⟦शिम्या⟧ is connected with the verb. ⟦गोषु⟧: many cows being the motive; ⟦गव्यवः⟧: desiring cows for themselves. Although here ⟦गव्यवः⟧ alone would be enough, and ⟦गोषु⟧ would not be needed, still in ⟦गव्यवः⟧ the ownership of cows is conveyed, and that could be had even with one cow, or with two or three. Therefore, to make it understood that many cows are to be longed for, there is the word ⟦गोषु⟧; just as in ⟦गवामसि गोपतिः⟧ (⟦ऋ ७-९८-६⟧), "You are the cow-lord of cows", and ⟦गणानां त्वा गणपतिं⟧ (⟦ऋ २-२३-१⟧), "You, the troop-lord of troops". Likewise ⟦स्वाध्यः⟧: those who meditate well, who bring forth the fruit, or who have fine meditation. ⟦विदथे⟧: in the sacrifice. ⟦अप्सु⟧: in the mid-region, where he is present in the form of lightning. ⟦अप⟧ is a name of the mid-region, because ⟦आपः पृथिवी⟧ is said in the list of its names. The arising of Agni in the mid-region is well known in other mantras such as ⟦यदग्ने दिविजा अस्यप्पुजा वा⟧ (⟦ऋ ८-४३-२८⟧), "Whether, O Agni, you are born in heaven or born in the waters", and ⟦अप्स्वगे सधिष्टव⟧ (⟦ऋ. ८-४३-९⟧), "In the waters, O Agni, is your seat". ⟦प्रियं⟧: the dearest of all, or the one who gladdens; ⟦यजतं⟧: Agni who is to be worshipped; ⟦जनुषां⟧: of those that have birth, of living beings; making their protection the motive (⟦प्रति⟧), by ⟦शिम्या⟧, by the action of churning and the like, the sacrificers ⟦जीजनन्⟧, bring him forth. Or else, the waters (⟦अप्सु⟧) being the motive, they brought him forth: the meaning is, for the benefit of all living beings and for the sake of rain. By the ⟦पाजसा⟧, the strength, of Agni thus brought forth, and by his ⟦गिरा⟧, his terrifying sound, ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth, ⟦अरेजेतां⟧, trembled. The trembling on account of Agni is well known in passages of the Veda such as ⟦अरेजेतां⟧ ⟦रोदसी होतृवूर्ये⟧ (⟦ऋ. १-३१-३⟧), "Heaven and earth trembled at the choosing of the hotṛ". Nor should it be objected, "Agni, brought forth for the protection of all, produced fear in the two worlds: what is this?", because this is praise in the form of blame; for it ends in the praise "He became so grown that the two worlds are afraid". Although Agni alone is set forth here, while Mitra is merely made the illustration, still, since Agni himself, whose place is heaven, is Mitra, the mantra's belonging to Mitra is not contradicted. Or else: the priests make manifest, in the sacrifice and in the mid-region, the god named Mitra, who stands like a friend (⟦मित्रं न⟧). The rest is to be construed in the same way, with this difference.

## Ṛk 2

O Mitra and Varuṇa, since (⟦यत्⟧), for you two (⟦वां⟧), the priests, who are friends, like friends (⟦प्र मित्रासो न⟧), hold forth in an eminent way (⟦प्र दधिरे⟧) that (⟦त्यत्⟧) rite, of the character described, of ⟦पुरुमीळस्य⟧, him who pours out much that is desired, of ⟦सोमिनः⟧ ⟦स्वाभुवः⟧, him who has Soma and whose nature it is to be fully present — the genitive is used in the sense of the object. ⟦ह⟧ is an expletive. ⟦अध⟧, for this reason, ⟦गातुं⟧, your going; ⟦अर्चते⟧ — the dative is used in the sense of the genitive — of the sacrificer who worships, ⟦क्रतुं⟧, the rite, ⟦विदतं⟧, know. From the root vid, in loṭ, the imperative, the vikaraṇa śa comes by interchange. ⟦उत⟧, and also, O ⟦वृषणा⟧, showerers of desires, you two, of ⟦पस्तावतः⟧: ⟦पस्ते⟧ is a name for a house, because ⟦पस्ता दुरोण⟧ is said in the list of its names **NOTE**{the reference text adds a reference for this word-list (Nighaṇṭu 3.4.6); the supplied text gives none}; of the sacrificer who has houses such as the sadas and the prācīnavaṃśa, the call or the praise ⟦श्रुतं⟧, hear. The dropping of the vikaraṇa is Vedic. Or else: since (⟦यत्⟧) those of the Soma-possessing (⟦सोमिनः⟧) king named Purumīḷha, who stand like friends (⟦मित्रासो न⟧), whose nature it is to be fully present (⟦स्वाभुवः⟧), who are each able in his own function, hold it (⟦दधिरे⟧), ⟦अध⟧, for this reason, of him as he worships, know the rite; and so on as before.

## Ṛk 3

O ⟦वृषणा⟧, showerers of desires, O Mitra and Varuṇa, the ⟦जन्म⟧, the birth, the origin, that belongs to you two (⟦वां⟧), from heaven and earth (⟦रोदस्योः⟧); because the origin of all is from heaven and earth alone. Or else: by "Mitra" is meant Agni, who benefits all, and by "Varuṇa" the Sun, who wards off darkness; because these two have the earth and heaven as their places. Then, in the earlier mantra Agni was praised by way of Mitra as the illustration. From this it is understood that Agni himself is Mitra. So it is elsewhere too: ⟦अग्निं मित्रं न⟧ ⟦क्षितिशु प्रशंस्यं⟧ (⟦ऋ. २-२-३⟧), "Agni, to be praised among the peoples like Mitra", and ⟦मित्रं न यं सुधितं भृगवो दधुः⟧ (⟦ऋ. ६-१५-२⟧), "whom the Bhṛgus set down, well placed like Mitra". ⟦प्रवाच्यं⟧, worthy of praise: such a birth of you two ⟦क्षितयः⟧, men, the sacrificers, ⟦आ भूषन्⟧, adorn on every side, for ⟦महे⟧, great, ⟦दक्षसे⟧, strength in every matter; the meaning is that they extol it. He states why the birth is worthy of praise. ⟦यदीं⟧: for the very reason that. Or else ⟦ईं⟧ is without meaning, because it has been said that in measured texts the meaningless words are kam, īm, id and u **NOTE**{the saying is supplied as ⟦मिताक्षरेष्टनर्थकाः कमीमिद्वितीत्युक्तत्वात्⟧ and is translated after the reference text (mitākṣareṣv anarthakāḥ kam īm id v iti); is the rendering "in measured texts" for the first word right?}. ⟦ऋताय⟧, for the sacrifice, ⟦भरथः⟧, you bear ability. And since (⟦यत्⟧) for ⟦अर्वते⟧, the sacrificer who has reached you, you bear the desired fruit. Or else, in both places the dative is used because the object is treated as the recipient: the meaning is that you support the sacrifice and the sacrificer. And since by ⟦होत्रया⟧, by speech in the form of praise, and by ⟦शिम्या⟧, by the action of offering the oblation and the like, ⟦प्र वीथः⟧, you reach in an eminent way: therefore they adorn the birth that is worthy of praise; this is the meaning. ⟦होत्रेति⟧: hotrā is a name for speech, because ⟦होत्रा गी⟧ is read among its names **NOTE**{the reference text adds a reference for this word-list (Nighaṇṭu 1.11.35); the supplied text gives none}.

## Ṛk 4

O ⟦असुरा⟧, that is, you two Asuras, mighty ones, Mitra and Varuṇa, that ⟦क्षितिः⟧ of yours, in the form of the place of sacrifice to the gods, which (⟦या⟧) is ⟦महि⟧, greatly, exceedingly, ⟦प्रिया⟧, dearer, or gladdening: that ⟦क्षितिः⟧ is ⟦प्र⟧, eminently, made ready. Because a preverb is heard, a suitable verb is to be supplied. "Sit down on it" is to be supplied. It is well known that the ground of sacrifice is dear to the gods. And having sat down, O ⟦ऋतावानाव⟧, you who possess ṛta, you two ⟦घोषथः⟧, with the preverb ā, our sacrifice, which is ⟦बृहत्⟧, full-grown **NOTE**{the reference text has the word ṛtam here before "our sacrifice" as the word of the Ṛk that is glossed; the supplied text does not have it}. The meaning is: praise it as accomplished without defect. Since ⟦युवं⟧, you two, of ⟦बृहतो⟧, the great, ⟦दिवो⟧, world of heaven, in ⟦धुरि⟧ ⟦दक्षं⟧, what is able in bearing satisfaction. Or else ⟦बृहतो दिवो⟧ means the great gods who dwell in the world of heaven, and with ⟦दक्षं⟧ "to gladden" is to be supplied. Or else the genitive is in the sense of the object: ⟦दिवो⟧, of a shining nature, ⟦बृहतः⟧, abundant, ⟦दक्षम⟧, able to accomplish what is desired, ⟦आभुवं⟧, whose nature it is to be present on every side, the ⟦अपः⟧, the rite, in the form of the Soma sacrifice, ⟦युंजाथे⟧ with ⟦उप⟧, you attend upon; the meaning is that you accept the oblation that is there. There is an illustration of this use. ⟦धुरि⟧: in the sustaining of the strength of the body, for the sake of that; ⟦गां न⟧, like a milch cow: as people make use of her, so. Or else the word for the source is used for its product: like milk and the rest. Therefore you proclaim the ṛta.

## Ṛk 5

O Mitra and Varuṇa, you two, ⟦मही⟧, on the great one — in the locative singular there is the long vowel of the same class as the preceding one; and by ⟦ईदूतौ च सप्तम्यर्थे⟧ (⟦पा. १-१-१९⟧), "and ī and ū in the sense of the locative", the word is pragṛhya — ⟦अत्र⟧, on this earth, by ⟦महिना⟧, by greatness, those cows which you ⟦ऋण्वथः⟧, cause to go, to ⟦वारं⟧, a choice place: this verb has the sense of the causative contained in it; for activity such as going depends on those two, and Mitra and Varuṇa are the presiding deities of heaven and earth. These same ⟦धेनवः⟧, cows, at evening time, through your greatness ⟦अरेणवः⟧, faultless, or not lost because they are not carried off by thieves and the like, ⟦तुजः⟧, givers of milk — the verb tuj has the sense of giving — in ⟦सद्मन्⟧, in the seat, the cow-pen, ⟦आ⟧, have taken their place, or come. Because a preverb is heard, a suitable verb is to be supplied. ⟦ता⟧, those cows, so made to act, ⟦उपरताति⟧, that is, in the locative, with the locative ending dropped: in the mid-region, which has the spread of the clouds, when it is covered with cloud, looking toward ⟦सूर्यं⟧, the impeller of all, or the one of good vigour, of fine ability, by another passage of the Veda, ⟦सुवीर्यो मर्या यथा गोपायत इति तत्सूर्यस्य सूर्यत्व⟧, "'Of good vigour, O men, in the way he guards': that is why the sun is Sūrya" **NOTE**{the reference text adds a reference for this quotation (Taittirīya-brāhmaṇa 2.2.10); the supplied text gives none}, that is, looking toward the Sun, they ⟦स्वरंति⟧, they make a sound, for the sake of roaming at ease. The root is svṛ, "in sounding and paining", of the first class. At what time? That is said: ⟦निम्रुचः⟧ ⟦उषसः⟧, that is, at all evening times and at the times of dawn. There is an illustration of this: ⟦तक्ववीरिव⟧. ⟦तक्वा⟧ is a thief; the man who goes after him is his ⟦वेता⟧. As he cries out, so do they. Thus the roaming at ease of the cows that had been penned in depends on you: this is the praise of those two.

## Ṛk 6

O Mitra and Varuṇa, the ⟦केशिनीः⟧, those that have hair, the flames of Agni, to you two (⟦वां⟧), ⟦ऋताय⟧, for the sake of the sacrifice, ⟦अनूषत⟧ with ⟦आ⟧: the meaning is that they honour you by handing over the oblation. Where? That is said. O ⟦मित्र⟧, O ⟦वरुण⟧, you both, ⟦यत्र⟧, in which sacrifice, ⟦गातुं⟧, the going, or the place of sacrifice to the gods — ⟦गातुरिति⟧: gātu is a name of the earth, because ⟦गातुः पूषे⟧ has been said **NOTE**{the reference text adds references for the two word-lists quoted in this block (Nighaṇṭu 1.1.20 and 2.21.1); the supplied text gives none} — ⟦अर्चथः⟧, you honour; the meaning is that you accept it. And having come, ⟦त्मना⟧, by yourselves alone, ⟦सृजतं⟧ with ⟦अव⟧, let the rain loose with its face downward. Having done so, ⟦धियः⟧, our rites, ⟦पिन्वतं⟧; the meaning is, make them grow. Having done thus, of ⟦विप्रस्य⟧, the wise sacrificer, of the ⟦मन्मनां⟧, the thoughtful praises, ⟦युवं⟧, you two, ⟦इरज्यथः⟧, you become the lords, the masters. The verb irajyati has the sense of lordship, because ⟦इरज्यति पत्यत⟧ is said in the list of words of that sense.

## Ṛk 7

⟦यो ह⟧: whichever sacrificer indeed, ⟦वां⟧, with you two in view, or to you two, by ⟦यज्ञैः⟧, by sacrifices as the occasion, ⟦शशमानः⟧, praising, ⟦दाशति⟧, that is, withdraws his own ownership of the oblation and the rest and makes it become your property; the same who is ⟦कविः⟧, learned, by the passage of the Veda ⟦येनूचानास्ते कवय⟧, "Those who are learned are kavis" **NOTE**{the reference text adds a reference for this quotation (Aitareya-brāhmaṇa 2.2); the supplied text gives none}; or else ⟦कविः⟧ is wise; ⟦होता⟧, the sacrificer who rightly accomplishes the offering in the fire, being ⟦मन्मसाधनः⟧, furnished with means such as substances and sacrificial fees that are worth thinking of, ⟦यजति⟧, performs the Soma sacrifice and the like: that very (⟦तं⟧) sacrificer, ⟦सुमतिं⟧, of fine understanding, ⟦उप⟧, marking him, ⟦गच्छथः⟧, you do indeed reach. ⟦अध्वरं⟧, his sacrifice, ⟦वीथः⟧, you desire. Moreover ⟦अस्मयू⟧, you two who desire us, ⟦गिरः⟧, the praises, ⟦अच्च⟧, toward them, ⟦गंतं⟧, go. The meaning is: having reached me, who am such a one, accept the praises. Although ⟦तं⟧ here appears to be a general reference, still, because ⟦अस्मयू⟧ is said, it is understood that Dīrghatamas has spoken of himself in an indirect way. Or else: he who gives and he who sacrifices, to him you go and you desire his sacrifice. Therefore, ⟦अस्मयू⟧, you two, go to me, ⟦सुमतिं⟧, of fine understanding, toward my ⟦गिरः⟧, my praises.

## Ṛk 8

O ⟦ऋतावाना⟧, you who possess the sacrifice, or who possess the water of rain, Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦युवां⟧, you two, ⟦प्रथमा⟧, the first, by ⟦यज्ञैः⟧, the means of sacrificing, by ⟦गोभिः⟧, products of the cow, milk and the rest, the sacrificers ⟦अंजते⟧, make manifest. The meaning is that in the sacrifices they offer the gods' share with milk and the like. Or else ⟦अंजते⟧ with ⟦गोभिः⟧ means that they praise. There is an illustration of this. ⟦प्रयुक्तिषु⟧, in undertakings, ⟦मनसो न⟧ — the genitive is in the sense of the object — like the mind: as they make that manifest first, make it the chief thing, because all activity is preceded by it. Moreover ⟦वां⟧, to you two, with ⟦मन्मना⟧, thoughtful, ⟦संयता⟧, going rightly to you alone, mind, they ⟦भरंति⟧, bring about, ⟦गिरः⟧, praises. Since this is so, therefore, being endowed with ⟦अदृप्यता⟧, not overpowering, ⟦मनसा⟧, an excellent mind, the ⟦रेवत्⟧, wealthy, rite of ours ⟦आशाथे⟧, you reach, you pervade; the meaning is that you give. From the root aś, in liṭ, the perfect, the augment iṭ is absent by the saying that the teaching of augments is not invariable.

## Ṛk 9

O Mitra and Varuṇa, you two ⟦दधाथे⟧ ⟦रेवत्⟧, wealthy, ⟦वयः⟧, food; and you hold both. Since this is so, therefore ⟦रेवत्⟧, wealthy — because it was seen together with it, ⟦वयः⟧ is understood — food marked by wealth, O ⟦नरा⟧, leaders, you two ⟦आशाथे⟧, you reach; the meaning is that you give it to us. Of what kind? By ⟦मायाभिः⟧, by the wisdoms of you two, ⟦इत्यऊति⟧, having a going hither, toward us, or having protection hither; ⟦माहिनं⟧ — this is a name for "great" — exceedingly great. Whence is such ability in you two, who are such? To this he says: ⟦देवत्वं⟧, the godhead, of you two (⟦वां⟧), ⟦अहभिः⟧, that is, with the nights — here by the word ahar night is denoted, because day is denoted separately by the word dyo — the ⟦द्यावः⟧, the days, together with them, ⟦नानशुः⟧, do not reach. The meaning is that both day and night do not pass beyond it. ⟦उत⟧, and also, the ⟦सिंधवः⟧, the rivers, whose nature it is to flow, did not reach it either. Likewise the ⟦पणयः⟧, the Asuras too, did not reach the godhead of you two. And the ⟦मघं⟧, the wealth, they did not reach. The meaning is that in the three worlds there are none at all who overcome you two.
