## Introduction

The hymn of five Ṛks beginning ⟦कथा त⟧ is the seventh hymn; it is of Dīrghatamas, is addressed to Agni, and is in the triṣṭubh metre. The Anukramaṇikā is the word kathā. The ritual use of this hymn in the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra has been stated by the sūtra "trimūrdhānam iti trīṇi", "the three hymns beginning trimūrdhānam".

## Ṛk 1

**NOTE**{The supplied block opens with a detached word, kāyābhyāsaṃ, which is not in the reference text and has been left untranslated; is it a stray line?} ⟦अग्ने⟧, O Agni, ⟦ते⟧, the rays that belong to you, which are ⟦शुचयंतो⟧, displaying, making manifest, their brightness everywhere, and ⟦आशुषाणा⟧: ⟦आशु⟧, quickly, partaking; or partaking of the swift ones; the meaning is that they quickly partake even of the wind and the like. Or else: drying up on all sides. Or else: pervading everything. Of the root aś there is kānac in place of liṭ; by an exchange there are the suffix u and sic, and thus the word has two conjugational signs. Or of the root śuṣ preceded by āṅ, with the sense of the causative included in it, before śānac there is the Vedic elision of śap. Or from the root san with the word āśu as the accompanying word, there is aṇ in the sense of the object. Such rays, ⟦कथा⟧, by what means, ⟦ददाशु⟧, do they give, ⟦आयोः⟧, life, length of life, together with ⟦वाजेभिः⟧, foods? The genitive is in the sense of the object. The root is dāś, in the sense of giving; it is a Vedic liṭ. **NOTE**{The supplied text cites the root as dāśya dāne, taken here as dāśṛ dāne; the reference text has dā dāne.} The meaning is: favour me with that means.

If it is asked where the granting of boons by the rays has been seen, it is told. ⟦यत्⟧, since, ⟦उभे⟧, both, the food and the life spoken of, ⟦तोके⟧, in the son, ⟦तनये⟧, in the one who extends the family without a break; or else ⟦तोके⟧, in the son, and ⟦तनये⟧, in his son and so on: ⟦दधाना⟧, holding these, the ⟦देवाः⟧, the sacrificers who carry on their business, ⟦रणयंत⟧, take delight, or cause delight, or simply make sound, ⟦सामन्⟧, in the sāman, the Rathantara and the like, which belongs to ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, the sacrifice. The meaning is: since, accompanied by food, life, sons and the like, having sacrificed with Soma, they take delight in the sāman, therefore it is understood that the rays give. The meaning is: favour me too with such rays of yours.

## Ṛk 2

⟦यविष्ठ⟧, O youngest one; ⟦स्वधावो⟧, O Agni who are possessed of it through the food that consists of the oblation; of ⟦मे⟧, my, ⟦वचसः⟧, speech which has the form of praise, which is ⟦मंहिष्ठस्य⟧, exceedingly worthy of honour, ⟦प्रभृतस्य⟧, brought about with excellence, ⟦अस्य⟧, which is being made now (the genitive is in the sense of the object): the speech so described ⟦बोध⟧, know; the meaning is: be praised. O Agni, in the world ⟦त्वः⟧ (here Yāska, having said "tvo nema ity ardhasya", "tva and nema are names of a half", cited this very passage as the example: ⟦नि. ३-२०⟧), one person, ⟦पीयति⟧: this word has the sense of killing; he injures; the meaning is that he does not honour you with sacrifice and the like. ⟦त्वः⟧, one, ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦गृणाति⟧, utters what is favourable. Between those two, ⟦अग्ने⟧, O Agni, I, ⟦वंदारुः⟧, whose habit is to salute, ⟦वंदे⟧, praise, ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦तन्नं⟧, body, your form.

## Ṛk 3

Here they tell a legend. There were two seers named Ucathya and Bṛhaspati. Of these, Ucathya had a wife named Mamatā. And she was pregnant. Bṛhaspati took hold of her and lay with her. When the moment of the emission of the semen had come, the seed that was in the womb spoke out: "O sage, do not discharge your seed; I dwell here from before; do not make a mingling of seed." Addressed thus, Bṛhaspati, his seed having been held back by force, cursed: "O embryo, since you have made an obstruction of the seed, therefore go to long darkness; be blind from birth." **NOTE**{The supplied text reads he garbha tvaṃ yato, "O embryo, since you"; the reference text reads he garbha evaṃ yato, "O embryo, since thus".} Being cursed thus, Dīrghatamas was born of Mamatā. And when he was born, through the pain of the darkness he praised Agni. And he, pleased by the praise, removed the blindness. That is what is said here.

⟦अग्ने⟧, O Agni, ⟦ते⟧, those belonging to you, ⟦ये⟧ ⟦पायवः⟧, which well-known protecting rays, ⟦पश्यंतः⟧, seeing, ⟦मामतेयं⟧, the son of Mamatā, Dīrghatamas, ⟦अंधं⟧, blind, that is, understanding "this one is blind, therefore he is to be protected by us", ⟦अरक्षन्⟧, protected him, ⟦दुःइतात्⟧, from the sorrow that had come upon him in an evil way: ⟦तान्⟧ ⟦सुकृतः⟧, those makers of happiness, ⟦विश्ववेदा⟧, he of all knowledge, ⟦ररक्ष⟧, protects. **NOTE**{After avagacchanto the reference text also has santaḥ, "being"; the supplied text does not.} The purport is: for the protecting of us. The meaning is that by them he will protect us too. Us who are thus protected, the ⟦रिपवः⟧, the enemies, desire and the like, ⟦दिप्संतो⟧, wishing to harm, ⟦नाह देभुः⟧: ⟦अह⟧ is a particle with the sense of restriction; they are indeed not able to harm. The root is dambh, in the sense of deceit. By the supplementary rule ⟦श्रंथिग्रंथिदंभिस्वंजीनामिति वक्तव्यं⟧, "it should be stated: of śranth, granth, dambh and svañj" (⟦का. १-२६-१⟧), liṭ is treated as having the indicatory k, and so by the rule "aniditām" there is the elision of n. Since that elision is treated as not having taken effect, the substitution of e and the elision of the reduplication do not apply, and so those two are enjoined by ⟦दंभेश्चेति वक्तव्यं⟧, "it should be stated: and of dambh" (⟦पा ६-४- १२०-५⟧). **NOTE**{The supplied reference ends in 120-5; the reference text has 6.4.120.4.} Or else the seer of the hymn, Dīrghatamas, himself speaks of himself in the third person.

## Ṛk 4

⟦अग्ने⟧, O Agni, ⟦नः⟧, us, who are protected by you: ⟦यः⟧, he who is ⟦अघयुः⟧, possessed of the wish for evil in the form of killing and the like, ⟦अररिऽवान्⟧, a non-giver, the meaning being one who obstructs gifts to us (of the root rā there is kvasu in place of the Vedic liṭ), ⟦अरातीवा⟧, himself possessed of non-giving (by the saying "chandasi vanipam icchanti", "in the Veda they want vanip", there is vanip in the sense of the possessive suffix), the enemy who, practising enmity, ⟦द्वयेन⟧, by what is twofold through the division into mental and verbal. **NOTE**{After dvividhena the reference text also has mantreṇa, "by a counsel"; the supplied text does not.} "I do not give" is the mental counsel. Preventing a gift by imputing blame and the like is the verbal counsel. Or else: by a twofold counsel of contradictory form, which has deceit as its cause. What contrary thing he does, that he does not proclaim; and what pleasant thing he says, that he does not do; he does one thing and says another: such is the twofoldness of the form of the counsel. Before it was by the division into mental and verbal, now it is by the division into verbal and bodily: such is the distinction. The enemy who by such a counsel ⟦मर्चयति⟧, threatens, or makes subservient, us: let that one part of the ⟦मंत्रः⟧, the counsel, the one of mental form, ⟦पुनः⟧ ⟦अस्तु⟧ ⟦गुरुः⟧ ⟦अस्मै⟧ (the dative is in the sense of the genitive): let it in return be the swallower of the very one who employs it. Likewise by ⟦दुःउक्तैः⟧, by evil words, verbal ones that have the form of blame, ⟦तन्वं⟧, his own body, ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦मृक्षीष्ट⟧, let him wipe away, let him destroy step by step. Or else: let ⟦सः⟧, that twofold counsel, be ⟦अस्मै⟧, of the very one who employs it, the ⟦गुरुः⟧, the swallower. There are other harsh speeches too, employed for our humiliation. By those ill-spoken words let that enemy destroy his own body alone; the meaning is: let it turn back and burn his own self alone.

## Ṛk 5

In the preceding mantra it was said: "Let that counselling reach the one who does it with a twofold crooked counsel, not us." Here, however, Agni is prayed to: "protect from that counselling." ⟦उत वा⟧, ⟦सहस्य⟧: ⟦सह⟧ is a name of strength; O Agni who are born in that; ⟦यः⟧ ⟦मर्तः⟧, which mortal, a man whose nature is to die, ⟦प्रविद्वान्⟧, knowing deceitful counselling excellently, ⟦द्वयेन⟧, with a twofold counsel in the manner stated before, ⟦मर्चयति⟧, makes subservient, or threatens, ⟦मर्तं⟧, a man: ⟦अतः⟧, from such threatening, or from the one who does it, ⟦स्तवमान⟧, O Agni who are being praised, ⟦पाहि⟧, protect, me, ⟦स्तुवंतं⟧, who am making praise. Moreover ⟦नः⟧, us, ⟦माकिर्धायीः⟧, do not set as fit ⟦दुःइतायॆ⟧, for the sorrow caused by him, brought on by the evil counselling; the meaning is: do not make us a vessel of misfortune. The root is dhi, in the sense of holding; by an exchange there is the augment iṭ; before sic there is vṛddhi; by the rule "na māṅyoge" the augment aṭ is absent.
