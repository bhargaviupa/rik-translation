## Ṛk 1

I have placed ⟦पुरः⟧, in front, on the uttaravedi, ⟦अग्निं⟧, the fire called Āhavanīya, ⟦धिया⟧, by the act of carrying it forward and the rest; ⟦दधे⟧ is "I have set it in place". ⟦तत्⟧ ⟦शर्धः⟧ is such a mighty one, or Agni; or else ⟦तत्⟧ ⟦शर्धः⟧ is such strength, which is the very form of the Maruts. That, which is ⟦दिव्यं⟧, existing in heaven, ⟦नु⟧, quickly, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦वृणीमहे⟧: we resort to it, turned towards it. Moreover we choose ⟦इंद्रवायू⟧, Indra and Vāyu. ⟦यत्⟧ is "for which reason": ⟦विवस्वति⟧, in the shining, ⟦नाभा⟧, in the navel, that is, in the place of the navel of the earth, the place of sacrifice to the gods which has the form of the vedi. Or else: in the navel, that is, in the sacrifice, which binds every fruit to itself, because of the scriptural text ⟦यज्ञमाहुर्भुवनस्य नाभिमिति⟧, "they call the sacrifice the navel of the world". ⟦क्राणा⟧ is "making", namely the manifesting of its own meaning; ⟦नव्यसी⟧ is the newer speech in the form of praise; ⟦संदायि⟧ is "is bound". This is a Vedic luṅ in the passive. The meaning is: it is employed by us. Since praise is being made, therefore ⟦अस्तु⟧ ⟦श्रौषट्⟧: let there be a hearing of this praise. Or else let the troop of the Maruts, or Agni, be a hearer. On the view that Indra and Vāyu are meant, the singular is used with regard to each one separately. ⟦अध⟧ is "thereafter"; ⟦नः⟧ ⟦धीतयः⟧, our acts in the form of praise and the like, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦सु⟧ ⟦उप⟧ ⟦यंतु⟧: may they approach you eminently and well. Moreover ⟦देवान्⟧ ⟦अच्छ⟧ ⟦न⟧, as if to reach **NOTE**{The supplied text has ⟦प्राप्तुमिव⟧, "as if to reach"; the reference text has the participle, "as if reached".} the gods, Agni and the rest, face to face, may ⟦धीतयः⟧, our acts, approach: may they reach the presence of those gods.

## Ṛk 2

O ⟦मित्रावरुणौ⟧, Mitra and Varuṇa: ⟦ऋतात्⟧, from the sun, ⟦त्यत्⟧, that water, ⟦यत्⟧, for which reason, ⟦अधि⟧, abundantly, ⟦आददाथे⟧, you give on every side. Of what kind is the water? ⟦अनृतं⟧, perishable, of a nature to dry up. By what means? ⟦स्वेन मन्युना⟧, by your own splendour. Mitra and Varuṇa, the deities who preside over day and night, indeed release the rain. The scriptural text beginning ⟦अहोरात्रॆ वा⟧ has already been quoted earlier. The ⟦मन्युना⟧ is further qualified: ⟦दक्षस्य⟧, of the capable one, ⟦स्वेन मन्युना⟧, by the capacity which is his own and which is worthy to be thought upon. Moreover, O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦युवोः⟧ (the dropping of the final is Vedic) is "belonging to you two"; ⟦हिरण्ययं⟧ is the golden form. In the rule that begins ⟦ऋत्ववास्तवास्त्यादौ⟧ the dropping of the syllable ma of the suffix mayaṭ is laid down as a ready-made form. ⟦इत्था⟧ is "thus", in the manner about to be stated; ⟦सद्मसु⟧, in the seats of sacrifice, ⟦अधि⟧ ⟦अपश्याम⟧: may we see it. By what means? That is stated: ⟦धीभिः⟧, by acts in the form of sacrifice and the like, directed to you; ⟦मनसा⟧ ⟦चन⟧, and by a mind attached to you; ⟦स्वेभिः⟧ ⟦अक्षभिः⟧, by our own senses and no others, the eye and the rest. Moreover ⟦सोमस्य⟧ is "the Soma", as object; ⟦स्वेभिः⟧ ⟦अक्षभिः⟧, with our senses. The plural is used with regard to the nose and the rest.

## Ṛk 3

On the sixth day of the Pṛṣṭhya **NOTE**{The supplied text has ⟦पृष्ठस्य⟧ here and on Ṛks 6 and 9; "Pṛṣṭhya" is translated from the context only, and the reference text does not have these opening passages on the ritual use of the verses.}, in the Praüga-śastra, the triplet addressed to the Aśvins is the one beginning ⟦युवां स्तोमेभिर⟧. It is laid down in the sūtra, in the section beginning ⟦षष्ठस्येति⟧: ⟦युवां स्तोमेभिर्देवयंतो आश्विनावर्मह इंद्र⟧ (⟦आ. ८_१⟧), "'You two with praises, desiring the gods, O Aśvins'; 'Downward, great Indra'".

O ⟦अश्विना⟧, Aśvins, gods: ⟦युवां⟧, you two, ⟦स्तोमेभिः⟧, with praises, ⟦देवयंतः⟧, desiring you two for themselves, the ⟦आयवः⟧ (this is a name for men), the sacrificers, praise you, ⟦आश्रवयंतः इव⟧, as though making ⟦श्लोकं⟧, speech in the form of praise of you, an object of hearing on every side. ⟦श्लोक⟧ is a name of speech, because it is stated among the names of speech in ⟦श्लोको धारेति⟧, "śloka, dhārā". Of what kind are they? Towards ⟦युवां⟧, you two, ⟦हव्या⟧, with an oblation of ghee and the like, they are ⟦अभि⟧ ⟦आयवः⟧, or else they cause it to reach you, face to face **NOTE**{The reference text has more here: "going towards you, face to face; 'in order to gladden' is to be supplied; or causing the oblations to reach you, face to face".}. Moreover, O ⟦विश्ववेदसा⟧, you who possess all wealth: ⟦युवोः⟧, belonging to you two, ⟦विश्वाः⟧ ⟦श्रियः⟧, all fortunes worthy to be resorted to, in the form of wealth, gold and the like, ⟦अधि⟧, abundantly; "they obtain" is to be supplied. Moreover ⟦पृक्षः⟧ ⟦च⟧, food also, they get abundantly by your grace. Moreover, O ⟦दस्रा⟧, destroyers of enemies: ⟦वां⟧ ⟦हिरण्यये⟧ ⟦रथे⟧, on your golden chariot, which is full of honey, ⟦पवयः⟧, its fellies, ⟦प्रुषायंते⟧: they trickle, they flow. The root is ⟦पुष प्लुष स्नेहनसेचनपूरणेषु⟧, "in the senses of moistening, sprinkling and filling". By the rule ⟦छंदसि शायजपीत्यहावपि⟧, "in the Veda śāyac also", the substitute śāyac takes the place of śnā even where hi does not follow. The sense is: because the chariot is furnished with vessels full of honey, the honey flows on the wheels through its swift going. ⟦पवी रथनेमिर्भवति⟧, "pavī is the felly of a chariot, because it cleaves the earth": so the Nirukta (⟦निरुक्तं । ५-५⟧). That the chariot of the Aśvins is full of honey is well known in passages such as ⟦मधुवाहने रथे⟧, "three fellies on the honey-bearing chariot" (⟦ऋ. १-३४-२⟧). Moreover, on such a golden chariot, which delights the heart **NOTE**{The supplied text has ⟦तादृशे हिरण्मये हृदयरमणे⟧, "golden, delighting the heart"; in the reference text the word of the Ṛk, ⟦हिरण्यये⟧, stands there and is itself glossed "delighting the heart".}, "carry the sweet oblation" is to be supplied.

## Ṛk 4

O ⟦दस्रा⟧, Aśvins: ⟦अचेति⟧, your deed, which is about to be told, is known by all. What is it? That is stated: ⟦नाकं⟧, to heaven, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦ऋण्वथः⟧, you go in a distinguished way. The root is ⟦ऋणु गत्⟧, "to go", of the tan class; in leṭ the augment aṭ is added. That is to say, after the completion of the sacrifice. The word u is for emphasis. Or else: ⟦नाकं⟧; aka is pain, and to the sacrifice, which is free of it, ⟦ऋण्वथः⟧, you do indeed go in a distinguished way. For that purpose ⟦वां⟧ ⟦रथयुजः⟧, the yokers of the chariot that belongs to you, your charioteers, ⟦दिविष्टिषु⟧, when there are seekings of heaven, or seekings of, that is, goings to, the sacrifice, whose nature is to shine, these being the occasion, ⟦युंजते⟧: they yoke the horses to the chariot; or else they join you to the yoked chariot. These same men are further qualified: ⟦दिविष्टिषु⟧, in the goings through heaven, the goings through the supportless sky, they are ⟦अध्वस्थानः⟧, causing no ruin of the chariot or of what rests on it. Moreover, O ⟦दस्रा⟧, Aśvins: ⟦वां⟧, of you two, ⟦वंधुरे⟧, furnished with three bandhuras. A bandhura is a particular piece of wood which is the support for fastening the yoke. That the chariot has three such is well known in passages such as ⟦रथो यो नां त्रिवंधुरः⟧, "the chariot of you two which has three bandhuras" (⟦ऋ ८-२२-५⟧). ⟦हिरण्यये⟧ ⟦रथे⟧ ⟦अधि⟧, upon such a golden chariot, ⟦स्थाम⟧: we have made you stand. In the Vedic laṅ the augment aṭ is absent by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीत्यडभावः⟧, "variously in the Veda". The udātta on the first syllable is Vedic. Of what kind are you two? ⟦पथेव⟧, as by a good path, a road, in the supportless sky, ⟦यंत्⟧, going towards ⟦रजः⟧, heaven, whose nature is to delight, because it has been said ⟦लोका रजांस्युच्यंत⟧, "the worlds are called rajas"; ⟦अनुशासता⟧, chastising the unfriendly enemies, making them submissive. Since śās, when the suffix śatṛ follows, is termed abhyasta by the rule ⟦जक्षित्यादय⟧, the udātta falls on the first syllable by the rule that the first syllable of abhyasta stems is accented. Moreover ⟦अंजसा⟧, principally, ⟦रजः⟧, the excellent water characterised as rain, ⟦शासता⟧: ruling it, making it submissive by force.

## Ṛk 5

O ⟦शचीवसू⟧: śacī is a name of action; you whose wealth is the acts, the Jyotiṣṭoma and the rest, performed by us. You two, ⟦शचीभिः⟧, because of our acts, sacrifice and the like, ⟦दिवा⟧ ⟦नक्तं⟧, by day and by night, ⟦दशस्यतं⟧: give what is desired. This is a Vedic form of the root ⟦दाश्य दान⟧, "to give". Or else ⟦दशस्यतिर्दानार्थः⟧, the verb daśasyati, meaning "to give", is to be looked for among the kaṇḍvādi roots. ⟦वां⟧, of you two, ⟦रातिः⟧, the giving, ⟦कदा⟧ ⟦चन⟧, always, at the time of sacrifice and also when it is not the time of sacrifice, ⟦मा⟧ ⟦उप⟧ ⟦दसत्⟧: may it not be exhausted. The root is ⟦दसु उपक्षये⟧, "to waste away"; in luṅ, aṅ takes the place of cli by the rule ⟦पुषादिद्युतादीति⟧. Not only yours, but ours also: ⟦अस्मत्⟧ ⟦रातिः⟧, our giving, the offering of oblations and the like, or the giving of all things to those who ask, ⟦कदा⟧ ⟦चन⟧, in every condition whatever, may it not be exhausted; may it go on always. The meaning is: may I too always give with you in view, and may you two also always give what I desire **NOTE**{The supplied text has ⟦मनभिमतं⟧, read as "what I desire"; the reference text has only "what is desired".}.

## Ṛk 6

On the sixth day of the Pṛṣṭhya, in the triplet to Indra of the Praüga-śastra, this verse beginning ⟦वृषन्निंद्रे⟧ is the third: ⟦वृषन्निंद्र वृषपाणास इंदवः सुषुमा यातमद्रिभिः⟧ (⟦आ. ८-१⟧), "'O showerer, Indra, the drops that are the showerer's drink'; 'We have pressed; come, you two, with the stones'". At that same pressing other verses are to be inserted before the prasthita-yājyās, and the offering is to be made with both together. There this verse is to be inserted before the prasthita-yājyā of the hotṛ. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦षष्ठस्य प्रातःसवने प्रस्थितयाज्यानां पुरस्तादन्याः कृत्वोभाभ्यामनवानंतो यजंति⟧ (⟦आ. ८-१⟧), "at the morning pressing of the sixth day, having placed other verses before the prasthita-yājyās, they offer with both without taking breath".

O ⟦इंद्र⟧, ⟦वृषन्⟧, showerer of desires: ⟦इमे⟧, these, seen in front, ⟦इंदवः⟧, Somas, are ⟦सुताः⟧, pressed for your sake. Of what kind are they? ⟦वृषपानासः⟧, to be drunk by you, the showerer of what is desired; ⟦अद्रिसुतासः⟧, pressed by stones, firm rocks that are the means of pressing; ⟦उत्भिदः⟧, making the rain break forth. The same are qualified again: ⟦उत्भिदः⟧, making strength break forth, or sprung up on a mountain or the like; such Somas are ⟦तुभ्यं⟧ ⟦सुतासः⟧, pressed for your sake alone. Moreover ⟦ते⟧, such Somas, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦मंदंतु⟧: may they satisfy. Where? That is stated: ⟦दावने⟧, at the sacrifice, which is marked by giving; or, for the giving of what is desired. Likewise ⟦महे⟧, for great, ⟦चित्राय⟧, revered, or variegated, of many kinds, ⟦राधसे⟧, wealth. Moreover, O ⟦गिर्वाहः⟧, bearer of words, of our praises (the suffix asun by the rule ⟦वहिहाधा इच्छ⟧, "after vah, hā and dhā"): ⟦गीकभिः⟧, by our praises, ⟦स्तवमानः⟧, being praised, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गहि⟧, come. ⟦नः⟧, to us, being ⟦सुमृळीकः⟧, one who makes us thoroughly happy, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गहि⟧, come.

## Ṛk 7

On the sixth day of the Daśarātra, at the morning pressing, this verse beginning ⟦ओ षू णो अग्ने⟧ is to be inserted before the prasthita-yājyā of the neṣṭṛ; the two are made into one and the offering is made with both. It is laid down in the sūtra, in the section beginning ⟦षष्ठस्येति⟧: ⟦ओ षू णो अग्ने शृणुहि त्वमीळितोऽग्निं होतारं⟧ (⟦आ. ८-१⟧), "'Hear us well, O Agni, you who are praised'; 'Agni the hotṛ'". At that same pressing it is the second verse in the Praüga-śastra. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦अस्तु श्रौषळो षू णो अग्ने⟧ (⟦आ. ८-१⟧), "'Let there be hearing'; 'Hear us well, O Agni'".

O ⟦अग्ने⟧: ⟦त्वं⟧, you, ⟦ईळितः⟧, being praised by us, hear well ⟦नः⟧, our invocation or praise. ⟦ओ इति⟧ is a single particle made up of a combination of two particles. Hear well, turned towards us indeed. The ending hi is not dropped, because under the rule ⟦उतश्च⟧, "and after the u of a suffix", there is the statement that in the Veda this is optional. And having heard, ⟦देवेभ्यः⟧, to those whose nature is to shine, ⟦यज्ञियेभ्यः⟧, who are worthy of sacrifice; the meaning is, to those who have a share in the oblation and a share in the praise. The suffix gha comes by the rule ⟦यज्ञर्त्विग्भामिति⟧. To them ⟦ब्रवसि⟧, tell, the rite that belongs to the ⟦यज्ञियेभ्यः⟧, the sacrificers, who are worthy of sacrifice. It is from the root of bravīti; in leṭ the augment aṭ is added. To what kind of gods? ⟦राजभ्यः⟧, to the shining ones. Now the gods are addressed directly. Here they tell a legend. The great seers named Aṅgirases, formerly, for the sake of sacrifice, pleased the gods with praise and asked for cows. The gods, being pleased, gave a wish-yielding cow. And being unable to milk the milk from that cow when they had obtained her, they entreated the god Aryaman. And he, being entreated, milked the milk for the Agnihotra and the like. That is what is said here. ⟦यत्⟧, which, ⟦त्यां⟧, that well-known, ⟦धेनुं⟧, cow that gladdens with milk and the like, ⟦ह⟧ ⟦देवाः⟧, O gods, ⟦अंगिरःभ्यः⟧, for the sake of the Aṅgirases, ⟦अदत्तन⟧, you gave. The substitution of tana comes by the rule ⟦तप्तनप्तनथनाश्चति⟧. ⟦तां⟧, that cow, ⟦अर्यमा⟧, the god who is the controller of all, ⟦कर्तरि⟧, in you, Agni, the producer of all, ⟦सचा⟧, together with the other gods, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦दुह्रे⟧: he milked in various ways. From duh, in liṭ, the augment ruṭ **NOTE**{The reference text does not have the word for the augment, ⟦रुट्⟧.} comes by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧, "variously in the Veda"; the absence of reduplication is because of the statement ⟦छंदसि वेति⟧, "optionally in the Veda". Or else it is in laṭ itself, with the dropping of t by the rule ⟦लोपस्त⟧. ⟦एषः⟧, this Aryaman alone, ⟦तां⟧, that cow, ⟦मे⟧ ⟦सचा⟧, in union with me, ⟦वेद⟧, knows. The meaning is: Aryaman knows her, and I too know her. Such a cow is milked for your sake alone: this is the praise of Agni.

## Ṛk 8

The verse beginning ⟦मो षु वो⟧ is a prasthita-yājyā. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦मो षु वो अस्मदभि तानि⟧ ⟦पौंस्यौ शू णो अग्ने⟧ (⟦आ. ८-१⟧), "'Let not those manly powers of yours overcome us'; 'Hear us well, O Agni'".

O ⟦मरुतः⟧: ⟦वः⟧, belonging to you, ⟦तानि⟧, those well-known, ⟦पौंस्या⟧, strengths, which are ⟦सना⟧, perpetual, eternal, and ⟦द्युम्नानि⟧, endowed with light. Or else ⟦सना⟧ is "to be partaken of" and ⟦द्युम्नानि⟧ is "possessing wealth" or "possessing fame"; the possessive suffix is dropped. In ⟦मो इति⟧, a combination of two particles, the word u is for emphasis. ⟦अस्मत्⟧, us, may they in no way overpower: may they indeed not thoroughly overpower us. Or else: may they not be gone away from us. ⟦उत⟧ is "and also". Or else the word ⟦द्युम्नानि⟧ is construed with what follows: ⟦द्युम्नानि⟧, our riches or our fames, ⟦मा⟧ ⟦जारिषुः⟧, may they not be wasted. ⟦उत⟧, and also, ⟦अस्मत्⟧ ⟦पुरा⟧, our cities, that is, by transfer of sense, the living beings who dwell in them, ⟦मा⟧ ⟦जारिषुः⟧: may they not be decayed, destroyed. Moreover whatever there is ⟦वः⟧, belonging to you, that is ⟦चित्रं⟧, to be revered, of many kinds, ⟦नव्यं⟧, new, or to be praised, ⟦अमर्त्यं⟧, not subject to death, or hard to obtain among mortals; and ⟦घोषात्⟧, which stands for the nominative plural, the ablative being used by interchange of cases, that is, the cows and the like, which are endowed with sound, or the villages, towns and the like which are indicated by the word ghoṣa, whatever there are: all that, ⟦युगे युगे⟧, at each several time, indicated by the word yuga, ⟦अस्मासु⟧; "let it be in us alone" is to be supplied, not another's. Moreover, O Maruts, ⟦यत्⟧ ⟦च⟧ ⟦दुस्तरं⟧, and the wealth that is hard to cross to, that is, hard to acquire, ⟦दिधृत⟧, hold: hold that in us. Moreover ⟦दुस्तरं⟧ is "unharmed by enemies": all that too establish especially in us.

## Ṛk 9

On the sixth day of the Pṛṣṭhya, this verse beginning ⟦दध्यज् ह म⟧ is put before the prasthita-yājyā of the acchāvāka. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦दध्यज' ह मे जनुषं पूर्वो अंगिरा इत्येवमेव माध्यंदिने⟧ (⟦आ. ८-१⟧), "'Dadhyañc knows my birth, the ancient Aṅgiras'; in just the same way at the midday pressing".

⟦दध्यज्⟧ is the great seer of this name, born in the lineage of Atharvan. And he is ⟦पूर्वः⟧, the one who fills all, or the ancient one; ⟦अंगिराः⟧ is a live coal: the meaning is, brilliant like one. Or else the coals themselves became the Aṅgirases, because of the scriptural text ⟦येऽंगारा आसंस्तेऽंगिरसोऽभवन्निति⟧, "those which were the coals became the Aṅgirases". ⟦अंगिरा अंगारा⟧, "Aṅgiras: the coals, which mark, which move": so the Nirukta. And it is a great seer of this name. ⟦प्रियमेधः⟧ is one to whom wisdom is dear. ⟦प्रिया अस्य मेधाः⟧, "Priyamedha: dear to him is wisdom" (⟦नि ३ १७⟧): so Yāska. And there is ⟦कण्वः⟧, the wise one; and ⟦अत्रिः⟧, the one free of the three pains. ⟦अत्रिर्न त्रय⟧, "Atri: not three": so the Nirukta (⟦निरुक्तं । ३-१७⟧). ⟦मनुः⟧ is the great seer of this name, who is possessed of thought. All these great-souled great seers ⟦विदुः⟧, know, ⟦मे⟧ ⟦जनुषं⟧, the birth of me, Parucchepa, the son of Divodāsa. From the root of vetti, us is substituted for jhi by the rule ⟦वेत्तेर्विदो लटो वेति⟧. Moreover ⟦ते⟧, those aforesaid, ⟦पूर्वे⟧, men of former time: ⟦मे⟧, the meaning is that they know our fathers and the rest. ⟦मनुः⟧ stands for the plural, "possessed of knowledge". Since they are ancient and possessed of knowledge, therefore they know: this is the sense. The ending jas is dropped by the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧. He states the ground of their knowing. ⟦तेषां⟧, of those, Dadhyañc and the rest, ⟦देवेषु⟧, among the others, the great seers whose nature is to shine, there is ⟦आयतिः⟧, a connection of long duration. Therefore they know: that is the meaning. And what follows from that? He says: ⟦अस्माकं⟧ ⟦नाभयः⟧, our vital breaths, which are bound up with the living self, or our sacrifices, which are bound to their fruit, are ⟦तेषु⟧, in them alone. The dependence on them lies in this, that they have to declare: "This man is of distinguished birth, and so is fit for sacrifice." ⟦तेषां⟧, of those, Dadhyañc and the rest, ⟦पदेन⟧, on account of their station, their greatness, ⟦गिरा⟧, with speech in the form of praise, ⟦महि⟧, greatly, exceedingly, ⟦नमे⟧: every person bows. Moreover, being fit for sacrifice by their favour, to ⟦इंद्राग्नी⟧, Indra and Agni, ⟦गिरा⟧, being furnished with speech in the form of praise, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦नमे⟧: I bow, turned towards them. The meaning is: I praise and I bow. The middle voice of nam is by interchange. Or else, in the reflexive passive, the suffix yak is prohibited by the rule ⟦दुहस्सु नमामिति⟧: "I am bowed of my own accord."

## Ṛk 10

This ⟦होता⟧, the divine one, Agni, or the human one, the invoker of the gods, ⟦यक्षत्⟧: let him recite the yājyā. From yaj, in leṭ, the augment aṭ is added, and sip by the rule ⟦सिब्बहुलमिति⟧. ⟦वनिनः⟧, the gods, who are possessed of partaking, or who desire the oblation, ⟦वार्यं⟧, the Soma, which is worthy to be chosen, ⟦वंत⟧: let them partake of it. From vanati, in the Vedic laṅ, the middle voice is by interchange; the dropping of śap is by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧, "variously in the Veda"; the dropping of the final is Vedic. For that purpose ⟦बृहस्पतिः⟧, the protector of the great mantra or of the great oblation, that is, the adhvaryu, or else the divine Bṛhaspati himself, ⟦यजति⟧: he performs the sacrifice. Of what kind is he? ⟦वेनः⟧, desiring. By what means? That is stated: ⟦उक्षभिः⟧, with Somas, which sprinkle, ⟦पुरुवारेभिः⟧, which are to be chosen by many; likewise ⟦उक्षभिः⟧, with waters, which are able to sprinkle and which are produced as rain, by way of the sun, through the oblations that are offered and are their cause. We, the sacrificers, grasp with our ears ⟦दूरेऽदिशं⟧, that whose announcement is in a distant place (the case-ending is not dropped, by the rule ⟦हलदंतात्सप्तम्याः⟧, ⟦पा. ६-३-९⟧), ⟦श्लोकं⟧, the voice, the sound, ⟦अद्रेः⟧ (the singular is used for the class), of the pressing-stone that is the means of pressing: ⟦जगृभ्य⟧ is "we grasp". The plural is honorific. Now the verse speaks in the third person. ⟦सुक्रतुः⟧, this sacrificer, whose acts are good, ⟦अध⟧, then, after the completion of the sacrifice, ⟦अररिंदानि⟧ (this is a name of water, because it is read among the names of water in ⟦अररिंदानि ध्वस्मन्वदिति⟧), the waters characterised as rain, ⟦त्मना⟧, by himself, ⟦अधारयत्⟧: he holds. ⟦ररिर्दाता⟧: rari is a giver. There is no other giver of this quenching of thirst; they give such a quenching of thirst, and so are ⟦अररिंदानि⟧. Or else arari is a going hither and thither; the suffix ari is one of the uṇādi suffixes. The suffix ka then comes by the rule ⟦आतोऽनुपसर्गॆ कः⟧. Because the word belongs to the pṛṣodarādi group, the desired form and accent are obtained. They give that, and so the waters are ⟦अररिंदानि⟧: the meaning is, givers of movement, because of the scriptural text ⟦आपोमयः प्राणः⟧, "breath consists of water" (⟦छां. उ. ६-५-४⟧). Or else ⟦ररिर्दानं⟧: rari is giving. Giving of such a kind does not exist in the other elements: that is arari. They give that, and so are ⟦अररिंदानि⟧. The meaning is: they give an enjoyment which benefits the world and cannot be given by others. The meaning is that he has produced such waters by the sacrifice. Moreover ⟦सुक्रतुः⟧, this man whose acts are good, ⟦पुरु⟧, many, ⟦सद्मानि⟧, seats, houses fit to dwell in at ease, ⟦अधारयत्⟧, has held.

Or else: this adhvaryu, who is ⟦बृहस्पतिः⟧ and ⟦सुक्रतुः⟧, has held ⟦अररिंदानि⟧, the waters of the kind described, which are the means of pressing. Likewise ⟦सुक्रतुः⟧, he of good understanding, has held ⟦पुरु⟧ ⟦सद्मानि⟧, seats, the places where the oblations are set down.

## Ṛk 11

In the Daśarātra, on the sixth day, in the Praüga-śastra, this verse beginning ⟦ये देवास⟧ is the second of the triplet to the All-gods. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦ये देवासो दिव्येकादश स्थेयमददाद्रभसु⟧ (⟦आ. ८-१⟧), "'You gods who are eleven in heaven'; 'She gave the impetuous one'".

O ⟦देवासः⟧, gods, whose nature is to shine: ⟦ये⟧, you who, ⟦दिवि⟧, in the world of heaven, ⟦एकादश⟧ ⟦स्थ⟧, are of this number, namely one and ten; likewise ⟦पृथिव्यां⟧ ⟦अधि⟧, upon the earth, ⟦एकादश⟧ ⟦स्थ⟧, are of this number; and moreover ⟦अप्स्युक्षितः⟧. The mid-region is called by that word because it is the cause of them. They dwell in the waters, that is, in the mid-region, and so are ⟦अप्स्युक्षितः⟧. The suffix kvip is added to the root of kṣiyati; the case-ending is not dropped, by the rule ⟦तत्पुरुषे कृतीत्यलुक्⟧. You gods who dwell in the mid-region ⟦एकादश⟧ ⟦स्थ⟧, are of that number. Or else this is a designation of the mid-region, because ⟦आपः पृथिवी⟧ is read among the names of the mid-region. Although the deities who preside over the earth and the rest are only three, since it has been said ⟦तिस्र एव देवता⟧, "there are only three deities", yet ⟦महिना⟧, by greatness, by the manifold power of each one, they become thirty-three in number, since it has been said ⟦तत्तत्स्थाना अन्यास्तद्विभूतय⟧, "the others, which have their several places, are the manifold powers of these". The two words ⟦महिना⟧ and ⟦देवासः⟧ are construed everywhere. ⟦ते⟧, you, O ⟦देवासः⟧, gods who taken together are of the number stated, ⟦इमं⟧, this our, ⟦यज्ञं⟧, sacrifice, ⟦जुषध्वं⟧: partake of it; or else, having enjoyed the oblation that belongs to it, gladden yourselves. One and ten are ⟦एकादश⟧. The long vowel comes from the ready-made form in the rule ⟦प्रागेकादशभ्यः⟧ (⟦पा. ५-३-४९⟧). By the rule ⟦संख्यॆति⟧ the first member of the compound keeps its original accent.
