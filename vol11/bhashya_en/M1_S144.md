## Introduction

The sūkta beginning ⟦एति प्र होतेति⟧, of seven Ṛks, is the fifth sūkta; it is of Dīrghatamas and in the jagatī metre. Because it was said earlier ⟦पूर्वत्राग्नेयं तु तदित्युक्तत्वादिदमप्याग्नेयं⟧, "but that belongs to Agni", this one also belongs to Agni. And so the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦एति प्र सप्त जागतमिति⟧, "The sūkta beginning eti pra: seven Ṛks; in jagatī." The employment in the Prātaranuvāka and the Āśvina-śastra has been stated.

## Ṛk 1

This ⟦होता⟧, the one who accomplishes the offering, the Adhvaryu, in order to do the ⟦व्रतं⟧ of this (⟦अस्य⟧) Agni, the action in the form of the giving of the oblation and the like, being endowed with ⟦मायया⟧, with knowledge of the particular things that are to be done, ⟦प्रैति⟧; that is, he goes forth excellently, to offer. ⟦मायेति⟧: māyā is a name of knowledge, because it is read among its names: ⟦माया वयुनमिति⟧, "māyā, vayuna" **NOTE**{Ṛk 1: the reference text adds here the reference "ni. 3. 9. 9", which the supplied text does not have.}. Of what kind is he? He is ⟦दधानः⟧, bearing, a ⟦धियं⟧, an understanding, that is ⟦ऊर्ध्वामुपर्यारूढां⟧, ūrdhvā, mounted on high, and ⟦शुचिपेशसं⟧, provided with a beautiful form. ⟦पेश⟧ is a name of form. This is because it is said: ⟦चतुर्थात्कर्मणोऽभिसमीक्षेत्रेदं करिष्यतीत्युक्तत्वात्⟧, "Up to the fourth action he should look ahead: 'this he will do'." And, having come, he ⟦क्रमते⟧, takes, the ⟦स्रुचः⟧, the juhū and the others, which are ⟦दक्षिणावृत⟧, moving in the rightward direction. Of what kind are the ladles? Those which (⟦याः⟧), ⟦प्रथमं⟧, at the time of the setting down of the vessels, ⟦निंसते⟧ the ⟦धाम⟧, the place, of this Agni — the root is ⟦निंस चुंबन⟧, "niṃs in the sense of kissing" — that is, they kiss it; the meaning is that they resort to it. The meaning is: first he goes forth with the order of what is to be performed in his mind; afterwards he takes the ladles. The word ha is used of what is well known. Or else: the ⟦होता⟧, the invoker of the gods, this Agni, being endowed with a special knowledge, ⟦एति⟧ to the ⟦व्रतं⟧, the rite such as the Jyotiṣṭoma, of this sacrificer; that is, he comes towards the place of the sacrifice. Of what kind is he? He is ⟦दधानो⟧, holding, a ⟦धियं⟧, an action in the form of the moving of the flames and the like, that is ⟦ऊर्ध्वामूर्ध्ववर्ति⟧, ūrdhvā, being above, and ⟦शुचिपेशसं⟧, provided with a beautiful form. And, having come, he ⟦अभिक्रमते⟧ the ladles of the character described, which are ⟦दक्षिणावृत⟧, and full of ghee, the juhū, the upabhṛt and the others; that is, he steps upon them. The meaning is that the flames creep forward to accept the ghee. Those ladles which ⟦प्रथमं⟧, before, at the time of the setting down of the oblation, ⟦निंसते⟧, kiss, having reached it, the ⟦धाम⟧, the place, of this Agni, characterised as the altar. The word ha is used of what is well known. The root is ⟦णिसि चुंबने⟧, "nis in the sense of kissing". The elision of śap is because the root belongs to the class beginning with ad. The augment num is because the root has an indicatory i.

## Ṛk 2

The ⟦दोहना⟧, the streams, of ⟦ऋतस्योदकस्य⟧, of ṛta, of water, in the ⟦योनावुत्पत्तिस्थाने⟧, in the yoni, the place of origin, in the ⟦देवस्य सदने⟧, in the place of the shining Sun, being ⟦परिवृताः⟧, pervaded all around by his rays, ⟦अनूषत⟧ (⟦अभि⟧); that is, they become new. Or, as soon as they come to be **NOTE**{Ṛk 2: the supplied text has ⟦अभवंत्येव⟧, the reference text "ābhavantya eva"; is "as soon as they come to be" what is meant?}, they are praised. When? To this he says: ⟦यत्⟧, when, in the ⟦उपस्थे⟧, in the lap, of the waters (⟦अपां⟧), within, being ⟦विभृतो⟧, being borne by them in a special way, he ⟦अवसत्⟧ (⟦आ⟧); that is, this Agni, having occupied it, dwells there: the meaning is "at that time". ⟦अध⟧, now, the ⟦स्वधा⟧, the waters that are like nectar, ⟦अधयत्⟧: the whole world drinks them. They are further described. Those together with which (⟦याभिः⟧) this Agni ⟦ईयते⟧, or comes together with the earth — those it drank. That in the form of lightning he makes the world drink the waters is the praise of Agni.

## Ṛk 3

⟦सवयसा⟧ is "of the same age". By the word vayas capability is indicated: of the same capability. ⟦तदित्तदानीमेव⟧: tad it is "at that very time"; when he becomes grown, at that very time. ⟦समानमर्थं⟧, a common purpose, an accomplishment, ⟦मिथः⟧, each one severally, ⟦वितरित्रता⟧, crossing intensely, the hotṛ and the Adhvaryu ⟦युयूषतः⟧ the ⟦वपुः⟧, the body of Agni; that is, they wish to mingle it, each by his own operation. After the praiṣa, the recitation of the yājyā and the like is the operation of the hotṛ; after the vaṣaṭ-call, the offering is that of the Adhvaryu: this is the division. Here this is to be kept in view. ⟦सवयसा⟧, of the same age, ⟦मिथः⟧, each one severally, ⟦वितरित्रता⟧, wishing in a special way to cross over to a common purpose, the attainment of happiness — from the root tṛ ending in the elision of yaṅ, before the suffix śatṛ, the form is laid down as irregular in the group beginning with dādharti **NOTE**{Ṛk 3: the reference text adds here the reference "pā. sū. 7. 4. 65", which the supplied text does not have.}; ā stands for the dual ending — a wife and a husband ⟦युयूषतः⟧ ⟦तद्वपुस्तदेव⟧, that very body, of each other; they wish to mingle it; they embrace each other. In that way the hotṛ and the Adhvaryu together wish to join Agni. ⟦आदीमनंतरमेव⟧: immediately afterwards, the ⟦हव्यः⟧, the āhavanīya Agni, being a ⟦सारथिः⟧, one given to moving, or, like a charioteer, the conductor of the chariot that consists of the sacrifice, the ⟦रश्मीन्⟧, the streams of ghee, long like reins, that belong to ⟦अस्मत्⟧, to us, ⟦आ⟧, on all sides, ⟦समयंस्त⟧; that is, he accepts them well. For this two examples are stated. ⟦भगो न⟧: the Sun, who is to be served by all; as he receives worship from all sides, in that way. A ⟦सारथी⟧, the driver of a chariot, ⟦रश्मीन्न⟧, like the reins, of the ⟦वोळ्हुः⟧, the carrier, the horse and the like: as he takes them, so this Agni also ⟦समयंस्त⟧ the oblations.

## Ṛk 4

⟦यमीमेनमग्निं⟧: this Agni whom ⟦द्वा⟧, two, who are ⟦सवयसा⟧, of the same age, or of the same capability, who are in a ⟦समाने योना⟧, that is yonau, in the place of the origin of the fruit, the sacrifice; who are ⟦मिथुना⟧, engaged in one task like a married couple; who are ⟦समोकसा⟧, having a common dwelling, having the place of sacrifice as their place — such a hotṛ and Adhvaryu ⟦सपर्यतः⟧; that is, they worship him; they gladden him with praise and with the giving of the oblation. When? He says: ⟦दिवा⟧ ⟦न⟧, as by day, so also ⟦नक्तं⟧, by night; the meaning is: always. Agni, being thus worshipped, ⟦पलितः⟧, though worn out before, in the state of embers, after the kindling ⟦अजनि⟧, is born, ⟦युवा⟧, young, or, the one who mingles all with his lustre. He, being thus born, with regard to the ⟦मानुषा युगा⟧, the pairs connected with Manu, in the form of wife and husband or in the form of hotṛ and Adhvaryu, ⟦पुरु चरन्⟧, eating much ghee and the like, remains ⟦अजरो⟧, not worn out, free from old age.

## Ṛk 5

⟦देवं⟧, the shining one, ⟦तं⟧, this Agni, the ⟦दश धीतयो⟧, the fingers, ten in number, which are ⟦व्रिशो⟧, set apart from one another, ⟦हिन्वंति⟧; that is, they gladden him at the time of the churning. Likewise we ⟦मर्तासो⟧, we men, the sacrificers, ⟦हवामहे⟧, call him, for ⟦ऊतये⟧, for protection. What is his distinction? This is said: this Agni — like arrows that go forth excellently from a ⟦धनोः⟧, from a bow; as people let them loose upon the enemy; the word ā is in the sense of a comparison — in that way ⟦सः⟧, that Agni, from his own body, which stands in the place of the bow, ⟦ऋण्वति⟧, makes go, the ⟦प्रवतः⟧, the rays, which have an eminent speed. ⟦रिविर्गत्यर्थः⟧: the root riv has the sense of going; here it has the causative sense contained within it; the augment num is because the root has an indicatory i; the saṃprasāraṇa of the r is Vedic. The word adhi has no meaning; it merely repeats the sense of the root. Moreover Agni, thus grown, with ⟦अभिव्रजत्भिः⟧, with those who move all around for the sake of the performance, or who approach the thing that is to be known, for that purpose ⟦अधित⟧ the ⟦नवा⟧, the new, ⟦वयुना⟧, knowledges, which have the performance as their object; that is, he holds them; the meaning is that he makes them known. Or else: he ⟦अधित⟧, holds, the new ⟦वयुना⟧, the praises and the like, which are distinguished by knowledge and are being made by ⟦अभिव्रजत्भिः⟧, by the hotṛ and the others who go facing him. From the root dhā, in the Vedic luṅ, the i is by ⟦स्थाघोरिच्चेतीत्वं⟧, "and i in place of the final of sthā and of the roots called ghu"; the sic that follows is treated as having an indicatory k; the elision of s is by ⟦ह्रस्वादंगादिति⟧, "after a stem ending in a short vowel".

## Ṛk 6

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦त्वं⟧ ⟦राजसि⟧, you rule, over ⟦दिव्यस्य⟧, over what is in heaven, the gods and the like, or, in the form of lightning, over rain and the like. This is a name of lordship. Likewise you ⟦राजसि⟧, are the lord, of ⟦पार्थिवस्य⟧, of what is connected with the earth, men and the like. Is it in dependence on another? No, he says: ⟦त्मना⟧, by yourself alone, by your own capability alone. For this there is an example: ⟦पशुपा इव⟧. As a herdsman by his own capability alone is able in the operations of penning the cattle, letting them out and the like, in that way. By this example complete independence is stated. Moreover, since it is so, therefore ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦बर्हिः⟧, sacrifice, ⟦एते⟧, these two well-known ones, which are ⟦एनी⟧, of white colour, bright, heaven and earth, ⟦अशाते⟧; that is, they reach it; they pervade it. The suffix ṅīp, and n in place of t, are by ⟦वर्णादनुदात्तादिति⟧, "after a colour-word that is not udātta" **NOTE**{Ṛk 6: the reference text adds here the reference "pā. sū. 4. 1. 39", which the supplied text does not have.}. Of what kind are they? They are ⟦बृहती⟧, great, very widely extended; ⟦अभिश्रिया⟧, having attained lordship, or to be served on all sides; ⟦हिरण्ययी⟧, beneficial and delightful; ⟦वक्वरी⟧, sounding very skilfully — from the root vac, with the suffix vanip, there are ṅīp and r by ⟦वनो र चेति⟧, "and r in place of the final of van"; the guttural is Vedic; or the word is from the root ⟦वकि कौटिल्य⟧, "vak in the sense of crookedness", and the absence of num is Vedic. Such a heaven and earth reach the ⟦बर्हिः⟧.

## Ṛk 7

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦जुषस्व⟧; that is, enjoy the oblation; or, be pleased with the praise. Moreover ⟦प्रति हर्य⟧, desire in return, ⟦तत्⟧, such, a pleasing, ⟦वचो⟧, praise in the form of speech. O ⟦मंद्र⟧, you who are given to gladdening, or who are worthy of praise; O ⟦स्वधावो⟧, you who possess food characterised as the oblation; O ⟦ऋतजात⟧, you who are born for the sake of the sacrifice; O ⟦सुक्रतो⟧, you of good actions, or of good understanding: you who (⟦यः⟧), being such, are ⟦प्रत्यज्⟧ ⟦असि⟧, that is, approved, favourable, ⟦विश्वतः⟧, to the whole world, the unmoving and the moving; the meaning is: not turned away. Likewise you are ⟦दर्शतो⟧, worthy to be seen by all. Moreover in ⟦संदृष्टौ⟧, in full sight, you are ⟦रण्वः⟧, given to delighting, or the one who makes all delight. Or else: when there is the sight of you, every man becomes ⟦क्षयो⟧, possessed of a dwelling. For this there is an example: ⟦पितुमानिव⟧. As the owner of very abundant food becomes one to be dwelt with and gone to by all, in that way.
