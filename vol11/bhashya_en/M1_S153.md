## Ṛk 1

**NOTE**{the supplied text has no introduction to this sūkta; the reference text has one} O ⟦घृतस्नू⟧, you who make ghee flow, Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦महः⟧, great ones, you two we ⟦यजामहे⟧, we worship, the sacrificers and the priests. Of what kind are we? ⟦सजोषाः⟧, joined in a like affection. By what means? That is said: by ⟦हव्येभिः⟧, by the puroḍāśa and the like, by ⟦नमःभिः⟧, by things marked by obeisance. ⟦अध⟧, and also, ⟦यत्⟧, for the reason that you two the ⟦अध्वर्यवः⟧, our adhvaryus too — here the word ⟦न⟧ is in the sense of "too" — they too by ⟦धीतिभिः⟧, by their own actions, ⟦भरंति⟧, nourish: therefore we worship.

## Ṛk 2

O Mitra and Varuṇa, of the sacrifice that belongs to you two (⟦वां⟧) there is the ⟦प्रस्तुतिः⟧, the introduction, only the resolve "I shall perform it"; ⟦न प्रयुक्तिः⟧, the whole performance has not been accomplished. By that much alone I ⟦अयामि⟧, I reach, the ⟦धाम⟧, the place of splendour, of you two. And I am ⟦सुवृक्तिः⟧, one who wins you two over well, because you have accepted me. But ⟦यत्⟧, when, in ⟦विदथेषु⟧, in the sacrifices, to you two the ⟦होता⟧, this adhvaryu who accomplishes the offering in the fire, ⟦अनक्ति⟧, comes with the oblation; the meaning is that he makes the offering. Or else the ⟦होता⟧ is the caller of the gods, the priest of that name, who ⟦अनक्ति⟧, makes you manifest, by the śastra and the like. Then I, ⟦सूरिः⟧, one who knows your greatness, ⟦इयक्षन्⟧, wishing to perform the sacrifice, O ⟦वृषणौ⟧, showerers of desires, the ⟦सुम्नं⟧ of you two (⟦वां⟧), the happiness that belongs to you, marked by godhead: "I reach" is to be supplied.

## Ṛk 3

O ⟦मित्रावरुणा⟧, Mitra and Varuṇa, for ⟦ऋताय⟧, for the sacrifice that belongs to you, for ⟦हविर्दे⟧, the giver of the oblation — the suffix vic comes by the rule ⟦आतो मनिन्नि⟧; when the stem is termed bha, the ā is dropped by the rule ⟦आतो धातोरि⟧ — and for ⟦जनाय⟧, the sacrificer, may the ⟦धेनुः⟧, the gladdening cow, being ⟦अदितिः⟧, not poor, rich in milk, ⟦पीपाय⟧, swell again and again with milk. From the root pyāy there is the Vedic liṭ, the perfect, in the sense of a prayer; the substitution of pī comes by the rule ⟦लिड्यजोश्चेति⟧. The meaning is: may she make swell the sacrifice whose oblations are the payasyā and the like, and the sacrificer in the vow of milk and the like. If it is asked "At what time?", to that he says: ⟦यत्⟧, when ⟦सः⟧, that well-known ⟦रातहव्यः⟧, the king of that name, ⟦सपर्यन्⟧, honouring with praise, ⟦मानुषो होता न⟧, like the priest of that name who belongs to the human sacrificer, in ⟦विदथे⟧, in the sacrifice, ⟦वां हिनोति⟧, gladdens you with the oblation and the like; then she swells: so he says.

## Ṛk 4

⟦उत⟧, and also, O Mitra and Varuṇa, for you two (⟦वां⟧), ⟦अंधः⟧, the food that is to be eaten, in the form of milk or in the form of the puroḍāśa and the like, may the ⟦देवीः⟧, whose nature it is to shine, ⟦गावः⟧, cows, ⟦आपश्च⟧, and the waters that make rice and the like grow, among ⟦मद्यासु⟧, those who are to be gladdened by you, ⟦विक्षु⟧, people, namely the sacrificers, for the sake of their increase, ⟦पीपयंत⟧, make swell. ⟦उतो⟧, and also, of this (⟦अस्य⟧) sacrifice that belongs to us (⟦नः⟧) the ⟦पूर्व्यः⟧, of former time, ⟦पतिः⟧, protector, Agni, ⟦दन्⟧, the giver: "may he be" is to be supplied. For all the oblations whatever are offered in Agni. Since this is so, therefore, O Mitra and Varuṇa, ⟦वीतं⟧, eat the puroḍāśa and the like. Of the ⟦उस्रियायाः⟧, of the cow that lets flow milk and the like, ⟦पयसः⟧, the milk, ⟦पातं⟧, drink. Or else: eat the solid part of the payasyā, drink what is in flowing form.
