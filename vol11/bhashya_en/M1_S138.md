## Introduction

The fifth sūkta, of four Ṛks, begins ⟦प्रप्र पूष्ण⟧; it is Parucchepa's, in the Atyaṣṭi metre, and has Pūṣan for its deity. It is so recorded in the Anukramaṇī: ⟦प्रप्र चतुष्कं पौष्णम⟧, "prapra, a group of four, addressed to Pūṣan". Its ritual use is to be gathered from the indications in its own wording.

## Ṛk 1

Of this Pūṣan, who is ⟦तुविजातस्य⟧, born for the sake of many yajamānas, of ⟦पूष्णः⟧, the nourisher, the god who bears this name, the ⟦महित्वं⟧ of ⟦तवसः⟧, that is, the greatness of his strength, ⟦प्रप्र शस्यते⟧: it is praised by all. The repetition is for filling out the pāda. ⟦न तंदते⟧: no one harms that either. Moreover the ⟦स्तोत्रं⟧, the praise, of this Pūṣan ⟦न तंदते⟧: does not harm, does not cease. The meaning is that every person praises him; the purport is: because he nourishes the world. For this reason ⟦अहं⟧, I too, the yajamāna, ⟦सुम्नयन्⟧, desiring happiness, ⟦अर्चामि⟧, praise him. Of what kind is he? ⟦अंतिऊतिं⟧, one whose protection is near. The k is elided by the rule that the elision of k and the like takes place variously. The meaning is: one who grants a boon immediately after the praise. ⟦मयोभुवं⟧: the producer of happiness, or the one who brings it into being. He, Pūṣan, who is ⟦मखः⟧, possessed of sacrifice (the suffix with the sense of possession is elided), ⟦आयुयुवे⟧, unites on every side, the ⟦मनः⟧, the mind, ⟦विश्वस्य⟧, of every praiser, the officiating priest. The purport is: because he grants boons quickly. Moreover, ⟦देवः⟧, this shining one, ⟦आयुयुवे⟧ the ⟦मखः⟧, that is, the makha, the sacrifice: he unites it right up to its completion. Or else: if it is asked what comes of it for the yajamāna when the mind of all is united, the answer is given: ⟦मखः⟧, the one who carries the sacrifice through, this god Pūṣan ⟦आयुयुवे⟧, unites on every side, the mind of the yajamāna. That it is the mind of the yajamāna is obtained from the force of the sense. The form is a Vedic liṭ of the root yu.

## Ṛk 2

O ⟦पूषन्⟧, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, who are ⟦अजिरं⟧, going towards the place of the sacrifice, ⟦यामनि⟧, the going being the occasion, ⟦स्तोमेभिः⟧, with praises, ⟦प्र कृण्वै⟧: I make eminent. An illustration of this: ⟦यामनि⟧, in swift going, ⟦अजिरं⟧ ⟦न⟧, like a swift-going horse; as that is praised, so. Moreover, I make you eminent in such a way ⟦यथा⟧ you ⟦ऋणवः⟧, may go, with ⟦मृधः⟧, battles, in view. Or else: I make you such that you ⟦ऋणवः⟧, may go towards our sacrifice. The root is ⟦ऋण गतौ⟧, "ṛṇ, in the sense of going", of the tan class; in leṭ there is the augment aṭ. Moreover, ⟦मृधः⟧, the harmful fighters, in battle ⟦पीपरः⟧: you carry across. It is the form of the causative of pṛ in luṅ with caṅ. An illustration of this: ⟦उष्टो न⟧. As a camel, a bearer, carries a load across, so you ⟦पीपरः⟧, carry across, from ⟦मृधः⟧, from battle. For this reason ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦मयोभुवं⟧, who bring happiness into being, ⟦देवं⟧, whose nature is to shine, Pūṣan, I, ⟦मर्त्यः⟧, a man whose nature is to die, ⟦हुवे⟧, call. For what purpose? ⟦सख्याय⟧: for being spoken of alike, for the doing of what is dear. Being thus called, our ⟦आंगूषान्⟧, the resounding praises (⟦अंगूषः स्तोम आघोष⟧, "aṅgūṣa is a praise, a resounding", says Yāska), ⟦म्युम्मिनस्कृधि⟧: make them possessed of brilliance. Or else: make the praises possessed of fame, or possessed of food. The meaning is: pleased by our praise, do so. Moreover, ⟦वाजेषु⟧, in battles, ⟦कृधि⟧, make, possessed of food. The meaning is: having conquered our enemies, place their food in me.

## Ṛk 3

O ⟦पूषन्⟧, god, ⟦यस्य ते⟧, of you who are well known as a protector, ⟦सख्ये⟧, when there is your friendship, your doing of good, ⟦क्रत्वा चित्⟧, by the rite alone, by excellent action (the substitution nā is absent by the supplementary rule that in jas and the rest it is optional in the Veda), ⟦संतः⟧, being those who please you, the ⟦विपन्यवः⟧, those whose habit it is to praise in a special degree; or else, this is a name for the wise: the wise; the yajamānas, being protected ⟦अवसा⟧, by the protection given by you, ⟦बुभुज्रिरे⟧: they enjoy enjoyments. ⟦इति⟧, in just this way, at all times ⟦क्रत्वा⟧, by action, ⟦बुभुज्रिरे⟧: they enjoy; or, all are protected. From the root bhuj, in the Veda, in liṭ, there is the augment ruṭ by the rule that it takes place variously in the Veda. ⟦तां⟧, such, ⟦नवीयसीं⟧, newer, or praiseworthy, protection ⟦अनु⟧, following, that is, after the protection, we ⟦ईमहे⟧, ask, you for ⟦नियुतं राय⟧, riches of that number. Moreover, O ⟦उरुशंस⟧, you who are to be praised in many ways, not being angry, being well disposed towards us, ⟦सरी भव⟧: be one who is to be approached by us. From the root sṛ there is the Uṇādi suffix ini; the first syllable is udātta. Moreover, ⟦वाजे वाजे⟧, in all battles too, ⟦सरी भव⟧: be one whose habit it is to go; the meaning is, be the one who goes in front.

## Ṛk 4

O Ajāśva, Pūṣan. Yāska says that the word Ajāśva denotes Pūṣan (⟦नि. ४-२५⟧). ⟦नः⟧, of us, ⟦अस्याः⟧, for this (the genitive stands in the sense of the dative) ⟦सातये⟧, gain, being ⟦अहेळमानः⟧, not angry, not showing disregard, and ⟦ररिवान्⟧, a giver, ⟦सु⟧, well, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦भुवः⟧: be one who stands near. The suffix kvasu replaces the Vedic liṭ of the root rā. Although the designation pada is wanting, a Vedic separation is seen in ⟦पपिवांसं⟧, tasthivāṃsam and the like. ⟦ऊ⟧ is a filler of the pāda. Moreover, ⟦श्रवस्यतां⟧, of us who desire food, O ⟦अजाश्व⟧, you who are such that goats themselves stand in the place of horses for him (this is a name of Pūṣan), and being a giver, be near. ⟦अजाः पूष्ण⟧, "the goats are Pūṣan's", says Yāska. ⟦ओ⟧ is a single particle consisting of the combination of two particles. O ⟦दस्म⟧, destroyer of enemies, Pūṣan, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦ववृतीमहि⟧: may we turn from every side indeed, thoroughly. From the root vṛt, with the sense of the causative included in it, in liṅ, ślu replaces śap by the rule that it takes place variously in the Veda. The meaning is: we turn you back from going to another sacrifice. By what means? That is stated: ⟦साधुभिः⟧ ⟦स्तोमेभिः⟧, with praises that are able to please you. Moreover, O ⟦पूषन्⟧, ⟦आघृणे⟧, you who make the rain flow on every side; or else, ⟦आघृणे⟧, you who bring, who accept, the oblations: you ⟦नहि⟧ ⟦अतिमन्ये⟧, I do not at all treat with disrespect. Not merely is there no overstepping; but ⟦ते सख्यं⟧, your friendship, your being a doer of good, I do not at all ⟦अपह्नुवे⟧, put away; I do not give it up. The meaning is: I make it known everywhere.
