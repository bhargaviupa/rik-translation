## Ṛk 1

**NOTE**{the supplied text has no introduction to this sūkta; the reference text has one} O Viṣṇu, ⟦मित्रो न⟧: one who protects from miti, that is, from pain, a friend, or else the sun, is mitra. ⟦प्रमीतेस्त्रायत⟧, "he protects from perishing": so the Nirukta (⟦१०-२१⟧). Like him, ⟦शेव्यः⟧, good in the matter of happiness, a maker of happiness; ⟦घृतासुतिः⟧, he by whom ghṛta, that is, water, is brought forth, one who is such. Or else: he to whom ghṛta, that is, clarified butter, is brought face to face, one who is such. ⟦विभूतद्युम्नः⟧, of abundant fame, or of abundant food. ⟦एवया⟧, the one who joins protection to us, who brings it. ⟦सप्रथाः⟧, broad on every side. With each epithet the connection is "be so to us". The long vowel is by the rule ⟦द्व्यचोऽतस्तिज⟧, "in place of the final a of a finite verb of two vowels". ⟦उ⟧ merely fills out the quarter-verse. O Viṣṇu, since you are such, ⟦अध⟧, for that reason, ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦स्तोमः⟧, the particular hymn of praise, ⟦विदुषा⟧, by the sacrificer who knows your greatness, is ardhyaḥ: it deserves to be increased again and again. The meaning is that it is not fulfilled by being done a single time. Likewise your ⟦यज्ञश्च⟧, the sacrifice also, ⟦हविष्मता⟧, by that sacrificer, ⟦राध्यः⟧, is to be duly accomplished. Or else: ⟦विदुषा⟧, by the hotṛ, the hymn of praise is to be accomplished, and by him who has the oblation the sacrifice is to be accomplished.

## Ṛk 2

⟦यः⟧, the mortal who, ⟦पूर्वाय⟧, to him who belongs to former time, the meaning is, to the eternal one, ⟦वेधसे⟧, to the maker of the manifold world, ⟦नवीयसे⟧, to the ever new, the meaning is, to the exceedingly delightful one, or to him who is to be praised, ⟦सुमज्ञानये⟧, to him who was born of himself alone. From the root jan there is the uṇādi suffix iṇ. Sumat has the meaning "by oneself" (⟦नि. ६-२२⟧): so Yāska. Or else: that which gladdens exceedingly is sumat; he who has a wife of that kind is so called; to him, the meaning is, to the husband of Śrī, whose nature is to gladden the whole world. In a bahuvrīhi, by ⟦जायायानिज्⟧, "niṅ in place of jāyā" (⟦पा. ५-४-१३४⟧), there is the substitute niṅ at the end of the compound. There is elision before a val sound. To ⟦विष्णवे⟧, the pervading one, who has the said qualities, ⟦ददाशति⟧: he gives the oblation and the like. Moreover, ⟦अस्य⟧, of this Viṣṇu, ⟦महतः⟧, of him of great majesty, ⟦महि⟧, the great, the venerable, ⟦जातं⟧, the birth, the origin, the birth in the form of Hiraṇyagarbha and the rest, ⟦ब्रवत्⟧: he should tell. The augment aṭ is added to the root brū in leṭ. He should proclaim it. ⟦सेदु⟧: the word u has the sense of the word api. He too, the giver and the praiser, being joined with ⟦श्रवःभिः⟧, with foods or with fames, ⟦युज्यं चित्⟧, that station which is indeed to be gone to by all, ⟦अभि⟧, facing it, asat: he goes to it, he reaches it.

## Ṛk 3

O praisers, ⟦तमु⟧, that Viṣṇu alone, ⟦पूर्व्यं⟧, who is worthy of the first place, who is established without beginning, ⟦गर्भं⟧ of ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, who is the embryo of the sacrifice; the meaning is, who has arisen as the sacrifice itself. For the Śruti says: ⟦यज्ञो वै विष्णुः⟧, "the sacrifice indeed is Viṣṇu" (⟦शत. १-१-२-१३⟧). Or else: of ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, of water, ⟦गर्भं⟧, the cause of the embryo; the meaning is, the producer of water. For the Smṛti says: ⟦अप एव ससर्जादौ⟧, "he created the waters alone in the beginning" (⟦मनु १-८⟧). Viṣṇu who is such, ⟦यथा⟧ ⟦विद⟧, as you know him, so, ⟦जनुषा⟧, by birth, of your own selves alone, not on account of any gaining of a boon or the like, ⟦पिपर्तन⟧: gladden him with praise and the like. The meaning is, to the extent that you know his greatness. Vida is the second person plural of the root vid in laṭ. In ⟦विद ऋतस्य⟧ here, in the Saṃhitā, the original form is kept by the rule "ṛty akaḥ". Moreover, ⟦अस्य⟧, of this Viṣṇu of great majesty, ⟦नाम चित्⟧, the appellation to which all are to bow, which sets forth that he is the self of all, this name "Viṣṇu", ⟦जानंतः⟧, understanding that it grants the aims of man, ⟦आ⟧, on all sides, ⟦विवक्तन⟧: speak it, proclaim it. Or else: ⟦नाम⟧, the bending in the form of the sacrifice, of Viṣṇu alone, for bringing about heaven and final release for all, his transformation as the iṣṭi and the rest, or as the substance and the deity, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦जानंतः⟧, you who know it, ⟦विवक्तन⟧: tell it, praise him. From the root vac in loṭ, the replacing of śap by ślu is Vedic. By "bahulaṃ chandasi" the reduplication takes the vowel i. As before, there is the substitute tana. Now, having beheld him face to face, he says: O Viṣṇu, god who are the self of all, ⟦महः⟧, of the great one, ⟦ते⟧, of you, ⟦सुमतिं⟧, the good praise, or the mind whose nature is splendour, ⟦भजामहे⟧: we serve it, we the sacrificers.

## Ṛk 4

In the carrying forward of Agni and Soma this verse, ⟦तमस्य राजे⟧, is to be employed. It is laid down in the sūtra, in the section beginning "when they are about to carry forward Agni and Soma": ⟦तमस्य राजा वरुणस्तमश्विनेत्यर्धर्च आरमेत्⟧, "at 'tam asya rājā varuṇas tam aśvinā', at the half-verse, he should stop" (⟦आ. ४-१०⟧). **NOTE**{the supplied text has this statement of the ritual use with its sūtra reference; the reference text does not have it}

⟦मारुतस्य⟧: the assemblage of the Maruts, that is, of the officiating priests, is māruta; of him who has that. The sense of the possessive suffix is dropped. Or else, of him who is connected with the Maruts, the gods. ⟦वेधसः⟧, of the wise one, ⟦अस्य⟧, of this sacrificer, ⟦तं⟧, that well-known ⟦क्रतुं⟧, the sacrifice, Viṣṇu who is the sacrifice itself, for the Śruti says "the sacrifice indeed is Viṣṇu"; ⟦राजा⟧, the shining ⟦वरुणः⟧, ⟦सचंत⟧: he attends on it. That same ⟦क्रतुं⟧, the sacrifice, ⟦अश्विना⟧, the two Aśvins attend on. The other gods also attend on it, serve it. Moreover ⟦विष्णुः⟧, Viṣṇu who pervades as the three pressings, ⟦सखिवान्⟧, being joined with friends such as the sacrificer, has held the uttama, that is, the most excellent, ⟦अहःविदं⟧, the knower of the day, the meaning is, the producer of heaven, ⟦दक्षं⟧, the strength that consists in the capacity to grant the fruit: ⟦दाधार⟧, he has held it. Moreover ⟦व्रजं च⟧, the cloud also. Vraja is a name of the cloud, because it is read among its names in "vrajaś caruḥ". **NOTE**{the reference text adds a Nighaṇṭu reference (1.10.11) after this quotation; the supplied text has none} That he ⟦अपऊर्णुते⟧ for the sake of the rain-water: he makes it to be without its covering. For it is the sacrifice alone that, by way of the oblation, produces the rain. Or else: ⟦मारुतस्य⟧, of the troop of the Maruts, of the host of gods, ⟦वेधसः⟧, of the ordainer, of the creator, Viṣṇu, ⟦क्रतुं⟧, the work that consists in protecting and the like, Varuṇa and the others attend on, because protecting depends on him. And he, ⟦सखिवान्⟧, being accompanied by helpers such as Indra and the Maruts, ⟦दाधार⟧ the ⟦दक्षं⟧ of the said character, the strength that consists in the capacity to produce rain and the like, and likewise he uncovers the cloud.

## Ṛk 5

⟦यः⟧, Viṣṇu, who, ⟦दैव्यः⟧, existing in heaven, ⟦सुकृत्तरः⟧, the best among those who grant good fruit, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦विवाय⟧: comes. It is the form of the root vī in liṭ. For what purpose? ⟦सचथाय⟧, for accompanying, for giving help in the sacrifice. For whom? ⟦इंद्राय⟧: he who makes irā, the food that consists of the oblation, flow is indra, the sacrificer; for the derivation stated by Yāska, ⟦इंद्र इरां वृणातीति इदंकरणादित्याग्रायण⟧, "Indra: because he tears the irā; 'because he does this', says Āgrāyaṇa", holds good here also (⟦नि. १०-८⟧). For that sacrificer of the said form, ⟦सुकृते⟧, the maker of good praise. And having come, ⟦वेधाः⟧, the ordainer of the desired fruit, ⟦त्रिसधस्थः⟧, he who has common abodes that are three in number, Viṣṇu whose abodes are the three pressings, or who has the three abodes beginning with the earth, ⟦आर्यं⟧, the sacrificer who is to be approached, ⟦अजिन्वत्⟧: he gladdens. The root jivi has the sense of gladdening; num is inserted because it has i as its indicatory letter. For that purpose, ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, of the sacrifice, ⟦भागे⟧, in the share that consists of what remains of the offering, that ⟦यजमानं⟧ ābhajat: he gives him a share; the meaning is, he brings him near. Or else: ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, of the sacrifice, ⟦भागे⟧, in the fruit, he ābhajat the sacrificer: he sets him there as its owner.
