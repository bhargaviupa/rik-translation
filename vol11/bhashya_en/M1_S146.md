## Introduction

The hymn of five Ṛks that begins with the word trimūrdhānam is the sixth hymn; it is of Dīrghatamas. It is addressed to Agni, because it was said earlier "but that is addressed to Agni". The Anukramaṇikā is the word trimūrdhānam. In the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra, in the triṣṭubh metre, there is the ritual use of the three hymns that begin with this one. It is laid down in the sūtra, in the section ⟦अथैतस्या⟧: ⟦त्रिमूर्धानमिति त्रीणि⟧, "the three hymns beginning trimūrdhānam" (⟦अ ४-१३⟧).

## Ṛk 1

By this Ṛk Agni is praised in the form of the sacrifice. ⟦त्रिमूर्धानं⟧: endowed with three heads in the form of the three pressings. By the rule "dvitribhyāṃ pāddanmūrdhasu bahuvrīhau" the final syllable of the latter member is udātta. Moreover ⟦सप्तरश्मिं⟧: joined with the seven regulating metres; ⟦अनूनं⟧: not defective, of complete fruit; ⟦अग्निं⟧: that which is to be accomplished by Agni, or that which is endowed with the qualities of moving and the like; ⟦पित्रोरुपस्थे⟧, in the lap of heaven and earth, ⟦निषत्तं⟧, seated; the meaning is that it is in the middle. That is to say, because it is to be accomplished by the substance that is on the earth and by the deities that are in the world of heaven. Moreover, the sacrifice that belongs to ⟦अस्य⟧, this Agni, ⟦ध्रुवस्य⟧, who is motionless, ⟦चरतः⟧, who eats the oblation, the sacrifice which is ⟦आपप्रिवांसं⟧, the filler on all sides, of ⟦विश्वा⟧, all, ⟦रोचना⟧, the celestial cars of the gods, which have come from ⟦दिवः⟧, from the world of heaven: that ⟦गृणीषे⟧, praise, laud.

Or else it is this Agni himself that is spoken of. As one having three heads, he is here endowed with the three places called earth, mid-region and the world of heaven; the meaning is that he pervades the three worlds. Or he abides in the three places, the Gārhapatya and the others. ⟦सप्तरश्मिं⟧: having seven flames; ⟦अनूनं⟧: not less; ⟦निषत्तं⟧ in the ⟦उपस्थे⟧, the lap, of ⟦पित्रोः⟧, heaven and earth. Because the form is given ready-made in the rule "nasattaniṣatta", the n of the niṣṭhā suffix is absent. It means seated. ⟦आपप्रिवांसं⟧: filling on all sides, of the desires. Agni, who is distinguished by the characteristics stated, ⟦गृणीषे⟧, praise, laud. Moreover, of Agni, ⟦चरतः⟧, who goes everywhere with his flames, ⟦ध्रुवस्य⟧, who is unshaken because he is held, ⟦दिवः⟧, who shines, ⟦विश्वा⟧, all, the ⟦रोचना⟧, the shining lustres, "pervade everywhere": this is to be supplied. **NOTE**{Before the word for Agni in this sentence the reference text also has asya, "of this"; the supplied text does not.} "Praise such an Agni": this is the direction of the inner self.

## Ṛk 2

⟦उक्षा⟧: the sprinkler; the meaning is that he is able to bestow the fruit. For that very reason ⟦महान्⟧: this Agni, who by his greatness, by his own form, is like an unrestrained bull, ⟦ववक्षे⟧, carries, having stepped over, having pervaded, ⟦एने⟧, heaven and earth; the meaning is that he pervades. **NOTE**{Before abhikramya the reference text also has the word abhi of the Ṛk as a separate lemma; the supplied text does not.} Of the root vah, in leṭ, there is the Vedic ślu-elision of śap. By the rule "lopas ta ātmanepadeṣu" there is the elision of t. By the rule "sib bahulam" there is sip. The meaning is: by bestowing rain and the like and by carrying the oblation he protects the gods and the men who dwell in the two worlds. Moreover, this one is ⟦अजरः⟧, free from old age, ⟦ऋष्टो⟧, great, worthy of worship. This is a name of "great", because of the reading ⟦ऋष्ट उक्ष⟧ among the names of that. **NOTE**{After this quotation the reference text gives a reference (Nighaṇṭu 3.3.3) which the supplied text does not have.} Likewise ⟦इतःऊति⟧: being one who has obtained protection, ⟦तस्थौ⟧, he stands, he exists. Or else: this one, who has the qualities stated, is ⟦इतःऊति⟧, for that very reason possessed of going, possessed of going towards our place of sacrifice to the gods, and ⟦तस्थौ⟧, he exists. **NOTE**{The supplied text reads ata eva gamanavān, "for that very reason possessed of going"; the reference text reads ita evaṃ gamanavān.} After that, ⟦सानौ⟧, on the elevated spot, which is the altar, of ⟦उवर्व्याः⟧, the wide earth, ⟦पदो नि दधाति⟧, he sets down, he makes, his steps. Moreover the ⟦अरुषासः⟧, the shining ones, of ⟦अस्य⟧, this Agni, ⟦रिहंति⟧, lick, the ⟦ऊधः⟧, the mid-region, which stands in the place of an udder. Or else: the sacrificers, who are ⟦अरुषासः⟧, shining with the lustre of sacred knowledge, ⟦रिहंति⟧ his ⟦ऊधः⟧, the sacrifice, which stands in the place of an udder; they lick the desired heaven and the like.

## Ṛk 3

⟦समानं⟧, one and the same, ⟦वत्सं⟧, Agni, who stands in the place of a calf, who is a cause of joy like a son: ⟦अभि⟧, towards him, ⟦संचरंती⟧, moving together, the two ⟦धेनू⟧, who gratify by doing what is good for Agni, the two cows who are the wife and the sacrificer, ⟦विष्वग्वि चरतः⟧, move about. The meaning is that they make him grow well by putting on fuel, by sweeping and the like, which resemble the giving of the breast to drink and the like. Of what kind are those two? ⟦सुमेके⟧: of good deeds, or of good pouring; the meaning is that they are skilled in attendance. Moreover ⟦अनपवृज्यान्⟧: free from anything that has to be removed. By the rule "nañsubhyām" the final syllable of the latter member is udātta. ⟦अध्वनो⟧: the paths, the regions bordering on Agni, which are free from hair and other impure things, ⟦मिमाने⟧, the two who bring them about. Moreover ⟦विश्वान्केतान्⟧: all the knowledges, which concern his being made to grow, ⟦महः⟧, great, ⟦अधि⟧, exceedingly, ⟦वधाने⟧, the two who hold them. Two such cows, in the form of the adhvaryu and the sacrificer, or in the form of wife and husband, move about in all directions.

## Ṛk 4

⟦धीरासो⟧: the wise, the intelligent, who know the performance, the adhvaryu and the others, ⟦नयंति⟧, lead, cause to reach, this Agni, who is ⟦अजुर्यं⟧, unaging, undecayed, to the ⟦पदं⟧, the place, which is the altar: from the place of the churning, or from the Gārhapatya. Or else the sacrificers and the others, whose characteristics are about to be stated, make him go to being a ⟦पदं⟧, an abode, the abode of all desires. Of what kind are they? ⟦कवयः⟧: those whose sight goes beyond, the intelligent; or the learned, because of the revealed text ⟦ये वा अनूचानास्त्ये कवय⟧, "those who are learned, they indeed are the kavis". **NOTE**{After this quotation the reference text gives a reference (Aitareya-brāhmaṇa 2.2) which the supplied text does not have.} Moreover ⟦नाना हृदा⟧, with an understanding of many kinds, ⟦रक्षमाणा⟧, holding him. Moreover they, being of this kind, are ⟦सिषासंतः⟧, wishing to share in, the ⟦सिंधुं⟧, the flowing one, Agni, who makes the fruits flow by way of the sacrifice. Of the root san, before the desiderative suffix san, the augment iṭ is absent because of the option given in the rule "sanīvantardha"; by the rule "janasanakhanām" there is the substitution of ā. ⟦पर्यपश्यंत⟧: they look all around; the meaning is that they wish to serve him. Moreover ⟦सूर्यः⟧, the impeller of all, this Agni, ⟦आविः⟧ ⟦अभवत्⟧ for ⟦एभ्यः⟧, for these alone who act thus, ⟦न्यान्⟧, for the men, the leaders; out of favour to them he became visible. **NOTE**{The supplied text reads ebhya eva, "for these alone"; the reference text reads ebhya evaṃ, "for these who act thus".} In the word ⟦न्यान्⟧ here there is an exchange of number. Or else it is to be construed thus: "in order to favour ⟦न्यान्⟧, living beings".

## Ṛk 5

This Agni, in ⟦काष्ठासु⟧, in the ten directions, which exist overstepping one another, is ⟦दिदृक्षेण्यः⟧: through being joined with favour he becomes joined with sight. Or else: he is one whom it is to be wished to see, who has become the object of the wish to see. From the root dṛś ending in the desiderative san, in the sense of the kṛtya suffixes, the suffix kenya comes by the rule "tavaikenkenya". For that very reason he is ⟦जेन्यः⟧: he becomes one whose habit is to appear everywhere, or one whose habit is to conquer. Moreover, at the time of giving, he is ⟦ईळेन्यः⟧, worthy of praise. For what purpose is this so? ⟦जीवसे⟧, for the living, of ⟦महो⟧, the great, a god and the like, or of ⟦अभार्य⟧, the small, the little, the sacrificer and the like. By carrying the oblation, and by bestowing wealth: such is the distinction. He states the ground for this: ⟦यत्⟧ ⟦अह⟧, since indeed, ⟦पुरुत्रा⟧, in many places (the suffix trā is by the rule beginning "devamanuṣya"), this Agni, ⟦मघवा⟧, who possesses the food that consists of the oblation, ⟦विश्वदर्शतः⟧, who possesses what is to be seen in all spheres, ⟦सूः⟧ ⟦अभवत्⟧, became, that is, is, the begetter, the producer, ⟦गर्भेभ्यः⟧ (the dative is in the sense of the genitive), of these priests, who are to be protected exceedingly like an embryo, like a child. Therefore ⟦नाना हृदा रक्षमाणाः⟧ and the rest, "the kavis, holding him with a manifold understanding, lead him to the place": thus the connection is with the preceding Ṛk.
