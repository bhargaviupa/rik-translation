## Introduction

The sūkta beginning ⟦प्र तव्यसीमित्यष्टर्चं⟧, of eight Ṛks, is the fourth sūkta; it is of Dīrghatamas. And so it is stated in the Anukramaṇī: ⟦तव्यसीमष्टावाग्नेयं तु तत्तिष्टुबंतमिति⟧, "The sūkta beginning pra tavyasīm: eight Ṛks; but that belongs to Agni; it ends in a triṣṭubh." Because "but that" is said, Agni is the deity of the eight sūktas that begin with this one. The last Ṛk is a triṣṭubh; the rest are jagatīs, by the convention of "ending in triṣṭubh". The employment in the Prātaranuvāka and the Āśvina-śastra has been stated. In the Agniṣṭoma, in the Āgnimāruta-śastra, this sūkta is the one in which the nivid of Jātavedas is to be placed. It is laid down in the sūtra, in the section ⟦अथ यथेतमिति⟧, "atha yathetam": ⟦प्र तव्यसीं नव्यसीमापो हि प्रीति⟧ ⟦तिस्रः⟧, "pra tavyasīṃ navyasīm; the three Ṛks beginning āpo hi ṣṭha" (⟦आ. ५-२०⟧). On the first day of the Daśarātra also there is the placing of the nivid of Jātavedas; and on the middle day of the Aśvamedha there is the placing of the nivid. And so there is the teacher's extension of the rule: ⟦ऐकाहिकान्युपसंशस्य तेषु⟧ ⟦निविदो दध्यादेवमेवाग्नि मारुते⟧, "Having recited in addition those of the one-day rite, he should place the nivids in them; just so in the Āgnimāruta" (⟦आ १-१०⟧) **NOTE**{Introduction: the supplied text gives this reference as 1-10, the reference text as "āśva. śrau. 10. 10"; which is to stand?}.

## Ṛk 1

I, for ⟦अग्नये⟧, ⟦प्र भरे⟧, that is, I perform excellently, a ⟦धीतिं⟧, an action characterised as sacrifice and having the qualities stated, which is ⟦तव्यसीं⟧, that is ⟦तवीयसीमतिशयेन⟧, causing increase in the highest degree — tav is a root of the sūtras with the sense of growth; from this root ending in tṛc there is the suffix īyasun by ⟦तुश्चंदसीतीयसुन्⟧, "after tṛ, in the Veda"; the elision of tṛ is by ⟦तुरिष्ठेमेयःस्विति⟧, "of tṛ before iṣṭhan, iman and īyas"; the elision of the ī is Vedic — and ⟦नव्यसीं⟧, newer, not made before. Likewise I ⟦भरे⟧ a ⟦वाचो मतिं⟧ of the character stated, an action in the form of praise. For what kind of Agni? For the ⟦सूनवे⟧, the son, of ⟦सहसो⟧, of strength. Moreover the Agni who (⟦यः⟧) is ⟦अपां⟧ ⟦नपात्⟧, the grandson of them: from the waters come the herbs, from the herbs Agni; thus Agni is the grandson. Or else: he is not one who makes the waters fall away; the idea is: because in the form of the fire of lightning he rains them down. Likewise he is ⟦प्रियो⟧, the one who gladdens the sacrificer, or the dearest; his ⟦होता⟧, the one who accomplishes the offering; that Agni, being ⟦ऋत्वियः⟧, one whose time has come, for whom the time of giving has arrived, on the ⟦पृथिव्यां⟧, characterised as the altar, accompanied by ⟦वसुभिः⟧, by wealth such as cows, which is fit for dwelling, ⟦न्यसीदत्⟧; that is, he sits down firmly.

## Ṛk 2

⟦सः⟧, that Agni mentioned before, ⟦जायमानः⟧, being produced from the two araṇis, or having become manifest in the pieces of wood, at that very time, in the ⟦परमे⟧, the excellent, ⟦विओमनि⟧, the place of the altar, which has protection of various kinds, to ⟦मातरिश्वने⟧, to Vāyu who moves in the atmosphere, first ⟦आविः⟧ ⟦अभवत्⟧; that is, he became visible; because this is well known from other scriptural texts such as ⟦त्वमग्ने⟧ ⟦प्रथमो मातरिश्वन आविर्भव⟧, "You, O Agni, the first, become manifest to Mātariśvan" (⟦ऋ. १-३१-३⟧). The meaning is that he blazed up through contact with the wind. Or else: he who breathes, who moves, in the mother, in the maker of the fruit, the sacrifice, is mātariśvan, the sacrificer; for his sake. Moreover, of ⟦समिधानस्य⟧, of Agni who is growing well through the fuel, by ⟦मज्मना⟧ — this is a name of strength — by a strong ⟦क्रत्वा⟧, that is, by action, by the activity of blazing and the like, ⟦शोचिः⟧, he ⟦प्रारोचयत्⟧, illumined excellently, ⟦पृथिवी⟧ with ⟦द्यावा⟧, heaven and earth. The word śociḥ standing in the middle is a Vedic usage. Or the words are to be construed as ⟦मज्मना क्रत्वा समिधानस्येति⟧: the meaning is "of him who is being kindled by the powerful activity of kindling and the like".

## Ṛk 3

Of this (⟦अस्य⟧) Agni who is being praised, the ⟦त्वेषाः⟧, the radiances, are ⟦अजरा⟧, free from old age, not worn out; the meaning is: unceasing. The first syllable of the second member is udātta by the rule beginning ⟦जरमरेत्यादिनोत्तरपदाद्युदात्तत्वं⟧, "after the negative, jara, mara". Likewise of this Agni who is ⟦सुप्रतीकस्य⟧, of beautiful face, the ⟦भानवो⟧, the rays — since the radiance has already been mentioned, the sparks are to be understood here — and they are ⟦सुसंदृशः⟧, seeing well and fully; the meaning is: pervading on all sides; and ⟦सुद्युतः⟧, shining well on all sides. Likewise of this Agni they are ⟦भात्वक्षसः⟧. ⟦त्वक्ष⟧ is a name of strength, because it is read among the names of strength: ⟦त्वक्षः शर्ध⟧, "tvakṣas, śardhas" **NOTE**{Ṛk 3: the reference text adds here the reference "ni. 2. 9, 6", which the supplied text does not have.}. They are of shining strength. ⟦अक्तुरिति⟧: this is a name of night, because it is stated among its names: ⟦अक्तुरूर्म्योति⟧, "aktu, ūrmyā" **NOTE**{Ṛk 3: the reference text adds here the reference "ni. 1. 7. 4", which the supplied text does not have.}. The first case stands in the sense of the second. Having passed over (⟦अति⟧) the night, the darkness of night which smears over the world, they are ⟦सिंधवः⟧, flowing, pervading everywhere; ⟦अससंतः⟧, not sleeping in their own operations, unceasing; for that very reason ⟦अजराः⟧; and they ⟦न⟧ ⟦रेजंते⟧; that is, they do not tremble. In burning, cooking and the like they do not move, or they are not moved by others. Or else: the word na expresses a comparison. Of ⟦भात्वक्षसो⟧, of the Sun, the one whose strength is light itself, ⟦सिंधवो न⟧, like the rays. As they, being given to flowing and pervading, having passed over (⟦अति⟧) the ⟦अक्तुः⟧, the darkness that smears over, ⟦रेजंते⟧ without sleeping, so the radiances, of the character described, of this Agni of shining strength also ⟦रेजंते⟧ everywhere; that is, they tremble; the meaning is that they pervade.

## Ṛk 4

⟦विश्ववेदसं⟧ is having all wealth. ⟦वेद⟧ is a name of wealth, because it is read among its names: ⟦वेदो वरिव⟧, "vedas, varivas" **NOTE**{Ṛk 4: the reference text adds here the reference "ni. 2. 10. 4", which the supplied text does not have.}. Such an Agni, ⟦यं⟧, whom, the ⟦भृगवो⟧, those born in the family of Bhṛgu, the roasters of sin, at the ⟦नाभा⟧ of ⟦पृथिव्या⟧, of the altar — the word for the whole is used of a part; or else, the altar is the earth because of scriptural texts such as ⟦एतावती वै पृथिवीत्यादिश्रुतेर्वेद्याः⟧, "So great indeed is the earth" **NOTE**{Ṛk 4: the reference text adds here the reference "tai. saṃ. 2. 6. 4. 3", which the supplied text does not have.} — at its navel, on the uttaravedi, by ⟦मज्मना⟧, by the strength, of ⟦भुवनस्य⟧, of the multitude of beings, as the occasion, ⟦आईरिरे⟧; that is, facing him, they set him in motion; they established him. ⟦तं⟧, that, ⟦अग्निं⟧, in ⟦स्वे⟧, his own, ⟦दमे⟧, house, on the uttaravedi, with ⟦गीर्भिः⟧, with praises, ⟦हिनुहि⟧ (⟦आ⟧); that is, reach. The root is ⟦हि गतौ वृद्धौ च⟧, "hi in the senses of going and of growing". The absence of the elision of hi is by ⟦प्रत्ययाच्चंदसि नावचनमिति⟧, "and after the u of a suffix; in the Veda there is a statement of option". The Agni who (⟦यः⟧), being ⟦एकः⟧, the chief one, or quite alone, ⟦राजति⟧, is the lord, of ⟦वस्वः⟧, of wealth such as cows; "in order to give it" is to be supplied. ⟦राजतीत्यैश्वर्यकर्मा⟧: rājati has the sense of lordship, because it is read among the names of that action: ⟦क्षियति राजतीति⟧, "kṣiyati, rājati" **NOTE**{Ṛk 4: the reference text adds here the reference "ni. 2. 21. 4", which the supplied text does not have.}. For this there is an example: ⟦वरुणो⟧ ⟦न⟧, like the Sun, the one who wards off. As he rules over all, in that way.

## Ṛk 5

The Agni who (⟦यः⟧) is not such as can be subjected to ⟦वराय⟧, to warding off, to restraint. For this three examples are stated. Like the ⟦स्वन⟧ of the ⟦मरुतां⟧: as that cannot be seized, in that way. Likewise like a ⟦सेनेव⟧ that is ⟦सृष्टा⟧, let loose by a powerful man for the destruction of his enemies: as that cannot be stopped by others, in that way. Likewise as (⟦यथा⟧) the ⟦दिव्या⟧, that which is in heaven, ⟦अशनिः⟧ simply falls and is not warded off, in that way. He shows that there is such power. This Agni, with ⟦तिगितैः⟧, with whetted ones, that have become sharp — the change of the final is Vedic — with ⟦जंभैः⟧, with teeth, with flames that stand in the place of teeth, ⟦अत्ति⟧; that is, he eats those who oppose us. Likewise he ⟦भर्वति⟧; that is, he injures. The root is ⟦भर्व हिंसायां⟧, "bharv in the sense of injuring". But Yāska says ⟦भर्वतिरत्तिकर्मेति⟧, "bharvati has the sense of eating" (⟦नि ९-२३⟧). Although the meaning of both atti and bharvati is simply eating, yet a subordinate difference between them is to be understood. For this there is an example: ⟦योधो न⟧, like a hero who strikes. As he ⟦भर्वति⟧, devours, his enemies, in that way. Moreover ⟦सः⟧, that Agni, ⟦न्यृंजते⟧ the ⟦वना⟧, the forests, the masses of trees and the like; that is, he adorns them thoroughly; the meaning is that he burns them. ⟦ऋंजतिः प्रसाधनकर्मेति⟧, "ṛñjati has the sense of adorning", says Yāska.

## Ṛk 6

This Agni, ⟦कुवित्⟧, many times, ⟦असत्⟧, let him be, ⟦वीः⟧, one who desires, ⟦नो⟧, our, ⟦उचथस्य⟧, uttered praise **NOTE**{Ṛk 6: the supplied text glosses the word by ⟦नोऽस्माकमुचथस्योक्तस्य⟧, "the uttered", where the reference text has "ukthasya"; which is to stand?}. In the leṭ of the root as there is the augment aṭ. Or else the connection is: of ⟦न⟧, of us, who are sprung from the family of the great seer who bears the name ⟦उचथस्य⟧. Likewise, being ⟦वसुः⟧, the one who makes all dwell, or one who stands in the place of wealth, with ⟦वसुभिः⟧, with riches that make men dwell, ⟦कामं⟧, exceedingly, or, what is desired, ⟦कुवित्⟧, very abundantly, let him ⟦आवरत्⟧; that is, let him cover. The meaning is: by giving what is desired let him make the desire cease. In the leṭ of the root vṛ there is the augment aṭ; the elision of the conjugational suffix is Vedic. This Agni, being ⟦चोदः⟧, the one who urges us on in our rites, the ⟦धियः⟧, the rites, for ⟦सातये⟧, for gain, ⟦कुवित्⟧, much, let him ⟦तुतुज्यात्⟧; that is, let him hasten them; the meaning is: let him urge them on. The root tuj has the sense of urging. The elision called ślu of śap is Vedic. ⟦तं⟧, that Agni, who is ⟦शुचिप्रतीकं⟧, having beautiful limbs, having beautiful flames, with ⟦अया⟧ ⟦धिया⟧, with this understanding in the form of praise, I ⟦गृणे⟧; that is, I utter him; the meaning is: I praise him.

## Ṛk 7

⟦घृतप्रतीकं⟧ is having ghee as his beginning, because he is offered to with ghee at the fore-offerings and the like. Or else ⟦प्रतीकमंगं⟧: pratīka is a limb; the meaning is: having a blazing flame. Moreover the ⟦अग्निं⟧ who is ⟦धूर्षदं⟧, sitting at the yoke, at the carrying through, of ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, of the sacrifice, that belongs to ⟦वः⟧, to you — the one who carries the sacrifice through — ⟦मित्रं न⟧, like a friend, one who is ⟦समिधान⟧, kindling him with pieces of fuel, ⟦ऋंजते⟧; that is, he adorns him. The verb ṛñj has the sense of adorning. ⟦इंधानः⟧, being well kindled, ⟦अक्रो⟧, overspread by flames, kindling-sticks and the like, or not overcome by others — the suffix ḍa after the root kram is Vedic — at the ⟦विदथेषु⟧, at the sacrifices, or at the praises that make known, which are the occasion, ⟦दीद्यत्⟧, himself shining, our ⟦धियं⟧, understanding, which has sacrifice and the like as its object, and is ⟦शुक्रवर्णां⟧, of bright colour, spotless — or, the rite such as the Jyotiṣṭoma — he ⟦यंसते⟧ (⟦उत्⟧); that is, he indeed sets it going. In the leṭ of the root yam there is the augment aṭ; and the suffix sip. The word u is for emphasis. ⟦धीरिति⟧: dhī is a name of action, because it is read among its names: ⟦धीः शमीति⟧, "dhī, śamī" **NOTE**{Ṛk 7: the reference text adds here the reference "ni. 2. 1. 21", which the supplied text does not have.}.

## Ṛk 8

**NOTE**{Ṛk 8: the supplied block opens with the stray words ⟦सः यज्ञभाष्यः⟧ between quotation marks, which the reference text does not have; they are left untranslated. Do they belong to the bhāṣya?} O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦अप्रयुच्छन्⟧, not being heedless towards us — the root is ⟦युच्छ प्रमादे⟧, "yuch in the sense of heedlessness" — being of unbroken activity, with ⟦अप्रयुच्छत्भिः⟧, with those that are not heedless, that are free from inattention, with ⟦शिवेभिः⟧, with those that are auspicious through mantras, with ⟦शग्मैः⟧, with those that cause happiness, with ⟦पायुभी⟧, with ways of protecting, ⟦पाहि⟧, protect, ⟦नः⟧, us. Moreover, O ⟦इष्टे⟧, O Agni who are to be sought by all, ⟦जा⟧, being born, being kindled by us, with ⟦अदभेभिः⟧, with those that are uninjured, with ⟦अदृपितेभिः⟧, with those that are not overcome by anyone at all — the root is ⟦दृप द्वन्न उत्केशे⟧, "dṛp and dṛmph in the sense of afflicting"; it belongs to the class beginning with tud — with ⟦अनिमिषत्भिः⟧, with those that are free from winking, whose nature is without sloth; with such characteristics ⟦पाहि⟧, guard, ⟦नः⟧, us, ⟦परि⟧, all around. Or else: those who are born one after another are ⟦जाः⟧. ⟦नो⟧ ⟦जा⟧, the offspring connected with us, in the form of sons, grandsons and the rest, ⟦परि पाहि⟧; that is, protect them all around. Protect not us alone, but our sons, grandsons and the rest as well.
