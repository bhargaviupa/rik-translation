## Introduction

The ninth sūkta, of ten Ṛks, beginning ⟦अभि तष्टवेति⟧. On this the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦अभि तष्टेव दश प्रजापतिः स वैश्वामित्रो वाच्यो ना द्वौ ना तौ न वैकोऽपीति⟧. That is: the sūkta beginning "abhi taṣṭeva" has ten Ṛks; Prajāpati; he is to be called Vaiśvāmitra; or the two; or those two not; or not even one. In the lineage of Viśvāmitra, Prajāpati, the son of Vāc, or Prajāpati is the ṛṣi. Both of them together are the two ṛṣis of this sūkta: this is the second view. Or else, neither of the two, but Viśvāmitra alone: this is the third view. The metre is Triṣṭubh. The deity is Indra.

On the Cāturviṃśika day, at the midday pressing, in the śastra of the Acchāvāka, the sūkta ⟦अभि तष्टेव⟧ is the one bearing the name "to be recited day after day". And it is taught in the sūtra ⟦उदु ब्रह्माण्यभि तष्टेवेतीतरावहरहःशस्यॆ⟧ (⟦आ ७-४⟧): thus, in the recitation of every day, the two others are the sūkta beginning "ud u brahmāṇi" and the sūkta beginning "abhi taṣṭeva". So everywhere in the ahargaṇas, on the second and the following days, at the midday pressing, in the śastra of the Acchāvāka, this ⟦एतत्तूक्तं⟧ sūkta is used.

## Ṛk 1

The sacrificer speaks to the praiser. O praiser, ⟦मनीषां⟧, the praise concerning Indra, ⟦दीधय⟧, make it shining on every side. Here is an illustration: ⟦तप्पेव⟧, as ⟦तप्पा⟧ fashions wood by hewing, in the same way. ⟦सुधुरः⟧, provided with a good yoke, ⟦वाजी⟧, swift, ⟦अत्यः⟧ ⟦न⟧, like a horse, ⟦जिहानः⟧, engaged in the work, ⟦पराणि⟧, the highest, ⟦प्रियाणि⟧, those most dear to Indra. Or else: ⟦पराणि⟧, the works performed on the later days, ⟦मर्मृशत्⟧, touching exceedingly, ⟦सुमेधाः⟧, I who have good intelligence, ⟦कवीन्⟧: those who, from the sacrifice performed before, attained the state of gods **NOTE**{the reference text has the nominative, "having performed sacrifices before", where the supplied text has the ablative ⟦यज्ञाद्⟧}, they are the poets, ⟦कवयः⟧. ⟦संदृशे⟧, to behold them I wish.

On what has been said there is a Brāhmaṇa: ⟦अभि तप्पेव दीधया मनीषामित्यच्छावाकोऽहरहः शंसत्यभिवत्तत्यै रूपम⟧: the Acchāvāka recites day after day; this is the form for the word that follows ⟦शंसत्य⟧ **NOTE**{the sense of the word that follows ⟦शंसत्य⟧ in this passage is not clear; both editions have it as it stands}. Next, the words ⟦प्रियाणि मर्मशत्वराणीति यान्येव पराण्यहानि तानि प्रियाणि तान्येव तदभिमर्तृशतो यंतभ्यारभमाणाः⟧: whichever days are the later ones, those are the dear ones; those very ones, thereupon, touching them, they go, setting about them. Next ⟦परो वा आस्माल्लोकात्स्वर्गो लोकस्तमेव तदभिनदति⟧: the heavenly world is beyond this world; that very world he thereby addresses. Next ⟦कवीरिच्छामि संदृशे सुमेधा इति ये वै ते न ऋषयः पूर्वे प्रेतास्ते वै कवयस्तानेव तदभ्यतिवदति⟧: those who are our ṛṣis and have departed before, they are indeed the poets; those very ones he thereby addresses (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. ६-२०⟧).

⟦तप्पेव⟧ is the form, with the suffix tṛn, of the roots ⟦तक्षू⟧ and ⟦त्वक्कू⟧, in the sense of making thin. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n (nit), ⟦नित्वरः⟧.

⟦दीधय⟧: it comes from the root ⟦दीधीज्⟧, in the senses of shining and playing, with the causative sense included in it, in the imperative. By the rule that in the Veda there is variety, śap is not elided. By interchange (vyatyaya) the parasmaipada is used.

⟦सुधुरः⟧: by the rule that in the Veda a word of two syllables has the udātta at its beginning, the latter member of the compound has the udātta on its first syllable.

⟦जिहानः⟧: it is the form, with śānac, of the root ⟦ओहाज्⟧, in the sense of going. By the rule on the roots bhṛ and the others, the reduplicative syllable takes the vowel i. Because of the cit-mark, the udātta on the final syllable is obtained; but by the rule "of reduplicated stems, the first", the udātta is on the first syllable.

⟦मर्मशत्⟧: it comes from the root ⟦मृश⟧, in the sense of touching, in yaṅ-luk. By the rule giving the augments rug and rik when luk follows, the reduplicative syllable takes the augment rug. The form is that of śatṛ after the stem ending in it. By the rule that num does not occur after a reduplicated stem before śatṛ, num does not occur. The accent is that of reduplicated words.

⟦संदृशे⟧: from the root ⟦दृशिर्⟧, in the sense of seeing, in the sense of the infinitive. By the rule ⟦दृशे विख्ये⟧ the form is given irregularly as ending in the suffix ken; therefore the udātta is on the first syllable. In the compound the accent is that of the latter member, which ends in a kṛt suffix.

⟦सुमेधाः⟧: by the rule ⟦नित्यमसिच्⟧, the suffix asic is added invariably after prajā and medhā. The accent is that of a cit suffix, ⟦चित्त्वरः⟧.

## Ṛk 2

⟦उत⟧ is used in the sense of address. O Indra, ⟦कवीनां⟧, of the poets who have done good works and have attained the state of gods, ⟦जनिम⟧, the birth: by what work did they go to heaven, or from where was their origin — this matter, ⟦पृच्छ⟧, ask, ⟦इना⟧, the lords, the elders. Those poets, ⟦मनोधृतः⟧, of controlled mind, and therefore ⟦सुकृतः⟧, of good works, being so, ⟦द्यां⟧, heaven, ⟦तक्षत⟧, made. Now, in this sacrifice, ⟦धर्मणि⟧, for you, ⟦प्रण्यः⟧, being led forth, ⟦मनोवाता⟧, with the speed of the mind, ⟦वर्धमानाः⟧, growing, and making you grow, ⟦इमाः⟧, these praises made by us, ⟦नु⟧, quickly, ⟦ग्मन्⟧, may they go to you. ⟦उ⟧ is for the purpose of renown everywhere.

⟦पृच्छ⟧: it is the form in the imperative of the root ⟦प्रच्छ⟧, in the sense of desire to know; because it belongs to the tudādi class, the vikaraṇa is śa. Because that is ṅit, the saṃprasāraṇa is made by the rule beginning with grah and jyā. The accent is nighāta.

⟦प्रण्यः⟧: from the root ⟦णीञ्⟧, in the sense of carrying, an Uṇādi kvip in the sense of the object. Because all rules are optional in the Veda, yaṇ is substituted, setting aside the substitution of iyaṅ. By the rule concerning udātta and svarita, there is the svarita accent.

⟦ग्मन्⟧: from the root gam, in laṅ; by the rule "variously in the Veda", śap is elided. Because the ending jhi is ṅit, by the rule beginning with gam and han the penultimate letter is elided. The accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 3

⟦अत्र⟧, in this world of earth, ⟦गुह्या⟧, hidden deeds, ⟦सीमित्⟧, everywhere indeed, ⟦नि दधानाः⟧, performing them, these poets, ⟦उत⟧, also, ⟦क्षत्राय⟧, for strength or for wealth, ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth, ⟦समंजन्⟧: they made the earth joined with the plants and heaven with the gods. Moreover, they, ⟦मात्राभिः⟧: the ⟦मात्राः⟧ are mountains, heaps of rock, so called because they are measured or because they measure; with those measures ⟦सं ममिरे⟧, they measured, and they made those two, heaven and earth, bounded by a definite extent. ⟦समृते⟧, joined with each other, ⟦उर्वी⟧, wide, ⟦मही⟧, great, those two, heaven and earth, ⟦येमुः⟧, they restrained them; with the atmosphere as the prop they held them up between. Likewise ⟦धायसे⟧, for the holding of those two, ⟦धुः⟧, they placed the atmosphere. ⟦इत्⟧ is in the sense of emphasis.

⟦गुह्या⟧: "existing in a cave". The suffix yat is in the sense of "existing in"; by the rule on words ending in yat, with its exclusion of the word nāva, the first syllable is udātta.

⟦दधानाः⟧: it is the form, with śānac, of the root dhā. The accent is that of reduplicated words.

⟦अंजन्⟧: it is the form, in laṅ, of the root ⟦अन्नू⟧, in the senses of manifesting, anointing and going. By the rule ⟦श्नसोरल्लोपः⟧, the vowel a of śna and of the root as is elided. The accent is nighāta.

⟦मात्राभिः⟧: from the root ⟦मा⟧ in the sense of measuring, or from the root ⟦माज्⟧ in the sense of measuring. By the rule beginning ⟦हुयाम⟧, the suffix tran. The accent is that of a nit suffix, ⟦नित्सर्वः⟧.

⟦ममिरे⟧: it is the form, in liṭ, of the root ⟦माज्⟧, in the sense of measuring. By the optional rule ⟦चादिलोपे⟧ there is no nighāta.

⟦येमुः⟧: it is the form, in liṭ with the ending us, of the root ⟦यम⟧, in the sense of ceasing. There is no nighāta, because it follows a finite verb ending in tiṅ.

⟦धायसे⟧: by the rule on vahi, hā and dhā in the Veda, the suffix asun. The accent is that of a nit suffix, ⟦नित्सर्वः⟧.

⟦धुः⟧: it is the form of the root dhā in luṅ.

## Ṛk 4

⟦विश्वे⟧, all the poets, ⟦पर्यभूषन्⟧, adorned all round, that is, made an ornament all round, Indra, who was mounting the chariot, ⟦आतिष्ठंतं⟧. ⟦स्वरोचिः⟧: he whose own brilliance alone it is, is ⟦स्वरोचिः⟧; the meaning is that he shines by his own radiance alone. Therefore, ⟦श्रियः⟧, the brilliances, ⟦वसानः⟧, covering, this Indra ⟦चरति⟧, is present everywhere. ⟦वृष्णः⟧, of the one who showers desires, ⟦असुरस्य⟧: because he impels all (⟦अस्यति⟧), as the inner controller, he is called asura. Of that Indra, ⟦तत्⟧, of such a kind, as experienced before, ⟦नाम⟧: ⟦नाम⟧ is deed, since by it he makes all bow, namely the enemies. Or else, ⟦नाम⟧ is what is bowed to, what is saluted by all: the body of Indra, or his deed. It is ⟦महत्⟧, great, endowed with wonder. Likewise ⟦विश्वरूपः⟧, partaking of manifold forms, that Indra, in the form of Varuṇa, ⟦अमृतानि⟧, the waters, ⟦तस्थौ⟧ with ⟦आ⟧, presides over.

⟦विश्वे⟧: śī in place of jas. ⟦अभूषन्⟧: it is the form, in laṅ, of the root ⟦भूष⟧, in the sense of adorning. ⟦वसानः⟧: it is the form, with śānac, of the root ⟦वस⟧, in the sense of covering. As to the accent of the lasārvadhātuka, the accent is that of the root, ⟦धातुस्वरः⟧.

⟦नाम⟧: from the root ⟦णमु⟧, in the sense of bowing; by the rule beginning ⟦ना मन्मीमन्⟧, it is given irregularly as ending in the suffix manin, and therefore the udātta is on the first syllable.

⟦विश्वरूपः⟧ is his name. In the bahuvrīhi, by the rule that viśva has this accent when a name is meant, the former member has the udātta on its last syllable.

⟦अमृतानि⟧: the accusative case, by the rule on continuous connection with time and distance, ⟦कालाध्वनोरत्यंतसंयोग⟧. ⟦तस्थौ⟧: it is the form, in liṭ with ṇal, of the verb tiṣṭhati.

## Ṛk 5

⟦वृषभः⟧, the one who showers desires, ⟦पूर्वः⟧, ancient, and therefore ⟦ज्यायान्⟧, Indra, ⟦असूत⟧, sent forth the waters. ⟦पूर्वीः⟧, many, these waters created by him are ⟦अस्य⟧, his, ⟦शुरुधः⟧: they are those that check ⟦शुचः⟧, of thirst. ⟦दिवः⟧ ⟦नपाता⟧: you two who do not cause heaven to fall, O ⟦राजाना⟧, kings, Indra and Varuṇa, ⟦प्रदिवः⟧, of the one who shines forth exceedingly, ⟦विदथस्य⟧, of the praiser who performs the sacrifice, ⟦धीभिः⟧, by the praises, ⟦क्षत्रं⟧, wealth, ⟦दधाथे⟧, you two hold, in order to give it to us.

⟦असूत⟧: it is the form, in laṅ, of the root ⟦षूज्⟧, in the sense of the release of the embryo of living beings. The accent is that of the augment aṭ, ⟦आडागमस्वरः⟧.

⟦ज्यायान्⟧: from the word praśasya, with the suffix īyasun, by the rule ⟦ज्य च⟧ **NOTE**{the reference text gives a Pāṇini reference here (5.3.61), which the supplied text does not} the substitute jya. By the rule ⟦ज्यादादीयस⟧ **NOTE**{the reference text gives a Pāṇini reference here (6.4.160), which the supplied text does not}, the initial vowel ī of īyas is replaced by ā. Because it is n-marked, the udātta is on the first syllable.

⟦शुरुधः⟧: formed as in the class beginning with pṛṣodara.

⟦नपाता⟧: since the word is given irregularly by the rule on nabhrāṇ and napāt, the negative nañ does not keep its natural form. Because it is a vocative, the accent is nighāta.

⟦धीभिः⟧: by the rule ⟦सावेकाच⟧, the case ending is udātta.

⟦राजाना⟧: because it is a vocative, the accent is nighāta.

⟦दधाथे⟧: it is the form of the root dhā in laṭ. The accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 6

O ⟦राजाना⟧, kings, Indra and Varuṇa, ⟦विदथे⟧, in this sacrifice, ⟦विश्वानि⟧, pervaded, ⟦पुरूणि⟧, full of Soma and the other things to be sacrificed with, ⟦त्रीणि सदांसि⟧, the three seats, that is, the pressings, ⟦परि भूषथः⟧, you two adorn on every side. O Indra, you, ⟦जगन्वान्⟧, have gone to the sacrifice. ⟦अपि⟧ is in the sense of probability. Since I, here, ⟦व्रते⟧, in the sacrifice, ⟦मनसा⟧, with the mind, ⟦अपश्यं⟧, saw ⟦वायुकेशान्⟧, those whose rays move like the wind, ⟦गंधर्वान्⟧, the protectors of Soma, Svāna, Bhrāja and the others. And those Svāna, Bhrāja and the others are clearly declared in the Taittirīya: ⟦स्नान भ्राजांघारे बंभारे हस्त सुहस्त कृशानवेते वः सोमक्रयणास्तान्रक्षध्वं मा वो दभन्⟧ (⟦स. १-२-७⟧): O Svāna, Bhrāja, Aṅghāre, Bambhāre, Hasta, Suhasta, Kṛśānu, these are your Soma-purchasers; protect them; let them not harm you.

⟦भूषथः⟧: it is the form, in laṭ, of the root ⟦भूष⟧, in the sense of adorning. ⟦अपश्यं⟧: it is the form, in laṅ, of the root ⟦दृशिर्⟧, in the sense of seeing. There is no nighāta, because it stands at the beginning of a pāda, ⟦सादादित्वाद⟧.

⟦जगन्वान्⟧: from the root gam, with kvasu. Since by the rule on ⟦गमहनविदविश⟧, the roots gam, han, vid and viś, the augment iṭ is optional, there is no iṭ here. By the rule that m of a root becomes n and by the rule on m and v, the letter m becomes n. The accent is that of the suffix.

⟦गंधर्वान्⟧: from the root ⟦धृञ्⟧, in the sense of holding, when the word go stands as the governing word (upapada), by the rule ⟦गवि गन्⟧, the suffix va. In conjunction with it, gan is substituted for the word go. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 7

Those sacrificers, for the sake of this Indra, ⟦वृषभस्य⟧, the giver of the fruit that is desired, ⟦धेनोः⟧, ⟦गोः⟧, the cow marked out by the names ⟦विश्वायुर्विश्वव्यचा⟧ and the like, ⟦नामभिः⟧, the oblation of that cow, its milk and the like, ⟦सक्म्यं⟧, fit to be shared, ⟦नु⟧, quickly, ⟦ममिरे⟧: they milk it, since roots have several meanings. And those poets who, having performed the sacrifice with that oblation, attained the state of gods, ⟦अन्यत्ऽअन्यत्⟧, new, ⟦असुर्यं⟧, the strength of the asuras, ⟦वसानाः⟧, covering, and therefore ⟦मायिनः⟧, have taken by māyā the forms they desire. Or else: ⟦मायिनः⟧, being possessed of wisdom, ⟦अस्मिन्⟧, in this Indra, ⟦रूपं⟧, each his own form, ⟦नि ममिरे⟧, having limited it by a definite extent, they set it down.

⟦इत्⟧ is a filler. ⟦ममिरे⟧: it is the form, in liṭ, of the root ⟦माज्⟧, in the sense of measuring. The accent is nighāta.

⟦सक्त्यं⟧: in the rule ⟦युजिरुचितिजां कुश्च⟧ **NOTE**{the reference text gives a reference to the Uṇādi-sūtras at this rule, which the supplied text does not}, since the word ca has the sense of adding what has not been mentioned, the suffix mak after the root ⟦सचते⟧ and the change to the velar. ⟦सचनं⟧ is ⟦सक्त्यः⟧. Then, in the sense of "existing in that", by the rule on "existing" in the Veda, the suffix yat. By the rule on words ending in yat, with its exclusion of the word nāva, the first syllable is udātta.

⟦गोः⟧: the accent is that of the single substitute.

⟦असुर्यं⟧: in the sense of ⟦असुरस्य स्वम⟧, "belonging to the asura", by the rule (⟦पा. ४-४-१२३⟧) the suffix yat. The accent is svarita, because of the t-mark of the suffix (tit).

⟦रूपं⟧: the root ⟦रूप⟧, in the sense of the act of forming, ending in ṇi. By the rule ⟦खष्पशिल्पशष्पवाष्परूपपर्पतल्पाः⟧ (⟦उ. ३-२८⟧), the word is given irregularly as ending in the suffix pa; the letter p is elided and there is no guṇa. ⟦रूपयति⟧ or ⟦रूप्यते⟧, "he forms" or "it is formed": thus ⟦रूपं⟧. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 8

⟦तत्⟧, therefore, ⟦सवितुः⟧, of this Indra, who impels the whole world as its inner controller, ⟦मे⟧, my, ⟦हिरण्ययीं⟧, golden, ⟦अमतिं⟧, radiance: ⟦नकिः⟧, who indeed can limit it? ⟦यां⟧, which radiance of mine, ⟦अशिश्रेत्⟧, he who resorts to it, he, praised with a good praise, ⟦सुष्टुती⟧, ⟦विश्वमिन्वे⟧, those who gratify everything, ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth, ⟦वव्रे⟧, he chooses on every side as his own. Here is an illustration: ⟦अपीव योषा जनिमानि⟧: as a woman chooses ⟦जनिमानि⟧, the born ones, the offspring, so he. **NOTE**{the reference text adds here, before the grammatical part, a remark that nu is a filler}

⟦नकिः⟧ is a particle.

⟦हिरण्ययीं⟧: in the sense of a product of gold, by the rule ⟦मयड्वैतयो⟧ **NOTE**{the reference text gives a Pāṇini reference here (4.3.143), which the supplied text does not}, the suffix mayaṭ. By the rule ⟦ऋत्ववास्तवास्त्वमाध्वीहिरण्ययानि⟧, the forms being given irregularly in the Veda, the letter m of mayaṭ is elided. Because the suffix is ṭ-marked, the feminine suffix ṅīp by the rule beginning ⟦टिद्दाण⟧. The accent is that of the suffix.

⟦अमतिं⟧: from the root ⟦अम⟧, in the senses of going and the like; by the rule ⟦अमेरति⟧, the suffix ati. The accent is that of the suffix.

⟦अशिश्रेत्⟧: it is the form, in laṅ, of the root śri; by the rule "variously in the Veda", śap is replaced by ślu. There is no nighāta, because of the connection with the relative construction.

⟦सुष्टुती⟧: by the rule ⟦सुपां सुलु⟧, in the instrumental the homogeneous long vowel results.

⟦विश्वमिन्वे⟧: from the root ⟦इवि⟧, in the senses of pervading and pleasing, the suffix aṇ in the sense of the object. Because the root has the marker i, there is num. By the rule ⟦तत्पुरुषे कृति⟧, "variously", the case ending of the accusative is not elided (⟦आलुक्⟧). The accent is that of the latter member, which ends in a kṛt suffix.

⟦योषा⟧: from the verb yauti, in the sense of mixing; by the rule ⟦वृत्यवदिहनिकमिकषियुमुचिभ्यः स⟧, the suffix sa. Because it belongs to the group beginning with grāma, the udātta is on the first syllable.

⟦जनिमानि⟧: from the root ⟦जनी⟧, in the sense of coming forth; by the rule ⟦हृजनिभ्यामिमनिन्⟧, the suffix imanin in the sense of past time. Because it is n-marked, the udātta is on the first syllable.

⟦ववे⟧: it is the form, in liṭ, of the root ⟦वृञ्⟧, in the sense of choosing.

## Ṛk 9

O Indra and Varuṇa, ⟦युवं⟧, you two, ⟦प्रत्नस्य⟧, of the praiser, ⟦महः⟧, such good, ⟦साधथः⟧, you accomplish; that is, you cause to be accomplished. Of what kind is it? Therefore he says ⟦यद्दैवी स्वस्तिः⟧: this good that belongs to the gods, marked by self-rule. Moreover, you two, ⟦नः⟧, us, ⟦परि⟧, on every side, ⟦स्यातं⟧, may you be our protectors. ⟦विश्वे⟧, all, ⟦मायिनः⟧, the gods, ⟦गोपाजिह्वस्य⟧: he whose speech is of this kind, a protecting tongue, "do not be afraid", is so called; ⟦तस्थुषः⟧, of Indra, who is the more firmly established, ⟦विरूपा⟧, of many kinds, the deeds done, ⟦कृतानि⟧, such as the slaying of Vṛtra and the rest, ⟦पश्यंति⟧, they see. Thus Indra's being of every form has been told.

⟦युवं⟧: in the dual of the word yuṣmad, by the rule ⟦युवावौ द्विवचन⟧ the substitute yuva. By the rule beginning ⟦जेप्रथमयोरम⟧ **NOTE**{the reference text gives Pāṇini references for both of these rules (7.2.92 and 7.1.28), which the supplied text does not}, the substitute am for the case ending.

⟦साधथः⟧: it is the form, in laṭ, of the root ⟦साध⟧, in the sense of accomplishing, with the causative ending ṇi. By the rule "variously elsewhere too", ṇi is elided. The accent is nighāta.

⟦दैवी⟧: by the rule on the suffixes yañ and añ after deva and the like, the suffix añ. The accent is that of a ñ-marked suffix, ⟦इत्यस्वरः⟧.

⟦स्यातं⟧: it is the form of the root as in liṅ.

⟦गोपाजिह्वस्य⟧: the root ⟦गुपू⟧, in the sense of protecting, ending in the suffix āya; in kvip, when the elision of a and the elision of ya have been made, the form is ⟦गोपा⟧. In the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the former member.

⟦तस्थुषः⟧: the root ⟦ष्ठा⟧, in the sense of cessation of motion, in kvasu. The iṭ obtained by the rule ⟦वस्सेकाजाद्भसा⟧ is stopped, since by the rule ⟦संप्रसारणाश्रयं च बलीय⟧ the saṃprasāraṇa is the stronger. By the rule ⟦वसोः संप्रसारणं⟧ the saṃprasāraṇa of vas takes place. The accent is that of the suffix.

⟦पश्यंति⟧: the root ⟦दृशिर्⟧, in the sense of seeing. The accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 10

O Indra, ⟦वाजसात्⟧: that in which there is ⟦सातिः⟧, gain, of ⟦वाजस्य⟧, food, this is ⟦वाजसातिः⟧. ⟦अस्मिन्⟧, in this ⟦भरे⟧: warriors bear (⟦बिभ्रति⟧) the fortune of victory by it; thus ⟦भरः⟧ is battle. In that battle, ⟦शुनं⟧, which is ⟦शूनम⟧, swollen, that is, grown great with enthusiasm. Or else ⟦शुनं⟧ is a name of happiness: bringing happiness. ⟦मघवानं⟧, possessed of wealth, and therefore ⟦इंद्रं⟧, endowed with unsurpassed lordship, ⟦नृतमं⟧, the supreme leader of the whole world, you, ⟦हुवेम⟧, we would call; we, the Kuśikas, would call you for the sacrifice. Likewise ⟦शृण्वंतं⟧, hearing the praise made by us, ⟦उग्रं⟧, terrifying to enemies, ⟦समत्तु⟧, in battles, ⟦वृत्राणि⟧, all the rākṣasas marked out as Vṛtra, ⟦घ्नंतं⟧, harming, ⟦धनानां⟧, of the wealth connected with enemies, ⟦संजितं⟧, glossed as ⟦समग्नेतारं⟧ **NOTE**{⟦समग्नेतारं⟧ is not a clear word; the reference text has "samagjetāram" (winner), the supplied text "leader", and neither reads as a whole word; I have not translated it}, you, ⟦ऊतये⟧, for protection, we would call.

⟦शुनं⟧: the root ⟦टुओश्वि⟧, in the senses of going and growing, in the niṣṭhā; by the optional rule beginning "yasya vibhāṣā", the augment iṭ is prohibited. Because it belongs to the yajādi group, the saṃprasāraṇa. The absence of lengthening is Vedic. The accent is that of the suffix.

⟦हुवेम⟧: from the root hve; by the rule "variously in the Veda", when the saṃprasāraṇa has been made and śap has been elided, the form is in liṅ. The accent is nighāta.

⟦भरे⟧: the root ⟦भृञ्⟧, in the sense of bearing, with the suffix gha. Because it belongs to the vṛṣādi group, the udātta is on the first syllable.

⟦वाजसातौ⟧: the root ⟦षणु⟧, in the sense of giving, with ktin in the sense of the action. By the rule beginning ⟦जनसन⟧, ā takes the place of the nasal. In the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the former member.

⟦समत्तु⟧: the root ⟦अद⟧, in the sense of eating, after the prefix sam, kvip. Since they together consume the lives of warriors, ⟦समदः⟧ are battles. The accent is that of the latter member, which ends in a kṛt suffix.

⟦घ्नंतं⟧: the root ⟦हन⟧, in the senses of harming and going, in śatṛ; by the rule beginning ⟦गमहन⟧ the penultimate letter is elided. By the rule ⟦हो हंते⟧ the substitution of gh.

⟦संजितं⟧: the root ⟦जि⟧, in the sense of conquering; kvip.
