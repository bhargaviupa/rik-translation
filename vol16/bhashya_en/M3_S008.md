## Introduction

The eighth sūkta, beginning ⟦अंजंति⟧, is Viśvāmitra's and has eleven Ṛks. Here the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦अंजंति यूपस्तुतिः षष्ठ्या द्याभिर्बहवोऽन्या व्रश्चिन्यष्टमी वैश्वदेवी वा तृतीया-सप्तम्यावनुष्टुभाविति⟧, "'They anoint': a praise of the yūpa; by the sixth and those that follow, many are praised; the last belongs to what has been hewn; the eighth is optionally addressed to the All-gods; the third and the seventh are anuṣṭubh". These two, ⟦उच्छयस्व वनस्पते⟧ and ⟦ये वृक्षास⟧, the third and the seventh, are anuṣṭubh; the rest are triṣṭubh. The yūpa is the deity of the whole sūkta. Of the Ṛk beginning ⟦वनस्पते शतवल्य⟧ the deity is the stump that is the root of the yūpa that has been cut. The eighth, ⟦आदित्या रुद्रा वसव⟧, is addressed to the All-gods or to the yūpa. Here, of the sixth and those that follow, many yūpas are the deity, because many are praised. The rest have a single yūpa as their deity.

At the anointing of the yūpa this Ṛk, the one beginning with "they anoint you", is used: ⟦प्रेषितो⟧ ⟦यूपायाज्यमानायांजंति त्वामध्वरे देवयंतः⟧ (⟦आ. ३-१⟧), "When he has been called upon, for the yūpa that is being anointed he recites the verse that begins so". At the raising of the yūpa the two Ṛks beginning with samiddhasya are used, because the sūtra lays down: ⟦उच्छयस्व वनस्पते समिद्धस्य श्रयमाणः पुर-स्तात्⟧ (⟦आ. ३-१⟧). At the girding of the yūpa, because the sūtra lays down: ⟦युवा सुवासाः परिवीत⟧ ⟦आगादिति परिदध्यात्⟧ (⟦आ. ३-१⟧), "He should conclude with ⟦युवा सुवासाः परिवीत⟧ …". At the raising of the yūpa it is laid down in the sūtra**NOTE**{the supplied text reads sūtritaṃ, "laid down in the sūtra"; the reference text reads sūcitaṃ, "indicated"} that he should stop at the half-verse ⟦जायते सुदिनत्ते आह्नाम⟧. At the set of eleven yūpas, and elsewhere too in a rite that has many yūpas, he should praise them with the six or the five Ṛks that begin ⟦यान्वो नर⟧: ⟦संस्तुयादनभिहिंकृत्य यान्वो नरो देवयंतो नि मिम्युरिति षड्भिः⟧ …, "He should praise them, without having uttered the sound hiṃ, with the six or the five beginning ⟦यान्वो नरो देवयंतो नि मिम्यु⟧" (⟦आ. ३-१⟧).

## Ṛk 1

⟦वनस्पते⟧, O yūpa, of the kind of the khadira, the palāśa and the like! ⟦अध्वरे⟧, in the sacrifice, ⟦देवयंतो⟧, desiring the gods, the adhvaryu and the others ⟦अंजंति⟧ ⟦त्वां⟧ ⟦दैव्येन⟧ ⟦मधुना⟧, with the madhu that belongs to the gods, with ghee — because it is handed down: ⟦एतद्वै मधु दैव्यं⟧ …, "This indeed is the divine madhu, namely the ghee" (⟦ऐ ब्रा. २२⟧) — with that ghee they anoint you. The meaning is that they pour ghee upon you. ⟦यत्⟧, if, you ⟦ऊर्ध्वः⟧ ⟦तिष्ठाः⟧, stand raised up; ⟦यद्वा⟧, or if, ⟦क्षयो⟧, your abode, is ⟦उपस्थ⟧, in the lap, ⟦मातुरस्या⟧, of this earth who is a mother: whether you stand upright or lie upon the ground, in both cases ⟦इह⟧, here, among us, ⟦धत्तात्⟧, place, ⟦द्रविणा⟧, riches.

This Ṛk is explained by the Brāhmaṇa: ⟦देवयंत इत्त्वाहाध्वरे हैनं देवयंतोs ज्ञंति वनस्पते मधुना दैव्यनेत्ये-तद्वै मधु दैवं यदाज्यं मदूर्ध्वस्था द्रविणमेवास्मासु धत्तादित्येव तदाह⟧, "He recites 'They anoint you in the sacrifice, desiring the gods'; for in the sacrifice those who desire the gods anoint it. 'O Vanaspati, with the divine madhu': this indeed is the divine madhu, namely the ghee. 'When you stand upright …'**NOTE**{the Brāhmaṇa quotation is shorter in the supplied text; after yad ājyaṃ the reference text has in addition the rest of the verse and the words yadi ca tiṣṭhāsi yadi ca śayāsai, "whether you stand or whether you lie"}: 'place wealth among us' — just that is what he says by it" (⟦ऐ ब्रा २२⟧). And so the Nirukta also says: ⟦अंजंति त्वामध्वरे देवान्कामयमाना⟧ ⟦वनस्पते मधुना दैव्येन च धृतेन च यदूर्ध्वः स्थास्यसि द्रविणानि च नो दास्यसि⟧ ⟦यद्वा ते कृतः क्षयो मातुरस्या उपस्थ उपस्थाने⟧, "They anoint you in the sacrifice, desiring the gods, O Vanaspati, with the divine madhu and with ghee; when you stand upright you will also give us riches, or when your abode has been made in the lap, the place near, of this mother" (⟦नि. ८.१८⟧).

⟦अंजंति⟧: the root is añj, "to make manifest, to be bright, to go and to anoint". Because it belongs to the class beginning with rudh, śnam comes. In the laṭ of it, because jhi is ṅit, the penultimate is elided by ⟦दनिदिता-मि⟧…; the a is elided by ⟦श्नसोरल्लोप⟧; by the accent of the suffix the udātta is in the middle. ⟦त्वां⟧: before am, the substitute tva for the word yuṣmad is by ⟦त्वमावेकवचन⟧. The ā is by ⟦द्वितीयायां च⟧. The single substitute is udātta by ⟦देश उदात्तीनोदात्त⟧. ⟦अध्वरे⟧: "that in which there is no dhvara, no harm": in this bahuvrīhi, although the first member would keep its original accent, the latter member has the udātta on its last syllable by the rule on nañ and su, ⟦नञ्चभ्याम⟧. ⟦देव संतः⟧: "desiring the gods for themselves". The suffix kyac is by ⟦सुप आत्मन⟧. The ī before kyac, ⟦कृचि चेतीत्वं⟧, and the lengthening by ⟦अकृत्सार्वधातुकयोरिति⟧ are forbidden by this rule: ⟦न छंदस्यपुत्रसे⟧. After the stem ending in it comes the suffix śatṛ. When śap has become anudātta because it is pit, and the other by the accent rule of a sārvadhātuka that replaces a tense-sign, then on the single substitute for those two, by ⟦उदात्तेनोदात्त⟧, the accent of kyac alone stands. ⟦वनस्पते⟧: the udātta on the first syllable is by ⟦अमंत्रितस्य च⟧.**NOTE**{after vanaspate the reference text has in addition an item on madhunā (the suffix u by the rule beginning phalipāṭinami, and the udātta on the first syllable); the supplied text has none} ⟦दैव्येन⟧: the suffix yañ is by ⟦देवाद्यइञाविति⟧. Because it is ñit there is vṛddhi. The elision is by ⟦यस्यॆति⟧. Because it is ñit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦शिष्टाः⟧: the root is sthā, "to cease from motion". In leṭ, sip. The i is elided by ⟦इतश्च लोप⟧. Then śap; because it is śit, the substitute tiṣṭha comes by the rule beginning ⟦साघ्रे⟧. The augment aṭ is by ⟦लेटोऽडाटाव⟧. Because it is connected with the word yad, the accent is not lost. Because the substitute tiṣṭha is laid down with the udātta on its first syllable, the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦द्रविणा⟧: the suffix inan is by ⟦नन्नितीनन्⟧. By ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ the case-ending is replaced by ḍā. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦इह⟧: after the word idam, in the sense of the locative, the suffix ha comes by ⟦इदमो ह⟧. ⟦इदम इशितीशादेशः⟧: the substitute iś comes for idam. Because it is śit it replaces the whole. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦धत्तात्⟧: of the root dhā, "to hold and to nourish", there is loṭ in the sense of prayer. Sip. Its substitute hi is by ⟦सेर्ह-पिच्चेति⟧. The substitute tātaṅ is by ⟦तुह्योः⟧ (⟦पा. ७-१-३५⟧). The ā is elided by ⟦श्नाभ्यस्तयोरात⟧. The change to bhaṣ is by ⟦दधस्तथोश्च⟧ (⟦पा. ८-२-३८⟧). There are the changes to jaś and to car. The accent is lost. ⟦वा⟧: it is anudātta because it belongs to the group beginning with ca. ⟦क्षयः⟧: "they dwell in it": the suffix gha in the sense of the location. The udātta on the first syllable is by ⟦क्षयो निवास⟧. ⟦मातुः⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦नप्तृनेष्ट⟧ it is laid down ready-made as ending in tṛc, and so the udātta is on the last syllable. By ⟦ऋतउदिति⟧ u takes the place of the preceding and the following vowels of ṅas. ⟦अस्याः⟧: the case-ending is udātta by the rule beginning ⟦ऊडिदम⟧. ⟦उपस्थे⟧: the root is sthā, "to cease from motion". The suffix ka is by ⟦आतश्चोपसर्ग⟧. ⟦तत्पुरुषे तुल्यार्थीति⟧; because it belongs to the group beginning with marudvṛdha, the first member has the udātta on its last syllable.

## Ṛk 2

O yūpa! ⟦श्रयमाण⟧, being there, ⟦पुरस्तात्⟧, in the eastern quarter, ⟦समिद्धस्य⟧, of the blazing fire called the āhavanīya; ⟦वन्वानः⟧, bestowing, ⟦ब्रह्म⟧, food — because ⟦ब्रह्म वर्च⟧ is read among the names of food — food that is ⟦अजरं⟧, free from decay, plentiful, and ⟦सुवीरं⟧, joined with fine offspring; ⟦बाधमानो⟧, driving away, ⟦आरे⟧, far off, ⟦अस्मदमति⟧, the hunger that is our enemy — because it is heard, ⟦आशनाया वै पाप्मामतिरिति⟧, "Hunger indeed is the evil, amati" — such an enemy; you ⟦उत्⟧ ⟦श्रयस्व⟧, be raised up, ⟦महते सौभगाय⟧, for the attainment of wealth and the like.

On the meaning stated there is the Brāhmaṇa: ⟦समिद्धस्य श्रयमाणः पुर-स्तादिति समिद्धस्य ह्येष एतत्पुरस्ताच्छयते ब्रह्म वन्वानो अजरं सुवीरमित्याशिष-मेवाशास्त्र आरे अस्मदमतिं बाधमान इत्यशनाया वै पाप्मामतिस्तामेव तदारान्नु-दते यज्ञाच्च यजमानाच्चोच्छयस्व महते सौभगायेत्याशिषमेवाशास्त्रे⟧, "'Resting to the east of the kindled one': for it here rests to the east of the kindled fire. 'Bestowing food that is undecaying and has fine offspring': he asks for a blessing by it. 'Driving amati far from us': hunger indeed is the evil, amati; that he thereby drives far away from the sacrifice and from the sacrificer. 'Rise up for great good fortune': he asks for a blessing by it" (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. २-२⟧).

⟦समिद्धस्य⟧: the root is indh, "to shine". In the niṣṭhā, in the sense of the object, the augment iṭ is forbidden by the rule beginning ⟦श्वेदितो⟧. "Well kindled": by ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦श्रयमाणः⟧: of the root śri, "to resort to", śānac takes the place of laṭ. When that has become anudātta by the accent rule of a sārvadhātuka that replaces a tense-sign, the accent of the root remains. ⟦पुरस्तात्⟧: for the word pūrva the suffix astāti comes by the rule beginning ⟦सप्तमीपंचमी⟧ (⟦पा. ५-३-२७⟧). By ⟦अस्ताति च⟧ (⟦पा. ५-३-४०⟧) pur takes the place of pūrva. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦ब्रह्म⟧: the udātta on the first syllable is by ⟦नब्विषयस्यानिसंतस्य⟧. ⟦वन्वानः⟧: the root is van, "to ask". Here this root is used in the sense of giving. The suffix śānac takes the place of laṭ. Because it is cit the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦अज-रं⟧: by ⟦नळो जरमरमित्रमृता⟧ the latter member has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦सुवीरं⟧: by the rule ⟦वीरवीयौ⟧ the latter member has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦आरे⟧: because it belongs to the group beginning with evam, the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦आस्मत्⟧: by ⟦युष्यसिभ्यां⟧ ⟦मदिगिति⟧ the accent is that of the suffix madik. By ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ the case-ending is elided. The accent is that of the suffix madik. ⟦आमतिं⟧: by the rule ⟦तत्पुरुष⟧ the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦उत्⟧: being a particle, it has the udātta on the first syllable. ⟦श्रयस्व⟧: of the root śri, "to resort to", in the second person singular of loṭ comes thās. The substitute is by ⟦थासः स⟧. By ⟦सवाभ्यां⟧ ⟦वामावि⟧ v takes the place of the e. The accent is lost. ⟦महते⟧: because it is laid down ready-made by ⟦वर्तमाने सृष्टद्वहन्महदिति⟧, the udātta is on the last syllable. The case-ending is udātta by ⟦बृहन्महतोरुपस्यांखा नमिति⟧. ⟦सौभगाय⟧: the suffix añ is by ⟦भृज्जातिवयोवचने⟧. The vṛddhi is by ⟦हृद्भगसिंध्यंते पूर्वपदस्य चेति⟧. Because all rules are optional in the Veda, the vṛddhi of the latter member does not take place. The accent is that of a ñit suffix.

## Ṛk 3

⟦वनस्पते⟧, O yūpa, ⟦पृथिव्या अधि⟧, on the earth, ⟦वर्ष्मन्⟧, in the excellent place called the devayajana, ⟦उच्छ्रयस्व⟧, be raised up. ⟦मीयमान⟧, being measured, being delimited as of such and such an extent, ⟦सुमिती⟧, with a beautiful thought or else with a fine measure, you ⟦वर्चो धाः⟧, bestow food, ⟦यज्ञवाहसे⟧, on the sacrificer who carries out the sacrifice.

This same meaning is shown by the Brāhmaṇa: ⟦उच्छ्रयस्व वनस्पत इत्युच्छ्रियमाणायाभिरूपा यद्यज्ञेऽभि-रूपं तत्समृद्धं । वर्ष्मन्पृथिव्या अधीत्येतद्वै वर्ष पृथिव्यै यत्र यूपमुन्नि-न्वंति सुमिती मीयमानो वर्चो धा यज्ञवाहस इत्याशिषमाशास्ते⟧, "'Rise up, O Vanaspati': this is appropriate to the one being raised; what is appropriate in the sacrifice is successful. 'On the varṣman of the earth': this indeed is the varṣman of the earth, where they set up the yūpa. 'Being measured with a fine measure, bestow varcas on the one who carries the sacrifice': he asks for a blessing" (⟦ऐ. प्रा. २-२⟧).

⟦वनस्पते⟧: because it does not stand at the beginning of a pāda, it has the anudātta that is taught in the eighth book. ⟦पृथिव्याः⟧: the case-ending is udātta by the rule beginning ⟦उदात्तयणो⟧. ⟦सुमिती⟧: of the root mā, "to measure", in ktin, the substitute i comes by ⟦द्यतिस्यतिमास्थे⟧. "With a beautiful measure." By ⟦तादौ च निति कृत्यताविति⟧ the preverb keeps its original accent. By ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ there is the long vowel for the two like vowels. ⟦मीयमानः⟧: of the root mā, "to measure", yak comes in the sense of the object. The ī is by ⟦घुमास्थे⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦धाः⟧: of the root dhā, in the sense of loṭ, luṅ comes by ⟦छंदसि लुज् लज्जति⟧. The i of sip is elided by ⟦इतश्चेति⟧. The sic is elided by ⟦गातिस्थेति⟧. By ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧ there is no augment aṭ. The accent is lost. ⟦यज्ञवाहसे⟧: "he carries, he attains, the sacrifice". Of the root vah, "to carry", the suffix asun comes by ⟦इच्छंदसी⟧. Because ṇit continues from the preceding rule, the penultimate takes vṛddhi. By ⟦गतिकारकयोरिति पूर्वपदप्रकृति-स्वरत्वं चेति⟧, "and after a preverb or a word in a case-relation the first member keeps its original accent", the first member keeps its original accent (⟦उ. ४-२२६⟧).

## Ṛk 4

⟦युवा⟧: firm in limb; the meaning is that it is marked by the characteristics of having eight corners and the rest. ⟦सुवासाः⟧: provided with a beautiful garment, the rope. ⟦परिवीत⟧: wound about with that rope. A yūpa of this kind ⟦आगात्⟧, comes. ⟦स उ⟧, that very yūpa, ⟦भवति⟧ ⟦श्रेयान्⟧ ⟦जायमानः⟧: it comes to be one that turns out superior to all the trees. ⟦तं⟧, that yūpa of this kind, the ⟦धीरासः⟧, the wise, ⟦मनसा⟧ ⟦देवयंतः⟧, desiring the gods with the mind, ⟦स्वाध्यः⟧, well and in every way engaged in meditation, the ⟦कवयः⟧, those of far-reaching sight, the adhvaryu and the others, ⟦उन्नयंति⟧; they make it raised.

On this meaning there is the Brāhmaṇa: ⟦युवा सुवासाः परिवीत आगादित्युत्तमया परिदधाति प्राणो वै युवा सुवासाः सोऽयं शरीरैः परिवृतः स उ श्रेयान्भवति जायमान इति।श्रेयाञ्छीयान्छेष एतद्भवति जायमानस्तं धीरासः कवय उन्नयंति स्वाध्यो मनसा देवयंत इति ये वा अनूचानास्ते कवयस्त एवै नं तदुन्नयंति⟧, "He concludes with the last, 'A youth, well clad, girt about, has come'. The breath indeed is the youth who is well clad; it is enclosed by the bodies. 'And he becomes better as he is born': for this one here becomes better and better as he is born. 'Him the wise kavis raise up, meditating well, desiring the gods with the mind': those who are learned are the kavis; it is they who thereby raise him up" (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. २-२⟧).

⟦युवा⟧: after the root yu the suffix kanin comes by the rule beginning ⟦कनिन्युवृषितक्षी⟧. Because it is kit there is no guṇa. There is the substitute uvaṅ. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦सुवासाः⟧: the root is vas, "to cover". With asun continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix asun comes by the rule that after vas it is ṇit: ⟦वसेर्णिदिश्यसुन्⟧. Because it is ṇit the penultimate takes vṛddhi. By ⟦सोर्मनसी⟧ the latter member has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦परिवीतः⟧: the root is vye, "to cover". The suffix kta is in the sense of the object. Because the root belongs to the group beginning with yaj, there is saṃprasāraṇa. By ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦अगात्⟧: in the luṅ of the root iṇ, "to go", the substitute gā comes by ⟦इणो गा लुङ्खोति⟧. The sic is elided by ⟦गातिस्थेति⟧. There is the augment aṭ. ⟦श्रेयान्⟧: for praśasya the suffix īyasun comes by ⟦द्विवचनविभज्योपपद⟧. Before it the substitute śra comes by ⟦प्रशस्यस्य श्र⟧. There is guṇa. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. The single substitute has the udātta on the first syllable. ⟦स्वाध्यः⟧: the root is dhyai, "to think". The two preverbs su and āṅ are placed before it. The suffix kvip is by ⟦अन्वेभ्योऽपि दृश्यंत⟧. Because of the mention of dṛśi, "is seen", there is saṃprasāraṇa. By ⟦संप्रसा रणाच्चेति⟧ the form of the preceding vowel alone remains. The lengthening is by ⟦हल⟧. Before jas the semivowel is substituted by ⟦एरनेकाच⟧. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. By ⟦उदात्तस्वरितयोर्यण⟧ the jas becomes svarita. ⟦देवयंतः⟧: the suffix kyac is by ⟦आत्मनः क्यजिति⟧. Because it is cit the udātta is on the last syllable.

## Ṛk 5

The yūpa, ⟦जातः⟧, first arisen on the earth, is ⟦समर्ये⟧ — marya is a name for men; accompanied by them — ⟦विदथे⟧ ⟦वर्धमानः⟧: vidatha is a name of the sacrifice, because ⟦विदथः सवनमिति⟧ is read among the names of the sacrifice; in that sacrifice it is ⟦आ⟧ ⟦वर्धमानो⟧, resplendent on all sides through the anointing with ghee, the girding with the rope and the rest; and being so it ⟦जायते⟧ ⟦सुदिनत्वे⟧ ⟦अह्नां⟧. The meaning is that, as the one that carries the sacrifice through by way of the tying of the animal to it and the rest, it makes the days good days. ⟦अपसः⟧, those who have rites to perform, ⟦धीराः⟧, of good understanding, the adhvaryu and the others, ⟦पुनंति⟧ that yūpa ⟦मनीषा⟧, with understanding; they cleanse it by washing and the like. And ⟦देवया⟧, the one who sacrifices to the gods, or the one who conveys the oblations to the gods, ⟦विप्रो⟧, the wise hotṛ, ⟦उत्⟧ ⟦इयर्ति⟧, sends forth, ⟦वाचं⟧, speech that concerns the yūpa and has the form of praise. The meaning is that he utters it.

On the meaning stated there is the Brāhmaṇa: ⟦जातो जायते सुदिनत्वे अह्नामिति जातो हैष एत-ज्जायते समर्य आ विदथे वर्धमान इति वर्धयंत्येवैनं तत्पुनंति धीरा अपसो मनीषेति पुनंत्येवैनं तद्देवया विप्र उदियर्ति वाचमिति देवेभ्य एवैनं तन्नि-वेदयति⟧, "'Born, he is born for the days to be good days': for born, this one is here born. 'Growing in the sacrifice that is attended by men': they make him grow by it. 'The wise who have rites to perform purify him with understanding': they purify him by it. 'The wise one who sacrifices to the gods sends forth speech': he announces him to the gods by it" (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. २-२⟧).

⟦सुदिनत्वे⟧: "the state of good days": the suffix tva is by ⟦तस्य भावस्वतलाविति⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦समर्यॆ⟧: after the root mṛ, "to give up life", there is guṇa because the word is laid down ready-made, by ⟦च्छंदसि निष्कर्ष्येति⟧, as ending in the suffix kyap. The udātta on the last syllable is by ⟦समासस्य⟧. ⟦पुनंति⟧: the root is pū, "to purify". Because it belongs to the class beginning with krī, śnā comes. Because it belongs to the group beginning with pū, there is the shortening. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦देवयाः⟧: the root is yā, "to cause to reach". The suffix vic is by ⟦आतो मनि-न्निति⟧. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦इयर्ति⟧: the root is ṛ, "to go". It belongs to the class beginning with hu. The i of the reduplicative syllable is by the rule on ṛ and pṛ: ⟦अभ्यासस्यार्तिपिपर्श्योश्चीतीत्वं⟧. The substitute iyaṅ is by ⟦अभ्यासस्यासवर्ण⟧. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 6

O yūpas! You ⟦यान्⟧ ⟦वः⟧, whom, the ⟦नरः⟧, the leaders of the rites, the adhvaryu and the others, ⟦देवयंतो⟧, desiring the gods, ⟦निमिम्युः⟧, threw into the pits; ⟦वा⟧, and also, ⟦वनस्पते⟧, whom, the yūpas that are yours, ⟦स्वधितिः⟧, the axe, ⟦ततक्ष⟧, cut: ⟦देवासो⟧, shining, ⟦स्वरवः⟧ — the word svaru denotes its own chip; they have them; the suffix of possession is elided — ⟦तस्थिवांस⟧, standing, let ⟦ते⟧, those yūpas that are such, ⟦दिधि-षंतु⟧, hold, ⟦अस्मे⟧, for this sacrificer, ⟦रत्न⟧, excellent wealth, ⟦प्रजावत्⟧, accompanied by offspring. The meaning is: let them bestow it.

⟦निमिम्युः⟧: of the root mi, "to throw", in liṭ with us, there is no guṇa because by ⟦असंयोगाल्लिट् किदिति⟧ the ending is treated as kit. Because it is connected with a form of yad, the accent is not lost. Because of the statement ⟦सति शिष्टः स्वरो बलीयानिति⟧, "the accent that is taught when another is already there is the stronger", the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦स्वधितिः⟧: after the root dhā, "to hold", with the word sva as the accompanying word, comes ktin. "By it its own sharp edge is placed, is thrown": hence svadhiti, a sword. ⟦ततक्ष⟧: after the roots takṣ and tvakṣ, "to make thin", it is the form in liṭ with ṇal; the accent is not lost. By ⟦लितीति⟧ the syllable before the suffix is udātta. ⟦स्वरवः⟧: after the root svṛ, "to sound and to pain", with nit continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix u comes by the rule beginning ⟦श्वस्वस्निहित्री⟧. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦तस्थिवांसः⟧: after the root sthā, "to cease from motion", there is liṭ in the Veda. Its substitute is kvasu. The augment iṭ is by ⟦वस्वेकाजाद्भसाम⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦प्रजावत्⟧: after the root jan, "to come forth", the suffix ḍa comes when there is a preverb, by the rule ending ⟦च संज्ञायामिति⟧, "and in a name". Then ṭāp. The suffix matup is by ⟦तदस्यास्तीति⟧. It is anudātta because it is pit. The accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦अस्मे⟧: for the case-ending of the word asmad the substitute śe comes by ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧. ⟦दिधिषंतु⟧: the root is dhiṣ, "to hold". It belongs to the class beginning with hu. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 7

The yūpas ⟦ये⟧, which, are ⟦वृक्षासः⟧, cut with the axe, ⟦अधि क्षमि⟧, on the earth; ⟦यत-स्रुच⟧ denotes the priests, because ⟦यतस्रुचो मरुत⟧ is read among the names of the priests; "those by whom the ladles are held are ⟦यतस्रुचः⟧"; by those priests they are ⟦निमितासो⟧, thrown well down into the pits; they are ⟦साधसः⟧ of the kṣetra, accomplishers of the kṣetra, the sacrifice. As a field gives its fruit by way of the crop and the like, so the sacrifice too gives its fruit, heaven and the like, by way of the unseen result: thus the kṣetra is the sacrifice. Let ⟦ते⟧, those yūpas that are of this kind, ⟦व्यंतु⟧, cause to go, ⟦देवत्रा⟧, among the gods, ⟦नो⟧, our, ⟦वार्यं⟧, oblation that is to be chosen, to be shared in. The meaning is: let them make our oblation subject to the gods.

⟦कृष्णासः⟧: of the root vraśc, "to cut", in the niṣṭhā, the augment iṭ is forbidden by ⟦यस्य विभाषे⟧. Because it is kit there is saṃprasāraṇa: ⟦कित्त्वाद्ग्रहिज्यावयित्यादिना संप्र-सारणं⟧. The n of the niṣṭhā is by ⟦ओदितश्चेति⟧. ⟦क्षमि⟧: the ā is elided by the separating off of ⟦आत⟧ in the rule ⟦आतो धातो⟧. ⟦निमितासः⟧: the root is mi, "to throw". The suffix kta is in the sense of the object. By ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦यतस्रुचः⟧: in the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦व्यंतु⟧: it is the form in loṭ of the root iṇ, "to go". The elision of the i is Vedic. It here includes the sense of the causative. ⟦वार्यं⟧: after the root vṛ, "to share in", the suffix ṇyat comes in the sense of the object by ⟦हलोर्ण्यदिति⟧. The udātta on the first syllable is by the rule beginning ⟦ईडवंदवृशंसदुहे⟧. ⟦देवत्रा⟧: with trā continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix trā comes in the sense of the location by the rule beginning ⟦देवमनुष्यपुरुषपुरुमर्त्येभ्य⟧. ⟦क्षेत्रसाधसः⟧: after the roots rādh and sādh, "to succeed", comes the uṇādi suffix asun. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent.

## Ṛk 8

⟦सुनीथा⟧, good leaders of the sacrifice, are ⟦आदित्या⟧, the twelve Ādityas, ⟦रुद्रा⟧, the eleven Rudras, and ⟦वसवो⟧, the eight Vasus. Or else ⟦सुनीथा⟧ means having good nīthas, having good praises; for it is heard: ⟦स्तुतशस्त्राणि नीथानि चोत्करा-मदानि च⟧, "the stutas and the śastras, the nīthas and the ukthāmadas" (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. २-३८⟧). ⟦द्यावाक्षामा⟧: heaven and earth. ⟦पृथिवी⟧ — there is a transposition of the case-ending — and the broad, the wide, ⟦अंतरिक्षं⟧. ⟦सजोषस⟧: let all these ⟦देवा⟧, having become united with one another in one mind, ⟦अवंतु⟧, protect, this ⟦यज्ञ⟧ of ours. Moreover: let them ⟦कृण्वंतु⟧ ⟦ऊर्ध्वं⟧, make raised up, this yūpa, which is the ⟦केतुं⟧, the banner, or the one that makes known, ⟦अध्वरस्य⟧, of the sacrifice.

⟦आदित्याः⟧: the offspring of Aditi. The suffix ṇya is by ⟦दित्यदित्यादित्यपत्युत्तरपदादिति⟧. Because it is ṇit the first vowel takes vṛddhi. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦रुद्राः⟧: "they make all weep at the time of the end". The suffix rak is by ⟦रोदेर्णिलुक् चेति⟧. Together with it the ṇi is elided. The accent is that of the suffix. Since jas is anudātta because it is a case-ending, the single substitute too is udātta by ⟦देश उदात्तेनोदात्त⟧. ⟦वसवः⟧: the root is vas, "to dwell". With nit continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix u comes by the rule beginning ⟦श्यस्वस्तिहित्रप्यसिवसी⟧. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦सुनीथाः⟧: the root is nī, "to lead to". The suffix kthan is by ⟦हनिकुषिनीरमिकाशिभ्य⟧. Because it is kit there is no guṇa. By ⟦थाथघइत्ते⟧ the latter member has the udātta on its last syllable. ⟦द्यावाक्षामा⟧: "heaven and earth". The substitute dyāvā is by ⟦दिवो द्यावेति⟧. By ⟦द्वंद्वे च⟧, the rule on a dvandva of deities, both members keep their original accent. ⟦पृथिवी⟧: the root is prath, "to be made known". By ⟦प्रथेः शिवन्⟧ … there are the suffix ṣivan and saṃprasāraṇa. Ṅīṣ comes by ⟦षिद्धौरादिभ्यश्चेति⟧. By the accent of the suffix the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦अवंतु⟧: the root is av, "to protect". The accent is lost. ⟦कृण्वंतु⟧: after the root kṛv, "to harm and to do", in loṭ, the suffix u comes by ⟦धिन्विकृष्णोरच्छे⟧, and a as the substitute for the final. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 9

⟦वसाना⟧, wearing, ⟦शुक्रा⟧, bright garments, ⟦स्वरवः⟧, having svarus, ⟦यतानाः⟧, moving forward, ⟦श्रेणिशः⟧, in rows. For this there is an illustration: ⟦हंसा इवेति⟧. As haṃsas flying in rows in the sky look beautiful, so, looking beautiful, those yūpas ⟦नः⟧ ⟦आ⟧ ⟦अगुः⟧, have reached us. Moreover: ⟦उन्नीयमाना⟧, being raised up, ⟦कविभि⟧, by the wise, the adhvaryu and the others, ⟦पुरस्तात्⟧, in the eastern part of the āhavanīya, those yūpas, ⟦देवा⟧, shining, ⟦अपि⟧ ⟦यंति⟧ ⟦देवानां⟧ ⟦पाथो⟧, the place of the gods, the atmosphere. ⟦पाथोऽन्तरीक्षं पथा व्याख्यातमिति⟧, "pāthas is the atmosphere; it is explained by path", says Yāska. On the sūtra ⟦पाथोनदीभ्यां ड्यण्⟧ (⟦पा. ४-४-१११⟧) the author of the Vṛtti too has said ⟦पाथोऽन्तरीक्षमिति⟧, "pāthas is the atmosphere". Such an atmosphere they reach. The meaning is: the yūpas raised up by the adhvaryu and the others, because of their great length, stand pervading the atmosphere.

⟦हंसा इव⟧: because of the statement ⟦इवेन विभक्त्य-लोप⟧, "with iva there is no elision of the case-ending", the accent is that of the first member. ⟦श्रेणितः⟧: the suffix śas is by ⟦बह्वल्पार्थादिति⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦श्रेणिशः⟧: the suffix śas is by ⟦बहुल्पार्थादिति⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦शुक्रा⟧: after the root śuc, "to shine", because the word is laid down ready-made as ending in the suffix ran by ⟦जेंद्रा ग्रेत्यादिना⟧, the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦अगुः⟧: the root is iṇ, "to go". It is the form of it in luṅ. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 10

⟦स्वरवः⟧, having svarus, and ⟦चषालऽवंतः⟧, with the thorn fastened upon them: yūpas of this kind, ⟦पृथिव्यां⟧, on the earth, ⟦सं⟧ ⟦ददृश्रे⟧, are indeed well seen, ⟦शृंगाणिऽइव⟧ ⟦इत्⟧, just like the horns, ⟦शृंगिणां⟧, of the buffalo and the like. ⟦वा⟧, and also, ⟦विऽहवे⟧ — "in it the oblations are offered in various ways", hence vihava, the sacrifice — in that sacrifice, ⟦श्रोषमाणाः⟧, hearing the sound in the form of praise that is made ⟦वाघत्ऽभिः⟧, by the priests — because ⟦वाघतो व्यक्तबर्हिष⟧ is read among the names of the priests — let such yūpas ⟦आवंतु⟧, protect, ⟦अस्मान्⟧, us the sacrificers, ⟦पृतनाज्येषु⟧ — the word pṛtanājya denotes battle, because ⟦आजौ पृतनाज्यमिति⟧ is read among the names of battle — in battles, that is to say, in places of slaughter.

⟦शृंगाणि⟧: after the root śṝ, "to harm", by ⟦च्छृणातेर्ह्रस्वश्च⟧ (⟦उ. १-१२५⟧) there are the suffix gan, the augment nuṭ and the shortening of the root. The accent is that of a nit suffix. ⟦ददृश्रे⟧: by ⟦छंदसि लुङ्लज्जिट⟧ the liṭ stands in the sense of laṅ. By ⟦इरयो र⟧ the substitute re comes for irec. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 11

⟦वनस्पते⟧, O stump whose root has been cut, being ⟦शतवल्यः⟧, of a hundred branches, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦रोह⟧; come forth in a special way. May ⟦वयं⟧, we too, being ⟦सहस्रवल्याः⟧, of a thousand branches through the succession of progeny, sons, grandsons and the rest, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦रुहेम⟧; may we come forth in a special way. ⟦यं त्वां⟧, you whom, O stump, ⟦स्वधितिः⟧, the axe, ⟦आयं⟧, this one, ⟦तेजमानो⟧, of sharpened edge, ⟦प्रणिनाय⟧, has made attain in full, ⟦महते सौभगाय⟧, great good fortune that consists in having many branches.

⟦सहस्रवल्याः⟧: the word sahasra has the udātta in the middle because it belongs to the group beginning with kardama. In the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦रुहेम⟧: after the root ruh, "to come forth", in the benedictive liṅ, the augment yāsuṭ comes by ⟦किदाशिषीति⟧. The s is elided by ⟦स्कोः संयोगाद्योरंते चेति⟧. The suffix aṅ is by ⟦लिज्याशिष्यङ्⟧. The substitute iy is by ⟦अतो येय⟧. The y is elided by ⟦लोपो व्योर्वलीति⟧. There is guṇa. ⟦प्रणिनाय⟧: after the root nī, "to lead to", it is the form in liṭ with ṇal. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda the accent is not lost. By ⟦लितीति⟧ the syllable before the suffix is udātta.
