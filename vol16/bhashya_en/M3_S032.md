## Introduction

The sūkta beginning ⟦इंद्र सोमं सोमपत⟧ has seventeen Ṛks; it is the third sūkta, Vaiśvāmitra, in the Triṣṭubh metre, addressed to Indra. And it has been listed thus: ⟦इंद्र सोमं त्र्यूनेति⟧.

On the second day of the Pṛṣṭhya and Abhiplava ṣaḍahas, at the Marutvatīya, this sūkta, ⟦इंद्र सोमं⟧, is the ⟦निविद्धानीयं⟧, the sūkta into which the nivid is set. It has been laid down in the sūtra, in the section beginning ⟦द्वितीयस्येति⟧: ⟦इंद्र सोमं या त ऊतिरवमेति मध्यंदिनः⟧ (⟦आ. ७-६⟧). **NOTE**{the reference text gives the number of this sūtra reference differently (7.6.4 against the supplied seven-six)}

At the Gosava and the Vivadha too, this sūkta is used at the Marutvatīya śastra. And it has been laid down in the sūtra: ⟦इंद्र सोममेतायामेति मध्यंदिनः⟧ (⟦आ. ९-८⟧). At the Udbhid and the Valabhid too, this sūkta is used at the Marutvatīya. And it has been laid down in the sūtra: ⟦इंद्र सोममिंद्रः पूर्विदिति मध्यंदिनः⟧ (⟦आ, ९-८⟧).

## Ṛk 1

Viśvāmitra says. ⟦सोमपते⟧, O lord of Soma, O Indra, drink ⟦इमं⟧, this, ⟦सोमं⟧, Soma-juice, that is being prepared by us at the midday pressing; ⟦यत्⟧, since, the ⟦माध्यंदिनं⟧, belonging to the midday, ⟦सवनं⟧, the śastra called niṣkevalya, that is being performed ⟦ते⟧, for you, is ⟦चारु⟧, delightful. ⟦मघवन्⟧, O possessor of wealth, ⟦ऋजीषिन्⟧: by the word ṛjīṣa the Soma-substance whose essence is gone is meant; O Indra, possessor of that: having released from the chariot ⟦हरी⟧, the two horses yoked to the chariot, and ⟦प्रपुथ्य⟧, having filled, ⟦शिप्रे⟧, the two jaws, with the mouthful that is here, ⟦इह⟧, here in the place of the sacrifice to the gods, ⟦मादयस्व⟧: gladden those two horses.

⟦प्रपुथ्य⟧ is the form, with lyap, of the root ⟦पोढृ⟧ in the sense of sufficiency. The shortening is Vedic. The accent is the lit-accent. ⟦ऋजीषिन्⟧: from the root ⟦अर्ज षर्ज⟧ in the sense of acquiring, by the rule ⟦वर्जेश्व ऋजि च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-२८⟧) the suffix īṣan is added and ṛji is the substitute for the root. That which is acquired (⟦अर्ज्यत⟧) is ṛjīṣa; the suffix ini is added in the sense "this has that". Because it is a vocative, there is nighāta. ⟦मादयस्व⟧: the root ⟦मदि⟧ in the senses of praising and so on; the causative ṇic. Nighāta.

## Ṛk 2

O Indra, drink this Soma which is ⟦गवाशिरं⟧: that in which there is the āśīr, the mixing, of the milk of the cow. And moreover it is ⟦मंथिनं⟧: joined with mantha; ⟦शुक्रं⟧: new; or else it is present in the Śukra-and-Manthin cup. Thus, being present in the three pressings, drink this Soma. For your ⟦मदाय⟧, that is, for your delight, we give (⟦ररिम⟧) that Soma. ⟦मारुतेन⟧: the troop of the Maruts is Māruta; and that is ⟦ब्रह्मकृत्⟧. ⟦ब्रह्म⟧ is praise; one who makes it is brahmakṛt. You, ⟦सजोषाः⟧, united, with the Māruta troop which is making praise of Indra, and with the Rudras too, the Rudras eleven in number, ⟦तृपत्⟧, being satisfied by the drinking of the Soma, ⟦आ वृषस्व⟧: pour this Soma all around into the belly; hold it.

⟦गवाशिरं⟧: from the root ⟦श्रीणाते⟧ preceded by ā, with kvip, the substitution āśir takes place by nipātana in the rule beginning apaspṛdhethām ānṛcuḥ. In the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the nature of the former member. ⟦पिबा⟧: the lengthening in the saṃhitā by the rule ⟦द्व्यचोऽतस्तिजि⟧. ⟦ररिम⟧: this is the form in liṭ of the root rā, used, as a Vedic usage, in the present. Because it is not in the same sentence, there is no nighāta. Since the augments are anudātta, the augment iṭ being anudātta, the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦तृपत्⟧: the root ⟦तृप⟧ in the sense of pleasing; it belongs to the tudādi class. Because the teaching of the augments is not constant, there is no num. ⟦वृषस्व⟧: the root ⟦वृषु⟧ in the sense of sprinkling; śa by vyatyaya.

## Ṛk 3

O Indra, those Maruts who increased your ⟦शुष्मं⟧, the radiance that dries up enemies, and those Maruts who increased your ⟦तविषीं⟧, strength, by being helpers in battle, those Maruts, ⟦अर्चंतः⟧, praising you, increased your ⟦ओजः⟧, your capacity in battle. ⟦वज्रहस्त⟧: he whose hand is joined with the thunderbolt, such; ⟦सुशिप्र⟧: of beautiful jaws. ⟦शिप्रे हनू नासिके⟧: the two śipras are the two jaws, or the two nostrils, says Yāska. O Indra, ⟦रुद्रेभिः⟧: they make the enemies weep, therefore the Maruts are Rudras. You, together with your troop, with them, drink Soma at the midday pressing.

⟦अवर्धन्⟧: the root ⟦वृधु⟧ in the sense of growing, with the causative sense included. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦माध्यंदिने⟧: the suffix añ because the word belongs to the utsādi group; because of the marker ñ, the first syllable is udātta.

## Ṛk 4

Those Maruts who were the strength of Indra, those very Maruts (⟦त इत्⟧) ⟦विविप्रे नु⟧: quickly urged this Indra (⟦अस्य⟧ is the genitive in the sense of the accusative), in such a way that the speech should be ⟦मधुमत्⟧, endowed with sweetness: "We are your helpers; go to the battle." The Maruts, the helpers in battle, by whom (⟦येभिः⟧) Indra, impelled, ⟦विवेद⟧, knew the ⟦मर्म⟧ of Vṛtra the Asura, who was ⟦अमर्मणः⟧, one whose vital spot was not known by others, and therefore ⟦मन्यमानस्य⟧, one who thought "No one is my slayer": that spot, that vital spot, on being struck at which he will die.

⟦विविप्रे⟧: in the liṭ of the root ⟦विप⟧, in the sense of throwing, there is ātmanepada by vyatyaya. For irec, by the rule ⟦इरयो र⟧, the substitute is re. Nighāta. ⟦आसन्⟧: the form in laṅ of the root ⟦अस⟧, in the sense of being. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦विवेद⟧: the form in liṭ of the root ⟦विद⟧, in the sense of knowing. The lit-accent. ⟦मर्म⟧: from the root ⟦मृज्⟧, in the sense of giving up life, the Uṇādi suffix manin. The nit-accent.

## Ṛk 5

O Indra, ⟦मनुष्वत्⟧, as Manu's sacrifice, so ⟦जुषाणः⟧, attending, this ⟦सवनं⟧, sacrifice, of mine, drink this Soma for ⟦शश्वते⟧, eternal, ⟦वीर्याय⟧, capacity, whose mark is the overcoming of others. O ⟦हर्यश्व⟧ **NOTE**{the reference text has a gloss of this word (endowed with tawny horses) that the supplied text lacks}, you, ⟦सः⟧, being such, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦ववृत्स्व⟧, come, together with ⟦यज्ञैः⟧, the Maruts, who are to be worshipped. And moreover, accompanied ⟦सरण्युभिः⟧, by those who wish for themselves mantras that have a single tone, or else ⟦सरण्युभिः⟧, by the Maruts, whose nature it is to move, ⟦सिसर्षि⟧, make ⟦अपः⟧, the waters that stand in the atmosphere, reach ⟦अर्णा⟧, the earthly waters. Or else ⟦अपः⟧: this word ends in the ablative; from ⟦अपः⟧, from the pervaded atmosphere, make the waters arrive.

⟦ववृत्स्व⟧: the root ⟦वृतु⟧ in the sense of turning; by vyatyaya ślu comes in place of śap; the form is in loṭ. ⟦सरण्युभिः⟧: ⟦रण⟧ is in the sense of sound; kvip. Those mantras of which the raṇa, the sound, is the same are saraṇa; those wishing for them: kyac after a noun; by the rule that teaches it after kya, the suffix u. Or else from the root ⟦सृ⟧, in the sense of going, by the rule ⟦सृयुवचिभ्योऽन्युजागूजक्नुचः⟧ (⟦उ. ३-८१⟧) the suffix anyuc; by ⟦रषाभ्यां⟧ (⟦पा. ८-४-१⟧) the change to ṇ; because of the marker c, the final syllable is udātta. ⟦अपः⟧: the root ⟦आप्ल⟧ in the sense of pervading; by the rule ⟦आप्नोतेः क्विप् ह्रस्वश्च⟧ there are kvip and the shortening of the root; by ⟦सावेकाच⟧ the case-ending is udātta. ⟦सिसर्षि⟧: the root ⟦सृ⟧ in the sense of going; as a Vedic usage it is of the juhotyādi class; by the rule "variously in the Veda" the reduplicative syllable has i.

## Ṛk 6

O Indra, ⟦यत्⟧, when, you ⟦जघन्वान्⟧, have slain, ⟦चरता⟧, with one directed at him, ⟦वधेन⟧, with battle, ⟦वृत्रं⟧, the cloud, who was ⟦परि⟧ ⟦वन्विवांसं⟧, staying having enclosed on all sides, ⟦अपः⟧, the waters, ⟦देवीः⟧, which are disposed to play, and who was for that very reason (⟦अत एनादेवं⟧) not shining, being unclear through the abundance of his water, and ⟦शयानं⟧, staying motionless in one place: then, as ⟦आजौ⟧, in battle, you urge on ⟦अत्यान्⟧, horses, ⟦सर्तवै⟧, to go with very great speed, so you ⟦प्रासृजो ह⟧, let loose the waters from the atmosphere, from the sky; having broken open the cloud, you let them loose in full measure, indeed. The connection is with the following Ṛk: "Him we worship."

⟦जघन्वान्⟧: of the root ⟦हन⟧, in the senses of injuring and going, with kvasu; since by the rule vibhāṣā gamahanavidaviśām the iṭ is optional, there is here no iṭ. By the rule abhyāsāc ca the h becomes gh. Suffix-accent. ⟦असृजः⟧: the root ⟦सृज⟧ in the sense of letting go; it has the vikaraṇa śa. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦सर्तवै⟧: the root ⟦सृ⟧ in the sense of going; the suffix tavai in the sense of tumun; by the rule antaś ca tavai yugapat both the root and the suffix are udātta. ⟦आजौ⟧: the root ⟦अज⟧ in the senses of going and throwing; by ⟦अज्यतिभ्यां च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-१३०⟧) the suffix iṇ; because of the marker ṇ, vṛddhi of the penultimate. They go in it (⟦अजंति⟧), thus āji is battle. Suffix-accent. In sartavājau, in the saṃhitā, when the substitution āy has been made, its y is dropped by the rule ⟦लोपः शाकल्यस्य⟧. Because all rules are optional in the Veda, when the savarṇa-dīrgha is to be done the dropping of y does not count as not having taken effect; so there is the savarṇa-dīrgha. ⟦शयानं⟧: the root ⟦शीज्⟧ in the sense of sleeping; śānac; because of the adādi class, śap is dropped (luk); because of the marker ṅ, śānac being anudātta by the accent of the lasārvadhātuka, the accent is that of the root. ⟦वधेन⟧: from ⟦हंते⟧ in the sense of the action, by ⟦हनश्च वध⟧ the suffix ap, and vadha as the substitute for the root, and udātta on the last syllable. ⟦वन्विवांसं⟧: of the root ⟦वृञ्⟧, in the sense of covering, with kvasu; the augment iṭ is Vedic. Suffix-accent.

## Ṛk 7

Him who is ⟦वृद्धं⟧, grown, ⟦नमसा⟧, by food in the form of the oblation, and for that very reason ⟦बृहंतं⟧, great; who is reached (⟦ऋष्यते⟧) by mantras in the form of praise, hence ⟦ऋष्वं⟧, to be praised; ⟦अजरं⟧, free from old age, and for that very reason ⟦युवानं⟧, ever young: such an ⟦इंद्रं⟧, Indra, ⟦यजाम इत्⟧, we worship. We Kuśikas honour him with words of praise for the accomplishment of the sacrifice. ⟦प्रिये⟧, most dear, immeasurable, ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth, ⟦न ममतुः⟧, are not able to measure, ⟦महिमानं⟧, the majesty, of you, the Indra ⟦यस्य⟧, who is ⟦यज्ञियस्य⟧, worthy of sacrifice. ⟦न ममाते⟧: the second verb is for the sake of respect.

⟦यजामः⟧: the form of the root ⟦यजे⟧ in laṭ. ⟦ऋष्वं⟧: the root ⟦ऋषी⟧ in the sense of going; it is formed by nipātana, ending in the suffix va, by the rule beginning ⟦सर्वनिघृष्टऋष्ट⟧. Suffix-accent. ⟦प्रिये⟧: by the rule īdūdet it has the name pragṛhya. ⟦ममतुः⟧: the form in liṭ, with atus, of the root ⟦मा⟧ in the sense of measuring. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. Suffix-accent. ⟦महिमानं⟧: from the word ⟦महि⟧ in the sense "its state", imanic, because it belongs to the pṛthvādi group. Cit-accent. ⟦ममाते⟧: the form in liṭ, with ātām, of the root ⟦माज्⟧ in the senses of measuring and of sounding.

## Ṛk 8

O Indra, all the gods (⟦विश्वे⟧ is glossed as all) do not ⟦मिनंति⟧ your works (⟦कर्म⟧ is glossed as works), ⟦सुकृता⟧, well made, the earth and the rest, and the many ⟦व्रतानि⟧, the sacrifices and the rest. They do not injure any of these works of yours; rather they honour them. And moreover, this Indra ⟦दाधार⟧ upheld the earth, the world of the earth, the sky, the world of the sky, and (⟦उत⟧ is glossed as moreover) this atmosphere, the world of the atmosphere (⟦इमां⟧), these three worlds. ⟦सुदंसाः⟧: of good deeds, of beautiful deeds; Indra ⟦जजान⟧, produced the sun, the Āditya, the illuminator of the worlds, and the dawn, the time of the dawn, which carries through the agnihotra and the other rites.

This group of words ⟦इंद्रस्य कर्म सुकृता पुरूणि⟧ is read in the group of verses beginning eko dve vasumatī (⟦ऋग्वे ३-२-३⟧). **NOTE**{the reference text gives this reference as 3.30.13, which differs from the supplied numbers} Here too the same is read. If it be said, "Why is it not dropped in the Pada-text?", no, because of the difference of sentence. There, ⟦इंद्रस्य कर्म सुकृता पुरूणि⟧ is one sentence; but here ⟦कर्म सुकृता⟧ is one sentence and ⟦पुरूणि व्रतानि⟧ another. Moreover, the group of words there is in the nominative, here in the accusative. Therefore, because of the difference of sentence and the difference of case, it is not dropped in the Pada-text.

⟦मिनंति⟧: the form in laṭ of the root ⟦मीञ⟧ in the sense of injuring. Nighāta. ⟦दाधार⟧: the form in liṭ of the root ⟦धृङ्⟧ in the sense of holding. Because it belongs to the tujādi group, the reduplicative syllable is lengthened. The lit-accent. ⟦जजान⟧: the form in liṭ of the root ⟦जन⟧ in the sense of birth, with the causative sense included. The lit-accent. ⟦सुदंसाः⟧: by the rule ⟦सोर्मनसी⟧ the latter member is udātta on its first syllable.

## Ṛk 9

⟦अद्रोघ⟧: free from malice, O Indra, that greatness (⟦महित्वं⟧) of yours, such greatness, is ⟦सत्यं⟧, real. Since you, born, ⟦सद्यः⟧, at that very time, drank the Soma being given by us, the Soma-juice (⟦ह⟧ is in the sense of being well known). And moreover, the capacity, the ⟦ओजः⟧ of you, ⟦तवसः⟧, the powerful, the ⟦द्यावः⟧, all the worlds represented by heaven, do not restrain (⟦न वरंत⟧). And moreover ⟦अहा⟧, the days beginning with pratipad, do not restrain; and likewise ⟦मासाः⟧, the months beginning with Caitra, and ⟦शरदः⟧, the years, do not restrain your capacity. But all exist following your capacity: this is the meaning.

⟦अद्रोघ⟧: by ⟦आमंत्रितस्य च⟧, the rule taught in the sixth book, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦अपिबः⟧: because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦मासाः⟧: the root ⟦मसी⟧ in the sense of measuring; it is measured (⟦मस्यते⟧) by thirty days, thus māsa; ghañ in the sense of the object. Because of the marker ñ, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦शरदः⟧: from the root śṝ, in the sense of injuring, by the rule ⟦शृद्यभसोऽदि⟧ the suffix adi in the locative sense. In it the splendours of grasses, shrubs and trees waste away (⟦शीर्यते⟧), thus ⟦शरत्⟧. Suffix-accent. ⟦वरंत⟧: the root ⟦वृङ्⟧ in the sense of covering; with the causative sense included it is used here in the sense of warding off; it is of the bhvādi class; the form in laṅ, as a Vedic usage. Nighāta.

## Ṛk 10

In the one-day rite named Sadyaskrī, **NOTE**{the reference text does not have this passage on the ritual use} at the niṣkevalya śastra, the Ṛk ⟦त्वं सद्यो अपिब⟧ is the ⟦सूक्तमुखीया⟧, the opening Ṛk of the sūkta. And it has been laid down in the sūtra: ⟦त्वं सद्यो अपिबो जात इंद्रानु त्वाहिष्टे अध देव देवाः⟧ (⟦आ. ९-५⟧).

O Indra, born ⟦सद्यः⟧, at that very time, standing in the ⟦परमे व्योमन्⟧, in the highest place, you drank the Soma for ⟦मदाय⟧, for delight. When (⟦यत्⟧ is glossed as when) you have entered ⟦द्यावापृथिवी⟧, heaven and earth, on all sides, intensely, ⟦ह⟧, indeed. Then, afterwards, being ancient, you became the maker of works (⟦कारुधायाः⟧), indeed.

⟦व्योमन्⟧: the root ⟦अव⟧ in the senses of protecting, going and so on; from this root preceded by vi, by the rule beginning ⟦नामन् सीमन्⟧, the suffix manin; by the rule beginning jvaratvara, ūṭ. It is that of which the oma, the pervasion, is of a special kind. By the rule beginning supāṃ suluk the case-ending is dropped. In the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the nature of the former member. ⟦अविवेशीः⟧: of the root ⟦विश⟧, in the sense of entering, in yaṅluk, in laṅ, before sip, by the rule yaṅo vā the augment iṭ. By the rule itaś ca the i of sip is dropped. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦कारुधायाः⟧: ⟦कारुः⟧: from ⟦करोतेः⟧, the suffix uṇ, by ⟦कृवापाजिमिस्वदिसाध्यतूभ्य उण्⟧; because of the marker ṇ, vṛddhi. From the root dhā, by the rule vahihādhāñbhyaś chandasi, the suffix asun. Since ṇit continues, by the rule āto yuk ciṇkṛtoḥ the augment yuk. By the sūtra ⟦समासे गतिकारकयोरपि पूर्वपदपृकृतिस्वरत्वं च⟧ the accent is that of the nature of the former member.

## Ṛk 11

⟦तुविजात⟧: he from whom many things have been born, the earth and the rest, this is Tuvijāta. O Tuvijāta Indra, you, ⟦तव्यान्⟧, exceedingly grown, ⟦अहन्⟧, have slain, in order to send forth the water, ⟦अहिं⟧, the cloud: it strikes on all sides (⟦आ⟧), it takes away the water, thus ahi is the cloud; this cloud, which was ⟦परिशयानं⟧, lying around, ⟦अर्णः⟧, the water, that is, staying all around with the water kept inside, and for that very reason ⟦ओजायमानं⟧, exerting strength. ⟦अध⟧, then, ⟦द्यौः⟧, heaven, ⟦न⟧ ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦भूत्⟧, does not experience, does not know, ⟦ते⟧ ⟦महित्वं⟧, your greatness; ⟦यत्⟧, since, ⟦अवस्थाः⟧, you stand covering, ⟦क्षां⟧, the earth, with ⟦अन्यया⟧, one of the two, ⟦स्फिग्या⟧, hips.

⟦अहन्⟧: the form of ⟦हंते⟧ in laṅ, with the dropping of sip. ⟦अहिं⟧: the root ⟦हन⟧ in the senses of injuring and going; by the rule āṅi śrihanibhyāṃ hrasvaś ca the suffix iṇ, and it is ḍit, and the shortening of the former member. Because it belongs to the vṛṣādi group, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦ओजायमानं⟧: from the word ⟦ओजः⟧, in the sense of conduct, by the rule kartuḥ kyaṅ salopaś ca the suffix kyaṅ and the dropping of s; because of the marker ṅ, ātmanepada. Suffix-accent. ⟦तव्यान्⟧: ⟦तु⟧ is a root given in a sūtra; from this, ending in tṛc, by the rule tuś chandasi, īyasun; by the rule turiṣṭhemeyaḥsu the tṛc is dropped; when it has the name bha, guṇa of u; the dropping of i is Vedic. Nit-accent. ⟦भूत्⟧: the form of ⟦भवते⟧ in luṅ. ⟦स्फिग्या⟧: the name bha given by the rule yaci bham being set aside, when it has the name pada by ⟦स्वादिष्टसर्वनामस्थान⟧ there is the change to the voiced unaspirate. For the instrumental ending there is the vowel homogeneous with the preceding one. ⟦क्षां⟧: the root ⟦क्षि⟧ in the senses of dwelling and going; by the rule ⟦अन्वेभ्योऽपि दृश्यत⟧ the suffix ḍa, even after a root without a preceding word; then ṭāp. Creatures dwell (⟦क्षियंति⟧) in it, thus ⟦क्षा⟧ is the earth. By the accent that follows on the removal of an udātta, the last syllable is udātta. ⟦अवस्थाः⟧: the root ⟦वस⟧ in the sense of covering; in laṅ, ātmanepada because the root is marked anudātta; before thās, because of the adādi class, śap is dropped. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. The accent is that of the augment.

## Ṛk 12

O Indra, our sacrifice is your ⟦वर्धनः⟧, that is, the one who makes you grow by the giving of oblation; it is so indeed (⟦भूद्धि⟧). And moreover ⟦सुतसोमः⟧: one possessing pressed Soma; the ⟦मियेधः⟧, the sacrifice, is dear to you. **NOTE**{the reference text has a gloss on this word that the supplied text lacks} You, being ⟦यज्जियो⟧, worthy of sacrifice, protect (⟦अव⟧) the sacrificer, the doer of the sacrifice (⟦यज्ञं⟧ is glossed as the doer of the sacrifice), by this sacrifice that is being performed, by the giving of the fruit. And moreover this sacrifice, in the work of slaying Vṛtra, in the ⟦अहिहत्ये⟧, let it protect (⟦आवत्⟧) your thunderbolt.

⟦वर्धनः⟧: of the root ⟦वृधु⟧, in the sense of growing, in the causative, the suffix lyu, because it belongs to the nandyādi group. The lit-accent. ⟦भूत्⟧: because of the connection with ⟦हि⟧, there is no nighāta. ⟦अव⟧: the form of ⟦अवते⟧ in loṭ. Nighāta. ⟦सन्⟧: the form of the root as with śatṛ. Suffix-accent. ⟦अहिहत्ये⟧: of the root ⟦हन⟧, in the senses of injuring and going, in the sense of the action, by ⟦हनस्त च⟧ kyap, and t as the substitute for the final. The accent is that of the latter member, a kṛt. ⟦आवत्⟧: the form of ⟦अवते⟧ in laṅ, as a Vedic usage. Nighāta.

## Ṛk 13

O Indra, every sacrificer, ⟦अवसा⟧, by the sacrifice that protects himself, ⟦अर्वाक्⟧ ⟦आ⟧ ⟦चक्रे⟧, makes you, ⟦इंद्रं⟧, Indra, face him, by the giving of oblation. Likewise I, for ⟦नव्यसे⟧, newer ⟦सुम्नाय⟧, wealth, may turn you here (⟦ववृत्यां⟧): may I make you, turned by the sacrifice, face me. What sort are you? You who grew with ⟦पूर्वेभिः⟧, ancient ⟦स्तोमेभिः⟧, hymns; and likewise Indra who grows with ⟦मध्यमेभिः⟧, hymns that belong to the middle, and (⟦उत⟧ is glossed as and) with ⟦नूतनेभिः⟧, the recent hymns being made by us.

⟦चक्रे⟧: the form of ⟦करोते⟧ in liṭ. ⟦नव्यसे⟧: from the word ⟦नव⟧, with īyasun, when it has the name bha, the dropping of a; the dropping of ī is Vedic. Because of the marker n, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦ववृत्यां⟧: the root ⟦वृतु⟧ in the sense of turning; in liṅ, by the rule "variously in the Veda", ślu in place of śap; by vyatyaya, parasmaipada. Nighāta. ⟦ववृधे⟧: the form in liṭ of the root ⟦वृधु⟧ in the sense of growing. Suffix-accent. ⟦मध्यमेभिः⟧: from the word madhya, in one of the remaining senses, by ⟦मध्यान्मः⟧ (⟦पा, ४ ३-८⟧) the suffix ma. Suffix-accent. ⟦नूतनेभिः⟧: by the statement that for the word nava the suffixes tnap, tanap and kha (⟦त्नप्तनन्बाश्च⟧) are to be taught, and the substitute ⟦नू⟧ **NOTE**{the reference text gives a reference for this statement (pā. ma. 5.4.30.6); the supplied text has none}, there are the suffix tanap and the substitute nū.

## Ṛk 14

⟦यत्⟧, when, ⟦धिषणा⟧, my thought, "Let me praise Indra", ⟦विवेष⟧, pervaded, ⟦मा⟧, me, then I ⟦जजान⟧, make, a praise. He shows the form of the thought: I ⟦स्तवै⟧, will praise, ⟦पुरा⟧, before, the day that is ⟦पार्यात्⟧, at the far end, very far off, and causes obstruction. ⟦यत्र⟧: when that very thought has arisen, ⟦यथा⟧, in such a way that, that Indra ⟦पीपरत्⟧, may carry us across, ⟦अंहसः⟧, from poverty, which is of the form of sin, so do the people who are on both sides of us, desiring wealth, ⟦हवंते⟧, call. An illustration for that: ⟦नावेव यांतं⟧: as ⟦उभये⟧, the people on both banks, call to a man going by boat on rivers and the like; like that.

⟦विवेष⟧: the form in liṭ, with ṇal, of the root ⟦विष्ल⟧ in the sense of pervading. The lit-accent. ⟦जजान⟧: the form in liṭ, in the first person, with ṇal, of the root ⟦जन⟧ in the sense of birth. Because of the difference of sentence, there is no nighāta. ⟦अंहसः⟧: the root ⟦अम⟧ in the senses of going and so on; by ⟦अमर्हुक् च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-२१२⟧) the suffix asun and the augment huk. The nit-accent. ⟦पीपरत्⟧: of the root ⟦पृ⟧, in the senses of protecting and filling, in luṅ, with caṅ, there are the shortening, the i of the reduplicative syllable, the lengthening and the guṇa. Because of the connection with ⟦यथा⟧, there is no nighāta. The lit-accent. ⟦नावेव⟧: from the root ⟦नुद⟧, in the sense of impelling, by the rule ⟦ग्यानुदिभ्यां डौः⟧ (⟦उ. २-६४⟧) the suffix ḍau. It is impelled, thus ⟦नौः⟧, a float. By the rule ⟦सावेकाच⟧ the instrumental ending is udātta. "With iva the compound is obligatory, and the case-ending is not dropped", and so on. ⟦उभये⟧: for tayap that follows the word ubha, by ⟦उभादुदात्तो नित्यं⟧ (⟦सा. ५-२-४४⟧) the substitute ayac; by the force of the express statement the first syllable is udātta. ⟦हवंते⟧: the form of hvayati in laṭ.

## Ṛk 15

At the midday pressing, ⟦अपूर्णो अस्य⟧ is the prasthita-yājyā of the Āgnīdhra (⟦आग्नीध्रीयस्य⟧). **NOTE**{this introductory passage is not in the reference text} And it has been laid down in the sūtra: ⟦अपूर्णो अस्यकलशः स्वाहेति माध्यंदिन्यः⟧ (⟦आ. ५-५⟧).

O Indra, ⟦कलशः⟧, the droṇakalaśa, for the drinking of ⟦अस्य⟧, of you, is filled with Soma. ⟦स्वाहा⟧: offered with svāhā and given, the vessel together with its Soma is ⟦पिबध्यै⟧, for your drinking. ⟦सिसिचे⟧: I pour that Soma into the prescribed receptacle. An illustration for that: ⟦कोशं सेक्तेव⟧: as a pourer, one who pours out, pours water from a kośa full of water, from a leather bag, into another vessel; like that. ⟦प्रियाः⟧, most sweet, ⟦सोमासो⟧, those Somas, which have been given, ⟦अभि⟧, having in view only ⟦इंद्रं⟧, you, ⟦मदाय⟧, for your delight, ⟦प्रदक्षिणित्⟧, in a rightward course, ⟦समाववृत्रन्⟧: let them abide, having turned fully.

⟦अपूर्णः⟧: of the root ⟦पृ⟧, in the senses of protecting and filling, the niṣṭhā in the sense of the object; by the rule radābhyām its t becomes n; by the rule ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the accent is that of the gati. ⟦स्वाहा⟧: because it is a nipāta it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦सेक्तेव कोशं⟧: by the rule ⟦न लोकाव्यय⟧ the genitive is prohibited. ⟦सिसिचे⟧: the form in liṭ of the root ⟦पिच⟧ in the sense of flowing. ⟦पिबध्यै⟧: of the root ⟦पा⟧, in the sense of drinking, the suffix śadhyain in the sense of tumun; because of the marker ś, the substitute piba; because of the marker n, it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦अववृत्रन्⟧: of the root ⟦वृतु⟧, in the sense of turning, in laṅ, śap; by the rule "variously in the Veda" ślu in place of that śap; by vyatyaya, parasmaipada; by the rule "variously in the Veda" the augment ruṭ for jhi.

## Ṛk 16

O ⟦पुरुहूत⟧, Indra, the ⟦गभीरो⟧, deep, ⟦सिंधुः⟧, ocean, does not stop ⟦त्वा⟧, you. Then the ⟦अद्रयः⟧, mountains, ⟦संतो⟧, being, ⟦परि⟧, all around, ⟦न वरंत⟧, do not stop you; rather they act in conformity with you. ⟦यत्⟧, since, ⟦इत्था⟧, thus, in this manner, ⟦इषितः⟧, entreated, ⟦सखिभ्यः⟧, by friends, the gods, you ⟦अरुजः⟧, broke up completely, the ⟦ऊर्वं⟧, the Ūrva-fire, which is ⟦गव्यं⟧, in the cow, present in the pit, ⟦चित्⟧, even though it was ⟦दृळ्हं⟧, exceedingly strong.

⟦गभीरः⟧: the root ⟦गाध्य⟧ in the senses of standing firm, wishing to obtain, and stringing together; from this the suffix īrac, bh for dh, and the shortening of ā: so it is formed by nipātana by ⟦गभीरगंभीरौ⟧ (⟦उ. ४-३५⟧). The cit-accent. ⟦सिंधुः⟧: the root ⟦स्कंदू⟧ in the sense of flowing; by the rule ⟦स्कंदेः संप्रसारणं धश्च⟧ the suffix u, saṃprasāraṇa, and dh for d. Since nit continues, it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦परि षंतः⟧. ⟦संतः⟧: the form of ⟦अस्तेः⟧ with śatṛ; by the rule upasargaprādurbhyām astir yacparaḥ, the ṣ. Some wish ⟦परि⟧ to be construed with the verb; then the ṣ is not justified. ⟦परि⟧ has the accent of a nipāta; ⟦संतः⟧ has the suffix-accent. ⟦वरंत⟧: the root ⟦वृञ्⟧ in the sense of covering; it is of the bhvādi class; the form in laṅ. Nighāta. ⟦अरुजः⟧: the form in laṅ of the root ⟦रुजो⟧ in the sense of breaking. ⟦गव्यं⟧: in the sense "a modification of go", by the rule gopayasor yat, the suffix yat; by the rule yato 'nāvaḥ it is udātta on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 17

O Indra, ⟦वाजसात्⟧: that in which there is ⟦साति⟧, gain, of ⟦वाजस्य⟧, food, this is vājasāti. ⟦भरे⟧: fighters bear (⟦बिभ्रति⟧) the fortune of victory by it, thus ⟦भरः⟧ is battle. In that battle, ⟦शुनं⟧, swollen, grown great with ardour, or else, ⟦शुनं⟧ being a name of happiness, the maker of happiness; ⟦मघवानं⟧, possessing wealth, and for that very reason ⟦इंद्रं⟧, endowed with unsurpassed lordship; ⟦नृतमं⟧, pre-eminently the leader of the whole world: you ⟦हुवेम⟧, we Kuśikas would call, for the sake of the sacrifice. Likewise ⟦शृण्वंतं⟧, hearing the praise being made by us, ⟦उग्रं⟧, terrible to enemies, ⟦समत्सु⟧, in battles, ⟦घ्नंतं⟧, destroying, ⟦वृत्राणि⟧, all the rakṣases indicated by Vṛtra, ⟦संजितं⟧, the complete conqueror, ⟦धनानां⟧, of the riches belonging to enemies: you we would call ⟦ऊतये⟧, for protection.

⟦शुनं⟧: of the root ⟦टुओश्वि⟧, in the senses of going and growing, in the niṣṭhā, by the rule yasya vibhāṣā the iṭ is prohibited. Because it belongs to the yajādi group, saṃprasāraṇa. The absence of lengthening is Vedic. Suffix-accent. ⟦हुवेम⟧: of hvayati, when saṃprasāraṇa has been made by the rule "variously in the Veda" and the luk of śap has been made, the form in liṅ. Nighāta. ⟦भरे⟧: the root ⟦भृङ्⟧ in the sense of bearing; the suffix gha; it is udātta on the first syllable because it belongs to the vṛṣādi group. ⟦वाजसात्⟧: the root ⟦षणु⟧ in the sense of giving; ktin in the sense of the action; by the rule beginning ⟦जनसन⟧, ā for the nasal. In the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the former member. ⟦समत्सु⟧: the root ⟦आद⟧ in the sense of eating; kvip after it when preceded by sam. They devour the lives of fighters, thus ⟦समदः⟧, battles. The accent is that of the latter member, a kṛt. ⟦घ्नंतं⟧: of the root ⟦हन⟧, in the senses of injuring and going, with śatṛ, by the rule beginning gamahana the penultimate is dropped; by the rule ho hanteḥ, gh. ⟦संजितं⟧: the root ⟦जि⟧ in the sense of conquering; kvip.
