## Introduction

The sūkta beginning ⟦इंद्र सोमं सोमपत⟧ has seventeen Ṛks; it is the third sūkta, Vaiśvāmitra, in the Triṣṭubh metre, addressed to Indra. And it has been listed thus: ⟦इंद्र सोमं त्र्यूनेति⟧.

On the second day of the Pṛṣṭhya and Abhiplava ṣaḍahas, at the Marutvatīya, this sūkta, ⟦इंद्र सोमं⟧, is the ⟦निविद्धानीयं⟧. It has been laid down in the sūtra, in the section beginning ⟦द्वितीयस्येति⟧: ⟦इंद्र सोमं या त ऊतिरवमेति मध्यंदिनः⟧ (⟦आ. ७-६⟧). **NOTE**{the reference text gives the number of this sūtra reference differently (7.6.4 against the supplied seven-six)}

At the Gosava and the Vivadha too, this sūkta is used at the Marutvatīya śastra. And it has been laid down in the sūtra: ⟦इंद्र सोममेतायामेति मध्यंदिनः⟧ (⟦आ. ९-८⟧). At the Udbhid and the Valabhid too, this sūkta is used at the Marutvatīya. And it has been laid down in the sūtra: ⟦इंद्र सोममिंद्रः पूर्विदिति मध्यंदिनः⟧ (⟦आ, ९-८⟧).

## Ṛk 1

Viśvāmitra says. ⟦सोमपते⟧: O Indra, lord of Soma, drink this Soma, the Soma-juice, being prepared by us at the midday pressing. Since the midday pressing that is being performed for you, the midday pressing, the śastra called niṣkevalya, belonging to midday, is delightful (⟦चारु⟧ is glossed as delightful). ⟦मघवन्⟧: possessor of wealth. ⟦ऋजीषिन्⟧: by the word ṛjīṣa the Soma-substance whose essence is gone is meant. O Indra, possessor of that: having released the two Hari, the two horses yoked to the chariot, from the chariot, and having filled the two jaws (⟦शिप्रे⟧ means ⟦हनू⟧) ⟦प्रपुथ्य⟧ with the mouthful of this place, rejoice here, in the place of the sacrifice (⟦मादयस्व⟧). Gladden those two horses.

⟦प्रपुथ्य⟧ is the form, with lyap, of the root ⟦पोढृ वर्याप्तावित्यस्य⟧ (in the sense of sufficiency). The shortening is Vedic. The accent is the lit-accent. ⟦ऋजीषिन्⟧: from the root ⟦अर्ज षर्ज⟧ in the sense of acquiring (⟦अर्जन⟧), by the rule ⟦वर्जेश्व ऋजि च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-२८⟧) the suffix īṣan is added and ṛji is the substitute for the root. That which is acquired (⟦अर्ज्यत⟧) is ṛjīṣa; the suffix ini is added in the sense "this has that". Because it is a vocative, there is nighāta. ⟦मादयस्व⟧: the root ⟦मदि⟧ in the senses of praising and so on; the causative ṇic. Nighāta.

## Ṛk 2

O Indra, drink this Soma which is ⟦गवाशिरं⟧: that in which there is the āśīr, the mixing, of the milk of the cow. And moreover it is ⟦मंथिनं⟧: joined with manthā; ⟦शुक्रं⟧: new; or else it is present in the Śukra-and-Manthin cup. Thus, being present in the three pressings, drink this Soma. For your ⟦मदाय⟧, that is, for your delight, we give (⟦ररिम⟧) that Soma. ⟦मारुतेन⟧: the troop of the Maruts is Māruta; and that is ⟦ब्रह्मकृत्⟧. ⟦ब्रह्म⟧ is praise; one who makes it is brahmakṛt. You, united (⟦सजोषाः⟧) with the Māruta troop which is making praise of Indra, and with the Rudras, eleven in number, and with the Rudras, satisfied (⟦तृपत्⟧) by the drinking of the Soma, ⟦आ वृषस्व⟧: pour this Soma all around into the belly; hold it.

⟦गवाशिरं⟧: from the root ⟦श्रीणाते⟧ preceded by ā, with kvip, the substitution āśir takes place by nipātana through the rule beginning ⟦पस्पृधेथामान्यचुरित्यादिना⟧. In the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the nature of the former member. ⟦पिबा⟧: the lengthening in the saṃhitā by the rule ⟦द्व्यचोऽतस्तिजि⟧. ⟦ररिम⟧: this is the form in the present sense, in the Vedic idiom, of the root, ⟦रातेश्छांदसे⟧, in lit. Because it is not in the same sentence, there is no nighāta. Since the augments are anudātta, the augment iṭ being anudātta, the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦तृपत्⟧: ⟦तृप प्रीणने⟧; it belongs to the tudādi class. Because the teaching of the augments is not constant, there is no num. ⟦वृषस्व⟧: the root ⟦वृषु⟧ in the sense of sprinkling; śa by vyatyaya.

## Ṛk 3

O Indra, those Maruts who increased your ⟦शुष्मं⟧, the radiance that dries up enemies, and those Maruts who increased your ⟦तविषीं⟧, strength, by being helpers in battle, those Maruts, ⟦अर्चंतः⟧, praising you, increased your ⟦ओजः⟧, your capacity in battle. ⟦वज्रहस्त⟧: he whose hand is joined with the thunderbolt, such; ⟦सुशिप्र⟧: of beautiful jaws. ⟦शिप्रे हनू नासिके वेति यास्कः⟧: Yāska says that the two jaws or the two nostrils are meant. O Indra, ⟦रुद्रेभिः⟧: they make the enemies weep, therefore the Maruts are Rudras. You, together with your troop, with them, drink Soma at the midday pressing.

⟦अवर्धन्⟧: the root ⟦वृधु⟧ in the sense of growing, with the causative sense included. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦माध्यंदिने⟧: the suffix añ because the word belongs to the utsādi group; because of the marker ñ, the first syllable is udātta.

## Ṛk 4

Those Maruts who were the strength of Indra, those very Maruts (⟦त इत्⟧) ⟦विविप्रे नु⟧: quickly urged this Indra (⟦अस्य⟧ is the genitive in the sense of the accusative), in such a way that the speech should be ⟦मधुमत्⟧, endowed with sweetness: "We are your helpers; go to the battle." The Maruts, the helpers in battle, by whom (⟦येभिः⟧) Indra, impelled, ⟦विवेद⟧, knew the ⟦मर्म⟧ of Vṛtra the Asura, who was ⟦अमर्मणः⟧, one whose vital spot was not known by others, and therefore ⟦मन्यमानस्य⟧, one who thought "No one is my slayer": that spot, that vital spot, on being struck at which he will die.

⟦विविप्रे⟧: the lit of the root ⟦विप क्षेपण⟧, with ātmanepada by vyatyaya. By the rule ⟦इरेच इरयो र⟧ the substitution is re. Nighāta. ⟦आसन्⟧: the form of the root ⟦अस भुवीत्यस्य⟧ in laṅ. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦विवेद⟧: the form of the root ⟦विद ज्ञान⟧ in lit. The lit-accent. ⟦मर्म⟧: from the root ⟦मृज् प्राणत्याग⟧ the Uṇādi suffix manin. The nit-accent.

## Ṛk 5

O Indra, ⟦मनुष्वत्⟧: as Manu's sacrifice; you, relishing (⟦जुषाणः⟧ is glossed as serving) this pressing of mine, this sacrifice, drink this Soma for ⟦शश्वते⟧, that is, eternal ⟦वीर्याय⟧, the strength whose mark is the overcoming of others, the capacity. O ⟦हर्यश्व⟧: you, such as you are, come (⟦आववृत्स्व⟧) together with the sacrificial ones, the Maruts. And moreover, ⟦सरण्युभिः⟧: by those wishing for themselves mantras having one accent; or else ⟦सरण्युभिः⟧: you, accompanied by the Maruts who are disposed to move, (⟦सिसर्षि⟧) cause to reach ⟦अपः⟧, the waters standing in the atmosphere, and ⟦अर्णा⟧, the earthly waters. Or else ⟦अपः⟧: this word ends in the ablative; from the pervaded atmosphere cause the waters (⟦सकाशादर्णांसि⟧) to reach.

⟦ववृत्स्व⟧: the root ⟦वृतु⟧ in the sense of turning; śap is replaced by lu by vyatyaya; the form is in loṭ. ⟦सरण्युभिः⟧: ⟦रण⟧ is in the sense of sound; kvip. Those mantras of which the raṇa, the sound, is the same are saraṇa; those wishing for them: kyac after a noun; by the rule ⟦क्यादि⟧ the suffix u. Or else from ⟦सृ गतौ⟧ by the rule ⟦सृयुवचिभ्योऽन्युजागूजक्नुचः⟧ (⟦उ. ३-८१⟧) the suffix anyuc; by ⟦रषाभ्यां⟧ (⟦पा. ८-४-१⟧) the change to ṇ; because of the marker c, the final syllable is udātta. ⟦अपः⟧: the root ⟦आप्लव्याप्तौ⟧; from āp, kvip, and the shortening of the root: ⟦ह्रस्वश्चति क्विप् धातोर्ह्रस्वश्च⟧; by ⟦सावेकाच⟧ the case-ending is udātta. ⟦सिसर्षि⟧: ⟦सृ गतौ⟧; the Vedic juhotyādi class; by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीत्यभ्यासस्यॆत्वं⟧ the reduplicative syllable has i.

## Ṛk 6

O Indra, you, ⟦देवीः⟧, disposed to play, the waters ⟦परि वन्विवांसं⟧: that which, having encircled them, exists, and therefore ⟦अत एनादेवं⟧: not appearing as clear because of the abundance of water, ⟦शयानं⟧: staying motionless in one place, Vṛtra, the cloud. When (⟦यत्⟧ is glossed as when) you slew him with ⟦चरता⟧, with the ⟦वधेन⟧, with the battle being directed towards him, you who have slain (⟦जघन्वान्⟧), then, in the ⟦आजौ⟧, in the battle, for ⟦सर्तवै⟧, for very swift going, as you urge horses, like ⟦अत्यान्⟧, so you released ⟦अपः⟧, the waters from the atmosphere, from the sky; ⟦प्रासृजो ह⟧: having made a breaking of the cloud with force, you released them, indeed. The connection is with the latter (verse): "We worship him."

⟦जघन्वान्⟧: the form of the root ⟦हन हिंसागत्योरित्यस्य⟧ with kvasu; since by the rule ⟦विभाषा गमहनविदविशामितीटो⟧ the iṭ is optional, there is here no iṭ. By ⟦अभ्यासाच्चेति⟧ the h becomes gh. Suffix-accent. ⟦असृजः⟧: ⟦सृज विसर्गे⟧; the vikaraṇa is śa. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦सर्तवै⟧: ⟦सृ गत⟧; the suffix tavai in the sense of tumun; by ⟦अंतश्च तनै युगपदिति⟧ both the root and the suffix are udātta. ⟦आजौ⟧: ⟦अज गतिक्षेपणयोः⟧; by ⟦अज्यतिभ्यां च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-१३०⟧) the suffix iṇ; because of ṇ-marking, vṛddhi of the penultimate. Those go in it (⟦अजंति गच्छंत्यस्मिन्⟧): āji is battle. Suffix-accent. In ⟦सर्तवाजावित्यत्र⟧, in the saṃhitā, when the substitution āy has been made, its y is dropped by the rule ⟦लोपः शाकल्यस्य⟧. Because all rules are optional in the Veda, when the savarṇa-dīrgha was to be done, the dropping of y is not invalid; thus the savarṇa-dīrgha. ⟦शयानं⟧: ⟦शीज् स्वष्टे⟧; śānac; because of the adādi class, śap is dropped (luk); because of ṅ-marking, by the accent of the lasārvadhātuka, śānac being anudātta, the accent is that of the root. ⟦वधेन⟧: from ⟦हंते⟧ in bhāva, by ⟦हनश्च वध⟧ the suffix ap and vadha as the substitute for the root; and it is udātta on the last syllable. ⟦वन्विवांसं⟧: ⟦वृञ् वरण⟧; with kvasu, the augment iṭ is Vedic. Suffix-accent.

## Ṛk 7

⟦नमसा⟧: with food whose mark is the oblation, ⟦वृद्धं⟧: grown, and therefore ⟦बृहंतं⟧: great; ⟦महांतमृष्टं⟧: ⟦ऋष्यते गम्यते स्तुतिलक्षणैर्मंत्रैरिति ऋष्वं⟧, he is approached by mantras whose mark is praise, thus ṛṣva, to be praised; ⟦अजरं⟧: free from old age, and therefore ⟦युवानं⟧: ever young; such an Indra we worship (⟦यजाम इत्⟧). We Kuśikas honour him with words of praise for the accomplishment of the sacrifice. ⟦प्रिये⟧: the dearest, the immeasurable ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth, of whom, of Indra, worthy of sacrifice (⟦यज्ञियस्य⟧ is glossed as worthy of sacrifice), of you, the greatness (⟦महिमानं⟧), the splendour, ⟦न ममतुः⟧: are not able to measure. ⟦न ममाते⟧: the second verb is for the sake of respect.

⟦यजामः⟧: the form of the root ⟦यजे⟧ in laṭ. ⟦ऋष्वं⟧: ⟦ऋषी गतौ⟧; it is formed by nipātana ending in the suffix va through the rule beginning ⟦सर्वनिघृष्टऋष्टेत्यादिना⟧. Suffix-accent. ⟦प्रिये⟧: by ⟦ईदूदेदिति⟧ the designation pragṛhya. ⟦ममतुः⟧: ⟦मा मान⟧; the form in lit, in atus. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. Suffix-accent. ⟦महिमानं⟧: from the word ⟦महि⟧ in the sense "its state", by the pṛthvādi group, imanic. Cit-accent. ⟦ममाते⟧: ⟦माज्⟧, the root in the senses of measuring and of sounding; the form in lit, in ātām.

## Ṛk 8

O Indra, all the gods (⟦विश्वे⟧ is glossed as all) do not ⟦मिनंति⟧ your works (⟦कर्म⟧ is glossed as works), ⟦सुकृता⟧, well made, the earth and the rest, and the many ⟦व्रतानि⟧, the sacrifices and the rest. They do not injure any of these works of yours; rather they honour them. And moreover, this Indra ⟦दाधार⟧ upheld the earth, the world of the earth, the sky, the world of the sky, and (⟦उत⟧ is glossed as moreover) this atmosphere, the world of the atmosphere (⟦इमां⟧), these three worlds. ⟦सुदंसाः⟧: of good deeds, of beautiful deeds; Indra ⟦जजान⟧, produced the sun, the Āditya, the illuminator of the worlds, and the dawn, the time of the dawn, which carries through the agnihotra and the other rites.

This group of words ⟦इंद्रस्य कर्म सुकृता पुरूणि⟧ is read in the group of verses beginning ⟦मेको द्वे वसुमती⟧ (⟦ऋग्वे ३-२-३⟧). **NOTE**{the reference text gives this reference as 3.30.13, which differs from the supplied numbers} Here too the same is read. If it be said, "Why is it not dropped in the Pada-text?", no, because of the difference of sentence. There, ⟦इंद्रस्य कर्म सुकृता पुरूणि⟧ is one sentence; but here ⟦कर्म सुकृता⟧ is one sentence and ⟦पुरूणि व्रतानि⟧ another. Moreover, the group of words there is in the nominative, here in the accusative. Therefore, because of the difference of sentence and the difference of case, it is not dropped in the Pada-text.

⟦मिनंति⟧: the form of the root ⟦मीञ हिंसायामित्यस्य⟧ in laṭ. Nighāta. ⟦दाधार⟧: the form of the root ⟦धृङ् धारण⟧ in lit. By the tujādi group the reduplicative syllable is lengthened. The lit-accent. ⟦जजान⟧: the form of the root ⟦जन जनन⟧, with the causative sense included, in lit. The lit-accent. ⟦सुदंसाः⟧: by the rule ⟦सोर्मनसी⟧ the latter member is udātta on its first syllable.

## Ṛk 9

⟦अद्रोघ⟧: free from malice, O Indra, that greatness (⟦महित्वं⟧) of yours, such greatness, is ⟦सत्यं⟧, real. Since you, born, ⟦सद्यः⟧, at that very time, drank the Soma being given by us, the Soma-juice (⟦ह⟧ is in the sense of being well known). And moreover, the capacity, the ⟦ओजः⟧ of you, ⟦तवसः⟧, the powerful, the ⟦द्यावः⟧, all the worlds represented by heaven, do not restrain (⟦न वरंत⟧). And moreover ⟦अहा⟧, the days beginning with pratipad, do not restrain; and likewise ⟦मासाः⟧, the months beginning with Caitra, and ⟦शरदः⟧, the years, do not restrain your capacity. But all exist following your capacity: this is the meaning.

⟦अद्रोघ⟧: by ⟦आमंत्रितस्य च⟧ the ṣāṣṭhika rule, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦अपिबः⟧: because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦मासाः⟧: ⟦मसी परिमाणे⟧; ⟦मस्यते परिमायते त्रिंशता दिनैरिति मासः⟧, it is measured by thirty days, thus māsa; ghañ in the sense of the object. Because of the marker ñ, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦शरदः⟧: from the root ⟦त्वा हिंसायामित्यस्मात्⟧, by the rule ⟦शृद्यभसोऽदि⟧ the suffix adi in the locative sense. ⟦शीर्यतेऽस्यां तृणगुल्मवनस्पतीनां तेजांसीति शरत्⟧: in it the splendours of grasses, shrubs and trees waste away, thus śarad. Suffix-accent. ⟦वरंत⟧: ⟦वृङ् वरणे⟧, with the causative sense included, here in the sense of restraining; of the bhvādi class; the form in laṅ in the Vedic idiom. Nighāta.

## Ṛk 10

In the one-day rite called ⟦सद्यस्क्रीनाम्मेकाहे⟧, **NOTE**{the first word of this passage is damaged and the passage is not in the reference text; what the supplied letters name is not certain} at the niṣkevalya śastra, the Ṛk ⟦त्वं सद्यो अपिब⟧ is the ⟦सूक्तमुखीया⟧. And it has been laid down in the sūtra: ⟦त्वं सद्यो अपिबो जात इंद्रानु त्वाहिष्टे अध देव देवाः⟧ (⟦आ. ९-५⟧).

O Indra, born ⟦सद्यः⟧, at that very time, standing in the ⟦परमे व्योमन्⟧, in the highest place, you drank the Soma for ⟦मदाय⟧, for delight. When (⟦यत्⟧ is glossed as when) you entered ⟦द्यावापृथिवी⟧, heaven and earth, completely, intensely: you entered indeed (⟦ह⟧ is glossed as indeed). Then, afterwards, being ancient, you became the maker of works (⟦कारुधायाः⟧), indeed.

⟦व्योमन्⟧: the root ⟦अव रक्षणगत्यादिषु⟧ preceded by vi; by the rule beginning ⟦नामन् सीमन्⟧ the suffix manin; by the rule beginning ⟦ज्वरत्वरेत्यादिना⟧ ūṭ. That of which the pervasion is special (⟦विशेषेणौम व्याप्तिर्यस्य तत्⟧). By the rule beginning ⟦सुपां सुलुगित्यादिना⟧ the dropping of the case-ending. In the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the nature of the former member. ⟦अविवेशीः⟧: the root ⟦विश प्रवेशन⟧ in yaṅluk, in laṅ, sip; by ⟦यजो वेतीडागमः⟧ the augment iṭ. By ⟦इतश्चति⟧ the i of sip is dropped. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦कारुधायाः⟧: ⟦कारुः⟧: from the root ⟦करोतेः⟧ by the rule ⟦कृवापाजिमिस्वदिसाध्यतूभ्य उण्रत्ययः⟧ the suffix uṇ; because of the marker ṇ, vṛddhi. From the root dhā, ⟦दधातेर्वहिहाधाञ्छंदसीत्यसुन्⟧: the suffix asun. ⟦णिदित्यनुवृत्तेरातो युक् चिण् कृतोरिति युगागमः⟧: by the continuation of ṇit, the augment yuk. By the rule ⟦समासे गतिकारकयोरपि पूर्वपदपृकृतिस्वरत्वं च⟧ the accent is that of the nature of the former member.

## Ṛk 11

⟦तुविजात⟧: he from whom many things have been born, the earth and the rest, this is Tuvijāta. O Tuvijāta Indra, you, ⟦तव्यान्⟧, exceedingly grown, ⟦त्वमर्णः परिशयानमुदकं⟧: the ⟦अर्णः⟧ is water; ⟦परिशयानं⟧ means lying around the water, that is, existing all around, keeping the water within; and therefore ⟦ओजायमानं⟧, exerting strength: the ⟦अहिं⟧, the cloud (⟦आ समंताद्धंत्यपहरत्युदकमित्यहिर्मेघः⟧: it strikes all around, it takes away the water, thus ahi is the cloud), this cloud you ⟦अहन्⟧ slew, for the impelling of water. ⟦अध⟧ means then: ⟦ते महित्वं⟧, your greatness, heaven (⟦द्यूर्नानुभूत्⟧) did not experience it; does not know. Since you, with ⟦अन्यया⟧, that is, with one of the two, ⟦स्फिग्या⟧, that is, with the hip, covering the earth (⟦क्षां⟧), stand (⟦अवस्थाः⟧), covering it.

⟦अहन्⟧: the form of the root ⟦हंते⟧ in laṅ, with the dropping of sib. ⟦अहिं⟧: ⟦हन हिंसागत्योः⟧; by the rule ⟦आजु श्रिहनिभ्यां प्रस्वश्चति⟧ the suffix iṇ, ḍit, and the shortening of the former member and the shortening. Because it belongs to the vṛṣādi group, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦ओजायमानं⟧: from the word ⟦ओजः⟧ in the sense of conduct, by ⟦कर्तु: क्यज् सलोपश्चति⟧ the suffix kyaṅ and the dropping of s; because of the marker ṅ, ātmanepada. Suffix-accent. ⟦तव्यान्⟧: ⟦तु⟧ is a root of sūtra-origin; from this, ending in tṛ, by ⟦तुश्छंदसीतीयसुन्⟧ the suffix īyasun; by ⟦तुरिष्ठमेयःस्थिति⟧ the tṛc is dropped; in the designation bha, guṇa of u; the dropping of i is Vedic. Nit-accent. ⟦भूत्⟧: the form of ⟦भवते⟧ in luṅ. ⟦स्फिग्या⟧: having set aside the designation bha by ⟦यचि भमिति⟧, in the designation pada by ⟦स्वादिष्टसर्वनामस्थान⟧ the change to the voiced unaspirate. In the instrumental, the homogeneous vowel of the former. ⟦क्षां⟧: ⟦क्षि निवासगत्योः⟧; by ⟦अन्वेभ्योऽपि दृश्यत इति⟧ the suffix ḍa even after a root without a preceding word; then ṭāp. Creatures dwell in it (⟦क्षियंति निवसंत्यस्यां प्राणिन इति⟧), thus kṣā is the earth. By the accent of the cessation of udātta, the last syllable is udātta. ⟦अवस्थाः⟧: the root ⟦वस आच्छादन⟧ in laṅ; because of anudāttatva, ātmanepada; because of the adādi class, śap is dropped. Because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. The accent of the augment.

## Ṛk 12

O Indra, this sacrifice of ours is your ⟦वर्धनः⟧, that is, the one who makes you grow by the giving of oblation; it is so indeed (⟦भूद्धि⟧). And moreover ⟦सुतसोमः⟧: one possessing pressed Soma; this sacrifice, ⟦मियेधः⟧, is dear to you. **NOTE**{the reference text has a gloss on this word that the supplied text lacks} You, being ⟦यज्जियो⟧, worthy of sacrifice, protect (⟦अव⟧) the sacrificer, the doer of the sacrifice (⟦यज्ञं⟧ is glossed as the doer of the sacrifice), by this sacrifice that is being performed, by the giving of the fruit. And moreover this sacrifice, in the work of slaying Vṛtra, in the ⟦अहिहत्ये⟧, let it protect (⟦आवत्⟧) your thunderbolt.

⟦वर्धनः⟧: ⟦वृधु वर्धन⟧ in the causative; by the rule that it belongs to the nandyādi group, the suffix lyu. The lit-accent. ⟦भूत्⟧: because of the connection with ⟦हि⟧, there is no nighāta. ⟦अव⟧: the form of ⟦अवते⟧ in loṭ. Nighāta. ⟦सन्⟧: the form of ⟦आसे⟧ in śatṛ. Suffix-accent. ⟦अहिहत्ये⟧: ⟦हन हिंसागत्योरित्यस्य⟧ in bhāva, by ⟦हनस्त च⟧ the suffix kyap and t as the substitute for the final. The accent of the latter member of a kṛt-compound. ⟦आवत्⟧: the form of ⟦अवते⟧ in laṅ in the Vedic idiom. Nighāta.

## Ṛk 13

O Indra, every sacrificer, ⟦अवसा⟧, by the sacrifice that protects himself, makes you, Indra, ⟦त्वामर्वागाचक्रे⟧ face him, by the giving of oblation. Likewise I, for ⟦नव्यसे⟧, newer ⟦सुम्नाय⟧, wealth, may turn you here (⟦ववृत्यां⟧): may I make you, turned by the sacrifice, face me. What sort are you? You who grew with ⟦पूर्वेभिः⟧, ancient ⟦स्तोमेभिः⟧, hymns; and likewise Indra who grows with ⟦मध्यमेभिः⟧, hymns that belong to the middle, and (⟦उत⟧ is glossed as and) with ⟦नूतनेभिः⟧, the recent hymns being made by us.

⟦चक्रे⟧: the form of ⟦करोते⟧ in lit. ⟦नव्यसे⟧: from the word ⟦नव⟧, īyasun; in the designation bha the dropping of a; the dropping of ī is Vedic. By the marker n, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦ववृत्यां⟧: ⟦वृतु वर्तने⟧ in liṅ; by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति शपः श्लुः⟧ śap is replaced by lu; by vyatyaya parasmaipada. Nighāta. ⟦ववृधे⟧: the form of ⟦वृधु वर्धन⟧ in lit. Suffix-accent. ⟦मध्यमेभिः⟧: from the word madhya in the śaiṣika sense, by ⟦मध्यान्मः⟧ (⟦पा, ४ ३-८⟧) the suffix ma. Suffix-accent. ⟦नूतनेभिः⟧: for the word ⟦नव⟧, the suffixes ⟦त्नप्तनन्बाश्च⟧ are to be stated, and the substitution ⟦नू⟧.

## Ṛk 14

When (⟦यद्यदा⟧ is glossed as when) ⟦धिषणेंद्रं स्तवानीति⟧, my intellect, thinking "Let me praise Indra", ⟦विवेष⟧, pervaded me (⟦व्याप्नोत्⟧), then I ⟦जजान⟧, make a praise (⟦करोमि⟧). It shows the nature of the intellect: I shall praise (⟦स्तवै⟧ is glossed as let me praise), before ⟦पार्यात्पारे भवादतिदूरवर्तिनो⟧, that which is at the far end, very far away, obstruction-causing, ⟦विघ्नकारिणोऽह्नः⟧, the day. ⟦यत्र⟧: in which intellect, once it is born, that Indra ⟦पीपरत्⟧ would take us across (⟦पारं नयेत्⟧) from ⟦अंहसः⟧, from sin-formed poverty and the like, in that manner the men who desire wealth and are on both sides of us ⟦हवंते⟧, call. There is an illustration for it: ⟦नावेव यांतं⟧: as a man going by boat in rivers and the like, going, the people on both banks (⟦उभयकूलवर्तिनो जना⟧) call. Like that.

⟦विवेष⟧: the form of the root ⟦विष्ल व्याप्तावित्यस्य⟧, in lit, in ṇal. The lit-accent. ⟦जजान⟧: the form of the root ⟦जन⟧, in the sense of birth, in lit, in the first person, in ṇal. Because of the difference of sentence, there is no nighāta. ⟦अंहसः⟧: ⟦अम गत्यादिषु⟧; by ⟦अमर्हुक् च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-२१२⟧) the suffix asun and the augment huk. The nit-accent. ⟦पीपरत्⟧: ⟦पृ पालनपूरणयोरित्यस्य⟧, in luṅ, in caṅ: ⟦प्रस्वाभ्यासेत्वदीर्घगुणाः⟧, the shortening, the reduplicative syllable, the i, the lengthening, the guṇa. ⟦यथायोगादनिघातः⟧, there is no nighāta because of the connection with ⟦यथा⟧. The lit-accent. ⟦नावेव⟧: from the root ⟦नुद⟧, in the sense of impelling, by the rule ⟦ग्यानुदिभ्यां डौः⟧ (⟦उ. २-६४⟧) the suffix ḍau. ⟦नुद्युत इति नौः प्लवः⟧: it is impelled, thus nau, a float. By ⟦सावेकाच इति⟧ the instrumental is udātta. ⟦इवेन नित्यसमासो विभक्त्यलोप इत्यादि⟧: the compound with iva is obligatory, the case-ending is not dropped, and so on. ⟦उभये⟧: after the word ubha, of the suffix tayap, by ⟦उभादुदात्तो नित्यं⟧ (⟦सा. ५-२-४४⟧) the substitution ayac; by the force of the statement, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦हवंते⟧: the form of ⟦प्वयतेर्लटि⟧ in laṭ.

## Ṛk 15

At the midday pressing, ⟦अपूर्णो अस्येति⟧ is the prasthitayājyā of the ⟦आग्नीध्रीयस्य⟧. **NOTE**{this introductory passage is not in the reference text} And it has been laid down in the sūtra: ⟦अपूर्णो अस्यकलशः स्वाहेति माध्यंदिन्यः⟧ (⟦आ. ५-५⟧).

O Indra, for your drinking, the ⟦कलशः⟧, the Droṇakalaśa, is ⟦सोमेनापूर्णः⟧, filled with Soma. ⟦स्वाहा⟧: it has been offered with svāhā and given: the vessel together with Soma, ⟦पिबध्यै⟧, for your drinking. ⟦सिसिचे⟧: I pour that Soma into the receptacle that has been set in motion (⟦चोदिताधारे⟧). There is an illustration: ⟦कोशं सेक्तेव⟧: as the pourer, the sprinkler, pours water from the water-filled kośa, from the ⟦दृतेः⟧, into another vessel; like that. ⟦प्रियाः⟧: the sweetest, the ⟦सोमासः⟧: those Soma-juices that have been given, ⟦इंद्रं⟧, you, ⟦अभि⟧: having you in view only, ⟦मदाय⟧, for your delight, ⟦प्रदक्षिणित्⟧: in a right-handed circuit, ⟦समाववृत्रन्⟧: having properly turned, let them abide.

⟦अपूर्णः⟧: ⟦पृ पालनपूरणयोरित्यस्य कर्मणि निष्ठा⟧; its n-substitution by ⟦रदाभ्रामिति तस्य नत्वं⟧; by ⟦गतिरनंतर इति गतिस्वरः⟧ the accent of the gati. ⟦स्वाहा⟧: ⟦निपातत्वादाद्युदात्तः⟧, because it is a nipāta it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦सेक्तेव कोशं⟧: ⟦न लोकाव्ययेति षष्ठिप्रतिषेधः⟧, the prohibition of the genitive. ⟦सिसिचे⟧: the form in lit of the root ⟦पिच⟧, in the sense of flowing. ⟦पिबध्यै⟧: ⟦पा⟧, the root in the sense of drinking; the suffix śadhyaiṅ in the sense of tumun; ⟦शित्वात्पिबादेशः⟧, because of the marker ś, the substitution piba; ⟦नित्त्वादाम्यदात्तः⟧, because of the marker n, it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦अववृत्रन्⟧: ⟦वृतु⟧, the root in the sense of turning, in laṅ, ⟦शप्⟧; ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति तस्य शपः श्लुः⟧, by "variously in the Veda" its śap is replaced by lu; ⟦व्यत्ययेन परस्मैपदं⟧, parasmaipada by vyatyaya; ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति झे रुडागमः⟧, by "variously in the Veda" the augment ruṭ in jhi.

## Ṛk 16

O Puruhūta Indra (⟦पुरूहुतेंद्र⟧), ⟦गभीरो गंभीरः सिंधुः समुद्रस्त्वा त्वां न वारयति⟧: the deep river, the ocean, does not restrain you. Therefore the rocks, being all around (⟦परि परितो⟧), do not restrain you (⟦न वरंत⟧); they do not restrain. But they exist following you. Since (⟦यत्⟧ is glossed as since) ⟦इत्था⟧, in this manner, ⟦सखिभ्यः⟧, by friends, by the gods, ⟦इषितः⟧, requested, you, ⟦दृश्यं चिदतिप्रबलमपि⟧, even though exceedingly strong, ⟦गव्यं गवि भवमवटे वर्तमानमूर्वमूर्वानलमारुजः⟧: the Ūrva-fire that is in the cow and is present in the pit, you properly broke (⟦सम्यगभांक्षीः⟧).

⟦गभीरः⟧: ⟦गाध्य प्रतिष्ठालिप्सयोग्रंथे च⟧; from this the suffix īra; ⟦धकारस्य भकार आकारस्य ह्रस्वश्च गभीरगंभीरौ⟧ (⟦उ. ४-३५⟧): by this rule the change of dh to bh and the shortening of ā are effected by nipātana, giving gabhīra and gambhīra. The cit-accent. ⟦सिंधुः⟧: ⟦स्कंदू प्रस्रवणे⟧; ⟦स्कंदेः संप्रसारणं धश्च⟧, saṃprasāraṇa and dh, the suffix u; ⟦संप्रसारणं दकारस्य धकारः⟧, saṃprasāraṇa, and dh for d. ⟦निदित्यनुवृत्तेराम्युदात्तः⟧, by the continuation of nit, it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦परि षंतः⟧: ⟦संतः⟧: the form of ⟦अस⟧ in śatṛ; ⟦उपसर्गप्रादुर्भ्यामस्तिर्य⟧ the ṣatva. ⟦केचित्सरे: क्रियान्वयमिच्छंति⟧: some wish the connection of ⟦परि⟧ with the verb. ⟦तदा षत्वमनुपपन्नं⟧: then the ṣatva is not explained. ⟦परि निपातस्वरः⟧: pari has the nipāta-accent. ⟦संतः⟧: santaḥ has the suffix-accent. ⟦वरंत⟧: ⟦वृञ्⟧ ⟦भौवादिकः⟧, the form in laṅ. Nighāta. ⟦अरुजः⟧: the form in laṅ of the root ⟦रुजो⟧, in the sense of breaking. ⟦गव्यं⟧: ⟦गोर्विकार इत्यर्थे गोपयसोर्यदिति यत्⟧, in the sense "modification of a cow", the suffix yat; by the rule beginning ⟦यतोऽना⟧ it is udātta on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 17

O Indra, ⟦वाजसात्⟧: ⟦वाजस्यान्नस्य सातिर्लाभो यस्मिन् सोऽयं वाजसातिः⟧, that in which there is gain, sāti, of vāja, food: this is vājasāti. ⟦आस्मिन्भरे⟧: ⟦बिभ्रति जयलक्ष्मीमनेन योद्धार इति भरः संग्रामः⟧, those who fight bear the fortune of victory by it, thus bhara is battle. In this battle, ⟦शुनं शूनमुत्साहेन प्रवृद्धं⟧, ⟦शुनं⟧ is swollen, grown with enthusiasm; or else ⟦शुनमिति सुखनाम⟧, ⟦शुनं⟧ is a name of happiness: the maker of happiness; ⟦मघवानं⟧: possessing wealth, and therefore ⟦इंद्रं⟧, endowed with unsurpassed lordship; ⟦नृतमं⟧: the supreme leader of the whole world; you we may call (⟦हुवेम⟧): we Kuśikas may call you for the sake of the sacrifice. Likewise ⟦शृण्वंतमस्माभिः क्रियमाणां स्तुतिं शृण्वंतमुग्रं⟧: hearing the praise being made by us, ⟦उग्रं⟧, terrible to enemies; ⟦समत्तु⟧, in battles, ⟦वृत्राधि⟧ all the rākṣasas marked by Vṛtra, ⟦घ्नंतं⟧, destroying them; ⟦संजितं⟧ **NOTE**{the gloss of ⟦संजितं⟧ in the supplied text is damaged (⟦समग्भेतारं⟧) and its meaning is not certain; the translation says only what the letters allow}, ⟦धनानां⟧, of the wealth belonging to enemies, the one who ⟦समग्भेतारं⟧ you we may call for protection (⟦त्वामूतये रक्षणाय वयमाह्वयेम⟧).

⟦शुनं⟧: ⟦टुओश्वि गतिवृद्योरित्यसृ⟧ in niṣṭhā; ⟦निष्ठायां यस्य विभाषेतीट् प्रतिषेधः⟧, the prohibition of iṭ. ⟦यजादित्वात्संप्रसारणं⟧: saṃprasāraṇa because it belongs to the yajādi group. ⟦दीर्घाभावश्चांदसः⟧: the absence of lengthening is Vedic. Suffix-accent. ⟦हुवेम⟧: ⟦हैयतेर्बहुलं छंदसीति संप्रसारणे कृते शपो लुकि कृते च लिजि रूपं⟧: the form in liṅ after saṃprasāraṇa has been made by "variously in the Veda" and the luk of śap has been made. Nighāta. ⟦भरे⟧: ⟦भृङ् भरणे⟧; gha; ⟦वृषादित्वादाद्युदात्तः⟧, udātta on the first syllable because it belongs to the vṛṣādi group. ⟦वाजसात्⟧: ⟦षणु दाने⟧; ⟦भावे क्तिन्⟧, ktin in the sense of the action; ⟦जनसनेत्यादिनानुनासिकस्यात्वं⟧, ā for the nasal by the rule beginning with ⟦जनसन⟧. ⟦बहुव्रीहौ पूर्वसदस्वरः⟧: in the bahuvrīhi, the accent of the former member. ⟦समत्सु⟧: ⟦आद भक्षणे⟧; kvip after the prefix sam. ⟦संभक्षयंति योद्धाणामायूंषीति समदः संग्रामाः⟧: they devour the lives of fighters, thus samada are battles. ⟦कृदुत्तरपदस्वरः⟧: the accent of the latter member of a kṛt-compound. ⟦घ्नंतं⟧: ⟦हन हिंसागत्योरित्यस्य⟧ in śatṛ, by the rule beginning ⟦गमहनेत्यादिनोपधालोपः⟧ the dropping of the penultimate; ⟦हो हंतेरिति घत्वं⟧, gh by that rule. ⟦संजितं⟧: ⟦जि जये⟧; kvip.
