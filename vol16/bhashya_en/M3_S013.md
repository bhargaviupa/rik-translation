## Introduction

In the second anuvāka there are seventeen sūktas. Of these the first**NOTE**{the supplied text has ādyam for "first" and introduces the Anukramaṇī with atrānukramaṇikā; the reference text has prathamam and tathā cānukrāntam}, of seven Ṛks, beginning ⟦प्र वो देवायेति⟧, has Ṛṣabha, the son of Viśvāmitra, as its seer, Agni as its deity, and is in the Anuṣṭubh metre. On this the Anukramaṇī says: ⟦सप्त ऋषभस्त्वानुष्टुभमिति⟧, "'pra vaḥ': seven; Ṛṣabha, however; in Anuṣṭubh". Ṛṣabha, the son of Viśvāmitra, is the seer. By the general rule about the beginning of a maṇḍala, Agni is the deity.

In the Agniṣṭoma this sūkta is the Ājya-śastra. It is laid down in the sūtra, in the section ⟦इति खंडे⟧ that begins "when the adhvaryu has turned away": ⟦प्र वो देवायेत्याज्यमुपसंतनुयात्⟧, "he should join on the Ājya, 'pra vo devāya'" (⟦आ.५-९⟧).

This same sūkta is employed also in the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra. And the sūtra is: ⟦प्र वो देवायाग्ने कदा त इति⟧ ⟦पंच⟧, "'pra vo devāya' and the five beginning 'agne kadā te'" (⟦आ. ४-१३⟧).

In the Abhyudayeṣṭi, the verse ⟦स यंते⟧, "he, the restrainer", is the yājyā of Agni the giver. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦स यंता विप्र एषां दीर्घस्ते अस्त्वंकुशः⟧, "'he, the restrainer, the wise one, of these', 'long be your goad'" (⟦आ. ३-१३⟧).

## Ṛk 1

The sacrificer speaks to the hotṛ and the others. O hotṛ and you others, to Agni, who is ⟦वो⟧, connected with you, who is ⟦देवाय⟧, shining, ⟦अस्मै⟧, who is to be praised in this rite, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦अर्च⟧, utter, this ⟦बर्हिष्ठं⟧, most ample**NOTE**{the supplied text reads pṛtha-tamaṃ, broken over two lines, as the first gloss of barhiṣṭham; the reference text reads vṛddhatamam, "greatest"}, abundant thing that has the form of praise: the stotra, the śastra and the rest, or the whole class of what belongs to the uktha. Praised in this way, may ⟦सः⟧, that Agni, accompanied by ⟦देवेभिः⟧, by the gods to whom sacrifice is to be offered, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गमत्⟧, come, towards ⟦नः⟧, towards us. And when he has come, may that Agni, ⟦यजिष्ठः⟧, the best of sacrificers, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦सदत्⟧ on the ⟦बर्हिः⟧, on the sacred grass strewn by us on the altar: may he sit down, may he take his seat.

⟦बर्हिष्ठं⟧: from the word barhitṛ the suffix iṣṭhan comes in the sense of the superlative by the rule tuś chandasi. ⟦अर्च⟧: ⟦अर्च पूजायां⟧, the root arc in the sense of honouring; it belongs to the class beginning with bhū. The singular stands for the plural. It loses its accent. ⟦गमत्⟧: the root gamḷ in the sense of going. Of this root the aorist comes in the sense of the imperative by the rule that in the Veda the aorist, the imperfect and the perfect are used. Because the root is marked with ḷ, aṅ takes the place of cli. The i is elided by the rule nityaṃ ṅitaḥ. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda it does not lose its accent. ⟦यजिष्ठः⟧: from the word yaṣṭṛ the suffix iṣṭhan comes by the rule tuś chandasi. By the rule turiṣṭhemeyaḥsu the suffix tṛc is elided. It has the accent of a suffix marked with n. ⟦सदत्⟧: as before, the aorist in the sense of the imperative. Of the root ṣadḷ in the senses of ⟦विशरणगत्यवसादने⟧, breaking apart, going and sinking down, aṅ comes because the root is marked with ḷ. It loses its accent.

## Ṛk 2

⟦यस्य⟧, of which Agni, who was set forth in the preceding mantra, ⟦रोदसी⟧, these two, heaven and earth, are in the power: the meaning is that, since Agni is the illuminator, they are such as have their light dependent on him. Moreover the ⟦ऊशयः⟧, the gods who protect the sacrificer by bestowing the fruit, ⟦सचंते⟧, serve, the ⟦दक्षं⟧, the strength, of which Agni. ⟦सचंते सेवंते⟧, "sacante means they serve" (⟦नि ७-२३⟧), says Yāska. The meaning is that they are dependent on him because Agni is the giver of the oblations given by the sacrificer**NOTE**{the supplied text reads, with misread letters, agner yajamānadattahaviṣāṃ pradātṛtvena; the reference text reads agner yajamānadattahaviṣpradatvena}. That Agni, being such, is ṛtāvā, one whose action is true; the meaning is: one whose resolve is true. Moreover the ⟦हविष्मंतः⟧, the sacrificers who have brought the oblations together, ⟦ईळते⟧ ⟦तं⟧, that Agni alone, for the carrying of the oblation: they honour him with words that have the form of praise. Moreover the ⟦सनिष्यंतः⟧, those who wish for the gaining of wealth and the like, ⟦अवसे⟧, for protection, resort to ⟦तं⟧, to that Agni alone.

⟦ऋतावा⟧: from the word ṛta the suffix vanip comes by ⟦छंदसीवनिपौ वक्तव्याविति⟧, "in the sense 'that belongs to it', ī and vanip are to be stated in the Veda". The long vowel is by the rule "it is seen in others also". Since vanip, being marked with p, is anudātta, the accent is that of the nominal stem. ⟦सचंते⟧: ⟦सच समवाये⟧, the root sac in the sense of being together. The ātmanepada is by interchange. Because it is connected with a form of yad it does not lose its accent. ⟦सनिष्यंतः⟧: sani is gain; "wishing for that". ⟦सुप आत्मनः क्यच्⟧: the suffix kyac comes after a noun in the sense of wishing for oneself. By the statement that the augment suk is to be stated after all nominal stems in the sense of ardent longing, the augment suk comes when kyac follows. The accent is that of kyac.

## Ṛk 3

⟦सः⟧, that Agni, ⟦विप्रः⟧, wise, acquainted with the rite, is, for ⟦एषां⟧, for these sacrificers who have made the pouring out of the oblation with an offence in respect of the time, the one who sets them to it by showing the rite as it has been prescribed. ⟦अथ⟧, moreover, of ⟦यज्ञानां⟧, of the Jyotiṣṭoma and the other sacrifices, that Agni is the restrainer, the controller, by bestowing the fruit. ⟦हि⟧ is used in the sense of what is well known: for that very reason he is indeed the restrainer of all. Moreover that Agni is ⟦दाता⟧, the giver of the fruit of the Jyotiṣṭoma and the other rites. ⟦मघं⟧ is wealth that deserves to be honoured, because of the reading ⟦मघं रेक्ण इति⟧ among the names of wealth**NOTE**{the reference text gives a Nighaṇṭu reference (2.10.1) for maghaṃ rekṇaḥ and one (3.5.5) for namasyati duvasyati; the supplied text has neither}; and he is ⟦यः⟧ ... vanitā, one who bestows that wealth. Such an Agni, who is ⟦वो⟧, connected with you, do you, O priests, ⟦दुवस्यत⟧, attend upon by the offering of oblations. The verb duvasyati has the sense of attending upon, because of the reading ⟦नमस्यति दुवस्यतीति⟧ among the verbs of that sense.

⟦यंता⟧: the form of the root ⟦यम⟧ in the sense of restraining, before tṛc. The substitute anaṅ comes by the rule beginning ṛduśanas. It has the accent of a suffix marked with c. ⟦अथा⟧: the long vowel is by ⟦निपातस्येति⟧. ⟦दुवस्यत⟧: ⟦दुवः परिचरणं⟧, duvas is attendance; "wishing for that"; kyac comes after the noun. It is the form, in the imperative, of the root that ends in that suffix. ⟦दाता⟧: the root dā in the sense of ⟦दाने⟧, giving. The suffix is tṛn in the sense of having that as one's habit. Because the suffix is marked with n it has the udātta on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 4

May ⟦सः⟧, that Agni, to ⟦नः⟧, to us who offer oblations, ⟦यच्छतु⟧, give, ⟦शर्माणि⟧ that are ⟦शंतमा⟧, productive of happiness in the highest degree by the abundance of cattle, sons and the like — the word śarman denotes a house, because of the reading ⟦छाया शर्म वर्मेति⟧ among the names of that**NOTE**{the reference text gives a Nighaṇṭu reference (3.4.21) for chāyā śarma varma; the supplied text has none} — houses of that kind, ⟦वीतये⟧, for resorting to, that is, in order to attain the Agnihotra and the other rites. ⟦यतो⟧, from which Agni, ⟦वसु⟧, wealth that is pruṣṇavat, possessed of abundance, growing exceedingly even when it is spent in giving, enjoying and the like, wealth that has arisen ⟦दिवि⟧, in the world of heaven, ⟦क्षितिभ्यः⟧, from the world of the earth, and ⟦अप्सु⟧, in the mid-region, and that exists in each of those worlds as peculiar to it, ⟦आ⟧, from all sides, comes near. "That Agni, through whose favour the wealth that exists in the three worlds comes to us": such is the connection with what precedes.

⟦वीतये⟧: the root ⟦वी⟧ in the senses of going, desiring and the rest. The suffix ktin comes by the rule beginning ⟦मंत्रे वृषेषपचे⟧. Because it is laid down ready-made as udātta, it is udātta. ⟦यच्छतु⟧: ⟦यम उपरमे⟧, the root yam in the sense of restraining. By ⟦इषुगमियमां छ इति⟧ ch takes the place of the m. ⟦पुष्पवत्⟧: the root, in the senses of ⟦स्नेहनसेचनपूरणेषु⟧, moistening, sprinkling and filling**NOTE**{the supplied text cites the root as puṣpa, the reference text as puṣa; the word explained is pruṣṇavat}. After it comes the Uṇādi suffix nak. The suffix matup comes in the sense "that belongs to it". Its not being udātta is Vedic.

## Ṛk 5

The ⟦ऋक्वाणः⟧, those who make praise, the possessors of the śastra, the hotṛ and the others, ⟦अग्निं⟧, that Agni, who is ⟦दीदिवांसं⟧, shining; apūrvya, new at every moment by reason of his brilliance; ⟦होतारं⟧, the caller of the gods; ⟦विशां⟧ ⟦विश्वतिं⟧, the protector, in the highest degree, of creatures: him, by ⟦वस्वीभिः⟧, excellent, ⟦अस्य⟧ ⟦धीतिभिः⟧, words characterised as praise which has Agni for its object — or else by ⟦वस्वीभिः⟧, possessed of wealth, ⟦अस्य⟧ ⟦धीतिभिः⟧, rites that are being performed with him in view — they ⟦इंथते⟧, kindle: by the activity of making the stotra, offering the oblation and the like, they bring about his thriving.

⟦दीदिवांसं⟧: the form of the root ⟦दिवु⟧ in the sense of playing and the rest, before kvasu. The reduplication is lengthened by ⟦तुजादीनामि⟧. The elision is by ⟦लोपो व्योरिति⟧**NOTE**{the supplied text reads lopo vyor iti lopaḥ; the reference text reads lopo vyor vali iti valopaḥ, "elision of v"}. ⟦वस्वीभिः⟧: from the word vasu the suffix ṅīn comes by interchange. It has the accent of a suffix marked with n. ⟦धीतिभिः⟧: ⟦धेट् पाने⟧, the root dhe in the sense of drinking. The suffix ktic comes by ⟦संज्ञायामिति⟧, the rule that ktic and kta come in a name. Because the suffix is marked with k, ī comes by the rule ghumāsthā. It has the accent of a suffix marked with c. ⟦ऋक्वाणः⟧: from the word ṛc the suffix vanip comes in the sense of matup. Because the word belongs to the group beginning with ayasmaya and so has the designation bha, the guttural comes in. Because it has not the designation pada, there is no change to a soft unaspirated consonant. ⟦इंथते⟧: the root ñiindhī in the sense of ⟦दीप्तौ⟧, shining. ⟦विश्वतिं⟧: the udātta stands on the first syllable of the second member by the rule parādiś chandasi bahulam. ⟦विशां⟧: the case-ending is udātta by ⟦सावेकाच इति⟧.

## Ṛk 6

⟦उत⟧, and also, O Agni, ⟦नः⟧, us, who perform the sacrifice, ⟦ब्रह्मन्⟧, in the stotra that is being performed by us, ⟦अविषः⟧: protect in such a way that there is no fault of deficiency or excess in respect of the stotra. Moreover, you who are ⟦देवहूतमः⟧, the best caller of the gods, in the ukthas, in the Ājya, the Prauga and the other śastras that are being performed by us, ⟦अविषः⟧: guard us. Moreover, you who are marudvṛdha, growing by the Maruts, and ⟦सहस्रसातमः⟧, the giver, in the highest degree, of wealth a thousand in number, ⟦शोच⟧, make shine, ⟦शं⟧, the happiness of this world and of the other world, ⟦नः⟧, of ours: the meaning is "make it grow".

⟦अविषः⟧: from the root ⟦अव⟧ in the senses of protecting and the rest, in leṭ, sip comes by ⟦सिब्बहुलमिति⟧. Because this is ārdhadhātuka, the augment iṭ comes. By ⟦इतश्च लोप इति⟧ the i of sip is elided. The augment aṭ comes by ⟦लेटोऽडाटावित्यडागमः⟧. It loses its accent. ⟦उक्षेषु⟧: ⟦वच परिभाषणे⟧, the root vac in the sense of speaking. After it the suffix thak comes in the sense of the object by the rule beginning pātṝtudivacirici. Because the suffix is marked with k there is saṃprasāraṇa. It has the accent of the suffix. ⟦देवहूतमः⟧: kvip comes after the root hvayati. By ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧ there are saṃprasāraṇa and the single substitution of the latter vowel. The long vowel is by ⟦हल इति⟧. It has the accent by which the second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦शोच⟧: ⟦शुच दीप्तौ⟧, the root śuc in the sense of shining. This root has here the sense of the causative included in it. It loses its accent. ⟦मरुद्वधः⟧: ⟦वृधु वर्धने⟧, the root vṛdh in the sense of growing. The suffix is ka, the one conditioned by a penultimate ik vowel. Because the word belongs to the group beginning with marudvṛdha, the first member has the udātta on its last syllable. ⟦सातमः⟧, with sahasra: ⟦षणु दाने⟧, the root san in the sense of giving. After it comes viṭ by ⟦जनसनखनक्रमगमो विडिति⟧. The ā comes by the rule viḍvanor anunāsikasya. The accent is that by which the second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent.

## Ṛk 7

O Agni, you who are being praised in this sūkta: ⟦वसु⟧, wealth characterised as jewels, gold, cattle and the like, which is ⟦सहस्रवत्⟧, furnished with the number of a thousand; ⟦तोकवत्⟧, accompanied by sons, grandsons and the like; ⟦पुष्टिमत्⟧, causing nourishment — by this are implied cows and the like, which give strength and health to the body by way of milk and the rest; ⟦द्युमत्⟧, having brightness — by this jewels, gold and the like are included; ⟦सुवीर्यं⟧, furnished with excellent capacity, fit for use in this and that business; ⟦वर्षिष्ठं⟧, grown exceedingly, abundant, and for that very reason ⟦अनुपक्षितं⟧, not wasted away even when expenditure is being made: wealth of this kind ⟦रास्व⟧, give, ⟦नः⟧, to us, ⟦नु⟧, quickly, without delay.

⟦नू नः⟧: the long vowel is by the rule beginning ṛci tunughamakṣu. ⟦रास्व⟧: the root ⟦रा⟧ in the sense of giving; it is its form in the imperative. The ātmanepada is by interchange. Because the root belongs to the class beginning with ad, śap is elided. It loses its accent. ⟦सहस्रवत्⟧: by ⟦मादुपधाया इति⟧ v takes the place of the m of matup. The word sahasra has the udātta on its middle syllable because it belongs to the group beginning with kardama. ⟦पुष्टिमत्⟧: the root ⟦पुष⟧ in the sense of nourishing. After it comes ktic. By the accent of the suffix it has the udātta on the last syllable. The matup is udātta by the rule hrasvanuḍbhyām. ⟦वर्षिष्ठं⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦प्रियस्थिरे⟧ the substitute ⟦वर्षि⟧ comes in place of vṛddha. It has the accent of a suffix marked with n. ⟦अनुपक्षितं⟧: of the root ⟦क्षि क्षय⟧, kṣi in the sense of wasting away, because there is no long vowel, the n of the niṣṭhā does not come in. Because it is a tatpuruṣa, the accent is that by which the first member, an indeclinable, keeps its original accent.
