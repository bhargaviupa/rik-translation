## Introduction
The sūkta beginning ⟦तिष्ठा हरी⟧ has eleven Ṛks; it is the sixth sūkta, belonging to Viśvāmitra, in the triṣṭubh metre, addressed to Indra. So says the Anukramaṇikā: ⟦तिष्ठा हरी⟧. In the Agniṣṭut (⟦अग्निष्टुति⟧), in the Marutvatīya śastra, it is the sūkta in which the nivid is set (⟦निविद्वानं⟧). And it is taught in the sūtra: ⟦तिष्ठा हरी यो जात एवेति मध्यंदिनः⟧ (⟦आ. ९-७⟧), that is, the sūkta beginning ⟦तिष्ठा हरी⟧ and the one beginning ⟦यो जात एव⟧ are for the midday.

In the Ekāha named the Kulāya of Indra and Agni, too, this is the sūkta in which the nivid is set, in the Marutvatīya. And it is taught in the sūtra: ⟦तिष्ठा हरी तमु ष्टुही⟧ — thus the midday (⟦आ. ९-७⟧).

In the samūḍha (⟦समूढे⟧) Daśarātra, in the third Chandoma, this sūkta is used in the Marutvatīya śastra. And it is taught in the sūtra: ⟦तिष्ठा हरी प्र मुंदिने इमा उ त्वेति मरुत्वतीयं⟧ (⟦आ. ८-७⟧).

In the Atirātra, in the third paryāya, when the hotṛ's śastra has been concluded, the first Ṛk, this one beginning ⟦तिष्ठा हरी⟧, is the yājyā of the śastra. And it is taught in the sūtra: ⟦विश्वजिते तिष्ठा हरी रथ आ युज्यमानेति याज्या⟧ (⟦आ. ६-४⟧).

## Ṛk 1
Viśvāmitra praises. O Indra, with regard to the ⟦हरी⟧, the two horses, ⟦युज्यमाना⟧, that are being yoked together, ⟦रथे⟧, to the chariot, stay (⟦तिष्ठ⟧) for some time. The illustration for this is ⟦वायुर्न⟧: as Vāyu stays for some time with regard to the ⟦नियुतः⟧, the mares, that are being yoked to his chariot, so do you. Then, mounted on the chariot, come (⟦याहि⟧) ⟦अच्छ⟧, towards, ⟦नो⟧, us; that is, come quickly. And having come, ⟦अभिसृष्टो⟧, permitted, ⟦अस्मे⟧, by us, drink (⟦पिबासि⟧) the ⟦अंधः⟧, the food that has the form of Soma. ⟦स्वाहा⟧: and this Soma has been given. For ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦मदाय⟧, delight, we give (⟦ररिम⟧) this Soma that has been offered with svāhā.

Here, in ⟦याहि वायुर्न नियुतो नो अच्छ⟧, the meaning is "come with the chariot". In the passage ⟦आपत्तित्पिप्युः स्तर्यो न गावः⟧ (even the waters have swelled, like barren cows) (⟦ऋग्वं ७-२३-४⟧) it is not so; for that reason these words are not left out at the time of the Pada recitation.

⟦तिष्ठ⟧: the form of the verb tiṣṭhati in loṭ; the substitute tiṣṭha has the first syllable udātta. ⟦याहि⟧: the form of the verb yāti in loṭ; because the ending hi is marked ṅ, the final syllable is udātta. ⟦वायुः⟧: from the root vā, in the senses of going and harming, by the Uṇādi rule beginning ⟦कृवापे⟧ the suffix uṇ; then the augment yuk is added after the ā, by ⟦आतो युक्⟧; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦पिबासि⟧: the form of the verb pāti in leṭ, with the augment āḍ; since the augment is anudātta, the accent is that of the root. ⟦अंधः⟧: from the root ad, in the sense of eating, by ⟦अदेर्नु० धश्च⟧ the suffix asun, the augment num, and dh as the substitute for the final letter. He explains it: ⟦आद्यते⟧, it is eaten by living beings, or it itself eats (⟦स्वयमत्ती⟧). And so the śruti says: ⟦अद्यतेऽत्ति च भूतानि तस्मादन्नं तदुच्यते⟧ (⟦त्व उ. २-२⟧), "It is eaten, and it eats beings; therefore that is called food." The accent is the nit-accent. ⟦अस्मे⟧: by ⟦सुपां सुलुग⟧ the instrumental ending is replaced by śe. ⟦ररिम⟧: the form of the root rā in liṭ; because the sentence is divided, there is no nighāta; the accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 2
O Indra, for you, ⟦पुरुहूताय⟧, who are called to the sacrifice by many, that is, by numerous sacrificers, I yoke (⟦उप⟧ … ⟦युनज्मि⟧) at the ⟦धूर्षु⟧, the ends of the yoke, of the chariot (⟦रथस्य⟧) the ⟦हरी⟧, the two horses named Hari, which are ⟦अजिरा⟧, of a nature to go swiftly, and ⟦सप्ति⟧, of a nature to glide. I yoke them so ("so" is to be supplied) that (⟦यथा⟧) that chariot may go (⟦द्रवत्⟧). To this ⟦यज्ञं⟧, sacrifice, which is ⟦संभृतं⟧, made complete ⟦विश्वतश्चित्⟧, on every side, with the requisites of sacrifice spoken of in ⟦अग्निर्यजुर्भिः सविता स्तोमै⟧ (Agni with the yajus formulas, Savitṛ with the stomas) and so on, may they, those two horses, bring (⟦उप⟧ … ⟦वहातः⟧) ⟦इंद्रं⟧, you; that is, may they bring you well.

⟦धूर्षु⟧: from the root dhurvī, in the sense of harming, the suffix kvip; the dropping of the letter v by the rule ⟦राल्लोप⟧; the lengthening by the rule ⟦र्वोरुप धाया⟧; by ⟦सावेकाच⟧ the case-ending is udātta. ⟦युनज्मि⟧: the root yujir, in the sense of joining, of the rudh class; the form in laṭ; the accent is nighāta. ⟦द्रवत्⟧: from the root dru, in the sense of going, the form in the Vedic laṅ; by vyatyaya the final syllable is udātta. ⟦वहातः⟧: the form of the root vah in leṭ, with the augment āḍ.

## Ṛk 3
O ⟦वृषभ⟧, showerer of desires, ⟦स्वधावः⟧, possessor of food, O Indra, bring up (⟦उपो नयस्व⟧) the two horses that are ⟦वृषणा⟧, able to sprinkle, and ⟦तपुष्पा⟧, protectors from the enemies who torment; that is, bring them near to us. ⟦उत⟧, and also, protect (⟦अव⟧) ⟦ईं⟧, this sacrificer. Unyoke (⟦वि मुच⟧) those two horses, which are ⟦शोणा⟧, of red colour, ⟦इह⟧, here at this place of sacrifice to the gods. Unyoked, let those two horses eat (⟦ग्रसेतां⟧); that is, let them feed on their fodder (⟦विघसं⟧). But you, eat (⟦अद्धि⟧) ⟦दिवेदिवे⟧, every day, the ⟦धानाः⟧, roasted barley grains, that are ⟦सदृशीः⟧, of one form.

⟦नयस्व⟧: the form of the verb nayati in loṭ. ⟦तपुष्पा⟧: the root pā, in the sense of protecting; the suffix ka after a root ending in ā when there is no preverb; by ⟦सुपां सुलुग⟧ the substitute ḍā; by the rule ⟦इसुसोः सामर्थ्य⟧, in the saṃhitā the visarjanīya becomes ṣ; the accent is that in which a second member ending in a kṛt suffix keeps its own accent. ⟦अव⟧: the form of the verb avati in loṭ. ⟦स्वधावः⟧: by ⟦मतुवसो⟧ the letter n is replaced by ru; the accent is nighāta. ⟦ग्रसेतां⟧: from the root grasu, in the sense of eating; the form in loṭ, in the ending ātām; because it stands at the beginning of the pāda there is no nighāta; as to the ending ātām, in the matter of the accent of the sārvadhātuka l-ending, the accent is that of the root. ⟦मुच⟧: the form of the verb muñcati in loṭ; since the teaching of augments is not constant, there is no num. ⟦सदृशीः⟧: when the word samāna stands as the upapada, from the root dṛśir, in the sense of seeing, the suffix kañ by ⟦समानान्ययोश्च⟧; the substitution of sa for samāna by ⟦समानस्य दृग्दश्वतुष्टिति⟧; the feminine suffix ṅīp (⟦जेप्⟧) by the rule beginning ⟦टिडाणञ्⟧; since it is marked p, it is anudātta, and so the accent is that of the root. ⟦अद्धि⟧: from the root ad, in the sense of eating, in loṭ; because the root is of the adādi class, śap is dropped; by ⟦हुरुल्भो हेर्धि⟧ the substitute dhi (for hi); the accent is nighāta. ⟦धानाः⟧: the root ḍudhāñ, in the senses of holding and nourishing; the suffix na by ⟦धाप्नवस्यज्यतिभ्यो न⟧; the accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 4
On the Cāturviṃśika day, in the midday pressing, in the śastra of the Brāhmaṇācchaṃsin, the verse beginning ⟦ब्रह्मणा ते⟧ is the ārambhaṇīyā. And it is taught in the sūtra: ⟦ब्रह्मणा ते ब्रह्मयुजा युनज्म्युरुं नो लोकमनुनेषि विद्वान्⟧ (⟦आ- ७-४⟧), "With the brahman I yoke your two brahman-yoked horses; knowing, you will lead us to the wide world."

O Indra, your (⟦ते⟧) ⟦हरी⟧, two horses, which are ⟦ब्रह्मयुजा⟧, to be yoked by brahman, that is, by a mantra, and which ⟦सधमादे⟧ (warriors exult, ⟦माद्यंति⟧, in it, and so māda is battle; sadhamāda is ⟦सहमादः⟧), in that battle, are ⟦सखाया⟧, of equal renown, and for that very reason ⟦आशू⟧, endowed with swift going: them I yoke (⟦युनज्मि⟧) ⟦ब्रह्मणा⟧, with a hymn of praise; that is, I join them to the chariot. ⟦रथमधितिष्ठन्⟧, having mounted the chariot, one such as is ⟦स्थिरं⟧, very firm, and ⟦सुखं⟧, having a good opening, come for the drinking of the Soma. What begins with ⟦प्रजानन्⟧ has gone before. **NOTE**{agamat, "has gone", is taken to mean that these words have been explained before; the reference text reads the same}

⟦ब्रह्मयुजा⟧: from the root yuj, the Uṇādi suffix kvip, in the sense of the object. ⟦युनज्मि⟧: the form of the root yuj in laṭ; the accent is nighāta. **NOTE**{the reference text has "liṭ" here where the supplied text has "laṭ"} ⟦सधमादे⟧: the root madī, in the sense of joy; the suffix ghañ in the sense of the locus; by the rule ⟦सध मादस्थयोष्पंदसी⟧ the substitute sadha for saha; the accent is that in which a second member ending in a kṛt suffix keeps its own accent. ⟦रथं⟧: by the rule ⟦अधिशीज्‌स्थासां कर्म⟧ it has the designation karman, object. ⟦सुखं⟧: the root khanu, in the sense of splitting; by ⟦अन्वेभ्योऽपि दृश्यत⟧ the suffix ḍa; the accent is that of a second member ending in a kṛt suffix.

## Ṛk 5
O Indra, let not ⟦अन्ये⟧, others than we, ⟦यजमानासः⟧, sacrificers, delight (⟦मा नि रीरमन्⟧) ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦हरी⟧, two horses, which are ⟦वृषणा⟧, sprinklers of desires, and ⟦वीतपृष्ठा⟧, of lovely backs; that is, let them not gladden them. But we, with Soma juices (⟦सोमैः⟧) that are ⟦सुतेभिः⟧, pressed, would make (⟦कृणवाम⟧) ⟦अरं⟧, enough, for you (⟦ते⟧); the meaning is that we act so that you are satisfied. But you, ⟦अत्यायाहि⟧ ⟦शश्वतः⟧: passing beyond the many others, come quickly for the drinking of the Soma.

⟦वीतपृष्ठा⟧: the root vī, in the senses beginning with kānti, desire; from it the suffix kta; in a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member in its natural form. ⟦रीरमन्⟧: the root ramu, in the sense of play, in its causative, the form in luṅ with caṅ; by the rule ⟦न माज्योग⟧ there is absence of the augment aḍ; the accent is nighāta. ⟦अत्यायाहि⟧: the form of the verb yāti in loṭ; by the rule ⟦गतिर्गता⟧ the preceding word has nighāta. ⟦कृणवाम⟧: the form of the verb kṛṇoti in loṭ; the accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 6
⟦तवायं सोम⟧ is, in the midday pressing, the prasthita-yājyā of the Neṣṭṛ. And it is taught in the sūtra: ⟦तनायं सोमस्य मेह्यर्नाजिंद्राय सोमाः प्रदिवः⟧ (⟦आ. ५-५⟧).

O Indra, this Soma is ⟦तव⟧, yours, pressed for your sake. Come (⟦इहि⟧) ⟦अर्वाज्⟧, facing the Soma; that is, come here. And having come, becoming ⟦सुमनाः⟧, of good mind, drink (⟦पाहि⟧) ⟦अस्य⟧, this Soma, ⟦शश्वत्तमं⟧, in great abundance. Having sat down (⟦निषद्य⟧), that is, having taken your seat, at this sacrifice (⟦अस्मिन्⟧ ⟦यज्ञे⟧), which is ⟦बर्हिषि⟧, grown great, hold (⟦दधिष्ट⟧) in the belly (⟦जठरे⟧) this Soma, the ⟦इंदुं⟧, whose nature is to moisten. Hold it so that it does not go below the navel.

In the passage ⟦अंगिरोभिरागहि यज्जियेभिः⟧ (come with the Aṅgirases, who are worthy of sacrifice) (⟦ऋग्वॆ. १०-१४-५⟧) barhis is not an epithet of the sacrifice, or else that passage is of a different form; for this reason these words are not left out.

⟦इहि⟧: the root iṇ, in the sense of going; the form of it in loṭ; the accent is nighāta. ⟦शश्वत्तमं⟧: because it belongs to the group beginning with uñcha, the final syllable is udātta. ⟦पाहि⟧: the root pā, in the sense of drinking, the form in loṭ; by the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧ śap is dropped; the accent is nighāta. ⟦निषद्य⟧: the form of the root sad in lyap; by the rule ⟦सदेरप्रते⟧ the change to ṣ; the accent is the lit-accent. ⟦दधिष्ट⟧: the form of the verb dadhāti in loṭ; by the rule ⟦छंदस्युभयथ⟧, since the ending thās is ārdhadhātuka, there is the augment iṭ; the accent is that of the suffix. **NOTE**{the reference text has only the word here and lacks this explanation of the augment} ⟦इंदुं⟧: the root undī, in the sense of moistening; by the rule ⟦उन्पेरिच्चादे⟧ the suffix u; the substitution of i for the initial letter of the root; because of the continuation of "nit", the first syllable is udātta.

## Ṛk 7
O Indra, for you (⟦ते⟧) to sit on, the ⟦बर्हिः⟧ is ⟦स्तीर्णं⟧, spread out. And likewise the Soma is ⟦सुतः⟧, pressed, for your sake. For your (⟦ते⟧) two horses (⟦हरिभ्यां⟧) ⟦अत्तवे⟧, to eat, the ⟦धानाः⟧, roasted barley grains, are ⟦कृताः⟧, made ready. ⟦तदोकसे⟧: to him whose ⟦ओक⟧, abode, is that barhis; ⟦पुरुशाकाय⟧, who is to be praised by many, or else who has many helpers; ⟦वृष्णे⟧, the showerer of desires; ⟦मरुत्वते⟧, who has with him the Maruts, the Maruts being the clans of the gods: to you (⟦तुभ्यं⟧), who are such, the ⟦हवींषि⟧, the oblations, Soma, ghee and the rest, are ⟦राता⟧, given by us. The purport is: partake of them. **NOTE**{the reference text reads devavitaḥ where the supplied text has ⟦देवविशः⟧; the translation follows the supplied text}

⟦स्तीर्णं⟧: the root stṛñ, in the sense of covering; by the rule ⟦ऋत इद्दातो⟧ the substitution of i; by the rule ⟦रदाभ्याम⟧ the n of the niṣṭhā ending; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अत्तवे⟧: the root ad, in the sense of eating, in the sense of the infinitive, the suffix taven; the accent is the nit-accent. ⟦तदोकसे⟧: in a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member. ⟦पुरुशाकाय⟧: the root śac, in the sense of clear speech, in the sense of the object the suffix ghañ; by the rule ⟦चजोः कु घिण्यतो⟧ the change to a guttural; the accent is that of a second member ending in a kṛt suffix. ⟦मरुत्वते⟧: by the rule ⟦तसौ मत्त्वर्थ⟧ it has the designation bha, and so there is no change of the letter t to its voiced counterpart; because the suffix is marked p, it is anudātta, and so the accent is that of the nominal stem. ⟦राता⟧: the root rā, in the sense of giving, the suffix kta in the sense of the object; the accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 8
O Indra, the ⟦नरः⟧, the leaders of the rites, the adhvaryu and the others, the ⟦पर्वताः⟧, the pressing stones, and the ⟦आपः⟧, the waters: all these, coming together, have ⟦तुभ्यं⟧, for your sake, made (⟦समक्रन्⟧) this Soma ⟦मधुमंतं⟧, endowed with sweetness, with ⟦गोभिः⟧, milk; that is, they have made it well. O ⟦ऋष्व⟧, worth seeing, O Indra, ⟦सुमनाः⟧, of good mind, ⟦विद्वान्⟧, versed in the rites, recognising (⟦अनु⟧ ⟦प्रजानन्⟧) the praises that are ⟦स्वाः⟧, your own, and ⟦पथ्याः⟧, good on the Vedic path, that is, in the rite, drink ⟦तस्य⟧; that is, drink this pressed Soma.

⟦अक्रन्⟧: the form of the verb karoti in luṅ; the accent is nighāta. ⟦आगत्य⟧: the form of the root gam in lyap; the accent is the lit-accent. ⟦पाहि⟧: the form of the verb pāti in loṭ.

## Ṛk 9
O Indra, the Maruts ⟦यान्⟧, whom, you honoured (⟦अभजः⟧) ⟦सोमे⟧, in the matter of the drinking of Soma; and the Maruts ⟦ये⟧, who, made you grow (⟦त्वां⟧ ⟦अवर्धन्⟧), that is, made you grow in battle by rousing your ardour; and the Maruts who became (⟦अभवन्⟧) ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦गणः⟧, troop, the meaning being that they became your helpers: O Indra, being ⟦सजोषाः⟧, united, ⟦तेभिः⟧, with those Maruts, who are such, and ⟦वावशानः⟧, desiring, ⟦एतं⟧, this pressed Soma, drink it with the ⟦जिह्वया⟧, the tongue that has the form of flame, ⟦अग्नेः⟧, of the Āhavanīya, which is the place where the oblation is laid. **NOTE**{the reference text reads juhvayā, "with the ladle", where the supplied text has ⟦जिह्वया⟧, "with the tongue"}

⟦अभजः⟧: the root bhaj, in the sense of serving, the form in laṅ; because of the connection with the relative there is no nighāta. ⟦अवर्धन्⟧: the root vṛdhu, in the sense of growth, in its causative, the form in laṅ; by the rule ⟦छंदस्युभयथ⟧, because the ending jhi is ārdhadhātuka, by the rule ⟦णॆरनिटी⟧ there is the loss of ṇi; here too, because of the connection with the relative, there is no nighāta. ⟦अभवन्⟧: because the sentence is divided, there is no nighāta. ⟦वावशानः⟧: the root vaś, in the sense of kānti, desire, the form with yaṅluk and the suffix cānaś; the accent is the cit-accent. ⟦पिब⟧: the root pā, in the sense of drinking; the accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 10
O Indra, ⟦स्वधया⟧: svadhā is strength, since it sustains (⟦दधाति⟧), that is, nourishes, ⟦स्वमात्मानं⟧, its own self; with that strength. ⟦चित्⟧ has the sense of an alternative. Having taken it away by force, drink ⟦सुतस्य⟧, the pressed Soma, or the offered. O ⟦यजत्र⟧, O Indra who are to be worshipped, or (⟦वा⟧) drink (⟦पाहि⟧) the Soma offered with the ⟦जिह्वया⟧, the tongue that has the form of flame, ⟦अग्नेः⟧, of Agni. O ⟦शक्र⟧, O able Indra, or drink the Soma that is ⟦प्रयतं⟧, begun to be given, ⟦हस्तात्⟧, from the hand, ⟦अध्वर्योः⟧, of the adhvaryu. Or else partake of (⟦जुषस्व⟧) what is ⟦होतुः⟧, the hotṛ's, the ⟦यज्ञं⟧, that which is to be sacrificed, accompanied by the vaṣaṭ call, the portion ⟦हविषः⟧, of the oblation. The purport is: in every case, come here and drink the Soma that is being given by us. **NOTE**{the reference text has "sumatam" where the supplied text has ⟦सुतमभिषुतं⟧, and a different word where the supplied text has ⟦वषट्कारोपेतं⟧}

⟦पिब⟧: because the vocative is treated as non-existent, and the verb stands at the beginning of the pāda, there is no nighāta. ⟦अध्वर्योः⟧: "he desires adhvara", with the suffix kyac; by the rule ⟦कव्यध्वर⟧ there is the loss of a; the accent is that of the suffix; then the accent of the single substitute. ⟦प्रयतं⟧: the root yama, in the sense of ceasing; the niṣṭhā suffix in the sense of the object. ⟦शक्र⟧: the root śaklṛ, in the sense of power; by the rule beginning ⟦स्थायितंची⟧ the suffix rak. ⟦हस्तात्⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦असिहसी⟧ the suffix tan; the accent is the nit-accent. ⟦होतुः⟧: from the verb juhoti, the suffix tṛn in its sense of habit; the accent is the nit-accent. ⟦जुषस्व⟧: the root juṣī, in the senses of liking and serving, the form in loṭ; the accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 11
O Indra, ⟦वाजसातौ⟧: that in which there is ⟦साति⟧, gain, of ⟦वाज⟧, food, is this vājasāti. In ⟦अस्मिन्⟧ ⟦भरे⟧: bhara is battle, since by it warriors bear (⟦बिभ्रति⟧) the fortune of victory. In that battle we would call (⟦हुवेम⟧) you, who are ⟦शुनं⟧, swollen, that is, grown great through zeal (or else śuna is a name of happiness: the giver of happiness), ⟦मघवानं⟧, wealthy, and for that very reason ⟦इंद्रं⟧, endowed with unsurpassed lordship, and ⟦नृतमं⟧, in the highest degree the leader of the whole world; that is, we, the Kuśikas, would call you for the sake of the sacrifice. Likewise, for ⟦ऊतये⟧, protection, we would call you, who are ⟦शृण्वंतं⟧, hearing the praise that is being made by us, ⟦उग्रं⟧, terrible to enemies, ⟦घ्नंतं⟧, slaying, ⟦समत्सु⟧, in battles, the ⟦वृत्राणि⟧, all the rākṣasas that are implied by Vṛtra, and ⟦संजितं⟧, the thorough conqueror, ⟦धनानां⟧, of the riches belonging to enemies. **NOTE**{the reference text reads sūnam where the supplied text has ⟦शूनमुत्साहेन⟧}

⟦शुनं⟧: the root ṭuośvi, in the senses of going and growth; in the niṣṭhā suffix, by the rule ⟦यस्य विभाष⟧ there is prohibition of iṭ; because the root belongs to the yajādi group, saṃprasāraṇa; the absence of lengthening is Vedic; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦हुवेम⟧: the verb hvayati; by the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧ saṃprasāraṇa having been done, and the dropping of śap having been done, the form is in liṅ; the accent is nighāta. ⟦भरे⟧: the root bhṛñ, in the sense of bearing; the suffix gha; because it belongs to the vṛṣādi group, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦वाजसातौ⟧: the root ṣaṇu, in the sense of giving; in the sense of the abstract noun the suffix ktin; by the rule beginning ⟦जनसन⟧ the nasal is replaced by ā; in a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member. ⟦समत्सु⟧: the root ad, in the sense of eating; after sam the suffix kvip; ⟦संभक्षयंति⟧: they consume the lives of the warriors; thus ⟦समदः⟧ are battles; the accent is that of a second member ending in a kṛt suffix. ⟦घ्नंतं⟧: the root han, in the senses of harming and going; in śatṛ, by the rule beginning ⟦गमहन⟧ the loss of the penultimate; by ⟦हो हंते⟧ the change to gh. ⟦संजितं⟧: the root ji, in the sense of conquering; the suffix kvip.
