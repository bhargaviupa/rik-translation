## Introduction

The third sūkta, of seven Ṛks, beginning ⟦वि पाजसा पृथुनेति⟧, has as its seer Utkīla, born in the gotra of Kata; it is in the Triṣṭubh metre and is addressed to Agni. On this the Anukramaṇī is as follows: ⟦वि पाजसा कात्य उत्कीलस्त्विति⟧, "'vi pājasā': Utkīla, the descendant of Kata, however". The seer is the one named Utkīla, born in the gotra of Kata.

In the Prātaranuvāka and the Āśvina-śastra the group of ten sūktas beginning ⟦होते⟧, "ā hotā", is employed; and among them this is the second sūkta.

## Ṛk 1

Utkīla praises. O Agni, you who are ⟦शोशुचानः⟧, shining exceedingly, with a ⟦पृथुना⟧, with a wide, ⟦पाजसा⟧, light, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦बाधस्व⟧, destroy utterly, the ⟦द्विषः⟧, the enemies who make obstacles to the performance of the rite. Likewise the ⟦रक्षसः⟧ that are ⟦अमीवाः⟧, endowed with capacity through freedom from disease — in both words there is an interchange of case-endings — those Rakṣases ⟦बाधस्व⟧: drive away those makers of obstacles to the rite. Or else ⟦अमीवाः⟧: harass also the female Piśācas, who are intent on coming on face to face and on going away. Of ⟦अग्नेः⟧, of Agni, who is ⟦सुशर्मणः⟧, the cause of excellent happiness characterised as heaven and the like; who is ⟦बृहतः⟧, great, who has attained greatness by victory over the enemies; who is ⟦सुहवस्य⟧, furnished with good invocation: in the ⟦शर्मणि⟧, in what is the cause of our happiness, in the ⟦प्रणीतौ⟧, in the carrying forward to places such as the uttaravedi — carrying forward in an eminent way is bringing to this place and that and depositing there — in such a carrying forward of you ⟦अहं⟧ ⟦स्यां⟧: may I be in the performance of a rite that is preceded by the carrying forward of Agni.

⟦पाजसा⟧: the root ⟦पा⟧ in the sense of protecting. While "asun" continues, asun comes by the rule that after pā, in the sense of strength, asun comes and also the augment juṭ; there is the augment juṭ. Because the suffix is marked with n it has the udātta on the first syllable. ⟦पृथुना⟧: ⟦प्रथ⟧ in the sense of becoming known. While "u" continues, the suffix u comes by the rule that there is saṃprasāraṇa of prath, mrad and bhrasj. The saṃprasāraṇa comes in conjunction with that suffix. It has the accent of the suffix. ⟦शोशुचानः⟧: of the root ⟦शुच⟧ in the sense of shining, in the elision of yaṅ, the reduplication takes guṇa by the rule on guṇa in yaṅ and in its elision. The suffix śānac comes by interchange. Because the form called carkarīta is treated like a root of the class beginning with ad, śap is elided. Though the udātta on the last syllable would result because the suffix is marked with c, the udātta stands on the first syllable by ⟦भ्यस्तानामादिरित्याद्युदात्तत्वं⟧, "of reduplicated stems the first". ⟦बाधस्व⟧: it is the form, in the imperative, of the root bādh in the sense of harassing. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda there is no loss of accent. ⟦सुशर्मणः⟧: the root ⟦शृ⟧ in the sense of injuring. "It destroys pain": hence the Uṇādi suffix manin. The second member has the udātta on its first syllable by ⟦सोर्मनसी⟧. ⟦स्यां⟧: of the root ⟦अस⟧ in the sense of being, in the optative, the augment yāsuṭ comes by the rule beginning yāsuṭ parasmaipadeṣu. The s is elided by the rule that the s of the optative is elided. Because yāsuṭ is marked with ṅ, the a is elided by ⟦श्नसोरल्लोपः⟧. It loses its accent.

## Ṛk 2

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦त्वं⟧, do you, at the ⟦व्युष्टौ⟧, at the dawning, the shining forth, of ⟦उषसो⟧, of the dawn, ⟦अस्या⟧, of this one that is present today, and when the ⟦सूरे⟧, the sun, is ⟦उदिते⟧, risen, ⟦बोधि⟧, know: "I am the ⟦गोपाः⟧, the protector, of ⟦नः⟧, of us who are engaged in the Agnihotra and the other rites". Some offer the Agnihotra before the rising of the sun; some offer when it has risen. The meaning is: be the guardian of both of these. ⟦सुजात⟧, with ⟦तन्ना⟧: O you who are well born, brightly shining, with a body surrounded by flames. Or else, with ⟦तन्ना⟧, well born: well born by himself alone; the meaning is, self-existent. Being such, ⟦जुषस्व⟧, ⟦नित्यं⟧, ⟦मे⟧, my, ⟦स्तोमं⟧, the stotra characterised as the Trivṛt, the Pañcadaśa and so on. For this there is an illustration, ⟦जन्मेवेति⟧: as a ⟦जन्म⟧, a begetter, a father, cherishes his ⟦तनयं⟧, his own-born son, with embraces and the like, in the same way cherish this stotra that is being made by us.

⟦व्युष्टौ⟧: ⟦उच्छि विवासे⟧, the root uch in the sense of driving away. "Darkness is driven away by it": the suffix ktin comes in the sense of the instrument. The ṣ comes by the rule beginning with vraśca. The preverb keeps its original accent by ⟦तादौ च निति कृत्यताविति⟧. ⟦गोपाः⟧: ⟦गुपू रक्षणे⟧, the root gup in the sense of protecting. The suffix āya comes by the rule that it comes after gup, dhūp, vicch, paṇ and pan. After the root that ends in that suffix comes kvip. The a is elided by ⟦अतो लोप⟧. The y is elided by ⟦लोपो व्योरिति⟧. The accent is that of the root. ⟦जुषस्व⟧: it is the form, in the imperative, of the root ⟦जुषी⟧ in the senses of pleasure and enjoying. It loses its accent.

## Ṛk 3

O ⟦वृषभ⟧, showerer of desires, O Agni, you who are ⟦नृचक्षा⟧, the seer of the good and bad deeds of men, and ⟦अरुषः⟧, shining, in the ⟦कृष्णासु⟧, in the nights, ⟦अनु⟧, in due succession, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦भाहि⟧, make shine in a special way, ⟦पूर्वीः⟧, many flames. O ⟦वसो⟧, O Agni who make all dwell in their several places by the giving of light, do you ⟦नेषि⟧ ⟦नो⟧, us: and make us attain the fruits that conform to our rites. And ⟦अंहः⟧, our evil, characterised as poverty, ⟦अति⟧ ⟦पर्षि⟧: carry us across it; that is to say, ward it off. Moreover, O ⟦यविष्ठ⟧, youngest one, O Agni, ⟦कृधि⟧, make, ⟦नः⟧, us, ⟦उशिजः⟧, desiring, ⟦राये⟧, for wealth: the meaning is, make us such as have attained our desires, by bestowing the wished-for fruit.

⟦नृचक्षाः⟧: the root cakṣ in the sense of ⟦व्यक्तायां वाचि⟧, distinct speech. Here it is used in the sense of seeing. While "asun" continues, asun comes by the rule that after cakṣ it comes variously and is treated as marked with ś. Because it is treated as marked with ś, the substitute khyāñ does not come. The accent is that by which the second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦वृषभ⟧: ⟦वृषु सेचने⟧, the root vṛṣ in the sense of sprinkling. While "abhac" continues, abhac comes by ⟦ऋषिवृषिभ्यां⟧ ⟦किदित्यभच्⟧, "after ṛṣ and vṛṣ, marked with k". Because the suffix is marked with k there is no guṇa. Because it does not stand at the beginning of a pāda it loses its accent. ⟦पूर्वीः⟧: of the word puru, the suffix ṅīṣ**NOTE**{the supplied text has, with a misread letter, the feminine suffix ṅīṣ; the reference text has ṅīp} comes by the rule voto guṇavacanāt. The lengthening of the first u is Vedic. By the accent of the suffix it has the udātta on the last syllable. ⟦भाहि⟧: the root ⟦भा⟧ in the sense of shining. It has the sense of the causative included in it. It is the form in the imperative. ⟦वसो⟧: ⟦वस निवासे⟧, the root vas in the sense of dwelling. While "u" continues, the suffix u comes by the rule beginning śṝsvṛsnihitrapyasivasi. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda it does not lose its accent. The udātta on the first syllable is the one taught in the sixth book. ⟦नेषि⟧: the root nī in the sense of ⟦प्रापणे⟧, leading. In the Veda the present comes in the sense of the imperative. By ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧ śap is elided. Because by ⟦आमंत्रितं पूर्वमविद्यमानवदि⟧, "a preceding vocative is as if not present", the vocative is as if not present, the word stands at the beginning of a pāda and so does not lose its accent. ⟦पर्षि⟧: the root pṝ in the senses of ⟦पालनपूरणयोः⟧, protecting and filling. By the rule cādiṣu ca it does not lose its accent. ⟦कृधि⟧: of the root ⟦डुकृञ् करण⟧, kṛ in the sense of doing, in the imperative, the substitute hi comes by the rule ser hy apic ca. In place of that the substitute dhi comes by the rule that in the Veda it comes after śru, śṛṇu, pṝ, kṛ and vṛ. The suffix u is elided by the rule bahulaṃ chandasi. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda it does not lose its accent. ⟦राये⟧: ⟦रा दाने⟧, the root rā in the sense of giving. The suffix ḍai comes by the rule that ḍai comes after rā. The case-ending is udātta by the rule beginning ūḍidam. ⟦उशिजः⟧: ⟦वश कांतौ⟧, the root vaś in the sense of desiring. The suffix iji comes by ⟦वशेः किच्चे⟧, "after vaś, and marked with k". Because the suffix is marked with k there is saṃprasāraṇa. It has the accent of the suffix. ⟦यविष्ठ⟧: from the word yuvan the suffix iṣṭhan comes in the sense of the superlative. By the rule beginning sthūladūrayuva the following part that begins with a semivowel is elided and the preceding vowel takes guṇa. Because it is a vocative it loses its accent.

## Ṛk 4

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, you who are ⟦अषाळ्वः⟧, not overcome by enemies, undefeated — the meaning is that he himself conquers the enemies — and ⟦वृषभः⟧, the showerer of desires: being such, ⟦संजिगीवान्⟧, conquering completely, ⟦विश्वाः⟧, all, ⟦पुरः⟧, the cities of the enemies, and ⟦सौभगा⟧, the fine, the best riches that are in them, ⟦दीदिहि⟧: blaze on all sides with flames. O ⟦सुप्रणीते⟧, you of excellent carrying forward, O ⟦जातवेदो⟧, you in whom knowledge has arisen, O Agni: of the ⟦यज्ञस्य⟧ that is ⟦बृहत⟧, great through the mass of things to be done, of two kinds, those directly taught and those extended to it; that is ⟦पायो⟧, a protector by the bestowing of its fruit; that is ⟦प्रथमस्य⟧, performed first as being the chief one — because of the Vedic text ⟦यज्ञानां यज्योतिष्टोम इति⟧, "this indeed is the first sacrifice among sacrifices, namely the Jyotiṣṭoma" — of such a sacrifice, called the Jyotiṣṭoma, be the ⟦नेता⟧, the one who carries through the actions of bearing the oblation, calling the deities and the rest.

⟦अषाळ्वः⟧: of the root ⟦षह⟧ in the sense of enduring, in the niṣṭhā, ḍh comes by ⟦हो ढ इति⟧. The dh comes in place of the t of kta by the rule jhaṣas tathor dho 'dhaḥ. By the change to a cerebral it becomes ḍh. When the ḍh has been elided, the long vowel comes by ⟦ढलोपे पूर्वस्य दीर्घ इति⟧, "when ḍh is elided the preceding vowel is long". The absence of o is Vedic. The cerebral comes in place of the s by ⟦साडः स इति⟧, the rule on the s of sah when it has become sāḍ. Under the accent by which an indeclinable first member keeps its original accent, the accent is that of the negative. ⟦वृषभः⟧: ⟦वृष सेचने⟧, the root vṛṣ in the sense of sprinkling. The suffix abhac comes by ⟦ऋषिवृषिभ्यां किदित्यभच्⟧. It has the accent of a suffix marked with c. ⟦दीदिहि⟧: of the root ⟦दिवु⟧ in the sense of playing and the rest, in the elision of yaṅ, in the second person singular of the imperative, the reduplication does not take guṇa, by the maxim ⟦संज्ञापूर्वको विधिरनित्य⟧, "a rule stated by means of a technical term is not invariable". Since by the maxim that all rules are optional in the Veda the rule pūrvatrāsiddham does not operate, the long vowel comes by the rule hali ca, because that rule is the later one. After that the v is elided. ⟦सौभगा⟧: because of the reading ⟦सुभगान्मंत्र⟧, "after subhaga, in a mantra", among the words beginning with udgātṛ, the suffix añ comes in the sense of the state. The first member takes vṛddhi by the rule hṛdbhagasindhvante. The second member does not take vṛddhi, because all rules are optional in the Veda. By ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति सुपो डादेशः⟧, by supāṃ suluk, the substitute ḍā comes in place of the case-ending. And by that good fortune riches are implied. Because the suffix is marked with ñ it has the udātta on the first syllable. ⟦संजिगीवान्⟧: ⟦जि जये⟧, the root ji in the sense of conquering. In the Veda the perfect comes; in its place kvasu. A guttural is substituted by the rule on ji in the desiderative and the perfect. It has the accent of the suffix. The long vowel is Vedic. ⟦पायोः⟧: ⟦पा रक्षणे⟧, the root pā in the sense of protecting. The suffix uṇ comes by the rule beginning ⟦कृवापाजि⟧. The augment yuk comes by ⟦आतो युक् चिण् कृतोरिति⟧. It has the accent of the suffix.

## Ṛk 5

O ⟦जरितः⟧, O Agni who make the whole world decay at the time of the end, you who are ⟦सुमेधाः⟧, endowed with excellent understanding, and ⟦दीद्यानः⟧, shining with your own light: ⟦अच्छ⟧, with regard to, ⟦देवान्⟧, the gods to whom sacrifice is to be offered, the ⟦पुरूणि⟧, the many, ⟦शर्म⟧, the Agnihotra and the other rites that are performed with them in view and are the means of the happiness of heaven and the like, make ⟦अच्छिद्रा⟧, without flaw: make them complete through freedom from any defect in the performer, the act or the means. Moreover, O Agni, you who are ⟦सस्निः⟧, held in — having held in your own constant going and staying here until the casting in of the oblation — ⟦वक्षि⟧, carry, make reach, ⟦वाजं⟧, the food characterised as oblation that is being given by ⟦नो⟧, by us, ⟦अभि⟧, in the direction of the gods. For this there is an illustration: ⟦रथो न⟧, like a chariot. As a chariot, having carried grain and the like, makes it reach its owner, in the same way. Moreover, ⟦त्वं⟧, do you, O Agni, make ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth, ⟦सुमेके⟧, endowed with a beautiful form, joined with light by your own radiances.

⟦दीद्यानः⟧: the verb dīdeti has the sense of shining. After it comes śānac. The udātta stands on the first syllable by the rule abhyastānām ādiḥ. ⟦सुमेधाः⟧: the suffix asic comes by ⟦नित्यमसिच् प्रजामेधयोरि⟧, "asic always after prajā and medhā". In the bahuvrīhi the second member has the udātta on its last syllable by the rule nañsubhyām. ⟦सस्निः⟧: the root ⟦षस⟧ in the sense of sleeping. After it comes the Uṇādi suffix nin. Because the suffix is marked with n it has the udātta on the first syllable. ⟦वक्षि⟧: the root vah in the sense of ⟦प्रापणे⟧, conveying. Of its imperative, before sip, ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति शपो लुक्⟧, śap is elided by bahulaṃ chandasi. There are the changes to ḍh and to k. The substitute hi does not come, because the form is Vedic. It loses its accent.

## Ṛk 6

O ⟦वृषभ⟧, showerer of desires, O Agni, ⟦त्वं⟧, do you, ⟦प्रपीपय⟧, ⟦नो⟧, of us: make the wished-for fruits increase. And ⟦जिन्व⟧, bestow, ⟦वाजान⟧, foods. Moreover, O ⟦देव⟧, shining one, O Agni, you who are ⟦रुचानः⟧, shining, ⟦सुरुचा⟧, with a beautiful brightness, accompanied by ⟦देवेभि⟧, by the gods who are worthy of sacrifice, make our ⟦रोदसी⟧, these two, heaven and earth, ⟦सुदोघे⟧, good yielders by the bestowing of the desired fruit by way of the rain. Likewise may the ⟦दुर्मति⟧ of ⟦मर्तस्य⟧, of the enemy, the overpowering that is set in motion by an evil mind, ⟦मा⟧ ⟦परि ष्ठात्⟧ ⟦नो⟧, us: may it not go around us.

⟦पीपय⟧: of this root, ⟦पा पान⟧, pā in the sense of drinking, when it ends in ṇi, in the aorist, before caṅ, there are the reduplication and the lengthening of the reduplication. The elision of sip is Vedic. ⟦जिन्व⟧: ⟦इवि जिवि धिवि⟧ have the sense of pleasing. Of this root, in the imperative, the augment num comes because the root is marked with i; there is śap; hi is elided by ⟦अतो हेरिति हेर्लुक्⟧. Because the vocative is as if not present, the word does not come after a word that is not a verb, and so it does not lose its accent. ⟦सुदोघे⟧: milking is ⟦दोघः⟧; the suffix ghañ comes in the sense of the state. The guttural is Vedic. The second member has the udātta on its first syllable by the rule ādyudāttaṃ dvyac chandasi. ⟦दुर्मतिः⟧: ⟦मनु अवबोधने⟧, the root man in the sense of understanding. "It thinks", hence mati, the mind; the suffix ktic comes in the sense of the agent. An evil mind. Though the accent by which an indeclinable first member keeps its original accent would result, the accent is that by which the second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent.
