## Introduction

The sūkta beginning ⟦वैश्वानरं मनसेति⟧ is the fourteenth, of nine Ṛks. On it the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦वैश्वानरं नव तृच् वैश्वानरीयमारुत् जागत् दृच आत्मस्तुतिर्वा⟧ and so on, that is: the sūkta beginning "vaiśvānaram" has nine Ṛks; there are two triplets, addressed to Vaiśvānara and to the Maruts, in the jagatī metre; a pair of Ṛks is a praise of the self, or the former of them is sung by the Self; the last Ṛk is a praise of the teacher. The two triplets that begin with "vaiśvānaraṃ manasā" are in the jagatī metre. Of the first the deity is Agni Vaiśvānara, of the second the Maruts. The three Ṛks beginning with the seventh are triṣṭubhs. Of the pair of Ṛks beginning "agnir asmi" the deity is Agni, or the supreme Brahman. Of the ninth, however, because the teacher of Viśvāmitra is the one to be praised, he himself is the deity. Of the sūkta without the seventh Ṛk Viśvāmitra is the Ṛṣi. Of the seventh, because it is the utterance of Brahman, Brahman is the Ṛṣi, by the maxim "he whose utterance it is, is the Ṛṣi".

The first triplet, beginning "vaiśvānaraṃ manasā", is employed on the fourth day of the Abhiplava, in the āgnimāruta-śastra, as the place where the nivid of Vaiśvānara is inserted. It is laid down in the section beginning ⟦तृतीयस्येति⟧: ⟦वैश्वानरं मनसेति तिस्सः⟧ (⟦आ. ७-७⟧), "the three Ṛks beginning 'vaiśvānaraṃ manasā'". This same triplet is also, in the Bṛhaspatisava, in the āgnimāruta-śastra, the one in which the nivid of Vaiśvānara is to be inserted. The second triplet, beginning ⟦प्र यंतु वाजा⟧, is in the Bṛhaspatisava the one in which the nivid of the Maruts is to be inserted. Both are laid down in the section beginning "uśanasaḥ stomena": ⟦वैश्वानरं मनसाग्निं निचाय्य प्रयंतु वाजास्तविषीभिरग्नयः⟧ (⟦आ. ९-५⟧), that is, the triplets beginning "vaiśvānaraṃ manasāgniṃ nicāyya" and "pra yantu vājās taviṣībhir agnayaḥ". In the piling of the fire-altar, in the recitation that follows upon the piled-up fire, the Ṛk beginning "agnir asmi" is used. It is laid down in the sūtra which begins "then he should recite after the piled-up fire, over which the chant has been sung": ⟦अग्निरस्मि जन्मना जातवेदा इति त्रिर्मध्यमया वाचा⟧ (⟦आ. ४-८⟧), "the Ṛk 'agnir asmi janmanā jātavedāḥ', three times, in the middle voice".

## Ṛk 1

Viśvāmitra praises. O Agni, ⟦हविष्मंतः⟧: having the oblation made ready, ⟦वसूयवः⟧: desiring wealth for ourselves, ⟦कुशिकासः⟧: born in the family of Kuśika, we, ⟦वैश्वानरं⟧: he to whom belong all men, the leaders of the acts of carrying forward the fire and the rest, is Viśvānara, Agni; what is connected with him is Vaiśvānara, that is to say, his offspring. Or else Viśvānara himself is Vaiśvānara: the secondary suffix has no meaning of its own, as in rākṣasa, vāyasa and the like. Him, ⟦अनुषत्यं⟧: accompanied by truth, true to his promise in giving the fruit that corresponds to the rite; ⟦स्वर्विदं⟧: the knower of the fruit that consists of heaven and the like; for that very reason ⟦सुदानुं⟧: the good giver of the fruit; ⟦रथिरं⟧: possessed of a chariot; for that very reason ⟦रण्वं⟧: going to the sacrifices; ⟦देवं⟧: shining; this ⟦अग्निं⟧, Agni, ⟦मनसा⟧ ⟦निचाय्य⟧: having known with the inner organ, "you are distinguished by such qualities", ⟦गीर्भिः⟧: with words that consist of praise, ⟦हवामहे⟧: we call you to this rite.

⟦वैश्वानरं⟧: by the rule ⟦नरे संज्ञायामिति⟧ the first member is lengthened. In the sense "this belongs to Viśvānara" the suffix aṇ comes by the rule "this is his". The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦निजाय्य⟧: it is the form before lyap of the root ⟦चाय्य⟧, in the senses of honouring and perceiving. The accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦अनुषत्यं⟧: accompanied by truth: the compound is by the rule beginning ⟦आवादयः⟧, "ava and the rest, in the sense of kruṣṭa and the rest, with a word in the third case". Because the word belongs to the group beginning with suṣāman, s becomes ṣ. Because it belongs to the group beginning with nirudaka, the last syllable is udātta. ⟦सुदानुं⟧: the root is ⟦डुदाण्⟧, in the sense of giving. By the rule ⟦दाभाभ्यां नुरिति⟧ the suffix nu comes in the sense of the agent. By the rule beginning ⟦परादिश्चंदसि⟧, "and the beginning of the latter, variously, in the Veda", the first syllable of the second member is udātta. ⟦रथिरं⟧: after the word ratha, in the sense of the suffix matup, the suffix irac comes by the statement ⟦मेधारथाभ्यामिरनिरचौ⟧, "after medhā and ratha, iran and irac are to be stated". The accent is that of a suffix marked with c. ⟦वसूयवः⟧: in the sense "desiring wealth for themselves" the suffix kyac comes by the rule ⟦सुप आत्मनः क्यच्⟧. The lengthening is by the rule ⟦अकृत्सार्वधातुकयोरिति⟧. The suffix u comes by the rule ⟦क्याच्छंदसी⟧, "after kyac, in the Veda". The a is elided. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦रण्वं⟧: ravi is a root with the sense of going. He goes to the sacrifices, hence raṇva. It ends in the suffix ac of the group beginning with pac. The accent is that of a suffix marked with c. ⟦कुशिकासः⟧: after the word kuśika, in the sense "family", the suffix añ comes because the word belongs to the group beginning with vida. In the plural it is dropped by the rule ⟦यञ्ञोश्च⟧ (⟦पा. २-४-६४⟧). ⟦हवामहे⟧: the root hve has saṃprasāraṇa by the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 2

The Agni who was set forth in the preceding mantra, ⟦तं⟧: him, ⟦शुभ्रं⟧: shining; ⟦वैश्वानरं⟧: connected with Viśvānara; ⟦मातरिश्वानं⟧: he breathes, that is, acts in the form of lightning and the like, in the mother, the mid-region: hence Mātariśvan, Agni; him, ⟦उक्ष्यं⟧: to be praised with ukthas, with śastras; ⟦बृहस्पतिं⟧: the lord of the great sacrifice, its master as its protector; ⟦विप्रं⟧: wise; ⟦श्रोतारं⟧: who hears the words of the mantras, the stotras, śastras and the rest; ⟦अतिथिं⟧: who has arrived of his own accord, as one who deserves the honour due to a guest; ⟦रघुस्यदं⟧: that is, laghusyada: raghu is light; he whose syada, speed, is light; thus, one who goes swiftly. Or else by the word syada the road is indicated; raghusyada means one who goes swiftly. That Agni, endowed with such qualities, ⟦अवसे⟧: for our protection, ⟦हवामहे⟧: we who are born in the family of Kuśika call. Moreover, ⟦मनुषः⟧: of man, ⟦देवतातये⟧: for the sake of the sacrifice, we call you.

⟦शुभ्रं⟧: the root is ⟦शुभ⟧, in the sense of shining. After it the suffix rak comes in the sense of the agent by the rule beginning ⟦स्थायितंची⟧. Because it is marked with k, there is no guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦मातरिश्वानं⟧: the mother is the mid-region, because it is the cause of the fashioning. He breathes there, that is, lives: hence Mātariśvan, Agni. Because by the rule that begins with śvan and ukṣan the word mātariśvan is laid down ready-made as ending in the suffix kan, the last syllable of the first member is udātta. ⟦उक्तं⟧: one who is to be praised with an uktha, a hymn of praise. In the sense "being there" the suffix yat comes by the rule ⟦भवे छंदसीति⟧. By the rule ⟦तित्सरितमिति⟧ it is svarita. ⟦देवतातये⟧: devatāti is simply deva; the suffix tātil is added without change of sense. By the word devatāti the sacrifice is indicated, because the sacrifice has the god for its recipient. The accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦रघुष्यदं⟧: the word syada, ending in the suffix ghañ, is from the root ⟦स्यंदू⟧, in the sense of flowing. The nasal is elided because the form is laid down ready-made in the rule ⟦स्यदो जव⟧, "syada, in the sense of speed". The accent is the original accent of the second member, which ends in a kṛt suffix.

## Ṛk 3

The ⟦वैश्वानरः⟧, Vaiśvānara, ⟦अग्निः⟧, Agni, who ⟦कुशिकेभिः⟧: by the hotṛ and the others who are making the hymn of praise, ⟦युगेयुगे⟧: day by day, ⟦समिध्यत⟧: is made to burn fully by the casting in of oblations of clarified butter. For this there is a comparison in the words "aśvo na": ⟦अश्वः⟧ ⟦न⟧: as a foal, ⟦क्रंदन्⟧: making the sound of neighing, is fully nourished day by day ⟦जनिभिः⟧: by the mares, its mothers, by the giving of milk from the udder: in that way. ⟦अमृतेषु⟧: among the gods, who are free from the nature of dying, ⟦जागृवि⟧: wakeful, blazing with flame, that Agni, ⟦सुवीर्यं⟧: accompanied by excellent offspring, ⟦स्वश्वं⟧: accompanied by excellent horses and other cattle, ⟦रत्नं⟧: the best wealth, ⟦नः⟧: as ours, ⟦दधातु⟧: may he assign, may he bestow.

⟦युगेयुगे⟧: the doubling is by the rule ⟦नित्यवीप्सयोरिति⟧. The latter word being anudātta because it is the repeated one, the first keeps its original accent. ⟦स्वश्वं⟧: the word aśva, ending in the suffix kvin,**NOTE**{The supplied text has the suffix kvin here; the reference text has kvan.} has the udātta on its first syllable. In the bahuvrīhi, by the rule "a word of two syllables with the udātta on the first, in the Veda", the first syllable of the second member is udātta. The added y is Vedic. Or else: those that are good among horses are aśvyas; the suffix yat comes by the rule ⟦तत्र साधुरिति⟧. By the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧ the first syllable is udātta. In the bahuvrīhi the accent is as stated before. ⟦दधातु⟧: it is the form of the root dhā in the imperative, loṭ. ⟦रत्नं⟧: the root is ⟦रमु⟧, in the sense of playing. By the rule ⟦रमेस्त⟧, "after ram, and t", the suffix na comes in the sense of the place, and t is the substitute for the final. One delights in it: hence ratna. As "marked with n" continues from the preceding rule, the word has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦जागृविः⟧: it ends in the suffix kvin. Because that is marked with n, the word has the udātta on its first syllable.

## Ṛk 4

⟦वाजाः⟧: swift, ⟦अग्नयः⟧: the fires, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦यंतु⟧: let them go, aiming at the water. And having gone, those fires, ⟦तविषीभिः⟧: with the Maruts, who are endowed with strength, ⟦शुभे⟧: in the water, ⟦संमिश्लाः⟧: being united with one another, ⟦पृषतीः⟧: the drops, ⟦अयुक्षत⟧: they joined; they made drops in the water. Or else: the swift fires, with the Maruts who are endowed with strength, let them go, aiming at the water. And those Maruts, in the water ⟦संमिश्लाः⟧: being united with the fires, ⟦पृषतीः⟧: the mares of variegated colour that serve as their draught animals, ⟦अयुक्षत⟧: they yoked them to the chariot for the purpose of cleaving the cloud. Mounted on the chariot, they, ⟦विश्ववेदसः⟧: possessed of the knowledge of the deeds done by living beings, ⟦अदाभ्याः⟧: not to be put down by anyone, ⟦मरुतः⟧: the winds, who are such, ⟦बृहदुक्षः⟧: that pour forth abundantly, ⟦पर्वतान्⟧: the clouds, ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦वेपयंति⟧: they shake the clouds in such a way that there is abundant rain.

⟦तविषीभिः⟧: ⟦तव⟧, in the sense of going, is a root that occurs in a sūtra. After it the suffix ṭiṣac comes by the rule "after tav, optionally marked with ṇ", and it is marked with n. They move about in the sky: hence taviṣas, the winds. The feminine suffix ṅīp comes by the rule ⟦टिड्ढाण⟧. There is interchange of gender. Because the suffix is marked with n, the word has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦पृषतीः⟧: the root is ⟦पृषु⟧, in the sense of sprinkling, or ⟦पृ⟧, in the senses of protecting and filling. After it, in the present, because the form is laid down ready-made in the rule ⟦पृद्बृहन्महदिति⟧, the suffix ati comes, and it is treated like śatṛ. The feminine suffix ṅīp comes by the rule ⟦उगितश्चेति⟧. They sprinkle: hence pṛṣatīs, drops of spray. They protect: hence pṛṣatīs, mares. ⟦आयुक्त⟧: it is the form, in the aorist, luṅ, with the suffix sic, of the root ⟦युजिर्⟧, in the sense of joining. The verb loses its accent. ⟦बृहदुक्षः⟧: the suffix kvip comes after the root ⟦उक्ष⟧, in the sense of sprinkling. The accent is the original accent of the second member, which ends in a kṛt suffix. ⟦वेपयंति⟧: the root is ⟦टुवेपृ⟧, in the sense of trembling. The verb loses its accent. ⟦पर्वतान्⟧: after the root ⟦पर्व⟧, in the sense of filling, the suffix atac comes by the rule beginning ⟦भ्रमृदृशी⟧. It fills the earth with water: hence parvata, a cloud. By interchange the word has the udātta on its first syllable.

## Ṛk 5

⟦अग्निश्रियः⟧: resorting to the fire of the lightning; ⟦विश्वकृष्टयः⟧: those from whom there comes the kṛṣṭi, the pulling, that is, the action that consists of bending down, rising up and the like, of everything, of the trees and the rest, are viśvakṛṣṭis. The ⟦मरुतः⟧, the Maruts, who are such; and of those Maruts, ⟦त्वेषं⟧: bright, ⟦उग्रं⟧: uplifted, ⟦अवः⟧: protection, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦ईमहे⟧: we, the Kuśikas, beg for it on every side. ⟦ते⟧: those Maruts, ⟦रुद्रियाः⟧: the sons of Rudra, ⟦वर्षनिर्णिजः⟧: the word nirṇij denotes form, because it is read among the names of form in ⟦निर्णिग्वव्रिरिति⟧; those whose form, whose nature, is raining are varṣanirṇij, the rainers. ⟦हेषक्रतवः⟧: those who have the kratu, the making, of the sound of neighing, that is, who have made a neighing sound; ⟦सिंहा न⟧: like lions, ⟦स्वानिनः⟧: possessed of sound. Those Maruts, who are such, are ⟦सुदानवः⟧: good givers of water.

⟦अग्निश्रियः⟧: the root is ⟦श्रिञ्⟧, in the sense of resorting. By the rule that begins with vaci and pracchi there are the suffix kvip, the absence of saṃprasāraṇa**NOTE**{The supplied text has "absence of saṃprasāraṇa", saṃprasāraṇābhāvo; the reference text has saṃprasāraṇabhāvo.} and the lengthening of the root. Before the ending jas the substitute is iyaṅ. The accent is that of the kṛt suffix. ⟦उग्रं⟧: the root is ⟦गम्लगतौ⟧, that is, gam in the sense of going, preceded by ud. Because the form is laid down ready-made in the rule ⟦ऋजेंद्राग्रेति⟧, there are the suffix ra, the elision of the m and the elision of the final of the preverb. It goes up: hence ugra. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦ईमहे⟧: the root is ⟦ई⟧, in the senses of desire, going and the rest. By interchange it takes the middle endings. Because it belongs to the class beginning with ad, the class-sign śap is dropped. The verb loses its accent. ⟦स्थानिनः⟧: svāna ends in the suffix ghañ. Those who possess it: the suffix ini comes as stated in ⟦अत इनिरनावितीनिः⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦रुद्रियाः⟧: after the word rudra comes the Vedic suffix gha. Its substitute is iy. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 6

We, the Kuśikas, ⟦अग्नेः⟧ ⟦भामं⟧: that which lights up Agni, ⟦मरुतां⟧ ⟦ओजः⟧: the brilliance of the Maruts, ⟦व्रातंव्रातं⟧: in troop after troop, in every group; ⟦गणंगणं⟧: in host after host, in the great assembly; with the ⟦सुशस्तिभिः⟧ that are being performed there: the mantras by which excellent śastis, praises, are made are suśastis; with those mantras ⟦ईमहे⟧: we beg for such brilliance of the Maruts. ⟦पृषदश्वासः⟧: those whose horses are pṛṣatīs, of variegated colour, are such. ⟦अनवभ्रराधसः⟧: that of which there is no falling away is anavabhra; those whose rādhas, wealth, is anavabhra are anavabhrarādhas;**NOTE**{Here the supplied text repeats the word, te 'navabhrarādhasaḥ; the reference text has te 'navabhraṣṭadhanāḥ, "they are those whose wealth has not fallen away".} that is to say, of complete wealth. ⟦धीराः⟧: knowing the rites, those Maruts, ⟦विदथेषु⟧: at the sacrifices, ⟦यज्ञं⟧: the oblation that is fit to be offered, aiming at it, ⟦गंतारः⟧: are in the habit of going.

⟦गणं गणं⟧: the root is ⟦गण⟧, in the sense of counting. The suffix is ac of the group beginning with pac. The doubling is by the rule ⟦नित्यवीप्सयोरिति⟧. The latter word being anudātta because it is the repeated one, the accent is that of the first. ⟦सुशस्तिभिः⟧: in the bahuvrīhi, by the rule "after nañ and su", the last syllable of the second member is udātta. ⟦भामं⟧: the root is ⟦भा⟧, in the sense of shining. The suffix man comes by the rule that begins with arti, stu and su. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦ओजः⟧: the root is ⟦उब्ब⟧, in the sense of straightness. The suffix asun comes by the rule ⟦उच्चीर्बले बलोपश्च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-१९१⟧), "after ubj, in the sense of strength, and there is elision of the b". Together with it there is the elision of the b. There is the guṇa that is conditioned by an ārdhadhātuka suffix. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦ईमहे⟧: the root is used in the senses of desire and the rest.**NOTE**{The reference text names the root here, ī kāntyādiṣu; the supplied text has only kāntyādiṣu.} The verb loses its accent. ⟦अनवभ्रराधसः⟧: in ⟦अनवभ्रं⟧, by the rule ⟦नञ्सुभ्या⟧, the last syllable of the second member is udātta. In the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member. ⟦गंतारः⟧: the suffix is tṛn, in the sense of habit. ⟦विदथेषु⟧: the root is ⟦विद⟧, in the sense of knowing. By the rule beginning ⟦रुदिविदिभ्यां⟧, "after rud and vid, marked with k", the suffix is atha. Because it is marked with k, there is no guṇa. In it the adhvaryu and the others know the whole body of the rites: hence vidatha, the sacrifice. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 7

Agni, whose form is the supreme reality directly realised, makes manifest in a pair of Ṛks his experience that his own self is the self of all. O Kuśikas! This whole world is indeed of two kinds, as enjoyer and as thing enjoyed, because of the revealed text ⟦एतावद्वा इदमन्नं चैवान्नादश्च सोम एवान्नमग्निरन्नाद⟧,**NOTE**{After this quotation the reference text has a reference, bṛ. u. 1. 4, 6, which the supplied text does not have.} "This much indeed is all this: food and the eater of food. Soma is the food, Agni the eater of food". Of these, Agni is the eater of food, in the form of the whole class of enjoyers. And he, having become threefold by the division into Agni, Vāyu and Āditya, presides over the worlds of earth, mid-region and heaven.**NOTE**{The supplied text has "the worlds of earth, mid-region and heaven", pṛthivyantarikṣadyulokān; the reference text has pṛthivyantarikṣalokān, without "heaven".} That is said in the Vājasaneyaka: ⟦स त्रेधात्मानं व्यभजत । आदित्यं तृतीयं वायुं तृतीयमिति⟧,**NOTE**{After this quotation the reference text has a reference, bṛ. u. 1. 2. 3, which the supplied text does not have.} "He divided himself into three: Āditya as one third, Vāyu as one third". There, I, that ⟦अग्निः⟧, Agni, ⟦जन्मना⟧: by birth itself ⟦जातवेदाः⟧ ⟦अस्मि⟧: without depending on means such as hearing and reflection, by my very nature, I am one whose own form is the supreme reality directly realised. ⟦घृतं मे चक्षुः⟧: this eye of mine, which lights up the universe and consists of the light that is my own nature, is ghṛta, that is, it is now exceedingly bright. ⟦अमृतं⟧: the fruit of the rites, which consists of the enjoyment of the various objects, heavenly and not heavenly; that ⟦मे⟧ ⟦आसन्⟧: is in my mouth, because I myself abide as the whole class of enjoyers. Having thus stated that he himself has the form of the one who presides over the earth, he states that, as Vāyu, he presides over the mid-region. ⟦अर्कः⟧: the breath, the creator of the world, because of the revealed text ⟦सोऽर्चन्न चरत्त स्यार्चित आपोऽजायंतार्चते वै मे कमभूदिति⟧ and so on (⟦श. ब्रा. १०-६-५⟧), "He went about praising. From him, as he praised, the waters were born. 'For me, as I praised, there came to be joy': that is why the arka is called arka". I, that breath, am ⟦त्रिधातुः⟧: having divided myself into three, there, as Vāyu, I am ⟦रजसः⟧: of the mid-region, ⟦विमानः⟧: the measurer, the one who presides. In the same way he states that, in the form of Āditya, he presides over the world of heaven, with the words ⟦अजस्रो घर्म⟧. ⟦अजस्रः⟧: not wasting away, ⟦घर्मः⟧: consisting of light, the one who presides over the world of heaven, Āditya, am I. Having thus brought to mind his own form as enjoyer, he brings to mind also his having the form of the thing enjoyed: the ⟦हविः⟧, the oblation, that is well known as the thing to be enjoyed, that too I alone am.

Or else: I am Agni: because I carry the oblation to the gods, I am endowed with the qualities of moving and the rest. Moreover, ⟦जन्मना⟧: by my coming into being, I am ⟦जातवेदाः⟧: one in whom wisdom is born; at the very moment of coming into being I am all-knowing. Or again: he knows all that is born as having the form of his own self, hence jātavedas; the meaning is, the self of all. How that is so, is told. ⟦घृतं मे चक्षुः⟧: this ⟦घृतं⟧, the well-known clarified butter, stands in the place of an eye for me. As in the world the eye gives light, so the clarified butter thrown into me, producing a flame, gives light to me. ⟦अमृतं⟧: in the form of radiance; the undying, imperishable light ⟦मे⟧ ⟦आसन्⟧: is in my mouth. ⟦त्रिधातुः⟧: existing in three ways, as prāṇa, apāna and vyāna, ⟦अर्कः⟧: worthy of praise, the breath that there is, that too I alone am. And likewise ⟦रजसः⟧: of the mid-region, ⟦विमानः⟧: the one who measures in a special way, who marks its limits, Vāyu, that also am I. Moreover, ⟦अजस्रो घर्म⟧: the sun, who causes heat without interruption, that also am I. Why say more? The ⟦हविः⟧, the oblation, in the form of clarified butter, the puroḍāśa and the rest, and all that is indicated by it, I am; because of the revealed text ⟦सर्वं खल्विदं ब्रह्म⟧,**NOTE**{After this quotation the reference text has a reference, chā. u. 3. 14. 1, which the supplied text does not have.} "All this indeed is Brahman". The word ⟦नाम⟧ is used in the sense of being well known. Thus, by this setting forth that Agni is the self of all, it is stated that he is the supreme Brahman.

⟦चक्षुः⟧: the root is ⟦चक्षिज्⟧, in the sense of distinct speech. While "usin" continues from the preceding rule, the suffix usin comes by ⟦चक्षेः शिच्चेत्युसिन्⟧, that is, by the rule "after cakṣ, and it is treated as marked with ś". Because it is treated as marked with ś, the substitute khyāñ does not come. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦आसन्⟧: for the word āsya the substitute āsan comes by the rule beginning ⟦पद्दन्नोमासि⟧. By the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ the ending of the seventh case is dropped. ⟦विमानः⟧: the root is ⟦माज्⟧, in the sense of measuring. By the rule ⟦कृत्यल्लुटो बहुलमिति⟧ the suffix lyuṭ comes in the sense of the agent. The accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦अजस्रः⟧: the root is ⟦जसु⟧, in the sense of releasing, preceded by the negative particle. By the rule ⟦नमिकंपिस्म्यजसकमहिंसदीपो रः⟧ (⟦पा. ३-२-१६७⟧) the suffix ra comes in the sense of habit. The root jas preceded by the negative particle is used in the sense of being uninterrupted. The accent is that of the negative particle. Or the root is ⟦जस⟧, in the sense of wasting away.

## Ṛk 8

Agni, the self of all, ⟦हृदा⟧: by the working of the inner organ, ⟦मतिं⟧: that which is to be reflected upon, ⟦ज्योतिः⟧: the brilliance called the supreme Brahman, whose form is light of itself, ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦प्रजानन्⟧: knowing it thoroughly as having the form of his own self, in the order of hearing, reflection and the rest, through the removal of the notions of doubt and of contrary conception; and being so, ⟦पवित्रैः⟧: purifying, ⟦त्रिऽभिः⟧: by the three, Agni, Vāyu and Sūrya, ⟦अर्कं⟧: his own self, which is worthy of praise, ⟦अपुपोत्⟧ ⟦हि⟧: he indeed determined it to be purifying, as being more spotless even than they; just as one purifies the Soma with the fringed strainer, in that way. Agni, knowing thus, ⟦वर्षिष्ठं⟧: his own self, the highest, ⟦स्वधाभिः⟧: it upholds the worlds by itself, hence svadhā; by those, Agni, Vāyu and Sūrya, ⟦रत्नं⟧: delightful, ⟦अकृत⟧: he made. ⟦आत्⟧ ⟦इत्⟧: immediately afterwards, when the self was known in this way, ⟦द्यावापृथिवी⟧: heaven and earth, which are imagined in it, and the whole world that is indicated by them, ⟦परि⟧ ⟦अपश्यत्⟧: he saw all around, on every side, as his own self; because of the revealed text ⟦आत्मनि विज्ञाते सर्वमिदं विज्ञातं भवतीति⟧, "When the self is known, all this becomes known".

⟦पवित्रैः⟧: the root is ⟦पूञ्⟧, in the sense of purifying. After it, by the rule ⟦कर्तरि चर्षिदेवतयोः⟧ (⟦पा. ३-२-१८६⟧), when a deity is to be denoted, the suffix itra comes in the sense of the agent. They purify: hence pavitras, namely Agni, Vāyu and Sūrya. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अपुपोत्⟧: it is the form of the root ⟦पूञ्⟧, in the sense of purifying, ending in the elided yaṅ suffix. Because a rule that is stated by means of a technical name is not invariable, there is no guṇa of the reduplicating syllable. By the rule ⟦यद्धितुपरं छंदसि⟧ (⟦पा. ८-१-५६⟧) the verb does not lose its accent. ⟦अर्क०⟧: the root is ⟦अर्च⟧, in the sense of honouring. The suffix ka comes by the rule ⟦कृदाधारार्चिकलिभ्यः क⟧. ⟦हृदा⟧: for the word hṛdaya the substitute hṛd comes by the rule beginning ⟦पद्दन्नोमासि⟧. ⟦मतिं⟧: the root is ⟦मनज्ञाने⟧, that is, man in the sense of knowing. By the rule that begins "in a mantra, after vṛṣ, iṣ, pac" the suffix ktin comes in the sense of the object, with the udātta. ⟦अकृत⟧: in the aorist, luṅ, of the root kṛ there is the suffix sic. It is elided by the rule ⟦ह्रस्वादंगादिति⟧. ⟦द्यावापृथिवी⟧: by the rule ⟦देवताद्वंद्वे⟧, "also in a dvandva of names of deities", both members keep their original accent.

## Ṛk 9

The teacher from whom Viśvāmitra came to know the supreme Brahman called Vaiśvānara, that teacher he praises with this Ṛk. O ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth, ⟦शतधारं⟧: furnished with unmeasured streams of water; ⟦अक्षीयमाणं⟧: whose flow is never cut off; ⟦उत्सं⟧: like a spring, like a stream that has a hundred currents, furnished with an unbroken course of speech;**NOTE**{The supplied text reads here utsaṃ utsam iva śatapravāhopetaṃ srota ivāvicchinnavākkaraṇīsametaṃ; the reference text reads utsam, luptopamam etat, utsam iva satatapravāhopeta- srota ivāvicchinnavā(k)saraṇīsametaṃ: it has in addition the remark "this is a comparison with the particle of comparison dropped", "constant flow" for "a hundred currents", and a different word after avicchinnavāk-, which is translated here as "course of speech".} ⟦विपश्चितं⟧: wise, knowing all the sciences; ⟦पितरं⟧: the protector of his pupils by the teaching of knowledge; ⟦वक्तानां⟧: of the sentences of the Veda that are to be uttered, ⟦मेळिं⟧: the one who brings them together, who utters the sentences found in the different branches of the Veda after gathering them into one meaning; ⟦पित्रोः⟧: of heaven and earth, ⟦उपस्थे⟧: in the neighbourhood; ⟦मदंतं⟧: rejoicing through the abundance of his knowledge; and for that very reason ⟦सत्यवाचं⟧: a speaker of the truth, because there is no matter unknown to him and because he has no reason to deceive others. ⟦तं⟧: this teacher, ⟦पिपृतं⟧: make him full, by giving the fruit he looks for. Or else: protect him.

⟦उत्सं⟧: the root is ⟦उन्वी⟧, in the sense of wetting. After it the suffix sa comes, marked with k, by the rule quoted here (⟦उ. ३-६८⟧).**NOTE**{The rule is quoted as asmād unmigudhikuṣibhyaḥ kit in the supplied text and as undyamigudhiṣibhyaḥ kit in the reference text; the roots it names are not certain in either.} Because it is marked with k, the n is elided. It wets: hence utsa. By interchange the word has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦विपश्चितं⟧: that which protects in a special way is vipā, speech. He gathers the vipas, words: thus the suffix kvip. The augment tuk comes by the rule ⟦ह्रस्वस्य पितीति⟧. By the rule ⟦तत्पुरुषे कृति बहुलमिति⟧, on the strength of the word "variously", there is non-elision of the ending of the second case also. The accent is that of the second member, which ends in a kṛt suffix. ⟦वक्तानां⟧: the root is ⟦वच⟧, in the sense of speaking. After it, in the sense of the kṛtya suffixes, the suffix tvan comes by the rule ⟦तवैकेन्नेन्यत्वन⟧. The change to k is by the rule ⟦चोः कुरिति⟧. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦मेळिं⟧: mili is a root with the sense of contact. After it comes the Uṇādi suffix i. ⟦रोदसी⟧: it loses its accent because it is a vocative. ⟦पिपृतं⟧: the root is given as ⟦पृपालनपूरणयोः⟧, that is, pṝ in the senses of protecting and filling. In the Veda it is of the class beginning with juhoti. The i of the reduplicating syllable is as stated in ⟦आर्तिपिपर्त्योश्चेत्यभ्यासस्यॆत्वं⟧. The verb loses its accent.
