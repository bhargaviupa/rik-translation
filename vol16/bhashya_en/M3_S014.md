## Introduction

The second sūkta, of seven Ṛks, begins ⟦आ होतेति⟧. The Anukramaṇī says ⟦आ होतेत्यनुक्रमणिका⟧, "ā hotā". Because of the use of the word tu in ⟦ऋषभस्त्विति⟧, "Ṛṣabha, however", Ṛṣabha is the seer of this sūkta also. Since nothing is stated, the metre is Triṣṭubh. Agni is the deity.

The ten sūktas that begin with this one, leaving out the third and the eighth, are employed in the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra, in the Triṣṭubh metre. It is laid down in the sūtra, in the section ⟦अथैतस्या इति⟧, "now, of this": ⟦उद्धरेदिति त्रिष्टुभं⟧, "of the ten beginning 'ā hotā' he should take out the third and the eighth: thus the Triṣṭubh portion" (⟦आ. ४-१३⟧).

## Ṛk 1

Ṛṣabha praises Agni. ⟦होता⟧, the caller of the gods; ⟦मंद्रः⟧, the gladdener of the praiser; ⟦सत्यः⟧, one whose action is true; ⟦कवितमो⟧, exceedingly wise; ⟦यज्वा⟧, the sacrificer to the gods; ⟦वेधाः⟧, the ordainer of the whole world: ⟦सः⟧, that Agni, endowed with greatness of this kind, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦अस्थात्⟧ the ⟦विदथानि⟧, the sacrifices that are being performed by us: he comes to them, he reaches them. Moreover, ⟦विद्युद्रथो⟧, furnished with a flashing vehicle; ⟦शोचिष्केशः⟧ — ⟦शोचींषि⟧, flames, are the hair of whom, he — furnished with hair that consists of flames; ⟦सहसस्पुत्रो⟧, the son of strength: that Agni, being such, ⟦अश्रेत्⟧, in ⟦पृथिव्यां⟧, in the wide mid-region, ⟦पाजः⟧, light, his own radiance: he resorts to it, he makes it reach there. Or else: being on ⟦पृथिव्यां⟧, on the earth, he resorts to ⟦पाजो⟧, strength.

⟦मंद्रः⟧: the root ⟦मदि⟧ in the senses of praise, joy, intoxication, sleep, beauty and going. After it the suffix rak comes by the rule beginning sphāyitañci. Because the root is marked with i, the augment num comes. ⟦अस्थात्⟧: ⟦ष्ठा गतिनिवृत्तौ⟧, the root sthā in the sense of the ceasing of motion. The aorist comes in the sense of the present by the rule that in the Veda the aorist, the imperfect and the perfect are used. The sic is elided by ⟦गातिस्थेति⟧. It loses its accent. ⟦यज्वा⟧: ⟦यज देवपूजासंगतिकरणदानेषु⟧, the root yaj in the senses of worship of the gods, bringing together and giving. The suffix ṅvanip comes by the rule that ṅvanip comes after su and yaj. Since that suffix, being marked with p, is anudātta, the accent is that of the root. ⟦कवितमः⟧: ⟦कु शब्दे⟧, the root ku in the sense of sounding. "He sounds", or "it is sounded": the suffix i comes in the sense of the agent or of the state by ⟦अच इरिति⟧. Since tamap, being marked with p, is anudātta, the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦शोचिष्केशः⟧: ⟦शुच दीप्तौ⟧, the root śuc in the sense of shining. The suffix isi comes by ⟦अर्चिशुचिहुसृपिच्छदिच्छर्दिभ्य इसिरितीसिः⟧, "isi comes after arc, śuc, hu, sṛp, chad and chard". By the accent of the suffix it has the udātta on the last syllable. In the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦अश्रेत्⟧: of the root ⟦श्रिञ्⟧ in the sense of resorting to, the imperfect comes in the Veda in the sense of the present. By ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति शपो लुक्⟧, by bahulaṃ chandasi, śap is elided. There is the guṇa that comes before a sārvadhātuka. It loses its accent.

## Ṛk 2

⟦ऋतावः⟧: ṛta is the sacrifice, which is true; O Agni who possess it, ⟦ते⟧, having you for its object, a ⟦उक्तिं⟧ of homage, a sentence of obeisance, I ⟦अयामि⟧: I send it forth, I utter it. O ⟦सहस्वः⟧ — sahas is strength; O Agni who possess it — ⟦तुभ्यं⟧, to you, who are ⟦चेतते⟧, the one who makes aware, who makes the rite known, an utterance of homage is made. ⟦जुषस्व⟧ it: accept the utterance of obeisance that is being made by us. Moreover, you who are ⟦विद्वान्⟧, acquainted with the calling of the gods in due order, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦वक्षि⟧, bring here, ⟦विदुषः⟧, those who are acquainted with the rite. Moreover, O ⟦यजत्र⟧, O Agni to whom sacrifice is to be offered, ⟦ऊतये⟧, for our protection, ⟦बर्हिः⟧ ⟦मध्ये⟧, in the middle of the strewn sacred grass, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦निषत्सि⟧: sit down all around.

⟦अयामि⟧: ⟦इण् गतौ⟧, the root i in the sense of going; it belongs to the class beginning with bhū. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda it does not lose its accent. ⟦जुषस्व⟧: ⟦जुषी प्रीतिसेवनयोः⟧, the root juṣ in the senses of pleasure and enjoying. It belongs to the class beginning with tud. It is the form in the imperative. It loses its accent. ⟦चेतते⟧: ⟦चिती संज्ञाने⟧, the root cit in the sense of perceiving. Since the suffix śatṛ is anudātta by the accent of a sārvadhātuka that replaces a tense-affix, and since for that reason there is no udātta on the last syllable, the case-ending is not udātta by the rule śatur anumaḥ. The accent is that of the root. ⟦सहस्वः⟧: the suffix matup comes in the sense "strength belongs to him". Because the stem has the designation bha by ⟦मत्वर्थ इति⟧, the rule on stems in t and s before a suffix with the sense of matup, ru does not come in place of the s. By ⟦मादुपधाया इति⟧ v takes the place of the m of matup. The augment num comes by the rule ugidacām. After the elision taught for what follows a consonant, ṅī and the rest, ru comes in place of the n by ⟦मतुवसो रुरिति⟧. It has the loss of accent of a vocative. ⟦विदुषः⟧: in place of the śatṛ that follows this root, ⟦विद⟧ in the sense of knowing, the substitute vasu comes by ⟦शतुर्वसुरिति⟧, "vasu in place of śatṛ after vid". When the stem has the designation bha, there is saṃprasāraṇa of vasu. It has the accent of the suffix. ⟦सत्सि⟧: the root ṣadḷ in the senses of breaking apart and the rest. In the present, śap is elided by the rule bahulaṃ chandasi. Because no suffix marked with ś follows, the substitute sīda does not come. The ṣ comes by ⟦सदेरप्रतेरिति⟧. ⟦यजत्र⟧: the root ⟦यज⟧ in the senses of worship of the gods and the rest. The suffix atran comes by the rule beginning ⟦अमिनक्षियजी⟧. It has the loss of accent of a vocative.

## Ṛk 3

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦उषसा⟧, Dawn and Night, the day and the night, who are ⟦वाजयंती⟧, making vāja, food, may they ⟦द्रवतां⟧ ⟦ते⟧: may they go towards you. You also, by the ⟦पथ्याभि⟧, by the paths, of ⟦वातस्य⟧, of the wind, ⟦अच्छ⟧, go quickly towards those two, the day and the night. ⟦यत्⟧: since by the going of the day and night and of Agni towards each other there is a mutual connection between them, for that reason, at the evening and morning times of the day and the night, the priests ⟦अंजंति⟧, sprinkle, you, the ⟦पूर्व्यं⟧, the ancient one, ⟦सीं⟧, on all sides, with ⟦हविर्भि⟧, with ghee and the other oblations. Moreover, ⟦बंधुरेव⟧: as the place of the pole of a chariot is furnished with the pair of shafts joined to each other, so those two, the day and the night, joined together, in ⟦दुरोणे⟧, in our house, in order that the Agnihotra and the other rites may be accomplished, ⟦आ⟧, having turned round, coming round again and again, ⟦तस्थतुः⟧: may they stand.

⟦द्रवतां⟧: it is the form, in the imperative, of the root ⟦द्रु⟧ in the sense of going. ⟦पथ्याभिः⟧: in the sense "good on paths", the suffix yat comes by ⟦तत्र साधुः⟧ (⟦पा. ४-४-९८⟧). The final part beginning with the last vowel is elided by ⟦नस्तद्धित इति⟧. It has the svarita by ⟦तित्स्वरित इति⟧. ⟦तस्थतुः⟧: it is the form of the root tiṣṭhati in the perfect, before atus. It loses its accent.

## Ṛk 4

O ⟦सहस्वो⟧, O strong ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦मित्रश्च वरुणश्च⟧, Mitra and Varuṇa, and ⟦विश्वे मरुतः⟧, all the gods, ⟦तुभ्यं⟧, for your sake, ⟦अर्चन्⟧, bear, ⟦सुम्नं⟧, a hymn of praise. Or else: all the gods ⟦तुभ्यं⟧, you, ⟦सुम्नं⟧, with happiness, ⟦अर्चन्⟧, honour. ⟦यत्⟧, for the reason that, O ⟦सहसस्पुत्र⟧, O Agni, as ⟦सूर्यः⟧ — a good lord**NOTE**{the supplied text reads suṣṭu āryaḥ svāmī; the reference text reads suṣṭhu aryaḥ svāmī}, a master; or else a good impeller, as being the inner controller of all; or else sūrya is one of excellent valour — ⟦क्षितीः⟧ — the word kṣiti denotes men, because of the reading ⟦विशः क्षितय इति⟧ among the names of these**NOTE**{the reference text gives a Nighaṇṭu reference (2.3.6) for viśaḥ kṣitayaḥ; the supplied text has none} — ⟦अभि⟧, in the direction of men, ⟦प्रथयन्⟧, spreading out on all sides, the ⟦नृन्⟧, your own rays, which are leaders as being the showers of the way, you ⟦तिष्ठाः⟧, you stand, being at one with ⟦शोचिषा⟧, with light: for that reason these gods honour you.

⟦अर्चन्⟧: it is the form, in the imperfect, of the root arc in the sense of honouring. The absence of the augment aṭ is Vedic. ⟦तिष्ठाः⟧: it is the form, in leṭ, of the root ṣṭhā in the sense of the ceasing of motion. Because it is connected with yad it does not lose its accent. Because the substitute tiṣṭha is laid down ready-made, with the udātta on its first syllable, by the rule beginning ⟦पाघ्रे⟧, it has the udātta on the first syllable. ⟦सूर्यः⟧: because the word is laid down ready-made as ending in kyap by the rule beginning rājasūyasūrya, all the meanings are established.

## Ṛk 5

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦वयं⟧, we, ⟦उत्तानहस्ता⟧, with palms turned upward because of the taking up of the oblation, ⟦ररिम⟧, give, ⟦ते⟧, to you, ⟦अद्य⟧, now, in this rite, ⟦कामं⟧, the desirable oblation, the cake and the rest. ⟦उपसद्य⟧, having become gracious, ⟦नमसा⟧, through the obeisance, with a ⟦मनसा⟧, with an inner organ, that is ⟦यजिष्ठेन⟧, the best at sacrificing, possessed of the wish to sacrifice, ⟦यक्षि⟧ ⟦देवान्⟧: sacrifice to the gods who are worthy of sacrifice, honour them. Moreover, you who are ⟦विप्रो⟧, wise, acquainted with the rite, honour the gods with a ⟦मन्मना⟧, with a hymn of praise that is to be thought upon, which is ⟦अस्रेधता⟧, not wasted away, abundant.

⟦अद्य⟧: it is laid down ready-made by the rule beginning ⟦सद्यः परुत्परार्यैषम⟧, and has the udātta on the last syllable. ⟦ररिम⟧: of the root rā in the sense of giving, in the perfect used in the sense of the present, the substitute ma comes in place of mas by the rule beginning ⟦परस्मैपदानां णलतुः⟧ (⟦पा. ३-४-८२⟧). The augment iṭ comes by the restriction about kṛ and the rest. The ā is elided by the rule āto lopa iṭi ca. Because it is connected with hi it does not lose its accent. Since iṭ is anudātta by ⟦आगमा अनुदात्ता⟧, "augments are anudātta", the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦उपसद्य⟧: the root ṣadḷ in the senses of ⟦विशरणगत्यवसादनेषु⟧, breaking apart, going and sinking down. After it comes ktvā. The substitute lyap comes by the rule that in a compound whose first member is not the negative, lyap takes the place of ktvā. It has the accent of a suffix marked with l. ⟦यक्षि⟧: ⟦यज देवपूजासंगतिकरणदानेषु⟧, the root yaj in the senses of worship of the gods, bringing together and giving. In the imperative, ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति शपो लुक्⟧, śap is elided by bahulaṃ chandasi. There are the changes to ṣ and to k. The absence of the substitute hi for sip is Vedic. ⟦अस्रेधता⟧: the root sridh has the sense of wasting away. The accent is that of the negative, by the rule tatpuruṣe.

## Ṛk 6

O ⟦सहसः पुत्र⟧, O son of strength, O Agni, ⟦त्वत्⟧, from you, there ⟦यंति⟧ ⟦हि⟧, there go out indeed in various ways, the ⟦ऊतयो⟧, the protections, in the form of the guarding of the rite by the removal of obstacles and the like, which are ⟦पूर्वी⟧, abundant, of the ⟦देवस्य⟧, of the sacrificer, whose habit is devana**NOTE**{devanaśīlasya is left as "whose habit is devana": Sāyaṇa does not say here in which sense of the root div he takes it} and who is engaged in performing the rite: they reach this sacrificer. In the same way the ⟦वाजा⟧, the foods, go out: they reach this sacrificer. Moreover, O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦त्वं⟧, do you, ⟦देहि⟧, give, ⟦नो⟧, to us, ⟦रयिं⟧, wealth, that is ⟦सहस्रिणं⟧, a thousand in number. In the same way give also a son who is ⟦सत्यं⟧, of a truthful habit, going on in the good path, and joined with ⟦वचसा⟧, with speech, that is ⟦अद्रोघेण⟧, free from malice, void of evil: bestow him.

⟦त्वत्⟧: for the word yuṣmad, in the ablative singular, the substitute tva comes. In place of ṅasi the substitute at comes by the rule ekavacanasya ca (⟦पा. ७-१-३२⟧). It has the accent of a single substitute. ⟦पुत्र सहसः⟧: because by the rule param api chandasi it is treated as part of the following word, both are without any udātta. ⟦देहि⟧: of the root dā in the sense of giving, in the imperative, there come the e of the root and the elision of the reduplication by ⟦घ्वसोरेद्धाविति⟧. It loses its accent. ⟦अद्रोघेण⟧: the root druh in the sense of ⟦जिघांसायां⟧, wishing to kill; the suffix ghañ; ⟦द्रोघः⟧. The gh is Vedic. The second member has the udātta on its last syllable by ⟦नञ्सुभ्यामि⟧.

## Ṛk 7

O ⟦दक्ष⟧, you who are endowed with capacity through being abundant; O ⟦कविक्रतो⟧, you who are endowed with knowledge of objects beyond the senses, all-knowing; O ⟦देव⟧, shining one; O Agni: we, ⟦मर्तासो⟧, the sacrificers, ⟦तुभ्यं⟧, for your sake, ⟦यानि⟧ ⟦इमा⟧, which these oblations, ⟦अध्वरे⟧, in the sacrifice, ⟦अकर्म⟧: we have made the giving up of the oblation. And ⟦त्वं⟧, do you, ⟦बोधि⟧ of ⟦विश्वस्य⟧, of every sacrificer, ⟦सुरथस्य⟧, who is furnished with a fine vehicle: know "I am his protector". O ⟦अमृत⟧, O Agni free from the property of dying, ⟦इह⟧, in this rite, ⟦तत्⟧ ⟦सर्वं⟧, all that oblation given by us, ⟦स्वद⟧, relish.

⟦अकर्म⟧: of the root ⟦डुकृञ् करण⟧, kṛ in the sense of doing, in the imperfect; because by the rule chandasy ubhayathā the ending mas is ārdhadhātuka, it is not treated as marked with ṅ, and so there is guṇa. Because it is connected with a form of yad it does not lose its accent. ⟦बोधि⟧: ⟦बुध⟧, the root budh in the sense of understanding. The aorist comes in the sense of the imperative by the rule on the aorist in the Veda. The substitute ta comes in place of sip by the maxim that personal endings come in place of personal endings. The suffix ciṇ comes in the sense of the agent by the rule beginning dīpajanabudha. By ⟦चिणो लुगिति⟧ the suffix ta is elided. The absence of the augment aṭ is Vedic. ⟦स्वद⟧: ⟦स्वद आस्वादने⟧, the root svad in the sense of relishing. It has the sense of the causative included in it. It is the form in the imperative. It loses its accent.
