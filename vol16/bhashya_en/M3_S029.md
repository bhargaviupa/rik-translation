## Introduction
The seventeenth sūkta, of sixteen Ṛks, beginning ⟦अस्तीदमिति⟧, is Viśvāmitra's. On it the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦अस्तीदं षोळशाद्याचतुर्थिदशमीद्वादश्योऽनुष्टुभः षष्ठैकादश्युपांत्ये च जगत्यः पंचम्यत्विग्भो वेति⟧, "'Astīdam': sixteen Ṛks; the first, the fourth, the tenth and the twelfth are Anuṣṭubh; the sixth, the eleventh and the two before the last are Jagatī; the fifth is optionally for the priests." The first, the fourth, the tenth and the twelfth are Anuṣṭubh. "The sixth, the eleventh and the two before the last are Jagatī" means that the sixth, the eleventh, the fourteenth and the fifteenth are Jagatī. The remaining eight are Triṣṭubh. The fifth, this Ṛk ⟦मंथता नर⟧, has the priests for its deity or has Agni for its deity. The rest have Agni for their deity.

The application of the sūkta is to be gathered from its indications. At the Varuṇapraghāsas, at the carrying forward of the fire, this Ṛk ⟦इळायास्त्वा पद⟧ is used. And this is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦इळायास्त्वा पदे वयमग्ने विश्वेभिः स्वनीक देवैरित्यर्धर्च आरमेत्⟧, "'iḷāyās tvā pade vayam'; at 'agne viśvebhiḥ svanīka devaiḥ' he should stop at the half-verse" (⟦आ. २-१७⟧). At the same carrying forward of the fire, when the fire has been placed on the uttaravedi, the Ṛk "sīda hotaḥ" is used. ⟦निहितेऽग्नौ सीद होतः स्व उ लोके चिकित्वान्⟧, "when the fire has been set down: 'sīda hotaḥ sva u loke cikitvān'" (⟦आ. २-१७⟧), so it is laid down in the sūtra. When the fire is made to mount upon the kindling-stick, the Ṛk "ayaṃ te yoniḥ" is used. And this is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦आयं शे योनिर्ऋत्तिय इत्यरणी गार्हपत्ये प्रतितपेत्पाणे वा⟧, "with 'ayaṃ te yonir ṛtviyaḥ' he should warm the two kindling-sticks at the Gārhapatya, or his two hands" (⟦आ. ३-१०⟧).

## Ṛk 1
The sacrificer speaks to the adhvaryu and the others. This chip of the sacrificial post, which is for the fire, is here. ⟦अधिमंथनं⟧: what is to be put on top of the kindling-stick and serves as the means of churning, the rod, the cord and the rest, ⟦अस्ति⟧, is here. ⟦प्रजननं⟧, the bunch of darbha grass which serves as the means of generating the fire, ⟦कृतं⟧, has been made ready. Or else, ⟦प्रजननं⟧ is a particular way of placing the churning-rod. Since these means of churning the fire, the chip of the post and the rest, have been fetched, therefore ⟦आ⟧ ⟦भर⟧, bring, O adhvaryu, this kindling-stick, ⟦विश्वत्नीं⟧, the protectress of the people, of creatures, because it brings about fruits such as heaven by way of the Agnihotra, which is accomplished through the fire. When it has been brought, ⟦पूर्वथा⟧, as we churned the fire at the time of the establishment of the fires, or as the great seers of old churned the fire, even so ⟦मंथाम⟧, let us churn, this fire in this rite: let us make it produced by churning.

The two words read "asti" stand each at the beginning of a pāda, and so they do not lose their accent. ⟦विश्वत्नीं⟧: to the word pati, by the rule cited in ⟦विभाषा सपूर्वस्येति⟧, the suffix ṅīp is added and the letter n is substituted for the final. By the rule cited in ⟦परादिश्चंदसि बहुलमित्युत्तरपदाद्युदात्तत्वं⟧ the first syllable of the second member is udātta. ⟦भर⟧: for the root hṛñ, "to carry", in loṭ, the letter bh comes by the rule cited in ⟦हृग्रहोर्भश्चंदसीति⟧. ⟦मंथाम⟧: the root is manth, "to stir". It is the form in loṭ. The verb loses its accent. ⟦पूर्वथा⟧: after the word pūrva, in the sense of "like", comes the suffix thāl by the rule cited in ⟦प्रत्यपूर्वविश्वेमात्पाल् छंसीति⟧. By the rule cited in ⟦लितीति⟧ the syllable before the suffix is udātta.

## Ṛk 2
⟦जातवेदाः⟧, possessed of the knowledge of all things, this Agni is ⟦निहितः⟧ in the two kindling-sticks: he has been firmly placed there by the gods for the sake of the sacrifice. For this there is a comparison, in the words ⟦गर्भ इवेति⟧: as an embryo is ⟦सुधितः⟧, well placed, in ⟦गर्भिणीषु⟧, in pregnant women, even so. That Agni, who is such, is ⟦ईड्यः⟧, to be praised with words in the form of praise, ⟦दिवेदिवे⟧, every day, so that he may arise, by us ⟦मनुष्येभिः⟧, by men, ⟦हविष्मभिः⟧, who have made the oblations ready, and who for that very reason are ⟦जागृवद्धिः⟧, wakeful in the rite.

⟦अरण्योः⟧: the root is ṛ, "to go". The suffix ani comes by the rule cited in ⟦आर्ति सृधृधमीत्यादिना⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦निहितः⟧: for the root dhā before the niṣṭhā suffix in the sense of the object, the substitute hi is put by the rule cited in ⟦दधातेर्हिरिति⟧. By the rule ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the preverb keeps its original accent. ⟦सुधितः⟧: the niṣṭhā suffix comes after the root dhā in the sense of the object. Before it the substitute dhi is put because the form is given ready made in the rule cited in ⟦सुधित वसुधितेत्यादिना⟧. ⟦दिवेदिवे⟧: the root is div, "in play and other senses". The suffix ka comes, which is conditioned by a penultimate vowel of the ik class. By the rule cited in ⟦नित्यवीप्सयोरिति⟧ the word is doubled. Because the second is a repetition it is unaccented, and so the accent is that of the first word. ⟦जागृवद्धिः⟧: it is the form of the root jāgṛ, "in the ending of sleep", before the suffix kvasu. Because of the option given by ⟦द्विर्वचनप्रकरणे छंदसि वेति वक्तव्यमिति⟧, "in the section on reduplication it is to be stated: optionally in the Veda", there is no reduplication. By ⟦वसुस्रंसुध्वंस्वनडुहां दः⟧, "d for vasu, sraṃsu, dhvaṃsu and anaḍuh" (⟦पा. ८-२-७२⟧), the letter s of vasu becomes d. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 3
⟦चिकित्वान्⟧, you, the adhvaryu, who have the knowledge: upon the ⟦उत्तानायां⟧, upon the lower kindling-stick which lies face upward, ⟦अव⟧ ⟦भर⟧ the upper kindling-stick: hold it with its face downward. After that let the lower kindling-stick, ⟦सद्यः प्रवीता⟧, having had the seed poured into her at that very moment, ⟦जजान⟧, bring forth, ⟦वृषणं⟧, Agni, the showerer of desires. Then ⟦अस्य⟧, of this Agni, ⟦रुशत्⟧, destroying the darkness, ⟦पाजः⟧, light and nothing more, came to be there. After that, ⟦अरुषस्तूपः⟧, endowed with a mass of shining light, ⟦इळायास्पुत्र⟧, the Agni who has arisen from the uttaravedi, ⟦अजनिष्ट⟧, was born, in ⟦वयुने⟧, in the kindling-stick, the place of the womb.

⟦जजान⟧: the root is jan, "to beget". In liṭ used in the sense of loṭ, the causative suffix ṇi is elided by the rule cited in ⟦बहुलमन्यत्रापि संज्ञा छंदसोरिति⟧. Because the form does not end in a suffix, ām does not come. The verb loses its accent. ⟦अरुषस्तूपः⟧: he who has no ⟦रुषः⟧, anger, is aruṣa. The meaning is "shining because of freedom from anger". By the rule cited in ⟦नञ्सुभ्यामित्युत्तर⟧ the last syllable of the second member is udātta. The root is ṣṭu, "to praise". By the rule ⟦स्तौतेर्दिीर्घश्च⟧ (⟦उ- ३-३०५⟧) come the suffix pa, which is marked with k, and the lengthening of the root. "It is praised", and so stūpa is a mass of light. In the sense "he whose mass of light is aruṣa, shining", a bahuvrīhi, the accent is that of the first member. ⟦रुशत्⟧: the root is ruś, "to harm". It is the form with the suffix śatṛ. ⟦वयुने⟧: the root is aj, "in going and throwing". By the rule ⟦अजि यमि शीज्‌भ्यश्च⟧ comes the suffix unan, which has the name cit. "It goes", or "it is driven", and so vayuna is a limb. By an exchange of the accent of the suffix the first syllable is udātta. ⟦अजनिष्ट⟧: the root is janī, "to come forth". It is the form in luṅ before sic. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 4
⟦जातवेदो⟧, you in whom wisdom has arisen, O Agni! We, the adhvaryu and the others, ⟦अधि⟧, upon the earth, on the uttaravedi, ⟦नाभा⟧, that is, in the navel, in its navel-place, the middle region. That same place is specified further: ⟦इळायाः पदे⟧, in the footprint of the cow Iḍā, the daughter of Manu, that is, in the place which is the uttaravedi and has the form of Iḍā's footprint, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦हव्याय⟧ ⟦वोळ्हवे⟧, to carry the oblation, the puroḍāśa and the rest, ⟦नि धीमहि⟧, we set down. On the meaning stated there is the Brāhmaṇa: ⟦इळायास्त्वा पदे वयं नाभा पृथिव्या अधीत्येदद्वा इळायास्पदं यदुत्तरवेदीनाभिर्जातवेदो नि धीमहीति निधास्यंतो ह्येनं भवंत्यग्ने हव्याय वोळ्वव इति हव्यं हि वक्ष्यन्भवति⟧, "'You in the footprint of Iḷā we, at the navel of the earth, upon it': this indeed is the footprint of Iḷā, namely the navel of the uttaravedi. 'O Jātavedas, we set down': for they are about to set him down. 'O Agni, to carry the oblation': for he is about to carry the oblation" (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. १-२८⟧).

⟦नाभा⟧: the root is ṇah, "to bind". While iñ is carried on, by the rule cited in ⟦नहो भश्चेतीञ्⟧ the suffix iñ comes and the letter bh is substituted for the final. Because the suffix is marked with ñ there is vṛddhi of the penultimate. "It binds", and so nābhi is the middle place. By the rule cited in ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ the substitute ḍā is put for the locative ending. The accent is that of a suffix marked with ñ. ⟦धीमहि⟧: it is the form of the root dhā in liṅ. By ⟦लिजः सीयुट्⟧ (⟦पा. ३-४-१०२⟧) there is the augment sīyuṭ. The absence of reduplication is Vedic. The verb loses its accent. ⟦वोळ्ववे⟧: the root is vah, "to convey". The suffix taven comes in the sense of the infinitive. By the rule cited in ⟦सहिवहोरोदवर्णस्यॆति⟧ the vowel a of the root becomes o. Then follow the change to ḍh, the change to dh, the change to a cerebral, and the elision of ḍh. Because the suffix is marked with n, the first syllable is udātta.

## Ṛk 5
⟦नरः⟧, O leaders of the rites, adhvaryu and the others! ⟦कविं⟧, far-seeing; ⟦अद्वयंतं⟧, not practising doubleness, acting without duality, doing the rite in one and the same way with mind and with speech, and for that very reason ⟦प्रचेतसं⟧, endowed with eminent knowledge; ⟦अमृतं⟧, free from the nature of dying; ⟦सुप्रतीकं⟧, furnished with fair limbs, the flames: this Agni ⟦मंथत⟧, bring into being by churning. Moreover, O ⟦नरः⟧, leaders of the rites, adhvaryu and the others! Do you ⟦जनयत⟧, generate, ⟦पुरस्तात्⟧, at the beginning of the rite, the Agni who is such: ⟦केतुं⟧, the one who makes known, of ⟦यज्ञस्य⟧, the sacrifice, the Jyotiṣṭoma and the rest; ⟦प्रथमं⟧, who is the chief part, since all the later rites have Agni before them; ⟦सुशेवं⟧, endowed with fair happiness. The purport is: make this Agni one who is endowed with these qualities.

⟦मंथत⟧: the root is manth, "to stir". It is the form in loṭ. Because it stands at the beginning of the pāda it does not lose its accent. ⟦नरः⟧: the root is ṇīñ, "to lead". By the rule ⟦नयतेर्डिच्च⟧ (⟦उ. २-२५७⟧) comes the suffix ṛ. Because it is marked with ḍ the last vowel with what follows it is elided. "They lead the rites", or "the rites are led by them", and so they are naraḥ. Because it is a vocative it loses its accent. ⟦अद्वयंतं⟧: in the sense "he behaves like two", the suffix kvip comes in the sense of behaving, by ⟦सर्वप्रातिपदिकेभ्यः क्विप् वक्तव्य⟧, "kvip is to be stated after all nominal stems". So the verb is dvayati. Its form with the suffix śatṛ is dvayan. "Not dvayan" is advayan; him. In a tatpuruṣa the accent is that of the negative particle. ⟦सुप्रतीकं⟧: in a bahuvrīhi, by the rule cited in ⟦क्रत्वादयश्चेत्युत्तरपदाद्युदात्तत्वं⟧, the first syllable of the second member is udātta. ⟦जनयत⟧: the root is janī, "to come forth". The suffix ṇic is added. When vṛddhi would result by the rule ⟦आत उपधाया⟧, the root is treated as one marked with m by the rule cited in ⟦जनीज्यस्नु सुरंजोऽमंताश्चेति⟧, and so the vowel is short by the rule ⟦मितां ह्रस्व⟧. The verb loses its accent. ⟦सुशेवं⟧: the word śeva, which ends in the suffix van, has the udātta on the first syllable. In a bahuvrīhi, by the rule cited in ⟦बहुव्रीहावाद्युदात्तं द्वच्छंदसीत्युत्तरपदाद्युदात्तत्वं⟧, the first syllable of the second member is udātta.

## Ṛk 6
⟦यदि⟧, if the adhvaryu and the others churn Agni with ⟦बाहुभिः⟧, with their own hands, then that Agni, being born, in ⟦वनेषु⟧, in the pieces of wood, ⟦वाजी⟧, moving swiftly, ⟦अरुष⟧, shining, pleasing to all, ⟦आ वि रोचते⟧: he shines on all sides and in a special way. For this there is a comparison, in the words ⟦अश्वो नेति⟧: as a horse in ⟦वनेषु⟧, in the pleasant riding-grounds, is swift and shining, even so. For the same there is a further comparison, in the words ⟦चित्रो नेति⟧: as in ⟦यामन्⟧, that is, in the course, in the swift going, the ⟦चित्रो⟧, many-coloured, chariot of the ⟦अश्विनोः⟧, the two physicians of the gods, is shining, even so. ⟦अनिवृतः⟧, whose going is not checked by anyone, this Agni, ⟦दहन्⟧, reducing to ashes, ⟦अश्मन⟧, the stones, and ⟦तृणा⟧, the grasses, ⟦परि वृणक्ति⟧: he leaves the burnt places all around. "Such an Agni shines": so it is construed with what precedes.

⟦मंथंति⟧: the root is manth, "to stir". It is the form in laṭ. Because of the connexion with ⟦यदि⟧ it does not lose its accent. ⟦रोचते⟧: the root is ruc, "to shine", of the class beginning with bhū. It is the form in laṭ. The verb loses its accent. ⟦यामन्⟧: the root is yā, "to reach". The suffix manin comes by the rule cited in ⟦अतो मनिन्क्वनिब्बनिपश्चेति⟧. By the rule cited in ⟦सुपां सुलुगित्यादिना⟧ the locative ending is dropped. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦अनिवृतः⟧: the root is vṛñ, "to cover". Before the niṣṭhā suffix the augment iṭ is forbidden by the rule cited in ⟦यस्य विभाषेतीट्⟧. The root vṛ preceded by ni is used in the sense of warding off. "Not nivṛta" is anivṛta. The accent is that of the negative particle. ⟦वृणक्ति⟧: the root is vṛjī, "to avoid", of the class beginning with rudh. By the rule cited in ⟦चोः कुरिति⟧ the guttural is substituted. The verb loses its accent. ⟦तृणा⟧: the root is tṛh, "to harm". By the rule ⟦तृहेः क्नो हलोपश्च⟧ (⟦उ. ५-८⟧) comes the suffix kna; because it is marked with k there is no guṇa; and the letter h is elided. "It is harmed by cows and other animals", and so the word is tṛṇa. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n.

## Ṛk 7
⟦जातः⟧, arisen after the churning, and ⟦चेकितानो⟧, knowing everything, ⟦वा नी⟧, whose nature is to move constantly, ⟦विप्रः⟧, versed in the rites, and for that very reason ⟦कविशस्तः⟧, praised by the wise, by the hotṛ priests, ⟦सुदानुः⟧, a good giver of the fruit of the rite: Agni, who is of this kind, ⟦रोचते⟧, shines. The Agni whom ⟦देवासः⟧, the gods, Indra and the rest, ⟦अदधुः⟧, appointed, in ⟦अध्वरेषु⟧, in sacrifices such as the Jyotiṣṭoma, as ⟦हव्यवाहं⟧, the carrier of the oblations, him who is ⟦ईड्यं⟧, to be praised by the hotṛ and the others, ⟦विश्वविदं⟧, the knower of the whole world: "that Agni shines", so it is construed with what precedes.

⟦चेकितानः⟧: for the root kit, "to know", when the intensive suffix yaṅ has been dropped, there is guṇa of the reduplication by the rule cited in ⟦गुणो यज्लुकोरित्यभ्यासस्य⟧. By the rule cited in ⟦अभ्यस्तानामादिरित्याद्युदात्तत्वं⟧ the first syllable is udātta. ⟦कविशस्तः⟧: by the rule cited in ⟦थाधघञ् क्ताजबित्रकाणामित्यंतोदात्तत्वं⟧ the last syllable is udātta. ⟦अदधुः⟧: for the root dhā in laṅ, jus is put in place of jhi by the rule cited in ⟦सिजभ्यस्तविदिभ्यश्चॆति⟧. Because of the connexion with a form of yad the verb does not lose its accent. The accent is that of the augment aṭ.

## Ṛk 8
The sacrificer speaks. ⟦होतरिति⟧: O you who accomplish the offering, O Agni! In ⟦स्वे⟧, your own, ⟦लोके⟧, place, the navel of the uttaravedi, ⟦सीद⟧, sit: do you indeed sit down for the purpose of the offering. In the same way ⟦चिकित्वान्⟧, you who have the knowledge, ⟦सादय⟧, set, ⟦यज्ञं⟧, the performer of the sacrifice, the sacrificer, in ⟦योन्⟧, in the place, the highest world, of ⟦सुकृतस्य⟧, the enjoyment that is born of good deeds. Moreover ⟦देवानीः⟧: he protects the gods by the giving of the oblation, and so he is devāvīḥ. You, who are such, ⟦यजासि⟧, worship, ⟦देवान्⟧, the gods, with ⟦हविषा⟧, with the offering, the puroḍāśa and the rest. Then do you ⟦धाः⟧, bestow, on ⟦यजमाने⟧, on me who am performing the sacrifice, ⟦बृहत्⟧, abundant, ⟦वयो⟧, food, accompanied by cattle, sons and the rest.

⟦सीद⟧: it is the form of the root sad in loṭ. ⟦सादय⟧: it is the form in loṭ of the root sad in its causative. The accent is that of ṇic. ⟦देवानीः⟧: the root is av, "in protecting and other senses". By the rule cited in ⟦आवित्यस्तंत्रिभ्य ईरितीप्रत्ययः⟧ comes the suffix ī. The accent is that of a final member which is a kṛt formation. ⟦यजासि⟧: for the root yaj in leṭ there is the augment āṭ. ⟦धाः⟧: it is the form of the root dhā in the Vedic luṅ.

## Ṛk 9
The sacrificer speaks to the adhvaryu and the others. O ⟦सखायः⟧, you who work together in the rite! Do you ⟦कृणोत⟧, make, ⟦वृषणं⟧, the showerer of desires, ⟦धूमं⟧: "he shakes, he makes tremble", and so dhūma is Agni; make Agni who is of this kind: bring him forth by churning. If Agni is not born, then he says: ⟦अस्रेधंतो⟧, not worn out, with your ability unceasing, do you ⟦इतन⟧, attain, ⟦वाजं⟧, the battle which consists in the churning, ⟦अच्छ⟧, with your faces towards Agni. ⟦सुवीरः⟧, endowed with fair ability, this Agni is ⟦पृतनाषाट्⟧, one who prevails in the battle that is indicated by the word for army. ⟦येन⟧, by which Agni, prevailing in battle, ⟦देवासो⟧, the gods, ⟦असहंत⟧, overcame, ⟦दस्यून्⟧, the Asuras, the destroyers. The purport is: therefore do you, with strength unwasted, do the churning.

⟦कृणोत⟧: the root is kṛvi, "in harming and doing". In loṭ the substitute tap is put for the ending ta by the rule cited in ⟦तप्तनप्तनथनाश्चति⟧. Because it is marked with p there is guṇa. Because the verb stands at the beginning of the pāda it does not lose its accent. The accent is that of the suffix u. ⟦धूमं⟧: the root is dhūñ, "to shake". The suffix mak comes by the rule cited in ⟦इषियुधींधिदसीत्यादिना⟧. Because it is marked with k there is no guṇa. "He shakes, he makes tremble, the things that are to be burnt", and so dhūma is Agni. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦इतन⟧: for the root iṇ, "to go", in loṭ, the substitute tana is put for the ending ta. The verb loses its accent. ⟦पृतनाषाट्⟧: the root is ṣah, "to endure". By the rule ⟦छंदसि सह⟧ comes the suffix ṇvi. Because it is marked with ṇ there is vṛddhi of the penultimate. By the rule ⟦सहे साडः स⟧ the letter ṣ is substituted. The final member, a kṛt formation, keeps its original accent. ⟦असहंत⟧: it is the form of the root ṣah, "to endure", in laṅ. Because of the connexion with yad it does not lose its accent. ⟦दस्यून्⟧: the root is dasu, "to waste away". The suffix yuc comes by the rule cited in ⟦यजिमनिशुंधिदसिजनिभ्यो युजिति⟧. "They lay waste", and so the dasyus are enemies. By an exchange the first syllable is udātta.

## Ṛk 10
O Agni! ⟦ऋत्वियो⟧, existing in a season such as spring, ⟦अयं⟧, this one, which is being pointed out with the finger, the kindling-stick, a particular piece of wood made of palāśa, aśvattha and the like, is ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦योनिः⟧, place of origin, ⟦यतो⟧, from which kindling-stick ⟦जातः⟧, being born, you ⟦अरोचथाः⟧, you were radiant. ⟦जानन्⟧, knowing, do you ⟦आ⟧ ⟦सीद⟧ ⟦तं⟧, that particular piece of wood which is the kindling-stick: being made to mount upon that kindling-stick, stay there without a break. ⟦अथ⟧, after that, ⟦वर्धय⟧, increase, ⟦नो⟧, our, ⟦गिरः⟧, words in the form of praise. Or else, by the word for "words" those who have them are meant; and thus: increase us, the praisers: make us prosper with cattle, sons and the rest.

⟦योनिः⟧: the root is yu, "to mix". The suffix ni comes by the rule cited in ⟦वहिसित्यादिना⟧. Because it is said there to be marked with n, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦ऋत्वियः⟧: by the rule "ghas in the Veda", cited in ⟦छंदसि घसित्युशब्दात्पाप्तार्थे⟧**NOTE**{the reference text gives a reference (Pāṇini 5.1.106) after this rule; the supplied text has none}, the suffix ghas comes after the word ṛtu in the sense "arrived". The verb loses its accent. ⟦अथा⟧: by the rule cited in ⟦निपातस्य चेति⟧ there is the lengthening that belongs to the Saṃhitā. ⟦वर्धय⟧: the root is vṛdhu, "to grow". ## Ṛk 11
When the Agni who is ⟦गर्भो⟧ exists in the kindling-sticks in the form of an embryo, then that Agni ⟦उच्यते⟧, is called, Tanūnapāt, from the derivation "he does not make the bodies of the sacrificers fall, does not burn them". Or else, by the word tanū the waters are denoted, and by the word napāt offspring at one remove; and thus he is the grandson of the waters. How is this? The waters are spread out in the mid-region. From them the herbs and the trees are born. From these, indeed, is he. So it is said. ⟦यत्⟧, when, ⟦विजायते⟧, he is born in a special way, visibly, then he is ⟦आसुरः⟧, the slayer of the Asuras who obstruct the rite, and for that very reason he becomes ⟦नराशंसः⟧, to be praised by the men, by the leaders of the rites, the adhvaryu and the others. ⟦यत्⟧, when, in ⟦मातरि⟧, in the mid-region, he ⟦अमिमीत⟧, fashions, his own light, then he becomes ⟦मातरिश्वा⟧, from the derivation "he breathes, he moves, in ⟦मातरि⟧, in the mid-region". In ⟦सरीमणि⟧, in the moving, in the swift going, of an Agni of this kind, ⟦सर्गः⟧, the creation, of ⟦वातस्य⟧, the wind, ⟦अभवत्⟧, comes about. When Agni exists in the kindling-sticks in the form of an embryo, then he has the name Tanūnapāt. When he is born, then he has the name Narāśaṃsa. When he flashes in the mid-region, then he has the name Mātariśvan. So it is.

⟦तनूपात्⟧: the root is tanu, "to stretch". By the rule cited in ⟦कृषिचमितनिधनिसर्जखर्जिभ्य ऊरित्यूप्रत्ययः⟧ comes the suffix ū. By an exchange the first syllable is udātta. ⟦नपात्⟧: the root is pā, "to protect", or patḷ, "to go", in its causative. The suffix kvip comes. "He does not protect", or "he does not make fall", and so the word is napāt. Because the form is given ready made in the rule cited in ⟦नभ्राण् नपान्नवेदा इत्यादिना⟧, the negative particle remains in its original state. The accent is that of the negative particle. ⟦तनूर्न पातीति⟧, "he does not protect the bodies", and so the word is tanūnapāt. Where the original accent of the final member, a kṛt formation, would result, both members keep their original accent by the rule cited in ⟦उभे वनस्पत्यादिषु युगपदित्युभयपदप्रकृतिस्वरत्वं⟧, "both at once in vanaspati and the rest". ⟦आसुरः⟧: "this one belongs to the Asura", and so he is āsura; he is connected with him as the one who does him harm. The suffix aṇ comes by the rule cited in ⟦तस्वेदमित्यण्⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦नराशंसः⟧: the root is nṝ, "to lead". The suffix ap comes by the rule cited in ⟦यदोरबित्यप्⟧. Because the suffix is marked with p, the word nara has the udātta on the first syllable. The word formed from the root śaṃs, "to praise", which ends in ghañ, has the udātta on the first syllable. The lengthening of the word nara is by the rule "it is seen in others too", cited in ⟦नरशब्दस्यान्येषामपि दृश्यत⟧. Because the word belongs to the group beginning with vanaspati, both members keep their original accent. ⟦विजायते⟧: the root is janī, "to come forth". Because it belongs to the class beginning with div it takes śyan. The accent is that of the root. ⟦अमिमात⟧: the root is mā, "to measure, and to sound". It belongs to the class beginning with hu. By the rule cited in ⟦भृञामिदित्यभ्यासस्यॆत्वं⟧ the vowel of the reduplication becomes i. Because of the connexion with yad the verb does not lose its accent. ⟦सरीमणि⟧: the root is sṛ, "to go". By the rule ⟦हृभृसृसूभ्यः⟧**NOTE**{the reference text cites this rule with one root more in it, the root stṛ after sṛ, than the supplied text has} (⟦उ. ४-१४७⟧) comes the suffix īmanin. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n.

## Ṛk 12
O Agni! ⟦कविः⟧, wise, you have been ⟦निर्मथितः⟧, thoroughly churned out, ⟦सुनिर्मथा⟧, by a fair churning. ## Ṛk 13
⟦मर्त्यासो⟧, the mortals, the priests, ⟦अजीजनन्⟧: they brought forth by churning him who is ⟦अमृतं⟧, free from the nature of dying, and for that very reason ⟦अस्रेमाणं⟧, free from decay, ⟦वीळुजंभं⟧, having firm teeth for eating the oblation, ⟦तरणिं⟧: "through him the sacrificers cross over sin", and so taraṇi is Agni. Then, with regard to that Agni who is ⟦जातं⟧, born, the ⟦स्वसारः⟧, moving, ⟦दश⟧, ten in number, ⟦आग्रुवः⟧: "they go to the front in order to do the work", and so they are agru, the fingers; such fingers, ⟦समीचीः⟧, being joined with one another, ⟦संरभंते⟧, make a sound. The meaning is: on seeing Agni born, the adhvaryu and the others out of joy make a sound with the hollow of their two hands. For this there is a comparison, in the word ⟦पुमांसमिति⟧: as, on getting a son who is born, the father and the others who are there cry aloud out of joy, even so.

⟦आजीजनन्⟧: it is the form of the root janī, "to come forth", in its causative, in luṅ before caṅ. ⟦अस्रेमाणं⟧: the root is srivu, "in going and drying". The suffix manin comes in the sense of the action by the rule cited in ⟦मुनिन्निति⟧. There is guṇa of the short penultimate. By the rule cited in ⟦लोपो व्योर्वलीति⟧ the letter v is elided. In a bahuvrīhi, by the rule cited in ⟦नञ् सुभ्यामित्युत्तर⟧, the last syllable of the second member is udātta. ⟦तरणिं⟧: the root is tṝ, "to float, to cross". By the rule cited in ⟦अर्तिसृधृधम्यम्यश्य वित्यभ्योऽनिरित्य⟧ comes the suffix ani. There is guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦स्वसारः⟧: from the root asu, "to throw", comes the suffix ṛn by the rule cited in ⟦इत्यस्मात्तुण्यसेऋन्निति⟧. "They cast the things well in the rites", and so they are svasāraḥ. By the rule ⟦न षट् स्वस्रादिभ्य⟧ the suffix ṅīp is forbidden. Because the suffix is marked with n, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦आग्रुवः⟧: the root is ag, "to go". By the rule ⟦जत्वादयश्च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-१०२⟧) comes the suffix ru. Because the form is given ready made, the letter n is elided. Before the ending jas the substitute uvaṅ is put, because the word belongs to the group beginning with tanū. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦समीचीः⟧: from the root iṇ, "to go", by the rule ⟦समीणः⟧ (⟦उ- ४-९२⟧) come the suffix caṭ and the lengthening of the root. Because it is marked with ṭ, the suffix ṅīp comes by the rule cited in ⟦ट्टिद्दाणइत्यादिना⟧. "They come together", and so they are samīcyaḥ. Or else, the suffix kvip comes after the root añc preceded by sam. By the rule cited in ⟦समः समिति⟧ the substitute sami is put. By the rule cited in ⟦अंचतेश्चोपसंख्यानमिति⟧ the suffix ṅīp comes. By the rule ⟦अच⟧ the letter a is elided. By the rule cited in ⟦चाविति⟧ there is lengthening. By the rule cited in ⟦वा छंदसीति⟧ there is the long vowel of the same class as the preceding one. The accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦पुमांसं⟧: from the root pā, "to protect", comes the suffix ḍumsun by the rule cited in ⟦इत्यस्मात्पातेर्डुम्सुन्⟧ (⟦उ. ४-१७७⟧). By ⟦पुंसोऽसुज्⟧ (⟦पा. ७-१-८९⟧) the substitute asuṅ is put. "He protects the family", and so the word is pumān. ## Ṛk 14
⟦सनकात्⟧, the everlasting Agni, ⟦सप्तहोता⟧: he who has seven hotṛs, the hotraka priests, is saptahotā; being so, he shone forth: he shone in the place of the Gārhapatya. ⟦यत्⟧, when, in ⟦उपस्थे⟧, in the lap, of ⟦मातुः⟧, the earth, and there too in ⟦ऊधनि⟧, in the place of the udder, on the uttaravedi, he ⟦अशोचत्⟧, was radiant, then that Agni, ⟦सुरणः⟧ ("he sounds well, makes a good sound", and so he is suraṇa), being looked at by all because he is lovely, ⟦दिवेदिवे⟧, day after day, ⟦न⟧ ⟦नि⟧ ⟦मिषति⟧, does not sleep; rather, he is always awake for the sake of the sacrifice. ⟦यत्⟧, because, he ⟦अजायत⟧, arose for the sake of the rite, from ⟦जठरात्⟧, the belly, of ⟦असुरस्य⟧, the piece of wood in the form of the kindling-stick, which sends Agni forth, therefore he does not sleep: so it is construed with what precedes.

⟦आरोचत⟧: it is the form of the root ruc, "to shine", in laṅ. The verb loses its accent. ⟦अशोचत्⟧: the root is śuc, "to shine". ⟦ऊधनि⟧: the letter n in place of s is Vedic. ⟦मिषति⟧: it is the form in laṭ of the root cited in ⟦मिषुसेचन⟧, "to sprinkle". ⟦सुरणः⟧: the root is raṇ, "to sound". The suffix ṇic comes by the rule cited in ⟦तत्करोतीति⟧. After that comes the suffix khal in the sense of "without difficulty". The accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦असुरस्य⟧: from the root asu, "to throw", comes the suffix uran by the rule cited in ⟦इत्यस्मादसेरुरन्नित्युरन्मत्तयः⟧. "He throws", and so he is asura. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦जठरात्⟧: from the root janī, "to come forth", by the rule ⟦जनेररष्ठच⟧ (⟦उ ५-३८⟧) comes the suffix ara, and the letter ṭh is substituted for the final. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अजायत⟧: for the root janī, "to come forth", in laṅ before śyan, the substitute jā is put for the root by the rule cited in ⟦ज्ञाजनोर्जॆति⟧. ## Ṛk 15
⟦अमित्रायुधो⟧, fighters with the foes, the Asuras who obstruct the rite. Or else, the foes are sins; fighters with them. These Kuśikas ⟦ईरिरे⟧, send forth on all sides to Agni, ⟦ब्रह्म⟧, a hymn of praise, ⟦द्युम्नवत्⟧, joined with the oblation. And they, in ⟦दमे⟧, each in his own house, ⟦एक एक⟧, each one singly, ⟦समीपिरे⟧: they light Agni up well with oblations of clarified butter and the rest.

⟦अमित्रायुधः⟧: the root is yudh, "to fight". The suffix kvip comes. The accent is that of a final member which is a kṛt formation. The lengthening of the first member in the Saṃhitā is Vedic. ⟦प्रयाः⟧: the root is yā, "to reach". The suffix vic comes by the rule "they are seen after others too", cited in ⟦अन्नोभ्योऽपि दृश्यंत⟧. ⟦प्रथमजाः⟧: the root is janī, "to come forth". The suffix viṭ comes by the rule cited in ⟦जन सनेत्यादिना⟧. By the rule cited in ⟦विड्वनोरित्यात्वं⟧ the long ā is substituted. ⟦विदुः⟧: in the laṭ of the root vid, us is put in place of jhi by the rule cited in ⟦विदो लटो वेति⟧. ⟦ईरिरे⟧: it is the form of the root īr, "to go", in liṭ. Because it is in a mantra, ām does not come. ⟦दमे⟧: the root is damu, "to calm". The suffix ghañ comes in the sense of the instrument. By the rule cited in ⟦नोदात्तोपदेशस्यॆति⟧ vṛddhi is forbidden. The accent is that of a suffix marked with ñ. ⟦ईधिरे⟧: for the root indhī, "to shine", in liṭ, the letter n is elided because the ending is treated as marked with k by the rule cited in ⟦इंदिभवतिभ्यां चेति⟧. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 16
⟦होतरिति⟧: O you who accomplish the offering! ⟦चिकित्सः⟧, you who have the knowledge about the rites, O Agni! ⟦अद्य⟧, now, in ⟦अस्मिन्⟧, this, ⟦यज्ञे⟧, sacrifice, the Jyotiṣṭoma or another, which is ⟦प्रयति⟧, going forward, ⟦यत्⟧, since, for this reason, ⟦अवृणीमहि⟧, we chose, ⟦त्वा⟧, you: we made the choice that concerns you; therefore do you, who are such, ⟦इह⟧, in this rite, ⟦ध्रुवमयाः⟧: make the oblations given by us reach the gods in such a way that it is sure. ⟦उत⟧, and also, ⟦अशमिष्ठाः⟧: you were made calm by the gods, who were satisfied by accepting the oblation. Then, ⟦प्रजानन्⟧, knowing this sacrifice well, ⟦विद्वान्⟧, knowing everything whatsoever, do you ⟦उप याहि⟧, come near, towards ⟦सोमं⟧, this pressed Soma that is being prepared by us.

⟦प्रयति⟧: it is the form of the root iṇ, "to go", with the suffix śatṛ. By the rule ⟦शतुरनुम⟧ the case-ending is udātta. ⟦अवृणीमहि⟧: the root is vṛñ, "to choose". Because a vocative is treated as if not present, the verb stands at the beginning of the pāda, and so it does not lose its accent. ⟦अयाः⟧: it is the form of the root yā, "to reach", in laṅ before sip. The verb loses its accent. ⟦अशमिष्ठाः⟧: for the root śamu, "to calm", in its causative, in luṅ in the sense of the object, the augment iṭ comes optionally by ⟦स्यसिच्‌ सीयुट्⟧ (⟦पा. ६-४-६२⟧). Because that is treated as not having taken effect, the causative suffix ṇi is elided by the rule cited in ⟦णेरनिटीति⟧. The verb loses its accent. ⟦प्रजानन्⟧: the root is jñā, "to understand". It is the form with the suffix śatṛ. ⟦याहि⟧: it is the form of the root yā in loṭ.
