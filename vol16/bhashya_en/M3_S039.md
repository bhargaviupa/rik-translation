## Introduction

Now, in the fourth anuvāka there are fifteen sūktas. Of these, the first sūkta, of nine Ṛks (it begins with the words ⟦इंद्रं⟧ and ⟦मतिर⟧), belongs to Viśvāmitra, is in the triṣṭubh metre, and is addressed to Indra. It has been listed in order thus: ⟦इंद्रं मतिर्नव⟧. The application of the sūkta is to be understood from the indication in it.

## Ṛk 1

Viśvāmitra praises. O Indra, the hymn ⟦मतिः⟧, that is, praise, being spoken, ⟦वच्यमान⟧, that is, being said, ⟦हृदो⟧, from the heart, ⟦हृदयाद्⟧, ⟦स्तोमतष्टा⟧, made by the makers of praise, ⟦पतिं⟧, the lord of the whole world, namely you, ⟦अच्छ⟧, turning towards you, ⟦जिगाति⟧, let it go to you from every side. That praise of mine which is ⟦जागृविः⟧, performing the waking of you, the one to be praised, and which is ⟦शस्यमाना⟧, recited, ⟦विदथे⟧, ⟦यज्ञे⟧, at the sacrifice. O Indra, ⟦ते⟧, for you, the hymn that arises from me, ⟦जायते⟧, that ⟦विद्धि⟧, know, that is, ⟦जानीहि⟧. ⟦तस्य⟧: the genitive is in the sense of the accusative.

The word ⟦मतिः⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦मंत्रे वृष⟧, the suffix ktin is udātta.

The word ⟦हृदः⟧: for the word ⟦हृदय⟧ there is the substitute ⟦हृद⟧ by the rule beginning ⟦पद्मन्नोमास⟧. **NOTE**{the reference text gives the reference Pā. Sū. 6.1.63 for this rule; the supplied text gives no reference}  By the rule ⟦ऊडिदम⟧ the case-ending is udātta.

The word ⟦वच्यमाना⟧: from the root ⟦वच⟧, in the sense of speaking, the suffix yak in the passive. The absence of saṃprasāraṇa is Vedic. **NOTE**{the reference text reads this sentence differently (it does not have the sense 'absence of saṃprasāraṇa'); the supplied text is translated} The accent is that of the suffix.

The word ⟦स्तोमतष्टा⟧: from the roots ⟦तक्षू⟧ and ⟦त्वक्कू⟧, in the sense of making thin, the niṣṭhā (kta) in the passive. By the rule ⟦तृतीया कर्मणी⟧ the first member keeps the accent of its own nature.

The word ⟦जिगाति⟧: by the rule ⟦गा स्तुतौ छंदसी⟧ the root ⟦गा⟧, in the sense of praising, belongs to the juhotyādi class in the Veda. By the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧ the reduplicative syllable gets the substitute i. The accent is nighāta.

The word ⟦जायते⟧: the form in laṭ of the root ⟦जनी⟧, in the sense of coming into being. Because of its connection with the yad-construction ⟦यद्वृत्त⟧, there is no nighāta.

The word ⟦विद्धि⟧: from the root ⟦विद⟧, in the sense of knowing, in the imperative; for hi, by the rule ⟦हुझल्फॊ हेर्धि⟧, the substitute dhi. There is no nighāta, since the sentence is broken.

## Ṛk 2

O Indra, ⟦दिवश्चित्⟧, even than the shining sun, ⟦पूर्व्या⟧, that is born earlier, belonging to the time of Uṣas, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦जायमाना⟧, being born, which speech, of the nature of praise, ⟦विभवति⟧, is — this speech, ⟦शस्यमाना⟧, recited, ⟦विदथे⟧, at the sacrifice, ⟦जागृविः⟧, performing the waking of you, the one to be praised, ⟦भद्रा⟧, wearing auspicious, white-coloured ⟦वस्त्राणि⟧, garments, that is, in keeping with their fitness, splendours, ⟦वसाना⟧, wearing; ⟦अस्मे⟧, our, that has come down in the line of fathers, ⟦सनजा⟧, and therefore ancient, being so, this ⟦धीः⟧ is your praise.

The word ⟦दिवः⟧: the suffix kvip after the root div. By the rule ⟦ऊडिदम⟧ the case-ending is udātta.

The word ⟦अर्जुना⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦अर्जीर्णिलुक्च⟧, the suffix unan, with the loss of ṇi. **NOTE**{the reference text gives the reference Uṇ. Sū. 3.338 for this rule; the supplied text gives no reference} The accent is the one that comes from the marker n (nit).

The word ⟦सनजा⟧: from the root jan, by the rule that says ⟦दृश्यत⟧, 'it is seen', after other roots also, the suffix ḍa. The accent is that of the suffix.

The word ⟦पित्र्या⟧: from the word pitṛ, in the sense of 'come from that', ⟦तत आगत⟧, by the rule ⟦पितुर्यच्च⟧ the suffix yat. By the rule ⟦रीजृत⟧ the substitute rīṅ. **NOTE**{the reference text gives the reference Pā. Sū. 7.4.27 for this rule; the supplied text gives no reference} By the rule ⟦यस्य⟧ there is elision. By the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧ the word is udātta on its first syllable.

## Ṛk 3

Incidentally he praises the time of dawn. ⟦यमसूः⟧: she gives birth to the twins, the two ⟦यमौ⟧; thus ⟦यमसूः⟧ is the goddess presiding over Uṣas. She bore here, at the time of Uṣas, ⟦यमा⟧, the twin Aśvins, ⟦असूत⟧. Because the Aśvins are praised at that time, in order to praise those Aśvins the tip of my tongue, ⟦जिह्वाया⟧ ⟦अग्रं⟧, ⟦पतत्⟧, unsteady, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦अस्थात्⟧ — it stands; ⟦हि⟧ — indeed. ⟦तमोहना⟧: the two destroyers of darkness. ⟦तपुषः⟧: ⟦तपुर्दिवसः⟧, the day is tapu, since in it the sun burns. **NOTE**{for 'burns' the reference text has a different word (it reads as if from tapas); the supplied text is translated} Of that day, ⟦बुध्ने⟧, at the base, ⟦मूल⟧, ⟦एता⟧, the two who have come, ⟦मिथुना⟧, the two joined together with each other, those two Aśvins; ⟦जाता⟧, arisen, ⟦वपूंषि⟧: they are called ⟦वपूंषि⟧ because they are sown and deposited, that is, the rites, the śastras and the like, that are performed at the time of Uṣas. ⟦सचेते⟧ means that the two come together.

The word ⟦यमसूः⟧: the suffix kvip after the root ⟦पूज्⟧, in the sense of releasing a living being from the womb. The accent is that of the latter member, a krt-formation.

The word ⟦असूत⟧: the form of that same root in laṅ; since it belongs to the adādi class, the elision of śap. The accent is nighāta.

The word ⟦पतत्⟧: from the root ⟦पत्ल⟧, in the sense of going. In the matter of the accent of śatṛ, a lasārvadhātuka suffix, the accent is that of the root.

The word ⟦अस्थात्⟧: the root ⟦ष्ठा⟧, in the sense of ceasing from motion; the form in luṅ. Because of the connection with ⟦हि⟧, there is no nighāta.

The word ⟦वपूंषि⟧: the root ⟦डुवप्⟧, in the sense of spreading seed. By the rule beginning ⟦आर्तिष्यवपियजितनिधनितपिभ्यो⟧ the suffix usi, which is nit. Since ⟦निच्च⟧ has been stated, the word is udātta on its first syllable.

The word ⟦सचेते⟧: the root ⟦षच⟧, in the sense of coming together; the form in laṭ.

The word ⟦तपुषः⟧: the root ⟦तप⟧, in the sense of burning; by the rule beginning ⟦आर्तिष्यवपी⟧ the suffix usi.

The word ⟦बुध्ने⟧: the root ⟦बंध⟧, in the sense of binding; by the rule ⟦बंधेर्ब्रधिबुधी च⟧ (⟦उ. ३ ५⟧) the suffix nak. **NOTE**{the reference given in the supplied text is damaged; the reference text gives Uṇ. Sū. 3.285 for this rule} These two, ⟦ब्रथिबुधी⟧, are the substitutes for the root. The accent is that of the suffix.

The word ⟦एता⟧: the niṣṭhā (kta) in the passive of the root ⟦इण्⟧, in the sense of going. By the rule ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the accent is that of the gati.

## Ṛk 4

O Indra, of those who are engaged in the sattra, of us, ⟦पितरः⟧, the fathers, the Aṅgirases, who, when the cows have been carried off by the Paṇis, ⟦योधाः⟧, are fighters, warriors, on that account, of these ⟦मर्त्येषु⟧, among mortals, ⟦निंदिता⟧, a reviler, that is, a defamer, ⟦नकिः⟧, no one at all exists. Why? To this he says: ⟦माहिनावान्⟧, endowed with greatness, ⟦दंसनावान्⟧, possessed of deeds such as the slaying of Vṛtra, Indra, of these Aṅgirases, ⟦दृंहिता⟧, ⟦दृंहितानि⟧, made prosperous, ⟦गोत्राणि⟧, herds of cows, ⟦उत्⟧ ⟦सस्यजे⟧, set free. He gave them to those Aṅgirases.

The word ⟦निंदिता⟧: the form with the suffix tṛc of the root ⟦निदि⟧, in the sense of reproach.

The word ⟦मर्त्येषु⟧: because the word belongs to the vasvādi group, the suffix yat, which has its own sense. By the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧ the word is udātta on its first syllable.

The word ⟦योधाः⟧: the form with the suffix ac of the pacādi group of the root ⟦युध⟧, in the sense of fighting.

The word ⟦दृंहिता⟧: the form in the niṣṭhā (kta) of the roots ⟦दृह⟧ and ⟦दृहि⟧, in the sense of growth.

The word ⟦महिनावान्⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦महेरिन⟧, the suffix inaṇ. **NOTE**{the reference text gives the reference Uṇ. Sū. 2.214 for this rule; the supplied text gives no reference} Then, by the rule ⟦तदस्यास्ती⟧, the suffix matup. ⟦माहिनावावान⟧ is the name of it. By the rule ⟦मतौ बह्वचोऽनजिरादीना⟧ there is lengthening in the saṃhitā. **NOTE**{the reference text gives the reference Pā. Sū. 6.3.119 for this rule; the supplied text gives no reference}

The word ⟦गोत्राणि⟧: in the sense of 'a collection of cows', with the continuation of the rule ⟦खलगोरथा⟧, by the rule ⟦नित्रकट्य चश्च⟧, the suffix tra after the word go. **NOTE**{the reference text gives the reference Pā. Sū. 4.2.51 for this rule; the supplied text gives no reference} The accent is that of the suffix.

The word ⟦सस्यजे⟧: the form in liṭ of the root ⟦सृज⟧, in the sense of letting go.

## Ṛk 5

**NOTE**{the reference text has nothing in this block before the gloss of 'sakhibhiḥ'; the passage up to the end of the sūtra reference is found only in the supplied text, and the last words of the sūtra quotation are damaged so that their construction is not clear}

In the Daśapeya, in the niṣkevalya, this Ṛk ⟦सखा ह यत्र⟧ comes before the ⟦निविद्वानीयस्य⟧. And it is put in sūtra form: ⟦सखा ह यत्र सविभिर्नवग्वै⟧, thus, ⟦निविद्वानीययोराद्दे⟧ (⟦आ- ९-३⟧).

⟦सखिभिः⟧: with friends, ⟦समानख्यानैः⟧, equally famed; ⟦सत्वभिः⟧, sitting on all sides around Indra; ⟦नवग्वैः⟧. Some Aṅgirases, Medhātithi and the others, performed a sattra for nine months and obtained the fruit; others performed it for ten months. Of these, those who, having performed the sattra for nine months, rose up with the fruit obtained are ⟦नवग्वाः⟧. With those Aṅgirases, ⟦सत्वभिः⟧, ⟦सखा⟧, the friend, ⟦समानख्यान⟧, equally famed Indra, ⟦यत्र⟧, that is, ⟦यस्मिन्⟧, in which cave, aiming at the cows shut in by the Paṇis, in the manner of ⟦अभिज्ञु⟧, that is, ⟦अभिगतजानुकं⟧, with the knees drawn near, ⟦अनुग्मन्⟧, following, Indra ⟦तत्⟧, there, in the cave, with the ⟦दशग्वैः⟧. Those who, having performed the sattra for ten months, rose up with the fruit obtained are ⟦दशग्वाः⟧. Together with ten Aṅgirases, ten in number, ⟦क्षियंतं⟧, dwelling, ⟦तमसि⟧, in the darkness that is in the cave, ⟦सत्यं⟧, true, of real light, ⟦सूर्यं⟧, the sun, he ⟦विवेद⟧, found, that is, obtained. Then, having obtained the light, that Indra brought those cows and gave them to the Aṅgirases: this is the idea. **NOTE**{the reference text has here the further words 'āhau pūraṇau'; the supplied text lacks them}

The word ⟦सखा⟧: the root ⟦ख्या⟧, in the sense of declaring; by the rule ⟦समाने ख्यश्चोदात्त⟧ the suffix iṇ; it is also ḍit. By the rule ⟦समानस्य छंदसी⟧ the substitute sa for samāna. Since ⟦उदात्त⟧ is stated, it is understood from the capability of the rule that the udātta belongs to the upapada. The word is udātta on its first syllable.

The word ⟦नवग्वैः⟧: from the root gam the Uṇādi suffix ḍva. In a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member.

The word ⟦अभिज्ञु⟧: the suffix jñu, which is enjoined by the rule ⟦प्रसंभ्यां जानुनो⟧, occurs, by reversal, after abhi.

The word ⟦सत्वभिः⟧: from the root ⟦षद्ध⟧, in the senses of withering, going and sinking, by the rule ⟦ईरसद्योस्तुट् च⟧ the suffix kvanip, and the augment tuṭ. Since it is nit, the word is udātta on its first syllable.

The word ⟦अनुग्मन्⟧: the participle of the root gam with the suffix śatṛ; with the elision of śap made by the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧, by the rule beginning ⟦गमहन⟧ the elision of the penultimate. It is a prādi-compound. The accent is that of śatṛ.

The word ⟦विवेद⟧: from the root ⟦विद⟧, in the sense of knowing, the form in liṭ, with ṇal.

The word ⟦क्षियंतं⟧: the form with śatṛ of the root ⟦क्षि⟧, in the senses of dwelling and going.

## Ṛk 6

⟦इंद्रः⟧ ⟦उस्रियायां⟧, in the cow that flows forth in the sequence of milk, curds and so on, that is rich in enjoyments, ⟦संभृतं⟧, gathered, that is, ⟦संपादितं⟧, prepared, ⟦मधु⟧, milk and the like, ⟦विवेद⟧, that is, ⟦अज्ञासीत्⟧, knew. Then, for that purpose, ⟦पद्वत्⟧, possessed of feet, ⟦शफवत्⟧, possessed of hoofs, the wealth of cows he ⟦विवेद⟧, that is, obtained. How he obtained it is told: ⟦नमे⟧ ⟦गोः⟧. ⟦गोः⟧: the genitive is in the sense of the accusative. The cow carried off by the asuras called Paṇis he ⟦नमे⟧, made one that was brought. Likewise that Indra, ⟦दक्षिणावान्⟧, generous and capable, ⟦गुहा⟧, in the cave, ⟦हितं⟧, placed, ⟦गुह्यं⟧, hidden, and therefore ⟦अप्सु⟧, in the intermediate region, ⟦गूढं⟧, ⟦गूढचारिणं⟧, moving hidden, ⟦मायिनमसुरं⟧, in the right hand, ⟦दक्षिणे⟧ ⟦हस्ते⟧, ⟦दधे⟧, held, that is, ⟦दधार⟧, seized: this is the sense. In the second aṣṭaka (⟦२-६-३⟧), in the varga ⟦श्रुधी हव⟧, this ⟦गुहा हित⟧ has been well stated. **NOTE**{the reference text gives here the reference Ṛk Saṃhitā 2.11.5; the supplied text gives 2-6-3}

The word ⟦उस्रियायां⟧: the manner of its formation has been well stated before.

The word ⟦विवेद⟧: the form in liṭ of the two roots ⟦विद⟧, in the sense of knowing, and ⟦विद्ल⟧, in the sense of obtaining.

The word ⟦शफवत्⟧: by ⟦शफोऽस्यास्ती⟧, the suffix matup. By the rule ⟦प्रस्वनुड्भ्यां मतु⟧ the matup is udātta.

The word ⟦नमे⟧: the form in liṭ of the root ⟦णमु⟧, in the sense of bowing. Since doubling is optional in the Veda, there is no doubling here. There is no nighāta, since the sentence is broken.

The word ⟦गुह्यं⟧: from the root ⟦गुहू⟧, in the sense of covering, the suffix kyap, by the statement that 'optionally' must be said after śaṃs, ⟦गुहिहुहिभ्यो⟧. Because it is kit, there is no guṇa. Because the suffix is pit and so anudātta, the accent is that of the root.

The word ⟦दधे⟧: the form in liṭ of the root dhā.

The word ⟦दक्षिणावान्⟧: the root ⟦दक्ष⟧, in the sense of growth; by the rule ⟦द्रुदक्षिभ्यामिनन्⟧ the suffix inan. Then, by ⟦तदस्यास्ती⟧, the suffix matup. By the rule ⟦संहितायामन्येषामपि दृश्यत⟧ the first member is lengthened. The accent is that of the nit-suffix.

## Ṛk 7

⟦तमसः⟧, from the night, ⟦रात्रेः⟧, from the side of the night, ⟦विजानन्⟧, knowing in a special way, that is, ⟦प्रादुर्भवन्⟧, coming into manifestation, Indra, who is the sun, ⟦वृणीत⟧, shared out ⟦ज्योतिः⟧, the light, which carries on the business of the world. Or: ⟦तमसः⟧, from darkness in the form of Vṛtra and the like, ⟦विजानन्⟧, coming into manifestation; having killed Vṛtra he obtained the light as his share. We, ⟦दुरितात्⟧, from evil, that is, from sin, ⟦आरे⟧, ⟦दूरे⟧, far away, being free from sin, ⟦अभीके⟧, in a place free from fear, ⟦स्याम⟧, may we be, that is, ⟦भवेम⟧. O Indra, ⟦सोमपाः⟧, drinker of Soma, and therefore ⟦सोमवृद्ध⟧, grown at the Soma-drinkings, ⟦पुरुतमस्य⟧: he who ⟦तामयति⟧ makes weary, that is, makes languish, many enemies, ⟦पुरून्⟧, is ⟦पुरुतमः⟧; of such a one, ⟦कारोः⟧, the priest who performs praise, ⟦इमाः⟧, these ⟦गिरः⟧, speeches, those of the nature of praise, ⟦जुषस्व⟧, enjoy, that is, ⟦सेवस्व⟧.

The word ⟦वृणीत⟧: the form in laṅ of the root ⟦वृच⟧, in the sense of sharing.

The word ⟦विजानन्⟧: the root ⟦ज्ञा⟧, in the sense of understanding. Knowing in a special way is the manifesting of light. The accent is that of the suffix.

The word ⟦अभीके⟧: that in which there is no fear. Then, by the rule ⟦ततः शेषाद्विभाषा⟧ (⟦पा. ५-४-१५४⟧), the suffix kap. When final udātta was due by the rule ⟦नञ्सुभ्या⟧, by the rule ⟦कपि पूर्वं⟧ (⟦पा. ६-२-१७३⟧) the preceding member has the final udātta.

The word ⟦पुरुतमस्य⟧: from the causative of the root ⟦तमु⟧, in the sense of languishing, with the suffix ac of the pacādi group; by the rule ⟦परादितृंदसि बहुल⟧ the latter member is udātta on its first syllable.

## Ṛk 8

O Indra, ⟦ज्योतिः⟧, the sun, the illuminator of the world, ⟦यज्ञाय⟧, for the performing of rites such as the Agnihotra, ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth, ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦स्यात्⟧, let him accompany; ⟦अनुभवतु⟧, let him accompany, ⟦प्रकाशयतु⟧, let him illuminate. We, ⟦भूरेः⟧, of abundant ⟦दुरितस्य⟧, evil, that is, sin, ⟦आरे⟧, ⟦दूरे⟧, far away, ⟦स्याम⟧, may be, that is, ⟦भवेम⟧. O ⟦वसवः⟧, Indra and the others, who cause all to dwell, ⟦सुपारासः⟧, ⟦सुपाराः⟧, having good pāra, ⟦शोभनपाराः⟧, who can be made to face by praise, you give ⟦भूरि⟧ ⟦चित्⟧, exceedingly abundant, ⟦बर्हणावत्⟧, wealth endowed with prosperity, to the mortal, ⟦मर्त्यस्य⟧, ⟦तुजतः⟧, who gives much. Here a suitable verb is to be supplied.

The word ⟦स्यात्⟧: the form in liṅ of the root as. For the verb as when it follows anu, by the rule ⟦प्रादुर्भामस्तिर्यच् पर⟧ there is the change to ṣ in the saṃhitā. **NOTE**{the reference text gives the reference Pā. Sū. 8.3.87 for this rule; the supplied text gives no reference} The accent is nighāta.

The word ⟦तुजतः⟧: the roots ⟦तुजि पिजि लजि⟧, in the senses of injuring, giving and dwelling. By the rule ⟦शतुरनुम⟧ the case-ending is udātta.

The word ⟦बर्हणावत्⟧: from the root ⟦वृहि⟧, in the sense of growth, in the abstract sense, the Uṇādi suffix yu. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 9

O Indra, ⟦वाजसात्⟧: that in which there is the gaining of ⟦वाजस्य⟧, food, is this ⟦वाजसातिः⟧. ⟦अस्मिन्⟧ ⟦भरे⟧: ⟦भरः⟧ is battle, since by it the warriors bear the fortune of victory. In that battle, ⟦शुनं⟧, ⟦शूनम⟧, swollen, increased by enthusiasm; or ⟦शुनं⟧ is a word for happiness; one who makes happiness, ⟦मघवानं⟧, possessed of wealth, and therefore ⟦इंद्रं⟧, possessed of unsurpassed lordship, ⟦नृतमं⟧, the best leader of the whole world, you ⟦हुवेम⟧, may we call. ⟦कुशिका⟧: we, the Kuśikas, may call for the sacrifice. Likewise ⟦शृण्वंतं⟧, hearing the praise that is made by us, ⟦उग्रं⟧, terrifying to enemies, ⟦समत्सु⟧, in battles, ⟦वृत्राणि⟧, all the rakṣasas marked by Vṛtra, ⟦घ्नंतं⟧, injuring, ⟦धनानां⟧, of the riches belonging to enemies, ⟦संजितं⟧, the conqueror of all **NOTE**{the gloss word after the one meaning 'conqueror' is damaged in the supplied text and differs in the reference text, which has a different damaged form; 'conqueror of all' is a guess}, you, ⟦ऊतये⟧, for protection, may we call.

The word ⟦शुनं⟧: the root ⟦टुओश्व⟧, in the senses of going and growing; in the niṣṭhā, by the rule ⟦यस्य विभाष⟧ the prohibition of iṭ. Since it belongs to the yajādi group, saṃprasāraṇa. The non-lengthening is Vedic. The accent is that of the suffix.

The word ⟦हुवेम⟧: from the root hve, by the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧ saṃprasāraṇa having been made, and the elision of śap having been made, the form in liṅ. The accent is nighāta.

The word ⟦भरे⟧: the root ⟦भ्रञ्⟧, in the sense of bearing; the suffix gha. Because it belongs to the vṛṣādi group, it is udātta on its first syllable.

The word ⟦वाजसात्⟧: the root ⟦कणु⟧, in the sense of giving; the suffix ktin in the abstract sense. **NOTE**{the reference text has a different root here; the supplied root is translated} By the rule beginning ⟦जनसन⟧ there is ā in place of the nasal. In a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member.

The word ⟦समत्सु⟧: the root ⟦आद⟧, in the sense of eating, with the prefix sam, the suffix kvip. They devour the lives of warriors; thus ⟦समदः⟧ are battles. The accent is that of the latter member, a krt-formation.

The word ⟦घ्नंतं⟧: the root ⟦हन⟧, in the senses of injuring and going; the form with śatṛ; by the rule beginning ⟦गमहन⟧ the elision of the penultimate. By the rule ⟦हो हंते⟧ the change to gh.

The word ⟦संजितं⟧: the root ⟦जि⟧, in the sense of conquering; the suffix kvip.
