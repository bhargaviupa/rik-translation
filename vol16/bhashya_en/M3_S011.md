## Introduction

The eleventh sūkta, beginning ⟦अग्निर्होता⟧, has nine Ṛks; it is Viśvāmitra's, in the gāyatrī metre, and addressed to Agni, because it is so recorded in the Anukramaṇī: ⟦अग्निर्होता गायत्र⟧…. This sūkta is used in the gāyatrī metre of the Prātaranuvāka and of the Āśvina-śastra; for the sūtra lays down: ⟦अग्निर्होताग्न इळेति चतस्रः⟧, "the sūkta that begins with the first two words, and the four verses that begin with the words that follow them" (⟦आ. ४-१३⟧). At the Ādhāna, in the first Pavamāna-iṣṭi, this Ṛk, the one beginning ⟦अग्निर्होता⟧, is the yājyā of the Sviṣṭakṛt, and ⟦स हव्यवाळमर्त्य⟧ is the puronuvākyā. In the second Pavamāna-iṣṭi ⟦साह्वानिति⟧ is the anuvākyā of the Sviṣṭakṛt. And the sūtra lays down: ⟦साह्वान्विश्वा अभियुजोऽग्निमाळे पुरोहितमिति संयाज्ये⟧, "the two saṃyājyās are the verses that begin thus" (⟦आ. २-१⟧). In the Ukthyas of the Abhiplava, at the third pressing, in the śastra of the Maitrāvaruṇa, the triplet beginning ⟦प्रयांसीति⟧ is the optional anurūpa. On this there is the sūtra: ⟦प्र वो वाजा अभिद्यवोऽभि प्रयांसि वाहसा⟧ (⟦आ. ७-८⟧).

## Ṛk 1

Viśvāmitra praises. ⟦होता⟧, the invoker of the gods; ⟦पुरोहितः⟧, the one who does good by bringing about the desired fruit at the very front. Or else: placed, set down, in front, in the eastern part, in the form of the āhavanīya; hence ⟦पुरोहितः⟧. ⟦विचर्षणि⟧, the one who sees in a special way, ⟦अध्वरस्य⟧, the sacrifice — because ⟦विचर्षणिर्विश्वचर्षणिरिति⟧ is read among the names for that. ⟦स⟧, that Agni who is such, ⟦वेद⟧, knows, ⟦यज्ञं⟧, the sacrifice, the Agniṣṭoma and the rest, ⟦आनुषक्⟧, joined together in due order.

⟦वेद⟧: the root is vid, "to know". By ⟦विदो लटो वेति⟧ ṇal is the substitute for tip. The light penultimate takes guṇa. The accent is lost. ⟦अनुषक्⟧: "it attaches itself on all sides", hence ānuṣak. The root is sañj, "to cling". S takes the place of the ṣ of the root. The suffix kvip is by ⟦क्विप् चेति⟧. The penultimate is elided by ⟦अनिदिताम⟧. The ṣ is by ⟦उपसर्गात्सु-नोतीति⟧.

## Ṛk 2

⟦सः⟧, he, ⟦हव्यवाट्⟧, the carrier of the oblation, ⟦अमर्त्यः⟧, free from the nature of dying, ⟦उशिक्⟧, desiring the oblation, ⟦दूतो⟧, the messenger of the gods, is ⟦चनोहितः⟧, placed for canas, for the food that consists of the oblation. Or else ⟦चनोहितः⟧: "he for whom canas, the food that consists of the oblation, is placed" is canohita. That Agni who is such ⟦समृण्वति⟧, comes together, ⟦धिया⟧, with understanding. He expresses the wish: "May Agni, who is endowed with understanding about the rite, make us too endowed with understanding of that kind".

⟦हव्यवाट्⟧: the root is vah, "to carry". The suffix ṇvi is by ⟦वहश्चेति⟧. There are the changes to ḍh, to jaś and to car. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦उशिक्⟧: the root is vaś, "to desire". With iji continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix iji comes by ⟦वशेः किच्च⟧. Because it is kit there is saṃprasāraṇa by ⟦ग्रहिज्येति⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦चनो-हितः⟧: the word canas ends in asun by ⟦चायतेरन्ने प्रस्वश्च⟧. Because of the word ca in it, the augment nuṭ comes. Before it the y is elided. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. As it has the name gati by ⟦पुनश्चनसोश्चंदसि गतिसंज्ञा वक्तव्या⟧, "it is to be stated that punar and canas have the name gati in the Veda" (⟦पा १-४-६०-२⟧), it keeps its original accent by ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧. But in the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦धिया⟧: the case-ending is udātta by ⟦सावेकाच⟧. ⟦ऋण्वति⟧: the root is ṛṇ, "to go". In leṭ the suffix u comes because the root belongs to the class beginning with tan. The augment aṭ is by ⟦लेटोऽडाटाव⟧.

## Ṛk 3

⟦केतुः⟧, the one who makes known, ⟦यज्ञस्य⟧, the sacrifice, the Agniṣṭoma and the rest, ⟦पूर्व्यः⟧, being in the earlier time, ancient, ⟦सः⟧, that Agni who is such, ⟦चेतति⟧ ⟦धिया⟧, by understanding; he knows everything. ⟦अस्य⟧, of this Agni who is such, the ⟦अर्थं⟧, whose nature is to go, the splendour, is ⟦तरणि⟧ ⟦हि⟧, indeed the one that carries across the darkness seen in this sky; that is to say, it is what removes it.

⟦चेतति⟧: the root is cit, "to perceive". The accent is lost. ⟦अर्थं⟧: the root is ṛ, "to go". The suffix than is by ⟦उषि-कुषिगर्तिभ्यस्थन्निति⟧. There is guṇa. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦तरणि⟧: the root is tṝ, "to float and to cross". The suffix ani is by the rule beginning ⟦आर्तिसृभृधधमी⟧. There is guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 4

⟦देवा⟧, the gods, the eaters of the oblation, ⟦अकृण्वत⟧, made, ⟦अग्निं⟧, this Agni — who is ⟦सहसः सूनुं⟧, the son of strength, because he is produced by strength in the form of churning, ⟦सनश्रुतं⟧, renowned as being eternal, and ⟦जातवेदसं⟧, one in whom understanding has arisen — ⟦वह्निं⟧, the carrier of the oblations.

⟦सनश्रुतं⟧: the root is śru, "to hear". The suffix kta is in the sense of the object. By ⟦कृतीया कर्मणीति⟧ the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦वह्निं⟧: the root is vah, "to carry". With ni continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix ni comes by the rule beginning ⟦वहिश्री⟧. ⟦अकृण्वत⟧: the root is kṛv, "to harm and to do". Because the root is idit, num comes in. By ⟦कृण्वोरच्च⟧ there are the suffix u and a as the substitute for the final. In laṅ the ātmanepada is by transposition. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 5

⟦पुरएता⟧, the one who goes in front by showing the right path, ⟦विशां⟧, of the creatures, ⟦मानुषीणां⟧, born of Manu; for that very reason ⟦तूर्णि⟧, in haste to favour those creatures by setting them to the Vedic rites, free from sloth; ⟦रथो⟧, like a chariot because he carries the oblations; ⟦सदा⟧, at all times, ⟦नवो⟧, new in that rite, fresh because he is churned out again: the Agni who is of this kind is ⟦अदाभ्यः⟧, not to be harmed. The meaning is that he is not to be slighted by anyone.

⟦अदाभ्यः⟧: the root is dambh, "to deceive". By ⟦दभेश्चति वक्तव्यमिति⟧, "it is to be stated that it comes after dabh too", ṇyat comes in the sense of the object. The penultimate takes vṛddhi. "Not dābhya", hence adābhya. In the compound with nañ, by ⟦तत्पुरुषे तुल्यार्थेति⟧ the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦पुरएता⟧: the root is iṇ, "to go". The suffix tṛc is by ⟦ण्वुल्बचाविति⟧. There is guṇa. Because it is cit the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦मानुषीणां⟧: the suffix añ is by ⟦मनो-र्जातावण्यत् शुक् च⟧. Ṅīp comes by ⟦टिड्ढाणञ्ति⟧. Because it is ñit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦तूर्तिः⟧: after the root tvar, "to hurry", with ni continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix ni comes by ⟦वहिश्रिश्रुयुद्रुग्लाहात्वरिभ्यो निदिति⟧. Ūṭh comes by the rule beginning ⟦ज्वरत्वरे⟧. Because the suffix is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. In the Saṃhitā the lengthening is by ⟦ढ्रलोपे पूर्वस्य⟧. ⟦रथः⟧: the suffix kthan is by ⟦हनिकुषिनीरमिकाशिभ्य⟧. Because it is kit the nasal is elided: ⟦कित्वादनुदा-त्तोपदेतेत्यादिनानुनासिकलोपः⟧. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦सदा⟧: because it is read among the words beginning with svar as having the udātta on the first syllable, the udātta is on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 6

⟦साह्वान्⟧, overcoming, setting at naught by his own strength, ⟦विश्वाः⟧, all, ⟦अभियुजो⟧, the armies that attack; for that very reason ⟦अमृक्तः⟧, unharmed by enemies; ⟦क्रतुः⟧, the maker, ⟦देवानां⟧, of the gods, their nourisher by giving them the oblation: the Agni who is such is ⟦तुविश्रवस्तमः⟧. The word tuvi denotes "many", because ⟦तुवि पुरु⟧ is read among the names for that. The word śravas denotes food, because it is stated in the Nirukta: ⟦श्रव इत्यन्ननाम श्रूयते⟧, "śravas is a name of food; it is heard" (⟦नि. १०-३⟧**NOTE**{the supplied reference is ni. 10-3; the reference text has 18-3 here}). He is endowed in the highest degree with food of many kinds. The sense is: since this is so, may he make us too endowed with food of many kinds.

⟦साह्वान्⟧: of the root sah, "to endure", in kvasu, because the word is laid down ready-made by ⟦दाश्वान्साह्वान्मीढ्वांश्चति⟧, there is no reduplication and no augment iṭ, and there is the parasmaipada. The accent is that of kvasu. ⟦क्रतुः⟧: ⟦कृञाः कतुः⟧: katu comes after kṛ. Because it is kit there is no guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦तुविश्रवस्तमः⟧: the root is tu, "to subsist, to harm and to fill". After it comes the uṇādi suffix ki. Because it is kit there is no guṇa. There is the substitute uvaṅ. The udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦श्रवः⟧: the root is śru, "to hear". "Food is heard of": the suffix asun is in the sense of the object. "In the highest degree tuviśravas": in the sense of surpassing, tamap comes by ⟦तमबिति⟧. In the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent.

## Ṛk 7

⟦दाश्वान्⟧, the giver of the oblations, ⟦मर्त्यो⟧, the man, the sacrificer, ⟦अभि⟧ ⟦अश्नोति⟧, obtains on every side, ⟦प्रयांसि⟧, foods, ⟦वाहसा⟧, through Agni, the carrier of the oblations. Moreover he also obtains ⟦क्षयं⟧, a house, from ⟦पावकशो-चिषः⟧, Agni of purifying brightness.

⟦प्रयांसि⟧: the root is prī, "to satisfy". After this root, which here includes the sense of the causative, comes the suffix asun. "They satisfy the eater", hence prayāṃsi, foods. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦वाहसा⟧: with ṇit continuing from the preceding rule, asun comes by ⟦वहिहाधाङ्छंदसी⟧. Because it is ṇit the penultimate takes vṛddhi. The accent is that of a nit suffix. ⟦दाश्वान्⟧: of the root dāś, "to give", in kvasu, because the word is laid down ready-made by ⟦दाश्वानाह्वानिति⟧, the augment iṭ and the reduplication do not come. The accent is that of the suffix kvasu. ⟦अश्नोति⟧: the root is aś, "to pervade". The parasmaipada is by transposition. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 8

We, ⟦जातवेदसो⟧, in whom understanding has arisen, ⟦विप्रासो⟧, the wise, the hotṛ and the others, may we ⟦परि⟧ ⟦अश्याम⟧, obtain on every side, ⟦विश्वानि⟧, all, ⟦सुधिता⟧, well placed, desired, goods, ⟦मन्मभिः⟧, by praises worthy to be thought upon, that relate to you, ⟦अग्नेः⟧. Or else ⟦जातवेदस⟧ is an epithet of Agni. "He knows all that are born", hence jātavedas; the meaning is "all-knowing". To be jātavedas in this way befits Agni alone; so it is right that the word should be his epithet.

⟦सुधिता⟧: after the root dhā, "to hold and to nourish", in the niṣṭhā, with chandasi, "in the Veda", continuing from the preceding rule, the word is laid down ready-made by the rule beginning ⟦सुधितवसुधिते⟧. The śi is elided by ⟦शेषृंदसि बहुलमिति⟧. By ⟦गतरनंतर⟧ the preverb keeps its original accent. ⟦अश्याम⟧: the root is aś, "to pervade". The suffix śnu is elided by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧. The parasmaipada is by transposition. Yāsuṭ comes by ⟦यासुट् परस्मैपदेषूदात्त⟧. The s is elided by ⟦लिज: सलोप⟧. Because the stem does not end in a, the substitute iy does not come. The s of mas is elided by ⟦नित्यं जित⟧. The accent is lost. ⟦मन्मभिः⟧: the root is man, "to understand". The suffix manin is by ⟦अन्वेभ्योऽपि दृश्यंत⟧. The accent is that of a nit suffix.

## Ṛk 9

O Agni! ⟦सनिषामहे⟧, we share in, ⟦विश्वानि⟧, all, ⟦वार्या⟧, the riches that are to be chosen, to be shared in, ⟦वाजेषु⟧, in battles; because ⟦देवासः⟧, the gods to whom we have sacrificed before, in order to give the fruit that we desire, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦ईरिरे⟧, have gone on every side, ⟦त्वे⟧, into you. The meaning is that they have entered into you. The sense is: in every way may we obtain the fruit from you.

⟦वार्या⟧: the root is vṛ, "to share in". The suffix ṇyat is by ⟦महलोर्ण्यदिति⟧. The śi is elided by ⟦शेश्चंदसि बहुलमिति⟧. The udātta on the first syllable is by the rule beginning ⟦ईडवंदे⟧. ⟦वाजेषु⟧: because the word belongs to the group beginning with vṛṣa, the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦सनिषामहे⟧: the roots are van and san, "to share in". ⟦धात्वादेः षः सः⟧: s takes the place of the ṣ at the beginning of the root. In leṭ, sip comes by ⟦सिब्बहुलमिति⟧. Because sip is ārdhadhātuka, the augment iṭ comes. The augment aṭ is by ⟦डाटाव⟧…. The ātmanepada is by transposition. The accent is lost. ⟦त्वे⟧: for the word yuṣmad in the locative there is the substitute tva. By ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ śe is the substitute. By ⟦शे⟧ (⟦पा. १-१-१३⟧) it has the name pragṛhya. ⟦ईरिरे⟧: irec is the substitute for the jha of the liṭ that follows the root īr, "to go". The ām taught by ⟦इजादेश गुरुमत⟧ does not come, because this is a mantra. The accent is lost.
