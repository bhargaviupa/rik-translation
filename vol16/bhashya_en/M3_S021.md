## Introduction

The hymn beginning ⟦इमं न इति⟧ is the ninth hymn and has five Ṛks. On it the Anukramaṇikā says this: ⟦पाद्ये अनुष्टुभौ विराड्रूपा सतो बृहती अंत्येति⟧, "Imaṃ naḥ: the two after the first are anuṣṭubhs; the last is a virāḍrūpā and a satobṛhatī." The second and the third are anuṣṭubhs; the fifth is a virāḍrūpā and a satobṛhatī. Because nothing is stated of them, the first and the fourth are triṣṭubhs. Of this hymn also Gāthin is the seer. This hymn is used in the animal sacrifice, at the recitation for the drops. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦जुषस्व सप्रथस्तम⟧ … ⟦मिमं नो यज्ञं⟧ (⟦आ. ३-४⟧), "'Juṣasva saprathastamam', 'imaṃ no yajñam'."

## Ṛk 1

Gāthin prays. O ⟦जातवेदो⟧, you in whom knowledge has arisen, Agni, place (⟦धेहि⟧) this (⟦इमं⟧) ⟦यज्ञं⟧, the animal sacrifice, which belongs to us (⟦नः⟧), among the ⟦अमृतेषु⟧, the gods to be worshipped, who are free from the nature of dying: deliver it to them as dependent on them. And do you ⟦जुषस्व⟧, enjoy, ⟦इमा⟧, these, ⟦हव्या⟧, oblations, which are being given by us. Moreover, ⟦होतरिति⟧, O caller of the gods — because it is handed down in the sacred text, ⟦अग्निर्वै देवानां⟧ and what follows, "Agni indeed is the hotṛ of the gods"**NOTE**{for this quotation the reference text gives the reference Aitareya-brāhmaṇa 3. 14; the supplied text gives none} — O Agni who are such, ⟦निषद्य⟧, having sat down on the vedi, being ⟦प्रथमः⟧, the first of all the priests, of ⟦मेदसो⟧, the oblation called the vapā, and of ⟦घृतस्य⟧, ghee, ⟦स्तोकानां⟧ — the genitive is in the sense of the accusative —: the drops that there are of the fat and of the ghee, those eat (⟦अशान⟧) in an eminent way; drink them, that is the meaning.

⟦धेहि⟧: a form in loṭ of the root dhā. ⟦जुषस्व⟧: a form in loṭ of the root ⟦जुषी प्रीतिसेवनयोरि⟧, juṣ in the senses of being pleased and resorting to, which takes the ātmanepada. ⟦अशान⟧: in the loṭ of the root ⟦अश भोजन⟧, aś in the sense of eating, the substitute śānac comes by the rule ⟦हलः श्यः शानज्ञौ⟧ (⟦सा. ३-१-८३⟧). After that the ending hi is dropped by the rule on hi after short a. The verb loses its accent. ⟦प्रथमः⟧: the root is ⟦प्रख्याने⟧, prath in the sense of being renowned. "Prathama: from him the second and the rest spread out." The suffix amac comes by the rule ⟦प्रथेरमच्⟧ (⟦उ. ५-६८⟧)**NOTE**{the supplied text gives the Uṇādi reference as 5-68; the reference text has 5. 746: which is to stand?}. Because the suffix is marked with c, the final syllable is udātta. ⟦निषद्य⟧: a form with the suffix lyap of the root sad, in the senses of breaking up, going and sinking down. The change to ṣ comes by the rule ⟦सदेरप्रतेरिति⟧. By the rule on a suffix marked with l, the syllable before the suffix is udātta.

## Ṛk 2

⟦पावक⟧, O purifier of sin, O Agni, ⟦स्वधर्मन्⟧, in this sacrifice with its subsidiary parts, whose dharmas depend on itself, for your eating, and ⟦देववीतये⟧, for the eating of the gods who are to be worshipped, the ⟦स्तोका⟧, the drops, of ⟦मेदसो⟧, of the oblation in the form of fat, ⟦घृतवंतः⟧, accompanied by ghee, ⟦श्चोतंति⟧, flow. Or else ⟦देववीतये⟧ is to be construed as "for the vīti of you, the god". Therefore give (⟦धेहि⟧) to us (⟦नो⟧) ⟦वार्यं⟧, what is to be chosen, to be taken as one's share, ⟦श्रेष्ठ⟧, the best, wealth.

⟦श्चोतंति⟧: a form in laṭ of the root ⟦श्चुतिर् क्षरण⟧, ścut in the sense of flowing. ⟦स्वधर्मन्⟧: "that which has dharmans that are its own, that are its subsidiary parts": in the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent. The locative ending is dropped by the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧. ⟦देववीतये⟧: that act of eating in which there is the vīti, the pleasure, of the gods is devavīti. In the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦श्रेष्ठं⟧: after the word praśasya the suffix iṣṭhan comes in the sense of excess. The substitute śra comes by the rule ⟦प्रशस्यस्य श्र⟧. There is guṇa. The accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦धेहि⟧: the loṭ of the root dhā. ⟦वार्यं⟧: the root is vṛ in the sense of taking as one's share. The suffix ṇyat comes by the rule on ṇyat after roots in ṛ and in a consonant. The first syllable is udātta by the rule beginning ⟦ईडवंदे⟧. The suffix kyap, by the rule beginning ⟦एतिस्तुशासि⟧, belongs to the root vṛ of the class of vṛṇoti only, not to this vṛ.

## Ṛk 3

⟦संत्य⟧, you who are to be resorted to by the sacrificers, or else you who give the fruit to the sacrificers, O Agni, ⟦तुभ्यं⟧, for you, ⟦विप्राय⟧, the wise one, there are ⟦स्तोकाः⟧, drops, ⟦घृतश्चुतः⟧, accompanied by the dripping of ghee. You, the ⟦ऋषि⟧, the seer of things that lie beyond the senses, and for that very reason ⟦श्रेष्ठः⟧, surpassing all, ⟦समिध्यसे⟧: you are well set ablaze by the drops of fat mixed with ghee. Do you, being such, become the ⟦प्राविता⟧, the protector in an eminent way, of the ⟦यज्ञस्य⟧, of the animal sacrifice that is being performed by us.

⟦तुभ्यं⟧: before the dative singular ending, in place of the word yuṣmad, the substitute tubhya comes by the rule ⟦तुभ्यमहौ जयि⟧ (⟦पा. ७-२-९५⟧). The substitute am takes the place of that ending by the rule ⟦प्रथमयोरमिति⟧. The first syllable is udātta by the rule ⟦जयि चे⟧. ⟦संत्य⟧: the root is ⟦षण संभक्तौ⟧, san in the sense of taking as one's share, or ⟦षणु दाने⟧, san in the sense of giving. The suffix ktic comes by the rule ⟦च संज्ञायामिति⟧, on ktic and kta in a name. By the rule ⟦न क्तिचि दीर्घश्चेति⟧ there are neither the lengthening nor the elision of the n. "Being in santi": so the suffix yat comes by the rule ⟦भवे छंदसीति⟧, or by the rule ⟦तत्र साधुरिति⟧. There is elision by the rule ⟦यस्येति⟧. Because it is a vocative it loses its accent. ⟦इध्यसे⟧: the root is indh in the sense of shining. The suffix yak comes in the passive. Because it is marked with k, the nasal is elided. The verb loses its accent. ⟦प्राविता⟧: the root is ⟦अव रक्षणादिषु⟧, av in the senses of protecting and the rest. It is a form with the suffix tṛc. The accent is that of the compound. ⟦भव⟧: it loses its accent.

## Ṛk 4

⟦अध्रिगो⟧, you whose going is not held back, whose nature it is to go constantly, and for that very reason ⟦शचीवः⟧, O powerful one, O Agni, ⟦तुभ्यं⟧, for you, the ⟦स्तोकासो⟧, the drops, of ⟦मेदसो⟧, of the oblation in the form of fat, and of ⟦घृतस्य⟧ ⟦श्चोतंति⟧: flow. Therefore do you, ⟦कविशस्तः⟧, praised by the kavis, the hotṛ and the others who know the rite, being accompanied by ⟦बृहता⟧, abundant, ⟦भानुना⟧, lustre, ⟦आगाः⟧: come to our animal sacrifice. And having come, ⟦मेधिर⟧, O Agni possessed of wisdom, ⟦जुषस्व⟧, enjoy, the ⟦हव्या⟧, the oblations, the vapā and the rest, that are being given by us.

⟦अध्रिगो⟧: "going is go": the suffix ḍo comes, in the sense of the action, after the root gam, of the roots gam and sṛp in the sense of going, by the rule ⟦गमेर्डोरिति⟧. In "he by whom going is not held back", the word go is shortened by the rule ⟦गोस्त्रियोरुपसर्जनस्यॆति⟧. The word dhṛta becomes dhri because it belongs to the group beginning with pṛṣodara. In the vocative there is guṇa by the rule ⟦संबुद्धौ च⟧ (⟦पा. ७ ३-१०६⟧). Because it does not stand at the beginning of the pāda it loses its accent. ⟦शचीवः⟧: the change of matup to ru comes by the rule ⟦मतुवसो रुरिति⟧. ⟦कविशस्त्रः⟧: before the niṣṭhā suffix, after the root śaṃs in the sense of praising, the nasal is elided. The augment iṭ is prohibited by the rule ⟦यस्य विभाषेती⟧. The final syllable is udātta by the rule ⟦थाथघञ्क्ताजबित्र काणामि⟧. ⟦भानुना⟧: the root is ⟦भा दीप्तौ⟧, bhā in the sense of shining. The suffix nu comes in the sense of the agent by the rule ⟦दाभाभ्यां नुरिति⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अगाः⟧: in the Vedic luṅ of the root ⟦इण् गतावि⟧, i in the sense of going, the substitute gā comes by the rule ⟦इणो गेति⟧. The sic is elided by the rule ⟦गातिस्थेति⟧. The verb loses its accent. ⟦मेधिर⟧: the suffix iran comes by the statement ⟦मेधारथाभ्यामिरनिरचौ वक्तव्यावि⟧, "after medhā and ratha the suffixes iran and irac are to be stated". Because it is a vocative it loses its accent.

## Ṛk 5

O Agni, the ⟦ओजिष्ठं⟧, exceedingly full of essence, ⟦मेदो⟧, the oblation called the vapā, which has been ⟦उत्भृतं⟧, taken out, ⟦मध्यतः⟧, from the middle part of the animal, ⟦ते⟧, for you, we (⟦वयं⟧), the adhvaryu and the others, at this animal sacrifice, give forth (⟦प्र⟧ ⟦ददामहे⟧) ⟦ते⟧, to you: we bestow that oblation called the vapā which has been taken out. ⟦वसो⟧, you who give a dwelling to the whole world, O Agni, the ⟦स्तोका⟧, the drops mixed with ghee, that are ⟦त्वचि⟧ ⟦अधि⟧, upon the vapā, they ⟦ते⟧, for you, ⟦श्चोतंति⟧, flow. Or else: they drip ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦त्वच्यधि⟧, upon your body called flame. Those (⟦तान्⟧) drops, ⟦देवशः⟧, among the gods, ⟦प्रति⟧ ⟦विहि⟧: distribute them to each one.

⟦मध्यतः⟧: after the word madhya the suffix tasi comes by the rule ⟦पंचम्यास्तसिः⟧ (⟦पा. ५-४-४४-१⟧)**NOTE**{the supplied text gives the reference as Pāṇini 5-4-44-1; the reference text has 5.4.44: which is to stand?}. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦उद्भृतं⟧: the root is ⟦हृञ् हरणे⟧, hṛ in the sense of taking. The suffix kta comes in the sense of the object. In place of the h there is bh by the rule on hṛ and grah in the Veda. By the rule ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the preposition keeps its original accent. ⟦ददामहे⟧: in the laṭ of the root ⟦दद दान⟧, dad in the sense of giving, the ātmanepada comes because the root is marked with ṅ. The vowel is lengthened by the rule ⟦अतो दीर्घो⟧ and what follows. The verb loses its accent. ⟦श्चोतंति⟧: the root is ścut in the sense of flowing. Because it stands at the beginning of the pāda it does not lose its accent. When the ending anti has become anudātta by the accent of a sārvadhātuka that replaces a tense-affix, the accent is that of the root. ⟦त्वचि⟧: the root is ⟦तनु विस्तारे⟧, tan in the sense of spreading. By the rule ⟦तनोतेरनश्च⟧ ⟦वः⟧ (⟦उ. २-६३⟧)**NOTE**{the supplied text gives the Uṇādi reference as 2-63; the reference text has 2.221: which is to stand?} the suffix cik comes in the sense of the agent or of the object; the substitute v takes the place of the a and the n. "It spreads, or it is spread", hence tvac. The case-ending is udātta by the rule ⟦सावेकाच⟧. ⟦विहि⟧: in the loṭ of the root vī, in the senses of desire, going and the rest, the vikaraṇa śap is dropped because the root belongs to the group beginning with ad. The short vowel is Vedic. The verb loses its accent. ⟦खिलं⟧: a khila.
