## Introduction

The fourth sūkta, of thirteen Ṛks, begins ⟦प्र पर्वतानां⟧. Here is the Anukramaṇī: ⟦प्र पर्वतानां सप्तोना संवादो नदीभिर्विश्वामित्रस्योत्तितीर्षोस्तत्र नदीवाक्यं चशुर्थिषष्ठष्टमीदशम्यः षष्ठिसप्तम्योस्त्विंद्रस्तुतिरंत्यानुष्टुबिति⟧. That is: the sūkta ⟦प्र पर्वतानां⟧ is ⟦सप्तोना⟧; it is a dialogue of Viśvāmitra, who wishes to cross, with the rivers; in it the speech of the rivers is in the fourth, sixth, eighth and tenth Ṛks; in the sixth and seventh there is praise of Indra; the last Ṛk is an Anuṣṭubh.

Here, since the fourth, sixth, eighth and tenth Ṛks are the speech of the rivers, the rivers themselves are the seers of these; since the rest are the speech of Viśvāmitra, he alone is the seer of the rest. The last Ṛk is an Anuṣṭubh; the rest are Triṣṭubh. The deity is Indra. Although in the sixth and the seventh Viśvāmitra and the rivers are praised, still Indra alone is the deity. The application of the sūkta is by its indication.

Long ago, it is said, Viśvāmitra became the house-priest of King Sudās, the Paijavana. Having obtained wealth through the priesthood, he took all the wealth and came to the confluence of the Vipāś and the Śutudrī, and the others followed him. Then Viśvāmitra, wishing to cross, saw those two rivers in water too deep to wade, and praised them with the first three Ṛks for the sake of crossing.

## Ṛk 1

⟦पर्वतानां⟧ means of the mountains, of the hills, of the rocks. ⟦उपस्थात्⟧ means from the lap. Coming forth from there, the two rivers are ⟦उशती⟧, desiring to go to the sea. An example is given for the going: ⟦अश्वे इव⟧. Just as two mares, ⟦विषिते⟧, set free from the stable, are ⟦हासमाने⟧, vying with each other in mutual speed. Or else: they are ⟦अश्वे इव⟧, like two mares, ⟦वडवे इव⟧, like two mares, going with haste and rejoicing in one another. In the same way, ⟦गावेव शुभ्रे⟧: just as two cows are shining, so these two are ⟦शुभ्रे⟧, shining. Further, ⟦मातरा⟧: just as two mothers, two cows, are ⟦रिहाणे⟧ — the root ⟦लिहिः⟧ has the sense of the desiderative contained in it — wishing to lick the calf with the tongue, they go quickly; in the same way these two, going with speed in order to reach the sea, are joined with water. ⟦विपाट्⟧ is so called either from tearing the banks, or from loosening, or from setting free. ⟦शुतुद्री⟧: ⟦शु⟧ means quickly; then, as one pushed on (⟦तुन्ना⟧), as if pushed on (⟦तुन्नेव⟧), it runs, it goes; thus it is ⟦शुतुद्री⟧. **NOTE**{the syllable ⟦तु⟧ in ⟦शु क्षिप्रं तु तुन्ना⟧ is not explained, and I have not rendered it} The two rivers of these names ⟦प्र जवेते⟧ go swiftly towards the sea.

Here is the Nirukta: ⟦पर्वतानामुपस्थादुपस्थानादुशत्यौ कामयमाने अश्वे इव विमुक्ते इति वा विषण्णॆ इति वा हासमाने हासतिः स्पर्धायां हर्षमाणेवा गावानिव शुभ्रे शोभने मातरौ संरिहाणे विपाट्⟧ and ⟦भुतुदौ पयसा प्रजवेते⟧ (⟦नि. ९-३९⟧). That is: from the lap, from the standing near, of the mountains, the two rivers are ⟦पर्वतानामुपस्थादुपस्थानादुशत्यौ⟧, desiring; they are like two mares set free, or, it is said, like two that are ⟦विषण्णॆ⟧; ⟦हासमाने⟧: the root ⟦हासतिः⟧ is used in the sense of rivalry, or they are rejoicing; like two cows ⟦शुभ्रे⟧, that is, beautiful; two mothers ⟦संरिहाणे⟧, licking together; the Vipāś and the Śutudrī go forth swiftly with water.

The grammatical part. ⟦उशती⟧: the root is ⟦वश⟧ in the sense of desiring. Because the suffix śatṛ of this root is marked with ṅ, the vowel-substitution samprasāraṇa takes place by the rule beginning ⟦ग्रहिज्यावयीत्यादिना⟧. ⟦विषिते⟧: from the root ⟦षिञ् बंधन⟧, in the sense of binding, the past participle in the passive. In the saṃhitā the change to ṣ takes place by the rule beginning ⟦परिनिविभ्यः सेवसितसयसिवुसहेत्यादिना⟧ (⟦पा. ८-३-७०⟧). By the rule ⟦गतिरनंतर गतेः प्रकृतिस्वरः⟧ the gati, here vi, which stands immediately before, keeps its natural accent. ⟦हासमाने⟧: the root ⟦हासतिः⟧ is in the sense of rivalry, or from ⟦हसे हसने ना⟧, the root hasa in the sense of laughing; then the suffix śānac. When its accent as a lasārvadhātuka suffix has been made, the accent of the root remains. ⟦रिहाणी⟧: the root ⟦लिह आस्वादने⟧, in the sense of tasting, takes both voices because it has a svarita as its indicatory vowel. Then the suffix śānac; the elision of śap, because the root belongs to the adādi class. The change of the letter l to r is Vedic. Resting on the r, the change of n to ṇ takes place. Because the suffix has c as its marker, the accent falls on the final syllable. ⟦विपाट्⟧: the two roots, ⟦पट गतौ⟧, in the sense of going, or ⟦पश बाधनस्पर्शनयोरिति⟧, in the senses of obstructing and touching, ending in the causative suffix, with the prefix vi. The change of the letter ś to ṣ is by the rule beginning ⟦व्रश्चादिना⟧. ⟦शुतुद्री⟧: the accomplishment of the form is Vedic. ⟦जवेते⟧: the root ⟦जुज् गतौ⟧, in the sense of going, of the first class; because it is marked with ṅ, it takes the ātmanepada. The substitution of iya is by the rule ⟦आतो जित इतीयादेशः⟧. The verb is unaccented.

## Ṛk 2

O two rivers, ⟦इंद्रेषिते⟧, sent forth by Indra, ⟦भिक्षमाणे⟧, begging, asking, ⟦प्रसवं⟧, the permission of that Indra, you go ⟦अच्छ⟧, towards, the sea: ⟦याथः⟧ means you go. The example is ⟦रथ्येव⟧: just as two charioteers go to the place that is their goal, so you do. Doing what? ⟦समाराणे⟧, meeting one another; ⟦ऊर्मिभिः⟧, with waves, that is, with billows; ⟦पिन्वमाने⟧, satisfying the country around; ⟦शुभ्रे⟧, shining. It is joined with what went before: you two go to the sea. And ⟦वां⟧, between you two, the one goes to the other river, ⟦नदीमष्येति⟧: ⟦अपिगच्छति⟧, she joins; the meaning is that they attain oneness with each other.

The grammatical part. ⟦इंद्रेषिते⟧: from the root ⟦इष गतावित्यस्य⟧, in the sense of going, the past participle in the passive; the augment iṭ is by the rule beginning ⟦तीषसहेत्यादिना⟧. By the rule ⟦तृतीया कर्मणीति⟧ the accent is that of the first member of the compound. ⟦प्रसवं⟧: the suffix ap after the root ⟦षू प्रेरण⟧, in the sense of impelling. The accent is that given by the rule beginning ⟦थाधादिस्वरः⟧. ⟦भिक्षमाणे⟧: the root ⟦भिक्ष⟧ in the sense of begging; it takes the ātmanepada. When the suffix śānac is made unaccented by the accent of the lasārvadhātuka suffix, the accent of the root remains. ⟦रथ्येव⟧: the word ⟦रथस्येम्⟧; in the sense of "this belongs to that", the suffix yat is added by the rule ⟦रथाद्यदिति⟧. Because it is marked with t, the accent is svarita. With iva the case-ending is not elided. ⟦याथः⟧: the form of the root ⟦या⟧ in the present tense. ⟦समाराणे⟧: of the root ⟦ऋ गतावित्यस्य⟧, in the sense of going, the perfect. Since the root ṛ with the prefix sam takes the ātmanepada by the rule beginning ⟦समो गमीत्यादिना⟧, the substitute for its perfect ending is kānac. The guṇa is by the rule ⟦ऋच्छत्यतामिति⟧. ⟦पिन्वमाने⟧: the root ⟦पिवि सेचने⟧, in the sense of sprinkling, of the first class. When the suffix śānac is made unaccented by the accent of the lasārvadhātuka suffix, the accent of the root remains.

## Ṛk 3

O two rivers, ⟦मातृतमां⟧, most of all a mother, ⟦सिंधुं⟧, the flowing one, the Śutudrī: you I, Viśvāmitra, have reached, ⟦अच्छ⟧, facing you, ⟦आयासं⟧. ⟦उर्वीं⟧, great, ⟦सुभगां⟧, fortunate, ⟦विपाशं⟧: you we have reached, ⟦अगन्म⟧. Doing what? ⟦मातरा⟧, two mothers, two cows, ⟦वत्समिव संरिहाणे⟧: the root ⟦लिहिः⟧ has the sense of the desiderative contained in it; as the two, wishing to lick with the tongue, follow the calf, so these two, ⟦समानं⟧, one, ⟦योनिं⟧, place, that is the sea, ⟦अनु⟧, with that as their aim, ⟦संचरंती⟧, move well. It is connected with what went before: I have reached you two.

The grammatical part. ⟦अयासं⟧: the form of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧, in the sense of causing to reach. The absence of the augment iṭ is Vedic. ⟦अगन्म⟧: the form of the root ⟦गम⟧ in the past tense; by the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧ there is elision of śap. By the rule ⟦म्वोश्चेति⟧ the letter m is changed to n. The verb is unaccented. ⟦संचरंती⟧: the root ⟦चरति⟧ is in the sense of going. Because there is no connection with the third case, the ātmanepada does not occur. When the suffix śatṛ is made unaccented by the accent of the lasārvadhātuka suffix, the accent of the root remains.

## Ṛk 4

Having been praised thus, the two rivers said to Viśvāmitra: ⟦एनैनेन⟧, by this ⟦पयसा⟧, water; we, ⟦पिन्वमानाः⟧, mingling, are ⟦चरंतीः⟧, going, ⟦अनु⟧, aiming at, ⟦योनिं⟧, the place, the sea, ⟦देवकृतं⟧, made by the god Indra, that is, ordered by him. **NOTE**{the supplied text has ⟦संपर्कयंतो⟧ for ⟦पिन्वमानाः⟧; the reference text has santarpayantyaḥ, "satisfying"} As for ⟦वयमास्मदे⟧, the plural for two is for honour. **NOTE**{the sense of ⟦वयमास्मदे⟧ is not clear to me} Of us, of such, ⟦सर्गतक्तः⟧, set to the going, the going forth, ⟦प्रसवः⟧, the effort, ⟦न वर्तवे⟧, does not occur for turning back. ⟦किंयुः⟧, wishing what, does this ⟦विप्रः⟧, brahmin, ⟦जोहवीति⟧, call ⟦नद्यः⟧, us rivers? That is, he calls greatly.

The grammatical part. ⟦एना⟧: for the third case of the word ⟦इदं⟧ there is the substitute ena. By the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ the substitute ā for the third case. By the rule ⟦ऊडिदमिति⟧ the case-ending is acute. ⟦पिन्वमानाः⟧: the root ⟦पिवि सेचने⟧, in the sense of sprinkling. ⟦देवकृतं⟧: by the rule ⟦तृतीया कर्मणीति⟧ the accent is that of the stem of the first member. ⟦वर्तवे⟧: the root ⟦वृतु वर्तने⟧, in the sense of turning. In the sense of the infinitive the suffix tavai is added. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦सर्गतक्तः⟧: by the rule ⟦क्ले चेति⟧ the accent is that of the stem of the first member. ⟦किंयुः⟧: wishing what. ⟦क्यचि मांताव्ययप्रतिषेधः⟧ (⟦पा. ३-१-८-१⟧): since this is Vedic, the prohibition does not apply here, and so the suffix kyac is added. By the rule ⟦क्याच्छंदसीत्युप्रत्ययः⟧ the suffix u is added; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦नद्यः⟧: the change to y is Vedic. ⟦जोहवीति⟧: when samprasāraṇa has been made by the rule ⟦ह्वयो यज्लुक्यभ्यस्तस्य चेति⟧, the guṇa of the reduplicating syllable is by the rule ⟦गुणो यज्लुकोरित्यभ्यासस्य गुणः⟧. By the rule ⟦यजो वेतीडागमः⟧ the augment iṭ is added. Guṇa. The verb is unaccented.

## Ṛk 5

Viśvāmitra speaks to the rivers. ⟦ऋतावरीः⟧: ⟦ऋतमुदकं⟧, ṛta is water; O rivers possessing it, you ⟦मे⟧, of me, Viśvāmitra, ⟦सोम्याय⟧, for the speech that effects Soma, thinking "having crossed, I shall prepare Soma"; ⟦वचसे⟧, for that purpose alone; ⟦एवैः⟧, the instrumental is used in the sense of the fifth case, away from quick goings, ⟦मुहूर्तं⟧, for a moment only, ⟦मुहूर्तमात्रमुप रमध्वं⟧: the root ⟦रमि⟧ with upa is used in the sense of stopping; for a moment only, cease from quick going. When the rivers, spoken to in general, were not doing the settled purpose **NOTE**{the supplied text has ⟦समाहितं⟧; the reference text has samīhitam, "desired"}, he speaks to the Śutudrī standing before him. ⟦कुशिकस्य सूनुः⟧: I, Viśvāmitra, son of Kuśika, the royal seer, ⟦बृहती मनीषा⟧, by great ⟦मनीषा⟧, that is, by praise, ⟦अवस्युः⟧, desiring protection for myself, ⟦सिंधुं⟧, the Śutudrī, to you, ⟦अच्छ⟧, facing you, ⟦प्राह्वे⟧, I call forth, I call intensely.

Here is the Nirukta: ⟦उपरमढ्वं मे वचसे सोम्याय सोमसंपादिन⟧ ⟦ऋतावरीर्यतवत्य⟧ ⟦ऋतमित्युदकनाम प्रत्यतं भवति⟧ ⟦मुहूर्तमेवैरयनैरवनैर्वा⟧ ⟦प्राभिह्वयामि सिंधुं बृहत्या महत्या मनीषया मनस ईषया स्तुत्या प्रज्ञया⟧ ⟦वावनाय कुशिकस्य सूनुः⟧ ⟦कुशिको राजा बभूव⟧ (⟦नि २-२५⟧). That is: cease, for my speech, for the preparer of Soma; ⟦ऋतावरीः⟧ are those possessing ṛta; ⟦ऋतमित्युदकनाम⟧: ṛta is a name of water, and it becomes ⟦प्रत्यतं भवति⟧. ⟦मुहूर्तं⟧ with ⟦एवैः⟧ means by goings or by protections. I call forth the Sindhu with great, that is ⟦बृहत्या महत्या⟧, ⟦मनीषया⟧, which is ⟦मनस ईषया⟧, by praise, by wisdom, for ⟦वावनाय⟧; the son of Kuśika. Kuśika was a king.

The grammatical part. ⟦रमध्वं⟧: the root ⟦रमु उपरमे⟧, in the sense of stopping; after the root ram with upa, which is optionally intransitive (⟦पा. १-३-८५⟧), the ātmanepada occurs. ⟦ऋतावरीः⟧: after the word ṛta, in the sense of "possessing", the suffix vanip is added in the Veda, by the rule ⟦ऋतशब्दान्मत्वर्थे छंदसीवनिपाति⟧. By the rule ⟦वनो र चेति⟧ the feminine suffix ṅīp and the substitute r for the final. By the rule ⟦वा छंदसीति⟧ the lengthening for the like vowel. Since a vocative stands at the beginning of a pāda, the initial acute taught in the sixth book occurs. ⟦एवैः⟧: the root ⟦इण् गत्⟧, in the sense of going; the suffix van by the rule ⟦इण्‌शीङ्ब्यां वन्⟧. The guṇa is that marked by the ārdhadhātuka suffix. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦बृहती मनीषा⟧: in both words the lengthening for the preceding like vowel in the third case. ⟦अवस्युः⟧: wishing protection (⟦अवो रक्षणमिच्छन्⟧). The suffix kyac after a noun in the sense of oneself. Since the pada-designation of a stem ending in n is limited by the rule ⟦नः क्य इति⟧, the s here does not become ru. By the rule ⟦क्याच्छंदसीत्युप्रत्ययः⟧ the suffix u. ⟦अह्वे⟧: after the root ⟦हृदयतेर्लुजसि च आत्मनेपदेष्पन्यतरस्यां⟧ (⟦पा ३-१-५४⟧), the substitute is aṅ, optionally, in the ātmanepada forms: ⟦इत्यजादेशः⟧. Guṇa. The verb is unaccented.

## Ṛk 6

The rivers answered. O Viśvāmitra: ⟦वज्रबाहुः⟧ means he whose arm is joined with the vajra; such a mighty Indra ⟦अरदत्⟧: the root ⟦रदतिः⟧ has the sense of digging. He dug the rivers, us. How did he dig? It is said: ⟦परिधिं⟧, the water of the rivers, which make a sound, placed all around, enclosing it within and existing all around; such a ⟦वृत्रं⟧: ⟦वृणोत्याकाशमिति⟧, since it covers the sky, Vṛtra is the cloud. ⟦तं मेघमपाहन्⟧: he struck that cloud, ⟦जघान⟧. When it was struck, the waters fell; by them, as they went, we were dug. Thus he dug by means of the slaying of the cloud. He did not only dig; what more? ⟦सविता⟧ is the impeller of the whole world; ⟦सुपाणिः⟧ means fair-handed, because he is the maker of origin and continuance. Such a god, Indra, shining, ⟦अनयत्⟧, led us: having split the cloud, he filled the sea by impelling the water. In the ⟦प्रसवे⟧, the permission, of that Indra, who has such power, we, ⟦उर्वीः⟧, abundant with water, ⟦यामः⟧, go. We go; we do not stop at your word.

Yāska states the meaning that has been stated: ⟦इंद्रो अस्मानरदद्वज्रबाहू रदतिः खनतिकर्मापाहन्वृत्तं⟧ ⟦परि ० नवीनानुति व्याख्यातं⟧ **NOTE**{the words ⟦परि ० नवीनानुति⟧ are damaged and I cannot tell what is said to be explained; the reference text has paridhiṃ nadīnānuti vyākhyātam}. ⟦देवॊnनयत्नविता सुपाणिः कल्याणपाणिः⟧: ⟦सुपाणिः⟧ is of auspicious hand. ⟦पाणिः⟧ is from ⟦पणायि. (३⟧ in the sense of worship **NOTE**{⟦पाणिः पणायि. (३ पूजाकर्मणः⟧ is damaged; the reference text has paṇāya here}. ⟦प्रगृह्य पाणी देवान्पूजयंति⟧: holding up their hands they worship the gods. ⟦तस्य वयं प्रसवे याम उर्विरुर्व्यः⟧: in his impulsion we go, ⟦उर्वीः⟧ being many (⟦नि. २-२६⟧).

The grammatical part. ⟦अरदत्⟧: the form of the root ⟦रदतेर्लजि⟧ in the imperfect. ⟦वज्रबाहुः⟧: in a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member. ⟦अहन्⟧: the form of the root han in the imperfect; the verb is unaccented. ⟦परिधिं⟧: from the root ⟦डुधाण् धारणपोषणयोरित्यस्मात्⟧, in the senses of holding and nourishing, when it has an upasarga and an object, the suffix ki is added by the rule ⟦घोः किरिति किप्रत्ययः⟧. The ā is elided. The accent is that of the latter member in a compound with a kṛt suffix. ⟦अनयत्⟧: the form of the root ⟦नयतेर्लजि⟧ in the imperfect. ⟦सुपाणिः⟧: the root ⟦पण व्यवहारे स्तुत् च⟧, in the senses of trading and praising; by the rule ⟦अशिपणायो रुडायलुकौ च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-१३२⟧), the suffix in is added, and the āya is elided. In a bahuvrīhi the accent is by the rule ⟦नञ्सुभ्यामिति⟧. ⟦प्रसवे⟧: the root ⟦हू प्रेरणे⟧, in the sense of impelling; by the rule ⟦यादोरबिति⟧ the suffix a is added in the sense of the action. The accent is the final acute of the latter member by the rule beginning ⟦थाथघइत्युत्तरपदांतोदात्तत्वं⟧. ⟦यामः⟧: the form of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧ in the present tense; the verb is unaccented. ⟦उर्विः⟧: after the word ⟦उरु⟧, the feminine suffix ṅīṣ by the rule ⟦उरुशब्दाद्वोतो गुणवचनादिति जीष्⟧. By the rule ⟦वा छंदसीति⟧ the lengthening for the like vowel. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 7

That deed of this Indra, ⟦यदहिं विवृश्चत्⟧, by which he cut the serpent, the cloud, and struck it for the sake of impelling water, which is of the nature of cutting, that is the ⟦वीर्यं⟧, the power, of that Indra, to be ⟦शश्वधा⟧, always, ⟦प्रवाच्यं⟧, spoken of, that is, to be declared eminently. And that Indra struck with the vajra ⟦परिषदः⟧, the Asuras sitting all around, seated, who cause obstruction. Then the waters, ⟦अयनं⟧, wishing for a place, ⟦इच्छमानाः⟧, wishing, ⟦आयन्⟧, came, that is, they go.

The grammatical part. ⟦प्रवाच्यं⟧: from the root ⟦वच परिभाषण⟧, in the sense of speaking, the suffix ṇyat is added by the rule ⟦ृहलोर्ण्यदिति⟧. Because it is marked with ṇ, there is vṛddhi of the penultimate. By the rule ⟦वचोऽशब्दसंज्ञायां⟧ (⟦पा. ७-३-६७⟧) the change of c to k does not take place. By exchange the accent is initial. Or else the suffix yat is added after the causative ⟦वाचयतेरचो यत्⟧; the accent is by the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव इति⟧. ⟦शश्वधा⟧: it should be seen that the suffix dhā in its own sense is added to the word ⟦शश्वच्छब्तात्⟧ and that the t is elided. ⟦विवृश्चत्⟧: the root ⟦ओव्रश्च छेदने⟧, in the sense of cutting, of the tudādi class. In the imperfect the vowel-substitution is by the rule beginning ⟦ग्रहिज्यावयियात्यादिना⟧. The compound is made by dividing the rule at ⟦सह⟧ in ⟦सह सुपेत्यत्र सहेति योगविभागात्समासः⟧; the accent is that of the compound. ⟦परिषदः⟧: the suffix kvip. In the saṃhitā the change to ṣ is by the rule ⟦सदेरप्रतेरिति⟧. ⟦जघान⟧: the form of the root han in the perfect, in the ending ṇal; the verb is unaccented. ⟦आयन्⟧: the form of the root ⟦अय गतावित्यस्य⟧ in the imperfect; since it stands at the beginning of a pāda, there is no unaccenting. ⟦इच्छमानाः⟧: from the root ⟦इषु इच्छायामित्यस्माद्व्यत्ययेन⟧, in the sense of wishing, śānac by exchange. When its accent as a lasārvadhātuka suffix has been made, the accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 8

The rivers, having by the way made praise of Indra, said to Viśvāmitra: ⟦जरितः⟧, O praiser, O Viśvāmitra, that speech of yours, of the nature of a dialogue, which you, ⟦नोऽभीत्या⟧ **NOTE**{the sense of ⟦नोऽभीत्या⟧ is not clear to me}, keep uttering as cries, ⟦घोषानुदोषयन्वर्तसे⟧, that speech ⟦मापि मृष्ठाः⟧, do not forget: ⟦मा विस्मार्षिः⟧. What is the reason? ⟦उत्तरा युगान्युत्तरेषु याज्ञिकेषु⟧, in the later sacrificial ages, ⟦युगेश्वहःसूक्षेषु⟧ **NOTE**{⟦युगेश्वहःसूक्षेषु⟧ is damaged; the reference text has yugeṣv ahaḥ sukthe...; I have rendered "in the days and recitations"} in the days and recitations, ⟦कारो शस्त्राणां कर्तस्त्वं⟧, O maker of śastras, you ⟦जुषस्व⟧, ⟦संवादात्मकेन तेन वाक्येन प्रतिसेवस्व⟧, serve us in return with that speech of the nature of a dialogue. Now, ⟦नोऽस्मान् पुरुषत्रा पुरुषेषु मा नि कः⟧, do not among men, by the teaching of the words of dialogue in the form of speech and reply, display exceedingly manly boldness toward us: ⟦ते तुभ्यं नमः⟧, homage to you.

The grammatical part. ⟦मृष्ठाः⟧: ⟦मृजूष् शुद्धावित्यस्य लजु व्यत्ययेनात्मनेपदं⟧, of the root meaning purifying, in the imperfect, the ātmanepada by exchange. ⟦अदादित्वाच्छपो लुक्⟧, the elision of śap, because it belongs to the adādi class. **NOTE**{the reference text lacks this clause on ⟦मृष्ठाः⟧} The change to ṣ is by the rule beginning ⟦प्रश्चादिना⟧. The verb is unaccented. ⟦घोषान्⟧: the root ⟦घुषिर् संशब्दन⟧, in the sense of sounding, in the form śatṛ; since all rules are optional in the Veda, there is no single replacement by the following form by the rule ⟦विकल्पितत्वादतो गुण इति⟧. There is lengthening for the like vowel. When the accent of the śatṛ as a lasārvadhātuka suffix has been made, the accent is that of the root. ⟦युगानि⟧: the root ⟦युजिर् योगे⟧, in the sense of joining; by the enumeration among the words beginning ⟦उंभादिषु⟧ as ending in ghañ, there is no guṇa; and the enumeration is desired for a particular domain: ⟦कालविशेषे रथाद्युपकरणे चेति⟧, for a particular time and for a chariot implement; from its reading there alone, the accent is the final acute. By the rule ⟦कालाध्वनोरत्यंतसंयोग इति⟧ the accusative. ⟦कारो⟧: the suffix u is added after the root ⟦करोतेः⟧ by the rule beginning ⟦कृपापाजिमीत्यादिना उणृत्ययः⟧. Since it is a vocative, the verb is unaccented. ⟦कः⟧: of the root ⟦करोतेर्लुज्⟧ in the aorist, the elision of the cli by the rule beginning ⟦चेर्मंत्रे घसेत्यादिना लुक्⟧; the elision of sic by the rule beginning ⟦हल्यादिना⟧; by the rule ⟦न माज्योग इत्यडभावः⟧ there is no augment a. ⟦पुरुषत्रा⟧: by the rule ⟦देवमनुष्यपुरुषपुरुमर्त्येभ्यो द्वितीयासप्तम्योर्बहुलमिति⟧ the suffix trā in the sense of the seventh case. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 9

Viśvāmitra replied to the rivers. ⟦स्वसारः⟧ are sisters; ⟦सिंधवः⟧, O rivers: of me, ⟦कारवे⟧, who am making praise, hear my speech ⟦सु सुष्टु ओ शृणोत⟧, well; ⟦शृणुतैव⟧, hear indeed. ⟦ययौ⟧: ⟦अनसा शकटेन रथेन च सह⟧, together with a cart and a chariot, from a place far away I have come to you; I have arrived. You ⟦सु सुष्टु नि नमध्वं⟧, bow well: ⟦आत्मना स्वयं प्रह्वा भवत⟧, become bowed of your own accord. Likewise ⟦सुपाराः⟧: so that descending from and mounting the bank may be easy for chariots and the like, be also of fair banks. Further, you ⟦स्रोत्याभिः⟧, with waters, which are of a flowing nature, ⟦अधोअक्षाः⟧, be below the axle, which is a part of the chariot. When the waters are below the axle, then chariots and the like can be led across. Therefore the intended meaning is: be of that measure of water.

The grammatical part. ⟦ओ इति प्रगृह्यसंज्ञा⟧: the word o has the designation pragṛhya. ⟦शृणोत⟧: the root ⟦श्रु श्रवण⟧, in the sense of hearing, in the imperative; the substitute tab for the ending ta by the rule ⟦तप्तनप्तनथनाश्चेति तबादेशः⟧. Because it is marked with p, there is guṇa. The verb is unaccented. ⟦ययौ⟧: the form of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧ in the perfect used for the past in general, in the first person, in the ending ṇal; the substitute au by the rule ⟦णल्यात औ णल इत्यौकारः⟧. The accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦वः⟧: the substitute vas for the accusative plural of the word ⟦युष्मच्छब्दस्य⟧ by the rule ⟦वस्न साविति वसादेशः⟧. ⟦षू⟧: by the rule ⟦निपातस्येति⟧ there is lengthening in the saṃhitā. ⟦नमध्वं⟧: of the root ⟦णमु प्रह्वत्वे शब्दे चेत्यस्य⟧, in the senses of bowing and sounding, when the agent is as an object, the absence of yak is by the prohibition in the rule ⟦न महस्सु नमां यक्षिणाविति⟧. ⟦अधोअक्षाः⟧: after the word ⟦अधरशब्दस्य⟧ the suffix asi and the substitute adh by the rule ⟦पूर्वाधरावराणामसि पुरधवश्चषामित्यसिप्रत्ययोऽधादेशश्च⟧. The word ⟦अक्ष⟧ is from the root ⟦अक्षशब्दोऽशू व्याप्तावित्यस्मादशेर्देवने⟧ (⟦उ. ३-६५⟧), ending in the suffix sa. The accent is that of the nature of the latter member in a kṛt compound. ⟦सिंधवः⟧: since it is a vocative, the verb is unaccented. ⟦स्रोत्याभिः⟧: after the word ⟦स्रोतःशब्दात्स्रोतसो विभाषा ड्यड् ड्यौ⟧ (⟦पा. ४-४-११३⟧) the suffix ḍya is added. Because it is marked ḍ, the ṭi is elided. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 10

The rivers, having first rejected the speech of Viśvāmitra, heard his speech with this Ṛk. ⟦कारो⟧, O one making praise, O Viśvāmitra, ⟦ते⟧ your ⟦वचांसि⟧, these speeches, ⟦आ शृणवाम⟧, we hear; we do your ⟦समाहितं प्रयोजन⟧ **NOTE**{the supplied text has ⟦समाहितं⟧; the reference text has samīhitam, "desired"}, your purpose, is the meaning. ⟦ययाथ⟧: you came together with a cart and a chariot, since you have come from afar. And we, ⟦ते⟧, for you, ⟦नि नंस्यॆ⟧, shall bow low: ⟦नीचैर्नमाम⟧. Here the singular is by the intention of each separately. The meaning is: in order that you may go by chariot, we shall become fordable water. The example there is ⟦पीप्यानेव योषा⟧: just as a mother, a woman, giving her son the breast to suck, bows low. Another example: just as a girl, a young woman, ⟦मर्यायेव⟧, to a man, or to a father or a brother, becomes bowed ⟦शश्वचै परिष्वजनाय⟧, for embracing, so, for you, they will become bowed. **NOTE**{⟦प्रह्वीभवादुः⟧ is not clear to me; I have rendered "they will become bowed"} The repetition ⟦ते इति⟧ is for respect. Yāska explains this Ṛk thus: ⟦आशृणवाम ते कारो वचनानि याहि⟧ ⟦दूरादनसा च रथेन च निनमाम ते पाययमानेव योषा पुत्रं मर्यायेव⟧ ⟦कन्या परिष्वजनाय निनमा इति वा⟧ (⟦नि. २-२७⟧). That is: we shall listen to your words, O maker of praise; come from afar with a cart and with a chariot; we shall bow to you, like a woman making her son drink, like a girl to a man for embracing; or, it is said, ⟦निनमा⟧.

The grammatical part. ⟦कारो⟧: in the vocative, by the rule ⟦संबुद्धौ शाकल्यस्येत्⟧ (⟦पा. १-१ १६⟧), the designation pragṛhya. ⟦शृणवाम⟧: of the root ⟦श्रु श्रवण⟧ in the imperative; in the first person the augment āṭ by the rule ⟦लोट्याडुत्तमस्य पिच्चेत्याडागमः⟧. Because it is marked with p, there is guṇa. The verb is unaccented. ⟦ययाथ⟧: of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧ in the perfect used for the past in general; in the ending thal, the prohibition of iṭ by the rule ⟦उपदेशेनुदात्तादितीट् प्रतिषेधः⟧. The accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦अनसा⟧: the third case in the sense of "together with". ⟦नंस्यॆ⟧: of the root ⟦णमु प्रहत्व⟧ in the leṭ in the first person, the suffix sip by the rule ⟦सिब्बहुलमिति सिप्⟧; the substitute ai by the rule ⟦वैतोन्यत्रेत्यैकारादेशः⟧. The verb is unaccented. ⟦पीप्यानेव⟧: the root ⟦पीज् पान⟧, in which the causative sense is contained, in the perfect, in the form kānac. The accent is that of a c-marked suffix. ⟦योषा⟧: from the root ⟦यु मिश्रणे⟧, in the sense of mixing, the suffix sa by the rule beginning ⟦वृत्यवदिहनीत्यादिना⟧ (⟦उ. ३-६२⟧). ⟦यौतीति योषा⟧: she mixes, therefore yoṣā. Because it belongs to the vṛṣādi group, the accent is initial. ⟦शश्वचै⟧: from the root ⟦स्वन्ज परिप्पंग⟧, in the sense of embracing, the suffix kvip in the sense of the action, by the rule of the sampadādi group. The desired form is accomplished by the rule of the pṛṣodarādi group, and the accent is final.

## Ṛk 11

Viśvāmitra replied to the rivers. ⟦अंग⟧ is in the sense of address. O rivers, since the crossing, by you, of me, who wish to cross, has been permitted, therefore the Bharatas, those born in the family of Bharata, all mine, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, the river who have attained oneness with one another, ⟦संतरेयुः⟧, may cross well; they may be well crossed. He makes this exact. ⟦गव्यन्⟧: wishing to cross the waters, ⟦गा उदकानि⟧; ⟦इषितः⟧, permitted by you; ⟦इंद्रजूतः⟧, impelled by Indra, who is your mover; ⟦ग्रामः⟧, the host of the Bharatas; ⟦अर्षात्⟧, may cross. Since ⟦सर्गतक्को⟧, set on going, ⟦प्रसवः⟧, their effort, ⟦अह⟧, before, was permitted by you. But I, ⟦स्तुतिमावृणे⟧, embrace, ⟦सुमतिं⟧, the fair praise, ⟦यज्ञयानां यज्ञार्हाणां वो युष्माकं⟧, of you who are worthy of sacrifice: ⟦सर्वतः संभजे⟧, I share it on all sides.

The grammatical part. ⟦भरताः⟧: from the word ⟦भरत⟧, the suffix añ because it belongs to the utsādi group; the elision of it by the rule ⟦यञञ्नोश्चति लुक्⟧. ⟦अतच् प्रत्ययस्वरः⟧ **NOTE**{⟦अतच्⟧ is not clear to me; the accent is said to be that of the suffix}. ⟦संतरेयुः⟧: the form of the root ⟦तरतेर्लिजु जुसि⟧ in the optative, in the ending jus. When the accent as a lasārvadhātuka suffix has been made, the accent is that of the root. By the rule ⟦तिजु चोदात्तवतीति⟧ the gati is unaccented. ⟦गव्यन्⟧: wishing for ⟦गा⟧ for oneself; kyac after a noun. The accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦ग्रामः⟧: by the rule ⟦ग्रसतेरा च⟧ (⟦उ. १-१४२⟧) the suffix man and the substitute ā. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦इंद्रजूतः⟧: ⟦जू⟧ is a root taught in a sūtra, in the sense of going. By the rule ⟦श्रुकः कितीति⟧ there is the prohibition of iṭ in the participle. By the rule ⟦तृतीया कर्मणीति⟧ the accent is that of the stem of the first member. ⟦अर्षात्⟧: of the root ⟦ऋ गतानित्यस्य⟧ in the leṭ, in the ending tip, sip by the rule ⟦सिब्बहुलमिति सिप्⟧. The augment aṭ of leṭ. The prohibition of iṭ by the rule ⟦एकाच इतीट् प्रतिषेधः⟧. Guṇa. Because the suffix is marked with p, it is unaccented, and the accent is that of the root. ⟦वृणे⟧: the form of the root ⟦वृज् संभक्तावित्यस्य⟧ in the present tense. ⟦यज्जियानां⟧: by the rule ⟦यज्ञर्त्वग्भ्यां घखञाविति⟧ the suffix gha. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 12

⟦गव्यवः⟧, wishing for ⟦गा⟧ for themselves, the Bharatas, those born in the family of Bharata, all ⟦अतारिषुः⟧: they crossed that river. ⟦विप्रः⟧, the wise Viśvāmitra, ⟦समभक्त⟧, ⟦समभजत⟧, shared the fair praise of the rivers. But you, as before, ⟦इषयंतीः⟧, making food by means of channels and the like, therefore ⟦सुराधाः⟧, provided with fair wealth, you ⟦वक्षणाः⟧, the artificial rivers, the channels, ⟦प्र पिन्नध्वं⟧, satisfy greatly; ⟦आ पृणध्वं⟧, fill them from all sides. ⟦शीभं⟧, quickly, ⟦यात⟧, go.

The grammatical part. ⟦अतारिषुः⟧: of the root ⟦त्र्य प्लवनतरणयोरित्यस्य⟧, in the senses of floating and crossing, in the aorist, with sic, vṛddhi by the rule ⟦परस्मैपदेष्टिति वृद्धिः⟧. The accent is that of the augment aṭ. ⟦गव्यवः⟧: kyac after a noun; the suffix u by the rule ⟦क्याच्छंदसीत्युप्रत्ययः⟧; its accent. ⟦अभक्त⟧: of the root ⟦भज सेवायामित्यस्य⟧, in the sense of serving, in the aorist, the elision of sic by the rule ⟦झलो झलीति⟧. Since it stands at the beginning of a pāda, the verb is not unaccented. ⟦पिन्नध्वं⟧: the form of the root ⟦पिवि सेचन⟧ in the imperative; the verb is unaccented. ⟦इषयंतीः⟧: making food. ⟦तत्करोतीति णिच्⟧: the suffix ṇic in the sense of doing that. By the rule ⟦णाविष्ठवत्प्रातिपदिकस्येतीष्ठवद्भावाट्टीरिति⟧ the stem is treated as before iṣṭha, and the elision of ṭi follows. By the rule ⟦वा छंदसीति⟧ there is lengthening for the like vowel. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦पृणध्वं⟧: the root ⟦पृण प्रीणने⟧, in the sense of pleasing; the form in the imperative. The ātmanepada is by exchange. The verb is not unaccented because the sentence is different. ⟦यात⟧: the form of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧ in the imperative; here too the verb is not unaccented. ⟦शीभं⟧: the root ⟦शीभृ कत्थने⟧, in the sense of boasting; ⟦श्लाघ्यतेऽनेन तद्वानिति⟧, he who has it is praised by it; the suffix ghañ in the sense of the instrument. The accent is that of the suffix marked with ñ.

## Ṛk 13

Viśvāmitra, who earlier wished to cross, having spoken to the rivers, now again wishing to cross, says: O rivers, ⟦वो⟧, your ⟦ऊर्मिः⟧, wave, ⟦शम्या युगकीला⟧, the yoke-pegs, ⟦युग्यकटपार्श्वादिसंलग्ना⟧ ⟦रज्जव⟧ **NOTE**{the supplied text has ⟦युग्यकटपार्श्वादि⟧; the reference text has yugyakaṇṭhapārśvādi}, the ropes fastened to the sides of the yoked animals and the like, ⟦उदूर्ध्वं यथा भवंति तथा हंतु⟧, so that they are upward, let it go. The intention is that the wave goes below the ropes. Likewise, O waters, ⟦योक्राणि⟧, those ropes, ⟦मुंचत⟧, release; the intention is that you go so that they do not touch. ⟦व्येनसा⟧, free of sin, ⟦आत एवाडुष्कृत्⟧, therefore doers of good deeds, ⟦अघ्न्य⟧, inviolable, not to be despised by anyone, the two, ⟦विपाट्⟧ and ⟦ळुतद्रौ⟧, ⟦शूनं समृद्धिं मारतां⟧, prosperity: may they come. Thus Viśvāmitra, having praised the rivers and having been permitted by them, crossed.

The grammatical part. ⟦ऊर्मिः⟧: ⟦ऋ गत्⟧, the root ṛ in the sense of going; by the rule ⟦आर्तरूच्चति⟧ the suffix mi. ⟦ऊरित्ययमादेशो धातोः⟧: this ūr is the substitute of the root. ⟦ऋच्छतीत्यूर्मिः⟧: it goes, therefore it is a wave. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦शम्याः⟧: the root ⟦शमु उपशमे⟧, in the sense of ceasing; the suffix yat by the rule ⟦पोरदुपधादिति यत्पत्ययः⟧. By the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव इत्याद्युदात्तत्वं⟧ the accent is initial. ⟦हंतु⟧: the form of the root ⟦हन हिंसागत्योरित्यस्य⟧ in the imperative; the verb is unaccented. ⟦योक्राणि⟧: the root ⟦युजिर् योगे⟧; in the sense of the instrument, by the rule beginning ⟦करणे दाम्मिशसयुयुजेत्यादिना⟧ a suffix. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦मुंचत⟧: the root ⟦मुच्च मोक्षणे⟧, in the sense of releasing; the verb is unaccented. ⟦अमुष्कृत्⟧: by the rule ⟦इसुसोः सामर्थ्य इति⟧ the visarga becomes ṣ. ⟦व्येनसा⟧: in a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member. The substitute ḍā for the case-ending. ⟦अघ्न्या⟧: of the root ⟦हन हिंसागत्योरित्यस्य⟧ with a negative, by the enumeration in the rule ⟦नर्वस्याघ्न्यादयश्चति निपातनाद्यक्⟧ the suffix yak. Because it is marked with k, the penultimate is elided. By the rule ⟦हो हंतेरिति⟧ h becomes gh. Since all rules are optional in the Veda, here there is no substitution of śī for the dual ending. The accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦शूनं⟧: by the rule ⟦स्वयतेर्नपुंसके भावे क्त इति क्तः⟧ the suffix kta in the neuter in the sense of the action. Because the root begins with a yaj-class letter, there is vowel-substitution. By the rule ⟦हल इति दीर्घत्वं⟧ lengthening. By the rule ⟦ओदितश्चति निष्ठानत्वं⟧ the n of the participle. By the rule ⟦निष्ठा च द्व्यजनादित्याद्युदात्तः⟧ the accent is initial. ⟦अरतां⟧: of the root ⟦ऋ गतावितस्य⟧, in the aorist, the substitute aṅ for cli by the rule ⟦सतिशास्त्रीर्तिभ्यश्चेत्यजादेशः⟧. Guṇa by the rule ⟦ऋदृशोऽजु गुणः⟧. By the rule ⟦न माज्योग इत्यडभावः⟧ there is no augment aṭ. The verb is unaccented.
