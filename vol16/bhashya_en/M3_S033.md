## Introduction

The fourth sūkta, of thirteen Ṛks, begins ⟦प्र पर्वतानां⟧. Here is the Anukramaṇī: ⟦प्र पर्वतानां सप्तोना संवादो नदीभिर्विश्वामित्रस्योत्तितीर्षोस्तत्र नदीवाक्यं चशुर्थिषष्ठष्टमीदशम्यः षष्ठिसप्तम्योस्त्विंद्रस्तुतिरंत्यानुष्टुबिति⟧. That is: the sūkta ⟦प्र पर्वतानां⟧ is ⟦सप्तोना⟧, less by seven; it is a dialogue of Viśvāmitra, who wishes to cross, with the rivers; in it the speech of the rivers is in the fourth, sixth, eighth and tenth Ṛks; in the sixth and seventh there is praise of Indra; the last Ṛk is an Anuṣṭubh.

Here, since the fourth, sixth, eighth and tenth Ṛks are the speech of the rivers, the rivers themselves are the seers of these; since the rest are the speech of Viśvāmitra, he alone is the seer of the rest. The last Ṛk is an Anuṣṭubh; the rest are Triṣṭubh. The deity is Indra. Although in the sixth and the seventh Viśvāmitra and the rivers are praised, still Indra alone is the deity. The application of the sūkta is by its indication.

Long ago, it is said, Viśvāmitra became the house-priest of King Sudās, the Paijavana. Having obtained wealth through the priesthood, he took all the wealth and came to the confluence of the Vipāś and the Śutudrī, and the others followed him. Then Viśvāmitra, wishing to cross, saw those two rivers in water too deep to wade, and praised them with the first three Ṛks for the sake of crossing.

## Ṛk 1

⟦पर्वतानां⟧ means of the mountains, of the hills, of the rocks. ⟦उपस्थात्⟧ means from the lap. Coming forth from there, the two rivers are ⟦उशती⟧, desiring to go to the sea. An example is given for the going: ⟦अश्वे इव⟧. Just as two mares, ⟦विषिते⟧, set free from the stable, are ⟦हासमाने⟧, vying with each other in mutual speed. Or else ⟦हासमाने⟧, rejoicing: ⟦अश्वे इव⟧, that is, like two mares (⟦वडवे इव⟧), going with haste, rejoicing in one another. In the same way, ⟦गावेव शुभ्रे⟧: just as two cows are shining, so these two are ⟦शुभ्रे⟧, shining. Further, ⟦मातरा⟧: just as two mothers, two cows, are ⟦रिहाणे⟧ — the root ⟦लिहिः⟧ has the sense of the desiderative contained in it — wishing to lick the calf with the tongue, they go quickly; in the same way these two, going with speed in order to reach the sea, are joined with water. ⟦विपाट्⟧ is so called either from tearing the banks, or from loosening, or from setting free. ⟦शुतुद्री⟧: ⟦शु⟧, quickly, ⟦तु⟧, pushed on (⟦तुन्ना⟧), as if pushed on (⟦तुन्नेव⟧), it runs, it goes; thus it is ⟦शुतुद्री⟧. The two rivers of these names ⟦प्र जवेते⟧ go swiftly towards the sea.

Here is the Nirukta: ⟦पर्वतानामुपस्थादुपस्थानादुशत्यौ कामयमाने अश्वे इव विमुक्ते इति वा विषण्णॆ इति वा हासमाने हासतिः स्पर्धायां हर्षमाणेवा गावानिव शुभ्रे शोभने मातरौ संरिहाणे विपाट्⟧ and ⟦भुतुदौ पयसा प्रजवेते⟧ (⟦नि. ९-३९⟧). That is: from the lap, from the standing near, of the mountains, the two are desiring; they are like two mares set free, or, it is said, like two that are ⟦विषण्णॆ⟧; ⟦हासमाने⟧: the root ⟦हासतिः⟧ is used in the sense of rivalry, or they are rejoicing; like two cows ⟦शुभ्रे⟧, that is, beautiful; two mothers ⟦संरिहाणे⟧, licking together; the Vipāś and the Śutudrī go forth swiftly with water.

⟦उशती⟧: the root is ⟦वश⟧ in the sense of desiring. Because the suffix śatṛ of this root is marked with ṅ, the vowel-substitution samprasāraṇa takes place by the rule beginning grahijyāvayi. ⟦विषिते⟧: from the root ⟦षिञ् बंधन⟧, in the sense of binding, the past participle in the passive. In the saṃhitā the change to ṣ takes place by the rule beginning ⟦परिनिविभ्यः सेवसितसयसिवुसह⟧ (⟦पा. ८-३-७०⟧). By the rule ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the gati keeps its natural accent. ⟦हासमाने⟧: the root ⟦हासतिः⟧ is in the sense of rivalry, or from ⟦हसे हसने ना⟧, the root hasa in the sense of laughing; then the suffix śānac. When its accent as a lasārvadhātuka suffix has been made, the accent of the root remains. ⟦रिहाणी⟧: the root ⟦लिह आस्वादने⟧, in the sense of tasting, takes both voices because it has a svarita as its indicatory vowel. Then the suffix śānac; the elision of śap, because the root belongs to the adādi class. The change of the letter l to r is Vedic. Resting on the r, the change of n to ṇ takes place. Because the suffix has c as its marker, the accent falls on the final syllable. ⟦विपाट्⟧: the two roots, ⟦पट गतौ⟧, in the sense of going, or ⟦पश⟧, in the senses of obstructing and touching, ending in the causative suffix, with the prefix vi. The change of the letter ś to ṣ is by the rule beginning vraśca. ⟦शुतुद्री⟧: the accomplishment of the form is Vedic. ⟦जवेते⟧: the root ⟦जुज् गतौ⟧, in the sense of going, of the first class; because it is marked with ṅ, it takes the ātmanepada. The substitution of iy is by the rule ⟦आतो जित⟧. The verb is unaccented.

## Ṛk 2

O two rivers, ⟦इंद्रेषिते⟧, sent forth by Indra, ⟦भिक्षमाणे⟧, begging, asking, ⟦प्रसवं⟧, the permission of that Indra, you go ⟦अच्छ⟧, towards, the sea: ⟦याथः⟧ means you go. The example is ⟦रथ्येव⟧: just as two charioteers go to the place that is their goal, so you do. Doing what? ⟦समाराणे⟧, meeting one another; ⟦ऊर्मिभिः⟧, with waves, that is, with billows; ⟦पिन्वमाने⟧, satisfying the country around; ⟦शुभ्रे⟧, shining. It is joined with what went before: you two go to the sea. And ⟦वां⟧, between you two, ⟦अन्या⟧, the one, ⟦अपि⟧ ⟦एति⟧, goes into, ⟦अन्यां⟧, the other river: she goes into her; the meaning is that they attain oneness with each other.

⟦इंद्रेषिते⟧: from the root ⟦इष⟧, in the sense of going, the past participle in the passive; the augment iṭ is by the rule beginning tīṣasaha. By the rule tṛtīyā karmaṇi the accent is that of the first member of the compound. ⟦प्रसवं⟧: the suffix ap after the root ⟦षू प्रेरण⟧, in the sense of impelling. The accent is that of the rule beginning thātha. ⟦भिक्षमाणे⟧: the root ⟦भिक्ष⟧ in the sense of begging; it takes the ātmanepada. When the suffix śānac is made unaccented by the accent of the lasārvadhātuka suffix, the accent of the root remains. ⟦रथ्येव⟧: these two belong to the chariot (⟦रथस्येम्⟧); in the sense of "this belongs to that", the suffix yat is added by the rule rathād yat. Because it is marked with t, the accent is svarita. With iva the case-ending is not elided. ⟦याथः⟧: the form of the root ⟦या⟧ in the present tense. ⟦समाराणे⟧: of the root ⟦ऋ⟧, in the sense of going, the perfect. Since the root ṛ with the prefix sam takes the ātmanepada by the rule beginning samo gami, the substitute for its perfect ending is kānac. The guṇa is by the rule ṛcchatyṝtām. ⟦पिन्वमाने⟧: the root ⟦पिवि सेचने⟧, in the sense of sprinkling, of the first class. When the suffix śānac is made unaccented by the accent of the lasārvadhātuka suffix, the accent of the root remains.

## Ṛk 3

O two rivers, ⟦मातृतमां⟧, most of all a mother, ⟦सिंधुं⟧, the flowing one, the Śutudrī: you I, Viśvāmitra, have reached, ⟦अच्छ⟧, facing you, ⟦आयासं⟧. ⟦उर्वीं⟧, great, ⟦सुभगां⟧, fortunate, ⟦विपाशं⟧: you we have reached, ⟦अगन्म⟧. Doing what? ⟦मातरा⟧, two mothers, two cows, ⟦वत्समिव संरिहाणे⟧: the root ⟦लिहिः⟧ has the sense of the desiderative contained in it; as the two, wishing to lick with the tongue, follow the calf, so these two, ⟦समानं⟧, one, ⟦योनिं⟧, place, that is the sea, ⟦अनु⟧, with that as their aim, ⟦संचरंती⟧, move well. It is connected with what went before: I have reached you two.

⟦अयासं⟧: the form of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧, in the sense of causing to reach. The absence of the augment iṭ is Vedic. ⟦अगन्म⟧: the form of the root ⟦गम⟧ in the past tense; by the rule "variously in the Veda" there is elision of śap. By the rule ⟦म्वोश्च⟧ the letter m is changed to n. The verb is unaccented. ⟦संचरंती⟧: the root ⟦चरति⟧ is in the sense of going. Because there is no connection with the third case, the ātmanepada does not occur. When the suffix śatṛ is made unaccented by the accent of the lasārvadhātuka suffix, the accent of the root remains.

## Ṛk 4

Having been praised thus, the two rivers said to Viśvāmitra: ⟦एनैनेन⟧, by this ⟦पयसा⟧, water; we, ⟦पिन्वमानाः⟧, mingling, are ⟦चरंतीः⟧, going, ⟦अनु⟧, aiming at, ⟦योनिं⟧, the place, the sea, ⟦देवकृतं⟧, made by the god Indra, that is, ordered by him. **NOTE**{the supplied text has ⟦संपर्कयंतो⟧ for ⟦पिन्वमानाः⟧; the reference text has santarpayantyaḥ, "satisfying"} So we are. **NOTE**{the supplied text and the reference text both have ⟦वयमास्मदे⟧; it is translated as vayam āsmahe, "we are"} The plural for two is for honour. Of us, of such, ⟦सर्गतक्तः⟧, set to the going, the going forth, ⟦प्रसवः⟧, the effort, ⟦न वर्तवे⟧, does not occur for turning back. ⟦किंयुः⟧, wishing what, does this ⟦विप्रः⟧, brahmin, ⟦जोहवीति⟧, call ⟦नद्यः⟧, us rivers? That is, he calls greatly.

⟦एना⟧: for the third case of the word ⟦इदं⟧ there is the substitute ena. By the rule supāṃ suluk the substitute ā for the third case. By the rule ūḍidam the case-ending is acute. ⟦पिन्वमानाः⟧: the root ⟦पिवि सेचने⟧, in the sense of sprinkling. ⟦देवकृतं⟧: by the rule tṛtīyā karmaṇi the accent is that of the stem of the first member. ⟦वर्तवे⟧: the root ⟦वृतु वर्तने⟧, in the sense of turning. In the sense of the infinitive the suffix taven is added. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦सर्गतक्तः⟧: by the rule ⟦क्ले च⟧ the accent is that of the stem of the first member. ⟦किंयुः⟧: wishing what. ⟦क्यचि मांताव्ययप्रतिषेधः⟧ (⟦पा. ३-१-८-१⟧), "before kyac, the prohibition of what ends in m and of indeclinables": since this is Vedic, the prohibition does not apply here, and so the suffix kyac is added. By the rule ⟦क्याच्छंदस⟧, "after kya, in the Veda", the suffix u is added; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦नद्यः⟧: the change to y is Vedic. ⟦जोहवीति⟧: when samprasāraṇa has been made by the rule ⟦ह्वयो यज्लुक्यभ्यस्तस्य च⟧, the guṇa of the reduplicating syllable is by the rule ⟦गुणो यज्लुको⟧. By the rule yaṅo vā the augment iṭ is added. Guṇa. The verb is unaccented.

## Ṛk 5

Viśvāmitra speaks to the rivers. ⟦ऋतावरीः⟧: ⟦ऋतमुदकं⟧, ṛta is water; O rivers possessing it, you ⟦मे⟧, of me, Viśvāmitra, ⟦सोम्याय⟧, for the speech that effects Soma, thinking "having crossed, I shall prepare Soma"; ⟦वचसे⟧, for the sake of that speech; ⟦एवैः⟧: the instrumental is used in the sense of the fifth case, away from quick goings, ⟦मुहूर्तं⟧, for a moment only, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦रमध्वं⟧: the root ⟦रमि⟧ with upa is used in the sense of stopping; for a moment only, cease from quick going. When the rivers, spoken to in general, were not doing the settled purpose **NOTE**{the supplied text has ⟦समाहितं⟧; the reference text has samīhitam, "desired"}, he speaks to the Śutudrī standing before him. ⟦कुशिकस्य सूनुः⟧: I, Viśvāmitra, son of Kuśika, the royal seer, ⟦बृहती मनीषा⟧, by great ⟦मनीषा⟧, that is, by praise, ⟦अवस्युः⟧, desiring protection for myself, ⟦सिंधुं⟧, the Śutudrī, to you, ⟦अच्छ⟧, facing you, ⟦प्राह्वे⟧, I call forth, I call intensely.

Here is the Nirukta: ⟦उपरमढ्वं मे वचसे सोम्याय सोमसंपादिन⟧ ⟦ऋतावरीर्यतवत्य⟧ ⟦ऋतमित्युदकनाम प्रत्यतं भवति⟧ ⟦मुहूर्तमेवैरयनैरवनैर्वा⟧ ⟦प्राभिह्वयामि सिंधुं बृहत्या महत्या मनीषया मनस ईषया स्तुत्या प्रज्ञया⟧ ⟦वावनाय कुशिकस्य सूनुः⟧ ⟦कुशिको राजा बभूव⟧ (⟦नि २-२५⟧). That is: cease, for my speech, for the preparer of Soma; ⟦ऋतावरीः⟧ are those possessing ṛta; ⟦ऋतमित्युदकनाम⟧: ṛta is a name of water, and it becomes ⟦प्रत्यतं भवति⟧. ⟦मुहूर्तं⟧, for a moment, ⟦एवैः⟧, by goings or by protections. I call forth the Sindhu with great, that is ⟦बृहत्या महत्या⟧, ⟦मनीषया⟧, which is ⟦मनस ईषया⟧, by praise or by wisdom, for protection; the son of Kuśika. Kuśika was a king.

⟦रमध्वं⟧: the root ⟦रमु उपरमे⟧, in the sense of stopping; after the root ram preceded by upa, by the rule "optionally, when it is intransitive" (⟦पा. १-३-८५⟧), the ātmanepada occurs. ⟦ऋतावरीः⟧: after the word ṛta, in the sense of "possessing", the suffix vanip is added in the Veda, by the rule chandasīvanipau. By the rule ⟦वनो र च⟧ the feminine suffix ṅīp and the substitute r for the final. By the rule vā chandasi the lengthening for the like vowel. Since a vocative stands at the beginning of a pāda, the initial acute taught in the sixth book occurs. ⟦एवैः⟧: the root ⟦इण् गत्⟧, in the sense of going; the suffix van by the rule ⟦इण्‌शीङ्ब्यां वन्⟧. The guṇa is that marked by the ārdhadhātuka suffix. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦बृहती मनीषा⟧: in both words the lengthening for the preceding like vowel in the third case. ⟦अवस्युः⟧: wishing protection (⟦अवो रक्षणमिच्छन्⟧). The suffix kyac after a noun in the sense of oneself. Since the pada-designation of a stem ending in n is limited by the rule ⟦नः क्य⟧, the s here does not become ru. By the rule ⟦क्याच्छंदस⟧, "after kya, in the Veda", the suffix u. ⟦अह्वे⟧: of hvayati in luṅ, for sic, by the rule ⟦आत्मनेपदेष्पन्यतरस्यां⟧, "optionally in the ātmanepada" (⟦पा ३-१-५४⟧), the substitute aṅ. Guṇa. The verb is unaccented.

## Ṛk 6

The rivers answered. O Viśvāmitra: ⟦वज्रबाहुः⟧ means he whose arm is joined with the vajra; such a mighty Indra ⟦अरदत्⟧: the root ⟦रदतिः⟧ has the sense of digging. He dug the rivers, us. How did he dig? It is said: the ⟦परिधिं⟧, that which is set all around, ⟦नदीनां⟧, of the waters, which make a sound; the meaning is: that which stays all around having put the water inside; such a ⟦वृत्रं⟧: since it covers the sky, Vṛtra is the cloud. ⟦तं मेघमपाहन्⟧: he struck that cloud, ⟦जघान⟧. When it was struck, the waters fell; by them, as they went, we were dug. Thus he dug by means of the slaying of the cloud. He did not only dig; what more? ⟦सविता⟧ is the impeller of the whole world; ⟦सुपाणिः⟧ means fair-handed, because he is the maker of origin and continuance. Such a god, Indra, shining, ⟦अनयत्⟧, led us: having split the cloud, he filled the sea by impelling the water. In the ⟦प्रसवे⟧, the permission, of that Indra, who has such power, we, ⟦उर्वीः⟧, abundant with water, ⟦यामः⟧, go. We go; we do not stop at your word.

Yāska states the meaning that has been stated: ⟦इंद्रो अस्मानरदद्वज्रबाहू रदतिः खनतिकर्मापाहन्वृत्तं⟧ ⟦परि ० नवीनानुति व्याख्यातं⟧: Indra, with the vajra in his arm, dug us; the root ⟦रदतिः⟧ has the sense of digging; "he struck away Vṛtra, the encloser of the rivers" has been explained. ⟦देवॊnनयत्नविता सुपाणिः कल्याणपाणिः⟧: ⟦सुपाणिः⟧ is of auspicious hand. ⟦पाणिः⟧ is from ⟦पणायि. (३⟧ in the sense of worship **NOTE**{⟦पाणिः पणायि. (३ पूजाकर्मणः⟧ is damaged; the reference text has paṇāya here}. ⟦प्रगृह्य पाणी देवान्पूजयंति⟧: holding up their hands they worship the gods. ⟦तस्य वयं प्रसवे याम उर्विरुर्व्यः⟧: in his impulsion we go, we who are ⟦उर्वीः⟧ (⟦नि. २-२६⟧).

⟦अरदत्⟧: the form of radati in the imperfect. ⟦वज्रबाहुः⟧: in a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member. ⟦अहन्⟧: the form of the root han in the imperfect; the verb is unaccented. ⟦परिधिं⟧: from the root ⟦डुधाण्⟧, in the senses of holding and nourishing, in the sense of the object, when there is an upasarga, the suffix ki is added by the rule ⟦घोः कि⟧. The ā is elided. The accent is that of the latter member in a compound with a kṛt suffix. ⟦अनयत्⟧: the form of nayati in the imperfect. ⟦सुपाणिः⟧: the root ⟦पण व्यवहारे स्तुत् च⟧, in the senses of trading and praising; by the rule ⟦अशिपणायो रुडायलुकौ च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-१३२⟧), the suffix in is added, and the āya is elided. In a bahuvrīhi the accent is by the rule nañsubhyām. ⟦प्रसवे⟧: the root ⟦हू प्रेरणे⟧, in the sense of impelling; by the rule ṛdor ap the suffix ap is added in the sense of the action. The accent is the final acute of the latter member by the rule beginning ⟦थाथघ⟧. ⟦यामः⟧: the form of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧ in the present tense; the verb is unaccented. ⟦उर्विः⟧: after the word ⟦उरु⟧, the feminine suffix ṅīṣ by the rule voto guṇavacanāt. By the rule vā chandasi the lengthening for the like vowel. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 7

The deed, ⟦कर्म⟧, in the form of cutting, ⟦यत्⟧, namely that this Indra ⟦विवृश्चत्⟧, struck, for the sake of impelling the water, ⟦अहिं⟧, the cloud: that is the ⟦वीर्यं⟧, the power, of that Indra, to be ⟦शश्वधा⟧, always, ⟦प्रवाच्यं⟧, spoken of, that is, to be declared eminently. And that Indra struck with the vajra ⟦परिषदः⟧, the Asuras sitting all around, seated, who cause obstruction. Then the waters, ⟦इच्छमानाः⟧, wishing for, ⟦अयनं⟧, a place, ⟦आयन्⟧, came, that is, they go.

⟦प्रवाच्यं⟧: from the root ⟦वच परिभाषण⟧, in the sense of speaking, the suffix ṇyat is added by the rule ṛhalor ṇyat. Because it is marked with ṇ, there is vṛddhi of the penultimate. By the rule ⟦वचो⟧, "of vac, not when it is a name for a sound" (⟦पा. ७-३-६७⟧) the change of c to k does not take place. By exchange the accent is initial. Or else, after the causative vācayati, the suffix yat by the rule aco yat; the accent is by the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧. ⟦शश्वधा⟧: it should be seen that the suffix dhā in its own sense is added to the word śaśvat and that the t is elided. ⟦विवृश्चत्⟧: the root ⟦ओव्रश्च छेदने⟧, in the sense of cutting, of the tudādi class. In the imperfect the vowel-substitution is by the rule beginning grahijyāvayi. The compound is made by dividing off ⟦सह⟧ as a separate rule in ⟦सह सुप⟧; the accent is that of the compound. ⟦परिषदः⟧: the suffix kvip. In the saṃhitā the change to ṣ is by the rule sader aprateḥ. ⟦जघान⟧: the form of the root han in the perfect, in the ending ṇal; the verb is unaccented. ⟦आयन्⟧: the form of the root ⟦अय⟧, in the sense of going, in the imperfect; since it stands at the beginning of a pāda, there is no unaccenting. ⟦इच्छमानाः⟧: from the root ⟦इषु⟧, in the sense of wishing, śānac by exchange. When its accent as a lasārvadhātuka suffix has been made, the accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 8

The rivers, having by the way made praise of Indra, said to Viśvāmitra: ⟦जरितः⟧, O praiser, O Viśvāmitra, that speech ⟦ते⟧, of yours, of the nature of a dialogue, ⟦यत्⟧, which, you, having approached us **NOTE**{⟦नोऽभीत्या⟧ is taken as "having approached us" followed by ⟦आ⟧}, go on ⟦घोषान्⟧, proclaiming aloud, that ⟦वचो⟧, speech, ⟦मापि मृष्ठाः⟧, do not forget. What is the reason? ⟦उत्तरा⟧ ⟦युगानि⟧, in later ages, on the days of sacrifice, ⟦उक्षेषु⟧ ⟦कारो⟧, O maker of the śastras, you ⟦प्रति⟧ ⟦जुषस्व⟧ ⟦नो⟧, us: attend on us in return with that speech of the nature of a dialogue. Now, ⟦मा नि कः⟧ ⟦नो⟧, us, ⟦पुरुषत्रा⟧, among men: do not, by the teaching of the words of the dialogue in the form of speech and reply, display exceedingly a manly boldness. ⟦ते⟧, to you, ⟦नमः⟧, homage.

⟦मृष्ठाः⟧: of the root ⟦मृजूष्⟧, in the sense of purifying, in the imperfect, the ātmanepada by exchange. The elision of śap, because it belongs to the adādi class. **NOTE**{the reference text lacks these two clauses on ⟦मृष्ठाः⟧} The change to ṣ is by the rule beginning ⟦प्रश्चादिना⟧. The verb is unaccented. ⟦घोषान्⟧: of the root ⟦घुषिर्⟧, in the sense of sounding, with śatṛ; since all rules are optional in the Veda, there is no single replacement by the following form by the rule ato guṇe. There is lengthening for the like vowel. When the accent of the śatṛ as a lasārvadhātuka suffix has been made, the accent is that of the root. ⟦युगानि⟧: the root ⟦युजिर्⟧, in the sense of joining; by its being laid down among the words beginning ⟦उंभादिषु⟧ as ending in ghañ, there is no guṇa; and this laying down is held to be for a particular domain: ⟦कालविशेषे रथाद्युपकरणे च⟧, for a particular time and for an implement of a chariot and the like; from its reading there alone, the accent is the final acute. By the rule ⟦कालाध्वनोरत्यंतसंयोग⟧ the accusative. ⟦कारो⟧: after ⟦करोतेः⟧ the suffix uṇ by the rule beginning ⟦कृपापाजिमी⟧. Since it is a vocative, it is unaccented. ⟦कः⟧: of karoti in the aorist, the elision of cli by the rule beginning mantre ghasa; the elision of sic by the rule beginning ⟦हल्यादिना⟧; by the rule na māṅyoge there is no augment aṭ. ⟦पुरुषत्रा⟧: by the rule ⟦देवमनुष्यपुरुषपुरुमर्त्येभ्यो द्वितीयासप्तम्योर्बहुलम⟧ the suffix trā in the sense of the seventh case. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 9

Viśvāmitra replied to the rivers. ⟦स्वसारः⟧ are sisters; ⟦सिंधवः⟧, O rivers: of me, ⟦कारवे⟧, who am making praise, ⟦सु⟧, well, ⟦ओ⟧ ⟦शृणोत⟧, hear my speech; hear indeed. ⟦ययौ⟧: ⟦अनसा⟧, with a cart, and together with ⟦रथेन⟧, a chariot, from a place far away I have come to you; I have arrived. You ⟦सु⟧, well, ⟦नि नमध्वं⟧, bow down: become bowed of your own accord. Likewise ⟦सुपाराः⟧: so that descending from and mounting the bank may be easy for chariots and the like, be also of fair banks. Further, you ⟦स्रोत्याभिः⟧, with waters, which are of a flowing nature, ⟦अधोअक्षाः⟧, be below the axle, which is a part of the chariot. When the waters are below the axle, then chariots and the like can be led across. Therefore the intended meaning is: be of that measure of water.

⟦ओ⟧ has the designation pragṛhya. ⟦शृणोत⟧: the root ⟦श्रु श्रवण⟧, in the sense of hearing, in the imperative; the substitute tab for the ending ta by the rule ⟦तप्तनप्तनथनाश्च⟧. Because it is marked with p, there is guṇa. The verb is unaccented. ⟦ययौ⟧: the form of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧ in the perfect used for the past in general, in the first person, before ṇal, the substitute au by the rule āta au ṇalaḥ. The accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦वः⟧: the substitute vas for the accusative plural of the word yuṣmad by the rule vasnasau. ⟦षू⟧: by the rule ⟦निपातस्य⟧ there is lengthening in the saṃhitā. ⟦नमध्वं⟧: of the root ⟦णमु⟧, in the senses of bowing and sounding, when the agent is as an object, the absence of yak is by the prohibition in the rule na duhasnunamāṃ yakciṇau. ⟦अधोअक्षाः⟧: after the word ⟦अधर⟧ the suffix asi and the substitute adh by the rule ⟦पूर्वाधरावराणामसि पुरधवश्चषाम⟧. The word ⟦अक्ष⟧ is from the root aś, in the sense of pervading, by the rule that has it in the sense of gaming (⟦उ. ३-६५⟧), ending in the suffix sa. The accent is that of the nature of the latter member in a kṛt compound. ⟦सिंधवः⟧: since it is a vocative, it is unaccented. ⟦स्रोत्याभिः⟧: after the word ⟦स्रोतः⟧, by the rule ⟦स्रोतसो विभाषा ड्यड् ड्यौ⟧ (⟦पा. ४-४-११३⟧) the suffix ḍya is added. Because it is marked ḍ, the ṭi is elided. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 10

The rivers, having first rejected the speech of Viśvāmitra, assented to his speech with this Ṛk. ⟦कारो⟧, O one making praise, O Viśvāmitra, ⟦ते⟧ your ⟦वचांसि⟧, these speeches, ⟦आ शृणवाम⟧, we hear; we do your ⟦समाहितं प्रयोजन⟧ **NOTE**{the supplied text has ⟦समाहितं⟧; the reference text has samīhitam, "desired"}, your purpose, is the meaning. Since ⟦ययाथ⟧, you have come, from afar, together with ⟦अनसा⟧, a cart, and a chariot, we too, ⟦ते⟧, for you, ⟦नि नंस्यॆ⟧, shall bow low: ⟦नीचैर्नमाम⟧. Here the singular is by the intention of each separately. The meaning is: in order that you may go by chariot, we shall become fordable water. The example there is ⟦पीप्यानेव योषा⟧: just as a mother, a woman, giving her son the breast to suck, bows low. Another example: just as a girl, a young woman, ⟦मर्यायेव⟧, to a man, or to a father or a brother, becomes bowed ⟦शश्वचै परिष्वजनाय⟧, for embracing, so, for you, we become bowed. **NOTE**{the supplied text and the reference text both have ⟦प्रह्वीभवादुः⟧; "we become bowed" is translated from the context} The repetition of ⟦ते⟧ is for respect. Yāska explains this Ṛk thus: ⟦आशृणवाम ते कारो वचनानि याहि⟧ ⟦दूरादनसा च रथेन च निनमाम ते पाययमानेव योषा पुत्रं मर्यायेव⟧ ⟦कन्या परिष्वजनाय निनमा इति वा⟧ (⟦नि. २-२७⟧). That is: we shall listen to your words, O maker of praise; come from afar with a cart and with a chariot; we shall bow to you, like a woman making her son drink, like a girl to a man for embracing; or, it is said, ⟦निनमा⟧.

⟦कारो⟧: in the vocative, by the rule ⟦संबुद्धौ शाकल्यस्येत्⟧ (⟦पा. १-१ १६⟧), the designation pragṛhya. ⟦शृणवाम⟧: of the root ⟦श्रु⟧, in the sense of hearing, in the imperative, the augment āṭ by the rule āḍ uttamasya pic ca. Because it is marked with p, there is guṇa. The verb is unaccented. ⟦ययाथ⟧: of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧ in the perfect used for the past in general; before thal, the prohibition of iṭ by the rule ekāca upadeśe 'nudāttāt. The accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦अनसा⟧: the third case in the sense of "together with". ⟦नंस्यॆ⟧: of the root ⟦णमु⟧, in the sense of bowing, in the leṭ in the first person, the suffix sip by the rule sib bahulaṃ leṭi; the substitute ai by the rule vaito 'nyatra. The verb is unaccented. ⟦पीप्यानेव⟧: the root ⟦पीज्⟧, in the sense of drinking, in which the causative sense is contained, in the perfect, in the form kānac. The accent is that of a c-marked suffix. ⟦योषा⟧: from the root ⟦यु मिश्रणे⟧, in the sense of mixing, the suffix sa by the rule beginning ⟦वृत्यवदिहनी⟧ (⟦उ. ३-६२⟧). She mixes (⟦यौती⟧), therefore ⟦योषा⟧. Because it belongs to the vṛṣādi group, the accent is initial. ⟦शश्वचै⟧: from the root ⟦स्वन्ज परिप्पंग⟧, in the sense of embracing, the suffix kvip in the sense of the action, by the rule of the sampadādi group. The desired form is accomplished by the rule of the pṛṣodarādi group, and the accent is final.

## Ṛk 11

Viśvāmitra replied to the rivers. ⟦अंग⟧ is in the sense of address. O rivers, since the crossing, by you, of me, who wish to cross, has been permitted, therefore the Bharatas, those born in the family of Bharata, all mine, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, the river who have attained oneness with one another, ⟦संतरेयुः⟧, may cross well; they may be well crossed. He makes this exact. ⟦गव्यन्⟧: wishing to cross the waters, ⟦गा उदकानि⟧; ⟦इषितः⟧, permitted by you; ⟦इंद्रजूतः⟧, impelled by Indra, who is your mover; ⟦ग्रामः⟧, the host of the Bharatas; ⟦अर्षात्⟧, may cross. Since ⟦सर्गतक्को⟧, set on going, ⟦प्रसवः⟧, their effort, ⟦अह⟧, before, was permitted by you. But I ⟦आ⟧ ⟦वृणे⟧, partake on all sides of, ⟦सुमतिं⟧, the fair praise, ⟦वः⟧, of you, ⟦यज्ञियानां⟧, who are worthy of sacrifice.

⟦भरताः⟧: from the word ⟦भरत⟧, the suffix añ because it belongs to the utsādi group; the elision of it by the rule yañañoś ca. ⟦अतच्⟧, the suffix-accent **NOTE**{⟦अतच्⟧ is not clear to me; the accent is said to be that of the suffix}. ⟦संतरेयुः⟧: the form of tarati in the optative, in the ending jus. When the accent of jhi as a lasārvadhātuka suffix has been made, the accent is that of the root. By the rule tiṅi codāttavati the gati is unaccented. ⟦गव्यन्⟧: wishing for ⟦गा⟧ for oneself; kyac after a noun. The accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦ग्रामः⟧: by the rule ⟦ग्रसतेरा च⟧ (⟦उ. १-१४२⟧) the suffix man and the substitute ā. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦इंद्रजूतः⟧: ⟦जू⟧ is a root taught in a sūtra, in the sense of going. By the rule śryukaḥ kiti there is the prohibition of iṭ in the participle. By the rule tṛtīyā karmaṇi the accent is that of the stem of the first member. ⟦अर्षात्⟧: of the root ⟦ऋ⟧, in the sense of going, in the leṭ, in the ending tip, sip by the rule sib bahulaṃ leṭi. The augment aṭ of leṭ. The prohibition of iṭ by the rule ⟦एकाच⟧. Guṇa. Because the suffix is marked with p, it is unaccented, and the accent is that of the root. ⟦वृणे⟧: the form in the present tense of the root ⟦वृज्⟧, in the sense of sharing. ⟦यज्जियानां⟧: by the rule yajñartvigbhyāṃ ghakhañau the suffix gha. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 12

⟦गव्यवः⟧, wishing for ⟦गा⟧ for themselves, the Bharatas, those born in the family of Bharata, all ⟦अतारिषुः⟧: they crossed that river. ⟦विप्रः⟧, the wise Viśvāmitra, ⟦समभक्त⟧, ⟦समभजत⟧, shared the fair praise of the rivers. But you, as before, ⟦इषयंतीः⟧, making food by means of channels and the like, therefore ⟦सुराधाः⟧, provided with fair wealth, you ⟦वक्षणाः⟧, the artificial rivers, the channels, ⟦प्र पिन्नध्वं⟧, satisfy greatly; ⟦आ पृणध्वं⟧, fill them from all sides. ⟦शीभं⟧, quickly, ⟦यात⟧, go.

⟦अतारिषुः⟧: of the root tṝ, in the senses of floating and crossing, in the aorist, vṛddhi by the rule sici vṛddhiḥ parasmaipadeṣu. The accent is that of the augment aṭ. ⟦गव्यवः⟧: kyac after a noun; the suffix u by the rule ⟦क्याच्छंदस⟧, "after kya, in the Veda"; the accent is its accent. ⟦अभक्त⟧: of the root ⟦भज⟧, in the sense of serving, in the aorist, the elision of sic by the rule jhalo jhali. Since it stands at the beginning of a pāda, the verb is not unaccented. ⟦पिन्नध्वं⟧: the form of the root ⟦पिवि सेचन⟧ in the imperative; the verb is unaccented. ⟦इषयंतीः⟧: making food. The suffix ṇic in the sense "he does that". Since by the rule ṇāv iṣṭhavat prātipadikasya the stem is treated as before iṣṭha, the ṭi is elided by the rule ṭeḥ. By the rule vā chandasi there is lengthening for the like vowel. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦पृणध्वं⟧: the root ⟦पृण प्रीणने⟧, in the sense of pleasing; the form in the imperative. The ātmanepada is by exchange. The verb is not unaccented because the sentence is different. ⟦यात⟧: the form of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧ in the imperative; here too the verb is not unaccented. ⟦शीभं⟧: the root ⟦शीभृ कत्थने⟧, in the sense of boasting; he who has it is praised (⟦श्लाघ्यते⟧) by it; the suffix ghañ in the sense of the instrument. The accent is that of the suffix marked with ñ.

## Ṛk 13

Viśvāmitra, who earlier wished to cross, having spoken to the rivers, now again wishing to cross, says: O rivers, let ⟦वो⟧, your, ⟦ऊर्मिः⟧, wave, ⟦हंतु⟧, go, in such a way that ⟦शम्या⟧, the yoke-pegs, and the ropes fastened to the hips, the sides and the like of the yoked animals **NOTE**{the supplied text has ⟦युग्यकटपार्श्वादि⟧; the reference text has yugyakaṇṭhapārśvādi}, are ⟦उदूर्ध्वं⟧, upward. The intention is: let that wave go below the ropes. Likewise, O ⟦आपो⟧, waters, ⟦मुंचत⟧, release, ⟦योक्राणि⟧, those ropes; the intention is: let them go so that they do not touch. ⟦व्येनसा⟧, free of sin, and for that very reason doers of good deeds (⟦अमुष्कृत्⟧), ⟦अघ्न्य⟧, inviolable, not to be slighted by anyone, may the two, ⟦विपाट्⟧ and the Śutudrī, ⟦मा⟧ ⟦आ⟧ ⟦अरतां⟧, not come to, ⟦शूनं⟧, increase. Thus Viśvāmitra, having praised the rivers and having been permitted by them, crossed.

⟦ऊर्मिः⟧: the root ⟦ऋ⟧ in the sense of going; by the rule arter ūc ca the suffix mi, and this ūr is the substitute of the root. It goes (⟦ऋच्छती⟧), therefore it is a wave. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦शम्याः⟧: the root ⟦शमु⟧, in the sense of ceasing; the suffix yat by the rule por adupadhāt. By the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧ the accent is initial. ⟦हंतु⟧: the form in the imperative of the root ⟦हन⟧, in the senses of injuring and going; the verb is unaccented. ⟦योक्राणि⟧: the root ⟦युजिर्⟧ in the sense of joining; in the sense of the instrument, by the rule beginning dāmnīśasayuyuja, the suffix ṣṭran. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦मुंचत⟧: the root ⟦मुच्च⟧, in the sense of releasing; the verb is unaccented. ⟦अमुष्कृत्⟧: by the rule ⟦इसुसोः सामर्थ्य⟧ the visarga becomes ṣ. ⟦व्येनसा⟧: in a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member. The substitute ḍā for the case-ending. ⟦अघ्न्या⟧: of the root ⟦हन⟧, in the senses of injuring and going, preceded by the negative, the suffix yak by its being laid down in the rule aghnyādayaś ca. Because it is marked with k, the penultimate is elided. By the rule ho hanteḥ h becomes gh. Since all rules are optional in the Veda, here there is no substitution of śī for the dual ending. The accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦शूनं⟧: after śvayati, kta by the rule ⟦नपुंसके भावे क्त⟧, "kta in the neuter in the sense of the action". Because it belongs to the yajādi group, there is vowel-substitution. By the rule ⟦हल⟧ lengthening. By the rule oditaś ca the n of the participle. By the rule niṣṭhā ca dvyaj anāt the accent is initial. ⟦अरतां⟧: of the root ⟦ऋ⟧, in the sense of going, in the aorist, the substitute aṅ for cli by the rule sartiśāstyartibhyaś ca. Guṇa by the rule ⟦ऋदृशोऽजु गुणः⟧. By the rule na māṅyoge there is no augment aṭ. The verb is unaccented.
