## Introduction
The sūkta beginning ⟦वार्त्रहत्याय⟧ has eleven Ṛks and is the eighth sūkta. The eleventh Ṛk is an anuṣṭubh; the rest are gāyatrī. And so the Anukramaṇī has it: ⟦वार्त्रहत्याय गायत्रमंत्यानुष्टुब⟧, that is, the sūkta ⟦वार्त्रहत्याय⟧ is in gāyatrī, and the last Ṛk is an anuṣṭubh.

In the Atirātra, in the first paryāya, in the śastra of the Brāhmaṇācchaṃsin, the sūkta ⟦वार्त्रहत्याय⟧ is to be recited with the exception of the last Ṛk. And so it is laid down in the Sūtra: ⟦वार्त्रहत्यायेत्युत्तमामुद्धरेत्⟧ (⟦आ. ६-४⟧), that is, in the sūkta beginning ⟦वार्त्रहत्याय⟧ he should take out the last Ṛk.

In the Mahāvrata, in the eighty tṛcas of gāyatrī, this sūkta, with the exception of the last Ṛk, is laid down in the Sūtra in the Fifth Āraṇyaka: ⟦वार्त्रहत्याय शवस⟧, he takes out the last Ṛk (⟦ऐ. आ, ५-२-५⟧).

## Ṛk 1
Viśvāmitra praises. O Indra, for ⟦शवसे⟧, power, ⟦वार्त्रहत्याय⟧, that has the slaying of Vṛtra as its cause, and also for ⟦पृतनासाह्याय⟧, the overcoming of the army of others, we ⟦वर्तयामसि⟧ turn you hither from every side; that is, we set you in motion.

On ⟦वार्त्रहत्याय⟧: in the sense "the action of the slayer of Vṛtra", the suffix ṣyañ is added, since the word belongs to the group beginning with brāhmaṇa. By the rule ⟦हनस्तोऽचिण्णलोः⟧ (⟦पा. ७-३-३२⟧) the letter t results. Because the suffix carries the marker ñ, the accent is udātta on the first syllable. On ⟦पृतनाषाह्याय⟧: from the root ⟦षह⟧, in the sense of enduring, the suffix yat is added in the sense of the action, by the rule ⟦शकिसहोश्च⟧. In the Saṃhitā, by the rule ⟦सहेः पृतनर्ताभ्यां च⟧ (⟦पा- ८-३-१०९⟧) the ṣ-change takes place. The lengthening is Vedic (⟦चांदसः⟧). On ⟦वर्तयामसि⟧: from the root ⟦वृतु⟧, in the sense of turning, in the causative, for the ending mas the substitute masi is made, by the rule ⟦इदंतो मसि⟧. The accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 2
O ⟦शतक्रतो⟧: ⟦शतं क्रतवो यस्यासौ शतक्रतुः⟧, Śatakratu is he who has a hundred kratus. O Indra, ⟦वाघतः⟧: ⟦वहंति यज्ञियां धुर⟧, they carry the burden of the sacrifice; thus the ⟦वाघतः⟧ are the praisers. May they ⟦कृण्वंतु⟧ make ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦मनः⟧ mind, which is ⟦सु⟧ good, that is, excellent by the bringing about of the fruit we desire, ⟦अर्वाचीनं⟧, turned towards us. And ⟦उत⟧, also, ⟦चक्षुः⟧, your sight: let them make your sight a look of favour on us.

On ⟦अर्वाचीनं⟧: the suffix kha, by the rule ⟦विभाषांचेरदिक् स्त्रीया⟧; the substitute ina for kha. On ⟦चक्षुः⟧: from the root ⟦चक्षेः⟧ the suffix usi, by the rule ⟦चक्षेः शिच्च⟧; because it is treated as having the marker ś, the substitute khyā for the root does not take place. Because the marker nit is carried over, the accent is udātta on the first syllable. On ⟦कृण्वंतु⟧: the root ⟦कृवि⟧, in the senses of injuring and doing, in its imperative form. Since, the vocative being treated as non-existent, the word stands at the beginning of the pāda, there is no nighāta. The accent is that of the suffix. On ⟦वाघतः⟧: the root ⟦वह⟧, in the sense of conveying; by the rule beginning ⟦संश्च द्वेहद⟧ the form is irregularly given as ending in the suffix ati, and therefore the penultimate vowel takes vṛddhi. The letter h is replaced by gh. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 3
O ⟦शतक्रतो⟧, O Indra, ⟦अभिमातिषाह्ये⟧: ⟦मातिर्मानो गर्वः⟧, māti is pride, arrogance. Those who have pride on every side (⟦अभितो⟧) are the ⟦तेऽभिमातयः⟧, the enemies. The withstanding of them is itself ⟦सह्यं⟧. When that battle has come upon us, ⟦ते तव नामानि⟧, your names, the names beginning with Śakra and Vajrahasta (⟦शक्रवज्रहस्तादीनि⟧); or else the powers that are marked by them: we ⟦ईमहे⟧ beg, with ⟦विश्वाभिर्गीर्भिः⟧, all words, which have the nature of praise (⟦स्तुतिलक्षणाभि⟧).

On ⟦ईमहे⟧: the root ⟦ई⟧, in the senses beginning with desiring. The middle voice is by interchange. Because it belongs to the group beginning with ad, śap is dropped. The accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 4
O Indra, we ⟦महयामसि⟧ praise: ⟦स्तोत्रं कुर्मः⟧, we make a hymn of praise, to Indra, ⟦पुरुष्टुतस्य⟧, who is praised by many (⟦पुरुभिर्बहुभिः⟧) praisers; who is endowed with ⟦धामभिः⟧, splendours (⟦तेजोभिर्युक्तस्य⟧), ⟦शतेन⟧, a hundred, that is, of unmeasured number; ⟦चर्षणीधृतः⟧, the supporter, by the bringing about of the fruit they desire, of the ⟦चर्षणीनां⟧, men who perform rites (⟦कर्मानुष्ठात्यणा⟧); of Indra. Or else: ⟦आकृषंति सर्वमनेन⟧, "by this they draw everything", is why ⟦चर्षणि⟧ is strength; we praise you, ⟦तव⟧, who are the supporter of that.

On ⟦पुरुष्टुतस्य⟧: the root ⟦ष्टुञ्⟧, in the sense of praising; the suffix kta in the passive. The accent is ⟦थाधादिस्वरः⟧, the accent taught by the rule beginning with thātha. In the Saṃhitā the ṣ-change takes place by the rule that teaches it for ⟦स्तुतस्तोमयो⟧, stuta and stoma, in the Veda. On ⟦महयामसि⟧: the root ⟦मह⟧, in the sense of worship, in laṭ, with masi ending in i (⟦लटीदंतो मसिः⟧). The accent is nighāta. On ⟦चर्षणीधृतः⟧: the root ⟦कृष⟧, in the sense of drawing; by the rule ⟦कृषेरादेश्च चः⟧ (⟦उ. २-१०५⟧) the suffix ani is added, and the letter k is replaced by c. Or else: the root ⟦कृष⟧, in the sense of ploughing; because they plough, the ⟦चर्षणयो⟧ are men. Then the root ⟦धृ⟧, in the sense of holding, with kvip. The accent is that of a last member formed with a kṛt suffix.

## Ṛk 5
O Indra, ⟦वृत्राय हंतवे⟧, to slay the Asura named Vṛtra; ⟦भरेषु⟧, in battles; and ⟦वाजसातये⟧, for the gain of wealth marked by food; ⟦पुरुहूतं⟧, called by many; ⟦इंद्रं⟧, you who are strong: I ⟦त्वामुपब्रुवे⟧ call. I, Viśvāmitra, summon you for the drinking of Soma.

On ⟦वृत्राय⟧: by the saying ⟦क्रियाग्रहणं कर्तव्य⟧, "the mention of the action must be made", the object takes the dative. On ⟦हंतवे⟧: from the root han the suffix taven is added in the sense of tum. The accent is that of the marker n. On ⟦ब्रुवे⟧: the root ⟦ब्रूञ्⟧, in the sense of clear speech; the first-person form in laṭ, with iṭ. The accent is nighāta. On ⟦वाजसातये⟧: ⟦सननं सातिः⟧, gaining is sāti; ⟦वाजस्य सातिर्वाजसातिः⟧, the gaining of vāja is vājasāti. Because it belongs to the group beginning with dāsībhāra, the accent is that of the original form of the first member.

## Ṛk 6
O ⟦शतक्रतो⟧, O Indra, ⟦वाजेषु⟧, in battles (⟦संग्रामेषु⟧), ⟦सासहिः⟧ ⟦भव⟧, be an overcomer of enemies. ⟦वृत्राय हंतवे⟧, to slay Vṛtra, ⟦त्वामीमहे⟧, we beseech you; that is, we pray to you.

On ⟦सासहिः⟧: by the saying ⟦सहिवहिचलिपतिभ्यो यजंतेभ्यः किकिनौ वक्तव्या⟧, "after sahi, vahi, cali and pati ending in yaṅ, ki and kin are to be stated", the suffix ki is added. The accent is that of it. On ⟦वृत्राय⟧: the object takes the dative. On ⟦हंतवे⟧: the suffix taven is added in the sense of tumun.

## Ṛk 7
O Indra, whatever rivals are proud in ⟦द्युम्नेषु⟧, shining riches that are to be obtained, and ⟦पृतनाज्ये⟧: ⟦पृतनानामजनं गमनं यस्मिन्⟧, that in which there is the ajana, that is, the going, of armies, is ⟦पृतनाज्यं⟧, battle. And in that battle, ⟦पृत्सुतूर्षु⟧, in the heroes whose habit it is to cross over among the armies, and ⟦श्रवःसु⟧, in strengths, whatever proud rivals there are, them, ⟦साक्ष्य⟧, overcome. Likewise ⟦अभिमातिषु⟧, among the enemies, the men, who are full of pride on every side, whatever rivals there are, overcome those too.

On ⟦पृतनाज्ये⟧: the root ⟦अज⟧, in the senses of going and throwing, in the causative, with the suffix yat by the rule ⟦अचो यत्⟧. ⟦पृतनानामाज्यं पृतनाज्यं⟧, "the ājya of armies" is pṛtanājya. The accent is the one belonging to the single ekādeśa substitute. On ⟦पृत्सुतूर्षु⟧: when the word ⟦पृतना⟧ is followed by su, the substitute pṛd is made, by the supplementary statement ⟦मांस्पृत्स्नौनामुपसंख्यान⟧. The root ⟦इत्वरा⟧, in the sense of haste; with kvip in the habitual sense; by the rule beginning ⟦ज्वरत्वर⟧ the substitute ūṭ is made. By the saying ⟦तत्पुरुषे कृति बहुल⟧ the locative ending of the first member is not dropped. The accent is that of a last member formed with a kṛt suffix. On ⟦साक्ष्य⟧: the root ⟦सह⟧, in the sense of enduring, in loṭ; by the rule that teaches it ⟦बहुलं⟧, variously, in the Veda, śap is dropped. The changes to ḍh and to k take place. The lengthening is Vedic (⟦कांदसः⟧).

## Ṛk 8
On the Cāturviṃśika day, at the midday pressing, in the śastra of the Brāhmaṇācchaṃsin, the tṛca ⟦शुष्मिंतमं न ऊतय⟧ is the optional anurūpa. And it is laid down in the Sūtra: ⟦शुष्मिन्तमं न ऊतये श्रायंत इव सूर्यं⟧ (⟦आ. ७-४⟧). **NOTE**{the supplied text has here the passage on the Caturviṃśa day and the Sūtra quotation; the reference text has no such passage at this Ṛk}

O ⟦शतक्रतो⟧, O Indra, ⟦शुष्मिंतमं⟧, extremely strong among the strong ones; ⟦द्युम्मिनं⟧, radiant. Or else: ⟦शुष्मिंतमं⟧, the greatest dryer-up of enemies; ⟦द्युम्मिनं⟧, famous; ⟦जागृविं⟧, given to waking: ⟦पीतः सोमो जागृविः स्वप्ननिवारक⟧, "Soma, when drunk, is wakeful, a remover of sleep". Such a ⟦सोमं⟧, Soma, ⟦नः⟧, ours, that is, belonging to us, ⟦ऊतये⟧, for our protection, ⟦पाहि⟧, drink: come here and drink.

On ⟦शुष्मिंतमं⟧: because the suffix tamap has the marker p, it is anudātta, and so the accent is that of the suffix. On ⟦पाहि⟧: ⟦पातेर्लोटि⟧, the root pā in loṭ; by the rule that teaches it ⟦बहुलं⟧, variously, in the Veda, śap is dropped. The accent is nighāta. On ⟦जागृविः⟧: it ends in the suffix kvin. Because of the marker n, the accent is udātta on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 9
O ⟦शतक्रतो⟧, O Indra, among the five peoples (⟦पंचसु जनेषु⟧) that belong to you, the Gandharvas, the Fathers, the Devas, the Asuras and the Rākṣasas, those ⟦इंद्रियाणि⟧, powers, the abilities, grasping of form and the like (⟦रूपग्रहणादिसामर्थ्यानि⟧), which are established in these: those powers of yours, ⟦आ वृणे⟧, I partake of. Or else: among the five peoples, that is, among the four peoples that have the Niṣāda as the fifth (⟦निषादपंचमेषु⟧), whatever powers there are, those powers of yours I partake of.

On ⟦इंद्रियाणि⟧: by the rule ⟦इंद्रियमिंद्रलिंगमिंद्रदृष्टमिंद्रसृष्टमिंद्रजुष्टमिंद्रदत्तमिति वे⟧ the word is irregularly given as ending in the suffix ghac, **NOTE**{the supplied text names the suffix ghac; the reference text names it dhac} and so the accent is udātta on the final syllable. On ⟦वृणे⟧: the root ⟦वृज्⟧, in the sense of partaking, in the form of laṭ. The accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 10
O Indra, ⟦बृहत्⟧, great, ⟦श्रवः⟧, the food that is characterised by Soma and is given by us, ⟦अगन्⟧, may it go to you. You, ⟦दधिष्व⟧, bestow on us ⟦द्युम्नं⟧, wealth, ⟦दुस्तरं⟧, hard to cross, that cannot be crossed by enemies. But we ⟦ते⟧, your ⟦शुष्मं⟧, strength, ⟦उत्⟧, excellent, ⟦तिरामसि⟧, make it grow with Soma, ghee and the like. **NOTE**{the reference text has the vocative vṛtrahan, "O slayer of Vṛtra", before the gloss of bṛhat; the supplied text has no such word}

On ⟦अगन्⟧: the root gam in laṅ; by the rule that teaches it ⟦बहुलं⟧, variously, in the Veda, śap is dropped. By the rule beginning ⟦हल्ञादिना⟧ the loss of ti takes place. By ⟦मो नो धातोः⟧ the m of the root becomes n. The accent is that of the augment āṭ. **NOTE**{the supplied text names the augment āṭ; the reference text names aṭ} On ⟦दधिष्व⟧: ⟦दधातेर्लोटि⟧, the root dhā in loṭ; since by the rule ⟦छंदस्यभयथ⟧ the ending thās is ārdhadhātuka, the augment iṭ results. The accent is nighāta. On ⟦दुस्तरं⟧: from the root ⟦तरतेः⟧, in the sense of difficulty, the suffix khal. Because it belongs to the group beginning with suṣāman, the ṣ-change takes place in the Saṃhitā. The accent is that of the marker l. On ⟦तिरामसि⟧: masi ending in i (⟦इवंतो मसिः⟧). The accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 11
O ⟦शक्र⟧, O Indra, strong one, ⟦अर्वावतो⟧, from a region near at hand, on this side, and ⟦अथो⟧, also, ⟦परावतः⟧, from a region very far away, looking towards us (⟦नोऽस्मानभिलक्ष्या⟧), ⟦गहि⟧, come. ⟦अद्रिवः⟧: ⟦अत्ति भक्षयति शत्रून⟧, that which eats, devours, enemies is adri, the thunderbolt; O Indra, possessing it, the world that is ⟦ते⟧, yours, ⟦य उ लोक⟧, which is the highest world, ⟦ततः⟧, from that world too, ⟦इह⟧, here, in this place of sacrifice to the gods (⟦देवयजने देशे⟧), for the drinking of Soma, come.

On ⟦गहि⟧: ⟦गमेर्लोटि⟧, the form of the root gam in loṭ. On ⟦परावतः⟧: by the saying ⟦उपसर्गाच्छंदसि धात्वर्थ⟧ the suffix vati is added. The accent is that of the suffix. On ⟦अद्रिवः⟧: by the saying ⟦मतुवसो रु⟧ the substitute ru is made. On ⟦ततः⟧: by the saying ⟦पंचम्यास्तसिल⟧ the suffix tasil is added. The accent is that of the marker l.
