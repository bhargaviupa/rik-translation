## Introduction
The fifteenth sūkta, of fifteen Ṛks, is ⟦प्र वो वाजा⟧. On it the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦प्र वः पंचोना गायत्रं त्वृतव्या वाद्येति⟧, "'Pra vaḥ': less by five; but in Gāyatrī; the first is optionally addressed to the Seasons." Viśvāmitra is the seer. Gāyatrī is the metre. Agni is the deity. Or the first Ṛk has the Seasons for its deity.

The application of this sūkta is in the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra, in the Gāyatrī metre, as is said: ⟦प्र वो वाजा उपसद्याय⟧ (⟦आ. ४-१३⟧). In the Ukthya days of the Abhiplava the triplet ⟦प्र वो वाजा⟧ is an optional stotriya. And this is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦प्र वो वाजा अभिद्यवोऽभि प्रयांसि वाहसा⟧ (⟦आ. ७-८⟧). The first Ṛk of the sūkta is a sāmidhenī verse. And this is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦प्र वो वाजा अभिद्यवोऽग्न आ याहि वीतये⟧ (⟦आ. १-२⟧). Among the sāmidhenī verses the one beginning ⟦समिध्यमानो⟧ is one. And this is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦समिध्यमानो अध्वरे समिद्धो अग्न आहुतेति द्वे⟧, "the two, 'samidhyamāno adhvare' and 'samiddho agna āhuta'" (⟦आ. १-२⟧).

At the establishment of the fires, in the third iṣṭi, among the sāmidhenī verses, between the verse that contains "being kindled" and the verse that contains "kindled", the two beginning ⟦पृथुपाजा⟧ are the dhāyyā verses. And this is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦प्रागुपोत्तमायाः पृथुपाजा अमर्त्य इति द्वे⟧, "before the last but one, the two beginning 'pṛthupājā amartyaḥ'" (⟦आ, २-१⟧). In the same way elsewhere too, wherever there are seventeen sāmidhenī verses, there these two are the dhāyyā verses: so it is to be understood. At the Viṣuvat there are twenty-one sāmidhenī verses. There the six Ṛks beginning ⟦पृथुपाजा अमर्त्य⟧ are the dhāyyā verses: ⟦पृथुपाजा अमर्त्य इति षट् धाय्काः सामिधेनीनां⟧, "the six beginning 'pṛthupājā amartyaḥ' are the dhāyyā verses of the sāmidhenīs" (⟦अ. ८-६⟧), so it is laid down in the sūtra. At the Mahāvrata too the sāmidhenī verses are twenty-five in number. There too these six Ṛks are to be understood.

At the carrying forward of Agni and Soma the triplet ⟦होता देवो अमर्त्य⟧ is used. And this is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦होता देवो अमर्त्यः पुरस्तादुप त्वाग्ने दिवेदिवे⟧ (⟦आ. ४-१०⟧). The three beginning ⟦ईळेन्नो नमस्यस्तियर⟧ are sāmidhenī verses. And this is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦ईळेन्नो नमस्यस्तिरोऽग्निं दूतं वृणीमहे⟧ (⟦आ. १-२⟧).

## Ṛk 1
O Seasons! ⟦वाजाः⟧: they go, they move, and so they are vājas, the months. ⟦अभिद्यवः⟧: those in which there are ⟦द्यवो⟧, days, on both sides are abhidyus, and these are the half-months. ⟦हविष्मंतो⟧: the gods, who have a share in the oblation. ⟦घृताच्या⟧: she who goes to ghee is ghṛtācī, the cow. And so the Taittirīyaka says: ⟦देवा हविष्मंतो गौर्घृताची⟧, "the gods are the ones who have the oblation, the cow is ghṛtācī" (⟦तै. सं. २-५-७-४⟧). Or else: ⟦हविष्मंतो⟧ are the cattle, the givers of the oblation such as milk; ⟦धृताच्या⟧: that which goes to ghee, reaches it, is ghṛtācī, the ladle. And so the Vājasaneyaka says: ⟦पशवो हविष्मंतः स्रुग्नताचीति⟧, "the cattle are the ones who have the oblation, the ladle is ghṛtācī". Accompanied by that ghṛtācī, the ones who have the oblation, namely the gods and the cattle, and the months and the half-months: all these are at hand for the worship of you (⟦वो⟧, "of you"). When these, the vājas and the rest, are at hand, the sacrificer, ⟦सुम्नयुः⟧, wishing happiness for himself, goes to the gods: through the sacrifice he reaches the gods. On the meaning stated there is the Taittirīya-brāhmaṇa: ⟦प्र वो वाजा इत्यन्वाह मासा वै वाजा अर्धमासा अभिद्यवो देवा हविष्मंतो गौर्घताची यज्ञो देवाङ्गगाति यजमानः सुम्नयुः⟧, "He recites 'pra vo vājāḥ': the months indeed are the vājas, the half-months are the abhidyus, the gods are the ones who have the oblation, the cow is ghṛtācī, the sacrifice goes to the gods, the sacrificer is the one wishing happiness" (⟦तै.सं. २-५-७-४⟧).

⟦अभिद्यवः⟧: in a bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦धृताच्या⟧: the root is ⟦धृ⟧, "to flow, to shine". From this root, by the rule ⟦अस्मादंजिघृषिभ्यः क्तः⟧ (⟦उ. ३-८९⟧), comes the suffix kta in the sense of the object. In the sense "it goes to ghee, the clarified butter", there is the suffix kvin by the rule cited in ⟦धृतमाज्यमंचतीत्यृत्विग्दधृगित्यादिना⟧. By the rule cited in ⟦अनिदितामित्युपधालोपः⟧ the penultimate letter is elided. By ⟦अंचतेश्चोपसंख्यानमिति⟧, "and an additional statement for the root añc", there is the feminine suffix ṅīp. By the rule ⟦चाविति⟧ the final of the first member is udātta. ⟦जिगाति⟧: the root gā has the sense of going. As a Vedic usage it belongs to the class of roots beginning with hu. The verb loses its accent. ⟦सुम्नयुः⟧: in the sense "wishing sumna, happiness, for himself" there is the suffix kyac by ⟦सुप आत्मनः क्यच्⟧. By the rule cited in ⟦क्याच्छंदसीत्युः⟧ there is the suffix u. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 2
⟦विपश्चितं⟧, wise; ⟦यज्ञस्य साधनं⟧, the one who carries the sacrifice through; ⟦श्रुष्टीवानं⟧, possessed of happiness, or else possessed of speed; ⟦धितानानं⟧: ⟦वानं⟧ is wealth that is to be won, ⟦धितं⟧ is "laid up"; he who has this, him whose wealth is laid up. This Agni, who is such, with ⟦गिरा⟧, with speech in the form of praise, ⟦ईळे⟧: I, Viśvāmitra, praise.

⟦ईळे⟧: the root is īḍ, "to praise". ⟦श्रुष्टीवानं⟧: from the root van, ṣaṇ, "to partake", comes the suffix vic. "He partakes of śruṣṭi", and so the word is śruṣṭīvā. The accent is that of a final member which is a kṛt formation. ⟦धितानानं⟧: for the root dhā before the niṣṭhā suffix the substitute dhi is put, which is Vedic. The root is van, "to partake". The suffix ghañ comes in the sense of the object. By the rule cited in ⟦निष्ठॆति⟧, on the niṣṭhā form in a bahuvrīhi, the word dhita is placed first. The first member keeps its original accent.

## Ṛk 3
O Agni, ⟦वाजिनः⟧, we who have made the oblations ready, may we be able to bring about ⟦यमं⟧, the restraining, that is, the staying here until the completion of the sacrifice, of you, ⟦देवस्य⟧, the shining one. ⟦शकेम⟧ means "may we be able". Or it is to be construed as "of the god who is ⟦वाजिनो⟧, swift". From that cause may we cross over ⟦द्वेषांसि⟧, our sins: may we be such as have come out of sins.

⟦शकेम⟧: for the root śak, "to be able", in the benedictive liṅ, the suffix aṅ comes by the rule "aṅ before liṅ in the benedictive". By the rule cited in ⟦किदाशिषीति⟧ there is the augment yāsuṭ. Then follow the elision of s, the substitution of iy, the elision of y, guṇa, and the elision of s. Because a vocative is treated as not present, the verb stands at the beginning of the pāda, and so it does not lose its accent. The accent is the one that arises when an udātta is removed. ⟦यमं⟧: from the root yam, "to cease", comes the suffix ghañ in the sense of the action. Because the form is given ready made in the wording "yama uparame", there is no vṛddhi. The accent is that of a suffix marked with ñ. ⟦तरेम⟧: the root is tṝ, "to float, to cross". It is the form in liṅ in the sense of a prayer. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 4
The Agni who, ⟦समिध्यमानो⟧, being made to blaze up thoroughly with oblations of ghee and clarified butter in ⟦अध्वरे⟧, the sacrifice, and for that very reason ⟦शोचिष्केशो⟧, having flames for hair, ⟦पावकः⟧, the purifier, is ⟦ईड्यः⟧, to be extolled by those who praise: him, this one, we ⟦ईमहे⟧, we beg, for the fruit that we desire.

⟦ईड्यः⟧: from the root īḍ, "to praise", comes the suffix ṇyat. Although by the rule cited in ⟦तित्स्वरितमिति⟧ the svarita would result for it, by the rule cited in ⟦ईडवंदेत्यादिनाद्युदात्तत्वं⟧ the first syllable is udātta. ⟦ईमहे⟧: the root is ī, "in desire and other senses". Here this root has the sense of begging. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 5
⟦पृथुपाजाः⟧, whose form is abundant splendour; ⟦अमर्त्यः⟧, free from the nature of dying; ⟦घृतनिर्णिक्⟧: he through whom the ghee is purified by being melted is ghṛtanirṇik. ⟦स्वाहुतः⟧, well, rightly worshipped with offerings by the hotṛ and the others. Agni, being such, is ⟦हव्यवाट्⟧, the carrier of the oblations, of ⟦यज्ञस्य⟧, the sacrifice, the Jyotiṣṭoma and the rest.

⟦पृथुपाजाः⟧: the accent is that of a final member which is a kṛt formation. ⟦स्वाहुतः⟧: from the root hu comes the suffix kta in the sense of the object. By the rule ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the preverb keeps its original accent. ⟦हव्यवाट्⟧: by the rule cited in ⟦वहश्चेति⟧ there is the suffix ṇvi.

## Ṛk 6
⟦सबाधः⟧: by uttering the mantras that slay the demons they are accompanied by the repelling that is directed at them, and so they are sabādhaḥ. ⟦यज्ञवंतो⟧, furnished with the oblations that are to be offered; ⟦यतस्रुचः⟧: they by whom the ladles, the juhū and the others, are held, held fast, are yatasrucaḥ, the priests. ⟦इत्था⟧, thus, with ⟦धिया⟧, praise performed in this manner, they ⟦चक्रुः⟧, they make, ⟦तं⟧, that Agni who is such, ⟦आ⟧, turned towards them, ⟦ऊतये⟧, for protection from the harm done by the Rākṣasas.

⟦सबाधः⟧: from the root bādh, "to stir up", comes the suffix kvip. The accent is that of a final member which is a kṛt formation. ⟦इत्था⟧: from the word idam, in the sense of manner, comes the suffix thamu, by the rule "thamu after idam". For its case-ending the substitute ḍā is put by the rule cited in ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧. By the accent that arises when an udātta is removed, the word has the udātta on the last syllable. ⟦चक्रुः⟧: for the root ḍukṛñ, "to do", in liṭ before the ending us, when the substitution of the semivowel has been made, the reduplication takes place because that substitute is treated like the original by the rule "when reduplication is to be made before a vowel". The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 7
⟦होता⟧, the one who accomplishes the offering; ⟦ऽमर्त्यो⟧, free from the nature of dying; ⟦देवो⟧, shining; ⟦प्रचोदयन्⟧, strongly urging on, ⟦विदथानि⟧, the rites that are to be known: that Agni, being endowed with ⟦मायया⟧, with thorough knowledge about the rites, ⟦एति⟧, comes to us, ⟦पुरस्तात्⟧, at the very time when the rite begins.

⟦मायया⟧: from the root mā, "to measure, and to sound", comes the suffix ya in the sense of the agent or of the object by the rule cited in ⟦चेत्यस्माच्छाससिसूभ्यो य इति⟧. "He measures, he knows, the rite", or "the rite is measured by it": so māyā is knowledge about the rites. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦विदथानि⟧: from the root vid, "to know", comes the suffix atha in the sense of the object by the rule cited in ⟦इत्यस्माद्रुदिविदिभ्यां किदिति⟧**NOTE**{the reference text gives a reference (Uṇādi-sūtra 3.395) after this rule; the supplied text has none}. Because the suffix is marked with k there is no guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 8
⟦वाजी⟧, the strong Agni, ⟦धीयते⟧, is placed, in ⟦वाजेषु⟧, in battles: he is set down by the gods for the killing of enemies. Moreover in ⟦अध्वरेषु⟧, in the Agnihotra and the other rites, he ⟦प्रणीयते⟧: by the adhvaryu and the others he is put, with distinction, in the places of the Āhavanīya and the other fires. For that very reason, being ⟦विप्रो⟧, wise, Agni is ⟦साधनः⟧, the accomplisher, of ⟦यज्ञस्य⟧, the sacrifice, the Agnihotra and the rest.

⟦धीयते⟧: from the root dhā comes yak in the sense of the object. By the rule cited in ⟦घुमास्थागापेत्यादिना⟧ the long ī is substituted. The verb loses its accent. ⟦नीयते⟧: it is the form of the root ṇīñ, "to lead", before yak in the sense of the object. By the rule cited in ⟦णोपदेशस्यॆति⟧, "after a preverb, even where there is no compound, for a root taught with ṇ", the cerebral ṇ is substituted. ⟦साधनः⟧: the root is sādh, "to be accomplished". Because it belongs to the group beginning with nand, the suffix lyu comes in the sense of the agent. The accent is that of a suffix marked with l.

## Ṛk 9
The Agni who ⟦चक्रे⟧, was made, in the form of the Āhavanīya, by ⟦धिया⟧, the rite which consists of the establishment of the fires and the Pavamāna iṣṭis, and who for that very reason is ⟦वरेण्यः⟧, to be chosen by all sacrificers as a part of the rite; and the Agni who ⟦आ⟧ ⟦दधे⟧, bore everywhere, within ⟦भूतानां⟧, the kinds of beings, stationary and moving, ⟦गर्भं⟧, his very own self, in the form of an embryo; ⟦पितरं⟧, the protector of the whole world: this Agni ⟦तना⟧, the daughter, of ⟦दक्षस्य⟧, of Dakṣa Prajāpati, the earth in the form of the vedi, bears, so that rites such as the Darśapūrṇamāsa and the Agnihotra may be accomplished. That the earth is the daughter of Dakṣa is shown by the mantra text ⟦अदितिर्ह्यजनिष्ट दक्ष या दुहिता तव⟧, "For Aditi was born, O Dakṣa, she who is your daughter" (⟦ऋग्वे १०-७२-५⟧).

⟦चक्रे⟧: it is the form of the root kṛ in liṭ in the sense of the object. ⟦गर्भं⟧: from the root gṝ, "to swallow", comes the suffix bhan by the rule "bhan after ṛ and gṝ". "It swallows", or "it is swallowed", and so the word is garbha. Because the suffix is marked with n, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦दधे⟧: it is the form of the root dhā in liṭ. ⟦पितरं⟧: the root is pā, "to protect". By the rule cited in ⟦नप्तनेष्ट⟧ the word is given ready made as ending in tṛc, and so it has the vowel i. "He protects, he guards", and so pitā is the protector. The accent is that of a suffix marked with c. ⟦तना⟧: the elision of ya is Vedic.

## Ṛk 10
The meaning stated is set out by this Ṛk. ⟦सहस्कृत⟧, you who are produced by the strength that is called churning, O Agni! ⟦सुदीतिं⟧, endowed with fair radiance; ⟦उशिजं⟧, desiring the oblation, the puroḍāśa and the rest; or else, desired by those who seek the rite; for that very reason ⟦वरेण्यं⟧, to be chosen by them: ⟦त्वा⟧, you who are such, ⟦इळा⟧, the daughter of ⟦दक्षस्य⟧, Dakṣa, the earth marked by the vedi and the like, ⟦नि⟧ ⟦दधे⟧: she bore you firmly so that rites such as the Agnihotra may prosper.

⟦इळा⟧: the root is īḍ, "to praise". Because the rule ⟦पुंसि संज्ञायामित्यत्र⟧, "in the masculine, in a name", speaks of what is mostly so, the suffix gha comes in the feminine sense too. The shortening is Vedic. Because a rule that is stated with a technical name is not invariable, there is no guṇa. "In her the gods are praised, lauded, by the sacrificers", and so iḷā is the earth. By the accent that arises when an udātta is removed, the last syllable is udātta. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦उशिजं⟧: from the root vaś, "to desire", comes the suffix iji by the rule cited in ⟦कांतावित्यस्माद्वशः किच्चेतीजिप्रत्ययः⟧, "after vaś, and it is marked with k". Because it is marked with k there is saṃprasāraṇa. "He wishes, he desires, the oblation", or "he is wished for, desired, by those who seek the rite", and so uśik is Agni. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 11
⟦वनुषः⟧, serving so that the rite may be accomplished, ⟦विप्राः⟧, the wise, the adhvaryu and the others: ⟦यंतुरं⟧, the controller of all, or else the one who goes quickly; ⟦अप्तुरं⟧, the impeller of the waters: this Agni they ⟦समिंधते⟧, they light up well, with ⟦वाजैः⟧, with foods in the form of oblations, ⟦योगे⟧, for the performance, of ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, the Jyotiṣṭoma and the other sacrifices, which is true.

⟦यंतुरं⟧: there is an interchange of letters. The meaning is "the controller". ⟦अपुरं⟧: the root is tur, "to hasten". "He speeds, he hastens, the waters", and so the suffix kvip comes. The final member, a kṛt formation, keeps its original accent. ⟦योगे⟧: the root is yujir, "to join". The suffix ghañ comes in the sense of the action. By the rule ⟦चजोः कु घिण्यतोरिति⟧, "a guttural for c and j before a suffix marked with gh and before ṇyat", the guttural is substituted. The accent is that of a suffix marked with ñ. ⟦वनुषः⟧: the root is van, ṣaṇ, "to partake". In the section governed by the rule cited in ⟦जनेरुसिरित्यधिकारे⟧, "usi after jan", the suffix usi comes after this root too, because "variously" is stated there. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦इंथते⟧: the root is ñi-indhī, "to shine". The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 12
⟦ऊर्जो नपातं⟧: the son of food, because he is kindled with foods in the form of oblations. Or else, the fourth from food: thus, from food the oblation, from the oblation the sun, from the sun Agni. ⟦उप⟧ ⟦द्यवि⟧, near the mid-region, ⟦दीदिवांसं⟧, shining exceedingly; ⟦कविक्रतुं⟧: he whose makers, by churning, are the ⟦कवयो⟧, the wise, the adhvaryu and the others; that is, the one who is produced by the adhvaryu and the others. This Agni, in ⟦अध्वरे⟧, in this sacrifice, ⟦ईळे⟧: I praise with words in the form of praise.

⟦ऊर्जः⟧: from the root ūrj, "in the sense of strength", comes the suffix kvip, which denotes one who has that habit, by the rule cited in ⟦इत्यस्माद्भाजभासेत्यादिना⟧. "It strengthens the body", and so ūrk is food. By the rule ⟦सावेकाच⟧ the case-ending is udātta. ⟦द्यवि⟧: from the root dyut, "to shine", comes the Uṇādi suffix ḍun. "It shines through its connexion with the rays", and so dyu is the mid-region. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦ईळे⟧: it is the form in laṭ.

## Ṛk 13
⟦ईळेन्यः⟧, to be praised by those who praise; for that very reason ⟦नमस्यः⟧, to be bowed to by all; ⟦तिरः⟧ ⟦तमांसि⟧, setting aside the darknesses by his own lights; ⟦दर्शतः⟧, worth seeing by all because he is lovely. Agni, who is such, ⟦वृषा⟧, the showerer of the sacrificer's desires, ⟦समिध्यते⟧: he is made to blaze up by the casting in of oblations. On the meaning stated there is the Vājasaneyaka: ⟦ईडेन्नो हैष नमस्यो हैष तिरस्तमांसि ददृशे समिद्ध⟧, "He indeed is to be praised, he indeed is to be bowed to; kindled, he has been seen across the darknesses."

⟦ईळेन्यः⟧: the root is īḍ, "to praise". By the rule cited in ⟦तवैकेन्नेन्यत्वन इति⟧, which teaches the suffixes tavai, ken, kenya and tvan in the sense of the kṛtya suffixes, there is the suffix kenya. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦नमस्यः⟧: from the verb namasyati comes the suffix yat by the rule cited in ⟦नमस्यतेरचो यदिति⟧, "yat after a vowel". Then follow the elision of a and the elision of ya. By the rule cited in ⟦तित्स्वरितमिति⟧ the word has the svarita. ⟦दर्शतः⟧: the root is dṛśir, "to look". From it comes the suffix atac in the sense of the object by the rule cited in ⟦अस्माद्भ्रमृदृशीत्यादिना⟧. The accent is that of a suffix marked with c. ⟦वृषा⟧: from the root vṛṣu, "to sprinkle", comes the suffix kanin by the rule cited in ⟦इत्यस्मात्कनिन्युवृषीत्यादिना⟧. Because the suffix is marked with k there is no guṇa. Because it is marked with n, the first syllable is udātta.

## Ṛk 14
⟦वृषो⟧: the showerer indeed, the one who showers desires**NOTE**{the supplied text glosses the word with eva, "the showerer indeed"; the reference text has iva there, "like a showerer"}. ⟦देववाहनः⟧: he makes the oblations be carried to the gods, makes them reach the gods, and so he is devavāhana. For this there is a comparison, in the words ⟦अश्वो नेति⟧: as a horse carries the king, makes him reach his own city. A horse is a carrier. In the same way the Agni who, being a carrier, ⟦समिध्यते⟧, is lit up well by the giving of oblations**NOTE**{the reference text has "by the giving of oblations of clarified butter"; the supplied text has only "oblations"}: that Agni, who is such, ⟦हविष्मंतः⟧, the sacrificers who have made the oblations ready, ⟦ईळते⟧, praise so that the rite may be accomplished.

⟦वृषा उ⟧: there is guṇa. With reference to the particle uñ the word has the name pragṛhya. ⟦देववाहनः⟧: from the root vah, "to convey", in its causative form, comes the suffix lyu in the sense of the agent. The accent is that of a suffix marked with l. In the compound the accent is that of a final member which is a kṛt formation.

## Ṛk 15
⟦वृषन्⟧, O showerer of desires, O Agni! We, ⟦वृषणो⟧, that is ⟦वृषाणो⟧, the pourers of oblations of ghee and clarified butter, kindle you, ⟦वृषणं⟧, the pourer of water by way of the oblation. And so the smṛti says: ⟦अग्नौ प्रास्ताहुतिः सम्यगादित्यमुपतिष्ठते⟧, ⟦आदित्याज्ञायते वृष्टिर्वृष्टेरन्नं ततः प्रजाः⟧, "The oblation duly cast into the fire reaches the sun; from the sun rain is born, from rain food, from that the creatures" (⟦मनु. ३-७६⟧). ⟦दीद्यतं⟧, shining, ⟦बृहत्⟧, and for that very reason great: this Agni**NOTE**{the supplied text has "this Agni" here; the reference text has "you, Agni", glossing the word tvā of the Ṛk} we ⟦सॆमिधीमहि⟧, we light up well.

⟦इधीमहि⟧: for the root ñi-indhī, "to shine", in liṅ in the sense of a prayer, the elision of the letter n is Vedic. The verb loses its accent. ⟦दीद्यतं⟧: the verb dīdeti has the sense of shining. It is its form with the suffix śatṛ. By the rule cited in ⟦अभ्यस्तानामादिरित्याद्युदात्तत्त्वं⟧, "the first syllable of reduplicated stems", the first syllable is udātta.
