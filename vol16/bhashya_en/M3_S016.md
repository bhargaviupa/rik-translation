## Introduction

The fourth sūkta, of six Ṛks, beginning ⟦अयमग्निरिति⟧, is addressed to Agni. On this the Anukramaṇī says: ⟦मग्निः षट् प्रगाथमिति⟧, "'ayam agniḥ': six; in Pragātha form". Because of the use of the word tu in ⟦उत्तीलस्त्विति⟧, "Utkīla, however", the same one is the seer of this sūkta also. Here the metre of the Ṛks of uneven number is Bṛhatī, because the third pāda has twelve syllables; but that of the Ṛks of even number is Satobṛhatī, because the first and the third pādas have twelve syllables. And it is said: ⟦अयुजौ जागतौ सतोबृहतीति⟧, "the two uneven pādas in Jagatī: Satobṛhatī"**NOTE**{the reference text gives a reference to the Anukramaṇī (8.4) for ayujau jāgatau satobṛhatī; the supplied text has none}.

## Ṛk 1

Utkīla praises. ⟦अयं⟧, this Agni, who is being pointed out with the finger as the one to whom sacrifice is to be offered, ⟦ईशे⟧, rules, is the lord, of ⟦सौभगस्य⟧, of good fortune, that is ⟦महः⟧, great, and ⟦सुवीर्यस्य⟧, endowed with excellent capacity: because, as the cause of the strength and health of all, he is the maker of good fortune. Likewise he ⟦ईशे⟧, rules, over ⟦रायः⟧, wealth, that is ⟦गोमतः⟧, joined with cows and other cattle, and ⟦स्वपत्यस्य⟧, having fine offspring: because he is the master of it as the one who brings about the fruit of the rites that are performed with sons, cattle and the like in view. Likewise Agni, being such, ⟦ईशे⟧, rules, also over ⟦हथानां⟧ of vṛtra — ⟦हननं हथः⟧, hatha is killing — over the destructions of the evils that are our enemies. Since for us, whose rites are made over to you, the wasting away of evil comes about through your favour, he is the master of that also.

⟦ईशे⟧: ⟦ईश ऐश्वर्ये⟧, the root īś in the sense of lordship. Because it belongs to the class beginning with ad, śap is elided. The t is elided by the rule on the elision of t in the ātmanepada endings. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda it does not lose its accent. ⟦सौभगस्य⟧: ⟦सुभगस्य भावः सौभगं⟧, the state of one who is fortunate is saubhaga. The suffix añ comes because the word belongs to the group beginning with udgātṛ. Because all rules are optional in the Veda, there is here no vṛddhi of the second member, which would result by the rule beginning ⟦हृद्भगे⟧. ⟦रायः⟧: the case-ending is udātta by the rule beginning ⟦ऊडिदमि⟧. ⟦ईशे⟧: it loses its accent. ⟦स्वपत्यस्य⟧: the second member has the udātta on its last syllable by ⟦नञ्सुभ्यामि⟧. ⟦हथानां⟧ with vṛtra: ⟦वृतु वर्तने⟧, the root vṛt in the sense of turning. After it the suffix rak comes by the rule beginning sphāyitañcivañci. Because the suffix is marked with k there is no guṇa. From the root ⟦हन⟧ in the senses of injuring and going, the suffix kthan comes by ⟦हनिकुषिनीरमिकाशिभ्य इति⟧, "after han, kuṣ, nī, ram and kāś". Because the suffix is marked with k the nasal is elided. The accent is that by which the second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent.

## Ṛk 2

O ⟦नरः⟧, leaders of the rites, O ⟦मरुतो⟧, O gods, do you ⟦सश्चत⟧, serve, in order to bring the oblation, ⟦इमं⟧, this Agni, who is being praised by us, the ⟦वृधं⟧, the increaser of cattle, sons and the like. To the question "of what kind is Agni?" he says: ⟦यस्मि⟧, the god in whom ⟦रायो⟧, riches, are**NOTE**{the supplied text has only santi here; the reference text has abhi abhitaḥ santi, "are on all sides"} ⟦शेवृधासो⟧, increasers of happiness by their use in giving, enjoying and the like. And ⟦ये⟧, the gods who, in ⟦पृतनासु⟧, in armies, that is, in the battles implied by these, ⟦अभि⟧ ⟦संति⟧, overcome, the ⟦दूढ्यो⟧, the enemies who are possessed of evil thought and do them harm; and who, ⟦विश्वाहा⟧ — the unbroken time in which all the days are, in that, at all times — ⟦आदभुः⟧, injure, the ⟦शत्रुं⟧, the one who hates them: "all you Maruts, being such, serve this Agni"; such is the connection with what precedes.

⟦सश्चत⟧: it is the form in the imperative from the root sasj in the sense of clinging. By interchange c stands for the j. It loses its accent. ⟦शेवृधासः⟧: there is an interchange of sounds. "It increases śam, happiness": the suffix asun comes by ⟦असुन्नित्यसुन्⟧. The absence of guṇa is Vedic. The first member keeps its original accent, from the Uṇādi sūtra ⟦गतिकारकयोरपि पूर्वपदप्रकृतिस्वरत्वं⟧, "and after a preverb or a word expressing a participant in the action also, the first member keeps its original accent". ⟦संति⟧: ⟦अस भुवि⟧, the root as in the sense of being. ⟦दूढ्यः⟧: the root dhyai in the sense of ⟦चिंतायां⟧, thinking. The suffix kvip comes by the rule "it is seen after others also". The saṃprasāraṇa is because of the mention of "is seen". The ū and the cerebral come by the statement that ū is to be stated for dur before dāśa, nāśa, dabha and dhya, and the change to a cerebral for the initial of the second member. By ⟦छंदस्युभयथेति⟧ the substitute yaṇ comes, setting aside the substitute iyaṅ before śas. By the accent by which the second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent, it has the udātta on the last syllable. When śas has become anudātta because it is a case-ending, śas has the svarita by ⟦उदात्तस्वरितयोर्यण इति⟧. ⟦आदभुः⟧: the root dambh in the sense of ⟦दंभे⟧, deceiving. It is the form from it in the perfect, before us. Because by ⟦श्रंथिग्रंथिदंभिस्वंजीनामुपसंख्यानमिति⟧, "there is an additional statement of śranth, granth, dambh and svañj", the suffix is treated as marked with k, the nasal is elided. By ⟦छंदसि वेति वक्तव्यमिति⟧, "in the section on reduplication it is to be stated: optionally in the Veda"**NOTE**{the reference text gives a reference (kā. 6.1.8) for this statement; the supplied text has none}, there is no reduplication. Because it is connected with a form of yad it does not lose its accent. The accent is that of the suffix by ⟦सति शिष्टस्वरो बलीयानिति⟧, "the accent that is taught while another is there is the stronger".

## Ṛk 3

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, ⟦त्वं⟧, you, being ⟦स⟧, of that kind, ⟦शिशीहि⟧ ⟦नो⟧, us, ⟦रायः⟧ — the genitive is in the sense of the instrumental — with wealth: make us sharp; make us abound in wealth. Moreover, O ⟦तुविद्युम्न⟧, you who are furnished with much wealth, and for that very reason ⟦मीढ्वो⟧, O sprinkler of desires, O Agni, do you make us connected with food that is ⟦वर्षिष्ठस्य⟧, greatest, abundant; ⟦प्रजावतो⟧, joined with offspring; ⟦अनमीवस्य⟧, a cause of health, and for that very reason ⟦शुष्मिणो⟧, a cause of strength; ⟦सुवीर्यस्य⟧, joined with excellent capacity. Or else all this is to be construed as qualifying the wealth. These genitives are in the sense of the instrumental. What is said is this: make us abound with wealth, with food that is furnished with offspring, health and bodily strength**NOTE**{the supplied text reads dhanenāpatyārogyaśarīrabalopetenānnenāsmān; the reference text has annena ca, "and with food"}.

⟦शिशीहि⟧: of the root ⟦शिञ् निशान⟧, śi in the sense of sharpening, in the imperative, there is ślu of the conjugational affix by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧. Since by ⟦वा छंदसीति⟧, "optionally in the Veda", it is by option treated as marked with ṅ, the substitute dhi for hi by the rule aṅitaś ca does not come. The long vowel is Vedic. It loses its accent. ⟦मीध्वः⟧: of the root ⟦मिह⟧ in the sense of sprinkling, before kvasu, there are the ḍh and the lengthening of the penultimate because the word is laid down ready-made by ⟦दाश्वान् साह्वान् मीढ्वांश्चेति⟧. In the vocative singular, when num has been added and the elision taught for what follows a consonant, ṅī and the rest, and the elision of the final of a conjunct have been made, ru comes by ⟦मतुवसो रुः संबुद्धौ⟧, "ru for matu and vasu in the vocative singular, in the Veda". Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda it has the udātta on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 4

⟦यो⟧, this Agni who is ⟦चक्रिः⟧, the maker of the whole world, he also ⟦अभि⟧, enters into, ⟦विश्वा भुवना⟧, all the worlds, because of the Vedic text ⟦तदेवानुप्राविशदिति⟧, "having created that, he entered into that very thing"**NOTE**{the reference text gives a reference (tai. u. 2.6) for this Vedic text; the supplied text has none}. ⟦चक्रिः⟧, the maker, that Agni, being ⟦सासहिः⟧, of a habit to bear the load of the cake and the other oblations, ⟦आ⟧, brings, ⟦दुवो⟧, the oblation given by us, among ⟦देवेषु⟧, among the gods who are worthy of sacrifice. And that Agni ⟦आ⟧ ⟦यतते⟧, goes face to face, among ⟦देवेषु⟧, among the praisers, whose habit is devana**NOTE**{devanaśīleṣu is left as "whose habit is devana": Sāyaṇa does not say here in which sense of the root div he takes it}. Or else: he is ⟦चक्रि⟧ in respect of all the worlds, and, with abhi, sāsahi, of a habit to overcome. The ā is in the sense of "and". And among the gods he is ⟦चक्रिः⟧, the doer, of ⟦दुवः⟧, of attendance. He who is such ā yatate, and so on, as before. ⟦उत⟧, and also, in the ⟦शंसे⟧ — praising is śaṃsa, the śastra — in that, of ⟦नृणां⟧, of the leaders of the sacrifice, the possessors of the śastra, that Agni comes. Likewise he comes into the ⟦सुवीर्ये⟧, into the battle that is furnished with excellent valour, of ⟦नृणां⟧, of men, in order to protect them. By this the all-pervadingness of Agni is set forth.

⟦चक्रिः⟧: the root kṛ in the sense of doing. The suffix kin comes by ⟦आदृगमहनजनः किकिनौ लिट् चेति⟧, "after roots in ā, in ṛ, and gam, han and jan, ki and kin come, and are like the perfect". Because it is treated like the perfect there is reduplication. Because the suffix is marked with k there is no guṇa. It has the accent of a suffix marked with n. ⟦सासहिः⟧: from the root ⟦षह⟧ in the sense of enduring, when it ends in yaṅ, the suffix ki comes by the statement that ki and kin come after sah, vah, cal and pat when they end in yaṅ (⟦पा ३-२-१७१-४⟧). It has the accent of the suffix. ⟦यतते⟧: ⟦यती प्रयत्ने⟧, the root yat in the sense of striving. Because it is connected with a form of yad it does not lose its accent. ⟦शंसे⟧: the suffix ghañ comes in the sense of the state. It has the accent of a suffix marked with ñ. ⟦नृणां⟧: by ⟦नृ चेति⟧ the long vowel is by option absent. The case-ending is udātta by ⟦नामन्यतरस्यामिति⟧. In ⟦विश्वे⟧ and the rest the genitive is barred by ⟦न लोकाव्ययेति⟧.

## Ṛk 5

O ⟦सहसस्पुत्र⟧, son of strength, O Agni, do you ⟦मा⟧ ⟦रीरधः⟧ ⟦नो⟧, us, to ⟦अमतये⟧, to poverty, which is an enemy: do not make us made over to it, subject to it. Likewise to avīratā, to sonlessness — by the word avīra**NOTE**{the supplied text reads avīraśabdena; the reference text reads vīraśabdena, "by the word vīra"} one who has it is indicated; the state of a vīra is ⟦वीरता⟧; what is not vīratā is ⟦अवीरता⟧; to that — do not make us subject to sonlessness. Likewise do not make us subject to agotā, to the absence of the wealth of cows and other cattle. Moreover do not make us subject ⟦निदे⟧, to blame; do not appoint us to it. Likewise ⟦द्वेषांसि⟧, the hatreds that are caused by offences in the rite, do you remove: put them away, ward them off.

Avīratā: "heroes belong to him", hence ⟦वीरः⟧; the suffix ac comes because the word belongs to the group beginning with arśas. The state of that is ⟦वीरता⟧: the suffix tal comes by ⟦तस्य भावस्त्वतलाविति⟧. What is not vīratā is ⟦अवीरता⟧. Though the accent of the negative would result, the first syllable of what follows the negative is udātta by the rule parādiś chandasi bahulam. ⟦रीरधः⟧: the roots ⟦राध साध⟧ in the sense of accomplishing. From this root, when it ends in ṇi, in the Vedic aorist, the substitute caṅ comes in place of cli by the rule that it comes after ṇi, śri, dru and sru. The ṇi is elided by ⟦णेरनिटीति⟧. The penultimate is shortened by the rule ṇau caṅy upadhāyāḥ. There is reduplication by the rule caṅi. It is treated as if san followed by the rule sanval laghuni caṅpare. The reduplication takes i by the rule sany ataḥ. That is lengthened by ⟦दीर्घो लघोरिति⟧. The augment aṭ is absent by the rule na māṅyoge. It loses its accent. Agotā: by the word go one who has cows is indicated. One who is not that is ago. ⟦अगोर्भावोऽगोता⟧, the state of one without cows is agotā. The suffix tal comes by ⟦तस्य भाव इति⟧. It has the accent of a suffix marked with l. ⟦सहसस्पुत्र⟧: because by the rule subāmantrite parāṅgavat svare it is treated as part of the following word, both of these, sahasas and putra, are without any udātta. ⟦निदे⟧: ⟦णिदि कुत्सायां⟧, the root nind in the sense of blaming. After it kvip comes in the sense of the state, because it belongs to the group beginning with sampad. The augment num is absent by ⟦अनित्यमागमशासनमिति⟧, "the teaching of an augment is not invariable". The case-ending is udātta by ⟦सावेकाच इति⟧.

## Ṛk 6

O ⟦सुभग⟧, you who are furnished with fine wealth, O Agni, in the ⟦अध्वरे⟧, in this rite, the Agniṣṭoma or another, that is being performed by us, you ⟦शग्नि⟧, you rule, over ⟦वाजस्य⟧, food, that is ⟦बृहतो⟧, great, abundant, and ⟦प्रजावतो⟧, furnished with offspring. Give us such food in this sacrifice. Moreover, O ⟦तुविद्युम्न⟧, O Agni joined with much wealth, ⟦सं⟧ ⟦सृज⟧, unite, us with ⟦राया⟧, with wealth of this kind: ⟦मयोभुना⟧ — mayas is a name of happiness, because of the reading ⟦मय इति तन्नामसु पाठात्⟧, "śevṛdham, mayaḥ", among the names of that**NOTE**{the reference text gives a Nighaṇṭu reference (3.6.7) for śevṛdhaṃ mayaḥ; the supplied text has none} — which brings happiness into being, as the cause of its use in giving and the like; and for that very reason ⟦यशस्वता⟧, possessed of fame; ⟦भूयसा⟧, very abundant.

⟦शग्नि⟧: of the root śak in the sense of being able, in the imperative, the conjugational affix is elided by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति विकरणस्य लुक्⟧. The substitute dhi comes by the rule that dhi takes the place of hi after hu and after roots ending in a jhal consonant. Because hi is not marked with p it is udātta. ⟦राया⟧: the case-ending is udātta by ⟦ऊडिदमिति⟧. ⟦भूयसा⟧: from the word bahu the suffix īyasun comes by ⟦द्विवचनविभज्योपपद⟧. While by ⟦बहोर्लोपो भू च बहोरि⟧, "elision after bahu, and bhū in place of bahu", the elision of the whole of īyasun would result, by ⟦आदेः परस्य⟧ (⟦पा. १-१-५४⟧) there is the elision of the initial of the suffix, and bhū is the substitute of bahu. It has the accent of a suffix marked with n. ⟦सृज⟧: ⟦सृज विसर्गे⟧, the root sṛj in the sense of letting go. It belongs to the class beginning with tud. It is the form in the imperative. It loses its accent. ⟦मयोभुना⟧: from the root bhū in the sense of being comes the Uṇādi suffix ḍu. ⟦तुविद्युम्न⟧: because it stands at the beginning of a pāda it does not lose its accent.
