## Introduction

The sūkta beginning ⟦अग्ने सहस्व⟧ is the twelfth, of five Ṛks. On it the Anukramaṇikā says, of the sūkta beginning "agne sahasva": ⟦गायत्रमाद्यानुष्टुबिति⟧, that is, it is in the gāyatrī metre and its first Ṛk is an anuṣṭubh. The Ṛṣi is Viśvāmitra, the son of Gāthin. Agni is the deity. The first Ṛk is an anuṣṭubh; the rest are gāyatrīs. The use of the sūkta is to be gathered from its indications. In the prātaranuvāka and in the āśvina-śastra the sūkta is used without its first Ṛk. It is laid down in the section beginning ⟦अथैतस्या⟧: ⟦अग्नि इळेति चतस्रः⟧ (⟦आ. ४-१३⟧), "the four Ṛks beginning 'agna iḷā'". In the Abhyudayeṣṭi the Ṛk beginning "agne dā dāśuṣe" is the invitatory verse for Agni the Giver. It is laid down in the section beginning "athāgneyyaḥ": ⟦अग्ने दा दाशुषे रयिं स यंता विप्र एषां⟧ (⟦अ. ३-१३⟧), "'O Agni, give wealth to the giver' and 'he, the wise one, the controller of these'".

## Ṛk 1

Viśvāmitra praises. O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦पृतनाः⟧: the attacking armies, ⟦सहस्व⟧: overpower. ⟦अभिमातीः⟧: the arrogant enemies who cause obstacles to the rites, ⟦अप⟧ ⟦अस्य⟧: drive away. Moreover, ⟦दुष्टरो⟧: one who cannot be overcome by anyone; and for that very reason, ⟦अरातीः⟧: the enemies, ⟦तरन्⟧: putting them down by your own brilliance, you, ⟦यज्ञवाहसे⟧: for the sacrificer who carries the sacrifice through, ⟦वर्चः⟧ ⟦धाः⟧: bestow food, make it.

⟦सहस्व⟧: it is the form in the imperative, loṭ, of the root ⟦षह⟧, in the sense of enduring. Because the vocative is treated as not present, the verb stands at the beginning of the pāda and so does not lose its accent. ⟦अभिमातीः⟧: the root is ⟦माज्⟧, in the sense of measuring. The measuring is māti: the suffix ktin in the sense of the action. Because this root has a class-sign that is dropped, the substitutions of ī and of i do not take place. In the sense "those who have a measure on every side", in the bahuvrīhi, the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦अस्य⟧: the root is ⟦असु⟧, in the sense of throwing, of the class beginning with div. It is the form in the imperative, loṭ. ⟦दुष्टरः⟧: the root is ⟦त्व⟧, in the senses of floating and crossing. After it the suffix khal comes in the sense of difficulty. Because the word belongs to the group beginning with suṣāman, the s that stands for the visarjanīya becomes ṣ. The accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦तरन्⟧: it is the form of the root tṝ before the suffix śatṛ. The suffix śatṛ having been made anudātta by the accent rule for the sārvadhātuka substitutes of la after a root taught with a, the accent is that of the root. ⟦धाः⟧: it is the form of the root dhā in the aorist, luṅ, in the Veda. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 2

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦वीतिहोत्रः⟧: he whose hotra, the Agnihotra and the like, is vīti, an object of delight, is vītihotra; the meaning is, one who delights in sacrifices. ⟦अमर्त्यः⟧: free from the nature of dying; you, ⟦इळा⟧: on the iḷā, the uttaravedi that is worthy of praise, ⟦समिध्यसे⟧: are made to blaze up fully by the casting in of oblations of clarified butter. You, being such, ⟦नः⟧: our ⟦अध्वरं⟧: this sacrifice, ⟦सु⟧: well, ⟦जुषस्व⟧: attend, by accepting the oblations.

⟦वीतिहोत्रः⟧: the root is ⟦वी⟧, in the senses of desire and the rest. After it, in the sense of the object, the suffix ktin comes with the udātta by the rule that begins "in a mantra, after vṛṣ, iṣ, pac". In the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦जुषस्व⟧: it is the form of the root juṣ in the imperative, loṭ. The accent is that of the class-sign śa. ⟦सु⟧: by the rule ⟦निपातस्य चेति⟧ it is lengthened in the Saṃhitā text.

## Ṛk 3

⟦द्युम्नेन⟧: together with your own brilliance; ⟦जागृवे⟧: he wakes, hence jāgṛvi; this is its vocative: O you who, for the protection of the world, are at all times wakeful together with your own brilliance; ⟦आहुत⟧: called upon by us in order to summon the deities; ⟦सहसः सूनो⟧: O son of strength, O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni; ⟦मम⟧: in this sacrifice which belongs to me, ⟦इदं⟧ ⟦बर्हिः⟧: this sacred grass that is strewn on the vedi, on it ⟦आ⟧ ⟦सदः⟧: sit down.

⟦जागृवे⟧: the root is ⟦जागृ⟧, in the sense of the ending of sleep. By the rule ⟦ज्ञाश्चास्त्यजागृभ्यः⟧, "kvin", the suffix is kvin. Because it is marked with k, there is no guṇa. He wakes, hence jāgṛvi. ⟦जागृविर्जागरणात्⟧ (⟦नि. ९-८⟧), "jāgṛvi is from waking", says Yāska. There is guṇa by the rule ⟦संबुद्धौ चेति⟧. The ending su is elided by the rule ⟦एज् ह्रस्वात्⟧ (⟦पा. ६-१-६९⟧). The word loses its accent. ⟦सहसः सूनो⟧: though the loss of accent would follow because a case-form before a vocative is treated as a part of the following word, the accent is not lost because the word stands at the beginning of a pāda. ⟦सदः⟧: of the root ⟦षद्लृ⟧, in the senses of breaking up, going and sinking down, in the Vedic aorist, luṅ, the suffix aṅ comes because the root is marked with ḷ. The verb loses its accent. ⟦मम⟧: of the word asmad, in the genitive singular, the substitute is mama. By the rule ⟦युष्मदस्मदोर्जसी⟧ the first syllable is udātta.

## Ṛk 4

O Agni, ⟦देवेभिः⟧: shining, ⟦विश्वेभिः⟧: all, ⟦अग्निभिः⟧: the fires that stand as those to whom sacrifice is to be offered; accompanied by them, you, ⟦चायवः⟧: those ⟦ये⟧, who, are worshippers with undivided mind, with words in the form of praises on the subject stated,**NOTE**{The supplied text has ukta-viṣaya-, "on the subject stated"; the reference text has tvad-viṣaya-, "of which you are the subject".} in their ⟦यज्ञेषु⟧: in the Jyotiṣṭoma and the like, ⟦गिरः⟧: the utterances that consist of stotras, śastras and the rest, ⟦महय⟧: honour.

**NOTE**{The reference text has the word mahaya standing here as the heading of the grammatical remark that follows; the supplied text does not have it.}The root is ⟦मह⟧, in the sense of honouring, of the class beginning with cur. Because the stem ends in a, there is no vṛddhi of the last vowel but one. The verb loses its accent. ⟦चायवः⟧: the root is ⟦चाय्⟧, in the senses of honouring and perceiving. By the rule ⟦मृगय्दादयश्चति⟧ the suffix is ku. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 5

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦दाशुषे⟧: to the sacrificer who has given the oblation to you, ⟦वीरवंतं⟧: accompanied by sons, ⟦परीणसं⟧: abundant, ⟦रयिं⟧: wealth, ⟦दाः⟧: give. ⟦सूनुमतः⟧: accompanied by sons, grandsons and the rest, ⟦नः⟧: us, ⟦शिशीहि⟧: make sharp by the gift of wealth.

⟦दाः⟧: it is the form, in the Vedic aorist, luṅ, of the root ⟦डुदाञ्⟧, in the sense of giving. Because the vocative is treated as not present, the verb stands at the beginning of the pāda and so does not lose its accent. ⟦दाशुषे⟧: of the root ⟦दाश्य⟧, in the sense of giving, before the suffix kvasu, because the form is laid down ready-made in the rule ⟦दाश्वान्साह्वान्मीढ्वांश्चति⟧, there is no reduplication and no augment iṭ. When the stem has the name bha, there is saṃprasāraṇa. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦परीणसं⟧: the suffix kvip comes after the root nas, in the sense of pervading, preceded by pari. The lengthening is by the rule "it is seen in others also". ⟦शिशीहि⟧: the root is ⟦शिञ्⟧, in the sense of sharpening. By interchange there is ślu. The lengthening is Vedic. Because the ending hi is not marked with p, the last syllable is udātta.
