## Introduction

The hymn beginning ⟦भवा नो अग्न⟧ is the sixth hymn and has five Ṛks. The Anukramaṇikā says ⟦भवा न⟧. Because the word tu is used in ⟦कतो⟧ ⟦वैश्वामित्रस्त्विति⟧, "Kata, the son of Viśvāmitra, however", the seer of this hymn also is Kata, the son of Viśvāmitra. Agni is the deity. The ritual use of the hymn in the prātaranuvāka and in the āśvina-śastra has been stated. In the Pravargya, at the praising, the pair of Ṛks beginning ⟦भवा नो अग्न⟧ is employed. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦भवा नो अग्ने सुमना उपेताविति दृचाः⟧ (⟦आ. ४-६⟧), "'Bhavā no agne sumanā upetau': the pairs of Ṛks."

## Ṛk 1

O Agni, at the approach, at your coming towards the rite called Pravargya which is being performed by us, having become ⟦सुमनाः⟧, of a mind favourable to the success of that rite, be ⟦साधुः⟧ for us (⟦नः⟧): be the accomplisher of the rite that belongs to us. For this there are two examples: ⟦सखेव सख्ये पितरेवेति⟧. As a ⟦सखा⟧, a well-wisher, is good to a ⟦सख्ये⟧, to a friend, by teaching what is beneficial, and as ⟦पितरा⟧, mother and father, are accomplishers for their son by teaching the right path, so be you. Moreover the ⟦क्षितयः⟧, the men, of ⟦जनानां⟧, of men, are indeed ⟦पुरुऽद्रुहः⟧, possessed of malice of many kinds. The word ⟦हि⟧ expresses the cause. Since in the world people live unable to bear one another because of mutual jealousy and the like, therefore do you burn against (⟦प्रति⟧ ⟦दहतात्⟧) the ⟦अरातीः⟧, the enemies, who are ⟦प्रतीचीः⟧, coming towards us in hostility: being hostile to them, reduce them to ashes.

⟦भवा नः⟧: the vowel is long in the Saṃhitā by the rule on the a of a verbal form of two syllables. ⟦उपइत्⟧: the root is i in the sense of going. "A going": so the suffix ktin comes in the sense of the action. It is a compound with pra and the rest. By the rule ⟦तादौ च निति⟧ and what follows, the preposition keeps its original accent. ⟦सख्ये⟧: by the rule ⟦समाने ख्यश्चोदात्त⟧ the suffix iṇ comes. Because the suffix is marked with ḍ, the last vowel with what follows it is elided. Together with that suffix the y is elided. The substitution of sa comes by the rule beginning ⟦समानस्य छंदसी⟧. Because "udātta" is stated in the rule, the accompanying word is udātta. ⟦साधुः⟧: the root is ⟦राध साध संसिद्धौ⟧, rādh and sādh in the sense of accomplishing. The suffix uṇ comes by the rule beginning ⟦कृवापाज⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦प्रतीचीः⟧: the root is ⟦अंचु गतिपूजनयोः⟧, añc in the senses of going and honouring. The suffix kvin comes by the rule beginning ⟦ऋत्विग्धधृगि⟧. In the feminine, when ṅīp has been obtained by the rule on what has uk as its marker, and has again been prohibited by the statement ⟦र्धातोरुगितः प्रतिषेध⟧**NOTE**{after this vārttika the reference text gives a reference to Pāṇini (4, 1. 6. 1), which the supplied text does not have}, "there is a prohibition for a root that has uk as its marker", ṅīp comes by the counter-exception that the root añc is to be additionally included. Before that suffix, when the stem has the designation bha, the a is elided by the rule on ac. The vowel is lengthened by the rule ⟦चावि⟧. ⟦दहतात्⟧: in the second person singular of the loṭ of the root ⟦दह भस्मीकरण⟧, dah in the sense of reducing to ashes, the substitute tātaṅ takes the place of hi by the rule on tu and hi in a benediction. The verb loses its accent. ⟦अरातीः प्रतीचीः⟧: in both there is an interchange of gender.

## Ṛk 2

O Agni, the ⟦अंतरान्⟧, the overpowering, ⟦अमित्रान्⟧, enemies, ⟦सु⟧, in such a way that it is well done, ⟦तपो⟧: do indeed burn them; oppress them. Moreover, of ⟦अररुषः⟧, of him who does not give the oblation to you, and who for that very reason is ⟦परस्य⟧, one who has become an enemy, his own ⟦शंसं⟧, desire, ⟦तप⟧: destroy it. ⟦वसो⟧, you who give a dwelling to all, O Agni, do you, ⟦चिकितानः⟧, knowing well the rite, ⟦तपो⟧, scorch, the ⟦अचित्तान्⟧, the men whose minds are not attached to the good rite. Since this is so, therefore let ⟦ते⟧, your, rays, being ⟦अजराः⟧, free from old age, free from obstruction, and for that very reason ⟦अयासः⟧, having the nature of going everywhere, ⟦वि तिष्ठंतां⟧: let them stand forth in a distinguished way.

⟦तपो⟧: it is ⟦तप उ⟧; there is guṇa. Having regard to the particle uñ, the designation pragṛhya comes by the rule ⟦उञ इति⟧. Because it stands at the beginning of the pāda it does not lose its accent. ⟦तपा⟧: a form in loṭ of the root ⟦तप संताप⟧, tap in the sense of heating. The vowel is long in the Saṃhitā by the rule on the a of a verbal form of two syllables. ⟦अररुषः⟧: when the suffix kvasu comes after the root ⟦रा दान⟧, rā in the sense of giving, the augment iṭ which has been obtained by the rule on vasu after a root of one vowel is set aside, because samprasāraṇa is the stronger by the maxim ⟦संप्रसारणं संप्रसारणाश्रयं च बलीय⟧, "samprasāraṇa, and what depends on samprasāraṇa, is the stronger". The samprasāraṇa comes by the rule ⟦वसोः संप्रसारणमिति⟧. In the compound the accent is that of the negative particle. ⟦चिकितानः⟧: after the root ⟦कित ज्ञान⟧, kit in the sense of knowing, liṭ comes in the Veda. In its place the substitute kānac comes by the rule ⟦लिटः कानज्वेति⟧. There is reduplication. Because the suffix is marked with k there is no guṇa. Because it is marked with c the final syllable is udātta. ⟦तिष्ठंतां⟧: the root is sthā in the sense of ceasing to move. After sthā preceded by vi the ātmanepada comes by the rule ⟦समवप्रविभ्यः स्थ⟧. The verb loses its accent. ⟦अयासः⟧: the root is ay in the sense of going. The suffix is the ac of the group beginning with pac. The accent is that of a suffix marked with c.

## Ṛk 3

O Agni, ⟦इच्छमानः⟧, desiring wealth, I, the sacrificer, with ⟦इध्मेन⟧, with the bundle of kindling-sticks which brings about the kindling, together with ⟦घृतेन⟧, with ghee, offer (⟦जुहोमि⟧) the ⟦हव्यं⟧, the oblation fit to be offered, the puroḍāśa and the rest. For what purpose? ⟦तरसे⟧, for speed: for the achievement of your constant going; ⟦बलाय⟧, for capacity: for the achievement of the carrying of the load of oblations, the puroḍāśa and the rest, I cast the oblation into you. Moreover I, ⟦ब्रह्मणा⟧, with a hymn of praise, ⟦वंदमानः⟧, praising you, ⟦यावदीशे⟧: as much homage as I am able to bear, so much give.**NOTE**{the supplied text reads ⟦यावद्वंदनं वोढुं शक्नोमि⟧, "as much homage as I am able to bear"; the reference text has "as much wealth (dhanam) as I am able to carry": which is to stand?} And do you make this (⟦इमां⟧) ⟦धियं⟧, the praise addressed to you that is being made by us, ⟦देवीं⟧, shining, exceedingly abundant, ⟦शतसेयाय⟧, so that it may end in unlimited wealth.

⟦इध्मेन⟧: the root is indh in the sense of shining. "The fire is kindled by this", hence idhma, a particular kind of wood. The suffix mak comes in the sense of the instrument by the rule beginning ⟦इषियुधींधिदसिश्याधूसूभ्यो⟧. Because the suffix is marked with k, the nasal is elided. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦इच्छमानः⟧: the root is ⟦इषु इच्छायां⟧, iṣ in the sense of wishing. The suffix śānac comes by interchange. The sound ch comes by the rule ⟦इषुगमियमां छ⟧. ⟦जुहोमि⟧: a form in laṭ of the root ⟦हु दानादनयो⟧, hu in the senses of giving and eating. Because it stands at the beginning of the pāda it does not lose its accent. ⟦हव्यं⟧: "it is worthy of the offering": so the suffix ya comes by the rule ⟦छंदसि चेति⟧. When the stem has the designation bha, there is elision by the rule ⟦यस्यॆति⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦यावत्⟧: after the word yad the suffix vatup comes by the rule ⟦यत्तदेतेभ्यः⟧ ⟦परिमाणे वतुबिति⟧. The long ā comes by the rule ⟦आ सर्वनाम्न⟧. The first syllable is udātta. ⟦ईशे⟧: in the first person singular of the laṭ of the root ⟦ईश ऐश्वर्य⟧, īś in the sense of lordship, the last vowel of the ending iṭ becomes e. Because the root belongs to the group beginning with ad, the vikaraṇa śap is dropped. ⟦शतसेयाय⟧: the root is so in the sense of ending. The long ā comes by the rule ⟦अदेच उपदेशेशिती⟧. The suffix yat comes in the sense of the action by the rule ⟦अचो यदिति⟧. The vowel becomes ī by the rule ⟦ईद्यती⟧. The guṇa is that conditioned by an ārdhadhātuka suffix. The second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent.

## Ṛk 4

O ⟦सहसस्पुत्र⟧, O Agni, with ⟦शोचिषा⟧, with your own radiance, ⟦उत्⟧: blaze up. ⟦स्तुतः⟧, praised with the mantras which are the means of the stotra and the śastra, do you, among the ⟦शशमानेषु⟧ — ⟦शशमानः शंसमानः⟧ (⟦नि.६-८⟧), "śaśamāna: one who praises", says Yāska — among those who are making praise addressed to you, among the ⟦विश्वामित्रेषु⟧, those born in the gotra of Viśvāmitra — "he to whom all is a friend" is Viśvāmitra, one man; the plural used of one is for the sake of honour — among us who are of that kind, bestow ⟦वयः⟧ that is ⟦रेवत्⟧, accompanied by wealth, and ⟦बृहत्⟧, abundant. The word vayas expresses food, because it is recited among the names of food in ⟦अर्को वयः⟧ and what follows. Such abundant food ⟦धेहि⟧, bestow. Moreover bestow on us ⟦शं⟧, the allaying of diseases. And bestow ⟦योः⟧, the warding off, the keeping apart, of dangers. ⟦शमनं च रोगाणां⟧ … ⟦च भयानाममिति⟧, "both the allaying of diseases and the warding off of dangers", says Yāska.**NOTE**{for this quotation from Yāska the reference text gives the reference Nirukta 4, 21; the supplied text gives none} ⟦कृत्वः⟧, O doer, as the one who brings about the rite that is being performed by us, O Agni, ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦तन्वं⟧, body, ⟦भूरि⟧, many times, ⟦मर्मृज्म⟧: again and again we sprinkle it with Soma, ghee, milk and the like.

⟦शशमानेषु⟧: this root, śaś in the sense of going by leaps, has here the sense of praising. The suffix cānaś comes by the rule on habit, age and capacity. The final syllable is udātta by the rule ⟦चित⟧. ⟦धेहि⟧: a form in loṭ of the root ḍudhāñ, in the senses of holding and nourishing. ⟦रेवत्⟧: "he has rayi": so the suffix matup. The suffix matup is udātta by the rule on matup after a short vowel and after the augment nuṭ. Its m becomes v by the rule ⟦छंदसीर⟧. The samprasāraṇa comes by the rule ⟦रयेर्मतौ बहुलं⟧ and what follows, "in the Veda". The preceding vowel alone takes the place of the two; there is guṇa. ⟦विश्वामित्रेषु⟧: the first member has a long vowel by the rule ⟦मित्रे चर्षौ⟧ (⟦पा ६-⟧ ⟦३-१३०⟧). By the rule ⟦बहुव्रीहौ विश्वं संज्ञायामिति⟧ the first member has the udātta on its final syllable. ⟦मर्मृज्म⟧: in the first person plural of the liṭ, used of the present, of the root ⟦मृजू शुद्धावि⟧, mṛj in the sense of cleansing, the substitute ma takes the place of mas. By the maxim ⟦अनित्यमागमशासनमि⟧, "the teaching of an augment is not invariable", there is no iṭ. The r is Vedic. Because it stands at the beginning of the pāda it does not lose its accent. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦भूरि⟧: the root is ⟦भू सत्तायां⟧, bhū in the sense of being. The suffix krin comes by the rule ⟦अदिशदिभूशुभिभ्यः क्रिन्निति⟧. Because the suffix is marked with k there is no guṇa. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦कृत्वः⟧: the root is ⟦डुकृञ् करणे⟧, kṛ in the sense of doing. Because it belongs to the group beginning with sampad, the suffix kvip comes in the sense of the action. "He has that": so the suffix matup. Its m becomes v by the rule ⟦झय⟧. Since the stem has the designation bha by the rule ⟦तसौ मत्वर्थ⟧, the t does not become a soft consonant. By the rule ⟦मतुवसो रुरिति⟧ the t becomes ru.

## Ṛk 5

⟦सुसनितः⟧, O good giver of the riches that are desired, O Agni, among ⟦धनानां⟧, among riches such as gold and cattle, ⟦रत्नं⟧, the best wealth, ⟦कृधि⟧: bestow it; give it to me. ⟦यत्⟧, when, you are ⟦संइद्धः⟧, well kindled with Soma, ghee and the rest, blazing, then ⟦स घेत्⟧: you, being just such, do indeed become the giver of wealth. Moreover, in the ⟦दुरोणे⟧, the house, of the ⟦स्तोतुः⟧, the sacrificer, who is ⟦सुभगस्य⟧, possessed of excellent wealth through the praise addressed to you, your two arms which are ⟦सृप्रा⟧, in the habit of moving forward, stretched forth in order to perform the rite, and ⟦करस्ना⟧, the cleansers of rites — ⟦करस्ना बाहू कर्मणां⟧ and what follows (⟦नि. ६-१७⟧), "karasnā: the two arms, the cleansers of rites", says Yāska — and ⟦वपूंषि⟧, forms, because ⟦वर्पो वपुरिति⟧ is recited among the names of form**NOTE**{for this citation from the names of form the reference text adds the reference Nighaṇṭu 3. 7. 4; the supplied text gives none}: such arms, and shining lights, ⟦रेवत्⟧, in such a way that they are accompanied by wealth, ⟦दधिषे⟧: you bear.

⟦सुसनितः⟧: a form with the suffix tṛc of the root ⟦षणु दान⟧, san in the sense of giving. It loses its accent as a vocative. ⟦सृप्रा⟧: the root is sṛp in the sense of going. After it the suffix rak comes by the rule beginning with sphāy, tañc and vañc. Because the suffix is marked with k there is no guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦करस्ना⟧: "it produces a result", hence kara, a rite. It ends in the suffix ac of the group beginning with pac. "Those two in which there is snā, cleansing, in the rite." Or else: "those two by which there is the cleansing of rites". In the bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member. ⟦दधिषे⟧: in the laṭ of the root ḍudhāñ, in the senses of holding and nourishing, the augment iṭ comes because the ending thās is ārdhadhātuka by the rule that in the Veda it is both. The ā is elided. The verb loses its accent.
