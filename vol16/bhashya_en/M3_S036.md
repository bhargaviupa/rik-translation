## Introduction

The seventh sūkta, beginning ⟦इमामू⟧, has eleven Ṛks, is in the Triṣṭubh metre, and is addressed to Indra. On this the Anukramaṇikā says: of the sūkta ⟦इमामू⟧ the penultimate Ṛk was seen by Ghora; it is heard that ⟦सा निर्दहेच्छस्यमान⟧, that is, that Ṛk, if recited, would burn. Of the tenth the seer is Ghora the Āṅgirasa; of the remaining Ṛks the seer is Viśvāmitra.

In the Agniṣṭoma, at the midday pressing, in the Acchāvāka's śastra: the sūkta ⟦इमामू⟧, with the tenth Ṛk excluded. And it is taught in a sūtra that in the sūkta beginning ⟦इमामू⟧ one should take out the penultimate Ṛk in every case (⟦आ. ५-१६⟧).

In the Pṛṣṭhya and Abhiplava ṣaḍahas, at the midday pressing, in the Acchāvāka's śastra, in the place of the ahīna-sūkta that is transferred by extension from the Cāturviṃśika, there are three sampāta-sūktas. Among them this is the first sūkta, with the penultimate Ṛk excluded. And it is taught in a sūtra: ⟦इमामू ष्विच्छंति त्वा⟧ (⟦आ. ७-५⟧). **NOTE**{The quoted sūtra ends with the word as supplied, meaning "you"; the reference text ends the same quotation with the particle meaning "or"; which is intended?}

## Ṛk 1

Viśvāmitra praises Indra. O Indra, ⟦ऊतिभिः⟧, with the Ūtis: the Ūtis are the Maruts, since they ⟦अवंति⟧, that is, protect, everything. With them, you who ⟦शश्वच्छश्वत्⟧, always, are ⟦यादमानः⟧, begging for union with them, ⟦प्रभृतिं⟧. What is ⟦भ्रियते⟧, that is, carried or upheld, pre-eminently for the sake of the sacrifice, is prabhṛti: this is Soma, or a praise. ⟦इमां⟧: this Soma, or this praise, ⟦सातये⟧, for our gaining of wealth, ⟦सु धाः⟧, accomplish well.

And that Indra, ⟦सुते सुते⟧, when Soma has been pressed, ⟦वर्धनेभिः⟧, by the causers of growth, the oblations of Soma, ghee and the like, ⟦वावृधे⟧, grows. Indra, who ⟦महद्भिः⟧, by great ⟦कर्मभिः⟧, acts, that is, by activities marked by the slaying of the Asuras, the splitting of the cloud and the like, ⟦सुश्रुतः⟧, well heard, that is, famed in the world, ⟦भूत्⟧, became: that Indra; the connection is with what precedes.

⟦प्रभृतिं⟧: the preverb keeps its own accent, by the rule beginning ⟦तादौ च निति⟧. ⟦धाः⟧: this is a form of ⟦दधाते⟧ in the Vedic luṅ. The accent is nighāta. ⟦शश्वच्छश्वत्⟧: the doubling is by the rule ⟦नित्यवीप्सयो⟧, in the senses of constancy and repetition. ⟦यादमानः⟧: the form is made from the root ⟦टुयाचृ⟧, in the sense of begging, with the suffix śānac; the substitution of d for the letter c is Vedic. ⟦वावृधे⟧: the form is in the liṭ from the root ⟦वृधु⟧, in the sense of growing; because it belongs to the group ⟦तुजादि⟧, the reduplicative syllable is lengthened in the Saṃhitā. The accent is nighāta. ⟦वर्धनेभिः⟧: the suffix lyuṭ is added to the causative of ⟦वर्धते⟧ because the root belongs to the nandādi group; the accent is lit-svara. ⟦सुश्रुतः⟧: from the root ⟦श्रु⟧, in the sense of hearing, with the suffix kta in the passive sense; the accent is that of the preverb, by the rule ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧. ⟦भूत्⟧: this is a form of ⟦भवते⟧ in the luṅ.

## Ṛk 2

At the midday pressing this Ṛk, ⟦इंद्राय सोमाः प्रदिव⟧, is the prasthita-yājyā of the Acchāvāka. And it is taught in a sūtra: ⟦इंद्राय सोमाः प्रदिवो विदाना आपूर्णो अस्य⟧ (⟦आ. ५-५⟧).

O Indra, ⟦इंद्राय⟧, to you, the ⟦सोमाः⟧, Somas, ⟦प्रदिवः⟧, in the former days that have gone by, ⟦विदानाः⟧, have been obtained by us. ⟦वृषपर्वा⟧: he whose ⟦पर्वाणि⟧, the parts of time, the rains and the rest, are ⟦वृषाणः⟧, showerers of fruit, is so called; the meaning is that he is of the nature of time. For that very reason he is ⟦विहायाः⟧: since he ⟦विजहा⟧, that is, gives away wealth to those who ask for it, he is vihāyāḥ, great. Such an Indra is ⟦ऋभुः⟧, radiant, through the Somas given, ⟦येभिः⟧, by which. O Indra, you, ⟦प्रयम्यमानान्⟧, these Somas that are being held up with mantras in an excellent manner, ⟦सु⟧, well, ⟦प्रति गृभाय⟧, accept. And having accepted, drink ⟦वृषधूतस्य⟧, this Soma pressed by the vṛṣans, that is, by the pressing stones, ⟦वृष्णः⟧, which rains down the fruit, heaven and the like.

⟦प्रदिवः⟧: the root ⟦दिवु⟧, in the sense of play and the like, with the suffix kvip. By the rule ⟦कालाध्वनो⟧, on time and path in unbroken connection, there is the accusative. The word ⟦दिव्⟧ denotes the day. The accent is that of the latter member of a compound ending in a kṛt suffix. ⟦विदानाः⟧: from the root ⟦विद्ल⟧, in the sense of obtaining, the suffix śānac comes by vyatyaya; by the rule beginning ⟦बहुलं⟧, in the Veda, the vikaraṇa is dropped. ⟦विहायाः⟧: from the root ⟦ओहाक्⟧, in the sense of abandoning, the suffix asun comes by the rule that names the roots vahi, hā and dhāñ in the Veda; since ṇit is carried on, by the rule that reads, in English, "of ā, yuk, in ciṇ and kṛt", there is the augment yuk. By the rule on the gati and kāraka, ⟦गतिकारकयो⟧, the first member keeps its own accent. ⟦गृभाय⟧: from the root ⟦ग्रह⟧, in the sense of taking, in the loṭ, when hi follows, ⟦हौ⟧, the sign śnā is replaced by śāyac, by the Vedic rule that names śāyac. By the rule ⟦हृग्रहो⟧ there is the substitution of bh. By the rule ⟦अते हे⟧ there is the dropping of hi. The accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 3

At the midday pressing, in the Acchāvāka's śastra, this Ṛk, ⟦पिबा वर्धस्व⟧, is the yājyā. And it is taught in a sūtra: ⟦पिबा वर्धस्व तव घा सुतास⟧ is the yājyā (⟦आ. ५-१६⟧).

O Indra, drink this Soma. Then ⟦वर्धस्व⟧, grow; become strong in body. For you the ⟦प्रथमाः⟧, first, that is, ancient Somas, ⟦उत⟧, and also ⟦इमे⟧, these new ones, Somas of both kinds, ⟦सुतासः⟧ ⟦घ⟧, have indeed been pressed by the pressing stones. O Indra, as you drank, ⟦अपिबः⟧, the former, ancient Somas, ⟦एव⟧, so, ⟦अद्य⟧, now, you who are ⟦पन्यः⟧, praiseworthy, and for that very reason ⟦नवीयान्⟧, exceedingly new, ⟦पाहि⟧, drink these new Somas.

⟦पिब⟧: this is a form of ⟦पाते⟧ in the loṭ. ⟦वर्धस्व⟧: because it follows a tiṅ, there is no nighāta. ⟦अपिबः⟧: because of the connection with ⟦यथा⟧ there is no nighāta. ⟦पाहि⟧: from ⟦पाते⟧ in the loṭ; by the rule beginning ⟦बहुलं⟧, in the Veda, śap is dropped. The accent is nighāta. ⟦पन्यः⟧: from ⟦पनते⟧, in the sense of praising, the suffix yat comes because the word belongs to the group beginning with aghnyā; the accent on the first syllable is by the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧. ⟦नवीयान्⟧: the form is from ⟦नव⟧ with the suffix īyasun; the accent is nit-svara.

## Ṛk 4

This Indra who is ⟦महान्⟧, great, possessed of surpassing power, ⟦अमत्रः⟧, the overcomer of enemies, in ⟦वृजने⟧, battle, which is marked by strength, and therefore ⟦विरप्सी⟧, given to crying out, who by slapping his arms calls out his enemies to battle; or else, varied crying, of the nature of praise, is his, so that the meaning is that he is praised by all. The ⟦उग्रं⟧, raised-up, ⟦शवः⟧, strength, of such an Indra, and his ⟦धृष्णु⟧, overpowering, ⟦ओजः⟧, vigour, that is, splendour marked by prowess, ⟦पत्यते⟧, spreads everywhere. ⟦एनं⟧, this Indra, endowed with such greatness, ⟦पृथिवी⟧, the broad earth, ⟦विव्याच⟧, does not pervade. The particle ⟦अह⟧ is in the sense of ⟦एव⟧, only; ⟦चन⟧ is in the sense of "even". The meaning is that even heaven does not pervade him. ⟦यत्⟧, when, the ⟦सोमासः⟧, Somas offered with svāhā, ⟦अमंदन्⟧, gladdened Indra, ⟦हर्यश्व⟧, then Heaven and Earth are not able to pervade him, this Indra.

⟦अमत्रः⟧: the root ⟦अम⟧, in the senses of going and the like; the suffix atran comes by the rule beginning ⟦अमिनक्षियजी⟧; the accent is nit-svara. ⟦विरप्सी⟧: the roots ⟦रप⟧ and ⟦लप⟧ are in the sense of articulate speech; the suffix is a Uṇādi śa. Those who cry out in many ways are ⟦विरप्याः⟧, the praisers; "they are his" is the meaning, and the suffix ini comes after a stem ending in a. Or else ⟦विरप्यं⟧ is varied ⟦रपणं⟧, crying; "that is his" is the meaning; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦पत्यते⟧: by vyatyaya, yak comes in the active sense; the accent is nighāta. ⟦धृष्णु⟧: from the root ⟦इाधृषा⟧, in the sense of boldness, the suffix knu comes by the rule ⟦त्रसिगृधिधृषिक्षिपे⟧; the change of n to ṇ is by the rule ⟦रषाभ्याम⟧; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦विव्याच⟧: from ⟦व्यचे⟧ in the liṭ, in ṇal, by the rule beginning ⟦लिट्यभ्यास⟧, which ends with "of both", there is saṃprasāraṇa of the reduplicative syllable. The accent is nighāta. ⟦चन⟧: this is a combination of particles, used in the sense of ⟦च⟧; because it belongs to the group ⟦एवमादि⟧, the accent is on the final syllable. ⟦अमंदन्⟧: this is a form in the laṅ of the root ⟦मदि⟧, in the senses of praising and the like. Because of the connection with ⟦यत्⟧ there is no nighāta.

## Ṛk 5

Indra, ⟦महान्⟧, great, that is, strong in the work of battle, ⟦उग्रः⟧, fearful to his enemies, ⟦वावृधे⟧, grows, for ⟦वीर्याय⟧, the achieving of valour. ⟦वृषभः⟧, the pourer-down of fruit, ⟦भगः⟧, to be worshipped by all: that Indra ⟦समाचक्रे⟧, comes together, ⟦काव्येन⟧, with praise: the ⟦कवयः⟧ are the praisers; their work is praise; he comes together with that praise. Of this Indra, such as he is, the ⟦गावः⟧, cows, ⟦वाजदाः⟧, which give food of the nature of milk and the like, are ⟦प्रजायंते⟧, born. And likewise ⟦पूर्वीः⟧, many, those cows of his are born as ⟦दक्षिणाः⟧.

⟦वावृधे⟧: this is a form of ⟦वर्धते⟧ in the liṭ. The accent is nighāta. ⟦वीर्याय⟧: what is in a hero is ⟦वीरे भवं वीर्यं⟧, valour; the suffix yat comes by the rule ⟦भवे छंदसी⟧. Because the non-universality of the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧ is indicated in the rule ⟦वीरवीर्यौ च⟧, the accent is tit-svarita. ⟦समाचक्रे⟧: this is a form of ⟦करोते⟧ in the liṭ. By the rule beginning ⟦गतिर्गता⟧ the earlier preverb has nighāta. ⟦काव्येन⟧: ⟦कवेः कर्म काव्यं⟧, the work of the kavi; because it belongs to the group ⟦ब्राह्मणादि⟧, the suffix ṣyañ comes; because of the indicatory ñ there is vṛddhi of the penultimate; the accent is ñit-svara. ⟦वाजदाः⟧: from ⟦ददाते⟧, by the rule on a root ending in ā when there is no preverb, the suffix ka comes. ⟦प्रजायंते⟧: this is a form in the laṭ of the root ⟦जनी⟧, in the sense of coming forth. ⟦दक्षिणाः⟧: from the root ⟦दक्ष⟧, in the sense of growth, the suffix inan comes by the rule ⟦द्रुदक्षिभ्यामिनन्⟧; because of the indicatory n, the accent is on the first syllable.

## Ṛk 6

⟦सिंधवः⟧, rivers, as ⟦प्रसवं⟧: ⟦प्रसवः⟧ is what is pre-eminently ⟦सूयत⟧, brought forth: desire. Following that, when ⟦प्रायन्⟧, they go far, very far, to the sea, then the ⟦आपः⟧, waters, ⟦जग्मुः⟧, went to the great ⟦समुद्रं⟧, sea, ⟦रथ्येव⟧, like those who have chariots. They go for the sake of pleasing it. In the same way, ⟦अतः⟧ ⟦चित्⟧ ⟦सदसः⟧, even than this atmosphere, that Indra is ⟦वरीयान्⟧, wider indeed. ⟦यत्⟧ ⟦ईं⟧: this Indra, whom the ⟦दुग्धः⟧, pressed, ⟦अंशुः⟧, stalk, which is a small ⟦सोमः⟧, Soma, in the form of a piece of the creeper, ⟦पृणति⟧, pleases. Just as small rivers and smaller waters please the great sea, so the Soma, which is smaller, being a stalk, pleases Indra, who is wider.

⟦प्रसवं⟧: from ⟦सवते⟧ the suffix ap comes in the sense of the object; the accent is that taught for thātha and the rest (⟦थाधादिस्वरः⟧). ⟦प्रायन्⟧: this is a form of the root ⟦आयते⟧ in the laṅ. Because of the connection with ⟦यत्⟧ there is no nighāta. ⟦रथ्येव⟧: the ones belonging to the chariot are ⟦रथस्येमे रथ्याः⟧; the suffix yat comes by the rule ⟦रथाद⟧; by the rule beginning ⟦सुपां सुलु⟧ the case-ending is replaced by ḍā; the accent is tit-svarita. By the rule ⟦इवेन विभक्त्यलोपः⟧, with iva the case-ending is not dropped, and the accent is the original accent of the first member. ⟦जग्मुः⟧: this is a form of ⟦गम⟧ in the liṭ; by the rule beginning ⟦गमहन⟧ the penultimate is dropped. The accent is nighāta. ⟦वरीयान्⟧: from ⟦उरु⟧ with the suffix īyasun, by the rule beginning ⟦प्रियस्थिर⟧ there is the substitute var; because of the indicatory n, the accent is on the first syllable. ⟦पृणति⟧: the root ⟦पृण⟧ is in the sense of pleasing; it belongs to the tudādi class. Because of the connection with ⟦यत्⟧ there is no nighāta. ⟦दुग्धः⟧: from ⟦दुहे⟧ the suffix kta comes; by the rule ⟦एकाच⟧ there is the prohibition of iṭ; by the rule ⟦दादेर्धातोर्घ⟧ the h becomes gh; by the rule ⟦झषस्तथोधोऽधः⟧ the t becomes dh. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 7

⟦समुद्रेण⟧: the waters in the form of rivers and the like run together to it; therefore it is ⟦समुद्रः⟧. ⟦यादमानाः⟧, begging for union with it, the ⟦सिंधवः⟧, rivers, as they fill it, so the ⟦हस्तिनः⟧, those who have hands, the Adhvaryu and the other priests, ⟦इंद्राय⟧, for you, Indra, ⟦सुषुतं⟧, well pressed, ⟦सोमं⟧, Soma, ⟦भरंतः⟧, bringing about, milk ⟦अंशुं⟧, the stalk; that is, they press the piece of the creeper. And likewise those Adhvaryu and other priests, by ⟦धारया⟧, the stream of the ⟦स्रवत⟧, flowing, juice, with ⟦पवित्रैः⟧, purifying, ⟦भरित्रैः⟧: ⟦भरित्रा⟧, since they bear things for the performance of the work, are the arms; with them they ⟦पुनंति⟧, that is, purify, ⟦मध्वः⟧, the Somas endowed with sweetness.

⟦समुद्रेण⟧: from the root ⟦द्रवते⟧ preceded by sam and ut, the suffix ḍa comes by the rule that it is seen also from others; because of the indicatory ḍ, ṭi is dropped. Or else: the root ⟦उन्दी⟧, in the sense of moistening, preceded by sam, takes rak by the rule beginning ⟦स्थायितंची⟧; because of the indicatory k, the penultimate is dropped. In both cases the accent is that of the suffix. When the single substitute with the case-ending has been made, the single substitute is udātta only. ⟦यादमानाः⟧: the root ⟦टु याचृ⟧, in the sense of begging, with śānac; by vyatyaya d replaces c; when śānac, a lasārvadhātuka suffix, is in question, the accent is that of the root. ⟦दुहंति⟧: this is a form in the laṭ of the root ⟦दुह⟧, in the sense of filling up. ⟦भरित्रैः⟧: from the root ⟦डुब्बञ्⟧, in the senses of holding and nourishing, the suffix itra comes by the rule beginning ⟦शित्रादिभ्य⟧, which names itra and otra; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦पुनंति⟧: this is a form in the laṭ of the root ⟦पूञ्⟧, in the sense of purifying.

## Ṛk 8

⟦कुक्षयः⟧: the bellies, that is, the abdomens, of Indra are ⟦सोमधानाः⟧: since Soma is deposited, ⟦निधीयते⟧, in them, they are receptacles of Soma. An example here: ⟦ह्रदा इव⟧, like lakes; as lakes are containers of water, so are they. ⟦ईं⟧: this Indra ⟦पुरूणि सवना⟧, the many pressings, that is, the three pressings, ⟦सं विव्याच⟧, pervades completely. Since ⟦व्याश⟧, Indra ate in particular, ⟦जघास⟧, the ⟦प्रथमा⟧ ⟦अन्ना⟧, the first foods that are to be eaten, Soma and the other foods, therefore Indra, ⟦वृत्रं जघन्वान्⟧, the slayer of Vṛtra, ⟦अवृणीत⟧, that is, ⟦समभजत⟧, apportioned Soma to the gods at the midday pressing.

⟦कुक्षयः⟧: the root ⟦कुष⟧, in the sense of drawing out; the suffix is by the rule ⟦प्लुषिकुषिशुषिभ्यः क्सिः⟧ (⟦उ. ३-१५५⟧). What is ⟦कुष्यत⟧, drawn out, is kukṣi. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦सोमधानाः⟧: from ⟦दधाते⟧ the suffix lyuṭ comes in the sense of the locus; the accent is lit-svara. ⟦विव्याच⟧: this is a form of ⟦व्यचे⟧ in the liṭ. ⟦अन्ना⟧: the root ⟦अद⟧, in the sense of eating; what is eaten, ⟦अद्यत⟧, is food. ⟦आश⟧: this is a form of the root ⟦अश⟧, in the sense of eating, in the liṭ, in ṇal; by the rule that reads, in English, "of the initial a" the reduplicative syllable is lengthened. Because of the connection with ⟦यत्⟧ there is no nighāta. The accent is lit-svara. ⟦वृत्रं⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦न लोकाव्यय⟧ the genitive is prohibited. ⟦जघन्वान्⟧: from ⟦हंते⟧ the suffix kvasu comes, without iṭ; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अवृणीत⟧: this is a form in the laṅ of the root ⟦वृज्⟧, in the sense of sharing.

## Ṛk 9

O Indra, ⟦तु⟧, quickly, ⟦भर⟧, bring wealth. ⟦माकिः⟧, who indeed could stand in the way, ⟦परि ष्ठात्⟧, obstructing this wealth that is given by you? ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦वसूनां⟧, of wealths, ⟦वसुपतिं⟧, the lord of the best wealth, ⟦विद्म⟧ ⟦हि⟧, we know indeed. ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦माहिनं⟧, venerable, ⟦यत्⟧ ⟦दत्रं⟧, wealth, that there is, O ⟦हर्यश्व⟧, that wealth ⟦अस्मभ्यं⟧, to us, ⟦प्र यंधि⟧, give.

⟦तु⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦ऋचि तुनुघ⟧ there is lengthening in the Saṃhitā. ⟦भर⟧: this is a form of ⟦भरते⟧ in the loṭ. ⟦माकिः⟧: this is a particle. ⟦स्थात्⟧: this is a form of ⟦तिष्ठते⟧ in the Vedic luṅ. By the rule beginning ⟦उपसर्गात्सुनोत⟧ there is the change to ṣ in the Saṃhitā. The accent is nighāta. ⟦विद्म⟧: this is a form of ⟦वेत्ते⟧ in the laṭ; by the rule ⟦विदो लटो⟧, optionally, there is the substitute ma for mas. The accent is that of the suffix. By the rule on the a of a verb form of two syllables there is lengthening in the Saṃhitā. ⟦वसुपतिं⟧: by the rule ⟦पत्यावैश्वर्य⟧ the first member keeps its own accent. ⟦माहिनं⟧: from ⟦महे⟧ the suffix inaṇ comes by the rule ⟦महेरिनण् च⟧; because of the indicatory ṇ there is vṛddhi of the penultimate; because it belongs to the group beginning with grāma, the accent is on the first syllable. ⟦यंधि⟧: the root ⟦यमु⟧, in the sense of stopping; in the loṭ, by the rule beginning ⟦बहुलं⟧, in the Veda, śap is dropped. The ending si becomes hi. Because the pit-ness of hi is made optional by the rule ⟦तस्य वा⟧, in the Veda, and because here it is not ṅit, by the rule "and of what is not ṅit" there is the substitution dhi for hi. The accent is nighāta.

## Ṛk 10

In the Jātakarman, at the touching of the shoulder, this Ṛk, ⟦अस्मे प्रयंधि⟧ (⟦आ. गृ. १-१५⟧). Although it is forbidden to recite this Ṛk by the statement ⟦सा निर्दहेद⟧, that it would burn, nevertheless, since the touching is enjoined with its recitation, the stated fault does not arise. **NOTE**{the construction of abhimarśanasya śaṃsane coditatvāt is not clear; it is rendered as "since the touching is enjoined in the recitation"; the reference text reads the same}

⟦मघवन्⟧, possessing wealth, O ṛjīṣin, possessor of Soma whose essence is gone, O Indra, ⟦विश्ववारस्य⟧: that which is chosen by all (⟦विश्वैः⟧), as a thing to be shared in; ⟦भूरेः⟧, much, ⟦रायः⟧, wealth; the genitive is in the sense of the accusative. ⟦अस्मे⟧, to us, ⟦प्र यंधि⟧, give. Further, ⟦अस्मे⟧, our, ⟦जीवसे⟧, for life, ⟦शतं शरदः⟧, a hundred years, ⟦धाः⟧, give. ⟦शिप्रिन्⟧, O Indra of fine jaws, ⟦अस्मे⟧, our, ⟦शश्वतः⟧: ⟦शश्वद⟧ is a name for "many"; give many heroes, that is, sons.

⟦जीवसे⟧: the root ⟦जीव⟧, in the sense of sustaining breath, takes the suffix ase in the sense of the infinitive tum. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦शश्वतः⟧: the root ⟦टुओश्वि⟧, in the senses of going and growing; from it, by the rule beginning ⟦संश्चद्वेहद⟧, the word is made to end in the suffix ati by nipātana, and so the desired form is accomplished.

## Ṛk 11

O Indra, ⟦वाजसात्⟧: that in which there is the gain of ⟦वाजस्य⟧, food, is this ⟦वाजसातिः⟧. ⟦अस्मिन्⟧ ⟦भरे⟧: ⟦भरः⟧ is battle, since by it warriors bear the fortune of victory. In that battle ⟦शुनं⟧, swollen, ⟦शूनम⟧, that is, grown great by zeal; or else ⟦शुनं⟧ is a name for happiness: bringing happiness; ⟦मघवानं⟧, possessing wealth, and therefore ⟦इंद्रं⟧, Indra, endowed with unsurpassed lordship, ⟦नृतमं⟧, the greatest leader of the whole world, you ⟦हुवेम⟧, we, the Kuśikas, would call for the sacrifice. Likewise ⟦शृण्वंतं⟧, hearing the praise that is made by us, ⟦उग्रं⟧, fearful to enemies, ⟦समत्सु⟧, in battles, ⟦वृत्राणि⟧, all the rakṣases marked by Vṛtra, ⟦घ्नंतं⟧, injuring, ⟦धनानां⟧, of the riches belonging to the enemies, ⟦संजितं⟧, the thorough conqueror, you we would call for ⟦ऊतये⟧, protection.

⟦शुनं⟧: from the root ⟦टुओश्वि⟧, in the senses of going and growing, in the suffix of the past participle, there is prohibition of iṭ by the rule beginning ⟦यस्य विभाष⟧; because the root belongs to the group ⟦यजादि⟧, there is saṃprasāraṇa; the absence of lengthening is Vedic. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦हुवेम⟧: from ⟦हैयते⟧, after saṃprasāraṇa has been made by the rule beginning ⟦बहुलं⟧, in the Veda, and after śap has been dropped, this is a form in the liṅ. The accent is nighāta. ⟦भरे⟧: the root ⟦भृञ्⟧, in the sense of bearing; the suffix gha; because the word belongs to the group ⟦वृषादि⟧, the accent is on the first syllable. ⟦वाजसात्⟧: the root ⟦पणु⟧, in the sense of giving; the suffix ktin in the abstract sense; by the rule beginning ⟦जनसन⟧ the nasal is replaced by ā. In a bahuvrīhi the accent is that of the first member. ⟦समत्सु⟧: the root ⟦आद⟧, in the sense of eating, preceded by sam, with kvip. ⟦समदः⟧ are battles, since they eat up, ⟦संभक्षयंति⟧, the lives of warriors; the accent is that of the latter member of a compound ending in a kṛt suffix. ⟦घ्नंतं⟧: the root ⟦हन⟧, in the senses of killing and going, in the suffix śatṛ; by the rule beginning ⟦गमहन⟧ the penultimate is dropped; by the rule ⟦हो हंते⟧ the h becomes gh. ⟦संजितं⟧: the root ⟦जि⟧, in the sense of conquering, with kvip.
