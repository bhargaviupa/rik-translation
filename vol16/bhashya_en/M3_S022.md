## Introduction

The sūkta beginning ⟦अयं स⟧ is the tenth, of five Ṛks. On it the Anukramaṇikā says this: ⟦उपांत्यानुष्टुप् पुरीष्येभ्योग्निभ्य⟧, that is, in the sūkta beginning "ayaṃ saḥ" the last Ṛk but one is an anuṣṭubh, and it is addressed to the purīṣya Agnis. The Ṛṣi is Gāthin, of the line of Kuśika. The Agnis in the form of the five layers of the fire-altar are the deity. The fourth Ṛk is an anuṣṭubh; the rest are triṣṭubhs. The use of the sūkta in the prātaranuvāka and in the āśvina-śastra has been stated. In the piling of the fire-altar, at the carrying forward of the bricks, the Ṛk beginning ⟦पुरीष्यास⟧ is to be recited. It is laid down in the sūtra which begins ⟦प्रेषितः पुरीष्यचितये⟧, "when directed, he recites for the purīṣya layer", and continues ⟦पुरीष्यादो अग्निय इति त्रिरुपांशु⟧, "the Ṛk 'purīṣyāso agnayaḥ' three times, in a low voice", with the syllable Om (⟦आ ४-८⟧).

## Ṛk 1

Gāthin speaks. ⟦अयं⟧: this ⟦अग्निः⟧, the fire that is seen on the uttaravedi in the form of the āhavanīya, the receptacle of the oblations, ⟦सः⟧: is indeed that one, of such a kind, ⟦यस्मिन्⟧: in which fire ⟦इंद्रः⟧, Indra, ⟦वावशानः⟧: desiring the Soma, ⟦दधे⟧: placed, ⟦जठरे⟧: in his own belly, the ⟦सोमं⟧, the Soma, that was ⟦सुतं⟧: furnished with the preparations of pressing and the rest. Therefore that Agni is possessed of such greatness: this is how it is construed with what precedes. Moreover, O ⟦जातवेदः⟧, Jātavedas: ⟦सहस्रिणं⟧: he who in battle takes on a thousand forms, that is, manifoldness of form, is a sahasrin; that ⟦अत्यं⟧: one whose nature it is to keep going by force of his speed; ⟦सप्तिं⟧ ⟦न⟧: like a horse; ⟦वाजं⟧: the food that consists of the oblation, of many kinds, clarified butter, plants, Soma and the rest; ⟦ससवान्⟧: having partaken of it, ⟦सन्⟧: being so, you ⟦स्तूयसे⟧: you stand being praised by us, the praisers.

⟦धधे⟧: in the perfect, liṭ, of the root dhā, by the rule that ends ⟦स्तझयोरेशिरेजिति⟧ the substitute eś takes the place of the ending ta. Because of the connection with a form of the relative pronoun, the verb does not lose its accent. The accent is that of the ending. ⟦वावशानः⟧: of the root ⟦वश⟧, in the sense of desire, in the intensive with elision of the yaṅ suffix, the reduplicating syllable is lengthened by the rule ⟦दीर्घोऽकित⟧. By interchange the suffix is śānac. The accent is that of a suffix marked with c. ⟦ससवान्⟧: the root is ⟦षण⟧, in the sense of sharing. Before the suffix kvasu the augment iṭ is absent. The n is elided. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦स्तूयसे⟧: the root is ⟦ष्टुञ्⟧, in the sense of praise. The suffix yak comes in the passive. The lengthening is by the rule ⟦अकृत्सार्वधातुकयोरिति⟧. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 2

⟦यजत्र⟧: O you who are worthy of sacrifice, O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦दिवि⟧: in the world of heaven, ⟦यत्⟧ ⟦वर्चः⟧: the light that is called Āditya, that light is ⟦ते⟧: yours alone. ⟦पृथिव्यां⟧: the brilliance that exists on earth in the form of the āhavanīya and the other fires, that is yours alone. Having spoken of the well-known brilliance, he speaks of the hidden light with the words "yad oṣadhīṣv apsu". ⟦ओषधीषु⟧: the brilliance that is in the pieces of wood, the fire-sticks and the like. Or else: ⟦ओषधीषु⟧: in the trees and the like, the brilliance that is called Soma; ⟦अप्ऽसु⟧: in the waters, the brilliance that is called Aurva; all that, ⟦आ⟧: the brilliance that exists everywhere, is yours alone. Moreover, ⟦येन⟧: the brilliance in the form of Vāyu by which you ⟦उरु⟧ ⟦आततंथ⟧: spread out, made wide, the ⟦अंतरिक्षं⟧, the sky. ⟦त्वेषः⟧: shining; for that very reason ⟦भानुः⟧: radiant; ⟦नृचक्षाः⟧: the beholder of men as their witness; ⟦अर्णवः⟧: great like the ocean. ⟦सः⟧: you, being such, spread out the mid-region: this is how it is construed with what precedes. On the meaning stated there is the text of the Vājasaneyaka: ⟦अग्ने यत्ते दिवि वर्च इत्यादित्यो वा अस्य दिवि वर्चः पृथिव्यामित्ययमग्निः पृथिव्यां यदोषधीष्टप्स्वा यजत्रेति य एवौषधीषु चाप्सु चाग्निस्तमेतदाह येनांतरिक्षमूर्वाततंथेति वायुः स त्वेषः स⟧ … ⟦नृचक्षा इति महांत्सभानुरणर्वो नृचक्षा इत्येतत्⟧ (⟦शत. ब्रा. ७-१-२३⟧), that is: "With the words 'O Agni, your light that is in heaven': the Āditya indeed is his light in heaven. With the word 'on earth': this Agni is on earth. With the words 'that is in the plants, in the waters, O you worthy of sacrifice': it is the Agni that is in the plants and in the waters that he speaks of by this. With the words 'by which you spread wide the mid-region': that is Vāyu. With the words 'that is the shining one, that the radiant, the ocean, the beholder of men': this means, that is the great one, the radiant, the ocean, the beholder of men."

⟦पृथिव्यां⟧: by the rule ⟦उदात्तयणो हल्बूनादिति⟧ the case-ending is udātta. ⟦ओषधीषु⟧: the lengthening is by the rule ⟦ओषधेश्च विभक्तावप्रथमायामिति⟧. ⟦अप्पु⟧: by the rule ⟦सावेकाच⟧ the case-ending is udātta. ⟦आततंथ⟧: of the root ⟦तनु⟧, in the sense of extension, in the perfect, liṭ, before the ending thal, because the form is laid down ready-made in the rule ⟦बभूथाततंथ जगृम्म ववर्धेति⟧, the augment iṭ, the substitution of e and the elision of the reduplicating syllable do not take place. By the rule ⟦लतीति⟧ the vowel before the suffix is udātta. Because of the connection with a form of the relative pronoun, the verb does not lose its accent. ⟦अर्णवः⟧: in the sense "waters are in it", that is, in the sense of the suffix matup, the suffix va comes by the statement ⟦वप्रकरणेऽन्येभ्योपि दृश्यंत⟧, "in the section on va it is seen after other words also".**NOTE**{After this statement the reference text has a reference with the numbers 5. 2. 109. 1, which the supplied text does not have.} The s is elided by ⟦अर्णगः सलोपश्चेति⟧, "and there is elision of the s of arṇas". The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦नृचक्षाः⟧: the root is ⟦चक्षिज्⟧, in the sense of distinct speech. This root, which denotes distinct speech, is used here of mere manifestation. By ⟦चक्षेर्बदुलं शिच्चेत्यसुन्⟧, that is, by the rule "after cakṣ, variously, and it is treated as marked with ś", the suffix is asun. The accent is the original accent of the second member, which ends in a kṛt suffix.

## Ṛk 3

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, you ⟦दिवः⟧ ⟦अर्णं⟧: the water that is in the world of heaven, ⟦अच्छ⟧: aiming at it, ⟦जिगासि⟧: you reach it by way of the smoke. Moreover, ⟦धिष्ण्याः⟧: those who heat, make warm, the dhī, that is, the body conditioned by the intellect, are the dhiṣṇyas, the gods who preside over the breaths. Those ⟦देवान्⟧, the gods called breaths, ⟦अच्छ⟧: in due order, ⟦ऊचिषे⟧: you bring together; the meaning is that you have said, "Be assembled". ⟦सूर्यस्य⟧: of the world of the sun, ⟦परस्तात्⟧: above, ⟦रोचने⟧: Rocana is the name of that world in which the light of Agni burns; ⟦याः⟧: the waters that exist in that shining world called Rocana, ⟦अवस्तात्⟧: and below the world of the sun, in the world of the mid-region, the ⟦आपः⟧, the waters that ⟦उपतिष्ठंते⟧: exist; both those waters you set in motion. The meaning stated has been clearly declared by the Vājasaneyins: ⟦आपो वा अस्य⟧ … ⟦एष धूमेनाच्छेत्यच्छा देवा ऊजिषे धिष्ट्या य इति प्राणा वै देवा धिष्ण्यास्ते हि सर्वा धिय इष्टंति या रोचने परस्तात्ूर्यस्य याश्चा⟧ … ⟦आप इति रोचनो ह नामैष लोको यत्रैष एतत्तपति तद्याश्चतं⟧ … ⟦याश्चावरेण ता एतदाह⟧ (⟦शत. ब्रा. ७-१-२४⟧), that is: "The waters indeed are his flood of heaven; to them he goes by means of the smoke. With the words 'you have spoken to the gods who are the dhiṣṇyas': the breaths indeed are the dhiṣṇya gods, for they set in motion all thoughts. With the words 'the waters that are in the Rocana beyond the sun and those that stand below': Rocana indeed is the name of that world where this one burns; so the waters that are beyond it and those that are on this side of it, of these he speaks by this." Both those waters you set in motion: such is the sense.

⟦अर्ण०⟧: from the root ⟦ऋ⟧, in the sense of going, by the rule "and nuṭ, in the sense of water", there come the suffix asun and the augment nuṭ. It is sought, that is, asked for, by those who thirst for it: hence arṇas. The elision of the s is Vedic. Because the suffix is marked with n, the word has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦जिगासि⟧: the root ⟦गा स्तुतौ च⟧, "gā, in the sense of praise also, in the Veda", being used in the sense of going on the strength of the word "also", belongs to the class beginning with juhoti. By the rule beginning ⟦बहुलं⟧, "variously in the Veda", i comes in the reduplicating syllable. The verb loses its accent. ⟦ऊजिषे⟧: of the root ⟦ब्रूञ्⟧, in the sense of distinct speech, because the perfect, liṭ, is ārdhadhātuka, the substitute vaci comes by the rule that vaci takes the place of brū (⟦पा. २-४-५३⟧). Because by the rule ⟦असंयोगाल्लिट्⟧, "the liṭ after what does not end in a conjunct is marked with k", the ending is treated as marked with k, there is saṃprasāraṇa by the rule that begins with vaci and svapi. In the reduplicating syllable there is saṃprasāraṇa by the rule ⟦लिट्यभ्यासस्योभयेषामिति⟧. The augment iṭ comes before the ārdhadhātuka ending. The verb loses its accent. ⟦धिष्ण्याः⟧: the root is ⟦उष प्लुष⟧, in the sense of burning. In the sense "they heat the thoughts", because the form is laid down ready-made in the rule ⟦सानसिधर्णसिपर्णसितंडुलांकुशचषालेल्वलपल्वलधिष्ण्यशल्या⟧, the suffix ya, the augment nuṭ, the elision of the beginning of the root, the shortening of the preceding word and the rest are all accomplished. Because the suffix is marked with n, the word has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦अवस्तात्⟧: after the word avara the suffix astāti comes by the rule that begins "after words of direction, in the seventh, the fifth". Before that suffix the substitute ava comes by the rule "optionally, of avara" (⟦पा.५-३-४१⟧). The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦उपतिष्ठंते⟧: the root is ⟦ष्ठा⟧, in the sense of ceasing to move. After the root sthā preceded by upa the middle endings come by the rule "and after an intransitive root" (⟦पा. १-३-२६⟧). Because of the connection with a form of the relative pronoun, the verb does not lose its accent.

## Ṛk 4

⟦पुरीष्यासः⟧: mixed with sand; ⟦अग्नयः⟧: the fires of the piled altar; ⟦प्रवणेभिः⟧: accompanied by the spades and the like that are the means of digging the earth; ⟦सजोषसः⟧: being united with one another; ⟦यज्ञं⟧: this Soma sacrifice which is being performed by us and is preceded by the piling of the fire-altar; ⟦जुषंतां⟧: let them attend it. Moreover, ⟦अद्रुहः⟧: you who do no injury; ⟦अनमीवाः⟧: free from disease and the like; ⟦महीः⟧: great; ⟦इषः⟧: nourishments, foods free from disease and the like, let them bestow on us.

⟦पुरीष्यासः⟧: the root is ⟦प्य⟧, in the senses of protecting and filling. While "īṣan" continues from the preceding rule, by the rule ⟦शृष्यभ्यां कित्⟧ (⟦उ. ४-२७⟧) the suffix īṣan comes, and it is marked with k. Because it is marked with k, guṇa is barred, and u comes in by the rule stated in ⟦उदोष्यपूर्वस्येत्युत्वं⟧; it is followed by r. It fills, or it is filled: hence purīṣa. ⟦पुरीषं पृणातेः पूरयतेर्वा⟧ (⟦नि.२-२२⟧), "purīṣa is from pṛṇāti or from pūrayati", says Yāska. Those that deserve it are purīṣyas. The suffix ya comes by the rule ⟦छंदसि चेति⟧. The stem having the name bha by the rule ⟦यचि भमिति⟧, there is elision by the rule ⟦यस्यॆति⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦प्रावणेभिः⟧: from the root ⟦वन षण⟧, in the sense of sharing, by the rule ⟦पुंसि संज्ञायां घः⟧, "gha, in the masculine, in a name, as a rule", the suffix gha comes in the sense of the instrument. Those by which the earth is thoroughly shared, joined, made to cling, are prāvaṇas, digging tools. The change to ṇ is by the rule beginning ⟦प्रनिरंतः⟧ (⟦पा. ८-४-५⟧). By the rule ⟦वनं समासे⟧ (⟦पा. ६-२-१७८⟧) the last syllable is udātta. The accent is the original accent of the second member, which ends in a kṛt suffix. Or else: the root is ⟦प्रज्⟧, in the sense of going. In the sense "they go towards the digging of the earth", the suffix yuc comes by the rule "yuc, after an intransitive root that means moving or sounding" (⟦पा. ३-२-१४८⟧). The accent is that of a suffix marked with c. On the former view it has to be said that the absence of the separation of the members in the Pada text is Vedic. ⟦जुषंतां⟧: it is the form in the imperative, loṭ, of the root ⟦जुषी⟧, in the senses of pleasure and attending. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda, it does not lose its accent. ⟦महीः⟧: after the word mahat the feminine suffix ṅīp comes by the rule in ⟦महच्छब्दामगितश्चेति⟧. The elision of the t is Vedic. By the accent of the suffix the last syllable is udātta.

## Ṛk 5

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦पुरुदंसं⟧: because it is read among the names of action in the list ⟦अपोऽपो दंसो वेष⟧, the word daṃsas denotes action. She who has ⟦पुरूणि⟧, many, deeds; her, of many deeds; ⟦गोः⟧ ⟦सनिं⟧: who procures cows and other cattle; ⟦इळां⟧: the deity of that name, who has the form of a cow; ⟦शश्वत्तमं⟧: without interruption; ⟦हवमानाय⟧: for me, the sacrificer; ⟦साध⟧: accomplish. Moreover, ⟦नः⟧: our ⟦सूनुः⟧, son, and ⟦तनयः⟧, grandson, ⟦स्यात्⟧: comes to be: ⟦ते⟧: that ⟦सुमतिः⟧, good thought, of yours which is thus, ⟦सा⟧: may it, being ⟦विजावा⟧, not barren, ⟦भूतु⟧: be ⟦अस्मे⟧: ours.

⟦शश्वत्तमं⟧: the last syllable is udātta because the word is read in the group named in ⟦उच्छादिषु⟧. ⟦साध⟧: the root is ⟦राध साध⟧, in the sense of accomplishment. Here it includes within itself the sense of the causative. In the imperative, loṭ, by interchange, the class-sign is śap. By the rule ⟦सेर्ह्यपिच्चेति⟧ the substitute hi comes. It is dropped by the rule on hi after a. The verb loses its accent. ⟦विजावा⟧: the root is ⟦जनी⟧, in the sense of coming into being. After it the suffix vanip comes by the rule "they are seen after other roots also". The substitution of ā is by the rule in ⟦विड्वनोरनुनासिकस्यादित्यात्वं⟧. The accent is the original accent of the second member, which ends in a kṛt suffix. There is interchange of gender. ⟦भूतु⟧: the root is ⟦भू⟧, in the sense of being. By the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧ the class-sign śap is dropped. By the rule ⟦भूसुवोस्तिजीति⟧ guṇa is barred.
