## Introduction

The hymn beginning ⟦अग्निं होतारमिति⟧ is the seventh hymn; it has five Ṛks, is in the triṣṭubh metre, is addressed to Agni, and its seer is Gādhin. And so it is stated in the Anukramaṇī: ⟦अग्निं होतारं गाधी हेति⟧, "Agniṃ hotāram: Gādhin indeed." Its ritual use in the prātaranuvāka and in the āśvina-śastra has been stated.

## Ṛk 1

Gādhin praises. O Agni, you who are ⟦गृत्सं⟧, praising, making the praise of the gods; ⟦कविं⟧, wise; ⟦विश्वविदं⟧, possessed of the knowledge of all objects; ⟦अमूरं⟧, not deluded; Agni, possessed of the qualities of moving and the rest: you I, at the ⟦मियेधे⟧, at this sacrifice that is being performed by us, choose forth (⟦प्र वृणे⟧) as ⟦होतारं⟧, the accomplisher of the offering or the caller of the gods: I take you to myself in an eminent way. May he (⟦स⟧), Agni who is of that kind, being ⟦यजीयान्⟧, the best of sacrificers, ⟦यक्षत्⟧, sacrifice to, our (⟦नः⟧) ⟦देवताता⟧, the gods who are connected with us as the ones to be worshipped. Moreover may that Agni, ⟦राये⟧, for wealth, and ⟦वाजाय⟧, for food, ⟦वनते⟧, take to himself, the ⟦मघानि⟧, the oblations, worthy of being bestowed, that are being given by us.

⟦वृणे⟧: the root is vṛ in the sense of taking to oneself. The verb loses its accent. ⟦मियेधे⟧: the augment iy between the m and the e is Vedic. ⟦गृत्सं⟧: the root is ⟦गृधु अभिकांक्षायां⟧, gṛdh in the sense of longing for. The suffix sa comes by the rule ⟦गृधिपण्योर्दकौ च⟧ (⟦उ. ३-६९⟧)**NOTE**{the supplied text gives the Uṇādi reference as 3-69; the reference text has 2. 349: which is to stand?}. In place of the final there is d. By interchange the first syllable is udātta. ⟦अमूरं⟧: it is a form with the niṣṭhā suffix of the root ⟦मुह वैचित्य⟧, muh in the sense of losing one's wits. The r is Vedic. When it is compounded with the negative particle, its accent is that of the negative particle. ⟦यक्षत्⟧: in the leṭ of the root yaj, sip comes by the rule ⟦सिब्बहुलं लेटीति⟧. The augment aṭ comes by the rule on the augments aṭ and āṭ of leṭ. The i is elided by the rule ⟦इतश्च लोप⟧. ⟦देवताता⟧: after the word deva the suffix tātil comes, without change of meaning, by the rule on tātil after sarva and deva. By the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ the substitute ḍā takes the place of the case-ending. The accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦वनते⟧: in the laṭ of the root ⟦वन षण⟧, van and ṣaṇ in the sense of taking to oneself, the ātmanepada comes by interchange. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 2

O Agni, the ladle that is ⟦सुद्युम्नां⟧, of excellent brilliance; ⟦हविष्मतीं⟧, furnished with the oblations, the puroḍāśa and the rest; ⟦रातिनीं⟧, the giver of the oblation; ⟦घृताचीं⟧, accompanied by ghee: the juhū or the upabhṛt; ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦अच्छ⟧, in the direction facing you, I send forth (⟦प्र⟧ ⟦इयर्मि⟧): I impel it. ⟦देवतातिं⟧, the gods, ⟦उराण⟧, making wide, many: ⟦उराण उरु कुर्वाणः⟧ (⟦नि. ६-१७⟧), "urāṇa: making wide", says Yāska. May you, sir, being such, being together with ⟦रातिभिर्वसुभि⟧, with the riches that have come as what is to be given to us, ⟦प्रदक्षिणित्⟧, by going round to the right, resort well (⟦सं⟧ ⟦अश्रेत्⟧): may you attend rightly on this same sacrifice.

⟦हविष्मतीं⟧: "it has havis": so the suffix matup. Since the stem has the designation bha by the rule ⟦तसौ मत्वर्थ⟧, the s does not become ru. The suffix ṅīp comes by the rule on what has uk as its marker. Because the maxim ⟦स्वरविधौ व्यंजनमविद्यमानवदिति⟧, "in a rule of accent a consonant is as if not present", is not resorted to, the suffix matup is not udātta. ⟦इयर्मि⟧: the root is ⟦ऋ गतौ⟧, ṛ in the sense of going; it belongs to the group beginning with hu. The vowel of the reduplication becomes i by the rule on ṛ and pṛ. The substitute iyaṅ comes by the rule ⟦अभ्यासस्यासवर्ण⟧. Because the ending mip is marked with p, there is guṇa. The verb loses its accent. ⟦अच्छ⟧: the vowel is long in the Saṃhitā by the rule ⟦निपातस्यॆति⟧. ⟦रातिनीं⟧: after the root ⟦रा दान⟧, rā in the sense of giving, the suffix kta comes in the neuter in the sense of the action. "It has what is given": so the suffix ini comes by the rule ⟦इनिठनावि⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦घृताचीं⟧: after the root añc preceded by ghṛta the suffix kvin comes by the rule beginning with ṛtvij and dadhṛk. The suffix ṅīp comes by the statement ⟦अंचतेश्चोपसंख्यानमिति⟧, "and añc is to be additionally included". When the stem has the designation bha, the a is elided by the rule on ac. The vowel is lengthened by the rule ⟦चाविति⟧. When the udātta of the suffix ṅīp has been obtained by the accent that follows on the disappearance of an udātta, the first member has the udātta on its final syllable by the rule ⟦चाविति⟧. ⟦प्रदक्षिणित्⟧: "he does pradakṣiṇa": the suffix ṇic, arising by the rule ⟦तत्करोती⟧, "he does that", comes after the word dakṣiṇa, since ṇic arises after a word with a preposition only in the case of saṃgrāmayati, not after any other. After the stem ending in that suffix comes kvip. The augment tuk comes by the rule ⟦ह्रस्वस्य पिति⟧ (⟦पा. ६-१-७१⟧). The absence of the elision of ṇic is Vedic. The accent is that of ṇic. ⟦देवतातिं⟧: after the word deva the suffix tātil comes by the rule ⟦त्सर्वदेवात्तातिलिति⟧. By the rule on a suffix marked with l, the syllable before the suffix is udātta. ⟦उराणः⟧: "he makes wide": so the suffix ṇic comes by the rule ⟦तत्करोती⟧. After the stem ending in it, liṭ comes in the Veda. Because it is a mantra, ām does not come. In its place comes kānac. The ṇi is elided by the rule ⟦णेरनिटीति⟧. The absence of the substitute vara is Vedic. Because the suffix is marked with c, the final syllable is udātta. ⟦अश्रेत्⟧: in the Vedic laṅ of the root ⟦श्रिञ् सेवायामि⟧, śri in the sense of attending on, the vikaraṇa śap is dropped by the rule that in the Veda it is variously so. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 3

O Agni, he who is ⟦त्वोतो⟧, being protected by you, he (⟦सः⟧) becomes joined with a ⟦मनसा⟧, an inner organ, that is ⟦तेजीयसा⟧, exceedingly sharp, possessed of a keen understanding in regard to the good rite. ⟦उत⟧, and moreover, to him — ⟦स्वपत्यस्य⟧: the genitive is in the sense of the accusative — wealth accompanied by excellent offspring ⟦शिक्ष⟧, bestow. The verb śikṣ has the sense of giving, because it is recited among the verbs of giving that begin with dāti. O Agni, in the ⟦प्रभूता⟧, in the might, the greatness, of you (⟦ते⟧) who are ⟦शिक्षोः⟧, wishing to be able to bestow the fruit that is desired, and ⟦रायो नृतमस्य⟧, in a surpassing degree the leader, the giver, of wealth, may we be. Moreover may we, ⟦सुस्तुतयः⟧, having excellent praise addressed to you, become (⟦भूयाम⟧) the vessel of ⟦वस्वः⟧, of wealth.

⟦तेजीयसा⟧: after the word tejitṛ the suffix īyasun comes in the sense of excess. The suffix tṛc is elided by the rule on tṛ before iṣṭhan, imanic and īyasun. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦त्वोतः⟧: the root is ⟦अव रक्षणादिषु⟧, av in the senses of protecting and the rest. Before the niṣṭhā suffix, ūṭh comes by the rule ⟦ज्वरत्वरस्रिव्यविमवामुपधाया⟧. The a is elided.**NOTE**{the supplied text reads ⟦अलोपः⟧, "elision of a"; the reference text has pūrvalopaḥ, "elision of the preceding": which is to stand?} ⟦त्वया ऊतः⟧, "protected by you": the compound is made by dividing the rule so as to read "tṛtīyā" alone. The elision of the last vowel with what follows it is Vedic. By the rule ⟦तृतीया कर्मणीति⟧ the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦शिक्ष⟧: this root, ⟦शिक्ष विद्योपादान⟧, śikṣ in the sense of acquiring knowledge, has here the sense of giving. After it, in loṭ, the parasmaipada comes by interchange. ⟦शिक्षोः⟧: when the suffix san comes after the root śak in the sense of being able, the augment iṭ is prohibited by the rule on a root of one vowel in the original teaching. By the rule ⟦मीमाघुरभलभशकपतपदामच इसिति⟧ the substitute is takes the place of the vowel of the root. The reduplication is elided by the rule ⟦अत्र लोपोऽभ्यासस्ये⟧. The suffix u comes by the rule ⟦सनाशंसभिक्ष उरि⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦प्रभूतौ⟧: after the root bhū in the sense of being, the suffix ktin comes in the sense of the action. By the rule ⟦तादौ च निति⟧ and what follows, the preposition keeps its original accent. ⟦भूयाम⟧: in the benedictive liṅ of the root bhū in the sense of being, the augment yāsuṭ comes by the rule ⟦किदाशिषीति⟧. Because it is sārvadhātuka by the rule ⟦छंदस्युभयथेति⟧, the s is elided. The accent is that of the augment. Or else: after that root, in the liṅ expressing a prayer, the augment yāsuṭ comes. The vikaraṇa śap is dropped by the rule that in the Veda it is variously so. Because the stem does not end in short a, the substitute iy does not come. ⟦सुष्टुतयः⟧: the second member has the udātta on its final syllable by the rule on a bahuvrīhi after the negative particle and su. ⟦वस्वः⟧: because the teaching of an augment is not invariable, there is no augment num. Because a rule that is stated by means of a technical name is not invariable, there is no guṇa. The semivowel is substituted.

## Ṛk 4

O Agni, the ⟦यज्यवो⟧, the sacrificers, the adhvaryu and the others, the ⟦जनासो⟧, the people, who belong to you, the ⟦देवस्य⟧, the shining one, have indeed placed (⟦दधिरे⟧ ⟦हि⟧) in you (⟦त्वे⟧), by casting in the oblations, ⟦भूरीणि⟧, many, ⟦अनीका⟧, lustres in the form of flames which spread everywhere in the manner of an army: they have indeed set them there; that is to say, they have made them. Do you (⟦स⟧), being such, bring here (⟦आ⟧ ⟦वह⟧), to this rite, the ⟦देवतातिं⟧, the gods who are to be worshipped: call them. O ⟦यविष्ठ⟧, O youngest one, ⟦यत्⟧, for which reason, ⟦आद्य⟧, in this rite, you worship (⟦यजासि⟧) the ⟦दिव्यं⟧, that which is in heaven, which belongs to the gods, ⟦शर्धः⟧, lustre; "therefore call the gods": this is to be supplied.

⟦दधिरे⟧: a form in liṭ of the root dhā. Because of the connection with ⟦हि⟧ it does not lose its accent. Because the ending is marked with c, the final syllable is udātta. ⟦यज्यवः⟧: the root is ⟦यज देवपूजादिषु⟧, yaj in the senses of worshipping the gods and the rest. The suffix yuc comes by the rule ⟦यजिमनिशुंधिदसिजनिभ्यो युजिति⟧. By interchange the first syllable is udātta. ⟦देवतातिं⟧: after the word deva the suffix tātil comes, without change of meaning, by the rule on tātil after sarva and deva. By the rule on a suffix marked with l, the syllable before the suffix is udātta. ⟦यजासि⟧: in the leṭ of the root yaj the augment āṭ comes. Because of the connection with yad it does not lose its accent.

## Ṛk 5

O Agni, ⟦यजथाय⟧, for the sake of worship, the ⟦देवाः⟧, the shining priests, ⟦निषादयंतो⟧, dwelling, at the ⟦मियेधे⟧, the medha, the sacrifice, ⟦यत्⟧, since, they anointed (⟦अनजन्⟧) you (⟦त्वा⟧), the ⟦होतारं⟧, the caller of the gods: sprinkled you with oblations of ghee; or else: the priests, ⟦निषादयंतो⟧, making you, the ⟦होतारं⟧, the accomplisher of the offering, sit down on the barhis, sprinkled you with ghee; therefore do you (⟦सः⟧ ⟦त्वं⟧), being of that kind, ⟦इह⟧, in this rite, ⟦बोधि⟧, know, "I am the ⟦अविता⟧, the protector, of us (⟦नः⟧)". Moreover in ⟦नः⟧ ⟦तनूषु⟧, in our offspring, those born of our bodies, ⟦श्रवांसि⟧, foods, ⟦अधि⟧ ⟦धेहि⟧: lay them down in abundance.

⟦अनजन्⟧: the root is añjū, in the senses of making manifest, going and smearing. After it, in laṅ, the vikaraṇa śnam comes because the root belongs to the group beginning with rudh. Because the form is Vedic, the a is not elided. The penultimate is elided by the rule ⟦अनिदितामित्यु⟧. ⟦निषादयंतः⟧: the root is sad, in the senses of breaking up, going and sinking down; it belongs to the group beginning with cur. The change to ṣ comes by the rule ⟦सदेरप्रतेरिति⟧. Or else: the suffix ṇic comes in the causal sense. When the suffix śatṛ has become anudātta by the accent of a sārvadhātuka that replaces a tense-affix, the accent is that of ṇic. ⟦बोधि⟧: the root is budh in the sense of understanding. In loṭ the vikaraṇa śap is dropped by the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧. The substitute hi takes the place of sip by the rule ⟦सेर्ह्यपिच्चेति⟧. Because by the rule ⟦वा छंदसीति⟧ it is optionally marked with p, there is guṇa. The substitute dhi takes the place of hi by the rule on hi after hu and after a root ending in a jhal consonant. The elision of the dh is Vedic. The verb loses its accent. ⟦धेहि⟧: a form in loṭ of the root dhā.
