## Introduction

The sūkta beginning ⟦अग्ने दिव⟧ is the thirteenth, of five Ṛks, and belongs to Viśvāmitra. On it the Anukramaṇikā says this: ⟦अग्ने दिवो वैराजमुपांत्याग्नेंद्रीति⟧, that is, the sūkta beginning "agne divaḥ" is in the virāj metre, and its last Ṛk but one is addressed to Agni and Indra. The metre is virāj. The Ṛk beginning "agna indraś ca" has Indra and Agni for its deity. The rest are addressed to Agni. In the Agniṣṭoma, in the ājya-śastra, the Ṛk beginning "agna indraś ca" is the offering verse, because it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦अग्न इंद्रश्च दाशुषो दुरोण इति याज्या⟧ (⟦आ. ५-९⟧), "the offering verse is 'agna indraś ca dāśuṣo duroṇe'".

## Ṛk 1

Viśvāmitra praises. O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦विश्ववेदाः⟧: possessed of the knowledge of all objects, ⟦प्रचेताः⟧: possessed of knowledge in the matter of the rites, you ⟦दिवः⟧: of the deity who presides over the world of heaven, ⟦सूनुः⟧ ⟦असि⟧: are the son. ⟦उत⟧: and also, ⟦पृथिव्याः⟧: of the deity who presides over the earth, ⟦तना⟧: you are the son. Moreover, ⟦चिकित्वः⟧: O you who are conscious, O Agni, ⟦इह⟧: in this rite which is being performed by us, ⟦देवान्⟧: Indra and the other gods who are worthy of sacrifice, ⟦ऋधक्⟧: separately, in due order, ⟦यज⟧: honour by the giving of the oblation.

⟦असि⟧: of the root ⟦अस⟧, in the sense of being, in the present, laṭ, the s is elided by the rule ⟦तासस्त्योर्लोप⟧. The verb loses its accent. ⟦विश्ववेदाः⟧: by the rule ⟦बहुव्रीहौ विश्वं संज्ञायामिति⟧ the word viśva has the udātta on its last syllable.

## Ṛk 2

⟦विद्वान्⟧: knowing the rites, the ⟦अग्निः⟧, Agni, who ⟦वीर्याणि⟧: the capacities that take the form of wealth in cattle, sons and the like, ⟦सनोति⟧: gives to the sacrificer; and the Agni who, ⟦भूषन्⟧: adorning, embellishing, the whole world by his own brilliance, ⟦अमृताय⟧: to the god, Indra and the others, who is free from the nature of dying, ⟦वाजं⟧: the food that consists of the oblation, ⟦सनोति⟧: bestows; ⟦पुरुक्षो⟧: O you who are furnished with food of many kinds, the puroḍāśa and the rest, O Agni, ⟦सः⟧: you, being such, ⟦इह⟧: in this sacrifice, which belongs to ⟦नः⟧, us, ⟦देवान्⟧: the gods who are worthy of sacrifice, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦वह⟧: summon.

⟦सनोति⟧: the root is ⟦षणु⟧, in the sense of giving. Because it belongs to the class beginning with tan, the suffix is u. The accent is that of the suffix. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda, the verb does not lose its accent. ⟦भूषन्⟧: the root is ⟦भूष⟧, in the sense of adorning. It includes within itself the sense of the causative. The suffix śatṛ being anudātta by the accent rule for the sārvadhātuka substitutes of la, the accent of the root remains. ⟦वह⟧: it is the form of the root vah in the imperative, loṭ. ⟦पुरुक्षो⟧: he who has many kṣudhs, that is, foods, is purukṣu. The elision of the final is Vedic. There is guṇa by the rule ⟦संबुद्धौ चेति⟧.

## Ṛk 3

⟦अमूरः⟧: not deluded, all-knowing; ⟦क्षयन्⟧: ruling over the whole world; for that very reason ⟦पुरुश्चंद्रः⟧: that is, purucandra, possessed of much gold; or else, one whose own nature is much brightness. Accompanied by ⟦वाजैः⟧, strengths, and by ⟦नमःभिः⟧, foods, that ⟦अग्निः⟧, Agni, ⟦विश्वजन्ये⟧: the two who bring forth all; or else, the two by whom all is to be brought forth, to be produced; ⟦देवी⟧: shining, ⟦अमृते⟧: free from the nature of dying, ⟦द्यावापृथिवी⟧: heaven and earth, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦भाति⟧: he lights up on every side.

⟦द्यावापृथिवी⟧: for the word div the substitute dyāvā comes by the rule ⟦दिवो द्यावेति⟧, with the udātta on its first syllable. The word pṛthivī has the udātta on its last syllable because it ends in the suffix ṅīṣ. By the rule ⟦देवताद्वंद्वे⟧, "also in a dvandva of names of deities", both keep their original accent. ⟦विश्वजन्ने⟧: in the bahuvrīhi, by the rule "viśva, in a name", the first member has the udātta on its last syllable. In the tatpuruṣa, however, the accent has to be considered. ⟦आ भाति⟧: the root is ⟦भा⟧, in the sense of shining. It includes within itself the sense of the causative. ⟦क्षयन्⟧: the root is ⟦क्षि⟧, in the sense of lordship. The suffix śatṛ being anudātta by the accent rule for the sārvadhātuka substitutes of la, the accent is that of the root. ⟦पुरुश्चंद्रः⟧: the augment suṭ comes by the rule ⟦ह्रस्वाच्चंद्रोत्तरपदे मंत्र⟧. By the change to the palatal class it becomes ś. In the section on the udātta of the last syllable of the second member, by the supplementary statement beginning ⟦त्रिचक्रादीनां⟧, "tricakra and the rest are to be added, in the Veda", the last syllable of the second member is udātta. Or else the last syllable is udātta by the rule on the compound.

## Ṛk 4

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, you and ⟦इंद्रः⟧, Indra, both, ⟦देवा⟧: the two gods, you two, ⟦अमर्धंता⟧: not doing harm to the sacrifice by not coming, and being so, ⟦सुतवतः⟧: who is performing the pressing of the Soma, ⟦दाशुषः⟧: who has given the oblation, of the sacrificer, ⟦इह⟧ ⟦दुरोणे⟧: in his house here, aiming at this ⟦यज्ञं⟧, sacrifice, which is being performed, ⟦सोमपेयाय⟧: for the drinking of the Soma, ⟦उप⟧ ⟦यातं⟧: come near.

⟦यातं⟧: it is the form in the imperative, loṭ, of the root ⟦या⟧, in the sense of reaching. The verb loses its accent. ⟦अमर्धंता⟧: it is the form before the suffix śatṛ of the root ⟦मृधु⟧, in the sense of harming. It is compounded with the negative particle. The substitute ḍā takes the place of the case-ending. In the tatpuruṣa the accent is that of the negative particle. ⟦सोमपेयाय⟧: the root is ⟦पा⟧, in the sense of drinking. The suffix yat comes by the rule ⟦अचो यदिति⟧. By the rule "ī, before yat" the substitute is the vowel ī. There is the guṇa that is conditioned by an ārdhadhātuka suffix. The accent is the original accent of the second member, which ends in a kṛt suffix.

## Ṛk 5

⟦सहसः सूनो⟧: O son of strength, ⟦जातवेदः⟧: O you in whom wisdom is born, O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦नित्यः⟧: imperishable, ⟦ऊती⟧: with protection, ⟦सधस्थानि⟧: the worlds that are the dwelling-places of living beings, ⟦महयमानः⟧: attending on them, you ⟦अपां⟧ ⟦दुरोणे⟧: in the place of the waters, the mid-region, ⟦समिध्यसे⟧: are fully kindled.

⟦दुरोणे⟧: from the root ⟦अव⟧, in the sense of protecting, comes the Uṇādi suffix nak. By the rule that begins with jvara and tvara there is ūṭh. There is guṇa. ⟦मरोण इति गृहनाम । दुरवा भवंति⟧ (⟦नि. ४-५⟧), "duroṇa is a name for a house; houses are hard to guard", says Yāska. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦महयमानः⟧: the root is ⟦मह⟧, in the sense of honouring. By interchange the suffix is śānac. The suffix śānac being anudātta by the accent rule for the sārvadhātuka substitutes of la, the accent is that of the suffix ṇic. ⟦ऊती⟧: the root is ⟦अव⟧, in the senses of protecting and the rest. Because the form is laid down ready-made in the rule beginning ⟦ऊतियूतिजूती⟧, the last syllable is udātta. By the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ the long vowel for the two like vowels takes the place of the instrumental ending.
