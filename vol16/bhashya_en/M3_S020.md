## Introduction

The hymn beginning ⟦अग्निमुषसमिति⟧ is the eighth hymn; it has five Ṛks and is in the triṣṭubh metre. On it the Anukramaṇikā says this: ⟦अग्निमुषसमाद्यांते वैश्वदेव्याविति⟧, "Agnim uṣasam: the first and the last are addressed to the Viśvedevas." Because the word ha is used in ⟦गाथी हेति⟧, Gāthin is the seer of this hymn also. The first and the fifth Ṛks have the Viśvedevas for their deity; the remaining three are addressed to Agni. The ritual use of this hymn has been stated. In the Marutvatīya-śastra the Ṛk beginning ⟦ग्निर्नेते⟧ is a dhāyyā. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦अग्निर्नेता त्वं सोम क्रतुभिः पिन्वंत्यप इति धाय्याः⟧ (⟦अ. ५-१४⟧), "'Agnir netā', 'tvaṃ soma kratubhiḥ', 'pinvanty apaḥ': these are the dhāyyās."**NOTE**{in this sūtra the supplied text reads kratubhiḥ in the second verse-opening; the reference text has ṛtubhiḥ}

## Ṛk 1

Gāthin speaks. The ⟦वह्निः⟧, Agni, the conveyer of the oblations, at the ⟦विउष्टिषु⟧, at the dawnings of the dawns, calls (⟦हवते⟧) ⟦अग्निं⟧, the god who is to be worshipped; ⟦उषसं⟧, the deity who presides over the dawn; ⟦अश्विना⟧, the two Aśvin gods; ⟦दधिक्रां⟧ — there is a certain god named Dadhikrāvan: that Dadhikrāvan —; these gods he calls with ⟦उक्तैः⟧, with śastras, with hymns that are the means of reciting praise and consist of mantras that are not sung. May all the gods (⟦देवाः⟧), ⟦सुज्योतिषः⟧, shining well, ⟦वावशानाः⟧, desiring, our (⟦नः⟧) ⟦अध्वरं⟧, this sacrifice that belongs to us, having become ⟦सजोषसः⟧, united with one another, hear (⟦शृण्वंतु⟧) that call.

⟦दधिक्रां⟧: the root is kram in the sense of setting down the feet. When dadhi is the accompanying word, the suffix viṭ comes by the rule ⟦जनसनखनक्रमगम⟧. The long ā comes by the rule ⟦विड्वनोरनुनासिकस्यादि⟧. The second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent.**NOTE**{after dadhikrām the reference text explains vyuṣṭiṣu as well (root uchī, the suffix ktin in the sense of the action, ṣ by the rule beginning with vraśc, the accent of the preposition); the supplied text does not have this} ⟦हवते⟧: the samprasāraṇa of the root hve comes by the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧. ⟦सुज्योतिषः⟧: when it is analysed as "shining well", after the root ⟦द्युत दीप्ता⟧, dyut in the sense of shining, the suffix isin comes, and j in place of the d, by the rule ⟦द्युतेरिसिन्नादेश जः⟧ (⟦उ. २-१११⟧)**NOTE**{the supplied text gives the Uṇādi reference as 2-111; the reference text has 2. 267: which is to stand?}; the second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. On the view that it is a bahuvrīhi, however, the second member would have the udātta on its final syllable by the rule on a bahuvrīhi after the negative particle and su; as there is the udātta on the final syllable in ⟦सुज्योतिषो अक्तवस्ता⟧ … ⟦ष्युः⟧ (⟦ऋग्वे १०-८९-१५⟧), "may the nights of good light prevail over them". Therefore here it is not that compound, but a tatpuruṣa only. ⟦शृण्वंतु⟧: after the root ⟦श्रु श्रवण⟧, śru in the sense of hearing, the vikaraṇa śnu comes by the rule ⟦शृ चेति⟧, and together with it the substitute ⟦शृ⟧ takes the place of the root. The verb loses its accent. ⟦वावशानाः⟧: it is a form, with the elision of yaṅ, of the root ⟦वश कांतावि⟧, vaś in the sense of desiring. The vowel of the reduplication is lengthened by the rule ⟦दीर्घोऽकित⟧. The suffix śānac comes by interchange. The accent is that of a suffix marked with c.

## Ṛk 2

O Agni, ⟦ते⟧, yours, are ⟦त्री⟧ ⟦वाजिना⟧, three foods, consisting of ghee, plants and Soma; ⟦त्री षधस्था⟧, three places, the dhiṣṇya hearths, the āgnīdhrīya and the rest. Or else: three places called ekāha, ahīna and sattra. Or else: the three worlds. ⟦ऋतजात⟧, you who are born in the ṛtas, in the sacrifices which are truth, O Agni, ⟦ते⟧, yours, are three (⟦तिस्रः⟧) ⟦जिह्वाः⟧, tongues, the gārhapatya and the rest, which are ⟦पूर्विः⟧, the fillers of the bellies of the gods.**NOTE**{before "the fillers of the bellies of the gods" the reference text has in square brackets a further passage on the three kinds of tongues, with a quotation from the śruti on the three Agnis; the supplied text does not have it} O Agni, ⟦ते⟧, yours, are three ⟦देववाताः⟧, desired by the gods, ⟦तन्नः⟧, three bodies consisting of Pavamāna, Pāvaka and Śuci. Do you, being such, ⟦अप्रयुच्छन्⟧, not being heedless, having become attentive, with those (⟦ताभिः⟧) bodies protect (⟦पाहि⟧) the ⟦गिरः⟧, the utterances, the stotras, śastras and the rest, of us (⟦नः⟧), the praisers.

⟦तिस्रः⟧: the ending jas is udātta by the rule ⟦तिसृभ्यो जस⟧. ⟦जिह्वाः⟧: the root is lih in the sense of tasting. "With these they lick the flavours": so the suffix van comes, by its being given ready-made in the rule ⟦यह्वजिह्वाग्रीवाप्वामीवा⟧. In place of the l there is j. There is no guṇa. By interchange the final syllable is udātta. ⟦देववाताः⟧: the root is vā in the senses of going and hurting. After it the suffix kta comes in the sense of the object. By the rule ⟦कर्मणीति⟧, on an instrumental before a past participle of the object, the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦पाहि⟧: a form in loṭ of the root ⟦पा रक्षण⟧, pā in the sense of protecting. The verb loses its accent. ⟦अप्रयुच्छन्⟧: the root is ⟦युच्छ प्रमादे⟧, yucch in the sense of being heedless. The accent is that of the negative particle.

## Ṛk 3

⟦देव⟧, O shining one, ⟦जातवेदः⟧, you in whom knowledge has arisen, ⟦स्वधावः⟧, you who possess food, O Agni, the gods who belong to you, the ⟦अमृतस्य⟧, the one free from the nature of dying, have placed together (⟦संदधुः⟧) in you (⟦त्वे⟧) ⟦भूरीणि⟧, many, ⟦नाम⟧, lustres. Moreover, ⟦विश्वमिन्व⟧, you who satisfy all, ⟦पृष्टबंधो⟧, you who are the kinsman of the praisers who are the object of the question about the fruit they look for, O Agni, the ⟦पूर्वीः⟧, the very many, ⟦माया⟧ of the ⟦मायिनां⟧, of the asuras, which (⟦याः⟧) the gods placed together in you, joined to you: these are in you. Therefore, having warded off the māyās of the possessors of māyā who cause obstruction to the sacrifice, protect our sacrifice; that is the sense.**NOTE**{after this the reference text adds that ca is expletive (ca pūraṇaḥ); the supplied text does not have it}

⟦स्वधावः⟧: "he has svadhā": so the suffix matup. By the rule ⟦मतुवसो रुरिति⟧ ru takes the place of the n. ⟦अमृतस्य⟧: the second member has the udātta on its first syllable by the rule ⟦जरमरमित्रमृता⟧, on these words after the negative particle. ⟦नाम⟧: the root is ⟦णम प्रह्वत्वे⟧, nam in the sense of bowing. By its being given ready-made, as ending in the suffix manin, in the rule beginning ⟦नामन् सीमन्⟧, there are the elision of the m and the long vowel. "They make all the men bow who are their own seat": hence nāmāni, lustres. The case-ending is dropped by the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧. Because the suffix is marked with n, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦मायिनां⟧: "they have māyās": so the suffix ini comes, because the word belongs to the group beginning with vrīhi. There is elision by the rule ⟦यस्यॆति⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦विश्वमिन्व⟧: after the root ⟦इवि व्याप्तिप्रीणनयोरि⟧, iv in the senses of pervading and gladdening, when the word viśva is the accompanying word, the suffix aṇ comes by the rule on aṇ when the object accompanies. Since the augment num has been obtained by the rule on num for roots marked with i, at the stage of the original teaching of the root, the guṇa of a light penultimate does not take place. "He gladdens all": hence viśvaminva. The accusative ending is not dropped, by the rule ⟦तत्पुरुषे कृति बहुलमिति⟧. Because it is a vocative it loses its accent. ⟦संदधुः⟧: a form in liṭ, before the ending us, of the root ḍudhāñ, in the senses of holding and nourishing. Because of the connection with a form of yad it does not lose its accent. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦पृष्टबंधो⟧: before the niṣṭhā suffix in the sense of the object, after the root pracch in the sense of wishing to know, the samprasāraṇa comes by the rule beginning ⟦ग्रहिज्यावयी⟧. The change to ṣ comes by the rule beginning with vraśc. After the root ⟦बंध बंधन⟧, bandh in the sense of binding, the suffix u comes by the rule beginning ⟦शृस्वस्निही⟧. It is a compound with a genitive. Because it is a vocative it loses its accent.

## Ṛk 4

This Agni, who is the ⟦नेता⟧, the controller, as the ruler within, of the ⟦क्षितीनां⟧, of men, and of the ⟦दैवीनां⟧, of the peoples that belong to the gods; for this there is an example, ⟦ऋतुपा भग इव⟧: as the sun, the protector of the seasons, spring and the rest, is the one who sets all in motion by bestowing his own light, so is he; and the Agni who is ⟦ऋतवा⟧, of true action, ⟦वृत्रहा⟧, the slayer of Vṛtra, ⟦सनयः⟧, everlasting, ancient, ⟦विश्ववेदाः⟧, possessing the knowledge of all objects, all-knowing, ⟦देवः⟧, shining: may he (⟦सः⟧), Agni who is of that kind, lead across (⟦पर्षत्⟧), having passed beyond (⟦अति⟧) ⟦विश्वा⟧ ⟦दुरिता⟧, all evils, the ⟦गृणंतं⟧, the praiser who is making praise addressed to him: may he bring him to the further shore.

⟦नेता⟧: a form with the suffix tṛc of the root ⟦णीञ् प्रापण⟧, nī in the sense of leading. The accent is that of a suffix marked with c. ⟦क्षितीनां⟧: the root is ⟦क्षि निवासगत्योः⟧, kṣi in the senses of dwelling and going. The suffix ktic comes by the rule on ktic and kta in a name. The final syllable is udātta by the rule ⟦नामन्यतरस्यामि⟧. ⟦दैवीनां⟧: after the word deva the suffix añ comes, in the sense of connection, by the rule on yañ and añ after deva. After añ the suffix ṅīp comes by the rule on stems marked with ṭ and ending in ḍha, aṇ, añ and the rest. Because the suffix is marked with ñ, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦ऋतुपाः⟧: after the root ⟦पा रक्षण⟧, pā in the sense of protecting, the suffix vic comes by the rule that such suffixes are seen after other roots too. "He protects the seasons": the compound is made by the rule on an accompanying word that is not a finite verb (⟦पा. २-२-१९⟧). The second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦वृत्रहा⟧: the root is ⟦हन हिंसागत्योः⟧, han in the senses of injuring and going. The suffix kvip comes by the rule ⟦वृत्रेषु क्विबिति⟧, on brahman, bhrūṇa and vṛtra. ⟦विश्ववेदाः⟧: by the rule ⟦बहुव्रीहौ विश्वं संज्ञायामिति⟧ the first member has the udātta on its final syllable. ⟦पर्षत्⟧: in the leṭ of the root pṝ, in the senses of protecting and filling, sip comes by the rule ⟦सिब्बहुलमिति⟧. There is the augment aṭ. The i is elided by the rule ⟦इतश्च लोप⟧. Because it stands at the beginning of the pāda it does not lose its accent. ⟦गृणंतं⟧: the root is gṝ in the sense of sounding. In place of laṭ there is śatṛ. Because the root belongs to the group beginning with krī, the vikaraṇa is śnā. Because the form ends in śatṛ, the ā is elided by the rule on the ā of śnā and of reduplicated stems. The accent is that of the suffix śatṛ.

## Ṛk 5

O all you gods, Dadhikrāvan and the rest, I, the hotṛ, call ⟦दधिक्रां⟧, Dadhikrāvan, ⟦अग्निं⟧, and ⟦उषसं⟧ ⟦च⟧ ⟦देवीं⟧, and the deity who presides over the dawn, ⟦बृहस्पतिं⟧, and the ⟦देवं⟧, the shining, ⟦सवितारं⟧, the god who impels the whole world; ⟦अश्विना⟧, the two Aśvin gods; Mitra and Varuṇa; and ⟦भगं⟧, the Sun; ⟦वसून्⟧, the eight Vasus; ⟦रुद्रान्⟧, the eleven Rudras; ⟦आदित्यान्⟧, the twelve Ādityas: all these, you who eat the oblations, ⟦इह⟧, in our rite, ⟦हुवे⟧: I call you so that you may accept the oblation.

⟦दधिक्रां⟧: "he holds", hence dadhi. The suffix ki comes by the rule beginning ⟦आदृगमहने⟧. When dadhi is the accompanying word, the suffix viṭ comes after the root kram by the rule beginning ⟦र्जनसने⟧. The long ā comes by the rule ⟦विड्वनोरि⟧. ⟦देवीं⟧: the wife of a deva is a devī. The suffix ṅīṣ comes by the rule on a name that arises from union with a male. There is elision by the rule ⟦यस्यॆति⟧. By the accent of the suffix the final syllable is udātta. ⟦बृहस्पतिं⟧: by the statement ⟦तद्बृहतोः करपत्योश्चोरदेवतयोः सुट्⟧ and what follows, "after tad and bṛhat before kara and pati, when a thief and a deity are meant, there are suṭ and the elision of t", when the word pati follows there are the elision of the t of the word bṛhat and the augment suṭ. By the rule ⟦उभे वनस्पत्यादिषु युगपदि⟧ both words have the udātta on their first syllable. ⟦सवितारं⟧: when the suffix tṛc comes after the root sū in the sense of impelling, the augment iṭ comes by the rule on an ārdhadhātuka suffix beginning with a val consonant. Because the suffix is marked with c, the final syllable is udātta. In ⟦रुद्रा आदित्या⟧, in the Saṃhitā, the n of the ending śas becomes ru by the rule ⟦दीर्घादटि समान⟧ and what follows, "after a long vowel, before a sound of the group aṭ, in the same pāda". Then the preceding a-vowel is nasalised by the rule ⟦आतोटि नित्यमि⟧. The substitute y takes the place of ru by the rule ⟦भो भगो⟧ ⟦अघो अपूर्वस्य योशीति⟧. It is elided by the rule ⟦लोपः शाकल्यस्यॆति⟧. Because the elision of y is treated as not having taken effect, the junction of the vowels does not take place. Hence it becomes ⟦रुद्रा आदित्या⟧. In the same way, within one pāda, when a sound of the group aṭ follows after ā, the substitutions for the n, namely ru, y and the nasalised sound homogeneous with what precedes, are to be inferred. ⟦हुवे⟧: in the laṭ of the root hve the samprasāraṇa comes by the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧. The ending iṭ becomes e by the rule ⟦त आत्मनेपदाना⟧. There is the substitute uvaṅ. The verb loses its accent.
