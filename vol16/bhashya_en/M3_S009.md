## Introduction

The ninth sūkta, beginning ⟦सखायस्त्व⟧, has nine Ṛks and is Viśvāmitra's. Here is the Anukramaṇikā: ⟦सखायो नव बार्हतं त्रिष्टुबंतमिति⟧, "'Friends': nine, in bṛhatī, ending with a triṣṭubh". The last is a triṣṭubh; the remaining eight are bṛhatīs. This sūkta is applied in the bṛhatī metre of the Prātaranuvāka and of the Āśvina-śastra. In the section beginning ⟦अथैतस्या⟧ it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦सखायस्त्वियमग्निरग्न आ याहि⟧ (⟦आ. ४-१३⟧).

## Ṛk 1

Viśvāmitra praises. O Agni! We, ⟦सखायः⟧, friends because we are of service by giving the oblations of Soma, ghee and the rest, ⟦मर्तासः⟧, men, the priests, ⟦ववृमहे⟧, choose, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, ⟦देवं⟧, a god who is such as this: ⟦अपां नपातं⟧, the grandson of the waters, ⟦सुभगं⟧, endowed with fine wealth, ⟦सुदीदितिं⟧, of beautiful brightness, ⟦सुप्रतूर्तिं⟧, of fine crossing, easy to approach for those who perform the rites, ⟦अनेहसं⟧, free from harm; we choose you ⟦ऊतये⟧, for protection.

⟦सखायः⟧: the root is khyā, "to tell". By ⟦समाने ख्यश्चोदात्त⟧, with ḍit continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix iṇ comes after khyā when samāna is the accompanying word. Because it is ḍit the last vowel with what follows it is elided. Together with it the y is elided. By ⟦समानस्य छंदस्यमूर्धो⟧… sa takes the place of samāna. The accompanying word is udātta. With ṇit continuing from the preceding rule, by ⟦सख्युरसंबुद्धौ⟧ (⟦पा. ७-१-९२⟧) the ending is treated as ṇit. There are vṛddhi and the substitute āy. The udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦ववृमहे⟧: the root is vṛ, "to share in". By ⟦छंदसि लुङ्गिट्⟧ the liṭ stands in the sense of the present. Because the root belongs to the group beginning with kṛ, there is no augment iṭ. The accent is lost. ⟦ऊतये⟧: of the root av, "to protect", with udātta continuing from the preceding rule, the word is laid down ready-made as ending in ktin by the rule beginning with ūti and yūti, and so the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦अपां⟧: the case-ending is udātta by ⟦ऊडिदमिति⟧. ⟦सुभगं⟧: by ⟦आद्युदात्तं द्वैच् छंदसी⟧ the latter member has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦सुप्रतूर्तिं⟧: by ⟦क्रत्वाद-यश्च⟧ the latter member has the udātta on its first syllable.

## Ṛk 2

O Agni! ⟦कायमानः⟧, desiring, ⟦वना⟧, the forests, ⟦यत्⟧, since, having left**NOTE**{the word translated "having left" is printed vijāya, also in the reference text; vihāya seems to be meant} them, you ⟦अजगन्⟧, went, to ⟦मात्यरपो⟧, the waters who are mothers; because you have entered the waters you are quenched; ⟦तत्⟧, therefore, ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦निवर्तनं⟧, your staying there altogether — and by that, destruction is indicated — that destruction ⟦न⟧ ⟦प्रमृषे⟧; it is not endured, it is not borne**NOTE**{this sentence is printed na vināśo na pramṛṣe, sa pramṛṣyate, also in the reference text; "that destruction is not endured" seems to be meant}. Why? To that he says: ⟦यत्⟧, because, ⟦दूरे⟧ ⟦सन्⟧, existing unseen, you ⟦अभवः⟧ ⟦इह⟧, here, in the pieces of wood in the form of the araṇis that belong to us; by churning you come to be near us in a mere moment. Therefore your staying far away does not please us. On this meaning Yāska says: ⟦कायमानश्चायमानः कामयमान इति वा । वनानि त्वं यन्मात्यरपोऽगम उपशाम्यन् । न तत्ते अग्ने प्रमृष्यते निवर्तनं दूरे यत्सन्निह भवसि जायमानः⟧, "'kāyamāna' is regarding, or else desiring. 'The forests, when you went to the waters, the mothers, being quenched: that withdrawal of yours, O Agni, is not endured, since, being far away, you come to be here, being born'" (⟦नि. ४-१४⟧).

⟦काय-मानः⟧: the root is kam, "to desire". After kam comes ṇiṅ; śānac takes the place of laṭ. The elision of the m is Vedic. ⟦अजगन्⟧: in the laṅ of the root gam, "to go", ślu takes the place of śap by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧. The si is elided by the rule beginning ⟦हल्या⟧. The n is by ⟦मो नो धातोरिति⟧. Because it is connected with the word yad, the accent is not lost. The accent is that of the augment aṭ. ⟦प्रमृषे⟧: the root is mṛṣ, "to endure". By transposition the suffix ta is in the sense of the object. The t is elided by ⟦लोपस्त आत्मनेपदेष्टिति⟧. The accent is that of the root. ⟦अभवः⟧: by ⟦छंदसि लुज्जि ज्लिट⟧ the laṅ stands in the sense of laṭ. Because it is connected with yad, the accent is not lost.

## Ṛk 3

O Agni! ⟦तृष्टं⟧, the thirst, the desire, of the praiser, in order to make it fruitful by giving the fruit that befits it, you ⟦अति⟧ ⟦ववक्षिथ⟧; you wish exceedingly to carry it. ⟦अथ⟧ ⟦एव⟧, and even so, you ⟦सुमना असि⟧; you remain with a contented mind. Moreover: of the sixteen priests ⟦येषां⟧, in whose, ⟦सख्ये⟧, friendship, you ⟦श्रितो⟧ ⟦असि⟧, abide, among them ⟦अन्वे⟧, some, the twelve priests beginning with the adhvaryu, ⟦प्रप्र⟧ ⟦यंति⟧; they go forward to offer. ⟦अन्य⟧, others, the four beginning with the udgātṛ, ⟦परि⟧ ⟦आसते⟧; they remain seated all around.

⟦ववक्षितथ⟧: it is the form in san of the root vah, "to carry". That the reduplicative syllable does not take i by ⟦सन्यत⟧ is Vedic. After the stem ending in that comes liṭ in the sense of the present. Before thal the augment iṭ is by ⟦अर्धधातुकस्येड्वलादे⟧. The ām taught by ⟦कास्पत्ययाडाम⟧ does not come, because this is a mantra. The accent is lost. ⟦प्रप्र⟧: the doubling is by ⟦प्रसमःपोद⟧. ⟦यंति⟧: it is the form in laṭ of the root iṇ, "to go". By ⟦एकान्याभ्यां समर्थाभ्याम⟧, because it is connected with the word anya, the first verb does not lose its accent. ⟦आसते⟧: the root is ās, "to sit". Because it is the later one, its accent is lost. ⟦असि⟧: the root is as, "to be". The s is elided by ⟦शासस्तो-र्लोप⟧. Because it is connected with a form of yad, its accent is not struck down.

## Ṛk 4

O Agni! ⟦अद्रुहो⟧, doing no injury, ⟦निचिरासो⟧, very long-lasting, ancient, the Viśve Devas ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦अविंदन्⟧, having followed, found, ⟦ईं⟧, this one, you, who were ⟦ईयिवांसं⟧, going, ⟦अति⟧, having overcome by strength, ⟦स्रिधः⟧, the enemies who dry one up; who were going having also overcome the ⟦सश्चतः⟧, the armies that bring on the encounter, ⟦तश्वती⟧, many; and who were ⟦अप्सु⟧ ⟦श्रितं⟧, hidden in the waters, and for that very reason ⟦सिंहमिव⟧, like a lion that has entered a cave, terrible like a lion.

⟦ईयिवांसं⟧: of the root iṇ, "to go", in kvasu, the word is laid down ready-made by the sūtra ⟦क्वसावुपेयिवाननाश्वाननूचानश्च⟧ (⟦पा. ३-२-१०९⟧). The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦स्रिधः⟧: after sridh, which has the sense of wasting or the sense of drying up, the suffix kvip comes by ⟦क्विड् चेति⟧. The accent is that of the root. ⟦सश्चतः⟧: the root is sasj, "to go". C takes the place of the j by transposition. Śatṛ is the substitute for laṭ. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अविंदन्⟧: it is the form in laṅ of the root vid, "to obtain". ⟦निजिरासः⟧: "very long-lasting". The augment asuk is by ⟦आज्जसे-रसुगित्यसुक्⟧. The accent is that of the compound. ⟦अद्रुहः⟧: the root is druh, "to wish to kill". Because it belongs to the group beginning with saṃpad, kvip comes in the sense of the action. In the bahuvrīhi, by the rule on nañ and su, ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम⟧, the latter member has the udātta on its last syllable. ⟦श्रितं⟧: of the root śri, "to resort to", the augment iṭ is forbidden: ⟦श्रुकः कितीतीट्प्रतिषेधः⟧. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 5

⟦मातरिश्वा⟧, Vāyu, ⟦आ⟧ ⟦नयत्⟧, brought, ⟦परावतो⟧, from a far-off place, ⟦देवेभ्यः⟧ — the dative is in the sense of "for the sake of" — for the sake of the gods, ⟦एनं⟧, this, ⟦अग्निं⟧, who was ⟦इत्था⟧, thus, ⟦तिरःऽहितं⟧, hidden, by entering the waters ⟦त्मना⟧, of his own will, and who was ⟦मथितं⟧, produced by churning, ⟦परि⟧, all around. For this there is an illustration: ⟦सस्यवांस-मिव⟧: as a father brings back by force a son who is ⟦सस्यवांसं⟧, running, going away, of his own free will, so.

⟦सस्यवांसमिव⟧: after the root sṛ, "to go", comes liṭ; its substitute is kvasu. Because ⟦इवेन विभक्त्यलोप⟧ has been stated, the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦त्मना⟧: of the word ātman the ā is elided by ⟦मंत्रेष्टाज्यादेरात्मन⟧. ⟦इत्था⟧: by ⟦था हेत् च छंदसीति⟧ the suffix thā comes, by transposition. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦एनं⟧: in a reference to what has been mentioned before, the substitute ena comes by ⟦द्वितीयाटौष्णेन⟧, and it is anudātta. ⟦नयत्⟧: it is the form in laṅ of the root nī, "to lead to". The absence of the augment aṭ is Vedic. ⟦मातरिश्वा⟧: "he breathes in the mother, the atmosphere": so the word is laid down ready-made by the rule beginning ⟦त्वन्नु क्षन्⟧.

## Ṛk 6

⟦हव्यवाहन⟧, O carrier of the oblations, O Agni! ⟦मर्ता⟧, men, the adhvaryu and the others, ⟦अगृभृत⟧, took hold of, ⟦तं⟧ ⟦त्वा⟧, you who were such, hidden in the waters, ⟦देवेभ्यो⟧, for the sake of the deities to whom sacrifice is to be made. ⟦मानुष⟧, O benefactor of men, ⟦यविष्य⟧, O youngest, O Agni! ⟦यत्⟧, because, ⟦तव क्रत्वा⟧, by your action, your greatness, you ⟦अभिपासि⟧, protect on all sides, ⟦विश्वान्⟧, all, the ⟦यज्ञान्⟧ that are performed by us, therefore they took hold of you: such is the connection with the preceding.

⟦अगृभृत⟧: the root is grah, "to take". Because it belongs to the class beginning with krī, śnā comes. The saṃprasāraṇa is by ⟦ग्रहिज्यावयिति⟧. By ⟦हृग्रहोर्भश्चंदसीति⟧ bh takes the place of the h. The accent is lost by ⟦तिज्जतिज⟧. ⟦हव्यवाहन⟧: after the root vah, "to carry", with ñyuṭ continuing from the preceding rule, ñyuṭ comes by ⟦हव्येनंतःपादमिति⟧. The penultimate takes vṛddhi. Ana is the substitute for yu. Because it does not stand at the beginning of a pāda, it has the anudātta that is taught in the eighth book. ⟦विश्वान्⟧: after the root viś, "to enter", the suffix kvan comes by the rule ending ⟦विशिभ्यः क्वन्निति⟧. Because it is kit there is no guṇa. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦अभिपासि⟧: the root is pā, "to protect". Because this root has the sense of protecting, the substitute piba does not come. Because it is connected with the word yad, the accent is not lost. The accent is that of the root. ⟦क्रत्वा⟧: the ending is ṭā. Because of the statement ⟦जसादयश्चंदसि विकल्प्यंत⟧, "jas and the rest are optional in the Veda", the substitute nā does not come here. The semivowel is substituted. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦यविष्य⟧: after the word yuvan, in the sense of surpassing, iṣṭhan comes by ⟦तमबिष्ठनाव⟧. By ⟦स्कूलदूरयुवे⟧ the part that begins with the semivowel is elided and the preceding vowel takes guṇa. Because it belongs to the group beginning with vasu, the suffix ya comes in the sense of the word itself.

## Ṛk 7

O Agni! ⟦तत्⟧, this, ⟦भद्रं⟧, beautiful, ⟦दंसना⟧, the action that is yours, the Agnihotra and the rest, ⟦छदयति⟧ ⟦पाकाय चित्⟧. The word cit has the sense of "even". ⟦पाकाये⟧ is a dative in the sense of the object. A pāka is a child. Even a sacrificer who is ignorant like one it honours by giving its fruit; the meaning is that it makes him surrounded by cattle, sons and the rest. The word chadayati has the sense of honouring, because ⟦छंदयति छदयतीति⟧ is read among the names for that. Or else the word pāka expresses "praiseworthy", because ⟦पाको नाम⟧ is read among the names for the praiseworthy. The meaning is: that auspicious action honours the praiseworthy sacrificer by giving the fruit he desires. ⟦यत्⟧, because, ⟦अपितर्वरे⟧, at the opening of the night, at the very time when the fire is carried apart, the ⟦पशवो⟧, the animals of both kinds, the two-footed and the four-footed, ⟦समासते⟧, sit well together, ⟦त्वां⟧, by you, who are ⟦संइद्धं⟧, blazing; the meaning is that they attend upon you. The meaning is: it bestows this fruit, consisting of cattle and the rest, on the sacrificer.

⟦भद्रं⟧: after the root bhad, "to be auspicious", because the word is laid down ready-made with the suffix rak after it, the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦पाकाय⟧: after the root pā, "to protect", the suffix kan comes: ⟦इत्यस्मादिण्भेकापातलीत्यादिना कान्⟧. "He is protected by the king and others", hence pāka. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦छदयति⟧: the root is chad, "to cover". Ṇic comes in the sense of the root itself. Because the root ends in a there is no vṛddhi. The accent is lost. ⟦समासते⟧: the root is ās, "to sit". Because it belongs to the class beginning with ad, the śap is elided. Because the stem does not end in a, there is the substitute for jh. Because it is connected with yad, the accent is not lost. By ⟦तिजि चोदात्तवतीति⟧ the preverb loses its accent. The accent is that of the root.

## Ṛk 8

The sacrificer urges the priests. O priests! ⟦आ⟧ ⟦जुहोत⟧, satisfy on every side with offerings, this Agni, who is ⟦पावकशोचिषं⟧, of purifying brightness, ⟦शीरं⟧, lying within the pieces of wood and the plants, and ⟦स्वध्वरं⟧, possessed of a fine sacrifice. Moreover: ⟦सपर्यत⟧, worship, ⟦श्रुष्टि⟧, quickly — ⟦श्रुष्टिति क्षिप्रनामेति⟧, "śruṣṭī is a name for quick", says Yāska — this Agni, who is ⟦आशुं⟧, pervading in the rite, ⟦दूत⟧, the messenger of the gods because he does the calling, ⟦अजिरं⟧, one who goes swiftly, ⟦प्रत्नं⟧, ancient, ⟦ईड्यं⟧, to be praised, and ⟦देवं⟧, shining.

⟦जुहोत⟧: the root is hu, "to give and to eat". After this root comes loṭ. By ⟦जुहोत्यादिभ्यः श्लुरिति⟧ ślu takes the place of śap. The reduplication is by ⟦श्लाविति⟧. As loṭ is treated like laṅ by ⟦लोटो लज्जदिति⟧, ta is the substitute for tha by ⟦तस्थस्थमिपामिति⟧. With chandasi, "in the Veda", continuing from the preceding rule, tap is the substitute for that by ⟦तप्तनप्तनथनाश्चेति⟧. Because it is pit there is guṇa. The accent is lost. ⟦स्वध्वरं⟧: in the bahuvrīhi, by the rule on nañ and su, the latter member has the udātta on its last syllable. ⟦शीरं⟧: the root is śī, "to sleep". After this root the suffix rak comes by the rule beginning ⟦स्पायितंचिवंची⟧. Because it is kit there is no guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix. In the word pāvakaśociṣam: pāva is purifying, purity; ⟦तं कायतीति पावकः⟧, "he utters it", hence pāvaka. ⟦आतोऽनुपसर्गे कः⟧: the suffix is ka. The accent is that of the suffix. In the bahuvrīhi, by ⟦प्रकृत्या पूर्वपदमिति⟧, the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦आशुं⟧: the root is aś, "to pervade". After this root the suffix uṇ comes: ⟦कृवासाजिमित्यादिना उण्⟧. The penultimate takes vṛddhi. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अजिरं⟧: after the root aj, "to go and to throw", because the word is laid down ready-made as ending in the suffix kirac by the rule that begins with ajira and śiśira, the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦प्रत्नं⟧: by ⟦प्रगस्यछंदसि गलो-पश्च⟧ (⟦पा. ४-३ २३-२⟧) the ga is elided. Because of the word ca in it, the suffix tna comes. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦ईड्यं⟧: the root is īḍ, "to praise". After it comes ṇyat. The udātta on the first syllable is by the rule beginning ⟦ईडवंदे⟧. ⟦सपर्यत⟧: after the word sapara comes yak, the suffix taught after the group beginning with kaṇḍū; the word is used in the sense of worship. It is the second person plural of loṭ. Śap. By ⟦अतो गुण⟧ the form of the preceding vowel alone remains. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 9

⟦त्रीणि शता⟧, three hundred; ⟦सहस्राणि⟧, three thousand; ⟦त्रिंशच्च नव च⟧: the ⟦देवा⟧, in number three thousand together with three hundred and thirty-nine, ⟦असपर्यन्⟧, worshipped, this ⟦अग्निं⟧. Moreover those gods ⟦औक्षन्⟧, sprinkled, this Agni ⟦धृतैः⟧, with ghee. Likewise ⟦अस्मै⟧, for this Agni, for his sake, they ⟦अस्त्रणन्⟧ the ⟦बर्हिः⟧; they made a strewing with the barhis, a covering of the vedi. ⟦आत्⟧ ⟦इत्⟧, immediately after that, those gods, having made this Agni the ⟦होतारं⟧, ⟦न्यसादयंत⟧, seated him, on that barhis. "Worship this Agni, who is to be worshipped by the gods": such is the connection with the preceding. And the number of the gods is stated in the Bṛhadāraṇyaka: ⟦महि-मान एवैषामेते त्रयस्त्रिंशत्त्वेव देवा इति । कतमे ते त्रयस्त्रिंशदष्टौ वसप एकादश रुद्रा द्वादशादित्यास्त एकत्रिंशदिंद्रश्च व प्रजापतिश्च त्रयस्त्रिंशौ⟧, "'These are only their powers; the gods are just thirty-three.' 'Which are those thirty-three?' 'The eight Vasus, the eleven Rudras, the twelve Ādityas: those are thirty-one; Indra and Prajāpati make up the thirty-three'" (⟦बृ. आ. ३-९⟧).

⟦शता⟧: for ten tens there is the form śa, and the suffix ta. "Ten tens are their measure": hundreds. The case-ending is replaced by ḍā by the rule beginning ⟦सुपां सुलुगि⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦त्री⟧: by ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ there is the long vowel like the preceding one. The accent is that of a nominal stem. ⟦सहस्राणि⟧: because it belongs to the group beginning with kardama, the udātta is in the middle. ⟦त्रिंशत्⟧: for three tens there is the form trin, and the suffix śat. "Three tens are its measure": thirty.
