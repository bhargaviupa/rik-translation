## Introduction

The sūkta beginning ⟦निर्मथितः सुधित⟧ is the eleventh, of five Ṛks. On it the Anukramaṇikā says, of the sūkta beginning "nirmathitaḥ": ⟦देवश्रवा देववातश्च भारतौ तृतीया⟧, "Devaśravas and Devavāta, the two descendants of Bharata; the third Ṛk is a satobṛhatī". The two sons of Bharata, Devaśravas and Devavāta, are both the Ṛṣis of this sūkta. The third Ṛk is a satobṛhatī. The rest are triṣṭubhs. Agni is the deity. The use of the group of ten sūktas beginning "ā hotā", in the prātaranuvāka and in the āśvina-śastra, has been stated. The use of the Ṛk beginning "iḷām agne" has been taught more than once.

## Ṛk 1

The two Ṛṣis, the descendants of Bharata, make a hymn of praise. The Agni who, ⟦निर्मथितो⟧: being thoroughly churned out of the two fire-sticks in rites such as the Agniṣṭoma, ⟦सधस्थे⟧: in the house of the sacrificer, ⟦सुधितः⟧: is well set down in the three fire-pits, that of the gārhapatya and the others; and who, having been set down, being made to blaze by the throwing on of wood, is ⟦युवा⟧: awakened; for that very reason, ⟦अध्वरस्य⟧: of the Jyotiṣṭoma and the like, ⟦प्रणेता⟧: the one who eminently brings it on, who carries it through; and just because of his being a leader, ⟦कविः⟧: one whose sight reaches beyond. He, being such, ⟦जातवेदाः⟧: possessed of the knowledge of all objects, that ⟦अग्निः⟧, Agni, ⟦वनेषु⟧: in the great forests, ⟦जूर्यत्सु⟧: which are wasting away through contact with the forest fire, even while they are coming to decay, to destruction, is himself ⟦अजरः⟧: free from decay, and on the contrary blazing; and being so, ⟦अत्र⟧: on the sacrificers who make praise in this way, ⟦अमृतं⟧: imperishable, abundant food ⟦आ⟧ ⟦दधे⟧: he bestows.

⟦सुधितः⟧: of the root dhā, before the participial suffix, niṣṭhā, in the sense of the object, the substitute dhi comes because the form is laid down ready-made in the rule that begins with sudhita, vasudhita and nemadhita. By the rule ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the preverb keeps its original accent. ⟦जूर्यत्तु⟧: the roots are ⟦जूरी घूरी⟧, in the senses of injury and loss of age. By interchange the suffix is śatṛ. Because the root belongs to the class beginning with div, the class-sign is śyan. Because that is marked with n, the word has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦अजरः⟧: from the root ⟦ज्ञ⟧, in the sense of loss of age, in the sense of the action, the suffix ap comes by the rule "ap after roots in ṝ and in u". There is guṇa. By the rule ⟦नञ्नो जरमरमित्रमृता⟧ the first syllable of the second member is udātta. ⟦दधे⟧: it is the form of the root dhā in the perfect, liṭ, used of present time. ⟦जातवेदाः⟧: of the root ⟦जनी⟧, in the sense of coming into being, before the suffix kta, ā comes in by the rule beginning ⟦जनसनखनां⟧, "of jan, san and khan, before san and before an ending that begins with a jhal consonant". In the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent.

## Ṛk 2

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦भारता⟧: the two Bhāratas, the two sons of Bharata, Devaśravas and Devavāta, both of them, ⟦सुदक्षं⟧: endowed with excellent ability, ⟦रेवत्⟧: possessed of wealth, ⟦अग्निं⟧: you who are endowed with the qualities of moving and the rest, ⟦अमंथिष्टां⟧: they churned, for the sake of attaining the rites such as the Agniṣṭoma; the meaning is that they brought you forth by churning. You, being such, accompanied by ⟦बृहता⟧, abundant, ⟦राया⟧, wealth, ⟦अभि⟧: having turned towards us, ⟦वि⟧ ⟦पश्य⟧: look on us in a special way, with a look of favour. Moreover, ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦द्यून्⟧: the word dyu denotes the day, because it is read among the names of the day in ⟦वस्तोर्द्युरिति⟧.**NOTE**{After this quotation the reference text has a reference with the numbers 1. 9. 2, which the supplied text does not have.} Throughout the days, on all days, ⟦नेता⟧: the bringer of ⟦नः⟧, our, ⟦इषां⟧, foods, ⟦भवतात्⟧: be.

⟦अमंथिष्टां⟧: from the root ⟦मंथ⟧, in the sense of stirring, in the aorist, luṅ, there is the suffix sic. When vṛddhi would follow by the rule ⟦वदप्रजहलंतस्याचः⟧ (⟦पा. ७-२-३⟧), it is barred by the rule "not before the augment iṭ". By the rule that begins with tas, thas, tha and mip, tām is the substitute of tas. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda, the verb does not lose its accent. The accent is that of the augment aṭ. ⟦भारता⟧: in the sense "offspring of Bharata", the suffix añ comes because the word belongs to the group beginning with utsa. By the rule in ⟦तद्धितेश्वचामादेरित्यादिवृद्धिः⟧ there is vṛddhi of the first vowel. The accent is that of a suffix marked with ñ. ⟦रेवत्⟧: after the word rayi comes the suffix matup. There follow the change to v, the udātta, the saṃprasāraṇa, the single substitute in the form of the former vowel, the guṇa and the elision of the case-ending. ⟦देवश्रवाः⟧: the root is ⟦श्रु⟧, in the sense of hearing. By the rule ⟦असुन्निति⟧ the suffix asun comes in the sense of the object. He is heard of, made famous, by the gods: hence the Ṛṣi Devaśravas. Where the original accent of the second member ending in a kṛt suffix would follow, the accent is that of the first member, by the Uṇādi rule ⟦गतिकारकयोरपि पूर्वपदप्रकृतिस्वरत्वं⟧, "and of a preverb and of a word denoting a participant in the action also, there is the original accent of the first member". ⟦देववातः⟧: the root is ⟦वा⟧, in the senses of going and of hurting. After it the suffix kta comes in the sense of the object. He who was reached as one desired by the gods is the Ṛṣi Devavāta. By the rule "a word in the third case, when the object is denoted" the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦द्यून्⟧: the root is ⟦द्यु⟧, in the sense of going towards, or ⟦द्युत⟧, in the sense of shining. While "and it is marked with ṅ" continues from the preceding rule, by the rule of Bhoja ⟦द्युयुभ्यां चेति⟧ the suffix ḍu comes, and it is marked with ṅ. From the root dyut the suffix ḍun comes by the rule "and aśva and the rest" (⟦उ. ५-२९⟧).**NOTE**{The rule quoted here ends -aśvādayaś ca in the supplied text and -aśrvādayaś ca in the reference text; which word the rule begins with is not certain.} Living beings go in it each towards his own desired place, or it shines through its connection with the rays: hence dyu, the day. By the rule ⟦प्रथमयोरिति⟧ there is the long vowel for the two like vowels. By the rule "after that, n in place of the s of śas, in the masculine" (⟦पा. ६-१-१०३⟧) n takes the place of s. The accent is that of the single substitute.

## Ṛk 3

O Agni, the ⟦क्षिपः⟧ of Devavāta: they are thrown out for the doing of work, hence kṣipas, the fingers. And they are ⟦दश⟧, ten: because the two arms are the means of the churning, the fingers on them are ten in number. Such fingers ⟦अजीजनन्⟧: brought forth you, ⟦पूर्व्यं⟧: the ancient one, ⟦सीं⟧: this one. O ⟦देवश्रवः⟧, Devaśravas, ⟦मातृषु⟧: in the two fire-sticks that are his mothers, ⟦सुजातं⟧: well born, and for that very reason ⟦प्रियं⟧: lovely, ⟦दैववातं⟧: churned by Devavāta, this ⟦अग्निं⟧, Agni, ⟦स्तुहि⟧: praise. The Agni ⟦यः⟧, who, ⟦जनानां⟧: of the praisers, the sacrificers, ⟦वशी⟧: abiding in their control, the one who of his own freedom sets the rites going, ⟦असत्⟧: is: praise him. This is how it is construed with what precedes.

⟦दश⟧: the root is ⟦दन्य⟧, in the sense of biting. While "kanin" continues from the preceding rule, the suffix kanin comes by the rule ⟦नुदशोर्गुणश्चति⟧. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦क्षिपः⟧: the root is ⟦क्षिप⟧, in the sense of impelling. The suffix is the Uṇādi kvip, in the sense of the object. They are thrown, that is, sent, to their tasks: hence kṣipas, the fingers. The accent is that of the root. ⟦अजीजनन्⟧: the root is ⟦जनी⟧, in the sense of coming into being. Of the stem that ends in the causative suffix ṇi, in the aorist, luṅ, there are the suffix caṅ, the reduplication, the treatment as before san, the i of the reduplicating syllable and its lengthening. The verb loses its accent. ⟦स्तुहि⟧: it is the form in the imperative, loṭ, of the root ⟦ष्टुञ्⟧, in the sense of praise. Because the root belongs to the class beginning with ad, the class-sign śap is dropped. The verb loses its accent. ⟦दैववातं⟧: in the sense "brought about by Devavāta", the suffix aṇ comes by the rule ⟦तेन निर्वृत्तं⟧ (⟦पा. ४-२-६८⟧). The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦असत्⟧: the root is ⟦अस⟧, in the sense of being. In the subjunctive, leṭ, it takes the augment aṭ. Because of the connection with a form of the relative pronoun, the verb does not lose its accent.

## Ṛk 4

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦इळायाः⟧: who bears the form of a cow, ⟦पृथिव्याः⟧: of the earth, ⟦वरे⟧: in the most excellent ⟦पदे⟧: on the uttaravedi, ⟦अह्नां⟧ ⟦सुदिनत्वे⟧: in order that the days of sacrifice may be fair days. The days on which Indra and the other more excellent gods are worshipped are fair days. For that purpose ⟦त्वा⟧: you ⟦आ⟧ ⟦नि⟧ ⟦दधे⟧: I set down all around. He points out the best places. ⟦दृषद्वत्यां⟧: there is a certain river named Dṛṣadvatī; on it. ⟦मानुषे⟧: on the bank that is the range where men move about. ⟦अपयायां⟧: there is a certain river named ⟦अपया⟧; on it.**NOTE**{The bhāṣya in the supplied text writes the name of this river with a short first vowel, apayāyāṃ and apayā; the Pada-pāṭha and the reference text have āpayāyāṃ and āpayā.} ⟦सरस्वत्यां⟧: on the river Sarasvatī. In these places**NOTE**{The reference text has "in these best places", eteṣu uttameṣu sthāneṣu; the supplied text has only "in these places".} you ⟦रेवत्⟧: in such a way that there is wealth, ⟦दिदीहि⟧: shine. The great Ṛṣis indeed performed their sacrifices and other rites on the bank of the Sarasvatī. And so the Brāhmaṇa says: ⟦ऋषयो वै सरस्वत्यां सत्रमासत⟧ (⟦ऐ. ब्रा. २-१९⟧), "The Ṛṣis indeed held a sattra on the Sarasvatī".

⟦दधे⟧: it is the form of the root dhā in the perfect, liṭ, in the first person singular. ⟦वरे⟧: the root is ⟦वृञ्⟧, in the sense of choosing. The suffix ap comes by the rule in ⟦ग्रहवृदृनिश्चि गमश्चेत्यप्⟧. The suffix being anudātta because it is marked with p, the accent is that of the root. ⟦दृषद्वत्यां⟧: the root is ⟦दृ⟧, in the sense of tearing. By the rule beginning ⟦दृणातेः षुग्गु स्वश्च⟧ (⟦उ. १-१३०⟧) there are the augment ṣuk and the shortening of the root. That in which there are dṛṣads, stones: thus the suffix matup. By the rule ⟦झय⟧ its m becomes v. Because the maxim ⟦स्वरविधौ व्यंजनमविद्यमानवदित्य⟧, "in a rule of accent a consonant is as if not present", is not resorted to here, the suffix matup is not udātta. The accent is that of the suffix adi. ⟦सरस्वत्यां⟧: the word saras has the udātta on its first syllable because it ends in the suffix asun. In the sense "it has that" comes the suffix matup. Because the last vowel but one is a, its m becomes v. Because the suffix is marked with an uk vowel, the feminine suffix ṅīp comes. ⟦दिदीहि⟧: it is the form, in the intensive with elision of the yaṅ suffix, of the root ⟦दिवु⟧, in the senses of playing and the rest.

## Ṛk 5

O ⟦अग्ने⟧, Agni, ⟦पुरुदंसं⟧: because it is read among the names of action in the list ⟦अपोऽपो दंसो वेष⟧, the word daṃsas denotes action. She who has ⟦पुरूणि⟧, many, deeds; her, of many deeds; ⟦गोः⟧ ⟦सनिं⟧: who procures cows and other cattle; ⟦इळां⟧: the deity of that name, who has the form of a cow; ⟦शश्वत्तमं⟧: without interruption; ⟦हवमानाय⟧: for me, the sacrificer; ⟦साध⟧: accomplish. Moreover, ⟦नः⟧: our ⟦सूनुः⟧, son, and ⟦तनयः⟧, grandson, ⟦स्यात्⟧: comes to be: ⟦ते⟧: that ⟦सुमतिः⟧, good thought, of yours which is thus, ⟦सा⟧: may it, being ⟦विजावा⟧, not barren, ⟦भूतु⟧: be ⟦अस्मे⟧: ours.

⟦शश्वत्तमं⟧: the last syllable is udātta because the word is read in the group named in ⟦उच्छादिषु⟧. ⟦साध⟧: the root is ⟦राध साध⟧, in the sense of accomplishment. Here it includes within itself the sense of the causative. In the imperative, loṭ, by interchange, the class-sign is śap. By the rule ⟦सेर्ह्यपिच्चेति⟧ the substitute hi comes. It is dropped by the rule on hi after a. The verb loses its accent. ⟦विजावा⟧: the root is ⟦जनी⟧, in the sense of coming into being. After it the suffix vanip comes by the rule "they are seen after other roots also". The substitution of ā is by the rule in ⟦विड्वनोरनुनासिकस्यादित्यात्वं⟧. The accent is the original accent of the second member, which ends in a kṛt suffix. There is interchange of gender. ⟦भूतु⟧: the root is ⟦भू⟧, in the sense of being. By the rule ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧ the class-sign śap is dropped. By the rule ⟦भूसुवोस्तिजीति⟧ guṇa is barred.
