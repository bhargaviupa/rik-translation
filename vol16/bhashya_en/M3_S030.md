## Introduction
**NOTE**{the reference text has, before this introduction, a verse of salutation which the supplied text does not have} In the third anuvāka there are nine sūktas. Among them the first sūkta, of twenty-two Ṛks, is the one beginning ⟦इच्छंति⟧. On it the Anukramaṇikā is this: ⟦इच्छंति द्व्यधिकैंद्रमिति⟧, "'Icchanti': (twenty) with two in excess, addressed to Indra". Viśvāmitra, the seer of the Maṇḍala, is the ṛṣi. Indra is the deity. As nothing is stated, the metre is Triṣṭubh.

On the first day of the Abhiplava, and on the first day of the Daśarātra, at the midday pressing, in the śastra of the Acchāvāka, in the place of the ahīna-sūkta, this is the second sampāta-sūkta. It is laid down in the sūtra, in the section ⟦अभिप्लवपृष्ट्याहानीति⟧: ⟦इमामू ष्विच्छंति त्वा शासद्वह्निरिति संपाताः⟧ (⟦आ. ७-५⟧), "'imām ū ṣu', 'icchanti tvā' and 'śāsad vahniḥ' are the sampātas".

In the animal sacrifice belonging to Indra the slayer of Vṛtra, ⟦सहदानुमिति⟧ is the yājyā of the omentum. It is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦सहदानुं पुरुहूत क्षियंतं स्तुत इंद्रो मघवा यद्ध वृत्र⟧ (⟦आ. ३-८⟧), "'Him who is with Dānu, O much-invoked one, …' and 'Praised, Indra the bountiful, when indeed the Vṛtras …'".

The verse ⟦स्वस्तये वाजिभिश्च⟧ is the yājyā of the oblation in the animal sacrifice to Indra. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦स्वस्तये वाजिभिश्च प्रणेतः शुची वा हव्या मरुतः शुचीनां⟧ (⟦आ. ३-७⟧), "'For well-being, and with horses, O leader' and 'Pure are the oblations for you, O Maruts, who are pure'".

In the Śunāsīrīya, ⟦शुनं हुवेम⟧ is the anuvākyā of the optional Indra Śuna. And it is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦शुनं हुवेम मघवानमिंद्रमश्वायंतो गव्यंतो वाजयंतः⟧ (⟦आ. २-२०⟧), "'May we call the śuna, the bountiful Indra' and 'Desiring horses, desiring cows, desiring booty'".

## Ṛk 1
Viśvāmitra praises. O Indra, the ⟦सोम्यासः⟧, the brāhmaṇas who are worthy of Soma, wish (⟦इच्छंति⟧) to praise ⟦त्वा⟧, you. For that very reason those brāhmaṇas, the friends (⟦सखायः⟧), press the Soma (⟦सुन्वंति⟧) for your sake; they extract it. They place (⟦दधति⟧) the ⟦प्रयांसि⟧, the other oblations also; they hold them for your sake. Being impelled by you, they endure (⟦तितिक्षंते⟧), they bear, the ⟦अभिशस्तिं⟧ of ⟦जनानां⟧, of rivals, the injury done by them. Other than you (⟦त्वत्⟧), who are such a favourer, what man indeed (⟦कश्चन हि⟧) is there who is ⟦आ प्रकेतः⟧, made known everywhere in the world? He who is made known, caused to be known, is prakeṭa.

⟦सोम्यासः⟧: in the sense "he deserves Soma", the suffix ya by the rule ⟦सोममर्हति य⟧, "ya, in the sense 'he deserves Soma'". The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦सुन्वंति⟧: ⟦षुञ् अभिषवे⟧, the root ṣuñ in the sense of pressing. As it belongs to the svādi class, there is śnu. By ⟦सति शिष्टस्वरो बलीयानिति⟧, "where one accent is already there, the accent taught afterwards is the stronger", the accent is that of the suffix jhi. ⟦दधति⟧: a form of the root dhā in laṭ. As to ⟦समानवाक्ये युष्मदस्मदादेशनिघाताः⟧ (⟦पा. ८-१-१८-५⟧), "the substitutes of yuṣmad and asmad and the losses of accent take place within one and the same sentence": because the word soma is construed with the other sentence, there is no loss of accent. By the rule "of reduplicated stems the first syllable (is udātta)" it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦तितिक्षंते⟧: of the root ⟦तिज निशान⟧, tij in sharpening, in the sense of forbearance, the suffix san by ⟦गुप्तिज्जिद्धृः सन्निति⟧, "san after gup, tij and kit". The accent is that of a reduplicated stem. ⟦अभिशस्तिं⟧: from the root ⟦शसु⟧, in the sense of injuring, the suffix ktin in the sense of the action. By ⟦तादौ च निति कृत्यताविति⟧, "and before a kṛt suffix that begins with t and has the marker n, other than tu", the preverb keeps its original accent. ⟦प्रकेतः⟧: ⟦कित ज्ञाने⟧, the root kit in knowing. It ends in ghañ in the sense of the object. By the rule on tha, atha, ghañ, kta and the rest it is udātta on the final syllable.

## Ṛk 2
⟦हरिवः⟧: he whose two horses are of tawny colour is this Harivat, Indra. O Harivat, even the farthest (⟦परमा चित्⟧) ⟦रजांसि⟧, places, even those that lie far away, are not (⟦न⟧) far (⟦दूरे⟧) for ⟦ते⟧, you. Therefore do you, furnished with the two horses (⟦हरिभ्यां⟧), ⟦तु⟧, quickly, come hither (⟦प्रयाहि⟧); come, with our sacrifice in view. For you who are ⟦स्थिराय⟧, firm of mind in the giving of fruits, and for that very reason ⟦वृष्णे⟧, the showerer of desires, ⟦इमा⟧, these, ⟦सवना⟧, pressings, the morning one and the rest, ⟦कृता⟧, have been made. Moreover, while the ⟦अग्नौ⟧, the Āhavanīya fire, is ⟦समिधाने⟧, blazing well as the receptacle of the offerings, the ⟦ग्रावाणः⟧, the stones, are ⟦युक्ताः⟧, made ready for the pressing of the Soma. Therefore come quickly: this is the sense.

⟦याहि⟧: a form in loṭ of the root ⟦या प्रापण⟧, yā in reaching. ⟦हरिवः⟧: the suffix matup in the sense "he has two horses". By ⟦छंदसीर इति⟧, "in the Veda, after i and r", its m becomes v. As it is a vocative, it loses its accent. ⟦स्थिराय⟧: from the root ⟦ष्ठा⟧, in the sense of ceasing to move, there is kirac by the rule beginning with ajira and śiśira. As the word is laid down ready-made as ending in that suffix, the ā is elided. The accent is that of a suffix with the marker c. ⟦सवना⟧: from the root ⟦षुञ् अभिषव⟧, ṣuñ in pressing, there is lyuṭ in the sense of the locus by ⟦ल्युट् चेति⟧, "and lyuṭ". Those at which the Soma is pressed are the pressings, the morning one and the rest. The accent is that of a suffix with the marker l. ⟦समिधाने⟧: a form in kānac of the root ⟦ञइंधी⟧, in the sense of shining. As the suffix has the marker k, the n is elided. The accent is that of a suffix with the marker c.

## Ṛk 3
⟦वृषभ⟧, showerer of desires, O Indra: you who are ⟦इंद्रः⟧, endowed with supreme lordship; ⟦सुशिप्रः⟧ — by the word śipra a guard for the head is denoted — furnished with a fine helmet; or else, having fine jaws: ⟦शिप्रे हनू नासिके वा⟧ (⟦नि. ६-१७⟧), "the two śipras are the two jaws or the two nostrils", says Yāska; ⟦मघवा⟧, possessing wealth; ⟦तरुत्रः⟧ — "by him the gods cross over their enemies", hence Indra is ⟦तरुत्र⟧; ⟦महाव्रातः⟧ — he who has, by the Maruts, a great troop, a multitude, is so called; ⟦तुविकूर्मिः⟧, the doer of deeds of many kinds in battle; ⟦ऋघावान्⟧, having violence directed against enemies; and for that very reason ⟦उग्रः⟧, terrifying to enemies — you who are such, being ⟦बाधितो⟧, pressed hard by the asuras in battle, whatever (⟦यत्⟧) heroic deeds you performed (⟦धा⟧) among the ⟦मर्त्येषु⟧, among the asuras who are killers: now where (⟦क्व⟧) are ⟦त्या⟧, those, ⟦वीर्याणि⟧, heroic deeds, of ⟦ते⟧, yours? Where do they abide? Tell.

⟦सुशिप्रः⟧: the root ⟦सृष्ट गत्⟧, in the sense of going; the Uṇādi suffix ran. By bāhulaka, the freedom allowed to such formations, śi is substituted for sṛ. In the bahuvrīhi, by the rule "(a latter member) of two syllables is udātta on its first syllable, in the Veda", the latter member is udātta on its first syllable. ⟦तरुत्रः⟧: from the root tṝ, the suffix utra (⟦इत्रोत्रावित्युत्र⟧) by the rule "after aśitra and the rest, itra and utra". By transposition it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦महाव्रातः⟧: by ⟦आन्महत⟧, "ā for mahat", ā is substituted for the t of mahat. ⟦तुविकूर्मिः⟧: from the root kṛ, the Uṇādi suffix mi. When guṇa has been made, the change of the a to u is Vedic. By ⟦हलि⟧, "and before a consonant", there is lengthening. ⟦ऋघावान्⟧: ṛghāḥ is "one who strikes …"**NOTE**{the word for what is struck is misread in the supplied text; the reference text has ṛn, with nṝn added in brackets}. The suffix vic by the rule ⟦अन्वेभ्योऽपि दृश्यंत इति⟧, "it is seen after others also". Because the word "is seen" is used in that rule, the n is elided and gh comes for the h. In the sense of matup, "that belongs to him", there is vanip by ⟦छंदसीवनिपाविति⟧, "in the Veda, ī and vanip". By transposition it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦क्व⟧: to the word kim, in the sense of the seventh case, the suffix at by the rule ⟦किमोऽदित्यत्पत्ययः⟧, "at after kim". Before it, kva is substituted by ⟦क्वातीति⟧, "kva before at". The accent is that of a suffix with the marker t.

## Ṛk 4
O Indra, you (⟦त्वं⟧), ⟦एकः⟧, quite without a helper, ⟦च्यावयन्⟧, causing to fall from their several places, the ⟦अच्युतानि⟧, the rākṣasas, your opponents, who are free from falling, even firmly rooted; and likewise ⟦जिघ्नमानो⟧, destroying, the ⟦वृत्रा⟧, the vṛtras, the evils — you go about (⟦चरसि हि स्म⟧); so indeed you abide. Moreover, for the ⟦व्रताय⟧, the work, the command, of ⟦तव⟧, you who are endowed with such power, heaven and earth and the mountains ⟦निमितेव⟧, as though dug in, motionless, stand in accord (⟦अनु⟧ … ⟦तस्थुः⟧); they stay compliant.

⟦स्म⟧: it is unaccented because it belongs to the group beginning with ca. ⟦च्यावयन्⟧: a form in śatṛ of the causative of the root ⟦च्युज्⟧, in the sense of going. The accent is that of ṇic. ⟦चरसि⟧: ⟦चर गतिभक्षणयोः⟧, the root car in going and eating. Because it is connected with hi, there is no loss of accent. ⟦जिघ्नमानः⟧: of the root ⟦हन⟧, in injuring and going, there is the suffix ka by ⟦घइर्थे कविधानमिति⟧, "ka is enjoined in the sense of ghañ". Because that is a sārvadhātuka by ⟦छंदस्युभयथेति⟧, "in the Veda, in both ways", there is śap. Of that there is ślu. By the rule "variously in the Veda", i comes in the reduplication. "He strikes": so jighna. Then kvip in the sense of behaving so. After this, cānaś in the sense of habit. śap. By transposition it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦द्यावापृथिवी⟧: by the rule "and in a dvandva of deities" both members keep their original accent. ⟦निमितेव⟧: the niṣṭhā participle, in the sense of the object, of the root ⟦डुमिञ् प्रक्षेपण⟧, ḍumiñ in throwing. By ⟦गतिरनंतर इति⟧, "the preverb immediately before", the preverb keeps its original accent. By ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧, ḍā is substituted for the case-ending. Because it has been stated ⟦इवेन विभक्त्यलोप⟧, "(composition) with iva, and the case-ending is not elided", the accent is that of the first member. ⟦तस्थुः⟧: of the root ⟦ष्ठा⟧, in ceasing to move, in liṭ before us, when the elision of the ā has been made, the reduplication takes place because that elision is treated as the original by the rule ⟦द्विर्वचनेऽचीति⟧, "when reduplication is to be made, before a vowel". Loss of accent.

## Ṛk 5
⟦उतेत्यामंत्रणे⟧: uta is used in addressing. O Indra, ⟦पुरुहूत⟧, called by many sacrificers; or else, called many times by the gods for protection from the asuras; furnished with ⟦श्रवोभि⟧, powers; you, ⟦एको⟧, without a helper, being ⟦वृत्रहा⟧, the slayer of the enemy Vṛtra: as to ⟦अभये⟧, the word which you spoke (⟦अवदः⟧) to the gods, "Do not be afraid of the asuras" — that word is ⟦दृढं⟧, true indeed. Moreover, ⟦मघवन्निंद्र⟧, O Indra possessing wealth, since (⟦यत्⟧) you hold together these two ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth, ⟦अपारे⟧ ⟦चित्⟧, even though their farther shore is far off — that is to say, you make them joined to each other as the lower and the upper — this greatness of ⟦ते⟧, yours, is ⟦काशिरित्⟧, a shining forth, well known in the world indeed. Or else: ⟦यत्⟧, that by which you hold together those two rodasī, that ⟦काशि⟧, fist, of ⟦ते⟧, yours, is great. ⟦काशिर्मुष्टिः⟧ (⟦नि. ६-१⟧), "kāśi is the fist", says Yāska.

⟦अभये⟧: from the root ⟦इभी⟧, in the sense of fearing, the suffix ac in the sense of the action by the rule ⟦एरजित्यच्‌प्रत्ययः⟧, "ac after a root in i". In the compound with nañ, the accent is that of the nañ. ⟦अवदः⟧: ⟦वद व्यक्तायां⟧ ⟦वाचि⟧, the root vad in articulate speech. A form in laṅ. Loss of accent. ⟦अपारे⟧: in the bahuvrīhi, by the rule "after nañ and su", the latter member is udātta on its final syllable. ⟦संगृह्नासि⟧**NOTE**{the supplied text has here the gloss-form saṃgṛhṇāsi as the word explained; the reference text has saṃgṛbhṇāḥ, the word of the Ṛk, to which the derivation belongs}: ⟦ग्रह उपादाने⟧, the root grah in taking. As it belongs to the kryādi class, there is śnā. By the rule beginning ⟦ग्रहिज्यावयीत्यादिना⟧ there is samprasāraṇa. By ⟦हृग्रहोर्भश्चंदसीति⟧, "bh for the h of hṛ and grah, in the Veda", bh comes for the h. By ⟦इतश्चेति⟧, "and of i", the i of sip is elided. By the rule "variously in the Veda" there is no augment aṭ. The accent is that of the compound. ⟦काशिः⟧: the root ⟦काश्य दीप्तौ⟧, in the sense of shining; the Uṇādi suffix i. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 6
O Indra, let ⟦ते⟧, your, chariot, yoked with ⟦हरिभ्यां⟧, the two horses, with the enemies in view go forth (⟦प्रैतु⟧) ⟦सु⟧, well, by ⟦प्रवता⟧, a sloping path; let it go swiftly. Likewise let ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦वज्रः⟧, thunderbolt, go forth ⟦प्रमृणन्⟧, destroying, the ⟦शत्रून्⟧, the asuras. And do you ⟦जहि⟧, kill, the ⟦प्रतीचः⟧, the enemies who come facing you. Likewise kill the ⟦अनूचः⟧, the asuras who come following, who seize from behind, and the ⟦पराचः⟧, those who are intent on flight. The enemies having thus been pressed hard, make (⟦कृणुहि⟧) ⟦विश्वं⟧, the world, ⟦सत्यं⟧; make it devoted to the works that are true, the Agnihotra and the rest. Let such power be (⟦अस्तु⟧) ⟦विष्टं⟧, entered, settled, in you.

⟦प्रवता⟧: after the word pra there is vati, by the rule "after a preverb, in the Veda, in the sense of a root". The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦प्रमृणन्⟧: ⟦मृण हिंसायां⟧, the root mṛṇ in injuring. ⟦जहि⟧: of the root ⟦हन⟧, in injuring and going, in loṭ, hi is substituted for sip. Before that, ja is substituted by ⟦हंतेर्ज इति⟧, "ja for han". The elision that falls due by ⟦अतो हेरिति⟧, "of hi after a", is set aside, because a rule within the section that runs up to bha is treated as not having taken effect. Because hi has the marker ṅ, it is udātta on the final syllable. ⟦प्रतीचः⟧: ⟦अंचु गतिपूजनयोः⟧, the root añc in going and honouring. By ⟦अंचेश्छंदस्य सर्वनामस्थानमिति⟧, "after añc, in the Veda, an ending that is not a sarvanāmasthāna", the case-ending is udātta. ⟦पराचः⟧: by ⟦अनिगंतोऽंचतौ वप्रत्यय इति⟧, "one not ending in ik, before añc with the suffix va", the preverb keeps its original accent. ⟦कृणुहि⟧: ⟦कृवि हिंसाकरणयोः⟧, the root kṛv in injuring and making. ⟦अस्तु⟧: ⟦अस भुवि⟧, the root as in being.

## Ṛk 7
O Indra, ⟦धायुः⟧, you who hold lordship without interruption: the ⟦मर्त्याय⟧, the man, ⟦यस्मै⟧, to whom, you give (⟦अदधाः⟧) — the power which you give — ⟦सः⟧, that mortal, obtains (⟦भजते⟧) the ⟦गेह्यं⟧, what belongs in houses, gold, cattle and the like, ⟦अभक्तं⟧ ⟦चित्⟧, even though not obtained before. Moreover, ⟦पुरुहूत⟧, O Indra called by many: ⟦घृताची⟧ — the word ghṛta stands for oblation in general; and so, "it goes to, reaches, the oblation that consists of Soma, clarified butter, the sacrificial cake and the like", hence ghṛtācī — such is ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦सुमतिः⟧, fine understanding, and it is ⟦भद्रा⟧, productive of welfare. Likewise your ⟦रातिः⟧, power of wealth**NOTE**{the supplied text has dhanaśaktiḥ, "power of wealth"; the reference text has dānaśaktiḥ, "power of giving"}, is ⟦सहस्रदाना⟧, attended by the giving of unmeasured wealth. On Indra's being a giver of much there is the mantra text: ⟦भूरिदा भूरि देहि नो मा दभ्रं भूर्या भर⟧ (⟦ऋग्वे ४-३२-२०⟧), "Giver of much, give us much, not little; bring much".

⟦धायुः⟧: from the root dhā, the Uṇādi suffix uṇ. ⟦अदधाः⟧: a form of the root dhā in laṅ. Because it is connected with a form of yad, there is no loss of accent. ⟦मर्त्याय⟧: marta itself is martya. Because it is read among vasu and the rest, there is yat in the word's own sense. By ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧, "of what ends in yat, except after nau", it is udātta on the first syllable. ⟦गेह्यं⟧: after the word geha, in the sense "being there", there is yat by ⟦भवे छंदसीति⟧, "in the sense 'being there', in the Veda". Through the marker t it is svarita. ⟦सुमतिः⟧: by the rule beginning ⟦मंत्रे वृषेत्यादिना⟧ the word mati is udātta on the final syllable. In the compound, the accent is that of a latter member formed with a kṛt suffix. ⟦रातिः⟧: ⟦रा दाने⟧, the root rā in giving. By the rule beginning ⟦मंत्रे वर्षेत्यादिना⟧ the ktin is udātta.

## Ṛk 8
O Indra called by many, you — ⟦सहदानुं⟧: Dānu is the Dānavī, the mother of Vṛtra; him who is together with her. And so the mantra text: ⟦दानुः शये सहवत्सा⟧ (⟦ऋग्वे १-३२-९⟧), "Dānu lies with her calf". Or else ⟦सहदानुं⟧: he who is together with the dānus, the Dānavas, is sahadānu. Him, ⟦क्षयंतं⟧, oppressing, ⟦कुणारुं⟧, given to making noise, some asura: having made him ⟦अहस्तं⟧, without hands, ⟦सं पिणक्⟧, grind him thoroughly to powder. ## Ṛk 9

## Ṛk 10
O Indra, ⟦अलातृणः⟧: because of its abundant water it is greatly, exceedingly, pierced, injured: hence alātṛṇa. The ⟦व्रजः⟧, that which has become the cow-pen, of ⟦गोः⟧, the speech of the middle region, the ⟦वलः⟧ — "it covers the sky", hence vala is the cloud — ⟦पुरा⟧ ⟦हंतोः⟧, before the blow of your thunderbolt, ⟦भयमानः⟧, being afraid, ⟦व्यार⟧, came apart. Thereafter he, Indra, made (⟦अकृणोत्⟧) the ⟦पथो⟧, paths, ⟦सुगान्⟧, easy to go, ⟦निरजे⟧, to bring out, the ⟦गाः⟧, the waters. Hence those waters, ⟦वाणीः⟧, worthy to be won, ⟦धमंतीः⟧, making a sound, came towards (⟦प्रावन्⟧) the ⟦पुरुहूतं⟧, called by many, the water of the earth; they approached it.

Yāska explained this Ṛk thus. ⟦अलातृणो वल इंद्र व्रजो गोः⟧: ⟦अलातृणो: लमातर्दनो मेघो वलो वृणोतेर्वजो व्रजत्यंतरिक्षॆ गोरेतस्या माध्यमिकाया वाचः प्ररा हननाद्वयमानो व्यार⟧, "alātṛṇa is the cloud that is pierced to the full; vala is from the root vṛ; vraja, it goes in the mid-region; of go, of this speech of the middle region; before the striking, being afraid, it came apart". ⟦सुगान्पथो अकृणोन्निरजे गाः⟧: ⟦सुगमनान्पथोऽकरोन्निर्गमनाय गवां⟧, "he made the paths easy of going, for the going out of the cows". ⟦प्रावन्वाणीः पुरुहूतं धमंतीः⟧: ⟦आपो वा वहनाद्वाचो वा वदनाद्बहुभिराहूतमुदकं भवति⟧, "they are the waters, from flowing, or voices, from speaking; the water is 'called by many'". ⟦धमतिर्गतिकर्मा⟧ (⟦नि. ६-२⟧), "the root dham has the sense of going".

⟦अलातृणः⟧: ⟦तृहि हिंसायां⟧, the root tṛh in injuring. After tṛh preceded by alam there is the suffix ka in the sense of the object, by ⟦घइर्थे कविधानमिति⟧, "ka is enjoined in the sense of ghañ". a comes for the m of alam. The accent is that of the compound. ⟦वलः⟧: ⟦वल संवरणे⟧, the root val in covering. "By it the sky is covered, concealed": hence vala is the cloud. The suffix gha by ⟦पुंसि संज्ञायामिति⟧, "in the masculine, when it is a name". The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦व्रजः⟧: ⟦व्रज गतौ⟧, the root vraj in going. By the rule beginning ⟦गोचरसंचरेत्यादिना⟧ (⟦पा. ३-३-११९⟧), because the word is laid down ready-made as ending in the suffix gha, there is no guttural. ⟦व्रजत्यंतरिक्ष इति⟧: "it goes in the mid-region". The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦भयमानः⟧: the root ⟦इतिभी⟧, in the sense of fearing. By transposition there is śap. ⟦आर⟧: a form of the root ⟦ऋ⟧, in the sense of going, in liṭ before ṇal. Loss of accent. ⟦सुगान्⟧: from the root ⟦गम्ल⟧, in the sense of going, there is the suffix ḍa in the sense of the locus, by ⟦सुदुरोरधिकरण इति⟧, "after su and dur, in the sense of the locus". Because the suffix has the marker ḍ, the final vowel with what follows it is elided. The accent is that of the kṛt formation. ⟦निरजे⟧: from the root ⟦अज⟧, in the senses of going and throwing, there is kvip in the sense of the action, of the kind taught for sampad and the rest. The accent is that of a latter member formed with a kṛt suffix. ⟦आवन्⟧: a form in laṅ of the root ⟦अव⟧, in the senses of protecting, going and the rest. ⟦पुरुहूतं⟧: of the root hve, in the niṣṭhā, there is samprasāraṇa because it belongs to the group beginning with yaj. Lengthening by ⟦हल इति⟧, "after a consonant". The accent is by the rule on tha, atha and the rest. ⟦धमंतीः⟧: ⟦ध्या शब्दे⟧, the root in the sense of sounding. Before śap, dhama is substituted by the rule beginning ⟦प्राघ्रेत्यादिना⟧. By ⟦ना छंदसीति⟧, "optionally in the Veda", there is the lengthening of jas into a single homogeneous long vowel. When the accent due to the sārvadhātuka that replaces la, in śatṛ, has been made, the accent is that of the root.

## Ṛk 11
This Indra who, being ⟦एकः⟧, quite without a helper, filled all around (⟦आ⟧ … ⟦पप्रौ⟧) the ⟦पृथिवीं⟧, earth, ⟦उत⟧, and also, ⟦द्यां⟧, heaven — these ⟦द्वे⟧, two, heaven and earth, being ⟦समीची⟧, united with each other, and ⟦वसुमती⟧, furnished with wealth: ⟦उत⟧, and also, ⟦शूर⟧, O you who are endowed with power, you who are such, ⟦रथीः⟧, possessing a chariot, ⟦इषः⟧, wishing to stay, in ⟦नः⟧, our, ⟦समीके⟧, vicinity — the ⟦सयुजः⟧, joined together, ⟦वाजान्⟧: "they go", hence the vājas are horses; drive those horses towards us (⟦अभि⟧) ⟦अंतरिक्षात्⟧, from the mid-region. ⟦वसुमती⟧: by ⟦ना छंदसीति⟧, "optionally in the Veda", there is the single long vowel homogeneous with the preceding one. ⟦पप्रौ⟧: of the root ⟦प्रा पूरण⟧, prā in filling, in liṭ before ṇal, there is au by ⟦आत औ णल⟧, "au for ṇal after ā". Loss of accent. ⟦इषः⟧: ⟦इषु इच्छायां⟧, the root iṣ in wishing. After it there is the ka that is taught for roots with i, u or ṛ as the penultimate. Because the suffix has the marker k, there is no guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦रथीः⟧: in the sense of matup, "a chariot belongs to him", there is the suffix ī by ⟦छंदसीवनिपाविति⟧, "in the Veda, ī and vanip". The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 12

## Ṛk 14

## Ṛk 15

## Ṛk 16

## Ṛk 17

Moreover ⟦वृश्च⟧, cut, the ⟦मध्यं⟧, its middle part. ⟦प्रति⟧ … ⟦अग्रं⟧: that of it which has come up to the front, ⟦शृणीहि⟧, slay. In the midst of that tribe of rakṣases the ⟦सललूकं⟧, the rakṣas that is given to moving about, ⟦आ कीवतः⟧ — ⟦आज् मर्यादायां⟧, "ā in the sense of a limit" — from how far, from a very distant place whose measure is not known, ⟦चकर्थ⟧, make it. And having made it, at that rakṣas which is ⟦ब्रह्मद्विषे⟧, a doer of hatred to brāhmaṇas, ⟦अस्य⟧, throw, a ⟦हेतिं⟧, a slaying weapon, that is ⟦तपुषिं⟧, scorching.

The sense that has been stated Yāska has spoken: ⟦उद्दर रक्षः सहमूलमिंद्र⟧, "Draw out the rakṣas together with its root, O Indra"; ⟦मूलं मोचनाद्दा नोषणाद्वा मोहनाद्दा⟧, "mūla is from releasing, or from robbing, or from bewildering". ⟦वृश्च मध्यं प्रति शृणीह्यग्रं⟧, "Cut the middle, crush the front"; ⟦अग्रमागतं भवति⟧, "agra is what has come". ⟦आ कियतो देशात्सललूकं सल्लुब्धं भवति पापकमिति नैरुक्ताः सररूकं वा स्यात् सर्तरभ्यस्तात्⟧, "From how far a place; salalūka is what is utterly confounded, evil: so the etymologists; or it may be sararūka, from the root sṛ reduplicated". ⟦तपुषिस्तपतेर्हॆतिर्हंतेः⟧ (⟦नि. ६३⟧), "tapuṣi is from the root tap, heti from the root han".

Loss of accent. ⟦कीवतः⟧: after the word kim, in the sense "what is its measure", because it is made known by ⟦किमिदंभ्यां वो घ इति⟧, "after kim and idam, gha for the v", that vatup exists after the word kim, there is vatup. The i for the a is Vedic. The accent is that of a suffix with the marker n. ⟦हेतिं⟧: ⟦हन हिंसागत्योः⟧, the root han in injuring and going. ## Ṛk 18

⟦आसत्सि⟧: the root ⟦षद्ल⟧, in the senses of splitting and the rest. ⟦आसत्सि⟧. By ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧, "variously in the Veda", the śap is elided. ## Ṛk 19

## Ṛk 20
