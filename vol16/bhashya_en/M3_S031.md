## Introduction

The hymn beginning ⟦शासद्वह्नि⟧, of twenty-two Ṛks, is the second hymn. On it the Anukramaṇikā says: ⟦शासत् कुशिको विश्वामित्र एव वा श्रुतेरिति⟧, that is, of the hymn beginning with this word the seer is Kuśika, or Viśvāmitra himself, according to the śruti. Kuśika, the son of Iṣīratha, or Viśvāmitra himself, is the seer. Indra is the deity. The metre is triṣṭubh.

On the caturviṃśa day, at the midday pressing, in the śastra of the acchāvāka, after the ārambhaṇīyā, this hymn, which bears the name of an ahīna-hymn, is to be recited. And this is laid down in the sūtra: ⟦आस्मा इदु प्र तवसे शासद्वह्निरितीतरावहीनसूक्ते⟧ (⟦आ. ७-४⟧), that is, the hymn named there by its opening words and this hymn are the other two ahīna-hymns.

On the first day of the pṛṣṭhya and abhiplava six-day periods, at the midday pressing, in the śastra of the acchāvāka, there are three saṃpāta-hymns in the place of the ahīna-hymn. Among them this is the third saṃpāta-hymn: ⟦इमामू ष्विच्छंति त्वा शासद्वह्निरिति संपाताः⟧ (⟦आ. ७-५⟧), that is, the three hymns named by their opening words, of which this is the last, are the saṃpātas.

## Ṛk 1

Kuśika, as the occasion offers, states a certain point of the law-books: the daughter of a father who has no son, being an appointed daughter, is his heir, since her son continues the line; this is what is said by this Ṛk. The sonless father who conveys his girl to another family is called ⟦वह्नि⟧. That father, ⟦शासत्⟧, making the declaration stated in the smṛti, ⟦अभ्रातृकां प्रदास्यामि तुभ्यं कन्यामलंकृतां । अस्यां यो जायते पुत्रः स मे पुत्रो भवेदिति⟧ (⟦वासि. १७-१७⟧), "I shall give you a girl who has no brother, adorned; the son who is born of her shall be my son", ⟦गात्⟧, goes to, the ⟦नप्तं⟧ of the ⟦दुहितुः⟧, of the appointed daughter, that is, to the grandson, the son's son. **NOTE**{the supplied text glosses the word here as the grandson, the son's son, and then adds "since the daughter is accepted as a son … the daughter's son"; the reference text has neither and gives only the explanation "what comes from the grandson, the offering of the piṇḍa and the rest"; is the fuller text to stand?} Since the daughter is accepted as a son, he goes to what belongs to the daughter's son, what comes from a grandson, the offering of the piṇḍa and the rest, as a thing that is to be done. Doing what? ⟦विद्वान्⟧, knowing that "the son born of this daughter will be the maker of the svadhā offering for me"; ⟦सपर्यन्⟧, honouring with clothes, ornaments and the like the ⟦दीधितिं⟧, the bearer, of ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, of the true seed that is able to produce a son, that is, the son-in-law, her husband; so the father goes to what comes from a grandson.

⟦यत्र⟧, in which matter, the father who has no son, the begetter, ⟦ऋंजन्⟧, arranging, the ⟦सेकं⟧, the pouring of seed, the pouring of seed into her, of his daughter, his own girl who has not yet been given away, ⟦संदधन्ने⟧, joins himself, with a ⟦मनसा⟧, a mind, that is ⟦शग्मन⟧, a cause of happiness, because the grief caused by having no son departs. **NOTE**{the explanation just given, of the sonless father who arranges the marriage of his ungiven daughter with a happy mind, stands only in the supplied text; the reference text has only the explanation that follows; is it to stand?} The ⟦पिता⟧, the protector, the husband, the son-in-law, of which daughter, arranging the pouring, the pouring of seed into her, joins his own body with her with a mind that is a cause of pleasure only, and not with a mind that has a son as its cause.

Yāska too states this meaning: ⟦प्रशास्ति वोढा संतानकर्मणे दुहितुः पुत्रभावं⟧, "the one who leads her away declares, for the work of continuing the line, that the daughter has the status of a son"; ⟦दुहिता दुर्हिता दूरे हिता दोग्धर्वा⟧, "a daughter is so called as one ill placed, or as one well placed when far away, or the word is from the root meaning to milk"; ⟦नप्तारमुपागमद्घित्रं पौत्रमिति⟧, "he has come to the grandson, that is, to the daughter's son as a son's son"; ⟦विद्वान्जननयज्ञस्य रेतसो वांगादंगात्संभूतस्य हृदयादधि जातस्य मातरि प्रकृतस्य विधानं⟧, "knowing; honouring the arrangement of the sacrifice of procreation, or of the seed that has arisen from limb after limb, is born from the heart and is lodged in the mother", and so on (⟦नि. ३-४⟧).

⟦शासत्⟧: when the suffix śatṛ follows the root śās, there is no augment num, because the root is one of the reduplicated class. ⟦दुहितुः⟧: the root is duh, in the sense of filling; by the rule that begins ⟦नप्तनेष्ट⟧ the augment iṭ comes before the suffix tṛc by a ready-made form; the accent is that of a suffix marked with c. ⟦नप्तं⟧: what is in the grandson; the suffix yat by ⟦भवे छंदसी⟧; the substitute rīṅ by ⟦रीज्यत⟧; the elision by the rule ⟦यस्यॆति⟧; the elision of the r is Vedic; the accent is svarita by ⟦तित्स्वरितः⟧. **NOTE**{the supplied text ends the derivation of this word with the svarita of a suffix marked with t; the reference text does not have this clause} ⟦गात्⟧: a form of the root iṇ, to go, in the Vedic luṅ. ⟦ऋंजन्⟧: the verb ṛñj has the sense of arranging. ⟦शग्मन⟧: "they go to ⟦शं⟧, happiness": after the root gam the Uṇādi suffix ka; because the suffix is marked with k, the penultimate is elided by the rule that begins with gam and han; in the sense "good in that" the suffix yat by ⟦तत्र साधु⟧; since it has been made known at ⟦वीरवीर्यौ चे⟧ that the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧ is not invariable, here the accent is that of a suffix marked with t. ⟦दधन्वे⟧: dhav is a root of the sūtras; the form is in liṭ; the accent is that of the suffix; because of the connection with a form of yad the verb does not lose its accent.

## Ṛk 2

It has been said that a daughter who has no brother, because she is made an appointed daughter, has a share in the inheritance. He now says that a daughter who has a brother has no share in the inheritance. The ⟦तान्वः⟧, the son born of the body, the legitimate son, did not ⟦अरैक्⟧, does not make over, does not give, the ⟦रिक्षं⟧, the father's wealth, to the ⟦जामये⟧, to the sister. What then? Of the ⟦सनितुः⟧, of the husband who takes her as his share, ⟦गर्भं⟧, the accusative standing in the sense of the genitive, the ⟦निधानं⟧ of the embryo, the receptacle for the deposit of the pouring of seed, he ⟦चकार⟧ her: he makes her one who is consecrated by the taking of her hand; but he does not give her an inheritance: this is the intention. For Yājñavalkya's smṛti says: ⟦असंस्कृतास्तु संस्कार्या भ्रातृभिः पूर्वसंस्कृतैः⟧, "those who have not been consecrated are to be consecrated by the brothers who were consecrated before", ⟦भगिन्यश्च निजवादंशाद्दश्वांतं तु तुरीयकमिति⟧, "and the sisters too, the brothers giving a fourth share out of their own share" (⟦२-१२४⟧).

Raising the doubt, "since offspring, female and male, do not differ in being begotten by one and the same person, what is the distinction of male offspring?", he shows the distinction. ⟦यदि⟧, although, the ⟦मातरो⟧, the mother and father, ⟦जनयंत⟧, beget, ⟦वह्निं⟧, offspring female and male of that kind: the one who is not ⟦वह्नि⟧ is the woman; the ⟦वह्नि⟧ is the man, because he leads away his own wife; the one who is not so and the one who is so are together called by the one word; yet of these two ⟦अन्यः⟧, the one, the male, becomes the doer of ⟦सुकृतोः⟧, of the good rite of offering the piṇḍa and the rest; the other, the female, is ⟦ऋंधन्⟧, is only made to prosper with clothes, ornaments and the like. Because he performs the offering of the piṇḍa and the rest, the son is entitled to the inheritance; the daughter is not so, and therefore is not entitled to it; she is only given to another.

And so the Nirukta says: ⟦न जामये भगिन्यै⟧, "not to the ⟦जामये⟧, to the sister"; ⟦जामिरन्येऽस्यां जनयंति जामपत्यं जमतेर्वा स्याद्गतिकर्मणो निर्गमनप्राया भवति⟧, "she is so called because others beget ⟦जा⟧, offspring, in her; or the word may be from the root jam, which means to go: she is for the most part one who goes out"; ⟦तान्व आत्मजः पुत्रो रिक्षं पारिचत्पादाच्च कारैनां गर्भनिधानीं सनितुर्हस्तग्राहस्य⟧, "the son born of oneself did not leave, did not give, the inheritance; he made her the receptacle of the embryo of the one who takes her as his share, of the one who takes her hand"; ⟦यदिह मातरोऽजनयंत वह्निं पुत्रमवह्निं च स्त्रियमन्यतरः संतानकर्ता भवति पुमान्दायादोऽन्यतरॊर्धयित्वा जामिः प्रदीयते परस्मै⟧, "when here the parents begot a son, who leads away, and a woman, who does not, one of the two becomes the continuer of the line, the male, the heir; the other, the sister, having been made to prosper, is given away to another" (⟦नि ३-६⟧).

⟦तान्वः⟧: because the body is the seat of the breath, the body is a thing that has breath; in the sense "a modification of that", at the rule ⟦प्राणिरजतादिभ्यो⟧, because a rule that is given by means of a technical name is not invariable, there is no guṇa. **NOTE**{the reference text reads here "by the rule … the suffix añ", where the supplied text has only "at the rule …" and does not name the suffix} ⟦रिक्कं⟧: the root is ric, to empty; ⟦पात्यतुदिवचिरिचिभ्यस्थक्⟧, the suffix thak; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦आरैक्⟧: of the root ric, to empty, in luṅ, before sic, the vṛddhi by the rule that begins ⟦वदव्रजे⟧; there is no augment iṭ, by ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧; the two elisions, that after a consonant and the rest and that of the final of a conjunct; the verb loses its accent. ⟦सनितुः⟧: a form, with the suffix tṛc, of the root ṣaṇ, to take as one's share. ⟦निधानं⟧: a form of the root dhā with the suffix lyuṭ; the accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦मातरः⟧: where by the rule ⟦पिता मात्रे⟧ the word for the father ought to remain, the remaining of the word for the mother is Vedic; the plural in place of the dual is to show honour. ⟦जनयंत⟧: a form of the root jan with ṇic. ⟦वह्निं⟧: by ⟦पुमान् स्त्रिया⟧ (⟦पा. १-२-६७⟧) the word ⟦वह्नि⟧ alone remains. ⟦सुकृतोः⟧: after the root kṛ, the Uṇādi suffix ktu. ⟦ऋंधन्⟧: the root is ṛdh, to prosper; the class-sign śnam comes by interchange.

## Ṛk 3

O Indra, for the ⟦प्रयक्षे⟧, for the sacrifice, of you who are ⟦अरुषस्य⟧, shining, Agni, ⟦रेजमानः⟧, quivering, with the ⟦जुह्वा⟧, with his flames, ⟦जज्ञे⟧, produced, ⟦पुत्रान्⟧, rays, that are ⟦महः⟧, abundant. The ⟦गर्भो⟧ of these rays, their enfolding in the form of water, was great. Moreover the union of these rays, ⟦आ जातं⟧, born on all sides in the form of plants, was ⟦महि⟧, great. Moreover the ⟦प्रवृत्⟧ of these rays, their going on in this manner, becomes ⟦मही⟧, great, by the ⟦यज्ञैः⟧, by the oblations of Soma that are fit for the sacrifice, of ⟦हर्यश्वस्य⟧, of Indra. **NOTE**{the supplied text has "oblations of Soma"; the reference text has "oblations of Soma and ghee"}

⟦जज्ञे⟧: a form in liṭ of the root jan, to come into being; the sense of the causative is contained in it. ⟦जुह्वा⟧: after the root hu, by ⟦जुहोतेर्द्वै दीर्घश्चे⟧, the suffix kvip, the reduplication, and the lengthening of the root; the svarita by the rule ⟦उदात्तस्वरितयो⟧. ⟦हर्यश्वस्य⟧: "he whose two horses are the two bays": in a bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its own accent.

## Ṛk 4

The ⟦जैत्रीः⟧, the conquering clans, the Maruts, ⟦असचंत⟧, came together from all sides, to Indra, who was ⟦स्पृधानं⟧, contending with Vṛtra. And those Maruts, when that Vṛtra was slain, ⟦निरजानन्⟧, knew as having come out, from ⟦तमसः⟧, from Vṛtra whose form is darkness, the ⟦महि⟧, great, ⟦ज्योतिः⟧, the brilliance called the sun. Then the ⟦उषसः⟧, ⟦जानतीः⟧, knowing Indra as the slayer of Vṛtra and the sun as risen, ⟦आयन्⟧, went, straight towards him. **NOTE**{the supplied text has "when that Vṛtra was slain" and "then … Indra as the slayer of Vṛtra"; the reference text has neither and says only that the Dawns knew the risen sun} Then of the ⟦गवां⟧, of the rays, that had arisen there, Indra, ⟦एकः⟧, he alone without a second, became the ⟦पतिः⟧.

⟦जैत्रीः⟧: a form of the root ji with the suffix tṛn; vṛddhi stands in place of guṇa by interchange; the accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦असचंत⟧: a form in laṅ of the root ṣac, to be joined together. ⟦स्पृधानं⟧: the root is spardh, to contend; in the Veda the substitute kānac; because reduplication is optional in the Veda there is no reduplication; the accent is that of a suffix marked with c. ⟦अजानन्⟧: a form in laṅ of the root jñā, to understand; the verb loses its accent. ⟦आयन्⟧: a form in laṅ of the root ay, read in ⟦अय पय⟧ in the sense of going.

## Ṛk 5

Formerly, it is told, the cows of the Aṅgirases were carried off by the asuras named Paṇis and placed in a certain hidden mountain. And those Aṅgirases praised Indra in order to get them back. And Indra, being praised, sent the bitch of the gods to search for the cows. And she, intent on the search for the cows, found that place. Indra, informed by her, brought those cows and gave them to the Aṅgirases: such is the story of the legend. And so the meaning of this Ṛk is as follows.

The ⟦धीरा⟧, the wise, ⟦सप्त विप्रा⟧, the sages, the Aṅgirases, seven in number, looking ⟦अभि⟧, towards, the cows that were ⟦सतीः⟧, shut up, in the ⟦वीळ्य⟧, in the firm mountain, ⟦आत्यंदन्⟧: they uncovered the hiding place; they showed disregard. **NOTE**{the supplied text has "shut up" and adds "they uncovered the hiding place"; the reference text has "hidden" and only "they showed disregard"} Then those Aṅgirases, having settled in their mind that the cows were in the cave of the mountain, ⟦प्राचा⟧, by the very path by which they had gone in, by that former path, ⟦अहिन्वन्⟧, drove out, those cows. Or else, ⟦प्राचा⟧: that which above all goes to, reaches, Indra is so called; with a ⟦मनसा⟧ that has Indra for its object; what is thought is ⟦मनः⟧, a hymn of praise; with that hymn of praise they ⟦अहिन्वन्⟧, made him grow: they made Indra pleased. And having praised, they ⟦अविंदन्⟧, got, the ⟦विश्वां⟧, the whole, of the stolen cattle, which are ⟦ऋतस्य पथ्यां⟧, good on the path of the sacrifice. Then that Indra, ⟦प्रजानन्नित्⟧, knowing well, ⟦ता⟧, those deeds of the Aṅgirases, Indra, honouring the Aṅgirases with ⟦नमसा⟧, with reverence, ⟦विवेश⟧, entered, the mountain that they had occupied.

⟦आत्यंदन्⟧: of the root tṛd, ⟦उत्तृदिर्हिंसानादरयो⟧, to injure and to disregard, in laṅ; the class-sign śnam because the root is of the rudh class; the elision of its a by ⟦श्नसो⟧; the verb loses its accent. ⟦प्राचा⟧: the case-ending is udātta by ⟦अंचेश्छंदस्य सर्वनामस्थान⟧. ⟦अहिन्सन्⟧: a form in laṅ of the root hi, to go and to make grow. ⟦अविंदन्⟧: of the root vid, to get, in laṅ; the augment num because the root is of the muc class; the verb loses its accent. ⟦पथ्यां⟧: in the sense "good on the path" the suffix yat by ⟦तत्र साधु⟧; the stem having the name bha by ⟦यचि भ⟧, its last vowel with what follows is elided by ⟦नस्तद्धित⟧; because the rule ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧ is not invariable, the accent is svarita by ⟦तित्स्वरित⟧. ⟦विवेश⟧: a form in liṭ of the root viś, to enter.

## Ṛk 6

⟦यदि⟧, when, ⟦सरमा⟧, the bitch of the gods named Saramā, sent by Indra to search for the cows, ⟦विदत्⟧, found, the ⟦रुग्णं⟧, the broken door, of the ⟦अद्रेः⟧, then Indra ⟦कः⟧, made, that is, gave to her, ⟦महि⟧, great, ⟦पाथो⟧, food consisting of the produce of the cow and the like, which was ⟦पूर्व्यं⟧, promised before, at the time of sending her, in the words "I shall make your offspring eaters of food", and ⟦सध्र्यक्⟧, accompanied by other things to eat as well. Then ⟦सुपदी⟧, having good feet, that Saramā ⟦नयत्⟧, reached, the ⟦अग्रं⟧, the edge, of the ⟦अक्षराणां⟧, of the cows that are free from decay. How so? He says: since, ⟦जानती⟧, recognising, ⟦प्रथमा⟧, first, their ⟦रवं⟧, their sound of lowing, she ⟦गात्⟧, went, ⟦अच्छ⟧, facing the sound.

⟦विदत्⟧: of the root vid, to get, in luṅ; because the root has the marker ḷ, the substitute aṅ for cli; the accent is that of the substitute. ⟦सरमा⟧: the root is sṛ, to go; the Uṇādi suffix ama; "she runs"; Yāska says ⟦सरमा सरणादिति⟧, "Saramā is so called from running"; the accent is that of the suffix. **NOTE**{the reference text gives a Nirukta reference, 11.24, for Yāska's words; the supplied text gives none} ⟦रुग्णं⟧: of the root ruj, to break, before the niṣṭhā suffix, the n by the rule on roots that have the marker o; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦पाथः⟧: the root is pā, to protect; by ⟦अन्ने च⟧ (⟦उ ४-२०४⟧) the suffix asun; the augment thuṭ; "it protects", so ⟦पाथो⟧ is food; the accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦सध्र्यक्⟧: the root is añc, to go and to honour; the suffix kvin by the rule that begins ⟦ऋत्वि⟧; "it goes along with"; the substitute sadhri by ⟦सहस्य सध्रि⟧; the change to a guttural that belongs to the suffix kvin; by the statement ⟦अद्रिसध्र्योरंतोदात्तत्वनिपातनं⟧, "the laying down of the udātta on the final of adri and sadhri is in order to bar the accent of the kṛt suffix", the substitute sadhri has the udātta on its final; when the semivowel has been substituted, the svarita after the semivowel by ⟦उदात्तस्वरितयोर्यणः स्वरित⟧. **NOTE**{the reference text gives a reference, Pā. 6.3.95.1, for the statement on adri and sadhri; the supplied text gives none} ⟦कः⟧: of the root kṛ in luṅ, before sip, the elision luk of cli by the rule that begins ⟦मंत्रे घसह्वरे⟧; when guṇa has been made, the elision of sip by the rule on what follows a consonant and the rest; the visarjanīya; the verb loses its accent. ⟦नयत्⟧: a form in laṅ of the root nī. ⟦सुपदी⟧: the feminine suffix ṅīp by ⟦पादोऽन्यतरस्यां⟧ (⟦पा, ४-१-८⟧). ⟦गात्⟧: a form in luṅ of the root iṇ.

## Ṛk 7

⟦विप्रतमो⟧, exceedingly wise, ⟦सखीयन्⟧, desiring for himself friendship with the Aṅgirases, Indra ⟦अगच्छत्⟧: he did indeed go to the mountain occupied by the cows. That ⟦अद्रिः⟧, desiring friendship, ⟦असूदयत्⟧, sent out, for the ⟦सुकृते⟧, for Indra who makes war well, the ⟦गर्भं⟧, the embryo consisting of the cows. Together with the ⟦युवभिः⟧, with the ever youthful Maruts, ⟦मखस्यन्⟧, desiring the wealth of cows of the Aṅgirases, the ⟦मर्यो⟧, Indra the slayer of the asuras, ⟦ससान⟧, got as his share those cows that the asuras had carried off. ⟦अथ⟧, thereafter, ⟦अंगिराः⟧, the seer Aṅgiras, the best among the seven Aṅgirases, having received those cows from Indra, ⟦सद्यः⟧, at that very time, became ⟦अर्चन्⟧, one who honours Indra.

⟦अगच्छत्⟧: a form in laṅ of the root gam. ⟦सखीयन्⟧: the suffix kyac by ⟦सुपः अत्मनः क्यच्⟧. ⟦असूदयत्⟧: a form in laṅ of the causative of the root ṣūd, to cast out. ⟦ससान⟧: a form in liṭ, with ṇal, of the root ṣaṇ, to take as one's share; the accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦मखस्यन्⟧: by ⟦छंदसि परेच्छाया⟧ the suffix kyac comes also in the sense of a wish on behalf of another; the augment suk by the statement ⟦सर्वप्रातिपदिकेभ्यो लालसायां सुग्वक्तव्य⟧, "after all nominal stems, in the sense of longing, suk is to be taught"; the accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦अंगिराः⟧: the root is sṛ, to go; when the word for a live coal stands before it, the suffix asun comes after the root sṛ; the i in the place of what follows the g of the preceding word, the elision of its final, and the elision of the root are laid down as a ready-made form by the sūtra ⟦अंगिराः⟧ (⟦उ. ४-२३५⟧). **NOTE**{the supplied text gives an Uṇādi reference for this sūtra; the reference text gives none} "In past time he issued from the coals", hence the seer Aṅgiras. Yāska says ⟦अंगारेष्ट्वंगिरा⟧, "Aṅgiras in the coals". **NOTE**{the reference text gives a Nirukta reference, 3.17, for Yāska's words; the supplied text gives none} The accent is that of a suffix marked with n.

## Ṛk 8

This Indra who is the ⟦प्रतिमानं⟧ of ⟦सतः सत⟧, of every excellent thing: that against which everything is measured, with which it is compared, is ⟦प्रतिमानं⟧, a counterpart. **NOTE**{the supplied text has "of every excellent thing"; the reference text has "of every excellent wealth"} Or else, he is the ⟦प्रतिमानं⟧ of ⟦सतः सत⟧, of everything that is born: by his own greatness he measures everything, that is, he marks its limit as "so much". And he is ⟦पुरोभूः⟧: "he reaches the enemies in front in battle"; he ⟦वेद⟧, knows, ⟦विश्वा जनिम⟧, all that are born. He ⟦हंति⟧ ⟦शुष्णं⟧, the asura named Śuṣṇa. ⟦पदवीः⟧, the best of poets, ⟦गव्युः⟧, desiring the wealth of cows of the Aṅgirases, our friend Indra, coming from ⟦दिवो⟧, from the world of heaven, ⟦प्रार्चन्⟧, honouring greatly, us his ⟦सखीन्⟧, ⟦निरमुंचत्⟧, may he free us entirely, from ⟦अवद्यात्⟧, from the evil that consists in oppression by Vṛtra.

⟦प्रतिमानं⟧: "it measures", so ⟦मानं⟧; the Uṇādi suffix nan; or else the suffix lyuṭ in the sense of the instrument. ⟦पुरोभूः⟧: the root is bhū, to reach; the suffix kvip. ⟦वेद⟧: a form in laṭ of the root vid. ⟦जनिम⟧: the root is jan, ⟦जनी प्रादुर्भावे⟧, to come into being; by ⟦जनिहृभ्यामिमनि⟧ the suffix imanin; "those that were born in past time", so ⟦जनिम⟧; the elision luk of the case-ending by ⟦सुपां सुलु⟧; the accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦हंति⟧: a form in laṭ of the root han; because the sentence is a separate one the verb does not lose its accent. ⟦शुष्णं⟧: "he dries up", so he is called by this name. ⟦प्र णः⟧: the ṇ in the Saṃhitā by ⟦उपसर्गाद्बहुल⟧. ⟦पदवीः⟧: the root is vī, in the senses of desire and the rest; the suffix kvip; "he ⟦वेत्ति⟧ words, he makes them fit with one another", so the word means a poet. ⟦गव्युः⟧: after the word for a cow, the suffix kyac by ⟦परेच्छायां छंदसी⟧; the substitute ending in v by ⟦वांतो यि⟧; the suffix u by ⟦क्याच्छंदसी⟧; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अर्चन्⟧: a form of the root arc with the suffix śatṛ. ⟦सखीन्⟧: in the Saṃhitā the n becomes ru by ⟦दीर्घादटी⟧; the nasal substitute for the preceding sound by ⟦अत्रानुनासिक⟧. ⟦अमुंचत्⟧: of the root muc, to release, in laṅ, the augment num by ⟦शे मुचादीना⟧; the verb loses its accent. ⟦अवद्यात्⟧: of the root vad, ⟦वद व्यक्तायां वाची⟧, to speak clearly, preceded by the negative, in the sense of what is to be blamed, the suffix yat by the ready-made form in ⟦वद्यपण्यवर्या⟧ (⟦पा, ३-१ १०१⟧).

## Ṛk 9

Joined with a ⟦मनसा⟧, a mind, that is ⟦गव्यता⟧, desiring cows, ⟦कृण्वानासः⟧, making, by ⟦अर्कैः⟧, by hymns of praise that are worthy of honour, a ⟦गातुं⟧ for ⟦अमृतत्वाय⟧, for the attaining of godhood: "they go by it", so the word means a path; making a path that is the means of attaining immortality, those Aṅgirases ⟦निषेदुः⟧, sat down. **NOTE**{after "sat down" the reference text has "they sat at the rite called sattra; this very seat of these Aṅgirases, this very sitting at the rite called sattra", glossing three more words of the Ṛk; the supplied text passes at once to "the sitting at the rite called sattra"} The sitting at the rite called a sattra is ⟦भूरि नु⟧, abundant: it is indeed a great means for the attaining of immortality. How so? He says: ⟦येन⟧, by which sattra rite, which is ⟦ऋतेन⟧, true, those Aṅgirases ⟦असिसासन्⟧, wished to obtain as their share, the ⟦मासान्⟧, the months that are formed of the abhiplava and pṛṣṭhya six-day periods.

⟦गव्यता⟧: a form with the suffix kyac after the word for a cow; the substitute ending in v by ⟦वांतो यि⟧; the accent is that of the single substitute. ⟦सेदुः⟧: of the root sad in liṭ, the e and the elision of the reduplication by ⟦अत एकहल्मध्येऽनादेशादेर्लिटी⟧; the verb loses its accent. ⟦कृण्वानासः⟧: of the root kṛv, ⟦कृवि हिंसाकरणयो⟧, to injure and to make, the suffix cānaś in the sense of a habit; the suffix u by ⟦धिन्विकृण्वोरच्चे⟧; the accent is that of a suffix marked with c. ⟦गातुं⟧: the root is gā, to go; the suffix tu in the sense of the instrument by ⟦कमिमनिजनिगाभाषायाहिभ्य⟧; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦असिषासन्⟧: of the root ṣaṇ, to take as one's share, before the suffix san, the ā by ⟦जनसनखनां सन्बलो⟧; the i of the reduplication by ⟦सन्यत⟧; the ṣ in the Saṃhitā by ⟦सनोतेरन⟧; from the stem ending in that suffix, a form in laṅ; because of the connection with a form of yad the verb does not lose its accent.

## Ṛk 10

Looking ⟦अभि⟧, towards, their ⟦स्वं⟧, their own wealth of cows, ⟦संपश्यमानाः⟧, recognising them as belonging to themselves, "these cows are mine", those Aṅgirases, ⟦दुघानाः⟧ ⟦पयो⟧, milking those cows of their milk, for the support of the ⟦रेतसः⟧ that is ⟦प्रत्नस्य⟧, born of old, that is, of the son who is indicated by it, ⟦अमदन्⟧, rejoiced. The ⟦घोषो⟧ of these Aṅgirases, the great sound that arose from their joy, or that was born of their praise, ⟦व्यतपत्⟧ the ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth: pervading those two, it heated them. Then those Aṅgirases made, in ⟦जाते⟧, in the thing, moving and unmoving, that had become their own, a ⟦निष्ठां⟧, a standing as it was before. In the same way, over the ⟦गोषु⟧, the cows that had been brought back, they ⟦अदधुः⟧, set, ⟦वीरान्⟧, men exceedingly brave in the work of guarding.

⟦संपश्यमानाः⟧: of the root dṛś preceded by sam, the ātmanepada by the statement that it is to be taught of dṛś also; the suffix śānac having been made anudātta by the accent of a sārvadhātuka that stands for a la, the accent is that of the root. ⟦अमदन्⟧: a form of the root mad, to rejoice, in luṅ, with ṇic and aṅ; the verb loses its accent. **NOTE**{the supplied text seems to read "with ṇic and aṅ"; the reference text has "before sic, with aṅ"; which is meant?} ⟦दुघानाः⟧: from the root duh, in the sense of filling, the suffix śānac; the elision luk of śap because the root is of the ad class; the gh is Vedic; the first syllable is udātta because the word is of the vṛṣa class. ⟦अतपत्⟧: of the root tap preceded by vi there is no ātmanepada here, because it is transitive; the form is in laṅ. ⟦घोषः⟧: the root is ghuṣ, to sound aloud; the word ends in the suffix ghañ; the accent is that of a suffix marked with ñ. ⟦निष्ठां⟧: the root is sthā, ⟦ष्ठा गतिनिवृत्तौ⟧, to cease from moving; the suffix kvip by ⟦स्थः क च⟧ (⟦पा. ३-२-७७⟧); the accent is that of the latter member, a kṛt form. ⟦अदधुः⟧: of the root dhā in laṅ, the substitute jus for jhi by ⟦सिजभ्यस्ते⟧; because the sentence is a separate one the verb does not lose its accent. ⟦गोषु⟧: where the udātta of the case-ending is due by ⟦सावेकाच⟧, because it is prohibited by ⟦न गोश्वन्नि⟧ the accent is that of the nominal stem.

## Ṛk 11

That Indra, being accompanied by the ⟦जातेभिः⟧, by the Maruts who have become his helpers, is ⟦वृत्रहा⟧, the slayer of the asura Vṛtra. ⟦सेदु⟧, that Indra alone, being accompanied by the Maruts who are ⟦अर्कैः⟧, worthy of honour, and for that very reason ⟦हव्यैः⟧, worthy of oblations, ⟦उदसृजत्⟧ the ⟦उस्रियाः⟧: "the oblation in the form of milk and the rest dwells upwards in them", so the word means cows; or else, "milk, curds, butter and the rest dwell upwards in them in due order"; Yāska says ⟦उस्रियेति गोनामोत्साविणोऽस्यां भोगाः⟧, "this is a name of the cow: in her are enjoyments that flow forth" (⟦नि. ४-१९⟧); those cows he released, he let them go duly for the sake of the sacrifice. Since this is so, therefore the ⟦गौः⟧, ⟦भरंती⟧, bearing, the oblation of milk and the rest that is ⟦धृतवत्⟧, accompanied by ghee, and for that very reason ⟦उरूची⟧: "she goes to, she reaches, the abundant agnihotra and the rest, in order to give the oblation"; ⟦जेन्या⟧, to be won, excellent, ⟦दुदुहे⟧, was milked, of ⟦स्वाद्म⟧, of sweeter milk and the rest, for ⟦अस्मै⟧, for this sacrificer.

⟦उस्रियाः⟧: from the root vas, ⟦वस निवास⟧, to dwell, the suffix rak by the rule that begins ⟦स्थायितंची⟧; after the word so formed, the suffix gha in the sense of the word itself; the accent is that of the suffix. **NOTE**{the reference text adds that the suffix gha comes because the word belongs to the pṛṣodara class; the supplied text does not say this} Or else the root is sṛ, to go: after sṛ preceded by ut, the suffix ka; the elision of the final of the first member is Vedic; here too the suffix gha in the sense of the word itself. ⟦असृजत्⟧: a form in laṅ of the root sṛj, to let go. ⟦उरूची⟧: "she goes to the wide", with the suffix kvin; the feminine suffix ṅīp by the additional statement ⟦आंचतेश्चोपसंख्यान⟧, "and after añc"; the final is udātta by the accent that follows the removal of an udātta. ⟦दुदुहे⟧: a form in liṭ of the root duh, in the sense of filling. ⟦जेन्या⟧: after the root ji, the suffix kenya in the sense of a kṛtya suffix; the elision of the i is Vedic; the accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 12

The Aṅgirases who were performing the sattra ⟦संचक्रुः⟧, made, for ⟦अस्मै⟧, for this Indra, who is ⟦पित्रे चित्⟧, a protector, a ⟦सदनं⟧, a highest place, that is ⟦महि⟧, great, and ⟦त्विषीमद्⟧, full of light. **NOTE**{the gloss of the word for the seat is damaged in the supplied text and seems to read "highest"; the reference text has "lofty"; which is meant?} If it is asked how, the answer is: since those Aṅgirases, ⟦सुकृतः⟧, who had earned their deeds, ⟦वि हि ख्यन्⟧, did indeed show in a special way, such a place, one fitting for Indra. How so? He says: ⟦आसीनाः⟧, attending the sattra, those Aṅgirases, ⟦विष्णभ्यंतो⟧ the two ⟦जनित्रि⟧, heaven and earth, the two that give birth to the whole world, with ⟦स्कंभनेन⟧, with the mid-region as the means of propping, that is, making them firmly propped so that those two worlds do not fall down, ⟦वि मिन्वन्⟧ that Indra, who is ⟦रभसं⟧, swift, above, in the world of heaven: they set him there in a special way so that he might accept the oblations.

⟦चक्रुः⟧: a form of the root kṛ in liṭ with us; the verb loses its accent. ⟦ख्यन्⟧: the root is khyā, ⟦ख्या प्रकथने⟧, to tell; in its luṅ the substitute aṅ for cli by the rule that teaches aṅ after as, vac and khyā; the elision of the ā by ⟦आतो लोप इटी⟧; because of the connection with the particle hi the verb does not lose its accent. ⟦विष्णभ्यंतः⟧: skambh, ⟦स्कन्भु रोधने⟧, to obstruct, is a root of the sūtras; from it a form with the suffix śatṛ; the ṣ by ⟦वेः स्कभ्नातेर्नित्यं⟧ (⟦पा. ८-३-७७⟧); the accent is that of the suffix śatṛ. ⟦जनित्रि⟧: the feminine suffix ṅīp by ⟦ऋन्नेभ्यौ⟧; the elision of ṇi by the ready-made form in ⟦जनिता मंत्र⟧; the long vowel for the two like vowels by ⟦वा छंदसी⟧. ⟦आसीनाः⟧: from the root ās, to sit, the suffix śānac; the ī by ⟦ईदासः⟧ (⟦पा ७-२-८३⟧); the accent of a sārvadhātuka that stands for a la having been applied to śānac, the accent is that of the root. ⟦मिन्वन्⟧: a form in laṅ of the root mi, ⟦डुमिञ् प्रक्षेपण⟧, to throw; the verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 13

⟦यदि धात्⟧, if the ⟦मही⟧, the great, ⟦धिषणा⟧, the praise that is being made by us with Indra as its object, made, for the ⟦रोदस्योः शिश्नथे⟧, for the separation of heaven and earth from each other, a means that is ⟦सद्योवृधं⟧, growing at that very moment, and ⟦विभ्वं⟧, mighty, able to hold those two, then, he being present there, Indra ⟦यस्मिन्⟧, in whom, the ⟦गिरः⟧, the words of praise, that are ⟦अनवद्या⟧, free from fault, are ⟦समीचीः⟧, joined together: in ⟦इंद्राय⟧ there is an interchange of case-ending; of such an Indra ⟦तविषीः⟧, all the powers, are ⟦अनुत्ताः⟧, not set in motion by another, established by his very nature.

⟦धिषणा⟧: the root is dhṛṣ, to be bold; after dhṛṣ the suffix kyu by ⟦धिष च संज्ञाया⟧; dhiṣ is substituted for the root; "one is bold by means of it", so the word means praise; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦शिश्नथे⟧: the root śnath has the sense of injuring; after it the suffix kvip; because that suffix is a sārvadhātuka by ⟦छंदस्युभयथे⟧, śap comes; in its place ślu by ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧; the i of the reduplication by ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧; the accent is that of the root. ⟦धात्⟧: a form of the root dhā in the Vedic luṅ; because of the connection with ⟦यदि⟧ the verb does not lose its accent. ⟦विभ्वं⟧: after the root bhū the suffix kvip; the substitution of the semivowel by ⟦छंदस्युभयथे⟧; the svarita by ⟦उदात्तस्वरितयोर्यण⟧. ⟦अनुत्ताः⟧: of the root nud, ⟦णुद प्रेरण⟧, to set in motion, preceded by the negative, before the niṣṭhā suffix, the t of the niṣṭhā does not become n, by the ready-made form in the rule that begins ⟦नसत्तनिषत्तानुत्ते⟧; the accent is that of the negative.

## Ṛk 14

O Indra, I ⟦वश्मि⟧, I long for, ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦महि⟧, great, ⟦सख्यं⟧. And I long for the ⟦शक्ती⟧, the gifts that belong to you. To you, ⟦वृत्रघ्ने⟧, the slayer of the asura Vṛtra, ⟦पूर्वीः⟧, many, ⟦नियुतो⟧, mares, ⟦आयंति⟧, come in order to carry. To you, the ⟦सूरेः⟧, the wise, we ⟦अगन्म⟧, we bring, a ⟦महि⟧, great, ⟦स्तोत्रं⟧, and ⟦अवः⟧, food in the form of the oblation. O ⟦मघवन्⟧, O Indra, do you, being such, ⟦बोधि⟧, be aware, thinking "I am ⟦सु⟧, well, the ⟦गोपाः⟧, the protector, of us".

⟦सख्यं⟧: the suffix ya in the sense of the state by ⟦सख्युर्य⟧; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦वश्मि⟧: the root is vaś, to desire; the form is in laṭ. ⟦नियुतः⟧: the root is yu, ⟦यु मिश्रणे⟧, to mix; the suffix kvip; "they thoroughly join, they mix, themselves to the chariots", so the word means mares. ⟦अगन्म⟧: of the root gam in laṅ, in the first person plural, the elision luk of śap by ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧; the n in place of the m by the rule ⟦मोश्चॆति⟧; the verb loses its accent. ⟦बोधि⟧: a form of the root budh in loṭ, with dhi in place of hi; the verb loses its accent. ⟦गोपा:⟧: the root is gup, ⟦गुपू रक्षणे⟧, to protect; the suffix āya by the rule that begins ⟦गुपूधूपे⟧; after the stem ending in that, the suffix kvip; the elision of the a and the elision of the y; the accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 15

This Indra who, ⟦विविद्वान्⟧, causing us, his ⟦सखिभ्यो⟧, who ask, to obtain a ⟦महि⟧, great, ⟦क्षेत्रं⟧, cultivated land and the like, and ⟦पुरु⟧, abundant, ⟦चंद्रं⟧, gold, ⟦आदित्⟧, thereafter, ⟦सम्मैरत्⟧, duly sent forth, also ⟦चरथं⟧, what consists of moving things, cows and the like: the meaning is that he gave it. ⟦दीद्यानो⟧, shining, that same Indra, being ⟦साकं⟧, accompanied, by the ⟦नृभिः⟧, by the leaders, the Maruts who are his helpers in his work, ⟦अजनत्⟧, brought forth, ⟦सूर्यं⟧, the Āditya who carries time along; ⟦उषसं⟧, the time of dawn; ⟦गातुं⟧: "on her they sing, they praise, Indra and the other gods", so the word means the earth; her; and ⟦अग्निं⟧, the fire in the form of the āhavanīya and the rest, which is the means of the rites such as the agnihotra.

⟦पुरु चंद्रं⟧: in the Saṃhitā the augment suṭ by ⟦चंद्रोत्तरपदे मंत्र⟧; its ś comes by the change to a palatal; at the time of the Pada text there is no augment suṭ. ⟦पुरु⟧: the accent is that of the suffix ku. ⟦चंद्रं⟧: the suffix rak; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦चरथं⟧: the root car has the sense of going; after it the Uṇādi suffix atha; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦ऐरत्⟧: of the root īr, to go, in laṅ, the augment āṭ by ⟦आडजादीना⟧; the verb loses its accent. ⟦अजनत्⟧: a form in laṅ of the causative of the root jan; the elision luk of ṇi by ⟦बहुलमन्यत्रापि संज्ञाच्छंदसो⟧. ⟦गातुं⟧: the root is gai, ⟦गै⟧, to sound; the suffix tu by ⟦गापायाहिभ्य⟧; the accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 16

⟦दमूनाः⟧: he whose mind is subdued is so called, one of subdued mind. Or else, "he is calm", so he is ⟦दमूनाः⟧; the meaning is, one who has come to rest. This very Indra ⟦प्रासृजत्⟧, created, ⟦चित्⟧, even, the ⟦अपः⟧, the waters, which are ⟦विभ्वो⟧, pervading, ⟦सध्रीचीः⟧, joined with one another, and ⟦विश्वश्चंद्रा⟧, gladdening all, or "those from which all have gladness"; waters that are of this kind. **NOTE**{the supplied text takes the particle with the waters, "even the waters"; the reference text does not, and takes it later with the nights, "by days and by nights also"} And those waters, having been created, ⟦पुनानाः⟧, purifying, the ⟦मध्वो⟧, the Somas that are full of sweetness, by the ⟦कविभिः⟧ ⟦पवित्रैः⟧, by Agni, Vāyu and Sūrya, and being ⟦धनुत्रीः⟧, gladdeners of the whole world, ⟦हिन्वंति⟧ by ⟦द्युभिः⟧, by days, and by ⟦अक्तुभिः⟧, by nights: the meaning is that night and day they make the whole world intent on its several activities.

⟦दमूनाः⟧: the root is dam, ⟦दमु उपशमे⟧, to be calm; the suffix ūnasi by ⟦दमेरूनसि⟧; "he is calm", so he is ⟦दमूनाः⟧. Or else, the word for "subdued" becomes da, and the a that follows the m of the word for "mind" becomes ū; this is Vedic. ⟦अस्पृजत्⟧: the root is sṛj, to let go. ⟦हिन्वंति⟧: the root is hi, to go; the verb loses its accent. ⟦धनुत्रीः⟧: from the root dhan, ⟦धन धान्य⟧, in the sense of grain, the suffix utra because the root is of the group that begins with aśitra; the feminine suffix ṅīn is to be seen as taught among śārṅgarava and the rest; the accent is that of a suffix marked with n.

## Ṛk 17

O Indra, by the ⟦मंहना⟧, by the greatness, the power, of you who are ⟦सूर्यस्य⟧, the good impeller of the world, those ⟦उभे⟧ ⟦कृष्णे⟧, day and night, which are ⟦वसुधिती⟧, furnished with the holding of the things that are to be dwelt in, and ⟦यजत्रे⟧, worthy of worship, ⟦अनु जिहाते⟧: they go on returning again and again. Moreover the Maruts, who are ⟦ऋजिप्या⟧, of straight course, ⟦सखायः⟧, your friends, and ⟦काम्याः⟧, desirable, ⟦वृजध्यै⟧, for the warding off, the overpowering, of the asuras who put obstacles in the way of the rites, following the ⟦महिमानं⟧, the power, of ⟦ते⟧, of you, ⟦यत्⟧, of whom it is, have power ⟦परि⟧, on all sides.

⟦जिहाते⟧: the root is hā, ⟦ओहाज् गतौ⟧, to go; it is of the hu class; the i of the reduplication by ⟦भृञामिदि⟧; the verb loses its accent. ⟦मंहना⟧: the root is ⟦महि वृद्धौ⟧, to grow; the suffix lyuṭ; the accent is that of a suffix marked with l. ⟦वृजध्यै⟧: the root is ⟦वृजी वर्जने⟧, to ward off; the suffix kadhyai in the sense of the infinitive; there is no guṇa because the suffix is marked with k; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦काम्याः⟧: of the causative of the root kam, the suffix yat by the rule that teaches yat after a vowel; the first syllable is udātta by ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧. ⟦ऋजप्याः⟧: the root is ⟦ओप्याया वृद्धौ⟧, to grow; the suffix kvip; the elision of the y by ⟦व्योर्वली⟧; the i in place of the u of the first member comes by interchange; the singular in place of the plural is Vedic; the latter member, a kṛt form, keeps its own accent.

## Ṛk 18

O ⟦वृत्रहन्⟧, O Indra, ⟦विश्वायुः⟧: he whose life is whole, complete, is so called, the imperishable; ⟦वृषभः⟧, the one who rains down the things desired; ⟦वयोधाः⟧, the giver of food; do you, being such, become the ⟦पतिः⟧, the lord, of the ⟦गिरां⟧ that are ⟦सूनृतानां⟧, of the most pleasing words that are being made by us. Likewise ⟦सरण्यन्⟧, desiring the going, the going for the sake of the sacrifice, ⟦महान्⟧, do you, accompanied by ⟦महीभिः⟧, great, ⟦ऊतिभिः⟧, protections, and by ⟦शिवेभिः⟧, auspicious, ⟦सख्येभिः⟧, friendships that are marked by the help that consists in the granting of results, ⟦गहि⟧, come for the sake of the sacrifice, towards ⟦नः⟧, towards us.

⟦सूनृतानां⟧: the root is ⟦ऊन परिहाणे⟧, to lessen; "it very much lessens what is unpleasant", so by the first part the pleasant is meant; what is so, and is also true (⟦तदृतं सत्यं⟧), is ⟦सूनृतं⟧; the first syllable of the latter member is udātta by ⟦परादिश्यंदसि बहुल⟧. ⟦वयोधाः⟧: when the word for food stands before it, the suffix vic after the root dhā. ⟦गहि⟧: of the root gam in loṭ, the elision luk of śap by ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧; hi is substituted for si, and it is not marked with p; because it is therefore treated as marked with ṅ, the elision of the m by the rule that begins with the roots taught as anudātta; because that elision is treated as not having taken place, the elision luk of hi that is taught after a does not occur; the verb loses its accent. ⟦सरण्यन्⟧: after the word for "going" the suffix kyac; the accent is that of the single substitute.

## Ṛk 19

O Indra, ⟦अंगिरस्वत्⟧, like the Aṅgirases, ⟦सपर्यन्⟧, honouring you, with ⟦नमसा⟧, with a hymn of praise, I, Viśvāmitra, ⟦नव्यं कृणोमि⟧ you, ⟦तं⟧, who are endowed with such greatness, ⟦पुराजां⟧, the ancient one, for ⟦सन्यसे⟧, for the obtaining of a share: I make you new as one who is to be praised. The ⟦बहुला⟧, the many, ⟦द्रुहो⟧, harmers, the rākṣasas who are our enemies, who are ⟦अदेवीः⟧, not shining because they are of the nature of darkness, ⟦वि याहि⟧, bring to death in a special way. O ⟦मघवन्⟧, O Indra, ⟦धाः⟧, grant, to ⟦नः⟧, to us, ⟦स्वः⟧, our own wealth also, for ⟦सातये⟧, for the obtaining of a share.

⟦अंगिरस्वत्⟧: like the Aṅgirases; the suffix vati by ⟦तेन तुल्य⟧; the stem having the name bha by the statement ⟦नभोसंगिरोमनुषां वत्युपसंख्यान⟧, "the additional mention of nabhas, aṅgiras and manus before vat", the s does not become ru. ⟦सपर्यन्⟧: the word sapara is of the kaṇḍū class. ⟦नव्यं⟧: from the root ṇu, to praise, the suffix yat by the rule that teaches yat after a vowel; the substitute ending in v by the rule ⟦धातोस्तन्निमित्तस्यॆति⟧; the first syllable is udātta by ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧. ⟦कृणोमि⟧: a form in laṭ of the root kṛv. ⟦सन्यसे⟧: after the root san the suffix kvip in the sense of the action; the root ⟦यसु प्रयत्ने⟧, to strive: after it the suffix kvip; "for ⟦यसे⟧, for the effort, of ⟦सनः⟧, of the obtaining of a share"; the meaning is, for the sake of obtaining a share. ⟦पुराजां⟧: a form of the root jan with the suffix viṭ and the change to ā. ⟦याहि⟧: a form in loṭ of the root yā. ⟦स्वः⟧: the word having the name of an indeclinable by ⟦स्वरादिनिपातमव्यय⟧, the elision luk of the case-ending by the rule on āp and the case-endings after an indeclinable. ⟦सातये⟧: the root is ⟦षण संभक्तौ⟧, to take as one's share; the suffix ktin in the sense of the action; the ā by the rule that begins ⟦जनसने⟧; the udātta by the ready-made form in the rule that begins ⟦ऊतियूती⟧.

## Ṛk 20

O Indra, your ⟦पावकाः⟧, purifiers of sins, ⟦मिहः⟧, the waters that are the means of sprinkling, ⟦अभूवन्⟧, have become, ⟦प्रतता⟧, widely spread out in the world. Of ⟦आसां⟧, of these waters, the ⟦पारं⟧, the bank, that is ⟦स्वस्ति⟧, free from destruction, ⟦पिपृहि⟧, make full with waters, for ⟦नः⟧, for our sake: the meaning is, make the water reach from the near bank to the far bank. Do you, ⟦रथिरो⟧, having a chariot, ⟦पाहि⟧, protect, us from the ⟦रिषो⟧, from the enemy who injures. After that, ⟦मक्षु मक्षु⟧, very quickly, ⟦कृणुहि⟧, make, us ⟦गोजितो⟧, winners of cattle, cows and the rest. The purport is: having filled the reservoirs, tanks and the rest, by the gift of rain, make us possessed of wealth in cows and the rest.

⟦अभूवन्⟧: a form in luṅ of the root bhū, to be; the verb loses its accent. ⟦पिपृहि⟧: the root is pṝ, to protect and to fill; it is of the hu class; the i of the reduplication by ⟦अर्तिपिपर्तोश्चे⟧; there is no guṇa because hi is treated as marked with ṅ; the verb loses its accent. ⟦रथिरः⟧: in the sense of the possessive suffix, the suffix irac by ⟦मेधारथाभ्यामिरनिरचावि⟧. ⟦रिषः⟧: the root is ⟦रिष हिंसायां⟧, to injure; the suffix kvip; the case-ending is udātta by ⟦सावेकाच⟧. ⟦मक्षु मक्षु⟧: the doubling by ⟦नित्यवीप्सयो⟧; the long vowel in the Saṃhitā by the rule on the Ṛk that names ⟦तुनुघमक्षु⟧.

## Ṛk 21

May Indra, ⟦वृत्रहा⟧, the slayer of Vṛtra, being ⟦गोपतिः⟧, the lord of cows, ⟦गा अदेदिष्ट⟧, assign cows to us. Likewise may he make the ⟦कृष्णान्⟧, the asuras who put obstacles in the way of the rites, ⟦गात्⟧, go, by his ⟦अरुपैः⟧, shining, ⟦धामभिः⟧, splendours, to disappearance, to destruction. Likewise Indra, ⟦प्रदिशमानो⟧, assigning to the Aṅgirases, by ⟦ऋतेन⟧, by a true word that is free from deceit, the ⟦सूनृताः⟧, the most dear cows, ⟦अवृणोत्⟧ ⟦विश्वाः⟧, all, the ⟦दुरो⟧, the doors by which the cows go out at their own will, and his ⟦स्वाः⟧, his own cows: the meaning is that, having placed the cows in the pen, he covered those doors.

⟦अदेदिष्ट⟧: from the root diś, to grant, in the intensive with the elision of yaṅ, in laṅ, the ātmanepada by interchange; the accent is that of the augment āṭ. **NOTE**{the supplied text names the augment āṭ; the reference text names the augment aṭ} ⟦गोपतिः⟧: the first member keeps its own accent by ⟦पत्यावैश्वर्य⟧. ⟦गात्⟧: the root is ⟦इण् गतौ⟧, to go; the sense of the causative is contained in it. ⟦दिशमान⟧: after the root diś the suffix śānac by interchange; it being anudātta by the accent of a sārvadhātuka that stands for a la, the accent is that of śa. ⟦अवृणोत्⟧: a form in laṅ of the root vṛ, ⟦वृञ वरण⟧, to cover.
