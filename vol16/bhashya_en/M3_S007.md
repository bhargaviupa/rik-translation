## Introduction

In the anuvāka beginning ⟦सोमस्य⟧ there are twelve sūktas. Among them the seventh sūkta, beginning ⟦प्र य आरु⟧, has eleven Ṛks. Viśvāmitra, the seer of the maṇḍala, is the ṛṣi. Since nothing else is stated, the metre is triṣṭubh. By the general rule given at the beginning of the maṇḍala, Agni is the deity. The application of the three sūktas in the Prātaranuvāka and in the Āśvina-śastra has already been stated with the words ⟦प्रत्यग्निरुषस-स्त्रीणीति⟧, "the three beginning thus". Among them this is the third sūkta.

## Ṛk 1

The rays of Agni, who is ⟦शितिपृष्ठस्य⟧, dark-backed, and ⟦धासेः⟧, the supporter of all, which ⟦आरुः⟧ with ⟦प्र⟧, go up exceedingly — those rays ⟦आ⟧ ⟦विविशुः⟧ the ⟦मातरा⟧, the two mothers, heaven and earth; they have entered them on all sides. They have also entered the ⟦वाणीः⟧, the rivers that are to be sought, which are ⟦सप्त⟧, of a gliding nature. ⟦परिक्षिता⟧, existing all around, the ⟦पितरा⟧, the two parents, heaven and earth, ⟦संचरेते⟧ with that Agni; they deal rightly with him. Likewise, ⟦प्रयक्षे⟧, in order to sacrifice excellently, they ⟦प्रसर्स्राते⟧, extend, ⟦दीर्घमायु⟧, Agni's long life, or abundant food; the meaning is that they bring it about. Or else: heaven and earth bring about long life for the sacrificer, ⟦प्रयक्षे⟧, so that he may sacrifice excellently and continually.

⟦प्र⟧: being a particle, it has the udātta on the first syllable. In the Veda, by the rule ⟦व्यवहिताश्च⟧, "and they also stand separated", it is connected with this word ⟦आरुः⟧. ⟦आरुः⟧: the root is ṛ, "to go"; it is the form in liṭ with us. Because it is connected with a form of yad, the accent is not lost; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦शितिपृष्ठस्य⟧: the word pṛṣṭha, which ends in the suffix thak, has the udātta on the last syllable; by the rule ⟦शितेर्निर्त्या बह्वच् बहुव्रीहावभसदि⟧ the latter member of the compound keeps its original accent. ⟦धासेः⟧: the root is dhā, "to hold and to nourish", or dheṭ, "to drink"; after it comes the uṇādi suffix si in the sense of the agent; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦मातरा⟧: by ⟦नप्तनेष्टृत्वष्टृक्षत होतृपोतृमातृपितृजा⟧ … (⟦उ.२-९६⟧**NOTE**{the supplied reference is u. 2-96; the reference text has 3.153 here}) the word is laid down ready-made as ending in tṛc, and so it has the udātta on the last syllable. The guṇa is by the rule ⟦ऋतो⟧. By ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ the case-ending is replaced by ḍā. ⟦विविशुः⟧: the root is viś, "to enter"; in liṭ, by ⟦असंयोगाल्लिट् किदिति⟧, the ending is treated as kit, and so there is no guṇa. The accent is lost. ⟦सप्त⟧: the root is sṛp, "to go". The suffix kanin and the augment tuṭ, which are enjoined after sap by the rule ⟦शूभ्यां तुट् च⟧ (⟦उ. १-५६⟧**NOTE**{the supplied reference is u. 1-56; the reference text has 1.155 here}), come after this root also, by the latitude of that rule; and a takes the place of the ṛ. Although the suffix is nit, the udātta is on the last syllable by transposition. ⟦क्षिता⟧: the root is kṣi, "to dwell and to go". The suffix kvip is by ⟦क्षिप् चेति⟧. Because the suffix is pit, the augment tuk comes in. It is a compound with pra and the like; the latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. By ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ the case-ending is replaced by ḍā. ⟦चरेते⟧: the root is car, "to go and to eat". After car preceded by sam the ātmanepada is by ⟦सम-स्कृतीयायुक्तात्⟧ (⟦पा. १-३-५४⟧). The accent is lost. ⟦सर्स्राते⟧: the root is sṛ, "to go". It here includes the sense of the causative. The r is Vedic. By ⟦ईदूदेदिति⟧ it has the name pragṛhya. The accent is lost. ⟦आयुः⟧: the root is iṇ, "to go". With usin continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix usin comes by the rule that after i it is also ṇit; because it is ṇit there is vṛddhi; because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦प्रयक्षे⟧: after the root yaj, "to worship the gods, to bring together and to give", comes the suffix sen in the sense of the infinitive. It is a compound with pra and the like; the latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. As it has the name indeclinable by ⟦कृन्मेजंत⟧, the case-ending is elided by ⟦अप्ययादाप्सुप⟧.

## Ṛk 2

The ⟦अश्वा⟧, the red horses that serve as the vehicle, of Agni who is ⟦वृष्ण:⟧, the showerer of desires, are ⟦दिवक्षसो⟧, existing pervading heaven, and are ⟦धेनवो⟧, the gladdeners of Agni. This Agni ⟦आ⟧ ⟦तस्थौ⟧ the ⟦देवी⟧, the shining rivers, that ⟦वहंती⟧, carry, ⟦मधुमत्⟧, water endowed with sweetness; he resorts to them, that is, he reaches them. The word go here denotes the speech of the middle region, because it is said: ⟦माध्यमिकां वाचमिति नैरुक्ता⟧, "'the speech of the middle region', say the etymologists". ⟦एका⟧ ⟦गौः⟧, a certain speech of the kind described, ⟦परिचरति⟧, serves, ⟦त्वा⟧, you, O Agni, who ⟦क्षेमयंतं⟧, desire kṣema, a dwelling, ⟦सदसि⟧, in the seat, the atmosphere, ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, of water, and who set ⟦वर्तनिं⟧, the flames, in motion.

⟦दिवक्षसः⟧: the root is akṣ, "to pervade"; "they pervade heaven", hence ⟦दिवक्षसः⟧. Or the root is vakṣ, ⟦वक्ष शोषे⟧**NOTE**{the sense of the root vakṣ is printed as śoṣe, drying, but the next sentence speaks of it as a root meaning anger; the reference text has the same; is the sense anger meant in both places?}, "to dry up"; although this root has the sense of anger, here it is used in the sense of pervading: "they vakṣ, that is pervade, heaven". The suffix asun is by the rule teaching asun. The v is elided by ⟦लोपो व्योर्वलीति⟧. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦धेनवः⟧: with nu continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix nu comes by ⟦धेट इच्चेति⟧. The i takes guṇa. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦वृष्णः⟧: the root is vṛṣ, "to sprinkle". The suffix kanin is by the rule beginning ⟦कनिन्युवृषितक्षिराजी⟧. Because it is kit there is no guṇa. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦अश्वाः⟧: the root is aś, "to pervade"; the suffix kvan is by the rule beginning ⟦अशिपुषिलटिकणे⟧. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦तस्थौ⟧: the root is sthā, "to cease from motion". By ⟦छंदसि लुळ्ळिट्ट⟧ the liṭ stands in the sense of the present; the au is by ⟦आत औ णल⟧. The accent is lost. ⟦मधुमत्⟧: with nit continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix u comes after man by the rule beginning ⟦फलिपाटिनमिमनिजनामि⟧. Together with it dh takes the place of the n. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦त्वा⟧: for the word yuṣmad ending in the accusative singular the substitute tvā comes by ⟦त्वामाविति⟧, and it is anudātta. ⟦क्षेमयंतं⟧: the root is kṣi, "to dwell and to go". The suffix man is by the rule beginning ⟦आर्तिसुसुहुसृधृक्षी⟧. "Desiring kṣema, a dwelling, for himself": the suffix kyac is by ⟦सुप आत्मनः क्यजिति⟧. The ī before kyac, ⟦क्यचि जेतीत्वं⟧, and the lengthening taught by ⟦अकृत्सार्धातुकयोर्दिर्घ⟧ are forbidden by ⟦न छंदस्सपु⟧…. Because kyac is cit the udātta is on the last syllable. After the stem ending in it comes the suffix śatṛ; then śap. Śap is anudātta because it is pit, and śatṛ is anudātta by the accent rule of the sārvadhātuka. When the single substitute for these two together with kyac has been made, that single substitute is udātta by ⟦एका-देश उदात्तेनोदात्त⟧. Then am: it is anudātta because it is a case-ending. By ⟦उदात्ता-दनुदात्तस्य⟧ the am becomes svarita. ⟦एका⟧: the root is iṇ, "to go". The suffix kan is by the rule beginning ⟦इण्कापे⟧. After the stem ending in it comes āp. The accent is that of a nit suffix. ⟦वर्तनिं⟧: the root is vṛt, "to turn". It includes the sense of the causative. The suffix ani is by ⟦वृतेश्च⟧ (⟦उ. २-१०७⟧). There is guṇa. The udātta is on the last syllable by transposition. ⟦गौः⟧: the root is gam, "to go"; the suffix ḍo is by ⟦गमे-र्डोः⟧ (⟦उ. २-६७⟧). Because it is ḍit the last vowel with what follows it is elided. Because the case-ending is ṇit by ⟦गोतो णिदिति⟧ there is vṛddhi. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 3

Agni, who is ⟦रयीणां रयिवित्⟧, the one who causes distinguished wealth among riches to be obtained, who is ⟦चिकित्वान्⟧, possessed of knowledge, and ⟦पति⟧, the lord of horses — the mares which ⟦सुयमाः⟧ ⟦भवंती⟧, are such as can easily be controlled, he ⟦आरोहत्⟧, caused to mount, ⟦सीं⟧, on all sides. That Agni, ⟦नीलपृष्ठः⟧, and ⟦पुरुधप्रतीको⟧, whose limbs are scattered in many ways, ⟦प्रावासयत्⟧ those mares, that is, he made them dwell thoroughly, ⟦धासेः⟧, for the sake of sustaining ⟦अतसस्य⟧, continual going. The meaning is: when the fatigue has been removed by dwelling, the continual going can be sustained.

⟦सीं⟧: it is anudātta by ⟦जादयोऽनुदात्ता⟧. ⟦अरोहत्⟧: the root is ruh, "to come forth". It is a form of laṅ. The accent is lost. ⟦सुयमाः⟧: the root is yam, "to cease". After it the suffix khal comes in the sense of what is possible, by ⟦ईषद्दु:सुष्टिति⟧. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦पतिः⟧: the root is pā, "to protect"; by ⟦पातेर्डति⟧ the suffix is ḍati; because it is ḍit the last vowel with what follows it is elided; the accent is that of the suffix. ⟦चिकित्वान्⟧: the root is cit, "to perceive". It is the form with kvasu. ⟦रयिवित्⟧: the root is vid, "to obtain"; the suffix is kvip. ⟦रयीणां⟧: the case-ending is udātta by ⟦नामन्यतरस्यामिति⟧. ⟦नीलपृष्ठः⟧: the word nīla has the udātta on the first syllable because it belongs to the group beginning with vṛṣa. In the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦आतसस्य⟧: after the root at, "to go continually", the suffix asac comes by ⟦मिनमिरभिलभिनभितपिपतिपनिपणिमहिभ्यः⟧ (⟦उ-११७⟧**NOTE**{the supplied reference is u-117; the reference text has u-112 here}). The accent is that of a cit suffix. ⟦आवासयत्⟧: the root is vas, "to dwell". The suffix ṇic is by ⟦हेतुमतीति⟧. The augment aṭ is by ⟦लुळ्ल्यज्श्वडि⟧. The accent is lost by ⟦तिङ्ङतिज⟧. ⟦पुरुधप्रतीकः⟧: the word puru here denotes a number. The suffix dhā is by ⟦संख्याया विधार्थे धेति⟧, "after a numeral, dhā in the sense of kinds". In the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent. The shortening is Vedic.

## Ṛk 4

The rivers, ⟦ऊर्जयंतीः⟧, making the weak strong, and ⟦वहतो⟧, intent on carrying, ⟦वहंति⟧, that is, hold, Agni, who is ⟦महि⟧, great, ⟦त्वाष्ट्रं⟧, the son of Tvaṣṭṛ, ⟦अजुर्यं⟧, not liable to be worn out by age, and ⟦स्तभूयमानं⟧, desiring the propping up, the supporting, of the worlds. ⟦सधस्थे⟧, near the waters, ⟦दिद्युतानो⟧, shining, with his ⟦अंगेभिः⟧, limbs, Agni ⟦व्याविवेश⟧ the ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth; he entered them in various ways. There is an illustration for the two worlds: ⟦एकामिव⟧ — as a man enters one woman, so.

⟦त्वाष्ट्रं⟧: in the sense "the offspring of Tvaṣṭṛ" the suffix aṇ comes by ⟦तस्यापत्यमित्यण्⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦स्तभूयमानं⟧: stabhu is propping; "desiring that": the suffix kyac is by ⟦सुप आत्मनः क्यच्⟧. The lengthening is by ⟦अकृत्सार्वधातु⟧…. After the stem ending in it comes śānac, by transposition. Because the stem ends in a, the augment muk comes before āna. When śānac has become anudātta by the accent rule of a sārvadhātuka that replaces a tense-sign, the root accent that follows from the ending in kyac remains. ⟦अंगेभिः⟧: by ⟦बहुलं⟧ ⟦छंदसीति⟧ the substitute ais does not take the place of bhis. The e is by ⟦बहुवचने झल्यॆदि⟧. ⟦दिद्युतानः⟧: the root is dyut, "to shine". In the Veda there is liṭ; its substitute is kānac. There is reduplication; when only the first consonant has been left, the reduplicative syllable dyu takes saṃprasāraṇa by ⟦द्युतिस्वाप्योः संप्रसारणं⟧ (⟦पा. ७-४ ६७⟧). By ⟦संप्रसारणाच्चेति⟧ the form of the preceding vowel alone remains. Because it is cit the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦एकामिव⟧: because of the statement ⟦इवेन विभक्त्यलोपः⟧ …, "with iva there is no elision of the case-ending, and the first member keeps its original accent", the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦विवेश⟧: it is the form of liṭ from the root viś, "to enter". The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 5

People ⟦जानंति⟧, know, the ⟦शेवं⟧, the happiness that lies in resorting to him, of Agni who is ⟦वृष्णः⟧, the showerer of desires, and ⟦अरुषस्य⟧ — ruṣas are those who do harm; he is without them; the meaning is that he shines because he has no enemies — of Agni who is such. ⟦उत⟧, and also, knowing it, they, all people, ⟦रणंति⟧, delight, ⟦शासने⟧, in the command, ⟦ब्रध्नस्य⟧, of the great Agni. And so a mantra says (⟦म १-६०-२⟧): ⟦अस्य शासुरुभयासः सचंते हविष्मंत⟧ ⟦उशिजो ये च मर्ता⟧, "Both kinds attend upon the rule of this one, the Uśij who have oblations and those who are mortals". Moreover: of these men the ⟦इळा⟧ ⟦गीः⟧, the speech in the form of praise, that concerns Agni is ⟦माहिना⟧, great, and ⟦गण्या⟧, to be counted, to be honoured; they become ⟦दिवोरुचो⟧, illuminators of the heavenly world, ⟦सुरुचः⟧, of beautiful brightness, and ⟦रोचमाना⟧, shining brightly.

⟦जानंति⟧: the root is jñā, "to know". Because it belongs to the class beginning with krī, its substitute jā comes by ⟦ज्ञा जनोर्जॆति⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अरुषस्य⟧: ruṣ and riṣ have the sense of harming. "They harm", hence ruṣas, those who do harm; the suffix ka is by ⟦इगुपधज्ञॆति⟧. "He who has no ruṣas is aruṣa." By the rule on nañ and su, ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम⟧, the latter member has the udātta on its last syllable. ⟦शेवं⟧: the root is śī, "to sleep". The suffix van is by ⟦इण्शीज्ञ्यां वन्निति⟧. The accent is that of a nit suffix. ⟦शासने⟧: the root is śās, "to instruct". After it comes lyuṭ in the sense of the action. By ⟦लिटीति⟧ the syllable before the suffix is udātta. ⟦रणंति⟧: the accent is lost. ⟦दिवोरुचः⟧: the root is ruc, "to shine". After it comes kvip. By ⟦तत्पुरुषे कृति बहुलमिति⟧ the genitive ending is not elided. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦सुरुचः⟧: in the bahuvrīhi, by the rule on nañ and su, the latter member has the udātta on its last syllable. ⟦इळा⟧: the root is īḷ, "to praise". The shortening is Vedic. As the word vāc ends in ṭāp, so does this. Since that suffix is anudātta because it is pit, the root accent stands. ⟦गण्या⟧: the root is gaṇ, "to count". After the root ending in ṇi in its own sense, the suffix yat comes: ⟦ण्यंतादचो यत्⟧. The udātta on the first syllable is by ⟦यतोऽनाव⟧. ⟦माहिना⟧: the suffix inaṇ is by ⟦महेरिनण् चेति⟧. Because it is ṇit the penultimate takes vṛddhi. By transposition the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦गीः⟧: the root is gṝ, "to sound". After it comes kvip. The i is by ⟦यूत इद्दातोरिती⟧. The lengthening is by ⟦र्वोरुपधाया⟧. The accent is that of a nominal stem.

## Ṛk 6

⟦उतो⟧, and also: the sacrificers ⟦अनयंत⟧ to Agni, made Agni attain, that ⟦शूषं⟧, happiness — because ⟦शूषं शुन-मिति⟧ is read among the names of happiness — which is ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦घोषं⟧, proclaimed after them, ⟦प्रविदा⟧, by a making known, ⟦पितृभ्यां⟧, to the two parents, heaven and earth, who are ⟦महो महद्भ्यां⟧, greater than the great. ⟦यत्र⟧, among which sacrificers, that Agni, ⟦उक्षा⟧, the sprinkler of water by producing rain, ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦ववक्ष ह⟧, indeed carries near, that is, makes present, ⟦स्वं⟧, his own, ⟦धाम⟧, splendour, which is ⟦परि धानं⟧, the holder all around, ⟦अक्तोः⟧, of that night, in order to hear the praise ⟦जरितु⟧, of the praiser. The word aktu denotes the night, because ⟦शर्वरी अक्कुरिति⟧ is read among its names, and also because of the derivation "in it the world is anointed, sprinkled, with mist".

⟦उतो⟧: it has the name pragṛhya by ⟦ऊदिति⟧. Because it belongs to the group beginning with evam, the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦घोषं⟧: the root is ghuṣ, "to sound"; after it comes ghañ in the sense of the object. The light penultimate takes guṇa. The accent is that of a ñit suffix. ⟦महः⟧: the elision of the a and of the t is Vedic. The case-ending is udātta by ⟦बृहन्महतोरुपसंख्यानमिति⟧. ⟦अनयंत⟧: it is the form in laṅ of the root nī, "to lead to"; the accent is lost. ⟦उक्षा⟧: the root is ukṣ, "to sprinkle". By the rule beginning ⟦श्व-न्नु क्षन्⟧ the word ukṣan is laid down ready-made as ending in the suffix kanin. By transposition the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦ह⟧: it is anudātta because it belongs to the group beginning with ca. ⟦यत्र⟧: by ⟦लितीति⟧ the syllable before the suffix is udātta. ⟦अक्तोः⟧: after the root añj, "to make manifest, to go and to anoint", the suffix ktu comes by ⟦क्तुरिति⟧. Because it is kit, the n is elided by the rule on roots that are not idit: ⟦कित्त्वादनिदितामिति नलोपः⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦ववक्ष⟧: the root is vah, "to carry". By ⟦छंदसि लुज्लज्लिट⟧ the liṭ stands in the sense of the present. The s is Vedic. Ṇal takes the place of tip. By ⟦लितीति⟧ the syllable before the suffix is udātta.

## Ṛk 7

Apart from the group of the udgātṛ there are twelve officiating priests. Of them seven are utterers of vaṣaṭ; the other five conduct the sacrifice. Together with those ⟦पंचभि⟧, five, ⟦अध्वर्युभिः⟧, ⟦सप्त विप्रा⟧, the seven wise hotrakas, ⟦रक्षंते⟧, protect, the ⟦प्रियं⟧, ⟦निहितं⟧ ⟦पदं⟧, the place called the āhavanīya, ⟦वेः⟧, of Agni whose nature is to go. ⟦प्रांचः⟧, going with their faces to the east in order to drink the Soma, ⟦अजुर्या⟧, impossible to wear out by age, ⟦उक्षणः⟧, pourers of the Soma juice, the ⟦देवाः⟧, the praisers, ⟦मदंति⟧, rejoice. Moreover those devas ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦गुः⟧, followed, the ⟦व्रता⟧, the sacrifices, that belong ⟦देवानां⟧, to the gods. ⟦हि⟧ is used of what is well known. The meaning is: by the favour of Agni the devas obtained the sacrifices.

⟦रक्षंते⟧: the root is rakṣ, "to protect". The ātmanepada is by transposition. The accent is lost. ⟦निहितं⟧: in the niṣṭhā suffix, in the sense of the object, of the root dhā, "to hold and to nourish", the substitute hi comes by ⟦दधा-तेर्हि⟧. By ⟦गतिरनंतर⟧ the preverb keeps its original accent. ⟦वेः⟧: the root is vī, "to desire, to go" and so on. After it comes vic. The guṇa is by ⟦सार्वधातुकार्थधातुकयोः⟧ (⟦सा. ७-३-८४⟧). By ⟦जसिजसोश्चे⟧ the form of the preceding vowel alone remains. ⟦प्रांचः⟧: by ⟦अनिगंतोऽंचताविति⟧ the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦मदंति⟧: the root is mad, "to rejoice". The accent is lost. ⟦गुः⟧: it is the form in luṅ from the root iṇ, "to go". The substitute gā is by ⟦इणो गा लुजीति⟧. By ⟦आत⟧ jus takes the place of jhi. By ⟦बहुलं छंदसी⟧ there is no augment aṭ. Because it is connected with the word hi, its accent is not struck down.

## Ṛk 8

The two who are ⟦दैव्या⟧, divine, having the form of deities, ⟦होतारा⟧, the two hotṛs, accomplishers of the offering, ⟦प्रथमा⟧, the first, the chief ones, this Agni and that one, the two Agnis, I ⟦न्यृंजे⟧. ⟦यंजतिः प्रसाधनकर्मेति⟧, "ṛñjati has the sense of adorning", says Yāska. I, Viśvāmitra, the seer of the mantra, adorn them. ⟦सप्त पृक्षासः⟧, the seven hotrakas, ⟦मदंति⟧, rejoice, ⟦स्वधया⟧, with Soma. ⟦शंसंतो⟧, reciting, ⟦ऋतं⟧, the hymn of praise, ⟦व्रतपा⟧, guardians of the rite, the sacrificial act, ⟦दीध्याना⟧, shining, those hotrakas, ⟦अनु व्रतं⟧, in the rite of the sacrifice, ⟦आहुः⟧, say, that Agni is ⟦ऋत-मित्⟧, the truth alone.

⟦यंजे⟧: the root is ṛj, read with bhṛj, "to roast". This root preceded by ni is used in the sense of adorning. The accent is lost. ⟦स्वधया⟧: "it places, it provides, the sacrificer's own world", hence svadhā, the Soma. The suffix ka is by ⟦आतोनुपसर्गे क⟧. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦आहुः⟧: in the laṭ of the root brū, "to speak articulately", by ⟦पंचानामादित आहो ब्रुव⟧ us takes the place of jhi and āha takes the place of brū. The accent is lost. ⟦दीध्यानाः⟧: after the root dīdhī, "to shine and to play", comes śānac. Although the udātta on the last syllable would result because it is cit, the udātta is on the first syllable by ⟦ऽभ्यस्तानामादि⟧.

## Ṛk 9

⟦देव⟧, O brightly shining one, ⟦होतः⟧, O invoker of the gods, O Agni: for you, on your behalf, who are ⟦महे⟧, great, ⟦अत्याय⟧, existing having surpassed all, ⟦चित्राय⟧, of manifold colours — or ⟦चित्राय⟧ means to be revered, to be worshipped, by the gods — and ⟦वृष्णे⟧, the showerer of desires, the ⟦रश्मयः⟧, the flames spread on all sides, which are ⟦पूर्वीः⟧, abundant, and ⟦सुयामाः⟧, exceedingly extended, ⟦वृषायंते⟧: they behave like a bull, they become like sprinklers. The hotṛs, on seeing Agni kindled with flames, throw into him the oblations, Soma, ghee, milk and the rest: this is why the flames are bulls. Or else the dative stands in the sense of the genitive. Then: the many rays of Agni, the great one, the surpassing one, the citra, the showerer, behave like a bull; the meaning is that by producing rain they behave like Parjanya. Moreover: you who are ⟦मंद्रतरो⟧, the greatest gladdener of the sacrificer, and ⟦चिकित्वान्⟧, endowed with knowledge, ⟦वक्षि⟧ with ⟦आ⟧, bring, ⟦इह⟧, here, to this Vedic rite, the ⟦देवान्⟧ who are ⟦महो⟧, great, venerable, to be sacrificed to, and ⟦रोदसी⟧, heaven and earth.

⟦वृषायंते⟧: with upamānāt, "after a word of comparison", continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix kyaṅ comes by ⟦क्यज् सलोपश्चेति⟧. The lengthening is by ⟦अकृत्सार्वधातुकयोरिति⟧. Because it stands at the beginning of a pāda its accent is not lost. The accent is that of the root. ⟦महे⟧: the elision of the a and of the t is Vedic. The case-ending is udātta by ⟦बृहन्महतोरिति⟧. ⟦पूर्विः⟧: after the root pṝ, "to protect and to fill", with ku continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix ku comes by the rule beginning ⟦पूभिदिव्यधिगृधी⟧. Guṇa being forbidden because it is kit, the u comes by ⟦उदोष्यपूर्वस्ये⟧. ⟦उरण्⟧ ⟦रपरः⟧: it is followed by r. After that, ṅīṣ comes by ⟦वोतो गुणवचनादिति⟧. When the semivowel has been substituted, the lengthening is by ⟦हलि चेति⟧. The single long vowel for the two like vowels is by ⟦ना छंदसीति⟧. ⟦रश्मयः⟧: the root is aś, "to pervade". "They pervade the whole world": with mi continuing from the preceding rule, the suffix mi and the substitute raś come by ⟦अश्नोते रश्च⟧ (⟦उ. ४-४६⟧). The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦सुयामाः⟧: yāma is restraining; it is ghañ in the sense of the action. In the bahuvrīhi, by the rule on nañ and su, the latter member has the udātta on its last syllable. ⟦देव⟧: because of the exclusion "not at the beginning of a pāda", it has the udātta on the first syllable that is taught in the sixth book. ⟦होतः⟧: after the root hve comes the suffix tṛn that denotes habit. The saṃprasāraṇa is by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति संप्रसारणं⟧. Because it is a vocative not at the beginning of a pāda, the accent is lost. ⟦मंद्रतरः⟧: the root is mad, "to praise" and so on. The suffix rak is by the rule beginning ⟦स्थायितंचिवंची⟧. Because the root is idit, num comes in. Since tarap is anudātta because it is pit, the accent is that of the suffix rak. ⟦वक्षि⟧: of the root vah, "to carry", the śap is elided by ⟦छंदसीति शपो लुक्⟧. There are the changes to ṭ, to k and to ṣ. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 10

⟦द्रविणः⟧: "he runs, he goes continually"; thus Agni is denoted by the word ⟦द्रविणः⟧. ⟦द्रविणः⟧, O Agni whose nature is continual going! ⟦पृक्षप्रयजः⟧: those dawns in which a beginning is made to sacrifice excellently the pṛkṣas, the foods that consist of oblations, are ⟦पृक्षप्रयजः⟧. ⟦सुवाचः⟧: those which have beautiful utterances, the Prātaranuvāka and the rest. ⟦सुकेतवो⟧: well recognised by the sounds of birds and of men. ⟦उषसो⟧, the dawns, that are of this kind ⟦ऊषुः⟧, shine forth, ⟦रेवत्⟧, in such a way that what is ours becomes endowed with wealth. ⟦उतो चित्⟧, and also, O Agni, ⟦महिना⟧, by the greatness, ⟦पृथिव्याः⟧, of the widespread flame, ⟦कृतं⟧ ⟦चित्⟧, whatever has been committed, ⟦एनः⟧, sin consisting in the injuring of living beings and the like, that ⟦दशस्य⟧ with ⟦सं⟧, destroy completely, ⟦महे⟧, for the great one, for me the sacrificer, on my behalf.

⟦पृक्षप्रयजः⟧: by ⟦अन्येभ्योऽपि दृश्यंत⟧ the suffix vic comes in the sense of the object; it has been said that it is entitled to apply because of the mention of dṛśi, "is seen", in that rule. Because it is a bahuvrīhi, the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦द्रविणः⟧: after the root dru, "to go", the suffix inan comes by ⟦द्रुदक्षिभ्यामिनन्⟧. There are guṇa and the substitute av. That the su is not elided in the vocative is Vedic. Because it is a vocative, the accent is lost. ⟦सुवाचः⟧: by the rule on nañ and su, ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम⟧, the latter member has the udātta on its last syllable. ⟦रेवत्⟧: "rayi, wealth, is found in it": hence matup. By ⟦द्रस्वनुड्व्यामिति⟧ the matup is udātta. The v is by ⟦छंदसीर⟧. The saṃprasāraṇa is by ⟦रयेर्मत्बहुलं छंदसीति⟧. When the following vowel has taken the form of the preceding one, there is guṇa. ⟦ऊषुः⟧: the root is uṣ, "to burn". Although this root has the sense of burning, here it is used in the sense of shining. By the rule that begins ⟦छंदसि⟧ the liṭ stands in the sense of the present. It is the form with us. The accent is lost. ⟦पृथिव्याः⟧: after the root prath, "to be made known", the suffix ṣivan comes by ⟦शिवन् संप्रसारणं चेति⟧. Together with it the root takes saṃprasāraṇa. Ṅīṣ comes by ⟦षिद्गौरादिभ्यश्च⟧. By the accent of the suffix the udātta is on the last syllable. The case-ending is udātta by ⟦उदात्तयणो हल्पूर्वादिति⟧. ⟦दशस्य⟧: the accent is lost.

## Ṛk 11

O Agni! ⟦पुरुदंसं⟧: because it is read among the names of action in ⟦अपोऽप्रो दंसो वेष⟧, the word daṃsas denotes action. "She who has ⟦पुरूणि⟧, many, daṃsas, actions": her, the one of many actions, ⟦इळां⟧, the deity of that name who has the form of a cow, who is ⟦सनिं⟧ ⟦गोः⟧, the procurer of cattle such as cows, ⟦शश्वत्तमं⟧, without interruption, ⟦हवमानाय⟧, for me the sacrificer, ⟦साध⟧, accomplish. Moreover: ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦सुमतिः⟧, good thought, which is "may there be ⟦सूनुः⟧, a son, and ⟦तनयः⟧, a grandson, ⟦नो⟧, of ours" — may ⟦सा⟧, that, being ⟦विजावा⟧, not barren, ⟦भूतु⟧, be, ⟦अस्मे⟧, ours.

⟦शश्वत्तमं⟧: because it is read in the group beginning with uñcha, the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦साध⟧: the roots are rādh and sādh, "to succeed". This one includes the sense of the causative. In loṭ, śap comes by transposition. The substitute hi is by ⟦सेर्ह्यपिच्छेति⟧. Its elision is by the rule cited in ⟦तस्यातो हेंति⟧. The accent is lost. ⟦विजाना⟧: the root is jan, "to come forth". After it the suffix vanip comes by the rule that these suffixes are seen after other roots also: ⟦आस्वादन्येभ्योऽपि दृश्यंत इति वनिष्⟧. The ā is by ⟦विड्वनोनुनासिकस्या⟧. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. There is a transposition of gender. ⟦भूतु⟧: the root is bhū, "to be". The śap is elided by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧. The guṇa is forbidden by ⟦भूसुवोस्तिजीति⟧.
