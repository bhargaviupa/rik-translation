## Introduction

The hymn beginning ⟦समिध्यमान⟧ is the fifth hymn; it has five Ṛks, is addressed to Agni, is in the triṣṭubh metre, and its seer is Kata, the son of Viśvāmitra. This has been stated: ⟦समिध्यमानः पंच कतो वैश्वामित्रस्त्विति⟧, "Samidhyamānaḥ: five; Kata, the son of Viśvāmitra, however." The ritual use of the group of ten hymns beginning ⟦आ होतेत्यादि⟧ has been stated: it is in the prātaranuvāka and in the āśvina-śastra. Among those this is the fourth. In the Vājapeya, at the caru offered to Bṛhaspati, the verse ⟦अग्निं सुदीतिं⟧ is the yājyā of the Sviṣṭakṛt. This is laid down in the sūtra, in the section beginning ⟦वाजपेयेनेत्यादि⟧: ⟦अग्निं सुदीतिं सुदृशं गृणंत इति संयाज्ये⟧ (⟦आ ९ - ९⟧), "'Agniṃ sudītiṃ sudṛśaṃ gṛṇantaḥ': these are the two saṃyājyās."

## Ṛk 1

Kata praises. ⟦धर्म⟧: it is dharma because of holding. Since Agni, as the one who carries the sacrifice through, is its holder, Agni is dharma. And so there is the mantra: ⟦धर्माणमग्निं विदथस्य साधनं⟧ (⟦ऋग्वॆ. १०-९२-२⟧), "Agni, the holder, the means of accomplishing the vidatha." Such an Agni, ⟦प्रथमा⟧, first, at the commencement of the sacrifice, ⟦अनु⟧, in due order in the places of the fires, ⟦संइध्यमानः⟧, being well set ablaze. Or else: ⟦अनु धर्म⟧, in the sacrifices which are the means of dharma, being kindled first; ⟦विश्ववारः⟧, to be chosen by all sacrificers as the one to be worshipped; ⟦शोचिःकेशः⟧: he whose hair is flames is so called; ⟦घृतनिर्णिक्⟧: the purifier of the ghee by melting it through heating; ⟦पावकः⟧, the purifier of our sins; ⟦सुयज्ञः⟧, possessed of an excellent sacrifice; Agni, being such, in order to worship (⟦यजथाय⟧) the gods (⟦देवान्⟧), the gods who are to be worshipped by us, is ⟦अक्तुभिः⟧, with the means of anointing, ghee and the rest, anointed all over: he is well sprinkled.

⟦प्रथमा⟧: by the rule ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ the substitute ḍā takes the place of the case-ending. ⟦धर्म⟧: from the root ⟦धृञ् धारण⟧, dhṛñ in the sense of holding, the suffix manin comes by the rule that such suffixes are seen after other roots too. The shortness of the vowel is Vedic. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦अक्तुभिः⟧: the root is ⟦अंजू व्यक्तिगतिम्रक्षणेषु⟧, añjū in the senses of making manifest, going and smearing. "One is anointed by these": so the suffix ktu comes in the sense of the instrument, because the rules apply variously. Because the suffix is marked with k, the penultimate is elided. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अज्यते⟧: the same root; the suffix yak in the passive; the penultimate is elided by the rule ⟦अनिदितामित्युपधालोपः⟧. ⟦विश्ववारः⟧: "he whose vāra, choosing, is done by all sacrificers through the hotṛ"; here vāra is a form of the root ⟦वृञ् वरण⟧, vṛñ in the sense of choosing, ending in the causative suffix. By the rule ⟦बहुव्रीहौ विश्वं संज्ञायामिति⟧ the first member has the udātta on its final syllable. ⟦घृतनिर्णिक्⟧: the root is ⟦णिजिर्⟧, in the senses of cleansing and nourishing; the suffix is kvip. "He cleanses the ghee": since it is a tatpuruṣa with an accusative first member, the accent is that of the first member. ⟦यजथाय⟧: the root is ⟦यज देवपूजासंगतिकरणदानेषु⟧, yaj in the senses of worshipping the gods, bringing together and giving. From it comes the Uṇādi suffix atha. The dative is in the sense of purpose. The accent is that of the suffix.

## Ṛk 2

O Agni, as you formerly, being the hotṛ of ⟦पृथिव्याः⟧, of the deity Earth who had become the sacrificer, sacrificed (⟦अयजः⟧) the ⟦होत्रं⟧, the oblation which is the means of the offering; and, ⟦जातवेदः⟧, you in whom knowledge has arisen, O Agni, as you, ⟦चिकित्वान्⟧, possessing knowledge concerning the rite, having become the hotṛ of ⟦दिवः⟧, of the deity Heaven who had become the sacrificer, sacrificed the oblation; ⟦एव⟧, so, do you, being the hotṛ in this rite, with this (⟦अनेन⟧) oblation that is being given by us, ⟦यक्षि⟧, sacrifice to, the gods. Moreover do you ⟦अद्य⟧, now, carry forward (⟦प्र⟧ ⟦तिर⟧) this sacrifice that is being performed by us: bring it to the further shore by the completion of its performance. The example for that is ⟦मनुष्वत्⟧: as you led the sacrifice of Manu to the further shore by the completion of its performance, so lead this sacrifice to the further shore; that is the meaning.

⟦यथा⟧: the suffix thāl comes by the rule ⟦प्रकारवचने थालिति⟧. By the rule on a suffix marked with l, the syllable before the suffix is udātta. ⟦अयजः⟧: a form of the root yaj in laṅ. Because of the connection with the word yad there is no loss of accent. ⟦होत्रं⟧: the root is ⟦हु दानादनयोः⟧, hu in the senses of giving and eating. With the suffix tran, by the rule ⟦हुयामाश्रुभसिभ्यस्त्रन्⟧, the word has the udātta on its final syllable. ⟦दिवः⟧: by the rule ⟦ऊडिदम⟧ the case-ending is udātta. ⟦चिकित्वान्⟧: a form of the root ⟦कित ज्ञान⟧, kit in the sense of knowing, with the suffix kvasu. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अनेन⟧: in the instrumental singular of the word idam the substitute ana comes by the rule ⟦अनाप्यकः⟧ (⟦पा. ७-२-११२⟧). ⟦यक्षि⟧: a form of the root yaj in loṭ.**NOTE**{On yakṣi the supplied text reads "a form of yaj in loṭ" (⟦यजेर्लोटि रूपं⟧); the reference text has leṭ here, as the supplied text itself has on yakṣi in Ṛk 3: which is to stand?} ⟦मनुष्वत्⟧: the suffix vati comes by the rule ⟦तत्र सस्येव⟧. By the supplementary rule ⟦नभोंगिरो मनुषां वत्युपसंख्यानम⟧, because the stem has the designation bha, there is no change to ru. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦तिर⟧: in the loṭ of the root tṛ, the vikaraṇa śa comes by interchange. By the rule ⟦ऋत इद्दातो⟧ the vowel becomes i. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 3

O ⟦जातवेदः⟧, you in whom knowledge has arisen, yours are three ⟦आयूंषि⟧, foods of three kinds, consisting of ghee, plants and Soma, appointed as your nourishment. O Agni, the three Dawns, the Dawns that belong to the ekāha, the ahīna and the sattra, the three deities who preside over the dawn, are ⟦ते⟧, your, ⟦आजानीः⟧, the mothers who gave you birth. Or else: the deities who preside over the dawn are your ⟦आजानीः⟧, the three sisters born after you. And so there is a mantra which has the sisters for its subject: ⟦प्रजामेका रक्षत्यूर्जमेका राष्ट्रमेका रक्षतीति⟧, "One protects offspring, one vigour, one protects the kingdom." Accompanied by them (⟦ताभिः⟧), the deities who preside over the dawn, do you ⟦यक्षि⟧ the ⟦अवः⟧, the food in the form of the oblation that is being given by us, ⟦देवानां⟧: bestow it on the gods who are to be worshipped. ⟦अथ⟧, thereafter, ⟦विद्वान्⟧, knowing, do you be ⟦शं⟧ for the sacrificer: be a cause of happiness; and ⟦योः⟧: be also one who joins him with happiness.

⟦तव⟧: it has the udātta on the first syllable by the rule ⟦युष्मदस्मदोर्जसी⟧. ⟦तिस्रः⟧: for the word tri in the feminine sense the substitute tisṛ comes by the rule ⟦त्रिचतुरोः स्त्रियावि ति⟧. The substitute r comes by the rule ⟦अचि र ऋत⟧. By the rule ⟦तिसृभ्यो जस⟧ the case-ending is udātta. ⟦आजानीः⟧: the root is ⟦जन जनने⟧, jan in the sense of begetting. By the rule ⟦जनिघसिभ्यामिण्⟧ the suffix iṇ comes in the sense of the agent. Because the suffix is marked with ṇ, the penultimate takes vṛddhi. By the rule ⟦वा छ दसीति⟧ the long vowel homogeneous with the preceding comes. The second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦अवः⟧: the root is ⟦अव रक्षणे⟧, av in the sense of protecting. The suffix asun comes by the rule ⟦असुन्नित्यसुन्⟧. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦यक्षि⟧: a form in leṭ of the root yaj in the sense of giving. ⟦यजमानाय⟧: the suffix śānan comes after yaj by the rule ⟦पूज्यजोः शानन्⟧ (⟦पा ३-२-१२८⟧). The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦योः⟧: from the root ⟦यु मिश्रण⟧, yu in the sense of mixing, the suffix vic comes by the rule that such suffixes are seen after other roots too. The guṇa is that conditioned by an ārdhadhātuka suffix. The second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent.

## Ṛk 4

O ⟦जातवेदः⟧, knower of all that is born, O Agni, we who are ⟦गृणंतः⟧, making praise that has you for its object, worship (⟦नमस्यामः⟧) with oblations you (⟦त्वा⟧) who are of this kind: ⟦अग्निं⟧, possessed of the qualities of moving and the rest; ⟦सुदीतिं⟧, of beautiful radiance; for that very reason ⟦सुदृशं⟧, good for all to look upon; ⟦ईड्यं⟧, worthy of praise. Moreover the gods (⟦देवाः⟧), Indra and the other gods who are to be worshipped by us, made you, who are ⟦अरतिं⟧ — he for whom there is no rati, pleasure, in the objects of sense is arati: not attached to the objects of sense — and who for that very reason are the ⟦नाभिं⟧, the place of the navel, of ⟦अमृतस्य⟧, of the nectar which is the food of the gods, you who are such, their ⟦हव्यवाहं⟧ messenger: they made you the carrier of the oblations, the messenger. For a messenger is of two kinds, the one who carries news and the one who carries out a task. Of these they made you the messenger who carries out a task; that is the sense.

⟦सुदीतिं⟧: the root is dī in the sense of wasting away. "By these the darkness is wasted, destroyed": so the suffix ktin comes in the sense of the instrument. ⟦दीतयो दीप्तयः⟧: dītis are radiances. In the bahuvrīhi the second member has the udātta on its final syllable by the rule ⟦नञ्सुभ्याम⟧. ⟦सुदृशं⟧: the root is ⟦दृशिर् प्रेक्षणे⟧, dṛś in the sense of seeing. From it the suffix khal comes in the sense of what is possible. By the maxim ⟦संज्ञापूर्वको विधि⟧ and what follows, "a rule that is stated by means of a technical name is not invariable", there is no guṇa. By the rule on a suffix marked with l, the syllable before the suffix is udātta. ⟦गृणंतः⟧: the verb gṛṇāti has the sense of praising. ⟦नमस्यामः⟧: namas is in the sense of worship. The suffix kyac comes by the rule ⟦नमो वरिवश्चित्रजः क्यजिति⟧. It is a form in laṭ from the stem ending in that suffix. Because it stands at the beginning of the pāda it does not lose its accent. ⟦दूतं⟧: the root is ⟦टुदु उपतापे⟧, du in the sense of tormenting. The suffix kta comes by the rule ⟦दुतनिभ्यां दीर्घश्च⟧ (⟦उ. ३-९०⟧)**NOTE**{the supplied text gives the Uṇādi reference as 3-90; the reference text has 3.370: which is to stand?}. Together with that suffix the vowel of the root is lengthened. A dūta is one who moves about here and there; that is the meaning. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦अरतिं⟧: the root is ⟦रमु क्रीडायां⟧, ram in the sense of playing. The suffix ktin comes in the sense of the action. ⟦अकृण्वन्⟧: in the laṅ of the root kṛvi, in the senses of injuring and making, the suffix u and the substitution of a for the final come by the rule ⟦धिन्विकृण्योरच्चे⟧. The verb loses its accent.

## Ṛk 5

O Agni, he who (⟦यः⟧), ⟦त्वत्⟧, even than you, was ⟦पूर्वः⟧, earlier in existence, ⟦यजीयान्⟧, a sacrificer in a surpassing degree, whoever he was, the ⟦होता⟧, the accomplisher of the offering, ⟦द्विता⟧, in two ways, in the middle and in the highest place, together with ⟦स्वधया⟧, the food called Soma, ⟦सत्ता⟧, being seated, became ⟦शंभुः⟧, a source of happiness for the sacrificers. Yāska explained the second pāda thus: ⟦द्विता च सत्ता स्वधया च शंभुः⟧, "Seated in two ways, in the middle place and in the highest; śaṃbhu, a source of happiness" (⟦नि. ५-३⟧). ⟦चिकित्वः⟧, you who possess awareness concerning dharma, O Agni, ⟦तस्य⟧, of him, of that hotṛ who has been described as earlier in existence even than you, ⟦अनु⟧ ⟦धर्म⟧, following the dharmas, having regard to the dharmas performed by him, ⟦प्र यज⟧: worship the gods in an eminent way. ⟦अथ⟧, thereafter, O Agni, do you ⟦धाः⟧, dispose, the ⟦अध्वरं⟧, this sacrifice, which belongs to us (⟦नः⟧), ⟦देववीतौ⟧, for the pleasure of the gods.

⟦यजीयान्⟧: after the word yaṣṭṛ the suffix īyasun comes by the rule that tṛ takes it in the Veda. Before it the suffix tṛc is elided by the rule ⟦तुरिष्ठेमेयःस्थिति⟧. Because the suffix is marked with n, the first syllable is udātta. ⟦द्विता⟧: after the word dvi the suffix dhā comes by the rule ⟦संख्याया विधार्थे धेति⟧. The t in place of dh is Vedic. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦सत्ता⟧: the root is sad, in the senses of breaking up, going and sinking down. After it comes the suffix tṛn in the sense of one who has the habit. The accent is that of a suffix marked with n. ⟦स्वधया⟧: "it bestows the world of heaven on the sacrificer", hence svadhā. The suffix ka comes by the rule ⟦आतोऽनुपसर्गे क⟧. The second member, ending in a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦शंभुः⟧: "śam, happiness, arises from him"; the root is ⟦भू सत्तायां⟧, bhū in the sense of being. The suffix ḍu comes because the group beginning with mitadru is supplementarily included under the rule which teaches ḍu, where no name is meant, after vi, pra and sam. Because the suffix is marked with ḍ, the last vowel with what follows it is elided. ⟦यज⟧: a form of the root yaj in loṭ. ⟦चिकित्वः⟧: a form of the root ⟦कित ज्ञान⟧, kit in the sense of knowing, with the suffix kvasu. By the rule ⟦मतुवसो रुः संबुद्धौ⟧ and what follows, "in the Veda", ru takes the place of the n. ⟦अथा नः⟧: the long vowel comes by the rule ⟦निपातस्यॆति⟧. ⟦धाः⟧: a form in the Vedic luṅ of the root ḍudhāñ, in the senses of holding and nourishing. The absence of the augment a is Vedic. ⟦देववीतौ⟧: that pleasure in which there is the vīti, the eating, of the gods is devavīti. From the root ⟦वी कांत्यादिष्वि⟧, vī in the senses of desire and the rest, the suffix ktin comes in the sense of the action. In the bahuvrīhi the first member keeps its original accent.
