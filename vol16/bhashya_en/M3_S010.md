## Ṛk 1

**NOTE**{the supplied text has no introduction to this sūkta; the reference text has one} O Agni! ⟦मनीषिणो⟧, the intelligent, the adhvaryu and the others, ⟦मर्तासो⟧, men, ⟦सं⟧ ⟦इंधते⟧, ⟦अध्वरे⟧, in the sacrifice, ⟦त्वां⟧, you, who are ⟦सम्राज⟧, the overlord, ⟦चर्षणीनां⟧, of creatures, and ⟦देवं⟧, shining; they make you blaze well with the offerings of Soma, ghee, milk and the rest.

⟦मनीषिणः⟧: "they have manīṣā": the suffix ini is by ⟦व्रीह्यादिभ्यश्चेति⟧. The a-vowel is elided by ⟦यस्येति च⟧. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦सम्राजं⟧: the root is rāj, "to shine". After this root preceded by sam the suffix kvip comes by ⟦सत्सद्विषे⟧. The kvip is elided. Although anusvāra would result for the m of sam by ⟦मोऽनुस्वार⟧, the m of sam stands by ⟦मो राजि समः क्याविति⟧. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦चर्षणीनां⟧: the case-ending is udātta by ⟦नामन्यतरस्यामिति⟧. ⟦इंधते⟧: the root is indh, "to shine". After this root ṇic comes by ⟦हेतुमति चेति⟧. In laṭ, ⟦झस्यात्मसेपदेप्तनत⟧: at is the substitute for jh. Since jh is ārdhadhātuka by ⟦छंदस्युभयथेति⟧, the ṇi is elided by ⟦णीरनिटीति⟧. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 2

O Agni! The adhvaryu and the others ⟦ईळते⟧, praise, ⟦त्वां⟧, you, ⟦यज्ञेषु⟧, in the sacrifices, the Agniṣṭoma and the rest, as ⟦होतारं⟧ ⟦ऋत्विजं⟧, the priest who has the name of hotṛ. You who are such, being ⟦गोपा⟧, the protector, ⟦ऋतस्य⟧, of the true one, the sacrifice, ⟦दीदिहि⟧, shine, ⟦स्वे दमे⟧, in your own house, the hall of sacrifice.

⟦दीदिहि⟧: the root is div, "to play" and so on. There is the elision of yaṅ. Loṭ. Because the root belongs to the group beginning with tuj, the reduplicative syllable is lengthened. As, by ⟦संज्ञापूर्व-को विधिरनित्य⟧, "a rule that is stated by means of a technical name is not invariable", there is no guṇa of the reduplicative syllable, the v is elided by ⟦लोपो व्योर्वलीति⟧.

## Ṛk 3

O Agni! ⟦यः⟧, the sacrificer who, ⟦ददाशति⟧, gives, ⟦ते⟧, to you, ⟦जातवेदसे⟧, in whom understanding has arisen, ⟦समिधा⟧, the oblations that bring about the kindling, the ghee, the puroḍāśa and the rest, ⟦सः⟧ ⟦घ⟧, he indeed, ⟦धत्ते⟧, holds, ⟦सुवीर्यं⟧, a son endowed with fine ability. And ⟦सः⟧, he alone, ⟦पुष्यति⟧; he becomes rich in cattle, sons and the rest.

⟦घ⟧: it is anudātta by ⟦चादयोऽनुदात्ता⟧. ⟦ददाति⟧: after the root dāś, "to give", śap comes in the sense of the agent. Tip. The reduplication is Vedic. Because it is connected with a form of yad, the accent is not lost. It is udātta by ⟦अभ्यस्तानामादि⟧. ⟦समिधा⟧: the root is indh, "to shine". After it kvip comes by ⟦क्विप् चेति⟧. The penultimate is elided. By ⟦सुपां सुलुगिति⟧ ḍā is the substitute for śas. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦जातवेदसे⟧: in the bahuvrīhi, by ⟦प्रकृत्या पूर्वपदमिति⟧, the first member keeps its original accent. ⟦सुवीर्यं⟧: by ⟦वीरवीयौ च⟧ the latter member has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦पुष्यति⟧: the root is puṣ, "to thrive". After it comes śyan. Because it is ṅit there is no guṇa. The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 4

⟦सः⟧, that, ⟦अग्निः⟧, who is ⟦केतुः⟧, the one who makes known, ⟦अध्वराणां⟧, the sacrifices, ⟦अंजानः⟧, being anointed with the offerings of Soma and ghee, ⟦होतृभिः⟧, by the utterers of vaṣaṭ, ⟦सप्त⟧, seven of them, may he ⟦आ⟧ ⟦गमत्⟧, come, ⟦देवेभिः⟧, together with the gods to whom sacrifice is to be made, ⟦हविष्मते⟧, for the sake of the sacrificer.

⟦गमत्⟧: by ⟦छंदसि लुङ्लज्लिट⟧ luṅ stands in the sense of loṭ. Because the root is ḷdit, aṅ comes. By ⟦बहुलं छंदस्यमाज्योग⟧ there is no augment aṭ. The accent is lost. ⟦अंजानः⟧: the root is añj, "to make manifest, to anoint and to go". By transposition śānac is in the sense of the object. Because it is cit the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦हवि-ष्कृते⟧: the word havis, which ends in the suffix isi by the rule ⟦जुहोशेरर्चिसुची⟧ …, has the udātta on the last syllable. Because the paribhāṣā ⟦स्वर-विधौ व्यंजनमविद्यमानवदिति⟧, "in a rule of accent a consonant is as if it were not there", is not resorted to, the matup is not udātta. The accent is that of the suffix isi.

## Ṛk 5

The sacrificer speaks to the hotṛ and the others. O hotṛ and the rest! ⟦प्र⟧ ⟦भरत⟧, bring about, ⟦वचः⟧, an utterance, the stotras, the śastras and the rest, that is ⟦बृहत्⟧, great, and ⟦पूर्व्यं⟧, made by the ancients, old, ⟦अग्नये⟧, for Agni, who is ⟦होत्रे⟧, the invoker of the gods, ⟦वेधसे⟧, the disposer of the world, and ⟦बिभ्रते⟧, who, as their cause, makes, ⟦ज्योतींषि⟧, the splendours to be won by the performance of good rites, ⟦विपां⟧, of the wise, the adhvaryu and the others. This ⟦न⟧ is a filler of the pāda, because it has no construction. Or else ⟦वेधसे⟧ ⟦न⟧ is: as the Disposer, the supreme Lord who orders the world, makes the lights, the sun and the rest, so.

The word pra, by ⟦व्यवहि-ताश्च⟧ in the Veda, is connected with this word ⟦भरत⟧. ⟦पूर्वं⟧ means "made by the ancients". The suffix ya is by ⟦पूर्वैः कृतमिनियौ च⟧. Because the stem has the name bha, the a-vowel is elided. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦भरत⟧: the root is bhṛ, "to bear", of the class beginning with bhū. The accent is lost. ⟦बृहत्⟧: because it is laid down ready-made by ⟦वर्तमाने बृषद्बृहदिति⟧, the udātta is on the last syllable. ⟦बिभ्रते⟧: the root is bhṛ, "to bear". ⟦जुहोत्यादिभ्यः श्लुः⟧: ślu comes. ⟦बृञामिदितीत्वं⟧: the i of the reduplicative syllable. The udātta on the first syllable is by ⟦अभ्यस्तानामादि⟧.

## Ṛk 6

This Agni who, ⟦यतो⟧, by which utterances in the form of praise, ⟦जायते⟧, becomes, ⟦उक्त्यः⟧ — the word ukthya denotes "praiseworthy", because ⟦अनभितस्त्य उक्त⟧ is read among the names for that; the meaning is that he becomes exceedingly worthy of praise — and this Agni who is ⟦दर्शतो⟧, worth seeing, ⟦महे⟧, for great, ⟦वाजाय⟧, food, and ⟦द्रविणाय⟧, wealth: that Agni let ⟦नो गिरो⟧, those utterances of ours in the form of praise, ⟦वर्धंतु⟧, make grow. The meaning is: let them honour him.

⟦वर्धंतु⟧: the root is vṛdh, "to grow". After it comes ṇic. The light penultimate takes guṇa. Since the ending is ārdhadhātuka by ⟦छंदस्युभयथे⟧, the ṇi is elided. Ant is the substitute for jhi. The u is by ⟦एरुः⟧ (⟦पा. ३-४-८६⟧**NOTE**{the supplied reference is pā. 3-4-86; the reference text has 2-4-86 here}). The accent is lost. ⟦उक्ष्यः⟧: the root is vac, "to speak". After it the suffix thak comes by the rule beginning ⟦पात्मतुदिवचि-रची⟧. Because it is kit there is saṃprasāraṇa by the rule beginning ⟦वचिस्वपी⟧. Uktha is the śastra. Being there, by being praiseworthy through it, he is ukthya. The suffix yat is by ⟦भवे छंदसीति⟧. The elision is by ⟦यस्यॆति⟧. It is svarita by ⟦तित्स्वरितमिति⟧. ⟦दर्शतः⟧: the suffix atac is by ⟦भ्रमृदृशियजी⟧. Because it is cit the udātta is on the last syllable.

## Ṛk 7

O Agni! You who are ⟦यजिष्ठो⟧, the best of sacrificers, ⟦अध्वरे⟧, in the sacrifice, ⟦यज⟧ ⟦देवान्⟧ ⟦देवयते⟧, for the sacrificer who desires the gods for himself; for his sake worship the gods to whom sacrifice is to be made, Agni and the rest. Moreover: you who are ⟦होता⟧, the invoker of the gods, and ⟦मंद्रो⟧, the gladdener of the sacrificer, ⟦वि राजसि⟧, shine in a special way, ⟦अति⟧, having overcome, ⟦स्रिधः⟧, the enemies who destroy.

⟦यजिष्ठः⟧: the suffix iṣṭhan is by ⟦तुश्चंदसी⟧. The tṛc is elided by ⟦तुरिष्ठेमेयःस्थिति⟧. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦देवयते⟧: because kyac is cit, it has the udātta on its last syllable. Śap is anudātta because it is pit, and śatṛ by the accent rule of a sārvadhātuka that replaces a tense-sign. When the single substitute for those two together with kyac has been made, by ⟦एकादेश उदात्तेनोदात्त⟧ the word devayat has the udātta on its last syllable. After that the case-ending is udātta by ⟦शतुर-नुम⟧. ⟦यज⟧: the root is yaj, "to worship the gods, to bring together and to give". After it comes loṭ. Sip. Hi is the substitute for sip. The hi is elided by ⟦अतो हेरिति⟧. The accent is lost. ⟦राजसि⟧: the root is rāj, "to shine". The accent is lost.

## Ṛk 8

⟦नः⟧ ⟦पावक⟧, O purifier of the sin that belongs to us, O Agni! ⟦सः⟧, you who were set forth in the preceding mantra, ⟦दीदिहि⟧, shine, ⟦अस्मे⟧, upon us, wealth that is ⟦द्युमत्⟧, possessed of lustre, and ⟦सुवीर्यं⟧, endowed with fine ability, fit to be given and to be enjoyed. Or else: ⟦दीदिहि⟧, shine, ⟦अस्मे⟧, for our sake, in such a way that it is ⟦द्युमत्⟧, possessed of brilliance, and ⟦सुवीर्यं⟧, endowed with fine vigour. Moreover: ⟦स्तोतृभ्यः⟧ — the dative is in the sense of the genitive — of the hotṛ and the others who make the praise that concerns you, ⟦स्वस्तये⟧, for their not being destroyed, for their well-being, ⟦भव⟧, be, ⟦अंतमः⟧, the nearest; stay exceedingly close to them.

⟦पावक⟧: the root is pū, "to purify". After this root comes the suffix ṇvuc. Because it is ṇit there is vṛddhi. Aka is the substitute for vu. Because it is a vocative it has the anudātta that is taught in the eighth book. ⟦दीदिहि⟧: the root is div, "to play" and so on. After it comes yaṅ. The lengthening of the reduplicative syllable is by ⟦तुजादीनाम⟧. By ⟦संज्ञापूर्वको विधिरनित्य⟧ there is no guṇa of the reduplicative syllable. The yaṅ is elided by ⟦बहुलं छंदसीति⟧. In loṭ, sip. Because of the statement ⟦चर्करीतं परस्मैपदमदादिवच्चेति⟧, "the form with yaṅ elided takes the parasmaipada and is like the class beginning with ad", the śap is elided. Hi is the substitute for sip. The v is elided by ⟦लोपो व्योर्वलीति⟧. Because hi is not pit there is no guṇa. The accent is lost. ⟦द्युमत्⟧: ⟦दिव उदित्युकारः⟧: the u is by the rule that ut comes for div. The matup is udātta by ⟦ह्रस्वनुड्बामिति⟧. ⟦सुवीर्यं⟧: by ⟦वीरवीर्यौ⟧ the latter member has the udātta on its first syllable. ⟦अंतमः⟧: "it has an anta", hence antika. The suffix ṭhan is by ⟦आत इनिठनाविति⟧. "Exceedingly antika": in the sense of surpassing, tamap comes by ⟦तमबिति⟧. By ⟦तमे तादेश्वेति⟧ the part that begins with t is elided. Because it is nit the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦स्वस्तये⟧: the accent is that of a nominal stem.

## Ṛk 9

O Agni! ⟦विप्राः⟧, those who send forth the praises, ⟦जागृवांसो⟧, awake through not being negligent in the rites, the Agnihotra and the rest, ⟦विपन्यवः⟧ — the word vipanyu denotes a wise man, because ⟦विपश्चिद्विपन्युरिति⟧ is read among the names for that — ⟦विपन्यवो⟧, the wise, the praisers, ⟦सं⟧ ⟦इंधते⟧, ⟦तं⟧ ⟦त्वा⟧, you who are such: ⟦हव्यवाहं⟧, the carrier of the oblations, ⟦अमर्त्यं⟧, free from the nature of dying, ⟦सहोवृधं⟧, growing by strength in the form of churning; they make you blaze well with the offerings of Soma and the rest.

⟦विप्राः⟧: the root is vap, "to sow seed". After this root, because the suffix ran and the i are laid down ready-made by ⟦ज्रेंद्राग्रवज्रविप्रेति⟧, the udātta is on the first syllable. ⟦विपन्यवः⟧: "he goes to pana, praising, praise, of various kinds", hence vipanyu. The suffix ku is by ⟦मृगया-वयश्चेति⟧. The elision of the ā is Vedic. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦जागृनांसः⟧: the root is jāgṛ, "to end sleep". After it comes liṭ. Its substitute is kvasu. Num comes by ⟦उगिदचामिति⟧. The absence of reduplication is Vedic. Because of the restriction ⟦वस्वेकाजा-द्वसामिति⟧ there is no augment iṭ. The guṇa by ⟦जाग्रोऽविचिण्णल् जित्सु⟧ (⟦पा. ७-३-८५⟧**NOTE**{the supplied reference is pā. 7-3-85; the reference text has 2-3-85 here}) does not take place, because in the word vi there kvasu too is included. The accent is that of the suffix. ⟦इंथते⟧: the root is indh, "to shine". It here includes the sense of the causative. The accent is lost. ⟦हव्यवाहं⟧: the root is vah, "to carry". The suffix ṇvi is by ⟦वहश्चेति⟧. The penultimate takes vṛddhi. The latter member, formed with a kṛt suffix, keeps its original accent. ⟦सहोवृधं⟧: the root is vṛdh, "to grow". The suffix kvip is by ⟦क्विप् चेति⟧.
