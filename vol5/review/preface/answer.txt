## Preface (Pīṭhike)
### Printed p. iii

This part of the Ṛgveda Saṃhitā, which contains the fourth adhyāya of the first aṣṭaka, comprises Sūktas 47–61 of the first Maṇḍala. Of these, the seer of Sūktas 47–50 is the ṛṣi Praskaṇva Kāṇva; of Sūktas 51–57, the ṛṣi Savya Āṅgirasa; and of Sūktas 58–61, the ṛṣi Nodhā Gautama. As we have already explained, the seers of this Maṇḍala are called Śatarcins, that is, each of them is the seer of about a hundred Ṛks.

Praskaṇva is the son of the famous ṛṣi Kaṇva. In the Nirukta Yāska says ⟦ಪ್ರಸ್ಕಣ್ವಃ ಕಣ್ವಸ್ಯ ಪುತ್ರಃ ಕಣ್ವಪ್ರಭವೋ ಯಥಾ ಪ್ರಾಗ್ರಂ⟧ (Ni. 3-17): Praskaṇva is the son of the ṛṣi Kaṇva; he says that, like Kaṇva and the others, he too is gifted with inspiration. This ṛṣi is the seer of Sūktas 44–50 of the first Maṇḍala of the Ṛgveda (82 Ṛks), of the 49th Sūkta of the eighth Maṇḍala (10 Ṛks), and of the 95th Sūkta of the ninth Maṇḍala (5 Ṛks), in all 97 Ṛks. This ṛṣi's name is mentioned in such Ṛks of the Ṛgveda as 1-44-6; 1-45-3; 8-3-9; 8-51-2; 8-54-8, and in Nirukta 3-17.

Little is clear about the ṛṣi Savya, born in the Aṅgiras gotra. The Sūktas seen by this ṛṣi are only Sūktas 51–57 of the first Maṇḍala of the Ṛgveda (72 Ṛks).

Concerning the ṛṣi Nodhā Gautama, Yāska in his Nirukta, explaining the expression, says ⟦ನೋಧಾ ಋಷಿರ್ಭವತಿ । ನವನಂ ದಧಾತಿ । ಸ ಯಥಾ ಸ್ತುತ್ಯಾ ಕಾಮಾನಾವಿಷ್ಣುರುತೇ⟧ (Ni. 4-16). The ṛṣi Nodhā, with his new praises of poetic form, reveals his hidden desires; likewise Uṣas too makes manifest her slumbering radiance—

⟦ಉಪೋ ಆದರ್ಶಿ ಶುಂಧ್ಯುವೋ ನ ವಕ್ಷೋ ನೋಧಾ ಇವಾವಿರಕೃತ ಪ್ರಿಯಾಣಿ ।
ಅದ್ಮಸನ್ನ ಸಸತೋ ಬೋಧಯಂತೀ ಶಶ್ವತ್ತಮಾಗಾತ್ಪುನರೇಯುಷೀಣಾಂ ॥⟧
(Ṛ. Saṃ. 1-124-4)

—so he says, in explaining this reasoning.

The ṛṣi Nodhā is a son of the ṛṣi Gotama, or a descendant of Gotama. This ṛṣi is famed as the seer of Sūktas 58–64 of the first Maṇḍala of the Ṛksaṃhitā (74 Ṛks), of the 88th Sūkta of the eighth Maṇḍala (6 Ṛks), and of the 93rd Sūkta of the ninth Maṇḍala (5 Ṛks), in all 85 Ṛks. He is spoken of in many places in the Ṛksaṃhitā itself—

⟦ತಂತ್ವಾ ವಯಂ ಪತಿಮಗ್ನೇ ರಯೀಣಾಂ ಪ್ರಶಂಸಾಮೋ ಮತಿಭಿರ್ಗೋತಮಾಸಃ ।
ಅಶುಂ ನ ವಾಜಂಭರಂ ಮರ್ಜಯಂತಃ ಪ್ರಾತರ್ಮಕ್ಷೂ ಧಿಯಾವಸುರ್ಜಗಮ್ಯಾತ್ ॥⟧
(Ṛ. Saṃ. 1-60-5)

In this and similar Ṛks it is said that they are sons of Gotama and that they praise Agni, the lord of wealth.

⟦ಏವಾ ತೇ ಹಾರಿಯೋಜನಾ ಸುವೃಕ್ತೀಂದ್ರ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಾಣಿ ಗೋತಮಾಸೋ ಅಕ್ರನ್ ।
ಐಷು ವಿಶ್ವಪೇಶಸಂ ಧಿಯಂ ಧಾಃ ಪ್ರಾತರ್ಮಕ್ಷೂ ಧಿಯಾವಸುರ್ಜಗಮ್ಯಾತ್ ॥⟧
(Ṛ. Saṃ. 1-61-16)

Here it is recited that the Nodhases, sons of Gotama, composed praise addressed to Indra.

The name of this ṛṣi is indicated in the Ṛgveda Saṃhitā at 1-61-14; 1-62-13; 1-64-1; 1-124-4; in Nirukta 4-16; in the Aitateya Brāhmaṇa 6-18 and other places. In the Pañcaviṃśa Brāhmaṇa (7-10-10; 21-9-12) he is called Kakṣīvata, a descendant of the ṛṣi Kakṣīvān. For this there is support also in the Aitareya Brāhmaṇa (Ai. Brā. 4-27; 8-12-17) and in the Atharvaveda (Atha. Saṃ. 15-2.4; 15-4-4).

Of the Śatarcins, only the three ṛṣis connected with this part are mentioned here. The account of all the seers of this Maṇḍala will be given in detail at the end of this Maṇḍala, that is, in the 14th part of the Ṛ. Saṃ.

To publish the Ṛgveda Saṃhitā—extremely ancient, extremely profound, extremely vast, and the foundation of all the śāstras—in simple Kannada together with a critical translation is no easy task. Yet with the weighty purpose that all people, scholars and laymen alike, may gain growth of knowledge through such a great undertaking and become recipients of divine grace, Śrī Jayachamarajendra Wodeyar Bahadur, G.C.B., G.C.S.I., the Mahāprabhu, lord of the throne of Karnataka, a patron of Vedic learning, himself a scholar, beloved of his subjects and devoted to God, is encouraging with his generous patronage the publication of this work, which requires great expense; this must be called the good fortune of the multitude of the people of Karnataka. To our generous-minded Lord, to his family, to the princess (who bears the name of Gāyatrī, the mantra regarded as the foremost of the Vedic mantras), and to the whole multitude of the people, may Sarveśvara, the Vedapuruṣa, grant in abundance the worldly fruits—life, health, prosperity and all wealth—and also spiritual knowledge of the highest good: so I pray. **NOTE**{ಇತೋಪ್ಯತಿಶಯವಾಗಿ printed; sense "even more abundantly" assumed}
