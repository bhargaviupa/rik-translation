# Vol 30 — independent QA sample 2 (English vs Kannada)

Date: 2026-11-25. Read-only check; no files changed except this report.

Files compared:
- E = `vol30/Rigveda_Samhita_Vol30_English_Translation.md`
- K = `vol30/Rigveda_Samhita_Vol30_Kannada_Text.md`

**Method.** Each page was cut out of both files by its page mark (E `### Printed p. N`, K `<!-- printed N -->`) and the two were read side by side. I checked:
- numbers and references;
- names, terms and quotations;
- negations and who says what;
- omissions and additions;
- each Pratipadārtha gloss against the Kannada gloss;
- stray running heads and remarks;
- word-list headwords without Devanagari;
- foreign-script letters.

Sanskrit blocks were not compared letter by letter. "Gist" paragraphs were checked against the Sanskrit as given in K and against the Kannada Anuvāda or Pratipadārtha. Line numbers are file line numbers.

**Pages sampled (24).** I avoided pages near 20, 130, 240, 350, 470, 580, 700, 810, 920, 1030, 1140 and 1250.
- Spread over the volume (12): 77, 187, 297, 411, 527, 641, 757, 863, 977, 1087, 1197, 1279.
- Pages with an Anuvāda, Pīṭhike or Nirukta/Yāska discussion (12): 51 (A), 101 (A+Nirukta), 172 (A+Nirukta), 310 (Pīṭhike+A), 363 (Yāska), 451 (A), 630 (Pīṭhike ref.+Yāska), 845 (Pīṭhike), 954 (Pīṭhike+Yāska), 1005 (Yāska+A), 1101 (Pīṭhike+A), 1214 (Yāska).

## p. 51 (E 1649–1680; K 2061–2097)
Checked: Gist, the Anuvāda of Sūkta 96 (anuvāka 8, sūkta 6, 13 Ṛks, metres 1–11 jagatī and 12–13 triṣṭubh, Ā. 6-4 and Ā. 6-2), and the header block (Vargas 5, 6, 7). All the numbers match.
- MINOR — E 1669 "Seer: Baru Sarvahari, son of Indra." K 2086 `ಋಷಿಃ_ಬರುಃ ಸರ್ವಹರಿರ್ವೃಂದ್ರಃ` names two possible seers. The Anuvāda (K 2070–2071) makes this explicit: `ಬರು ಎಂಬುವನು ಋಷಿಯು ; ಅಥವಾ ಇಂದ್ರನ ಪುತ್ರನಾದ ಸರ್ವಹರಿ` ("Baru … or Sarvahari, son of Indra"). The English header merges them into one person. It should read "Baru, or Sarvahari son of Indra".
- MINOR — E 1663 "The Anukramaṇī says what has been quoted above. It is explained by the Āśvalāyana Śrauta Sūtra (Ā. 6-4) …". The Kannada Anuvāda (K 2074, 2076–2078) repeats the Anukramaṇī text and both sūtra quotations (`ಪ್ರ ತೇ ಮಹ ಊತೀ … ಯಾಜ್ಯಾ`, `ಪ್ರ ತೇ ಮಹೇ ವಿದಥೇ … ಜಗತ್ತಃ`). The English Anuvāda leaves out all three quotations. They do appear in the Gist above it, so nothing is lost on the page, but the Anuvāda is shortened.

## p. 411 (E 13931–13950; K 15237–15271)

The bhāṣya (K 40292) has `ರೋಗಾದನೈಷಂ` ("I have led [him] away from the disease").
